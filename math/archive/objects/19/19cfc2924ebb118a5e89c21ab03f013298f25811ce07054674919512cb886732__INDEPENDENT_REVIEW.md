# Independent review: signed poles and the common-pole sector span

## Result

**Accepted within the declared N64 route and left-mask grammar.**

1. Every one of the 256 schedules with each pole independently equal to +1/2 or -1/2 excludes a commuting or anticommuting inner prefix. This is a finite classification, not an arbitrary unequal-pole theorem.
2. At every common complex pole t with t != 0 and t^64 != 1, the minus-input row frame has rank eight at every physical output row. Consequently a sum of the eight left-masked Greens that annihilates minus input has all 512 coefficients zero. There is no nonzero pure plus-to-minus component in this span.
3. The operator-level assertion remains true at t=0, but coefficient injectivity and rank eight do not.
4. At t=1/2, every plus-annihilating operator is diag(a)(G_0-G_1). This classification is proved only at that pole. Its pure minus-to-plus members have odd high-x masks and rank 16 when the masks are nowhere zero.
5. For every nonzero legal common pole, K=G_0-G_1 has rank 16 and is supported entirely on the minus sector. D K, with D the high-x character, is an explicit paid two-Green rank-16 mixer in the original left-mask grammar.

An immediate additional consequence of item 2 is that **every commuting or anticommuting prefix is excluded at every legal common pole**, including zero by a separate argument. This does not exclude general unequal pole schedules, genuinely mixing prefixes, products of one-way components, changed endpoint grammar, or the full N64 coefficient problem.

All constructor programs remained inert. The reviewer independently regenerated the routes and all arithmetic, including 96 complete integer determinant polynomials. The proofs below make no numerical spectral, numerical root, numerical seed, or coefficient-fit assumption.

The actual N64 routes are the previously defined nested lifts of the least-representative-spliced 4 by 4 covers. They are not independently spliced 8 by 8 covers. For completeness, geometry.py reconstructs them as follows:

- splice the small cover by rotating the old heads at the minimum vertex of every cover cycle;
- set eta=P_small T_small^-1;
- apply eta to the low coordinates of each large cover destination while preserving its two high fiber bits;
- the resulting four 16-cycles are merged by rotating heads at tails 0,4,32,36 in that order.

Every resulting route is one 64-cycle. The regenerated successor arrays match the complete saved arrays entry for entry.

Permutation matrices use P e_v=e_p(v). Define X e_v=e_(v xor 32), Pi_±=(I±X)/2, and E_± e_k=e_k±e_(k+32) for 0<=k<32. Every route commutes with X and satisfies P_j^32=X. The sector embeddings are unnormalized, but they are injective and have disjoint complementary images. No projector or basis change is being counted as a free executable primitive.

Green operators are G_j(t_j)=(I-t_j P_j)^-1. Their exact inverse guard is t_j^64 != 1. An additional t_j != 0 guard is stated whenever coefficient injectivity, nonzero weighted edges, or the rank-16 difference is asserted.

## 2. Exact row formulas and necessary pole equations

Let pos_j(v) be the position of v in route j starting at zero, and set

    d_j(i,k)=(pos_j(i)-pos_j(k)) mod 64,
    h_j(i,k)=d_j(i,k) mod 32,
    e_j(i,k)=floor(d_j(i,k)/32).

The geometric sum for a 64-cycle gives G_j[i,k]=t_j^d/(1-t_j^64). Pairing columns k and k+32, using P_j^32=X, gives

    V_i^sigma[j,k]
      =G_j[i,k]+sigma G_j[i,k+32]
      =sigma^e_j(i,k) t_j^h_j(i,k)/(1-sigma t_j^32).

Both sector denominators are nonzero under t_j^64 != 1. Also V_(i+32)^sigma=sigma V_i^sigma, so 32 physical rows suffice for a common-pole certificate.

For M=sum_j diag(a_j)G_j, the i-th row of M E_sigma is a_i V_i^sigma. The eight coefficients a_j(i) can be chosen independently at each physical output row. This independence is exactly what allows a row-frame rank theorem to address arbitrary left masks.

A nonzero row annihilating the forbidden sector exists precisely when

    rank(full 8 by 64 row frame) > rank(forbidden 8 by 32 frame).

Indeed, this says that the forbidden kernel is not contained in the full-row kernel. If all eight physical coefficients are required nonzero, the forbidden kernel must also fail to lie in every individual coordinate hyperplane. These conditions are jointly sufficient over C: a vector space cannot be covered by finitely many proper linear subspaces. They establish one pure-character row, not a whole factorization.

The forbidden-sector determinants become explicit multivariate polynomial equations after clearing the nonzero row denominators. Every eight-minor has total degree at most 8*31=248, with one pole variable associated to each row. Rank deficiency alone is insufficient. At t_j=0 for every j, all Greens are I, and both full and projected row frames have rank one; this is not a nonzero pure-character row.

## 3. Finite signed-half-pole classification

For each of the 256 sign choices t_j=s_j/2, all rational Green denominators are nonzero. At the verified prime p=998244353, 2 and 2^64-1 are units. Thus nonzero determinant residues of the normalized rational frames certify nonzero characteristic-zero minors.

The independent checker regenerated and verified:

- an eight-minor and full rank eight for row 0 on minus input;
- an eight-minor and full rank eight for row 1 on minus input;
- row-0 plus rank seven in exactly 128 schedules, precisely when s_0=s_1;
- row-0 plus rank eight in the other 128 schedules.

Every saved minus and seven-minor residue was reproduced. For the rank-eight plus cases, the source supplies a determinant without recording its column list; the checker independently rebuilds the lexicographic pivot columns and reproduces that determinant too.

In the rank-seven cases, equality of the H0 rows and a nonzero seven-minor establish that the only coefficient kernel is span(1,-1,0,0,0,0,0,0). Any such pure-minus row forces six coefficients zero. This fact alone would still not settle a full anticommuting prefix, but the row-1 minus rank-eight certificate does.

To see the prefix exclusion, suppose F64=O C, where O belongs to the declared outer span and C X=epsilon X C, epsilon=±1. Since F64 is invertible, C must be invertible. Fourier row f_i obeys f_i X=(-1)^i f_i. The row f_i C^-1 therefore has character epsilon*(-1)^i. A commuting prefix makes row 0 of O pure plus, which would annihilate minus. An anticommuting prefix makes row 1 pure plus. Both contradict the corresponding rank-eight minus frame. This argument allows zero outer coefficients and excludes singular prefixes separately by invertibility.

It covers only the specified 256 schedules. Distinct arbitrary complex poles are not classified by this test.

## 4. Universal common-pole minus rank

Now set every t_j=t. Remove the common minus denominator 1+t^32. The numerator frame consists of integer signed monomials (-1)^e t^h.

For each independent physical row i, the source supplies two eight-column sets, complete integer determinant polynomials f_i,g_i, their monic common divisor d_i, and a guard factorization. The reviewer regenerated both polynomials by the full Leibniz sum, enumerating all 8! terms. This is independent of the constructor's subset determinant algorithm. All coefficients, exponents and signs agree.

The reviewer then proves that the saved divisor is the exact Q[t] gcd as follows:

1. Divide both regenerated polynomials by d_i using exact integer polynomial division, with zero remainder.
2. Verify that both quotient leading coefficients remain nonzero modulo p=998244353.
3. Compute the quotient polynomial gcd modulo p and obtain 1.

The last step certifies their resultant is nonzero modulo p, hence nonzero over Q. Thus the rational quotients are coprime. This is an exact characteristic-zero coprimality certificate, not a sampled complex evaluation or an assumption that every finite-field specialization preserves rank.

The factorization is checked by exact polynomial multiplication. Every nonzero-root factor is independently verified by exact division to divide t^64-1. Repeated factors are permitted. Thus d_i has no root outside zero and the 64th roots of unity.

For example, the row-0 gcd is

    t^40 (t-1) (t^16+1)^2.

Its two column sets are [0,1,2,3,5,8,9,10] and [26,12,6,0,14,3,24,18]. The review verifies all 32 rows, not just this example. At every t != 0 with t^64 != 1, f_i and g_i cannot both vanish, so at least one eight-minor is nonzero. Consequently every V_i^-(t) has rank eight. The partner rows follow from the exact sign relation.

This proves the all-legal-common-pole result without finding, approximating or enumerating any complex roots.

## 5. Operator span and signed-prefix consequences

For nonzero legal common t, M Pi_-=0 forces every physical coefficient a_j(i)=0, since each minus row frame has rank eight. In particular:

- the full coefficient-to-operator map is injective and has dimension 512;
- its minus-input restriction is injective and also has dimension 512;
- no nonzero pure plus-to-minus operator exists in this left-mask span;
- no nonzero pure-plus output row can be supplied where the Fourier signed-prefix argument requires it.

The last point extends the signed-prefix exclusion to every nonzero legal common pole, rather than only ±1/2. The argument from section 3 uses row 0 for commuting prefixes and row 1 for anticommuting prefixes.

At t=0, each Green is I, so M is diagonal with entries sum_j a_j(i). A diagonal operator annihilating E_- is zero. Likewise a nonzero coordinate row cannot be a pure X character. Thus the operator no-go and signed-prefix no-go extend to zero, while the coefficient representation has a large kernel there. The nonzero-pole injectivity statement must not be extended to t=0.

None of these facts prohibits an operator with both sector inputs present or a product whose cancellations occur only after composition.

## 6. Rank-16 difference and the one available direction

The H0-minus and H0-plus permutations induce the same 32-cycle on the plus sector. Their quotient successor arrays agree exactly. Therefore G_0(t) Pi_+=G_1(t) Pi_+ for every legal common t.

The independently regenerated relative permutation P_0^-1 P_1 has 16 two-cycles and 32 fixed points. Hence rank(P_0-P_1)=64-48=16 over C. For t != 0 and t^64 != 1,

    K(t)=G_0(t)-G_1(t)=t G_0(t)(P_0-P_1)G_1(t)

has rank 16 because the two Greens are invertible. Both commute with X and their difference annihilates plus, so

    K=Pi_- K Pi_-.

Let D=diag(1,...,1,-1,...,-1), with 32 entries of each sign. D exchanges the sectors. Then

    M=D K=Pi_+ M Pi_-,  rank M=16,  M^2=0.

This is exactly two permitted terms, with common pole and left masks chi_x and -chi_x. Those two masks are everywhere nonzero. It is not a full eight-term coefficient layer with all eight masks nonzero, and it supplies just one pure mixing direction.

The universal minus-frame theorem also ensures every row of K(t) is nonzero at every nonzero legal common t. Since K_(i+32,:)=-K_(i,:), the component diag(a)K is pure minus-to-plus if and only if a(i+32)=-a(i), at every such t. If a is nowhere zero its rank remains 16. Masks with zeros can lower the rank.

At t=1/2, all plus frames additionally have rank seven, proved by the exact H0 row equality and 32 independently regenerated seven-minor polynomials with nonzero rational evaluation. Thus the complete plus-annihilator space is precisely {diag(a)K}, of dimension 64. Its pure minus-to-plus subspace has dimension 32. The coefficient map restricted to plus input has rank 448 and kernel dimension 64. The constant-coefficient Green span has dimension eight, with plus restriction dimension seven and minus restriction dimension eight.

The full plus-kernel classification has not been proved for every legal common t. The odd-mask characterization within the explicit component family does extend to every nonzero legal t, as explained above.

## 7. Costs, seed compatibility, and boundaries

Applying K or D K pays two length-64 Green scans, one subtraction, and, for D K, one left character mask. In the original term grammar those are two paid masked Green occurrences. A projector, a right mask, an inverse route, or an additional composition cannot be introduced for free from the operator identity.

The accepted N16 seed uses distinct poles 1/2,1/3,...,1/9. A common-pole component changes the schedule and does not automatically preserve that seed or its coefficient law. The present result is not an all-size recursion or a Fourier factorization. It gives no arithmetic lower bound from rank alone and makes no faster-transform claim.

The next paid bidirectional-shear construction, if accepted, will explicitly change this grammar and be reviewed separately. It cannot be presented as contradicting the one-way span theorem.
