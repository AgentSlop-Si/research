# Phase20 precision and singularity gate

The mathematical result and code are exact over supplied modular fields containing Nth roots of unity. `==0`, polynomial gcd, resultant interpolation, phase-node matching, and division are **exact field operations**. These properties are not automatically preserved in IEEE floating point.

The rational spectral division is `R_k = H(omega^k) / (1-alpha omega^(k delta))`. For `alpha` near a reciprocal root, the denominator can have arbitrarily small magnitude even if it is nonzero, so relative perturbation in the numerator is amplified by the reciprocal magnitude. Near repeated roots the Sylvester resultant/gcd reconstruction itself may be ill-conditioned. The two-root Prony/Vandermonde step in Phase19 has its own independent near-collision difficulty.

At an **exact** denominator zero, the implementation never divides: if gcd(delta,N)=1 it directly computes the single missing frequency at O(N) additional field cost, otherwise this fast path declines and the ordinary exact FFT fallback is run.

No claims about stable floating-point FFT, fixed machine precision, integer multiplication, quantum circuits or bit complexity follow from this exact field arithmetic theorem. A future result would need a uniform precision/error bound and corresponding bit-work ledger.
