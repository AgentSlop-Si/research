# Prior art and scope — Phase19

The mathematical tools used here are established: cyclic convolution / Fourier diagonalization, Prony/annihilating-filter estimation, Berlekamp–Massey, Vandermonde interpolation, sparse polynomial root enumeration over a supplied root-of-unity subgroup, exact convolution certificates, fast ordinary DFT fallback and finite-rate-of-innovation models.

- Marvasti et al., *A Unified Approach to Sparse Signal Processing* (2009/2012): https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.1853
- Structured low-rank/annihilating-filter survey: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8754413/
- Prony methods for structured-function recovery (Plonka and Tasche, 2014): https://doi.org/10.1002/GAMM.201410011
- The independent CrocSwap conditional multiplication line: https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds

**Claimed project-specific addition:** a reproducible factor-blind spectral inverse algorithm, with exact all-coordinate cyclic certificate and explicit fallback, integrated into the user's Green graph research; plus coprime shift ratio-multiplicity discovery, bounded multi-defect class gate and negative controls. These materials do **not** claim the first discovery of Prony, Fourier interpolation, sparse inverse convolution, or a world-first FFT bound.

CrocsSwap's proof discipline (paid operations, immutable certificates, exhaustive corruption controls) is useful; its conditional asymptotic exponent framework is not imported into the Phase19 mathematics.
