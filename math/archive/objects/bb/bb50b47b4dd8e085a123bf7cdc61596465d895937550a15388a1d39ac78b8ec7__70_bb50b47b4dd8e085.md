# A different O(N)-apply global stage that escapes the fixed-order cut-rank gate

**October 9, 2026.** Exact all-N stage construction, exact bounds, separate finite-field dense-inverse audit, and explicit warnings. **Not an FFT factorization, not a speedup, and the Householder 1-condition amplification alone is NOT substantive progress toward a factorization.**

## Construction (N even)

Let `T=tridiag(-1,3,-1)`, `G=T^-1`. Let `sigma` be the *parity ordering* of the coordinates:

    sigma = (0,2,4,...,N-2, 1,3,5,...,N-1).

Let `P` be the permutation matrix that simultaneously reorders rows/columns into this parity order. Define, with `eps=1/20`,

    M = P G P^T + eps G.

Let `b=N-1`, `z=ceil(sqrt(b))` (an integer), `u=(z,1,...,1)^T`, and let the rational Householder reflection be

    H = I - 2 u u^T / (u^T u).

Finally,

    S = H M.

## All-N exact properties

**1. Full density of M.** Since `T=3I-A_path` with `A_path>=0` irreducible and `rho(A_path)<3`, `G=(1/3) sum_{j>=0} (A_path/3)^j` is entrywise strictly positive, so the sum `M` is entrywise positive. This is true for all N.

**2. O(N) arithmetic and input-independent setup.** An application of M consists of (i) permuting input by sigma, (ii) one tridiagonal solve with T, (iii) inverse permutation of output, (iv) another T solve, (v) multiplication by fixed rational epsilon and addition. All passes are O(N), with O(N) vectors and O(N) fixed tridiagonal-factor preparation. Applying H requires one dot product and one rank-one vector subtraction, O(N). Neither dense-matrix materialization nor a precomputed FFT result is used by the executor. The provided `apply_mixed` tests this exact circuit against the explicit matrix in the finite field, but only the explicit circuit is the algorithm under consideration.

**3. Guaranteed linear cut rank.** For `N=2m`, let E/O be the even/odd original indices. In the parity order, the upper-right half block of `PGP^T` is `G[E,O]`. Reorder T by parity:

    T_parity = [[3 I_m, C],
                [C^T, 3 I_m]],

where C is a nonsingular bidiagonal matrix with diagonal -1. The inverse block formula gives

    G[E,O] = -(3 I_m - C C^T/3)^-1 C/3.

Both factors are invertible, so `rank(G[E,O])=m`. Meanwhile, `G[0:m,m:N]` has rank <=1 (inverse of an irreducible tridiagonal). Therefore, by the rank perturbation inequality,

    rank(M[0:m,m:N]) >= m-1.

Since `S=M - 2u(u^T M)/(u^T u)` differs from M by rank one,

    rank(S[0:m,m:N]) >= m-2.

This is `Omega(N)` natural-order quasiseparable cut rank despite an O(N) single-stage algorithm. Therefore, the earlier two-stage **constant-channel quasiseparable** obstruction does not apply to this new stage.

**4. Uniformly bounded spectral condition.** Since T is SPD and its eigenvalues lie in [1,5], `||G||_2<=1` and `sigma_min(G)>=1/5`. Permutation conjugation does not change singular values. The perturbation satisfies `||eps G||_2<=1/20`, so

    sigma_max(M) <= 1+1/20 = 21/20,
    sigma_min(M) >= 1/5-1/20 = 3/20,
    kappa_2(M) <= 7.

H is exactly orthogonal, hence `kappa_2(S)=kappa_2(M)<=7` for all N.

**5. Induced 1-condition grows linearly, even while kappa_2 is bounded.** The Neumann expansion of G and `||A_path||_1<=2` show `||G||_1<=1`; `||T||_1<=5`. Hence

    ||M||_1 <= 21/20,
    ||M^-1||_1 <= 5/(1-5/20) = 20/3,
    kappa_1(M) <= 7.

H is a symmetric involution. Its first column has off-diagonal entries `-2z/(z^2+b)`. Since `sqrt(b)<=z<=2sqrt(b)` for b>=1,

    ||H||_1 >= 2bz/(z^2+b) >= (2/5)sqrt(b),
    kappa_1(H) = ||H||_1^2 >= 4b/25.

By submultiplicativity of condition numbers, `kappa_1(H)<=kappa_1(HM) kappa_1(M)`. Thus

    kappa_1(S) >= 4(N-1)/175,
    kappa_2(S) <= 7.

Every scalar in the stage is a rational function of the integer z. Thus its coefficients can be prepared with basic exact arithmetic, without sqrt constants or uncharged lookup tables.

## Finite tests (not all-N proofs)

The exact `mixed_order_green.py` audit over F_998244353 tested N=8,16,32,64. In all four cases:

- `PGP^T`, M and S had full half-crossing rank N/2;
- M and S had N² nonzero entries;
- both M^-1 and S^-1 had N² nonzero entries;
- four fresh vectors per length agreed between the O(N) algorithm and explicit full matrices.

The inverse-density and *exact full* half-rank observations remain finite checks, not universal theorems. Uniform claims proved above are full density of M, rank at least m-1 for M and m-2 for S, and the two condition-number bounds.

## Why this does NOT beat FFT

This is a **genuine escape from a previously restricted stage family**, not an exact Fourier factorization. The rank-1 H multiplier inflates the 1-condition with no new information mixing. A proposed Fourier decomposition using this stage must show that the Householder updates do NOT telescope away as a trivial gauge, and that the total number of stages times their charged O(N) cost is o(NlogN). Having one strong mixing stage is not sufficient; a complete exact all-N identity is still absent.

If S factors are proposed, strip rank-one H components before claiming a true improvement.

## An exact complex-phase-capable version

An all-rational, all-real stage cannot by itself produce an N-point complex Fourier matrix for N>2: products of rational real matrices remain rational real. To avoid that trivial algebraic obstruction, define `omega=exp(-2 pi i/N)` and

    D_L[i,i] = omega^(i*i),
    D_R[j,j] = omega^j,
    S_phase = D_L * H * M * D_R.

For every nonzero unit-magnitude root, these two diagonal transforms preserve the ranks of *corresponding* matrix subblocks, full density, both induced 1-norms/condition numbers and spectral condition numbers. They take two additional O(N)-work passes, and their scalars can be prepared in O(N) operations from a supplied primitive root by running recurrences. `phase_diagonals` implements this and checks exact modular Fourier-phase versions at all four audited sizes.

This makes the candidate family algebraically compatible with complex-valued DFT operations but **is not itself a factorization of a Fourier matrix**. The proposed phases could still telescope or otherwise fail to generate enough independent structure. A genuine result must demonstrate a full exact decomposition and nontrivial asymptotic saving.
