# Independent review of N64 sector obstructions and one inner layer gates

## Accepted result and independent strengthening

For the declared N16-to-N64 nested routes and fixed poles 1/2,...,1/9, a row-masked eight-Green outer layer cannot complete any commuting or anticommuting inner prefix to F64. Both proposed signed-symmetry one-inner branches are therefore empty. This includes the plus-symmetric mean-preserving 128-variable branch and subsumes the earlier two-frozen-inner rejection.

The four-row sector certificates also imply a stronger unconditional mixing requirement than the constructor's frozen note states: in every successful one-inner lift, each high-x cross-sector block of the varying layer must have rank at least four, even when its diagonal sector blocks are singular. Consequently its high-x-odd part has rank at least eight, and at least four unequal high-x coefficient pairs across the eight masks are necessary. The extra assumption that the commuting part is invertible is unnecessary for these bounds.

The unrestricted one-inner systems and full 1536-variable N64 system remain open. These are fixed-route, fixed-pole coefficient restrictions, not an arbitrary Green-family no-go or an operation lower bound. No constructor program or numerical solver was executed. The original frozen packages remain unchanged; the strengthened kernel argument below is an independent review addition.

## All signed inner prefixes are excluded

Suppose F64=O C, O in S, and C X=epsilon X C for epsilon=+1 or -1. A singular C is impossible because F64 is invertible. For invertible C, its inverse has the same signed covariance. The nonzero all-ones zeroth Fourier row f0 satisfies f0 X=f0, so

    row_0(O)=f0 C^-1,
    row_0(O)X=epsilon row_0(O).

This forces one projected sector of a nonzero available outer row to vanish, contradicting the corresponding rank-eight frame. The proof permits arbitrary outer coefficients, including zeros.

In either one-inner branch, the fixed factors commute with X. A varying L with XL=epsilon LX makes the entire inner prefix signed with the same epsilon. Thus both signed branches are excluded. The earlier paired rank-six reduction remains a correct general algebraic identity; its solution set is empty for these actual fixed frames. It is no longer a viable branch for coefficient fitting.

This conclusion does not depend on the accepted seed values, beyond identifying the particular fixed inner geometry in which the branch was proposed. Any singular fixed factor already prevents success; any invertible commuting fixed factors preserve the argument.

## Stronger unconditional mixing lemma

Write either one-inner problem as O' L R'=F64. In the input-variable case O'=O K, K=H1* B, and R'=I. In the middle-variable case O'=O and R'=B H0*. In any successful product these fixed factors are invertible and commute with X. Multiplication by K maps each sector invertibly, preserving the direct-sum row conclusion; multiplication of the target by R'^-1 preserves its pure-character rows.

For either sign, the zero target sector gives

    A D + B0 C0 = 0,
    A=O'[I_sigma,:]Pi_sigma,  rank A=4,
    D=L_(sigma,sigma),        C0=L_(-sigma,sigma).

Every successful L is invertible, so its restricted block column [D;C0] is injective. In particular D is injective on ker C0. On that kernel the displayed equation says A D=0. Thus A annihilates the subspace D(ker C0), whose dimension is 32-rank C0. Consequently

    rank A <= 32-dim D(ker C0) = rank C0.

This proves, without any diagonal-block nonsingularity assumption,

    rank L_(+,-)>=4,       rank L_(-,+)>=4.

The constructor's inequalities rank C0+nullity D>=4 and rank C0>=nullity D are valid, but combining only those two loses information. Injectivity of the whole block column supplies the sharper conclusion. Equivalently, this is the off-diagonal rank preservation phenomenon for an invertible block matrix and its inverse.

The independent checker exhausts all 210 injective 4-by-2 stacked columns over GF(2), verifying the underlying left-nullspace projection identity. A separate 64-by-64 partial-swap control has diagonal sector rank 28, cross rank four and whole rank 64, with a rank-four A satisfying the required zero equation. It demonstrates that diagonal singularity does not invalidate the strengthened argument and that four is sharp for this abstract rank lemma. It does not construct an N64 Green/Fourier coefficient solution.

## Coefficient pair count

The high-x-odd component L_odd=(L-XLX)/2 has only the two off-diagonal sector blocks. Their domains and ranges are disjoint, hence

    rank L_odd = rank L_(+,-)+rank L_(-,+) >= 8.

Because every G_j commutes with X,

    L_odd=sum_j diag(a_j,odd)G_j,
    a_j,odd[i]=(a_j[i]-a_j[i+32])/2.

One unequal pair of coefficients contributes exactly two nonzero entries to that odd diagonal. Invertibility of G_j preserves its rank. Subadditivity yields

    rank L_odd <= 2 sum_j #{i<32:a_j[i]!=a_j[i+32]}.

At least four unequal pairs across all eight masks are therefore necessary, unconditionally within a successful one-inner problem. Term multiplicities are counted. This is only a necessary condition. Dense diagonal masks, high-rank odd parts and Green scans remain linear-work operations; no superlinear runtime conclusion follows.

## Complete one-inner outer elimination

There are two unrestricted choices, each with 512 variable inner and 512 variable outer complex coefficients, while 512 physical slots are fixed:

    input-variable:  O K L=F,  K=H1* B;
    middle-variable: O L R=F,  R=B H0*.

No inclusion between these two product families follows from their equal parameter counts. A successful L and O must be invertible. Singular L is an explicitly impossible stratum, rather than a case lost to a generic pivot assumption.

Right-multiply by the known F^-1. Set U=K L F^-1 or U=L R F^-1. For each row i define the 8-by-64 matrix T_i with rows row_i(G_j U). This is linear in the 512 varying inner coefficients. The outer row equation is c_i^T T_i=e_i^T.

For every rank r=0,...,8, membership is equivalent to rank([T_i;e_i^T])=rank(T_i)=r. It can be stated using all augmented (r+1)-minors and a nonzero r-minor, with the zero-rank convention handled separately. Given a particular solution and kernel basis, a solution with all eight entries nonzero exists over C exactly when no coordinate is identically zero on that affine solution space. A finite union of proper coordinate hyperplanes cannot cover an affine space over an infinite field. This is the complete nonzero guard, including lower-rank cases.

After rejecting singular L, U is invertible and every T_i has rank eight, because the original rational Green row frame has rank eight. Therefore the outer row, if it exists, is unique. Removing column i gives W_i of rank seven or eight; membership is equivalent to rank W_i=7. Choose seven independent columns, form their signed row-deletion cofactors k_i, and impose vanishing against the remaining 56 columns. Also require delta_i=k_i^T T_i[:,i]!=0 and all k_i[j]!=0. Then c_i=k_i/delta_i. The cofactors have degree seven, compatibility degree at most eight. There are 64 times 56=3584 scalar equations, plus invertibility, inner nonzero and chart guards. The union of all valid pivot charts is complete; failure of one chart does not exclude the family.

## Signed pairing and its now empty scope

Both branches have T_i[j,:]=row_i(A_j L S0), where A_j commutes with X and X S0=S0 Dpar, Dpar=diag((-1)^k). For signed L, row independence of the Green frame makes XL=epsilon LX equivalent to a_j[i+32]=epsilon a_j[i]. It follows that

    T_(i+32)=epsilon T_i Dpar.

The paired outer problems require e_i and e_(i+32) in row(T_i), with the second reconstruction scaled by epsilon*(-1)^i. Deleting these two columns gives an 8-by-62 matrix of rank at least six; simultaneous membership is equivalent to rank exactly six. A six-row/six-column nonzero pivot produces a degree-six two-vector kernel basis. The remaining 56 columns give 112 degree-seven equations per pair. The two target-coordinate columns must give an invertible 2-square normalization matrix, whose determinant has degree at most 14. Both reconstructed outer vectors must have every entry nonzero. Thirty-two pairs again give 3584 compatibility equations.

These chart identities are correct, and the independent checker validates positive and negative examples for all 32 row pairs, including explicit cofactor reconstruction. The signed branch has 256 varying inner parameters. A nonzero anchored seed mean excludes the odd branch; anchoring the plus mean leaves 128 parameters. Any anchored outer means remain additional conditions after reconstruction.

For the actual fixed N64 frames, however, the wrong-parity 8-by-32 template block retains rank eight under every invertible signed prefix. It cannot satisfy even the necessary rank-at-most-seven condition, much less the paired rank-six gate. Fresh actual-route controls verify the signed row identities and this wrong-parity rank-eight obstruction in all four sign/inner-position cases. Their auxiliary fixed commuting factors are independent rational controls, not reductions of the algebraic seed.

## Costs and replay

Applying a supplied coefficient product still uses all 24 length-64 Green scans, 24 endpoint diagonals, three eight-term sums and four F8 blocks, with routing charged. Under the stated two-pass scan convention, a conservative ledger is 72N+24 multiplications and 69N additions outside the bank; eight fixed wrap inverses may be shared across layers. The row-elimination reformulation does not make these terms free.

Preparation via all N basis inputs applies two inner eight-term layers, one bank and F^-1, then the eight outer Green templates to each column. With ordinary FFT evaluation this is O(N^2 log N), plus O(8N^2) storage and O(8^2 N^2) row-rank work. Dense O(8N^3) preparation is another safe bound. Polynomial solving, algebraic root representation and bit complexity are additional costs. No uniform fast coefficient generator is supplied.
