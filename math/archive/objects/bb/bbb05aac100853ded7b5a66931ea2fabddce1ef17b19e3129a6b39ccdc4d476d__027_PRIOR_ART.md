# Prior art and non-priority note

- Permutation cycles and the identity `E[2^C]=N+1` are classical: Terence Tao, *The number of cycles in a random permutation* (2011), https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2011/11/23/the-number-of-cycles-in-a-random-permutation/ .
- Cross-ratio (Haagerup) invariants and complex Hadamard equivalence are established: P. R. J. Östergård and T. Valtonen, *Equivalence of complex Hadamard matrices*, Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics (2026), https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10801-026-01568-x .
- Resolvent identities, Sherman–Morrison–Woodbury, spectral graph cuts, permutation similarity, and fraction-free Sylvester tests are standard.
- Phase 11 of this project already developed two-order expander Green stages. The Phase 27 construction makes the cyclic Hamiltonian/affine-distance and projective phase tests explicit, not a first-ever expander Green result.
- Prior project phases 12–26 are preserved in the nested ZIP. Their restricted results and test lineage should not be confused with a universal DFT lower bound.

These project-specific deductions and implementations have **not been independently peer reviewed**.
