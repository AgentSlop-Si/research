## Mathematical and operational scope

O(N) Haar butterflies with retained details, optionally following the Green sum. The real orthonormal Haar transform has kappa2=1; numerical condition and rank reports are sanity checks, not a Fourier factorization.

## Control/dataflow and algebra

haar recursively transforms pairs and reverses detail levels into the output order. apply_haar_green composes that map with the two-path stage. numerical_audit explicitly constructs H, G and S and reports norm/condition estimates.

## Input/guard and acceptance review

Definite custom-modulus bug at line 26: when p is not None, scale defaults to root_half_mod() rather than root_half_mod(p). Therefore a constant computed for 998244353 is reused under another modulus. root_half_mod itself assumes generator 3 is suitable but checks only 8|(p-1), not primality or exact eighth-root order. No assertion that 2*scale^2=1 modulo the requested p. Numerical audit reports orthogonality error and a claimed lower-bound value without asserting either.
