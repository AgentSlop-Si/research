# Exact Fourier operator compression — tested barriers and an honest next target

This project examines the user's original conjecture: can reusable exact FFT structure turn an **arbitrary fresh full N-point DFT** into O(N) work, with no cached answers, multithreading, uncharged lookup tables, or relaxed precision?

**Verdict:** Not solved. We built a compact exact rank-one *operator description*, but proved two independent limits on how it can be evaluated by restricted stable/bounded-complexity gates. We also tested full rank of natural Fourier blocks and the cost of decoding a symbolic rational representation. The mathematics is established prior art and classical linear algebra, not a new universal FFT lower bound.

## Reproduce

Requires **Python 3.10+ standard library only**. No network access, external math packages, or precompiled tables.

```bash
python -m unittest discover -s tests -v
python exact_audit.py > audit_results.json
python complex_check.py > complex_results.json
```

The audit checks identities exactly in the finite field mod 998244353, while the analytic determinant/entropy theorems are about exact complex transforms. Both settings and their logical separation are explained in `RESEARCH.md`.

## Results to look at

- `exact_audit.py`: exact DFT vs FFT on every basis vector, rank-one shift/diagonal displacement, ranks of contiguous Fourier blocks, pole-representation evaluation, stage-support entropy and macro cost checks.
- `tests/test_exact_audit.py`: regression and independent direct-sum checks.
- `complex_check.py`, `tests/test_complex_check.py`: independent numerical check of the complex determinant, unitary entropy and DFT map (not a substitute for proof).
- `audit_results.json`: machine-readable operation counts and finite exact certificates at N=4,8,16,32,64,128.
- `RESEARCH.md`: derivations, scope and external sources.
- `RESULTS_SUMMARY.md`: method-separated results and fair macro work table.
- `NEXT_EXACT_TARGET.md`: precise research direction that doesn't duplicate the past phases.

### What did NOT happen

- No arbitrary exact O(N) transform was found.
- No novel asymptotic lower bound is claimed.
- No real-world runtime speedup is claimed.
- No results are compared against CrocSwap as if solving the same computational task.
