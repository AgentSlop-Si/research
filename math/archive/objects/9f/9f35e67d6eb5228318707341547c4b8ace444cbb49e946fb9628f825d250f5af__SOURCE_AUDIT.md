## What is different here

1. Use **Rigby's all-infinite-chord-line incidence theorem** for odd regular polygons to limit cancellation to **at most two jointly zero columns per adjacent two-row primitive-affine Fourier sandwich**. Earlier FCUT only bounded these jointly zero columns by about half the length via 3-column full spark. New local lower bound is `n-2`, including odd composite n.
2. Prove local optimality by explicit symbolic chord-pair construction, with exact modular specialization checks for all 14 tested odd n in `[5,31]`.
3. Count local progression triples `g` in an otherwise arbitrarily non-affine Hamiltonian first cycle, creating a new project-internal mixed bound `nnz >= ceil((g(p-2)+(p-g)ceil(p/2))/2)` at prime p.
4. Transfer the strengthened density requirement into a **logarithmic polynomial-degree** lower bound on proposed exact polynomial replacements of the unresolved sparse-bridge inverse. This is a restricted computation grammar, NOT general scalar complexity.
