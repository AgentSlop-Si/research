## 1. Exact gauge and mask identities

Let N=M^2, eta=omega^M, and lambda_j(h)=omega^(j-Mh a_j), 0<=j<M. Equality of two nodes implies j=j' after reducing exponents modulo M; all V_h are invertible for any shear list. The polynomial geometric kernel satisfies K_h=A_h V_h, so K_h V_h^(-1)=A_h. This holds for every input and every admissible basis. It is an identity of the chosen factorization, not a claim that every Fourier algorithm must execute the row and temporal Fourier banks separately.

For these clustered omega^j gauges, lambda_j(h)^M=eta^j. The Phase4 routing condition therefore reduces to j=h independently of a_j. Each channel has M^2-M+1 nonzero kernel entries. The actual routed row depends on the shear, s=-h a_h mod M; it need not remain -h^2 outside the canonical a_j=j gauge. No rational 0/0 expression may replace that routed entry, whose value is M.

The channel mask E_Delta[t,j]=eta^(-Delta t a_j) consists of columns of an invertible M-Fourier matrix, with repetitions exactly at equal residues Delta a_j. Thus its ordinary rank equals the number of distinct residues. This is precisely the minimum number of separable row-column terms in a Schur-mask decomposition, not a count for arbitrary shared circuits. For coprime Delta every rank 1 through M occurs among universally invertible clustered gauges. Canonical shears give M/gcd(M,Delta), including rank one at Delta=0.

## 2. Weighted tree proxy

An edge difference e in the additive cyclic group has additive order M/gcd(M,e), which is its mask-rank weight. At threshold d, the allowed differences generate the unique subgroup with order equal to the lcm of all encountered divisor weights. Its cosets are exactly the graph components. Kruskal therefore needs M/L_prev-M/L_new edges at each successive weight d, proving the displayed sum.

For M=2^m, each of m merging levels costs M, giving M log2 M. For prime M the complete graph has constant weight M, giving M(M-1). For M=6 and 12 the budgets are 12 and 28. The all-zero-shear gauge instead has every mask rank one and tree proxy M-1 for the identical composed Fourier map. The proxy is therefore explicitly gauge-dependent. No conversion from this proxy into an unrestricted arithmetic lower bound is justified.

## 3. Conditioning and stable gauges

All clustered gauges share V_0[t,j]=omega^(tj). The binomial vector test for (1-z)^(M-1) gives the source condition-number lower bound. This duplicates the mechanism independently derived in the paired report; the earlier factor M^2/(2pi(M-1)) is slightly sharper than M/(2pi). Both grow at least exp(c M log M). Optimizing shears cannot change V_0. This is a condition-number statement about these explicit coordinates, not a sufficient precision budget or a bit-complexity lower bound. Uploaded high-precision numerical singular-value measurements were not rerun here and remain source-reported diagnostics.

With mu_j=eta^j and common shear, V_h is a unit-modulus row scaling of the positive-sign Fourier matrix, hence has complex condition number one. At common shear zero, V^(-1)=F_M/M and K_h=M F_M diag(omega^(-ht))F_M^(-1); K_h is circulant. The product K_h V^(-1) is the usual negative-sign Fourier stage with its twiddle. At h=0 all columns route; at h!=0 the kernel is dense. This validates the source's stable positive control and its cost qualification.

## 4. Independent composite-length extension

For Fourier-root gauges mu_j=eta^j at arbitrary M, universal invertibility is equivalent to

    gcd(a_j-a_k,M) does not divide j-k  for every j!=k.

Indeed, a collision exists at some channel h exactly when h(a_j-a_k)=j-k mod M is solvable. At prime M any unequal shear pair yields a collision, proving the source's rigidity theorem. At composite M nonconstant shear schedules can remain universal.

For the affine subclass a_j=cj+d, all node maps are

    j -> (1-ch)j-dh mod M.

They are permutations for every h iff every prime divisor of M divides c, equivalently rad(M)|c. Necessity follows by choosing h=c^(-1) modulo any prime not dividing c; sufficiency follows from 1-ch=1 modulo every prime dividing M.

For dyadic M>=4 take c=2. Every V_h is a row-scaled, column-permuted Fourier matrix and has condition number one, but its adjacent-channel mask rank is M/2. Large transition-mask rank is therefore compatible with perfect conditioning. This is a stronger diagnostic, not a faster transform: the same exact gauge cancellation still applies. Exhaustive non-affine schedules at M=6 also show why the affine classification should not be mistaken for the complete composite theorem.

## 5. Cost and source boundaries

The shipped direct reference algorithm charges raw fiber transforms, dense inverse recovery, and direct kernel evaluation in its actual loop structure. With bases already available, direct per-channel matvec work is Theta(M^3); dense inversion/preparation can cost Theta(M^4) arithmetic overall. Root discovery, modular exponentiation, inversions and bit operations are not automatically constant-time in an arbitrary finite-field implementation. The tests use small supplied suitable fields and are exact controls, not an asymptotic implementation benchmark.

The new gauge algebra is independent of Phase7's input-dependent low-template promises. Universality of an invertible coordinate system says that every input has coefficients; it does not make coefficient discovery or dense application cheap. Conversely Phase7's certified one/few-template families accelerate only restricted input sets. Neither statement implies inclusion of the other algorithmic contract.
