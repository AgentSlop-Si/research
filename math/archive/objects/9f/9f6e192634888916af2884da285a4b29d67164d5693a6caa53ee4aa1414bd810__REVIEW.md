# Green geometry source review, 0430

## Result

All 45 assigned exact source hashes received full static review: 221,428 bytes, 5,090 lines. All input hashes and sizes match; none overlaps the frozen prior coverage snapshot. No assigned program was executed, imported, compiled or AST-parsed. The package adds 45 full static reads against that snapshot without changing the central registry.

## Main findings

- Fresh N2048 cut check: 676 deleted vertices separate a 683-by-683 Fourier block whose determinant is 24,105 modulo 65,537. This is a valid obstruction for the represented four-stage graph.
- Phase32 probe labels are overwritten after testing: 16 of 17 saved records are mislabeled. The construction remains partial-column interpolation; q=N is quadratic online work.
- The public two-Green sandwich checker lacks denominator-invertibility guards. A=B=0 falsely satisfies its polynomial residual while the Green inverses are undefined.
- Dense inverse and full realignment rank can coexist with a sparse-plus-rank-one inverse, as a fresh exact rational N4 control confirms.
- Every stored growing-fiber arithmetic profile exceeds its own FFT arithmetic baseline; the largest ratio is about 1.024. No arithmetic or measured end-to-end FFT speedup is demonstrated.
- C++ rank-audit input/output validation, extraction/transfer trust boundaries and runner isolation limits are individually documented.

See PROOF_AND_SCOPE.md for mathematical arguments and PROVENANCE_AND_METHOD.md for evidence limits. INDEPENDENT_CHECKS.json distinguishes newly executed reviewer checks from unrun supplied programs.
