## Most consequential findings

- **Use the final coalesced-view result:** zero of eight setup-inclusive wins.
  Row27 explicitly supersedes the preliminary one-of-eight/1.13x result after
  charging dictionary compaction and reclamation. Its sole1.03x stream-only
  advantage is not robust. Full query cost includes +u snapshot destruction,
  and a severe floating cancellation counterexample remains.
- **Correct the original projected-view cost:** row38's q min(H,r+k) omits
  returned-output copying. Rows7/26 specify O(q[1+min(H,r+k)]) with valid history,
  O(q[1+r+k]) after history loss, and repair the older O(E) history scan.
  Retained event payload and backing-array ownership also matter.
- **Keep implementation bugs tied to source versions:** row67's claimed
  benchmark seed is contradicted by exact-hash row91's later audit, alongside
  its dispatch and memory-accounting defects. Row58's valid bivariate identity
  does not repair the numerical symbolic-coefficient defect reported in row101.
- **Preserve the Phase7 source-audit costs:** tuple hashing, linear seed scans,
  unbalanced concatenation and root/subtree cache differences prevent promoting
  structural O(log m) node changes to unconditional O(log m) API runtime.
  Its main benchmark checked three output positions, not every coefficient.
- **No new general FFT algorithm is supplied.** Restricted reuse, exact
  algebraic identities and architecture-specific negative results remain
  useful, without universal runtime or novelty claims.
