## New conclusive results

1. **Known entropy principle, project application:** a 2-coordinate unitary gate adds at most 2 bits of matrix entropy. `Phi(Fhat_N)=N log2N` => at least `N log2N/2` gates. This method is due to Nir Ailon (2013); NOT claimed as novel.
2. **Phase15 all components gate-optimal:** after normalization `Fhat = P_out (⊕F_d/sqrt d) Q R_u (I_L⊗F_B/sqrt B) P_in`, with `R_u=diag(sqrt(d/L))R`. The three entropies are `N log2B`, **`N-B`**, and `B+N(log2L-1)`. Their sum is `Nlog2N`. Every butterfly in the Phase15 executor individually gains 2 bits; the gate audit checks this at sizes 16..512 with max Fourier entry errors <2e-14. Pure reordering/fusing 2-coordinate unitary gates cannot reduce work.
3. **PATH GREEN UNITARY GIVENS THEOREM:** a path Cayley `C=(I+iαA)(I-iαA)^-1`, arbitrary real α, is unitary and strict-lower rank-one semiseparable. Use `(N-2)` bottom-up Givens (pivot nearest shared column) to make upper-Hessenberg, then `(N-1)` QR Givens to diagonalize: at most **`2N-3`** gates plus phases. Verified on weighted path Cayleys for N4..128 and α=1/32,1/2,2. Dense parameter construction is an audit only, **not a charged O(N) compiler** (the theorem counts existence of gates).
4. **HAAR-GREEN ALL-STRENGTH NO-GO:** Haar H_m is `m-1` pairwise unitary gates, and V_H= W (I+iεSwap)/sqrt(1+ε²) W* is `3m-2` gates. Each full Phase13 S_H=V_H C1 C2 thus has <=`(11N/2)-8` unitary pair gates for any real α,ε and any path orders. If a product of t such stages/inverses, permutations and unimodular phase diagonals equals normalized F, then
   `t >= N log2 N/(11N-16) = Ω(log N)`.
   This is **stronger in generality, not numeric constant**, than Phase13's fixed-strength ℓp bound. N16,32,64,128 full-stage compiled controls matched original code to <4e-15.
5. **HAAR ENTROPY:** `Phi(H_m)=2(m-1)`; with `p=ε²/(1+ε²)`, `Phi(V_H)=N h2(p)+2p(N-2)`. At N128, V_H ~0.01045 bits; the full Haar-Green S_H ~19.0213 bits versus Fourier 896 bits. Global cut rank and ℓp norm growth are not measures of the full Fourier entropy.
