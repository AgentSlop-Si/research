## Mathematical and operational scope

Two Hamilton-path Green sum in O(N) field operations for a valid permutation and usable modular pivots. A deterministic seeded order does not itself establish the all-order probabilistic expansion theorem stated in a separate document. The source explicitly disclaims a DFT algorithm.

## Control/dataflow and algebra

random_order constructs a permutation; apply_two_path computes G*x, scatters x, solves a second path, and gathers according to order. path_union_edges correctly uses the inverse permutation to express PTP^T edges. Failure bound computes an entropy-expression bound, capped at one.

## Input/guard and acceptance review

Only order length is asserted; duplicates, negative indices and non-bijections are not rejected. A same-length duplicate order overwrites scatter coordinates and ceases to implement a conjugation. Parameter p is passed to tri_solve, but prime/invertibility conditions are implicit. Bound guard requires N divisible by 16 and >=32; binary_entropy silently maps any value outside (0,1) to zero.

## Cost accounting

Permutation generation, two solves and scatter/gather use O(N) operations/memory at fixed p. Python modular inversion is not unit-cost when field size varies. The entropy formula is a numerical upper-bound evaluation, not an exact rational probability enclosure.
