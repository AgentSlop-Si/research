# Exact-source static review: constructor batch 2

## Result and limits

All eight assigned exact source hashes, totaling 54,430 bytes and 919 lines, were read completely as inert text in this review. No supplied source was executed, imported, compiled, or parsed into an AST. Source bytes were not modified. This closes the full static-read gap for these eight hashes only; it does not claim execution coverage, dynamic testing, or a new proof of the previously reviewed mathematics.

The eight programs are newly authored constructor/control and reporting utilities preserved in the construction addition. They are not established original uploaded algorithm implementations. The source headers, construction reports, and exact manifests establish this distinction. The adjacent independent reviews explicitly state that constructor programs were not imported or executed. Those reviews establish theorem/certificate evidence using separate authored checkers; neither their mathematical acceptance nor their manifest checks constitute whole-source coverage of these eight programs.

The audit coordinator confirmed this batch is disjoint from Queue01–07 and the completed ten-hash constructor batch. The current post-v21 mapping and inventory had no exact whole-source read mapped to any of the eight. Accordingly all eight records are new complete static reads, without inherited coverage. Similarity was not used as a substitute: the two 227-line scale programs were each read fully, although their only byte-content difference is the dependency path on line 5.

## Findings that affect use

1. **Fixture-specific research controls, not hardened general verifiers.** Many structural, arithmetic, branch-completeness, and identity obligations are Python assertions. Optimized Python removes these checks and can still emit literal PASS fields. Trusted dimensions, canonical field representatives, field primality, and valid permutation/subset inputs are generally assumptions, not uniformly validated interfaces. Treat emitted reports as the output of the documented fixed fixtures, and retain the separate independent certificate audit.
2. **Query counts are not total execution or traffic measurements.** The scale controls count at most four target entries per sparse sandwich output, but also construct dense matrices, multiply/invert them, recompute inverses of permutations, and verify all basis vectors. The dense helpers are separately covered by `construction_source_code_review_0241` at hash `3f2d3e66…`. The literal control execution has cubic dense work and quadratic storage. The recovered one-vector cyclic scan is linear; that fact does not make construction, full verification, field preparation, or this literal Python program linear.
3. **The N5 summary trusts intermediate JSON.** It validates selected consistency properties and recomputes the small target formula, but does not recompute any supplied determinant, rank, direction, or transition certificate. For example, replacing a saved nonzero minor value with another nonzero pair leaves its determinant check satisfied. Several theorem/counter fields are literal constants. Its PASS is not an independent verifier for arbitrary or tampered certificate data. The distinct N5 independent review explicitly recomputed all 624 supplied determinants and branch sets, which is the relevant stronger evidence for the preserved dataset.
4. **Completion code is intentionally exclusion-fixture-specific.** It requires exactly two projective roots per component, no repeated quadratic root, and, after any finite column construction, an 11-row nonsingular augmented minor. A rank-compatible branch would abort at that assertion before the later general-looking PASS branch could be used. Rank-zero column continua are reported and not silently called excluded. This is not an all-input completion classifier.
5. **Keep finite-field diagnostics and complex exclusion separate.** The finite source checks do not themselves implement the properness/specialization proof. The N5 closed projective necessary system and separate reviewed proof supply the characteristic-zero implication, including singular endpoint limits. No fast general Fourier algorithm, general FFT lower bound, unrestricted monomial-endpoint exclusion, or numerical stability certificate follows.
6. **Portability differs between exact near-versions.** `check_scale_completion.py` imports an absolute workspace pathname; `_scale_completion_helpers.py` imports the adjacent helper. The observed absolute target currently hashes to the reviewed helper, but a packaged copy alone is not portable and the import does not authenticate its dependency. The local helper is path-portable within the full package; the other classification scripts still require specific sibling data and helpers in a prescribed order. `check_restriction_extension.py` additionally requires SymPy, without a pinned version.

## Per-hash analysis

### 1. `check_scale_completion.py`

SHA-256: `b79dde214193d9720f9a9b7b62c43a4eb654a90be4a1acd58d40deb049d76bbf`  
Extent: 14,336 bytes; lines 1–227, all read.

**Semantics.** Lines 9–44 recover the two relative cycle permutations and edge ratios from supplied two-supported directions. They check permutation supports, nonzero base coordinates, single-cycle structure and nonzero Green closure denominators. Zero edge weights are allowed. Lines 46–54 form every entry of UBV from sparse row/column supports and compare with a dense product. Lines 56–101 solve `V_ij x_i + U_ij y_j = K_ij`: two-coefficient equations induce affine graph transitions, unary equations pin a component, and residual equations detect contradictions or conflicting pins. Each free parameter avoids every zero-scale value. The final substitution and all-nonzero checks validate the returned scales when assertions are enabled.

Lines 103–151 reconstruct both inverse branches, check the full sum and inverse identity, exhibit compatible original endpoint permutations, and compare cyclic scans on every basis vector. The chosen original left permutations in lines 127 onward are generated controls, not extra user-supplied endpoint permutations accepted by the `recover` API. Thus the theorem's additional individually prescribed-permutation test is demonstrated on fixtures, not exposed as a general extra API input.

Lines 153–167 check the directional subsystem without confusing it with scale completion. Lines 169–221 construct 12 positive targets at N=5,8,9, with noncommuting cycles, monomial supports and zero edges, plus a joint-direction-pass/scale-failure example, zero-base rejection, singular-denominator rejection, and finite-field exhaustion/larger-field controls. The positive output counts 680 target matrix entries and 88 basis vectors. These are constructed targets, not newly discovered Fourier factorizations.

**Guards and acceptance.** Explicit REJECT paths retain useful certificates. Many format/identity checks nevertheless use assert, and noncanonical modular inputs can be nonzero as Python integers while zero modulo p. Global mutable `h.p` makes helpers unsuitable for concurrent independent field contexts. `green_apply` relies on its caller for a valid full cycle and nonzero closure. No broad shape or dimension schema is implemented; n=1 is outside its disjoint-support assumptions. Singular endpoint masks are outside the inverse-branch sufficiency claim.

**Costs.** `target_entry_queries` counts algebraic B accesses in one sparse sandwich only. The dense equality on line 52, structural dense products, reconstruction/inverse checks, endpoint controls, target generation, extra directional/gauge calls, and all basis checks are additional work. `h.pinv(r0)` is recomputed for each i on line 27. `scale_solve` scans n² positions and uses sorted forbidden values followed by list membership, giving a conservative quadratic comparison budget even though the supported affine graph has O(n) edges. Returned linear-size plans and O(n) single-vector scans are distinct from this literal dense control program.

**Provenance/execution.** The construction report says the verifier is fresh and no uploaded program was executed. Its exact manifest binds this source and matching `FIXED_DIRECTION_RESULTS.json`/`CHECK_RUN.log`. These are preserved authored-run declarations and output artifacts, not a reconstructed command/exit/source-hash execution receipt. The separate independent review expressly did not execute this constructor. No execution was performed in this review.

Accordingly historical execution is classified as a reported authored-run artifact; no new exact command/exit receipt is invented.
