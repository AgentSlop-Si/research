# Next exact research gate (do not repeat completed work)

The persistent target is a **uniform O(N)** arithmetic algorithm for an arbitrary *fresh complex vector*, producing *all N explicit complex scalars*. Input-dependent repeated values, outputs in implicit symbolic form, branch-based algebra and unit-cost Galois automorphism oracles do not qualify.

## Verified obstacles in this pass

1. Cyclotomic descriptions are O(N) to build, but the primitive orbit's all-output numerical expansion is a half-size DFT. A new evaluator must beat this circular equivalence with **ordinary charged operations**.
2. Algebraic branching or input-adaptive rational k, on a full-dimensional real/complex domain, reduces (nonuniformly) to an ordinary linear circuit by selecting a generic branch and differentiating. It cannot alone evade generic linear-circuit complexity.
3. Binary-only nonlinear gates can evade that argument through x_i^2=x_i. However, globally multilinear output circuits cannot, by Boolean cube uniqueness; explicitly expanding the Boolean quotient can be exponential (S^N has 2^N-1 monomials).

## A falsifiable direction, not a discovery

**Test whether a compact Boolean-quotient circuit can support an exact Fourier-specific basis conversion with sub-NlogN total word/bit cost, WITHOUT expanding all 2^N monomials.** A pass requires every fresh binary input, N explicit outputs in an explicitly priced format, small uniform code and polynomial (ideally near-linear) memory. An initial countertest is the x_i^2=x_i identity that breaks standard differential linearization; a second countertest is the circular-orbit expansion that hides an FFT.

For unrestricted complex inputs, any surviving candidate must present a **new all-output linear evaluator**, with uniform coefficient preparation, numeric representation and memory operations charged. It must either account for large/unbounded coefficients (Morgenstern/Ailon obstacles) or provide a genuinely different model with a meaningful reduction to the standard one.
