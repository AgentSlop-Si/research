# Prior art and appropriate attribution

The individual technical tools here are established: permutation cycle-rank identity, geometric-series evaluation, batch inversion, cyclic first-order linear recurrences, low-bandwidth elimination, the Sherman–Morrison–Woodbury identity, and singular-value approximation bounds. The novel-to-this-project element is the *combination*, not a claim of mathematical priority.

A particularly relevant publication predating this research is Paul D. Gader, “Tridiagonal factorizations of Fourier matrices and applications to parallel computations of discrete Fourier transforms,” *Linear Algebra and its Applications* 102 (1988), 169–209, DOI 10.1016/0024-3795(88)90325-4. It studies exact tridiagonal/permutation Fourier decompositions. General butterfly factorizations, including Li, Yang, Martin, Ho, Ying, *Butterfly Factorization* (2015), DOI 10.1137/15M1007173, also precede the present work and achieve O(NlogN) application in their model.

The Phase25 findings are **not independently externally vetted**, and do not assert any faster worst-case DFT or integer-multiplication algorithm. All earlier project work is retained in the nested Phase24 archive; it is not implied that the historical archive's every result was independently rechecked here.
