# Prior art and priority scope

- R. Craigen, **Equivalence classes of inverse orthogonal and unit Hadamard matrices**, Bulletin of the Australian Mathematical Society (2009), https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/bulletin-of-the-australian-mathematical-society/article/equivalence-classes-of-inverse-orthogonal-and-unit-hadamard-matrices/2577D37B7693C908DE93004BD008AB40 . It treats entrywise reciprocal/transpose orthogonality (type-II matrices) and equivalence by row/column permutations and scalings. The Phase 28 ratio-correlation operator is a convenient reorganization of these classical structures; we do not assert its entire theory is newly invented.
- P. R. J. Östergård and T. Valtonen, **Equivalence of Complex Hadamard Matrices** (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21653 . Existing algorithms examine monomial equivalence, so our implementation is a restricted Green-synthesis gate, not a claim to general algorithmic priority.
- N. Ailon, **A Lower Bound for Fourier Transform Computation in a Linear Model Over 2x2 Unitary Gates Using Matrix Entropy** (2013), https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.4745 . It addresses a restricted lower-bound model and does not mean Green-resolvent circuits have been universally ruled out.
- N. Ailon, **An n log n Lower Bound for Fourier Transform Computation in the Well Conditioned Model** (2014), https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.1307 . Relevant to conditions/precision accounting, not a direct theorem for the three-mixer Green family.
- Characteristic-zero reduction, polynomial resultants, Bezout identities, Cauchy–Schwarz, and generic (proper Zariski-closed exceptional sets) arguments are standard mathematics. Phase 28 combines them into **specific**, independently testable Green phase-matching restrictions.

No independent peer review or proof of priority is asserted. The exact code and certificates support the stated restricted claims.
