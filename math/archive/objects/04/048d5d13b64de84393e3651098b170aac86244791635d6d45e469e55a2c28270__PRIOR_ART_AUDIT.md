# Limited prior-art audit — v13

This is a scoping audit, not a novelty proof.

## Adjacent established areas

1. **Cooley--Tukey and mixed-radix FFTs.** Classical exact FFTs exploit factorizations of the transform length. v13's direct route also depends on a factorization `N=KM`, but computes a randomized projected spectrum and uses a different one-cycle recurrence.

2. **Ramanujan sums and unit characters.** The identity

   `c_N(t)/phi(N) = mu(N/g)/phi(N/g), g=gcd(N,t)`

   is classical. v13 uses the corresponding unit-orbit Fourier projector to certify a covariance norm.

3. **Random Fourier/partial Fourier projections and compressed sensing.** These typically recover sparse or structured signals, or approximate selected information. v13 instead keeps arbitrary dense inputs and explicitly returns all `N` approximate coefficients under an additive `ell_infinity/ell_2` guarantee.

4. **NUFFT/nearby-grid correction.** The inherited Bessel route is adjacent to low-rank and interpolation-based nonequispaced Fourier algorithms. It remains a fallback, not the new v13 theorem.

## What the limited search did not establish

The search did not locate the exact compound statement:

- unit-step chirp block projection;
- one `K`-FFT plus one `N` scan;
- covariance certificate
  `2 sum_{d<M} 1/phi(N/gcd(N,d))`;
- least-prime-factor and balanced-semiprime all-output consequences.

Absence from a limited search is not evidence of novelty. The package makes no priority claim and requires expert literature review before publication language is used.
