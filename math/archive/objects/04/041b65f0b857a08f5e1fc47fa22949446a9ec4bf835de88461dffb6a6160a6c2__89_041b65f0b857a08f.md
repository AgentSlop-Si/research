## Why full-field tabulation does not transfer directly to NTT

A length-`N` NTT over the prime field `F_q` needs a primitive `N`th root of unity. The multiplicative group has order `q-1`, hence `N | (q-1)` and `q >= N+1`. Consequently the field's alphabet grows with N.

A complete table of only the two-point butterfly over F_q has `q^2 >= (N+1)^2` input cases. It is already larger than the whole length-N problem. This rejects the **naive exhaustive value-table transfer**, not all conceivable NTT accelerations.

A second, independent barrier arises under a sequential bit model: explicitly writing N field elements using `ceil(log2 q)`-bit slots requires at least `N ceil(log2 q) = Omega(N log N)` output-bit writes. This does **not** apply in a word RAM with `Theta(log N)`-bit words (N word outputs take O(N) word operations), to compressed outputs, or directly to integer multiplication (whose final product has O(n) bits).

## Low-rank search audit

`phase3_barrier.py` uses a restricted sign-only rank-one search for a decomposition `H_N = u v^T + E` over F3. Applying this specific decomposition by computing one dot product and then adding every nonzero residual contribution has a simple *optimistic* addition count `(N-1) + nnz(E)`, ignoring scalar multiplications, accesses, and possible further common-subexpression sharing.

| N | Best residual nonzeros in this search | Simple rank-one+residual count | Standard WHT additions |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 |
| 4 | 4 | 7 | 8 |
| 8 | 22 | 29 | 24 |
| 16 | 96 | 111 | 64 |

Thus the restricted rank-one decomposition beats the standard count *slightly at N=4*, but is **worse at N=8 and N=16**. This is not an impossibility result for general low-rank/sparse decompositions or shared circuits. More meaningful search should optimize the total cost of the circuit, not just its rank or residual sparsity.

## Next falsifiable research target

To approach OpenAI/CrocSwap's integer-multiplication construction, look for a **fixed, input-independent, reversible linear network** with rank-weighted edge budget `s < Wm`, preserving arbitrary scratch values and verified tape scheduling. Its cost metric is not the number of equivalent input patterns or the number of butterfly multiplications; it is the total ranks of *representation changes* relative to the network's width and block parameter. Begin with small networks and exact rational/finite-field certificates, and reject candidates without end-to-end cost accounting.
