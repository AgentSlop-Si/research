# Green-stage FFT research: continuation checkpoint (2026-10-09)

The original archive is preserved unchanged under `../fft_global_green_gauge/`. This continuation follows the **global Green/overlap, graph, algebraic-reuse direction**, not the CrocSwap / integer-multiplication exponent-network track.

## Read these first

1. `HALFCUT_THEOREM.md` — **strongest proved negative result**: any exact two-stage `F_N=A P B` with natural-order quasiseparable stages must satisfy `r_A+r_B >= N/4` (even N), for **every** intermediate monomial shuffle; equal ranks need `r >= N/8`.
2. `MIXED_ORDER_GREEN.md` — **new fully charged O(N)-apply stage candidate/control**: conjugate a tridiagonal Green inverse into parity order, add a second Green inverse and optional rational Householder + complex phase diagonals. Provably has `Omega(N)` natural cut rank, `kappa_2<=7` and `kappa_1=Omega(N)`, with exact cost O(N). **Not an FFT factorization.**
3. `THREE_STAGE_CONTROL.md` — exact rank-one quasiseparable counterexample showing why the two-stage halfcut argument cannot be blindly iterated to three stages.
4. `THEOREM.md` — the **older, strictly weaker** sqrt-N obstruction and its first finite-field certificate. Retained for history.
5. `NEXT_GATE.md` and top-level `../CHAIN_RECOVERY.md` — what is unresolved and how to continue without retreading older attempts.

## Verified outcomes

- Exact linear two-stage obstruction for Vandermonde/Fourier, independent of coefficient magnitudes, norms, intermediate permutations, or condition numbers.
- In particular, two rank-2 sums of Green solves cannot implement an exact length-32 DFT (or any larger power-of-two DFT). The archive's failed N=32, r=3 numerical fit also has a rigorous explanation.
- Exact finite-field audit of the new mixed-order Green stage at `N=8,16,32,64`: full halfcut rank, full density of stage and inverse for these **tested** sizes, 8 fresh vector checks at each size (plain and phase-dressed).
- All-N proofs for mixed-order stage: `rank(S[I,R]) >= N/2-2`; full density for M; `kappa_1(S)>=4(N-1)/175`, `kappa_2(S)<=7`; O(N) arithmetic per application, including permutations, two tridiagonal solves, rank-one reflection and phase passes.
- **No exact Fourier factorization from these new stages, no O(N) FFT, and no o(NlogN) algorithm in this package.**
