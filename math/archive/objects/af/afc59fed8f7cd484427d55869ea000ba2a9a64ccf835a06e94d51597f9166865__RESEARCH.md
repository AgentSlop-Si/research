# Research report — paired Fourier blocks and dual polynomial feasibility

**Date:** October 10, 2026. **Goal:** independent exact Fourier arithmetic savings on all fresh inputs, counting every operation and setup cost. **Outcome:** a stronger mathematical test of the fixed 256-point four-stage candidate, not a faster FFT.

## Starting point

The prior checkpoint had 32 half-block rank gates on the middle two stages, an exact singular-value certificate for mask64, and a near-diagonal outer-inverse exclusion. The unrestricted simultaneous coefficient problem was not solved.

## What changed

1. **Complement symmetry doubles the rank-gate catalog.** Each saved parity set has an opposite-half partner with the same natural-order boundary count. The 32 old blocks yield **64** necessary rank restrictions. At the strongest mask64, both diagonal blocks of the middle product have rank at most 16; invertibility forces both opposite cross-blocks to have rank at least 112.
2. **Paired Fourier singular spectra and unitarily invariant norms.** The corresponding Fourier blocks have identical singular values by the ordinary cosine–sine decomposition. Applied to both rank-16 constraints, this yields `||C4 U C1-D4 U D1||_F^2>=96` and `||...||_*>=96` for arbitrary diagonal unitary baselines. Relative Frobenius separation is at least `sqrt(96)/||U||_F=sqrt(3/8)≈0.61237`. The earlier **operator-norm** threshold remains stronger in its norm.
3. **A denominator-free dual problem.** The Jacobi/nullity theorem rewrites all 64 half-block rank equations for `H=P3^-1 C4 U C1 P1^-1` as equivalent rank equations for `G=P1 S1 U^-1 S4 P3`, bilinear in entries of the **original** outer stages. This avoids inverse-variable denominators and handles zero scalar generators naturally. The remaining constraints on middle factorization are still required.
4. **Honest negative result.** The 64 elementary Frobenius-energy bounds, viewed merely as per-cell scalar inequalities, cannot improve the paired bound of 96. A concrete rational cell-energy assignment saturates them all. The next attack must use coefficient directions or algebraic minors, not just sum more scalar rank-energy lower bounds.
5. **Unitary-subclass boundary.** If the two outer inverse stages happen to be exactly unitary, a min–max vector argument strengthens the operator-norm separation to `>sqrt(2-2sqrt(7)/5)≈0.970412`; symmetric near-diagonal radius `>0.485206`. This does not apply to the general nonunitary research target.

## Best next gate

Use the **64 denominator-free dual rank equations** for `G=P1 S1 U^-1 S4 P3` simultaneously, with exact scalar quasiseparable recurrences for `S1,S4` and independent bilinear lifting/syzygies. Concentrate initially on mask64 and a second independent mask (168/156), preserving all zero-transfer branches. Find either (a) a genuine characteristic-zero contradiction, with a checkable polynomial identity, or (b) exact invertible outer stages satisfying these gates, then check whether the middle two-stage factorization exists. Do not infer a complex-number proof from optimizer failure or missing finite-field points.

The original asymptotic `o(N log N)` target remains unsolved in this architecture. Contemporary exact-complex DFT work claiming small all-length savings is a separate construction, not reimplemented or improved here.
