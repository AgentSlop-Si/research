# Independent audit: exact three-Green chirp B5

## Decision and scope

**Accepted.** The frozen constructor report `THREE_GREEN_B5_CERTIFICATE.md` provides an exact three-term decomposition of the forced five-coordinate chirp target into diagonal-ended weighted Hamiltonian-cycle Green matrices. Every endpoint is nonsingular in this construction. Two branches have reciprocal nonzero algebraic holonomies; the third uses zero weights and contributes a diagonal correction.

Together with the separately sealed all-order two-term exclusion, the minimum number of terms for this fixed target is **exactly three**, in the grammar allowing arbitrary diagonal endpoints, arbitrary Hamiltonian structural orders, and zero weights. This is a finite-size result. Neither the positive construction nor its minimality supplies an asymptotic Fourier algorithm, an all-size three-term family, or a conclusion about a larger grammar with unmatched endpoint permutations or products.

All mathematical sections 1–6, the displayed coefficient data, both-root/all-embedding guard claims, and the linkage to the previously audited lower bound were reviewed. The constructor's implementation was kept inert and was not code-audited or run. Its timing statement is platform-specific; our independent replay finishes in under a second here, but no asymptotic inference is drawn from it. There are no omitted mathematical appendices in this report.

## Independent derivation and controls

The reviewer authored `check_independent.py` from scratch using Python rational arithmetic. The base field is represented by four rational coordinates modulo 1+z+z²+z³+z⁴. Inversion uses an independently written rational elimination on a 4-by-4 multiplication matrix. The extension uses d²=−p1 d−p0 and conjugation d↦−p1−d. No constructor program is imported.

The checker independently constructs

and verifies both products with F5^−1+diag(z^(3j²)) are identity, checking 50 entries. It reconstructs the four normalized off-diagonal parameters from these entries, derives the quadratic rather than loading its coefficients, and obtains exactly

The source's rational reconstruction is then performed in the extension. The resulting target, pole, endpoint vectors, diagonal correction and discriminant agree exactly with the inert source JSON after normalizing its representation format.

### Why the synthesis lemma is valid

Write the normalized constants as α,β,γ,ε. With E=γ(d+2)−α−β, the formulas imply κr4=1+d. Three equations involving row 1 and the two equations involving column 4 follow directly. The remaining normalized (2,3) residual is exactly

P(d)/(d E),

where P is the displayed quadratic. Indeed its numerator is

(β−γ)[βd²+(β−α)d+β−γ]−(ε−β)d[γd+2γ−α−β].

Expansion gives precisely the three coefficients in P. The displayed guards justify every division. The four equations involving index 0 are immediate from vj+t v0 uj=B0j. Thus the derivation is constructive algebra, not a parameter count.

Independently, 379 legal root/parameter cases over F101 were generated from unrelated random reversal-symmetric off-diagonal data. All 3,790 resulting identities pass. These controls supplement, rather than replace, the symbolic proof.

### Actual Green operators

For row-successor natural cycle weights (1,1,1,1,t), W^5=tI. The reviewer builds the inverse by the finite series (I+W+...+W^4)/(1−t), independently of the triangular kernel asserted in the report. Both left and right inverse identities are verified for each reciprocal pole: 100 entries. Multiplying by the stated endpoint diagonals and adding the zero-weight diagonal branch reconstructs all 25 target entries exactly. Conjugating the entire result verifies all 25 entries for the other root as well.

### All guards and field degree

Twenty-seven norms are checked: the six base-field reconstruction/discriminant quantities, d, the t denominator, κ, t, 1+t, 1−t, and all fifteen entries of u, v and the diagonal correction. Every norm is a nonzero element of Q(z); every one also has nonzero residue at z=91 modulo 241. Norm(t)=1 and conjugate(t)=1/t hold exactly. Therefore both Green guards and all endpoint invertibility claims hold for both roots and all four complex embeddings.

The monic quadratic reduces to d²+30d+124 over F241, with discriminant 163 and 163^120=−1. Its coefficients are regular at the corresponding prime of the cyclotomic integer ring. If it had a root in Q(z), that root would be integral in this integrally closed localization and would reduce to a residue-field root, a contradiction. Thus the extension really has degree two over Q(z), and degree eight over Q. The nonzero norm certificates remain valid independently of this irreducibility proof.

### Exact minimality

The accepted predecessor review `review_n5_all_orders`, manifest 1dd18f780d9be8f19766fc37463036a704a5bebd2d64d07d97ffdf0d8c0fdeac, already excludes all 24² pairs of Hamiltonian orders with arbitrary diagonal endpoint masks. Its closed-projective proof includes zero endpoint entries and zero edge weights. Hence it excludes a one-term construction too, because an additional zero term would produce a two-term representation. The present three legal summands prove the matching upper bound. Repeated structural cycles are allowed by that grammar and introduce no mismatch.

## Cost and unresolved questions

Given a primitive fifth root, the certificate uses explicit rational field operations and one quadratic-root choice. The use of an algebraic root is disclosed; no claim is made that it comes for free in a different computational model. At this fixed dimension all reconstruction and application work is finite and explicit. Whether base-field-only or fixed rational-pole factors exist, whether the ansatz extends to other sizes, and whether a uniformly generated family improves fully charged FFT costs remain unresolved.
