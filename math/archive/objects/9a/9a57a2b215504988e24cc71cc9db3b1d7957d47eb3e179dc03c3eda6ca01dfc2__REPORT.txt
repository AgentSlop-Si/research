COMPLEX FOURIER NETWORK LABEL COMPRESSION
Bounded exact certificate experiment, 8 October 2026

RESULT

A structural change to the pinned round-six complex network improves its
certified recurrence saving from 0.000073861113 to 0.000073910830, a relative
increase of 0.06731147%. The experiment shrinks 1,968 intermediate binary
subspace labels by one dimension while preserving every tested local
residual condition. Parameters, circuit, retained centres, role count and
total residual rank stay fixed. This is a child-rank-distribution improvement.
Because W, m and s are unchanged, the single-direction recurrence has no
exponent improvement. Transfer to the earlier exact-DFT paper has not been
proved; our earlier exact-DFT exponent is not improved by this certificate.

The result does not improve the repository's integer-multiplication kappa:
its bit-side certificate is the bottleneck. It is not a proved improvement
to the earlier exact-DFT construction, a practical runtime claim, a Lean
formalization, or a literature-wide novelty claim.

THE BOUNDED CHANGE

Each producer node is a sum of weight-three indicator inputs. Let S_n be
their exact linear span over F_2. Upstream generally labels the node by its
full coordinate cover, with a tighter rule for inputs and common-pair sums.

For each node in topological order, candidates are S_n and S_n plus one
coordinate unit from its cover, retaining only dimensions strictly below
the original label. Candidates are sorted by dimension and canonical binary
basis; the first legal one is accepted. This bounded greedy family tests
25,828 candidates. It is not an optimization over all subspaces.

A candidate must remain nondegenerate and nonalternating, have a valid full
space complement, contain its current argument labels with valid residuals,
sit below its current user labels with valid residuals, and remain below
every target triple's perpendicular space with valid residuals. Every final
condition is checked again after all replacements. Retained total labels
are required to remain exactly unchanged.

Accepted dimension changes:
  1,320 nodes: 5 to 4
    600 nodes: 7 to 6
     48 nodes: 11 to 10

For a nested edge U <= V, the residual is V intersect U-perp. Validity means
that a nonzero residual has nonsingular Gram matrix and at least one
odd-weight vector. Those conditions permit an orthonormal basis over F_2.
Using smaller support spans without checking these conditions is invalid.

VALIDATION AND NEGATIVE CONTROL

The independent binary-form analyzer and an upstream-checker cross-check
both pass all 140,064 local checks:
  30,968 labels, 30,968 full-space complements,
  57,888 producer edges, 20,240 output-piece edges.

The wholesale support-span replacement is a failed negative control:
  10,962 invalid labels and 10,962 invalid complements;
  25,126 invalid producer edges and 792 invalid piece edges.
Thus rank reduction alone does not justify the result.

An independent ten-operation event replay rebuilds the complete child-rank
histogram. A second check using the original hist.build schedule, with only
the label dimensions substituted, matches every modified histogram entry.
The two histograms have identical total rank and identical largest child.

For child multiplicities n_r, the recurrence moment is

  M(a) = sum_r [r*n_r/(W*m)] * (m/r)^a.

The exact rational certificate proves M(a) < 1 at
a = 7391083/100000000000. Its conservative gap is about 4.85653e-15.
A separate Fraction-only implementation, using 50-term atanh logarithm
bounds and a 12-term exponential expansion with an exact tail bound,
also proves the inequality and proves M(a + 10^-12) > 1. The baseline's
same-grid boundary is 73861113/10^12. The absolute certified gain is
49717/10^12. These rational checks use no floating-point acceptance test.

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES

The scalar producer and arbitrary-scratch algebra are unchanged. Each mix
still occurs at one common frame; all new frame transitions satisfy the
same local admissibility checks. Copied retained centres, data endpoints
and correction children retain their original interfaces and charges.
Under the upstream whole-residual recursion and compiler assumptions, the
modified histogram therefore supplies the improved complex certificate.

This is a finite, reproducible certificate modification with a mathematical
reduction to the upstream interface. It does not independently formalize
that interface or its asymptotic transfer. No complete Fourier transform,
tape implementation, finite-precision analysis, Lean build or benchmark was
run. No external repository was edited, submitted to, or published.

REPRODUCTION

Python 3.12, standard library only. From the extracted bundle directory:
  python3 audit_reductions.py 24
  python3 verify_independently.py

The first command writes reduction_results.json, including all 1,968
concrete old/new bases and both complete histograms. The second writes
independent_verification.json. REPORT.txt explains scope; upstream/manifest.json
records source hashes; upstream/LICENSE and NOTICE preserve attribution.

Falsification targets are a failed exact residual condition, a mismatch in
any histogram entry or endpoint/copy charge, a failed rational moment
inequality, or an invalid upstream compiler/interface transfer. A larger
unsearched label family could give a different result; no optimality is
claimed. The bounded experiment stops here.
