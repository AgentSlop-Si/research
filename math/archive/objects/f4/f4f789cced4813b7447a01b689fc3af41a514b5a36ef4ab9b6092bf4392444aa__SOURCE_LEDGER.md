## What the experiments establish

The new Fourier plan obtains its weights only from the supplied primitive root and integer recurrences. It is not fitted against or defined by a dense Fourier oracle. Independent direct/radix-2 transforms verify its output. Independent dense matrices, interpolation, modular inverses and dual numbers test the claimed identities.

Those expensive verification methods are not a proposed fast evaluator. The marked-cycle speedup remains a precise conjecture. No benchmark ratio, finite-field pass count, or scalar unmarked trace result has been used to infer a generic sub-N-log-N algorithm.
