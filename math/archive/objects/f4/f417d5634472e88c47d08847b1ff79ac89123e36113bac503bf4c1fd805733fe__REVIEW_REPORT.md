## Findings that affect use

1. The F8 contraction certificates are finite positive existence results, not the adjacent-anchor inverse-sum impossibility certificate. Sources 41–42 support a fixed three-stage quasiseparable factorization of normalized positive-sign F8 with 56 frozen and 64 solved complex variables. Their static proof structure is coherent: multilinear exact differences/analytic derivatives, a valid complex-coordinate l1 Banach norm, a conservative degree-six Hessian bound, and strict rational contraction/self-map inequalities. The candidate copies match, and fresh metadata arithmetic checks all stored rational relationships and inequalities. The matrix residual and inverse-Jacobian values themselves were not recomputed here. No all-length family, exact closed-form coefficient list, uniform synthesis or asymptotic improvement follows.

2. The 04:07 cut-rank proof remains the simpler explanation of the different adjacent-anchor inverse-sum obstruction. Lengthy exact certificates, noncommuting examples, finite modular minors or low-degree annihilators do not establish methodological novelty. The assigned inverse-cut controls explicitly use this classical rank mechanism. The N=13 minor example only demonstrates failure of its particular tested half-cut gates, not every possible classical cut argument.

3. The structured shear algorithms retain genuine restrictions. Multishear costs include g³M Hankel work; Green recurrence expansion has 2^g−1 subset terms. O_g(N) is appropriate only for fixed g, balanced Q/M, a clean nondegenerate pilot and sparse total innovations. Public certificates are trusted by output routines. Arbitrarily large mode budgets, growing order, malformed fields or forged certificate values are outside the shortcut interpretation. The universal M-orbit representation has superlinear construction/application and does not satisfy the production Q>=2g pilot condition at Q=g=M.

4. The complex sparse-phase prototype is approximate and not hardened against nonfinite input. In source 15, a NaN in the input makes the global scale NaN; the reconstruction comparison with that threshold is false, allowing acceptance without discovered modes and a zero output. No supplied program was run to reach this static branch diagnosis. Positive factors, finite input and a valid tolerance need explicit API guards. A discovery block is not rechecked after thresholding new coefficients. These issues do not refute the separately scoped exact finite-field algorithm.

5. Work counters are not complete runtime proofs. The complex sparse-phase routine sums separate Green decoders, while the explicit polyphase routine does implement a mixed-radix complete-frame collapse at O(Q log Q). The dyadic aggregate verifier computes modular powers separately per event/output bin, introducing logarithmic word work absent from its partial recognition counter. Dense inversions, rank elimination, source fixture preparation, field/root search and large direct-reference computations are checker costs, not hidden free parts of a fast transform.

6. The fixed-direction completion source closes a constrained scale-solving problem. It correctly keeps offsupport equations, nonzero base coordinates, cycle guards and finite-field exhaustion distinct. Passing eliminated row constraints or column nullspaces is not sufficient: its N=5 negative example still has incompatible global scales. Dense construction of U B V remains in the current checker; unknown direction search is not solved.

7. Scalar fields and models matter. The chord source explicitly gives an F19 counterexample to transport of a complex-only geometry bound. Cyclotomic prime full-spark controls do not imply arbitrary composite or finite-field full spark. The entropy lower bound is for two-coordinate unitary gates, not arbitrary arithmetic circuits or qubit gates. The finite N=4 three-mixer and N=16 zero-mask exterior constructions remain fixed-model exceptions.

8. Historical execution labels must be interpreted literally. check_hankel_gate.py and explore_kernel_rank.py dynamically execute a sibling if run. The all-subsets Python wrapper compiles and executes its C++ checker, and isolated_replay.py executes that wrapper. A stored source_code_executed=False can refer to the earlier submitted/reviewer distinction and cannot mean no program executed anywhere. No such action occurred in this review.
