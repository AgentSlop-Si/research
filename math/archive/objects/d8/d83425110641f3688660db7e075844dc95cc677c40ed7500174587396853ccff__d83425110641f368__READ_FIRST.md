## The most useful gains

1. **A larger exact structured fast path.** The common-quadratic-chirp recognizer's polynomial eliminant gives O(Nr^4+r^6.7549) field arithmetic and O(N+r^4) space. Thus ranks growing as r=o((log N)^(1/4)) still admit sub-N-log-N recognition and accepted full-output transforms, within that restricted family and the stated field/root/inversion model. Start with `addenda/GROWING_RANK_CHIRP_COROLLARY.md`.
2. **Two genuine repairs.** Phase25 now has a whole-matrix exact frontier solver covering nonsingular cases missed by the supplied unpivoted schedule. The sparse-defect audit supplies the missing proof against arbitrary row-sparse baselines, beyond the source's bandwidth-only argument.
3. **Better scoped certificates.** Centered v12 has sharper approximation bounds and a conditional carrier-precision repair; Phase26 has a tighter common-cut allowance and exact witness construction; v13 has a randomness refinement and a corrected one-row phase check. None removes the overall exact-transform bottleneck or supplies a whole-pipeline floating-point certificate.
