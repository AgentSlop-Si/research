# Defensible claim scope

## Provisional theorem centered summary

We study the ordinary derivative discriminant of Rogers–Szegő polynomials near fixed odd roots of unity. Under explicit simple-residual-root and nonintersection hypotheses, we determine the leading polynomial at a shared root and the resulting exact discriminant order and coefficient. The shared polynomial is a standard quasi-Hermite combination, equivalently a quasi-Laguerre pullback. Combining the local coefficient with an inverse-root parameter identity and established Hermite congruences yields a paired-residue squarefreeness criterion. Exact fixed-base calculations give five infinite prime families, with individually certified exceptional orders stated separately. In the separated case, a related local product order yields an exact conditional rational-content valuation.

This is suggested scope, not cleared publication language asserting priority. It intentionally makes no claim that q-Lucas, Hermite limits, quasi-orthogonality, the prime-characteristic Wronskian identity, or cyclotomic ramification are new.

## Required theorem hypotheses

- Local overlap: odd `d`, odd `1≤b<d`, integer `A≥1`, primitive `ω`, `n=Ad+b`, and `G=H_b=(z+1)U`. Require `Disc(U) Res(z^d+1,U) Disc J_(A+1)≠0`.
- Paired application: `d≡3 mod4`, `b=(d−1)/2`, prime `p≡3 mod4`, `p∤d`, and nonzero paired content not divisible by `p`.
- Five uniform bases: `3,7,11,15,19`. Only the listed old finite certificates at `N=9,45,49,121,361` remove the corresponding `p∣d` exceptions.
- Content valuation: positive `s≡1 mod4`, prime `p≡1 mod4`, `c_s≠0`, and `p∤s c_s`.
- Reduced unramified determinant: `d=4r+1≥5`; the zero-degree/base-one convention is handled separately.
- One good residue factor is an existential hypothesis and conclusion. Do not replace it with goodness at every prime.

## Claims outside scope

No all-composite squarefreeness, general repeated-prime theorem, unconditional prime-support formula for all lower contents, unrestricted inverse-bank minimality, new polynomial family, new general Wronskian congruence, or asymptotically faster FFT is established. Project-open problems are not automatically literature-open problems.

## Publication gate

Before making an originality claim, compare legitimate full texts of Lubinsky–Saff (1987) and Zhedanov (1997), together with relevant cited predecessors, against the exact fixed-root local coefficient and ordinary discriminant. The independently accepted mathematics can be developed and circulated privately within the already authorized scope while that priority uncertainty remains. Public posting or outreach requires separate authorization and was not performed here.
