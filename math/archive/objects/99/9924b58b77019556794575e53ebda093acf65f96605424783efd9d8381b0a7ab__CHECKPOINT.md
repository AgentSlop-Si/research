## Verified advances

1. **Two-mask exact first-cross existence.** For invertible strictly positive bi-regular `A,C` and positive `B`, the problem of matching the Fourier matrix's complete first row and column is exactly the positive matrix-scaling problem for `B` with marginals `r=A[0,:]/a` and `s=C[:,0]/c`. Positive masks exist. This is *not* full Fourier equality.
2. **Linear-work approximate preprocessing.** Pick `K=(1-beta)(I-beta W)^-1`, `beta=1-1/N`. Then its entries have maximum/minimum ratio < `e`, allowing a projective Sinkhorn contraction factor bounded independently of `N`. Each matrix-vector multiply is `O(N)`; mask preparation to relative marginal tolerance `epsilon` is `O(N log(1/epsilon))` real arithmetic. Online applications cost `O(N)` after masks are prepared. Large numerical checks pass through `N=65536`.
3. **All-size spectral-gap theorem for two masks.** If `B` is entrywise positive and `||A-I||2<=delta_A`, `||C-I||2<=delta_C`, then every `M=A D1 P1 B P2 D2 C` obeys `||Fhat-M||2 >= [(1-delta_A)(1-delta_C)(1+c_N)-1]_+`, where `c_N=max{(1/2)sqrt(1-1/N),sqrt((1-sqrt(gcd(2,N)/N))/2)}`. Masks may be arbitrary nonzero complex scalings. At the explicit Green `eta=1/100`, outer delta <=0.02 and the floor tends to about 0.63951, never zero for powers of two `N>=4`.
4. **General complex phase-rank theorem.** If the middle matrix has nonzero entries and entrywise phase matrix rank `r`, the unsandwiched error is >= `(1/2)sqrt(1-r/N)` in spectral norm. At outer deltas <=.02, exact Fourier equality requires `r/N >= 1-4((.98)^(-2)-1)^2`, about 0.9932. This is NOT a computational lower bound and full phase rank is NOT sufficient.
5. **Sharp complex Green counterexample.** For a *single* cyclic Green `(I-alpha W)^-1`, its phase matrix has rank 1 if the phase of `alpha` has Nth power 1, and otherwise has **full rank N**. Yet all Green cross-ratios are among `{1,alpha^N,alpha^-N}`. The Fourier matrix for `N` divisible by 4 has `N` distinct cross-ratios. In particular `alpha=exp(pi*i/N)` produces a flat-modulus, full-phase-rank, `O(N)` Green transform with only two cross-ratio values, not an FFT.
6. **Exact characteristic-zero positive control.** SymPy computes an explicit nonreal cross-ratio for `A^-1 F_4 C^-1` with rational Green outer stages. Numerical spectral tests through 64 and positive calibration tests through 65536 agree with the theorems; positive factorization control with expensive outer `A` shows the small-outer assumption is essential.

## No-go / scope rules to preserve

- Do not claim two-mask positive Green scaling produces a complete Fourier transform; it matches *only the first row and column*. The second Fourier column fails in every large test.
- The strong spectral gap applies to entrywise **positive** middle stages and sufficiently near-identity outer stages. It is not a theorem against arbitrary complex noncommuting Green stages.
- The generic phase-rank bound is valid for complex middle matrices, but a single non-root-coupling Green can already achieve rank `N` cheaply; phase rank is an incomplete criterion.
- A single Green transform has ≤3 cross-ratio values irrespective of phase rank. This is **not** a proof for products/sums of multiple independently ordered Green stages.
- Preprocessing `O(N log(1/epsilon))` is numerical **real-arithmetic** complexity, not an exact bit-complexity statement. No precision-safe, exact, worst-case `o(N log N)` FFT has been found.
