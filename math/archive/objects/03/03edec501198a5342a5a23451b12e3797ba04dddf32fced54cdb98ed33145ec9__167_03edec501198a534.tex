\subsection*{Reduced-center variant of the scalar invocation}

Let $\mathcal T=\binom{[h]}3$.  Replace the $h+1$ center coordinates
$(c_1,\ldots,c_h,c_*)$ by only $(c_1,\ldots,c_h)$ and define
\[
 (G'x)_j=\sum_{T\ni j}x_T,
 \qquad
 (R'c)_S=\frac12\sum_{j\in S}c_j-\frac16\sum_{j=1}^h c_j.
\]
Then the coefficient of $x_T$ in $(R'G'x)_S$ is
\[
 \frac{|S\cap T|}{2}-\frac16|T|
 =\frac{|S\cap T|-1}{2},
\]
since $|T|=3$.  Thus $R'G'$ is identical to the center contribution
$RG$ in the original construction.  Keeping $J,V$ unchanged gives
\[
 R'G'+JV=I.
\]
Consequently the same eight-row scratch-restoring schedule implements
$y\gets y+x$ for arbitrary initial auxiliary values, with one fewer
center role per invocation.

With $v=\binom h3$, $N_0=v^3$, $I_0=3v^2$, and
$d_{\rm nbr}=\binom{h-3}{3}+3(h-3)$, the physical wire count and
directional saving become
\[
 W=2N_0+I_0(vd_{\rm nbr}+h),
 \qquad
 \Delta=2N_0-2I_0h^2
       =2v^2(v-3h^2).
\]
For $h=24$:
\[
 v=2024,\quad m=13824,\quad W=34666930287616,\quad
 W_*=2^{45},\quad \Delta=2425172992.
\]
Hence, for the original power-of-two batching,
\[
 \theta=\log_{13824}\!\left(13824-\frac{2425172992}{2^{45}}\right),
 \qquad
 1-\theta\approx 5.22969896327091\times10^{-10}.
\]
