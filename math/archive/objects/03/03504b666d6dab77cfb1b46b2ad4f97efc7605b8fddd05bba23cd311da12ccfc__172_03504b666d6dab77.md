# Prior-work guard

Do not restart: exact-subtree memoization, real-pair packing, split radix, fixed 4/8/16-bit lookup codelets, multilevel complete-state tabulation, sampled/pruned approximate FFT, input-dependent shifts/low-rank/delta views, persistent convolution DAGs, short annihilating filters, dense 2N-1 bilinear evaluation, tensor/local two-round search, chirp-convolution recurrence, rank-one displacement encoding, or integer-multiplication network parameter tuning. The canonical history in the working container and previous shared research packages already cover these.

**Distinct falsification tested here:** even a complicated nonlinear polynomial/rational *intermediate circuit* computing a *linear exact map* can be differentiated into an ordinary linear circuit at O(1) gate overhead. This standard theorem rejects the unsupported claim that variable-dependent k or nonlinear cancellations, **alone**, produce faster fresh arbitrary-input FFT work. It is not a stronger FFT complexity lower bound.
