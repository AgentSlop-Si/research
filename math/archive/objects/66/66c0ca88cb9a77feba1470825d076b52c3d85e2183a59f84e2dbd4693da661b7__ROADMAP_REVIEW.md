# Review of the eight direction Fourier roadmap

9 October 2026 UTC. Review against the verified version 10 master.

## Conclusion

The note is a useful inventory of research themes, but its priority labels are not supported by comparative evidence and several claim statuses are stale. It establishes no new exact arbitrary-input Fourier algorithm. The original goal remains an exact transform of every fresh arbitrary input, with every output written and total charged work below N log N.

The central exact proposal needs an architectural correction before further search: a product of single weighted-cycle Green resolvents cannot have sublogarithmic depth, even with arbitrary non-affine cycle orders. Its inverse has at most 2^t nonzeros per row after t factors, whereas the inverse Fourier matrix is dense. Exactness therefore requires 2^t >= N. This is an inherited consequence of the master's inverse-support result, not a new theorem. Sums of resolvents, more general separated-generator scans with dense inverses, and genuinely fused circuits require their own analysis and are not excluded by this particular argument.

The most important status corrections are:
- The N=8 graph result is exhaustive, but dimension 60 does not decide Fourier membership.
- Exact phase-bank completion and its ordinary Cooley–Tukey fusion already exist.
- The present finite Bessel lift is approximate, with an exact separation-rank obstruction at odd lengths.
- Growing-rank tensor/fiber bounds and a broader output-rank convolution fallback are already in v10.
- The direct divisor theorem survives, while the adaptive offset/Bessel planner's execution certificate is false.
- The signed-atom Word-RAM proof is complete under a precise coarse-accuracy bounded-input model; a full practical asymptotic implementation is not.

## The eight directions

### 1 Non affine Green cycles and Weyl certification

Status: a broader family remains unexcluded, but the literal pure-resolvent product proposed in the note is already excluded at sublogarithmic depth.

Phase26 treats a specific grammar with bounded affine steps, bridges, diagonal gates, and affine connectors. Its common-character obstruction does not extend automatically to arbitrary internal permutations. The v10 strengthened necessary term budget is Q = Omega(log N / log log N) in its stated restricted grammar.

That remaining scope does not make arbitrary non-affine single-cycle products viable. For G_i = (I - A_i)^(-1), where A_i is a weighted permutation matrix, each inverse factor has at most two entries per row. Arbitrary monomial wiring preserves the row-support bound, so a t-factor product has inverse row support at most 2^t. Because every entry of F_N^(-1) is nonzero, t >= ceil(log_2 N). The same audit gives, for unitary Fourier normalization, the approximation obstruction ||M-F_N||_2 >= sqrt(max(0, 1-2^t/N)). Noncommutativity and non-affine order do not remove this premise.

Full Weyl identities plus one scalar calibration are an exact target certificate once conventions are fixed. They do not construct a circuit or reduce its work. The existing sharp Frobenius gap is 2 sin(pi/N), so a small unscaled numerical residual is not a uniform accuracy guarantee as N grows.

Actionable correction: retain #1 only after explicitly allowing an architecture that leaves the sparse-inverse product class. “Highest priority” should be replaced by “unresolved after specifying an admissible enlarged architecture.”

Evidence: CURRENT_CHECKPOINT.md, sections “Phase26 simultaneous common-character cuts” and “Green Phases19 and20”; additions/1717/astra_exact_bridge/EXACT_BRIDGES.md, section 5; additions/1930_phase26/PHASE26_AUDIT.md.

### 2 Superconcentrator graph and algebraic compatibility

Status: exact finite-size graph and dimension facts; Fourier feasibility remains unresolved.

For middle permutation [7,0,6,3,4,1,2,5], all 12,869 nonempty equal-size terminal subset pairs were checked, and unequal-size cases follow from the graph argument. Thus “every tested graph condition” understates the established N=8 result. The complex image dimension of the fixed two-stage global rank-one separated-generator family is exactly 60, rather than the ambient 64. This excludes generic matrices, not F8.

The note's proposed polynomial attack has already progressed substantially. The master contains an eight-parameter regular-chart reconstruction with twelve residual rational equations, 29 nonzero guards, earlier 30-quartic and 25-sextic reductions, and a complete 176-quadratic lifted formulation retaining exceptional charts. The local rank-60-to-61 jet obstruction does not establish global Fourier nonmembership.

Existing counterexamples forbid dropping denominators or anchor guards. An invertible zero-anchor matrix can satisfy all anchored minors without having global separated generators; the inverse-diagonal and ordinary-diagonal normalizations are different; the reduced onion chart omits actual exceptional partial points. Finite-field branch failures and characteristic-zero slice exclusions do not settle the full complex F8 problem.

Actionable correction: reuse the complete current systems and exception record; do not restart with dimension counting or describe explicit constraints as still absent. Any exact solution must be checked as a full matrix factorization. Any global exclusion must cover omitted charts. A successful N=8 instance alone gives no scalable bound.

Evidence: nonlinear/NONLINEAR_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md; additions/1450/algebraic/ALGEBRAIC_JET_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md; CURRENT_CHECKPOINT.md, “Exact algebraic route” and “Latest exact-branch refinement”; additions/1646/SPECTRAL_DETERMINANT_AUDIT.md.

### 3 Exact phase bank fusion

Status: exact identity and ordinary fusion are established; faster fusion is an unproved algorithmic requirement.

The scaling matters. Each P_h has rank K and satisfies P_h^2 = M P_h. E_h = P_h/M is the orthogonal projector, and sum_h E_h = I. Therefore F_N x = (1/M) sum_h F_N P_h x is exact. Calling the unscaled P_h a projection without qualification is inaccurate.

The full bank retains N independent summary coordinates. Fewer than M rank-K summaries cannot recover every input if the decoder receives only those summaries. Arbitrary-input information is not removed by calling the combined forcing compressed.

Separate row execution costs O(MN + N log K). Algebraic cancellation already removes the aggregate scans and yields ordinary Cooley–Tukey: O(N log M + N log K) = O(N log N). Consequently a proposed o(MN) implementation is not itself a new asymptotic result; it must beat the already known complete O(N log N) schedule with all setup and outputs charged.

The v13 source's one-row finite-field phase test reverses a convention that complete-bank averaging conceals. The independent row-first certificate, randomness qualifications, and conditioning corrections must be retained.

Actionable correction: ask for a specific additional identity and executor that beats both existing schedules. The complete-bank identity is not a new v13 discovery and is not evidence of such an executor.

Evidence: additions/1717/astra_exact_bridge/EXACT_BRIDGES.md, section 1; additions/1930_v13/AUDIT_AND_EXTENSION.md.

### 4 Bessel or Chebyshev correction as a shared operator

Status: exact identities inside a finite approximate lift; no exact all-length FFT follows.

The note's “Exact FFT candidate” label needs an explicit qualifier: its existing finite R-channel evaluator approximates a nonseparable near-grid correction. For dyadic lift length Q, the exact residual matrix has rank N/gcd(N,Q); at odd N it has rank N. An exact separated correction therefore needs at least that many terms. Completing every phase bank evaluates the truncated center exactly but does not eliminate truncation bias.

A different, genuinely nonseparable global evaluator is outside that rank obstruction. It must be specified and proved; writing the forcing as one polynomial or resolvent does not establish cheap evaluation and decoding.

The existing blocked scan result is already conditional and fully scoped: O(Q(R/B+B) log^2(M+R)) scan arithmetic, plus conservative O(KR^2) coefficient conversion in the Bessel/Chebyshev branch. The R short transforms and decoder/output passes leave the recorded overall order O(RN). Correlated forcings leave room for specialized fusion, while a full-rank weighted-scan commutator rejects a simple constant-rank correction on unrestricted forcings.

Numerical obligations are substantive. The forward Bessel order recurrence has factorial amplification; centering does not stabilize it. Faster arithmetic with uncharged or uncertified coefficients is not a complete evaluator.

Actionable correction: classify the current branch as approximate. Treat an exact nonseparable evaluator as a separate proposed architecture, with explicit correctness and complete arithmetic/precision ledgers.

Evidence: additions/1717/astra_exact_bridge/EXACT_BRIDGES.md, section 2; CURRENT_CHECKPOINT.md, “HERO0: operator bridge, precision obstruction and conditional scan improvement”; additions/1847/independent_bridge/BRIDGE_AUDIT_AND_CONSTRUCTION.md; additions/1906_centered/.

### 5 Growing rank tensor fibers and graph union

Status: the roadmap understates the verified structured results and omits a stronger fallback.

The standalone raw-input tensor and selected-divisor fiber branches already have uniform O((1+r)N) field-arithmetic bounds under their stated balance and supplied-field/root contracts. Thus r = o(log N) gives sub-N-log-N recognition and complete transforms on accepted structured inputs. The separate common-quadratic-chirp branch has a different bound, O(Nr^4 + r^(2+3 log_2 3)), and permits r = o((log N)^(1/4)). These are different structured families.

For convolution, fixed input-mode bounds plus S <= C Q give the established linear-time graph-union route. The v10 output-rank fallback is strictly broader: fixed rank of the merged coefficient matrix can succeed even when S/Q grows without bound. The exact state bound is S <= min(N, E, sqrt(Q Kx Ky)); E counts events and cannot silently be replaced by final surviving states.

The shipped dispatcher is narrower than the mathematical design. It selects one default divisor and misses an explicit N=12 input accepted at Q=2; its default is Q=3. Its chirp engine still has a factorial eliminant. The independently specified separate-budget polynomial dispatcher is not uploaded functionality. Maximal ordinary matrix rank in selected dense examples is not arbitrary-input coverage.

Actionable correction: start from these existing growing-rank and output-rank results. Any multiscale extension must count all recognition attempts, intermediate events, inversions, preparation and outputs, and state exactly which additional inputs are accepted.

Evidence: additions/1954/tensor_audit/AUDIT_REPORT.md and EXTENSION_AND_FUSION.md; additions/1954/graph_audit/GRAPH_UNION_AUDIT_AND_RANK_FUSION.md; GROWING_RANK_CHIRP_COROLLARY.md.

### 6 Divisor spectrum randomized approximation

Status: valid direct composite theorem, inherited stronger covariance, false generic planner certificate.

For fixed input and suitable composite lengths, direct rows and a fixed number of coordinate-median repetitions give O(N) ideal-arithmetic, all-output additive approximation. Balanced semiprimes permit every fixed error exponent alpha < 1/4 on the ||x||_2 scale, with a suitable constant row count for the requested polynomial failure exponent. This is neither exact DFT recovery nor one seed working simultaneously for all inputs.

The v13 divisor-sum bound was already in the master and is weaker than the earlier mask-count bound. The square case must be separated: at N=p^2, M=p, the v13 bound is 2/p and the older bound is 1/p. The new least-prime spectral theorem refines constants, without changing the asymptotic frontier.

The adaptive offset/Bessel execution certificate is false. For an exact N=3, x=e_0 example it fails at tolerance 2 with probability one while advertising a failure bound below 0.00008. It executes quantized three-grid aggregation but cites a coordinate-median theorem. The Bessel branch also omits addition error and can execute a different block size from the certified one. Those are established defects, not tasks awaiting mere benchmarking.

A sharp prime-length divisor estimate is not a direct extension of this algorithm: a prime N has no divisor 2 <= M <= sqrt(N). A prime-length route therefore needs a different measurement construction or a separately analyzed lift, not just a better inequality for the same eligible divisor.

Actionable correction: preserve the standalone theorem and mark the adaptive planner uncertified. Remove “strongest” unless a common input domain, accuracy norm, failure probability, and computational model are selected for comparison.

Evidence: additions/1954/divisor_audit/DIVISOR_SPECTRUM_AUDIT.md; additions/1916/; CURRENT_CHECKPOINT.md, first divisor-spectrum section.

### 7 Signed atoms and Word RAM

Status: the proof-incomplete label is stale; the model-specific approximation theorem is complete.

The master already contains a fully charged two-table proof, then a simpler single-table guarded-addition replacement. Under bounded-integer or common bounded-width fixed-point inputs, Theta(log N)-bit words, fixed epsilon and fixed polynomial failure exponent c, it writes all N outputs in O(N) word operations, including table construction, exact capped sampling, key formation, finite constants, packing, rounding, and output scaling. Its guarantee is

Pr(max_m |output_m - (U_N x)_m| > epsilon L_box(x)/sqrt(N)) <= N^(-c).

The two-table transpose is proved, not an open packing step. The later guarded design removes it, provided every table row is stored already packed and every returned word is charged. The newer eager implementation fixes the older prototype's online cache-miss phasors and repeated repacking.

The complete growing-parameter whole-input implementation and practical benchmark remain absent. Fixed small B,g tests do not instantiate the asymptotic schedule; theoretical thresholds and constants can be enormous. The model excludes arbitrary exact complex inputs and unrestricted exponent ranges. For many dense inputs this global L_box/sqrt(N) allowance is much larger than an individual Fourier coefficient; it is not per-bin relative accuracy or ordinary FFT-quality precision.

Actionable correction: rename this “proved coarse approximation under a bounded-input word model; full asymptotic implementation remains unfinished.” Do not keep repeating the old v2 proof gap.

### 8 Sparse defect escape

Status: a genuine separation between operator classes, with an important provenance correction.

The source's banded-only witness did not prove escape from every arbitrary row-sparse baseline. The independent v10 repair does. For the separated scan S with lower/diagonal/upper entries 1/3/2 and any row-q-sparse B,

rank(S^(-1) - B) >= ceil(floor(N/(q+2))/(2q+1)).

At fixed q this is linear in N, including arbitrary support patterns and endpoint monomial changes. Both S and S^(-1) admit linear exact-scalar application. This defeats the attempted reduction of every cheap scan to a sparse inverse plus bounded-rank correction.

It does not make every separated scan Fourier compatible or provide a factorization. Setup remains charged: Woodbury costs O(N+Nr^2+r^3) before fresh application O(N+Nr+r^2); no-pivot Thomas code covers only a restricted chart. Exact rational explicit outputs can require quadratic total bits despite linear scalar counts.

Actionable correction: #8 is the appropriate architectural distinction to make before reviving #1. Attribute the arbitrary-row-sparse escape proof to the independent repair, not to the original incomplete witness.

Evidence: additions/1930_sparse/AUDIT_REPORT.md, ARBITRARY_SPARSE_REPAIR.md and SUMMARY.md.

## Evidence based prioritization

There is no justified probability-of-success ranking of these eight directions. “Unexcluded,” “high mathematical interest,” and “highest priority” are different claims. In particular, being outside one lower-bound grammar gives no constructive evidence of Fourier compatibility.

For the original exact target, the order of obligations is:
1. Specify an architecture outside already excluded classes. #8 clarifies the distinction missing from the literal #1 product proposal.
2. Apply the existing complete algebraic and Weyl certificates. #2 supplies finite compatibility tools.
3. Supply a new charged executor. #3 supplies an identity but already collapses to ordinary Cooley–Tukey under the known fusion.
4. Prove an all-size rule and its total work. A “plausible” scalable pattern and finitely many successful sizes are not this theorem.

For concrete scoped advances, #5 already has the strongest directly verified structured exact statements in this list. That is an evidence-status observation, not a claim that it will lead to arbitrary-input FFTs. #6 and #7 are separately useful approximate models with incomparable error and representation contracts. #4 must not be silently shifted from approximate to exact.

## One bounded next task

Perform one exact candidate-certificate check at N=8, after a complete candidate is supplied. Use an explicitly fixed architecture outside the pure single-resolvent product restriction; provide its permutations, every coefficient in an exact represented field, all solve denominators, normalization, and a straight-line operation schedule.

The check has three terminal outcomes:
- PASS: exact full Fourier equality, including both Weyl identities and scalar calibration, all required inverses/guards, and a fully enumerated per-instance work ledger.
- FAIL: an exact nonzero residual, zero forbidden denominator, or an applicable architectural impossibility certificate, with a reproducible witness.
- INCOMPLETE: a specific missing coefficient, field embedding, gate definition, or cost item. No numerical optimizer success can be substituted.

Stop at this one-instance result. Do not launch an indefinite architecture search, claim global infeasibility from a failed candidate, or infer an asymptotic improvement from PASS. The present roadmap provides no such candidate, so candidate specification is currently the missing input. An existing dense Fourier matrix or ordinary FFT is a correctness control, not evidence for the new architecture.

This deliberately modest next task is falsifiable. It prevents another round of graph-only or partial-phase “success” from being promoted to an exact construction.

## Lower bound wording and primary references

The roadmap's generic “arXiv +1” is not a usable citation. Its caution is directionally right but should name the model and normalization.

Morgenstern's classical result is for the unnormalized Fourier transform under bounded constants in a linear model; determinant growth does not directly give the same statement for unitary normalization. Ailon's well-conditioned model gives a lower bound Omega(N log N / R) under its specified gate, intermediate-conditioning and workspace hypotheses; constant R recovers Omega(N log N). These are not unrestricted arithmetic lower bounds, nor permission to treat huge coefficients or precision as free.
