Rectangular Fourier research checkpoint
9 October 2026

Outcome: no independent arbitrary-support o(N log k) algorithm was established.

PRIOR_ART.md identifies a primary-source rational-series baseline and charges its preprocessing. CANDIDATE.md gives exact prime-length coefficient-density and recurrence-support obstructions, a scheme-specific dyadic pruning analysis, and a constructive restricted coset algorithm. These are not general FFT time lower bounds or novelty claims.

verify_prime_annihilators.py and prime_annihilator_checks.json contain exact integer checks for all 10,400 nonempty proper root subsets at prime lengths 2,3,5,7,11,13, covering 75,415 nonzero coefficients. The proof, not this finite enumeration, establishes the general statement.

streaming/ contains an independently written exact modular implementation of the known blocked rational-series method. All 184 cases agree with a full NTT, and 76 also agree with direct evaluation. Its fixed modulus and supported sizes are explicit. It is a reproducible baseline, not a speedup claim or an implementation over arbitrary exact complex numbers.

The useful computational distinction is that modal state advancement can already cost O(N); applying the output map to each block remains the bottleneck. A successful next mechanism must improve that application cost without hiding equivalent convolution work.
