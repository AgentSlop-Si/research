# A quotient-compatible hierarchical Hamiltonian family

## Status and separation from previous families

This is an alternative to independently minimum-splicing the MGG covers at every size. It is explicitly a **new geometry family**. It starts from the already certified independent-splice bases N0=2^17 for odd exponents and N0=2^18 for even exponents. It preserves the large-size balanced cut-count guarantee while making the N-to-4N route quotient exact.

It is not the family used by the proposed N16 coefficient fit. A successful N16 seed would not automatically belong to, or lift into, these independently chosen large bases. A genuine Fourier construction must either supply coefficient-compatible seeds at these large bases, or give a route/parameter bridge from a small exact seed with its own proofs and cost ledger. Neither is supplied here.

The prerequisite is the original square/rectangular MGG geometry proof supplied with this package. Its sole external spectral input is the established unnormalized5sqrt(2) bound in the official AFP formalization: https://isa-afp.org/browser_info/current/AFP/Expander_Graphs/Expander_Graphs_MGG.html . This note derives the hierarchical extension, not a new expander theorem or a publication-priority claim.

## 1. Deterministic construction of one lift

Let T_small be one labeled MGG cover on Z_a x Z_b, and let P_small be an already chosen Hamiltonian permutation on those vertices. At the next size let T_large be the same labeled cover on Z_(2a) x Z_(2b). Let pi(x,y)=(x mod a,y mod b). The unspliced covers satisfy pi T_large=T_small pi.

Define the small permutation

    eta=P_small T_small^(-1).

Every large vertex has a unique low vertex (x mod a,y mod b) and two high fiber bits (floor(x/a),floor(y/b)). Define eta_hat by applying eta to the low vertex and leaving those two high bits unchanged. This is a fully specified permutation of the large vertices and satisfies pi eta_hat=eta pi.

Put P0=eta_hat T_large. Then

    pi P0 = eta T_small pi = P_small pi.

Consequently each directed cycle of P0 projects onto the entire small Hamiltonian cycle. Its length is a positive multiple of N=ab. Since there are4N large vertices, P0 has c<=4 cycles.

To merge them without changing the quotient, traverse each P0 cycle and choose its least large vertex whose projection is the small vertex0. Such a vertex exists in every cycle. Order these at most four chosen tails by integer label and cyclically rotate their successors. If c=1, change nothing. All chosen tails project to0, and all their old and new successors project to P_small(0). Thus the resulting P_large is Hamiltonian and still satisfies

    pi P_large=P_small pi.

There is no search, optimization, arbitrary representative choice, or unspecified fiber bijection in this rule.

## 2. Edit count and preserved cut guarantee

Let h_small=#{v:P_small(v) differs from T_small(v)}. The support of eta has exactly h_small vertices. Its lift changes exactly4h_small vertices, so P0 differs from T_large in exactly4h_small outgoing edges. The final quotient-preserving merge changes at most four more. Hence

    h_large <= 4h_small+4.

For the specified starting bases, there is a stronger invariant. Vertex0 is one of the minimum splice representatives, and every cover has more than one cycle, so P_small(0) differs from T_small(0) at the base. If this holds at a later level, all large tails over0 are already edited by eta_hat: their new projected destination is P_small(0), different from the old projected destination T_small(0). The final merge touches only these already edited tails and keeps that new projection. Consequently no new edited tails are introduced and none disappear. In fact, writing C for the changed-tail set,

    C_large=pi^(-1)(C_small),
    h_large=4h_small,
    P_large(0) differs from T_large(0).

This proves the invariant by induction and sharpens the safe general bound above to an exact count for this family.

For any two permutations differing on h tails, their old and new heads on those tails form the same set. The endpoint-preserving half-loss lemma therefore gives incoming-count difference at most floor(h/2) across every subset, even when the edit is not a single splice.

Across all eight labels put H_l=sum_j h_(l,j). Starting from a base with total edits H0=2D0, the invariant gives

    H_l=4^l H0,
    H_l/2=4^l D0.

At each level the comparison graph is the genuine MGG square or rectangular quotient, so its incoming-count spectral bound remains (8-5sqrt(2))N_l/4 on every balanced subset. Subtracting the half-edit bound is valid irrespective of how the edits accumulated.

As in the original geometry proof, sqrt(2)<17/12 implies that the three-layer additive cut-count budget exceeds N_l/2 by strictly more than

    3N_l/16 - 3*4^l D0 = 4^l(3N0/16-3D0).

For the odd-exponent base N0=131072, D0=7936, this is768*4^l>0. For the even-exponent base N0=262144, D0=11264, it is15360*4^l>0. Therefore the new hierarchical family has the same common-balanced-subset necessary-cut escape for every dyadic N>=2^17. The earlier safe bounds752*4^l+16 and15344*4^l+16 remain valid but are weaker.

The edit counts are exact for this family; the cut-loss bounds need not be attained. They do not prove that any matrix sum attains its additive rank budget.

As before, only the common balanced-subset additive cut-count obstruction is defeated. Unbalanced cuts, separators with changing intermediate subsets, and all Fourier coefficient constraints remain separate questions.

## 3. Enumeration, resolvents, and coefficient obligations

Computing eta, its fiber-label lift, P0, at most four cycle tails, and the final successor array takes O(N) word operations for a single4N-sized lift. Enumerating every preceding level from the fixed base costs a geometric sum O(N_final), not N_final times the number of levels. The constant eight labels is charged. Working storage is O(N_final) words.

Because the route quotient is now exact, matching scalar poles give

    Q(I-tau P_large)^(-1)=(I-tau P_small)^(-1)Q

whenever both inverses exist. Compatible nonconstant weights and endpoint masks require the explicit pullback laws described in `N_TO_4N_LIFT_REQUIREMENTS.md`; arbitrary coefficients do not inherit them automatically.

The bank still has its exact even/odd channel mismatch. In fact the literal all-mask pullback law with unit multiplicative scaling and unnormalized Fourier targets is excluded for any proper active bank by the Fourier-defect rank argument in that note. This does not exclude unitary-normalized targets. Thus removing the route defect sharpens the coefficient problem: a successful lift must supply genuine fiber-channel or normalization structure, not merely repeat a small solution.

## 4. Verification controls

`check_hierarchical_lift.py` is a newly authored exact checker of the fiber-label construction, quotient equality, at-most-four-cycle statement, Hamiltonicity, edit recurrence, rational margins, and matching-pole resolvent commutation. It imports only helpers authored in this constructor track. `HIERARCHICAL_LIFT_CONTROLS.json` records the bounds and checked sizes. No uploaded source is executed, and no Fourier coefficient fit is attempted.
