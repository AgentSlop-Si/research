# Independent direct audit: global-chirp median v7

Date: 9 October 2026. Scope: the complete 813-line RESEARCH.md, README.md, prototype.py, test_checkpoint.py, and saved results.json in the supplied v7 bundle. Uploaded code was inspected as text only. The executed checker in this directory was independently authored and imports no uploaded module.

## Verdict

The central theorem is correct in its stated regime: for dyadic N=KM, M>=2, K>=2M, independently uniform odd a modulo N and uniform beta modulo N, the one-row estimator is unbiased and its per-output covariance operator has norm exactly 2M/N. The exact two-cycle recurrence costs O(N+K log K). The independent-row componentwise median argument and both asymptotic approximation regimes follow in the exact scalar arithmetic plus comparison model.

There is no counterexample to the central claim in this review. There are minor parameter/model qualifications, listed below, and a useful strengthening: the covariance is a scalar times an orthogonal projector, with every nonzero eigenvalue equal to 2M/N and rank K(M-1)/2. The source does not establish finite-word complexity, recurrence stability at large N, an optimized wall-clock improvement, or exact arbitrary-input DFT evaluation.

The historical comparison with older chirp, v5/v6, and rank-barrier material is being handled separately. This report concentrates on direct v7 correctness and implementation evidence.

## 1. Exact assumptions and quantifiers

1. N=2^L, K and M powers of two, N=KM, M>=2 and K>=2M. M=1 is an exact identity projection with zero variance, not norm 2M/N.
2. The input x is any fixed vector in C^N, independent of the subsequently drawn row seeds. The probability statement is for each fixed x, simultaneously over all N output coordinates. It does not promise a single compressed seed set that works uniformly for every possible x, including inputs chosen after seeing the seeds.
3. Within one row, a is uniform over the N/2 odd residues and beta is uniform over all N residues, independently. Rows use independent pairs (a,beta). Correlations between blocks and between output coordinates within a row are permitted and handled by the covariance calculation; they must not be replaced by an independence assumption.
4. U_N is the unitary negative-sign DFT. The approximation is absolute per-output error relative to ||x||_2. It is not relative error for each true coefficient.
5. Scalar arithmetic and real comparisons for median selection are counted as unit cost. Exact compatible roots and 1/sqrt(N) are available scalar constants, or must be supplied/prepared under an explicitly specified algebraic model. Preparing the table of N root powers from one supplied root takes O(N) multiplications and O(N) storage, so arbitrary phase lookup need not hide O(log N) exponentiation per entry.
6. For the simultaneous probability bound, 0<delta<1 and s is a positive integer with R=2s-1. A useful shrinking-error linear-time corollary needs fixed s>=2 at fixed delta.

The block map called a rank-one “projection” is M times an orthogonal rank-one projector. Its square is M times itself. This wording does not affect the formulas but should not be confused with an idempotent projection.

## 2. Decoder checked independently

With zeta=exp(2 pi i/N) and P_n=sum_(t=0)^(M-1) zeta^(-a t^2-nt), shifting t by one gives

    P_n = 1 + zeta^(-a-n) P_(n+2a) - zeta^(-a M^2-nM).

Substituting into the block-sum expression gives the source's recurrence exactly:

    Xhat_m = D_(m mod K)/sqrt(N)
             + zeta^(-a-m-beta) Xhat_(m+2a)
             - zeta^(-aM^2-mM-beta M) D_((m+2aM) mod K)/sqrt(N).

The factor zeta^(-2aMr) in the shifted chirp term is precisely the block Fourier phase for the frequency shift m -> m+2a. The boundary term similarly shifts the block Fourier index by 2aM. This is the necessary closure; it has not been assumed away.

Odd a gives gcd(2a,N)=2, so there are exactly two cycles. Two independently evaluated O(N)-work seeds plus N-2 recurrence steps determine every coordinate. Each recurrence division is by a unit-modulus nonzero scalar. These observations give the stated arithmetic cost, but alone do not give a backward-stability theorem.

Charged per row: O(N) summaries; O(K log K) compatible FFT; O(N) seeds; O(N) traversal/indexing; O(N) output storage/writing. Root-table construction can be shared or charged once per row without changing the order. Thus T_row=O(N+K log K) and S_row=O(N).

The independent checker compares this recurrence with literal projected-signal DFTs over the exact finite field of order 998244353. It uses unnormalized DFTs to avoid a normalization square root. Fifteen cases, N through 512, agree exactly. This verifies the algebra in finite instances, separately from floating-point tolerances.

## 3. Covariance proof and stronger spectral statement

Write n=rM+u and D_u={u-t: 0<=t<M, t!=u}. The coefficient of x_n in the output error is

    h_n = zeta^(-mn)/sqrt(N) sum_(d in D_u) zeta^(a(2nd-d^2)+(beta+m)d).

The covariance used for E|sum_n h_n x_n|^2 is Q_(n,n')=E[conj(h_n)h_(n')]. In the theorem regime, |d-d'|<N, so beta orthogonality forces d=d'. The formula in the source follows, including its diagonal modulation sign.

Averaging over odd a gives a nonzero term precisely when N divides 4d(n'-n), with sign +/-1. If d=2^v q for odd q, 0<=v<log2 M, then n'-n is a multiple of N/2^(v+2). Under K>=2M this modulus is a multiple of M, forcing u'=u. Thus there is no hidden cross-column covariance term.

For fixed u and v, let g=2^(v+2). On block indices r in Z_K the character matrix has entries zero except when r'-r is a multiple of K/g; there they alternate +1,-1. Its Fourier eigenvalues are g on modes ell congruent to g/2 modulo g and zero elsewhere. Different v select disjoint Fourier modes.

### Strengthening the counting step

For any u, the integer interval [u-M+1,u] contains exactly M/2^(v+1) representatives congruent to 2^v modulo 2^(v+1), because its length is M and M is divisible by 2^(v+1). None is zero. Equivalently, the source's two floors sum exactly, rather than merely satisfy an upper bound:

    c_(u,v) = M/2^(v+1).

Consequently every selected eigenvalue is

    c_(u,v) g/N = 2M/N.

Therefore, throughout the theorem regime,

    Q_m = (2M/N) P_m,
    P_m*=P_m, P_m^2=P_m,
    rank(P_m)=K(M-1)/2.

For m=0, P_0 selects, independently in each length-K column transform, exactly the Fourier indices ell that are even but not divisible by 2M. The dimension per column is K/2-K/(2M); multiplying by M gives the rank above. Output modulation unitarily conjugates this projector.

More explicitly, let A_u[p] be the unitary K-point DFT along the block index of x_(rM+u). Then

    E|Xhat_m-X_m|^2
      = (2M/N) sum_u sum_(ell even, 2M does not divide ell)
                       |A_u[(ell+m) mod K]|^2.

The covariance depends only on m modulo 2M. This formula is a sharper input-dependent identity, not a claim that its energies can be evaluated for free. It immediately implies the source's bound and explains the extremizer supported on u=0 and block mode ell=2 at m=0.

The trace is M-1. Summing the per-output variances gives the familiar (M-1)||x||_2^2 total MSE, fully consistent with the much smaller norm of each individual covariance operator. A trace or total-error obstruction cannot refute the separate per-coordinate guarantee.

## 4. Median theorem, constants, and corrected schedule

The needed real-median inequality has a short direct proof. For real errors z_1,...,z_(2s-1), at least s are at least as large in absolute value as the median error and on its corresponding side of zero. Hence pointwise

    |median(z)|^(2s)
      <= sum_(S subset {1,...,2s-1}, |S|=s) product_(j in S) |z_j|^2.

Taking expectations and using independence yields C_s times the s-th power of the one-row second moment, where C_s=binom(2s-1,s). This does not assume that the median itself is unbiased. The known general theorem is Theorem 5 of Larsen, Pagh, and Tětek, “CountSketches, Feature Hashing and the Median of Three”; the primary manuscript was checked directly at https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.02193, PDF page 4.

Apply this argument separately to real and imaginary components, use their second moments <=2M||x||_2^2/N, use Markov, and union over 2N real coordinates. The resulting complex infinity-norm bound is exactly the supplied one:

    ||Xtilde-U_N x||_infinity
      <= 2 (2N C_s/delta)^(1/(2s)) sqrt(M/N) ||x||_2

with probability >=1-delta.

### Fixed row count

For M a dyadic value within constant factors of log N, one row costs O(N). Fixed s and fixed 0<delta<1 give O(N) time, O(N) storage, and

    O(sqrt(log N) N^(-1/2+1/(2s)) ||x||_2).

If delta=N^(-a), a>0, the exponent instead becomes -1/2+(1+a)/(2s); fixed s>1+a gives polynomial shrinkage. These are valid arbitrary-fixed-input guarantees, including sparse inputs, at the stated energy-relative scale.

### Growing rows: explicit nonvacuous parameters

Let 1<=f(N)=o(log log N), choose dyadic M=Theta(log N/f(N)), fix a>=0 and epsilon>0, and set

    delta = min(1/2, N^(-a)),
    s = max(1, ceil(log(2N/delta)/(2 epsilon log log N))),
    R = 2s-1.

Natural logs are used here; changing bases changes only constants. For sufficiently large N, C_s^(1/(2s))<2 and (2N/delta)^(1/(2s))<=(log N)^epsilon. Thus the displayed schedule yields

    T = O(((1+a)/epsilon) N log N f(N)/log log N)
      = o(N log N),

    ||Xtilde-U_N x||_infinity
      = O((log N)^(1/2+epsilon)/sqrt(f(N)) * ||x||_2/sqrt(N)).

The exact medians in the supplied algorithm require O(RN) stored values in its straightforward implementation. This is an implementation upper bound and an explicit source limitation, not a lower bound ruling out alternative robust aggregators or other scheduling strategies.

## 5. Corrections and implementation qualifications

- RESEARCH section 7.2 sets delta=N^(-a) for a>=0. At a=0 it becomes delta=1, so success probability >=0 is vacuous and the prototype's bound routine rejects it. Use min(1/2,N^(-a)) or treat a=0 as any fixed failure probability in (0,1).
- A generic s=Theta((1+a)log N/(epsilon log log N)) does not, for every hidden constant, imply the exact requested exponent epsilon. The explicit ceiling above fixes this without changing the asymptotic runtime.
- The formal covariance theorem states M>=2 correctly. README and top-line summaries omit that exception; M=1 has variance zero. The exact norm headline should carry M>=2 or mention the exact limit separately.
- O(RN) median work needs linear-time selection per real component. Sorting each set of R values would cost O(NR log R) and can consume the strict sub-N-log-N saving in the growing-row regime. The research prototype calls NumPy median; the abstract complexity theorem should specify a linear-selection algorithm rather than infer a complexity guarantee from a library call.
- The five saved covariance test pairs include N=64,M=8,K=8, outside K>=2M. Its numerical match is an extra observation, not verification of the proved regime or a proof of an extension.
- The covariance formula's beta-orthogonality shortcut need not apply unchanged for K=1, because displacements can alias modulo N. The theorem regime avoids that issue. Helpers with a wider accepted parameter range are not by themselves general theorems.
- The supplied code uses NumPy int64 products and float64 phases. Its own comment limits the integer products to moderate validation sizes. Exact arithmetic asymptotics must not be interpreted as arbitrarily large-N correctness of this specific implementation.
- N=1 is not handled by the prototype's odd-state/two-seed interface, despite its broad power-of-two input check. This is outside the main M>=2,K>=2M regime and is trivial to special-case.
- Saved test output and prose claims that “all 10 tests pass” were not adopted as fresh execution results. The source tests were read, but not run. The median-interface test only checks shape/finite bound, and the asymptotic proxy tests do not verify wall-clock scaling, stability, or the confidence bound empirically.

No listed qualification invalidates the central asymptotic arithmetic result after its assumptions are stated precisely.

## 6. Independent evidence produced here

Files: independent_checks.py and independent_results.json.

1. Exhaustive direct global-chirp constructions for (N,M)=(8,2),(16,2),(32,4),(16,1), totaling 800 seed states. The first includes every output, the others selected output frequencies. Maximum covariance discrepancy: 4.31e-16. Maximum mean-coefficient discrepancy: 1.70e-16.
2. Fifteen exact modular recurrence tests, at N=8,32,64,128,512 and three deterministic odd a/beta choices per length. Every output agrees exactly with the independently evaluated literal projected-signal DFT.
3. Seven dyadic theorem-regime covariance spectra through N=512. Every eigenvalue is within 2.30e-16 of either zero or 2M/N; the computed rank is K(M-1)/2 in every case. Independent enumeration of the displacement counts confirms equality c_(u,v)=M/2^(v+1).
4. The pointwise real-median product-subset inequality holds in all 81,380 integer cases tested for R=1,3,5,7. Its proof above, rather than finite checking, establishes the general statement.

These exact and numerical checks are supplementary evidence. The proofs establish the general claims; numerical tolerances do not establish exact complex equality or numerical stability.

## 7. Research boundary

The meaningful v7 result is a genuine stronger approximation theorem using the earlier cheap global-chirp decoder, with a covariance calculation that supports output-domain robustness directly. It should remain labeled an independently reviewed project theorem, without a literature-wide novelty claim. The median inequality is established prior art. The exact covariance decomposition and its combination with this decoder merit a separate, broad prior-art review before any priority claim.

Finite precision remains a real next obligation: each recurrence multiplier has unit magnitude, but accumulated forcing/seed/phase rounding can propagate along N/2 steps. Root lookup, exact phase preparation, cancellation, and an honest total precision budget require analysis. Neither the source's small-N agreement nor this audit's exact finite-field checks supplies that analysis.
