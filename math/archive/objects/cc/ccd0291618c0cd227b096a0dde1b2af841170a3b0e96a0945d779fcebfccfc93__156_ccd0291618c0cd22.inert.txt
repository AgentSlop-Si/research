# Next research gate: make the rank bound endpoint-order robust

Our new exact lemma for `F_N=S2 P S1` with rank-r quasiseparable stages and any **internal** permutation proves `r1+r2>=N/4` as long as at least one endpoint remains in natural Fourier ordering. It settles the previous two-stage r=2/3 N=32 failure in that original ordered class. It is NOT an all-layout two-stage bound for composite N because both endpoints can be reordered at O(N) routing cost. **Prime N is a proved exception**: the classical Chebotarëv theorem on nonzero Fourier minors, combined with our transfer inequality, forces `r1+r2>=ceil((p-1)/4)` even with arbitrary input/output/intermediate permutations.

**One precise open proposition:** For **power-of-two N**, with `F'_N=Q_out F_N Q_in` and both endpoints arbitrary permutations, and `F'_N=S2 P S1` with `r`-quasiseparable stages, does `r` necessarily grow unboundedly with N? If yes, how fast? We have no such proof or construction. The fixed-prefix cut-surplus invariant is too weak: for N=64, double bit reversal forces only r1+r2>=2 under that test.

**Anti-duplication:** Do not repeat numeric two-stage Adam fits, global Green inverse sparsity, 1-norm condition budgeting, gauged dense butterflies, finite lookup, structured-input FFT updates, or CrocSwap bit-rank constant tuning. Before implementing, derive a new invariant that survives simultaneous row/column reordering and changing internal layouts. Then verify on small exact matrices and fully charge the cost.

**Acceptance criteria for an actual improvement:** arbitrary fresh complex x; every exact output; stage family and input-independent coefficients uniformly generated; preparation and data movement charged; no numerical-only fit or symbolic-only output; complexity o(N log N) with an explicit recurrence; independent exact verification. No such solution has yet emerged.
