# Independent review: Hero0 Phase10

## Verdict

The learned rational-cycle construction is a valid exact **promised-input** algorithm, with the stated asymptotic bound after making the guard `1 <= d` and `2d <= M` explicit. Randomized membership checks do not turn it into a deterministic exact algorithm on arbitrary input. The same-register operator-Schmidt theorem and its flat-spectrum approximation consequence are correct in the stated dyadic split. Neither result establishes a universal fast FFT, a general Fourier-circuit lower bound, a numerical-stability result, or a bit-complexity improvement. Novelty is unassessed.

One ledger correction is needed: the prototype's printed field-operation counts omit some root preparation. This does not invalidate the proved promised-family asymptotic bound when root preparation is explicitly charged or compatible roots are supplied. Source finite timings and witness counts are recorded evidence, not independently rerun results.

## Learned recurrence and rational synthesis

For N=KM and negative Fourier convention, define f_t=F_t[ell] from the length-K column spectra. The full output at k=ell+jK is S_M(z)=sum_t f_t z^t, z=omega_N^(-k). Assume the entire stream has a recurrence of order r<=d starting at t=r.

The first 2d samples suffice for a compatible order-at-most-d recurrence under this promise, including repeated roots and zero characteristic roots. A fitted recurrence's continuation and the promised continuation have difference annihilated by a monic shift polynomial of degree at most 2d. Agreement on 2d values therefore forces agreement throughout. Minimal fitted order may be smaller than d; checks for the early recurrence equations are already provided by the training prefix. The source implementation enforces `2*d <= M`. This guard should also be explicit in the theorem statement and storage claim.

For Q(z)=1+sum_{i=1}^r q_i z^i, direct coefficient multiplication gives

    Q(z) S_M(z) = P(z) + z^M B(z),
    P_j = sum_{i=0}^j q_i f_(j-i),
    B_j = sum_{i=j+1}^r q_i f_(M+j-i),  0<=j<r.

The middle coefficients vanish by recurrence; both boundary polynomials have degree below r. Trailing zero coefficients and r=0 are harmless; r=0 is the zero stream. At the relevant output nodes z^M=omega_K^(-ell). Nonzero denominators use the low-degree rational expression. A zero denominator is handled by evaluating S_M directly. Q has constant term one, so it is nonzero and has at most r distinct roots, even with repeated poles. These exceptions cost O(rM) per frequency. Batch inversion must exclude zero denominators.

Accounting before absorbing M=Theta(log N) is

    O(d K log K + R d K log K + R d N + d^2 K + d N + N).

For R>=1 this gives O(R d N+d^2 K+N), with O(N) memory when 2d<=M. Root tables, scans, indexing and output writes can be supplied within the stated overhead in the compatible exact-root arithmetic model. A single fixed finite field is only a finite test model, not an unbounded family.

## Membership and capacity

The transpose-circulant identity uses q_(i,-ell), not q_(i,ell), under ordinary transpose. Sharing one forward transform of the random test vector and performing d inverse transforms per trial is valid. The source checks every raw residual column after fitting.

For residual matrix R0, a uniform finite-field vector lies in its left kernel with probability q^(-rank R0); independent trials multiply this probability. The learned model and input must be fixed before the fresh random draws. Over complex arithmetic, independently sampled coordinates from a finite set give the corresponding degree-one polynomial bound. A predictable seeded check is not deterministic certification. A nonpromised input can pass and then receive a wrong result; exact promised behavior and one-sided randomized recognition must remain distinct.

At most q^(2d) length-M streams can arise from d recurrence coefficients and d starting values. This is an upper bound, not a bijection. Independence of the K invertible column-frequency coordinates proves probability at most q^(-K(M-2d)) when 2d<M. The bound is restricted to this representation. Taking d=M/2 does not represent every finite stream: a delayed last impulse is a counterexample. The source's particular generic successful tests at that width do not assert otherwise, and already compute all M initial spectra.

## Same-register operator-Schmidt gate

With input x=aB+b and output y=cB+d, N=AB, A<=B dyadic, A divides B. The unitary Fourier realignment is

    R[(a,c),(b,d)] = (AB)^(-1/2) omega_A^(-(ad+cb)) omega_N^(-bd).

The last factor is a unit-modulus column scaling. Orthogonality over the B-element complete residue ranges gives RR*= (B/A) I_(A^2). Thus rank is A^2 and all nonzero singular values are sqrt(B/A). The exact optimal relative Frobenius error at realignment rank at most s is sqrt(1-s/A^2), for 0<=s<=A^2.

Realignment rank is operator-Schmidt rank. A product's rank is at most the product of its factors' ranks at the **same fixed split**. This is an architecture restriction, not a scalar-work lower bound: controlled-phase diagonal maps have rank A and O(N) application cost. Changing routes or split conventions requires its own analysis. The result is standard tensor linear algebra; priority is not assessed.

This cut is different from the Hero17 Phase6 four-leg tensor's published cross cuts. It directly overlaps the Fourier realignment ingredient used in the independently developed weighted-cycle/mask-rank gate, but does not itself prove that construction's cycle rank bounds or mask requirements. The dense recurrence family extends Phase9's particular sparse-innovation promise, while remaining a restrictive input family.

## Prototype ledger qualification

The new `Count` structure includes input reads and output writes, while `total()` intentionally sums its field-operation/comparison ledger rather than those memory fields. However, `root_of_unity()` exponentiations and the negative-root inversion inside `ntt()` are not charged through `Count`; stage-base powers are charged. The theorem's wording that the finite count includes all root powers is therefore too broad. Finite totals should be labeled partial prototype field counts until corrected. Charging or caching those operations preserves the asymptotic promised-family result. This review has not benchmarked or rerun the submitted implementation.

## Independent checks

`independent_checks.py` uses direct DFTs and its own linear-system recurrence fitter, rather than the source's Berlekamp-Massey implementation. Over GF(65537), it checks 15 constructed recurrence cases, all 1,632 explicit Fourier outputs, 27 removable-singularity outputs, repeated poles, zero characteristic roots, zero streams, and every coefficient of the boundary identity. It also checks the late-impulse negative control and exhaustively counts small-field recurrence families.

Nine balanced/unbalanced splits have exact realignment rank A^2. Exact root-inverted bilinear Gram checks are kept distinct from complex conjugation; separate complex normalized Gram checks satisfy a 1e-10 tolerance. Controlled-phase rank-A controls illustrate the gate's cost limitation. These tests support the algebraic proofs; finite tests do not replace the all-size proofs or establish the source's unrerun large-size claims.

`check_integrity.py` independently verifies every source hash and archive member as data. The folder is self-contained; only Python and NumPy are required to replay these independently authored checks.
