## Mathematics and flow

Reads supplied outer archives and recursively inventories ZIP member bytes, validates embedded SHA256SUMS, compares hashes against a prior content set, and writes new-unique/manifest/provenance JSON records.

## Guards and contracts

Rejects duplicate member names, absolute/parent paths, POSIX symlinks, excessive nesting and cumulative declared uncompressed size through asserts. Outer size is compared with identity metadata, while its digest is recorded rather than checked against an external expected digest. Version is read from xattrs or defaults to zero. No archive member is executed or extracted by this source, but output JSON files are written.
