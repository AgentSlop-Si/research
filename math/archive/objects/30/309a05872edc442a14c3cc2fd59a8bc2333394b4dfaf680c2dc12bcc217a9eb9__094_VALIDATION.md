## Scope / counterchecks

All complex-algebraic lifting claims require (and the scripts check) valid modular denominators, preserved polynomial degrees, and the known removable factor where applicable. Passing a finite-field random-vector test alone would *not* prove a characteristic-zero identity; the lifting is based on **nonzero exact polynomial expressions**, not on probabilistic success.

The generic all-N theorem rules out almost all freely chosen mixer parameters and leaves exceptional tuned parameters unresolved. The fixed-instance N=4,8,16 result is an exact impossibility for **one remaining complex scalar Green coefficient** and **fixed outer/non-affine orders/gauges**. These results do not show that arbitrary-input FFT can be done in o(N log N), nor do they impose an unrestricted lower bound.
