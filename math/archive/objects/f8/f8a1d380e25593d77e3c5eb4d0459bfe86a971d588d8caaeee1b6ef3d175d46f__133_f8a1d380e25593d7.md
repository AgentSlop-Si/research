## Claims supported

- Correct sum/product convolution DAG calculations on tested numerical inputs.
- Exact symbolic polynomial delta identities for one varied signal (all occurrences), with degrees 1–4 in the independent F_97 audit.
- Dynamic single-edit reconstruction costs O(dN) for a full result, O(dQ) for Q selected output coefficients, after a charged one-time O(E d² N+(d+1)N log N) preparation plus seed transforms; d denotes polynomial degree in the varied input, E the reachable DAG size.
- Canonical sharing modulo commutativity and associativity; no global distributive canonicality.
- Concrete working compatibility with Phase 7 immutable FFT seeds and sparse/shifted leaf versions.

## Claims not supported

- A new general sub-N-log-N FFT algorithm, a new asymptotic integer multiplication bound, or a speedup over the strongest specialized incremental methods.
- Practical performance superiority for arbitrary unrelated input versions or long/high-degree expression graphs.
- Rigorous float64 error bounds in worst-case pathological convolution chains.
- A direct implementation of OpenAI exact Fourier result #130 or CrocSwap's Turing machine.
