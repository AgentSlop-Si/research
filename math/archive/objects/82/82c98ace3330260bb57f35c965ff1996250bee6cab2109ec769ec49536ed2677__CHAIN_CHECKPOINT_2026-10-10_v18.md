## New all-`t` theorem

For `t` total orders on `N` coordinates, construct disjoint equal sets `A,B` of size at least

`floor(N/2^t)`

that are separated in every order.

On such a rectangle, each diagonally gauged Hamiltonian Green kernel

`L_r (I-alpha_r P_r)^(-1) R_r`

has rank at most one. Therefore a sum of `t` terms has rectangle rank at most `t`.

For prime `N`, every square Fourier minor is nonzero. Hence a monomially gauged/permuted Fourier representation requires

`floor(N/2^t) <= t`,

or equivalently

`N < 2^t (t+1)`.

Thus

`t >= log2 N - log2 log2 N - O(1)`.

Three Hamiltonian Green terms are already impossible for every prime `N>=37`; eight are impossible from prime `N=2309` onward.

## Bounded-cycle extension

If term `r` uses a permutation with `c_r` cycles, a necessary condition is

`floor(N / (2^t product_r c_r)) <= t`.

For a fixed cycle bound `c`, the number of terms is still `Omega(log N)`.

## Arithmetic consequence

A literal schedule applying the terms separately pays `Theta(tN)=Omega(N log N)`. A genuinely joint evaluator for `Theta(log N)` orders is not ruled out.
