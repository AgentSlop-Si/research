## 2. Contract and exact identities

Let N=QM, y_t[u]=x[t+Qu], eta=omega^Q, and F[k]=sum_j x[j] omega^(-kj). Work over a supplied prime field F_p with supplied primitive Nth root omega, p>N and p=N^O(1). Both Q^2 and M^2 are O(N). The root table validates omega's order by a complete O(N) scan. The code assumes primality; it does not prove p prime or discover a suitable field/root. Input elements, modular arithmetic, addressing and tables use the bounded-word field/word-RAM model.

For C_t[h]=DFT_M(y_t)[h] and k=h+sM, F[k]=sum_t omega^(-kt) C_t[h]. This identity fixes all signs and the interleaved layout.

### A. Single unknown shear and scale plus Newton differences

If y_t[u]=mu^t v_t[u-at], then C_t[h]=mu^t eta^(-aht) D_t[h]. The output is the Newton-series evaluator of D_t[h] at z=mu omega^(-(k+Qah)). Its baseline and event kernels are the inherited truncated binomial-geometric identities, including the exact z=1 value binom(L+d-1,d).

Gauge discovery uses C0,C1 from two charged direct M-DFTs. It needs all C0[h] nonzero and at most e<M/4 corrupt ratios relative to rho_h=mu eta^(-ah). There are M phi(M)/2 unordered unit-separated pairs; corrupt channels touch at most e phi(M) pairs. Thus good-good votes form a strict majority, even for composite M. The source's filtering of zero/non-root ratios cannot remove a good vote and cannot create more bad votes than this bound. Its stable radix join is deterministic O(M^2) when p is polynomial in N and M is balanced; this is not a free discrete logarithm.

After selecting a candidate the source constructs every gauged raw sample and invokes the existing exact Newton recognizer. Thus an accidental candidate majority does not certify a wrong output. However, it tests only that candidate: this is not a complete recognizer of all possible gauged Newton signals without the pilot hypothesis. Order d>1 alone says nothing about the first-pair ratio pattern.

Explicit counterexample: N=16, Q=M=4, p=17, omega=3. Take row spectra C0=[1,1,1,1] and C1=[1,13,1,13], with every later row affine in t. Second differences vanish exactly, so d=2,R=0 with gauge (0,1) is valid. The four unit-separated pair votes all disagree and gauge discovery rejects. The raw vector is preserved in EXPLICIT_WITNESSES.json. This confirms the stated identifiable-pilot caveat; it is not a contradiction to the promised theorem.

### B. Unknown fixed-g dense orbit union

For y_t[u]=sum_j mu_j^t b_j[u-a_jt], each spectral channel is C_t[h]=sum_j B_j[h] lambda_j(h)^t, lambda_j(h)=mu_j eta^(-a_jh).

Require Q>=2g, distinct shifts, distinct eigenvalues at every h, and every B_j[h] nonzero. Then H_h=V_h diag(B_j[h]) V_h^T is invertible. The coefficient of z^(g-1) in its recovered characteristic polynomial gives the trace s[h]=sum_j mu_j eta^(-a_jh). One charged direct DFT of s reveals M mu_j at index -a_j. Transposed Vandermonde solves recover the spectra, charged inverse direct transforms recover the bases, and all N raw samples are checked.

The exact all-output formula is F[k]=sum_j B_j[h] G_Q(mu_j omega^(-(k+Qa_jh))), h=k mod M. The z=1 branch returns Q. A batch inverse table per scalar costs O(N), including its one inversion; no inverse is taken at zero.

The entry point uses **exact g**, not an automatic at-most-g promise. Fewer active orbits, zero component spectra, repeated shifts, or colliding eigenvalues can cause rejection. A fixed finite list of g values is a valid larger dispatcher only if its attempts are charged. Reconstructed nonzero component spectra need not be tested separately: invertible pilot Hankels plus complete pilot validation imply the necessary nondegeneracy.

### C. Multi-orbit Green innovations

The first 2g rows must obey the clean nondegenerate model B. Subsequent residuals are formed from the raw samples using

    r_t = product_j (1 - mu_j S_(a_j) L) y_t,

where L is backward time shift and S_a is the circular u-shift. The source expands 2^g-1 shifted terms. This backward-shift statement removes a harmless indexing ambiguity in the writeup: with forward E, product_j(E-mu_j S_a) acts on y_(t-g) to produce r_t, rather than on y_t.

If r_t contains normalized Fourier character c eta^(hu), its spectrum is M c. For distinct lambda_j, the causal impulse is H_v=sum_j w_j lambda_j^v with w_j=lambda_j^(g-1)/product_(i != j)(lambda_j-lambda_i). Its contribution at k=h+sM is

    M c omega^(-kt) sum_j w_j G_(Q-t)(mu_j omega^(-(k+Qa_jh))).

The source discovers all residual modes from raw data, then independently reconstructs every raw sample. For times g through 2g-1 it inserts zero forcing, correctly relying on the already checked clean pilot. All residual times at and after 2g are charged and checked. Full raw reconstruction establishes soundness for accepted inputs; it does not extend promised coverage to dirty or degenerate pilots.

## 3. Complete work, inversion and storage ledger

All bounds below cover failed bounded recognition as well as acceptance. R_b is the supplied total event budget; R_a<=R_b is the actual accepted count. Invalid field/root/parameter inputs may raise errors and are outside the field contract.

Single-gauge Newton, fixed order d:

    O_d(N + (d+1)M^2 + M(Q+R_b) + Q R_a)

field/word work, plus O(M+R_b+1) scalar field inversions and O(M log M) integer gcd/inverse preprocessing. The direct pilot transforms, quotient-record allocations and radix passes are included in M^2. Baseline transform/evaluation factors depend on fixed d. Raw scanning and output writes are included in N.

Clean multi-orbit:

    O(gN + (3g+1)M^2 + g^3 M + g^2 M)

field work, O(gM+g+1) scalar inversions, and O(gN+gM+N) storage for the literal source tables and arrays. The 3g+1 direct-transform factor comprises 2g pilot transforms, one trace transform and g inverse baseline transforms. Gaussian solves, Vandermonde preparation, full validation, phase/root tables, and output updates are additional to those direct-transform counts.

Green multi-orbit:

    O(g M^2 + g^3 M + 2^g N + M(Q+R_b)
      + gN + g Q R_a + g^2 M + g 2^g)

field work, O(gM+R_b+g+1) scalar inversions, and O(gN+N+R_b+2^g) live words for the literal tables, reconstruction and event data. The two residual/reconstruction passes both cost O(2^g N). Event expansion during raw reconstruction costs O(M R_a). Green weights require O(g^2 M) products and gM separate scalar inversions in the supplied code; they are not covered by the printed g batch-inversion counter.

The inherited geometric-cap sparse recognizer charges O(M(k+1)) on a k-supported accepted row. The final failing row is charged O(M(b+1)) to remaining budget b, including doubling and its possible dense fallback. Therefore aggregate residual discovery costs O(M(Q+R_b)); rejection does not get an uncharged search. This reuses the prior independently audited module, whose bytes are unchanged.

Under fixed g,d, fixed balance ratio, R_b=O(Q), and polynomial-size p, all are O(N). Scalar inversions cost O(log p) word operations; O((gM+R_b+1)log p) is absorbed by balanced O(N) for fixed parameters. This omitted explicit R_b log p term does not invalidate the fixed-parameter linear statement, but it should appear in a growing-budget ledger. The formulas are neither uniform in growing g,d nor O(N) bit complexity. In particular, 2^gN cannot be hidden for a growing orbit count.

Printed counters are selected structural counts, not an exhaustive operation counter or a fair timing comparison with an FFT/NTT. They omit Hankel/Vandermonde solves, several scalar inversions, sparse recognition, recurrence work, and table/setup costs. The asymptotic proof, rather than those counters, carries the fixed-parameter result.

## 4. Coverage, capacity and universal-basis boundary

For fixed g, the clean family has at most binom(M,g)(p-1)^g p^(gM) vectors. The Green family adds at most

    sum_(e=0)^min(R,(Q-2g)M) binom((Q-2g)M,e)(p-1)^e.

The gauged Newton upper bound is M(p-1)p^(dM) times the analogous support sum over (Q-d)M positions. These are upper bounds, since representations can overlap and pilot/nondegeneracy conditions remove additional inputs. For fixed structure and R=O(Q), all occupy at most a p^(-N+O(sqrt N)) fraction in the polynomial-field balanced regime. High ordinary matrix rank does not imply high input description capacity.

At square N=M^2, fixed input-independent choices a_j=j, mu_j=omega^j, 0<=j<M, suffice for a universal orbit basis. Their ratios lie in distinct cosets of <eta>, so each spectral-channel Vandermonde is invertible. This avoids any need for an input-specific coset search in the existence argument. Every raw vector has an exact representation with g=M.

This does not run the fixed-g recognizer: it immediately rejects at Q=M<2g. The separate universal-basis routine directly recovers a supplied chosen basis and is a different, superlinear path. Even given all spectral weights, the literal formula requires gN=N^(3/2) geometric terms and O(gN) inverse-table memory. The supplied direct recovery also has Theta(M^3) short-transform/Vandermonde work, plus its chosen coset-search overhead. A faster joint representation recovery and evaluator are both still missing.

Source UNIVERSAL_BASIS_AUDIT.json calls 64,512,4096 'orbit_terms_per_output' for N=16,64,256. These are **total** gN terms; per output is 4,8,16 respectively. Its 'direct_dft_reference_matched' fields compare direct DFTs of two vectors already asserted identical; they are not runtime evidence for the universal executor. Our 24 separate universal checks actually evaluate the orbit output identity against the raw direct DFT.

## 6. Exactness, conditioning and integration

All accepted-output mathematics is exact in F_p under the contract. A nonzero field determinant is the appropriate algebraic condition; it supplies no complex numerical condition number. For g=2, the Hankel determinant contains B1 B2 (lambda1-lambda2)^2, which can be arbitrarily small over the complex numbers. Green weights contain inverse eigenvalue gaps. Gauge matching uses exact zeros, equality and root membership. There is no floating-point error tolerance or stable-complex theorem here.

A safe implementation-level fusion is a **fixed finite public list** of the old recognized branches plus selected new (split,d,budget), (split,g) and (split,g,budget) branches. Charge all failed attempts; skip ineligible factors/orders explicitly; accept only after the full raw certificate. An ordinary FFT fallback would make this a complete arbitrary-input algorithm at the fallback's worst-case complexity, not a new arbitrary-input sub-N log N result. The upload has not implemented this dispatcher.

The independently derived confluent extension merges polynomial/Newton evolution and multiple shear orbits in one annihilator. Its pilot and fixed-total-order conditions matter; it does not subsume every original dirty-pilot or early-innovation case. See CONFLUENT_FUSION.md for the constructive recovery/evaluation theorem and boundaries.

No comparison with unrelated new branches on different layouts establishes inclusion or incomparability without a witness. A constant-size union is safe; claims that one representation removes another's entropy or growing-parameter bottleneck are not justified.
