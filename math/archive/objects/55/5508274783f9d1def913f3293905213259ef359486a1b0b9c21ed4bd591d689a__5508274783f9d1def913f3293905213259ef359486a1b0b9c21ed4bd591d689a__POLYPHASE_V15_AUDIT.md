## 2. Exact demixing proof and normalization

Write Q=KM, zeta=exp(2 pi i/Q), and omega_d=exp(2 pi i/d). The base local phase has the separable form

    S_0[b,t] = c_t omega_K^(a b t),
    c_t = zeta^[a t(t-1)/2 + beta_0 t] exp(pi i (Q mod 2)t/Q).

For beta_r=beta_0+rK, character orthogonality gives

    D_r[b] = sum_t c_t x[bM+t] omega_K^(a b t) omega_M^(r t).

Let H_t[q]=sum_b x[bM+t] omega_K^(-q b). Taking the negative-sign K transform of D in b yields

    F_r[p] = sum_t c_t omega_M^(r t) H_t[p-a t].

Therefore a negative-sign M transform across r, divided by M, gives

    G_t[p] = (1/M) sum_r F_r[p] omega_M^(-r t)
           = c_t H_t[p-a t].

The source's negative roll is correct: H_t[q]=G_t[q+a t]/c_t. Finally

    (U_Q x)[q+Ks]
      = (1/sqrt(Q)) sum_t H_t[q] zeta^(-q t) omega_M^(-s t).

These identities verify the signs, cyclic routing and the single final 1/sqrt(Q) normalization. The input positive-sign M transform and channel negative-sign M transform cancel after commuting across the transform of the independent block coordinate. The remaining chirp modulation becomes the displayed frequency shift and scalar dechirp. This is an identity of the full circuit, not permission to commute an arbitrary diagonal chirp through a Fourier transform without its shift correction.

The complete bank satisfies P_r^2=M P_r and (1/M)sum_r P_r=I. Thus P_r itself is a scaled orthogonal projection; E_r=P_r/M is the idempotent projection. Omitting the 1/M creates an M-fold error.

The complete raw summary map A:x→D has A* A=M I. Consequently A/sqrt(M) is unitary, and the exact D→U_Qx synthesis has every singular value 1/sqrt(M). In normalized summary coordinates z=D/sqrt(M), synthesis is unitary. The raw single-block map to one selected M-frequency fibre has singular values 1/sqrt(K). These distinct coordinate conventions must not be conflated with a general Fourier arithmetic lower bound.

The demixing/Cooley–Tukey portion of the algebra needs only positive K,M, Q=KM, nonzero c_t, and an integer a. Unit slope and K>=M are required by the inherited single-cycle Green helper, not by the demixing/Cooley–Tukey identity. The independent checker includes nonunit and zero slopes, K<M, M=1 and K=1 as algebraic controls. Arbitrary nonzero c_t would preserve inversion; the orthogonal complete-frame identities and unitarity additionally require unit-modulus phases. This is a clarification of the existing identity's domain, not a new fast evaluator.

## 3. Charged exact cost and what has not been excluded

For fresh raw input, the uncollapsed source program performs:

1. K length-M positive-sign transforms for D: O(Q log M).
2. M length-K transforms for F: O(Q log K).
3. K length-M demixing transforms: O(Q log M).
4. Q dechirp/routing operations and K final length-M transforms: O(Q+Q log M).

It also reads Q inputs, writes Q outputs and prepares or reads phases. The number of M transforms is 3K when summary formation is included. Its O(Q) temporary arrays and output are charged. Given exact suitable roots/bases, structured phase tables can be generated in O(Q) scalar operations; root/coefficient provision remains an explicit model contract. General lengths use an ordinary all-length FFT implementation rather than an uncharged oracle for a newly faster transform.

After cancellation, only the M length-K bank, twiddle multiplication and K length-M bank remain: ordinary O(Q log Q) Cooley–Tukey. For Q approximately 4N, this is O(N log N). The source's Theta notation describes its chosen conventional FFT-bank implementation; it does not establish an unrestricted matching arithmetic lower bound.

In particular, “the family closes at Cooley–Tukey” must mean that this displayed program has that exact endpoint. It does not prove that every conceivable output-only circuit for the same operator has to materialize these banks or take Q log Q work. A faster joint contraction would require a further concrete identity and evaluator. The master already maintains that distinction.

Fewer than M fixed complex-linear measurements on a fully occupied M-cell block have a common nonzero kernel. No decoder with only those measurements can exactly reconstruct every input in that block. This remains an information-model obstruction, even with nonlinear postprocessing. It does not prohibit globally coupled observations, raw-input side information, or a differently organized full transform. Padded input occupies an N-dimensional subspace, so independence of all Q summary coordinates must not be asserted on that domain.

## 4. Approximation, sampler and inherited precision limits

The rough-padding construction and weighted covariance are inherited byte-for-byte. With prime M=Theta(log N), no prime below M dividing K, and Q=MK=4N+N^o(1), all nonzero in-block displacements are units modulo Q. Uniform independent a in the units and beta modulo Q kill mixed displacements. Parseval over the full Q-character set and the count of M-1 partners per input yield

    E|Delta_xi|^2 <= (Q/N) R^2 (M-1)/phi(Q) ||x||_2^2,
    R = exp[pi sigma^2(2 tau M/Q + (M/Q)^2)].

Here Delta includes the normalized N-output scaling; the local unscaled Q-row has no extra Q/N. Gaussian preweights cancel locally through g_j/g_l before bounding covariance. The copied older `LOCAL_CANCELLATION_PROOF.md` has the valid but weaker M(M-1) factor for arbitrary composite Q. It is not a new v15 strengthening.

The master already sharpens the rough-modulus totient estimate: if every prime dividing Q is at least M, sum_{p|Q}1/p <= log Q/(M log M), and phi(Q)/Q >= 1-sum_{p|Q}1/p tends to one. Thus the relevant variance is O(log N/N). This changes the explicit logarithmic factor from a generic totient estimate, not the accepted alpha<1/2 range.

The Gaussian alias, FIR truncation and phase-bucket bias remain separate deterministic terms. With sigma^2=L=Theta(log N), midpoint buckets H=ceil(N^alpha), and 0<alpha<1/2, the reviewed construction gives O(N^-alpha)||x||_2 all-bin additive error. Independent rows and coordinatewise real/imaginary medians give failure N^[1-h(1-2alpha)+o(1)] for 2h-1 rows. For any fixed c>0, h>(1+c)/(1-2alpha), plus the sampler-abort budget, gives at most N^-c eventually. Inputs are fixed independently of fresh random states; a fixed default PRNG seed does not establish that quantifier.

Fully charged work is O_(alpha,c)(N log log N) in the already specified supplied-constant exact-complex/RAM model. It includes rough-padding search, all coefficient recurrences, Green scans, short convolution/kernel preparation, segment overhead, median formation and N explicit outputs. The padded length-K FFT costs O(N) per row because K=Theta(N/log N). This is neither exact Fourier output nor bit complexity.

The shipped `draw_state` caps attempts at max(64,8 ceil(log2 Q)^2) and aborts visibly. Conditional acceptance is uniform among units, and beta is drawn independently. The cap bounds calls to the bounded-integer RNG, not its internal fair-bit work. The master's already accepted repair samples independent ell-bit A,B, ell=ceil(log2 Q), accepting only units 1<=A<Q and 0<=B<Q, with a bounded number of pair trials. Acceptance probability is Q phi(Q)/2^(2ell); independent pairs give exactly the required product distribution conditional on success. Counting all rejected bit draws produces bounded polylogarithmic sampling work and exponentially small-in-log-squared abort. That repair is not implemented by this v15 source. The independent checker exhaustively verifies the finite accepted-pair distributions for five moduli.

The numerical programs use complex128, NumPy FFTs, floating phases and int64 modular products. These can lose phase bits or overflow at sufficiently large sizes. Exact normalized conditioning is favorable but is not an end-to-end rounding bound. Root generation, Gaussian pre/postweights, FFT errors, scan errors, overflow prevention and output tolerance still need a complete precision contract. Saved residuals below about 2.5e-15 are source-reported finite tests, not asymptotic precision evidence, and were not rerun here.

There is an inherited reproducibility documentation gap: `LOCAL_CANCELLATION_PROOF.md` names `verify_local_cancel.py` and `verified_local_cancel.json`, neither present in the v15 outer or embedded Phase 4 archive. This does not invalidate the independently reconstructed proof, but those particular claimed tests cannot be reproduced from this package alone.
