# Next research gate after the strengthened half-cut theorem

**Objective:** a uniform exact DFT family over unbounded N, with O(N) actual input/output work per nontrivial new stage, charged scalar/index preparation, and total o(N log N) arithmetic. A proven O(N) DFT remains aspirational.

## What to stop repeating

- Exact finite-alphabet FFT cache / unlimited table oracle, generic numerical collision oracles.
- Fixed-order constant-channel two-Green stages for t=2: **impossible for N>16** (new half-cut rank theorem).
- Claiming an exponent saving merely from removing a fixed number of layers.
- Rank-one gauge dressing, high 1-condition alone, or apparent dense stages with sparse hidden inverse.
- Small numerical optimizer success without exact all-N identities.
- Copying existing CrocSwap/OpenAI integer-mult network certificate races rather than exploring our own Green/graph/structural approach.

## Two mathematically honest branches

**Branch A, obstruction for t>=3:** Seek a genuinely multiscale partition/communication invariant that survives arbitrary interstage permutations. The halfcut proof cannot simply be iterated: `THREE_STAGE_CONTROL.md` gives an exact counterexample using a rank-one block-diagonal stage whose four arbitrarily selected half-by-half submatrices all have full rank. A new proof must use multiple nested cuts or preserve correlation across stages, not isolated block rank.

**Branch B, constructive all-N factorization:** The new `M = P G P^T + (1/20)G` has full density and Omega(N) natural cut rank, but O(N) execution. The phase-capable `D_L H M D_R` escapes the former condition-budget test without losing spectral conditioning, but H and D factors can be superficial gauges. Search for an exact factorization of F_N into a *sublogarithmic* number of genuinely non-gauge high-cut-rank stages (using variable permutations/Green parameters/complex phases); prove every stage's execution and parameter synthesis have O(N) cost. Do not infer factorization from matrix density or norm results.

## Immediate falsification checklist for each candidate

1. Prove the full N x N equality symbolically for infinitely many N (or give a rigorous recursive construction and proof).
2. Audit exact equality on **fresh full-length vectors**, independently from the synthesizer, over a suitable finite field and complex arithmetic where applicable.
3. Count all field operations, permutations, scalar generation, address work, and output materialization.
4. Strip diagonal/Householder/permutation gauges; show no disguised ordinary radix-2 FFT or trivial telescoping.
5. Track coefficient magnitude, kappa_2 and bit-complexity caveats; don't confuse exact-register and stable/bit-cost results.
6. Only after proving a uniform cost bound compare against O(N(log N)^(1-10^-13)), the September 2026 published exact complex-register DFT comparator (OpenAI, scoped model).

## Current status

- An **exact** structural theorem and a **new** O(N)-apply primitive with independent full-matrix audits are established in this checkpoint.
- Neither constitutes a faster FFT; all-N factorization remains OPEN.
- The path forward is multiscale algebra/communication or construction, not another memoization-based collision heuristic.
