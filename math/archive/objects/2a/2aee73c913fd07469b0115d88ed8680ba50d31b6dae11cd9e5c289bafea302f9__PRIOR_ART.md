# Prior art and scope notes

- The Segre embedding describes rank-one matrices as a projective variety and all 2×2 minors as defining equations. Its dimension/codimension formulas are classical. Reference: https://www.math.uni-konstanz.de/~plaumann/CAG15/cagslides04.pdf
- Projective intersection/dimension theorems explain why q-dimensional matrix pencils inevitably meet the rank-one variety above the codimension threshold. Reference: https://iris.sissa.it/retrieve/[private-id]/PhD%20Thesis%20-%20IRIS%20version.pdf
- Macaulay matrices / homogeneous Nullstellensatz linear certificates are classical algebraic methods. Our use of a square determinant is elementary polynomial ideal linear algebra.
- Fourier cross-ratios cancel invertible row/column diagonal scalings; related invariants are classical in complex Hadamard equivalence.

The project-specific advances are *not* these general facts: they are finding a rectangular seven-minor cubic witness for a specified five-term Green family, providing exhaustive N8 finite-field verification, and connecting that obstruction to an independently tested O(N) Green-sum executor. No publication priority, peer review, or universal FFT-complexity result is asserted.
