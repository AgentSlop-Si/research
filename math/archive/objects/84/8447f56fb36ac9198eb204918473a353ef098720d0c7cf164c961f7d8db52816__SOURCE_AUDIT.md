# Scope and originality audit — 10 October 2026

The following *available local files* were inspected or used for comparison:

- `fft_research_master_20261009.zip`: 3,532 archive member paths, many duplicate nested prior versions. Its earlier source files already discuss inverse-nullity, finite-field graph min-cuts, independent row/column permutations and affine/cyclic Green rank bounds. We do **not** claim those ideas as new.
- `fft_green_research_phase26_2026-10-10.zip`: restricted affine-run multi-Green products and phase-character balanced-cut obstruction. It does not provide the present all-two-sided interface cost formula.
- `fft_sparse_defect_green_gate_2026-10-10.zip`: sparse-inverse/low-rank-defect restrictions and an escaping dense scan.
- `fft_hero1_mixedradix_v13_20261010.zip`: exact phase-bank frame, not a faster arbitrary-input FFT.
- `fft_hero17_tensor_fiber_graph_union_2026-10-10.zip`: exact O(N) results restricted to recognized sparse-fiber inputs.
- `fft_divisor_spectrum_v13(1).zip`: randomized approximate all-output transform, not exact FFT.
- Phase 22, phase 23, phase 24, and prior project `FFT_*` theorem files: complementary Green/coset and polynomial rank findings; no all-length exact O(N) FFT.

This audit used selected proofs, archive metadata, and keyword searches across the master text sources, **not an independent proof check of every historical source**. Numerically checking finite instances does not establish global complexity or novelty.

**External prior art / provenance boundaries:**

- Fourier subgroup/coset orthogonality and its singular values are classical finite-group harmonic analysis.
- The complementary submatrix nullity theorem is established; see Strang & Nguyen, *The Interplay of Ranks of Submatrices* (SIAM Review 2004), DOI 10.1137/S0036144503434381.
- Forest-supported matrices' rank/matching relationships have established literature; see Britz, Olesky & van den Driessche, *The Moore–Penrose inverse of matrices with an acyclic bipartite graph*, Linear Algebra Appl. 390 (2004), 47–60.
- Linear matroid rank is submodular, and polynomial-time submodular minimization/matroid intersection is classical.
- The public OpenAI Math #130 project separately reports an **all-length** `O(N(log N)^(1-10^-13))` exact DFT in a permissive exact-complex model. This work is not competitive with that positive worst-case bound, because it constructs no new universal algorithm.

**Potential contribution (needs a prior-art review):** combining two-sided Fourier coset singular values, a matching-based exact rank oracle for arbitrary independently dressed cyclic Green inverses, and submodular moving-interface optimization into a weight-independent, numerically robust fast-rejection framework for the exact two-Green-sum-product hypothesis.

Claim as *new-to-project theorem and reproducible constructive counterexample to fixed-cut sufficiency*, NOT as a published or established novel mathematical theorem or a faster FFT.
