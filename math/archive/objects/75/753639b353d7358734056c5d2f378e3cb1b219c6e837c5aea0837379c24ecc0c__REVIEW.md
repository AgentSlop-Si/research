## Findings that affect interpretation

- Shared exact_utils.root checks exact order only at dyadic n. For non-dyadic n it checks an n-th root, requiring a separately certified generator/exact-order hypothesis. A fresh bounded example n=6,p=7,g=2 produces order3 while passing z^6=1. This does not invalidate the reviewed callers that use certified dyadic roots.
- The Phase21 inherited solver countercontrol is substantive: at N8 over F1201, omega=-7, one diagonal vanishes while the complete matrices are invertible. Fresh Leibniz determinants confirm det(L)=945 and the recorded nonzero det(P). A solver that inverts every diagonal has a stricter implementation guard than whole-matrix invertibility. The growing-fiber audit also uses that per-diagonal solver.
- Weyl packet support/completeness needs more than the nonpole condition. The source explicitly treats alpha=0 and alpha=-1 exceptions. A fresh N5,F11 block calculation gives rank2 rather than4 at alpha=-1 although it is nonpole. Generalized exact_utils roots and packet block invertibility should not be silently inferred from the weaker checks.
- The Dickson trace sieve requires its base-field root domain. Fresh arithmetic verifies a trace root for N3 over F5 despite no nonidentity cubic root in F5. This is the source's explicit out-of-domain countercontrol.
- The compressed Schur implementation has a finite horizon. solve enforces H<=log2N; const_feedback has no independent guard. A fresh full-space killed-state walk at N16,b2,degree5 confirms [0,57313,0,510], versus the compressed extension's recorded zero vector. This does not invalidate its stated within-horizon calculations.
- The two-sum bridge is an equivalent synthesis obligation, not a Fourier construction. Its own controls distinguish full matrix equality from determinant equality and show that an adjugate polynomial equation can be vacuous without denominator guards. Monomial gauge changes can invalidate an ungauged sparsity bound.
- Bank/batched-interface programs carefully charge hidden transforms, support-cover oracle calls, packing/recovery and small-radix work. Their explicit N16 and butterfly constructions do not extend by assertion to arbitrary size. The unique exterior is not itself a factorization algorithm.
- The N16 contraction source verifies a local affine-chart existence certificate, conditional on exact input data and its analytic enclosures. Receipt fractions and chart binding were reconciled, but the complete Jacobian and analytic argument were not independently rerun here. The outer-only N64 replay does not decide the full N64 family.
- Joint D8 equality is checked only on enumerated instances. Pulled-back bank symmetry is explicitly different from the accepted N16 seed bank. The spectral expansion certificate and coefficient gate give necessary restrictions, not coefficient feasibility.
- The restricted infinite three-term construction is a 5x5 block synthesis at physical N=5q^2, with two reciprocal Green terms plus diagonal correction. Its Bluestein and supported-length padding steps are reductions; they do not provide the missing full supported-length evaluator.
- Packed binary-field programs separate huge-integer algebra from bounded-word work. Large displayed exponents, occupancy profiles and recurrence ratios are count/formula evaluations, not exponentially large transforms. The word-array counter is a selected boundary-work ledger, not full machine execution cost.
- The historical coverage constructor contains sixty manual review assertions. Hashing and copying their source texts verifies identity only; it cannot freshly establish the historical reading or proof acceptance it records.

These limitations are scoped. No unrestricted Fourier lower bound, all-size construction or asymptotic FFT speedup is accepted from these finite controls.
