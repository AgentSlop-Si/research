## Mathematics and flow

Builds F=H*Q*C with reflection-pair H, 1/-i diagonal Q and real-cosine/sine formal residual. apply_H/apply_Ht match the explicit ordering. apply_R builds paired rotations; real_covariance_gate checks the seed and sampled C*Z=R*C equations.

## Guards and contracts

Dense parity_factors validates dyadic n>=4; public apply_H/Ht and several helper paths lack equivalent length guards. The oracle test assumes a fixed correctly sized linear map; zip comparison does not itself enforce output length, seed is deterministic, and trials may be zero/negative. Such calls cannot furnish the claimed independent-random linear-map soundness. Stored controls use correct sizes, two trials and explicitly linear dense oracles.

## Costs

The advertised O(N) apply_R work is not delivered by this implementation: real_rotation_coefficients recomputes positive and negative powers for every j, yielding O(N log N) ordinary field multiplication work each call. The assigned linear_rotation_reference supplies the recurrence-based repair. Dense creation remains cubic, residual entropy numerical/quadratic, and materializing C is not a fast evaluator.

## Tests and actual stored witness impact

The test suite checks complete matrices/orthogonality plus sampled covariance and rejects a scalar-normalization error. Fixed-seed covariance acceptance is a regression result, not proof or an adversarial certificate. These limitations and the cost defect were already recognized in the historical independent Phase22 audit.
