## Checks and implications

- The complex Hoeffding/union-bound argument gives the stated factor-four
  sample bound: split a magnitude deviation into real/imaginary deviations at
  threshold t/sqrt(2), obtaining `4 exp(-k t^2/4)` per bin.
- The deterministic cutoff rho_s<=epsilon and the exact-support bypass are
  correctly included in the piecewise objective. The cutoff causes a jump;
  a grid rounded downward cannot inherit the stated factor-two proof.
- Upward geometric rounding gives g<=2s and k_g<=k_s, hence B_g<=2B_s. The
  argument remains valid at the deterministic discontinuity.
- Evaluating all H+1 prefixes costs O(H), already covered by constructing their
  prefix sums. It gives the exact capped optimum without a geometric loss.
- The choice of head depends only on the input and is fixed before fresh random
  draws. A union bound over tested heads is therefore unnecessary.
- Fallback before sampling ensures k<m on sampled paths. It justifies charging
  sorted uniforms explicitly and then absorbing k log k into N log B.
- Uniform normalized compressibility is necessary for asymptotic family claims.
  An unnormalized upper envelope alone can be manufactured by rescaling and
  does not control normalized residual mass.
- The distinction between a vanishing normalized tolerance and vanishing
  absolute error is correct. In particular, epsilon_N sqrt(N) need not vanish
  on the all-ones sequence merely because epsilon_N tends to zero.
- The two-sided power-law certificate analysis concerns the minimum of this
  head/tail support objective, not a lower bound on arbitrary approximants.

The source's prior-art discussion appropriately treats importance sampling and
pruned transforms as established ingredients and does not infer novelty from
an unsuccessful exact-title search. Its competing sparse-spectrum theorem
adaptation is a separate claim about source results; this implementation does
not rely on that adaptation, and this review does not substitute a fresh check
of those primary papers. The present contribution is an executable deterministic
full-prefix planner and clearer construction accounting.
