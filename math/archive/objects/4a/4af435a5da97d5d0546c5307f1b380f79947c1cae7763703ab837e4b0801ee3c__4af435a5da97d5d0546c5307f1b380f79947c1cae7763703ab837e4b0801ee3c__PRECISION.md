# Finite-precision perimeter for mixed-radix v13

The exact-arithmetic O(N(1+log m)) result is **not yet an unconditional bit-complexity guarantee**. A native implementation must charge coefficient precision, primitive roots, FFT_m and FFT_(G/M) stability, scan errors, and input representation.

## A certifiable single-root twiddle lemma

Let `N=Gm`, let the exact primitive root be `zeta=exp(-2pi i/N)` and its computed approximation satisfy `|ztilde-zeta|<=eta`. Suppose every complex multiplication is modeled with absolute error at most `u_c * |input1|*|input2|` (with `u_c` absorbing complex-real rounding constants). Put `h=eta+u_c` and assume `h<1/(4N)`.

We generate `alpha_k=zeta^k` by a length-G product chain and `twiddle[k,s]=alpha_k^s` by length-m chains. An elementary magnitude perturbation induction gives

    | computed_twiddle[k,s] - zeta^(k*s) |
         <= (1+h)^(k*s+s+k+1) - 1
         <= (1+h)^(2*N+1)-1,

for `0<=k<G`, `0<=s<m`, with a conservative all-index bound; identity endpoints `k=0` or `s=0` are actually exact in the construction. In particular, when `(2N+1)h` is small, the error is `O(Nh)`; the balanced structure of the rest of the algorithm does **not** cancel inaccurate root propagation.

At every input column `s`, the exact dyadic sketch map `F_G P_s` has operator norm `M` because `P_s^2=M P_s`. Thus the global error at any output induced **only** by twiddle-table perturbations is bounded by

    |error_k_from_twiddles| <= M ||x||_2 * max_twiddle_error.

Consequently a sufficient conditional target for twiddle contributions of at most `epsilon ||x||_2` is

    (1+h)^(2N+1)-1 <= epsilon/M.

For a polynomially shrinking epsilon and `M=polylog N`, this requires `p=O(log N)` root/multiply precision, *assuming* root creation at p-bit precision and the stated complex-multiply model. The bound is conservative and does not cover error introduced by the subsequent short FFT, modular gauges, block summaries or majority-grid classification.

## Why global accuracy needs more than this

Independently of floating point, a single exact-arithmetic sketch row has spectral error `M-1`, Frobenius error squared `N(M-1)`, and expected `l2` error squared `(M-1)||x||^2`. No amount of twiddle precision can repair that stochastic projection error. The per-output MSE guarantee and high-probability *uniform absolute* bound use independent randomness and extra rows, respectively.

This note is a partial precision obligation, not a completed full floating-point or bit-TM theorem.
