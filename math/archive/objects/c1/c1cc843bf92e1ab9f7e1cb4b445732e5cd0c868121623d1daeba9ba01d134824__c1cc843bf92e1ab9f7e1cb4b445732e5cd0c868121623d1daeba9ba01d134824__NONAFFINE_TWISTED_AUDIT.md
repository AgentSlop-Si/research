# Independent audit: Phase 27 non-affine Greens and twisted-resolvent scans

Audit date: 9 October 2026 UTC. Source filenames/internal notes are dated 10 October 2026; those labels are preserved as source metadata. Baseline: read-only master checkpoint version 10. This report concerns exact, all-output transforms of arbitrary fresh inputs. It does not certify a new general FFT, a bit-complexity improvement, practical speed, or publication novelty.

## 1. Result

The principal mathematical statements in both new archives survive independent audit in their stated restricted models. Phase 27 supplies genuinely non-affine finite-instance rank/expansion examples and a useful Fourier-gauge candidate recovery gate. The twisted-resolvent archive excludes broader two-stage envelope scans and three homogeneous scans. None supplies an all-length arbitrary-input sub-N-log-N Fourier evaluator.

Two concrete implementation qualifications and a bounded new construction should travel with these sources:

1. Phase 27's dense `direct_green` helper is correct for Hamiltonian successors but wrong for general permutations, including the identity affine reference. Its cycle-count all-affine audit remains correct and its shipped dense checks only use Hamiltonian references. The theorem itself is not affected.
2. The twisted test module places its `unittest.main()` block before later classes. The documented module-discovery command includes them, while direct script execution omits them. Source logs are historical evidence, not tests run by this audit.
3. The transcendental mixer coefficient can be replaced by the explicit positive integer

   `lambda_N = m! (2 * 20^N)^m + 2`, where `m=N/2`.

   For any two Hamiltonian orders, this preserves the simultaneous complementary-cut lower bounds. It uses O(N) coefficient-preparation scalar arithmetic, but has Theta(N^2) bits. The proof is in Section 7. No cheap-bit implementation is inferred.

A further integration warning is essential: the non-affine Green **sum** has linear-time forward/transpose application and a bounded whole-matrix condition number, but no linear-time inverse algorithm is supplied. One cannot give its inverse to a Weyl compiler for free.

## 3. Phase 27: the accepted rank and expansion statements

### Resolvent distance, with all field assumptions explicit

For nonzero alpha and invertible `I-alpha W`, `I-alpha A`, the resolvent identity gives

`G_W-G_A = alpha G_W (W-A) G_A`.

Multiplication by invertible matrices preserves rank. Also `A^-1(W-A)=A^-1W-I`; the fixed vectors of a permutation are precisely those constant on each cycle. Consequently

`rank(G_W-G_A)=N-cycles(A^-1 W)`

over every field, including characteristics dividing some cycle lengths. No semisimplicity argument is needed. For arbitrary permutations, denominator admissibility is cyclewise: `alpha^ell != 1` for every cycle length ell. Alpha=1/4 over the rationals makes every affine reference admissible.

For uniform permutations, inserting the newest label proves the cycle generating identity `E[t^C]=t(t+1)...(t+N-1)/N!`. Setting t=2 gives N+1. Conditioning on being one N-cycle costs at most N. A union bound over the N^2/2 affine maps at dyadic N gives failure at threshold `C>=6 log_2 N` at most `(N+1)/(2N^3)<=N^-2`. The source's weaker integer conclusion `rank>=N-6 log_2 N` is therefore valid. This does not establish separation from all sums/products of affine resolvents or from every other cheap representation.

Our independent enumeration reproduces the minimum ranks 24, 52, 114, 240 at N=32,64,128,256 against all 512, 2,048, 8,192, 32,768 affine maps respectively. This is 43,520 exact reference comparisons, not a random reference sample.

### Dense Fourier image of a rank-two perturbation

For the transposition exchanging 0 and odd `b=N/4+1`, the conjugated shift differs from the shift by rank exactly two. Write `tau=I-vv^T`, `v=e_0-e_b`, and `u=Fv` for normalized F. The source expansion

`F W F^-1 = D-u u*D-Du u*`

is valid because `u*Du=v*Zv=0`. Its off-diagonal entry vanishes exactly when one index is zero or the two nonzero indices differ by N/2. Its diagonal entries are nonzero for N>=16 because `2|u_i|^2<=8/N<1`. Thus precisely `3N-4` entries vanish, leaving `N^2-3N+4` nonzeros. Independent integer cyclotomic reduction reproduces all counts at N=16,32,64,128. This proves the intended warning about using density as a complexity proxy.

### Finite expansion certificates

For the undirected multigraph formed by both successor edge sets, let L be its integer Laplacian. At `K=2NL+2J-NI`, the constant eigenvalue is N; the remaining eigenvalues are `N(2lambda_i(L)-1)`. Strictly positive leading principal minors therefore certify `lambda_2(L)>1/2`. The symmetric fraction-free elimination produces those minors exactly, not floating pivots.

All four source pivot digests were independently reproduced, including N=256 digest `8f4e9dfb215562a3c8bdec3b8d458b43cd323fbb79fd8ff0a29c4ab1b1b3994f`. They certify the specified seeded graphs only. On the centered indicator of any balanced cut, the Rayleigh bound gives more than N/8 undirected crossing edges. This is simultaneous over all complementary balanced cuts, not a sample-based claim.

The helper defaults to `delta_den=4`, a weaker gap, whereas the advertised certificates call it with 2. That is a documentation/default distinction, not a failed claimed certificate. The API also accepts `delta_den=1`, for which the constant eigenvalue becomes zero and positive definiteness is impossible; it should reject or document that vacuous setting.

### From expansion to Green cross-cut rank

For a proper complementary partition I,J of a Hamiltonian cycle, the induced restriction of the successor on either part is nilpotent. Therefore the diagonal blocks of `I-alpha W` are invertible. The block inverse formula implies

`rank(G_W[I,J])=rank(W[I,J])=number of successor edges J -> I`.

The crossing submatrix is a partial permutation matrix, so its rank is exactly that edge count. Each directed cycle contributes twice this count to the undirected boundary. If the union graph has Laplacian gap greater than c, the sum of the two counts exceeds cN/8 and their maximum exceeds cN/16.

For each cut select a nonzero minor of the larger-rank summand. Its determinant in `G_Z+lambda G_W` is a nonzero polynomial in lambda: either its constant or leading coefficient is nonzero. Avoiding all finitely many exceptional roots therefore gives every cut rank at least the maximum individual cut rank. Rational coefficients make `lambda=e` an admissible ideal exact-complex witness. Section 7 replaces e constructively without a transcendental oracle.

The independently reproduced lambda=3/2 audit over GF(998244353) covers all 12,870 balanced N=16 cuts and yields minimum rank 4. The source's 80 cuts each at N=32,64,128 remain sampled evidence for that **specific** rational coefficient. The generic/integer-coupling theorem is separate. Modular nonzero minors for rational matrices also imply the corresponding rational minors are nonzero when all denominators survive reduction; modular zero minors need not imply rational zero minors.

### Conditioning and the phase obstruction

For alpha=1/4, beta=1/5, and real lambda>0,

`||S-(1+lambda)I|| <= 1/3+lambda/4`.

Hence `sigma_min(S)>=2/3+3lambda/4` and `sigma_max(S)<=4/3+5lambda/4`, giving condition number below 2. This is a whole-matrix spectral statement. It does not make the selected cross-cut minors uniformly well conditioned and does not imply a backward-stable implementation or a cheap inverse.

Both Green kernels have strictly positive real entries. If their positive sum were monomially Fourier-equivalent, every cross ratio would be a positive real Nth root, hence 1; vanishing of all 2-by-2 minors would then force rank one. Invertibility contradicts that for N>=2. For arbitrary complex lambda, one anchored cross-ratio equation for each root is a nonzero quadratic: at lambda=0 its ratio is `alpha^-N=4^N`, not a root of unity. Thus at most 2N complex parameters survive this single necessary test. Parameters causing zero entries are already invalid Fourier-monomial candidates. Passing the test is not sufficient.

## 4. Four-probe candidate recovery: correct, not an equality proof

The algebra is sound under a **consistent pair of linear forward and transpose oracles**. After finding a column whose relative Fourier frequency is a unit mod N, its normalized column phases give `a_i=(p_i-p_0)d`. The row with a_i=1 gives `b_j=(q_j-q_0)d^-1`. The only candidate compatible with these data is

`Mhat_ij=M_i0 M_0j M_00^-1 omega^(a_i b_j)`.

The unit-column condition guarantees both a and b are permutations. A primitive dyadic Fourier matrix has N/2 useful columns among N-1 nonzero-index columns. In the source's uniform shuffle without replacement, expected number of attempted columns is exactly `N/(N/2+1)=2N/(N+2)`, so expected core oracle calls are `3+2N/(N+2)<5`, not deterministically four. Four refers to the successful structural probe set, after finding the needed column.

Independent full-matrix checks recover all entries for 35 hidden-gauge controls across N=4,8,16,32,64,128,256. To expose the limitation exactly, at N=8 take `M=F+e_7 e_7^T`. Rows 0,1 and columns 0,1 are unchanged, so the four structural probes recover F although M differs from it by rank one. This is not a flaw in the source's explicit `passes_sample` status; it explains why validation is necessary.

Once the candidate is fixed, a nonzero linear difference over GF(p) has kernel fraction at most 1/p. Fresh independent uniform vector trials therefore have false-pass probability at most p^-t. This assumes the candidate is fixed before these trials and the oracle does not adapt adversarially to queries. A reproducible fixed PRNG seed is a deterministic test suite, not a universal soundness guarantee. Passing finite-field trials is also not an identity proof in characteristic zero. The source correctly records both limitations.

The code assumes that the two oracles are true transposes of one operator. It does not independently validate that contract or check every intersecting probe entry. Integration should either obtain transpose by a verified circuit reversal or add explicit consistency checks; a forward oracle alone does not grant free transpose access. Green-term transpose is obtainable by reversing cycle orientation and swapping diagonal gauges; a product additionally reverses layer order.

### Fully charged recognition ledger

Let T_f and T_t denote one forward and one transpose call, k the number of second-column attempts, and t the number of validation vectors. With a supplied certified root and field, structural work is

`(1+k)T_f + 2T_t + O((k+1)N)`

plus a constant-per-attempt number of scalar inversions and expected O(N)-size root-lookup/permutation bookkeeping. The source uses a hash dictionary, whose expected constant lookup is a word/data-structure assumption, not field arithmetic. Its sorting validation in the positive-control constructor is not part of recovery. Batch inversion reduces the O(N) division lists to O(N) multiplications and one inversion each; inversions are not inherently unit cost in a bit model.

Validation adds `t(T_f+O(N log N))` because its predictor runs a conventional FFT. True positive instances have constant expected k. Arbitrary negative instances can require k=N-1, so worst-case structural work is O(N T_f+N^2), even before validation. A column budget can return inconclusive. The fixed source prime supports dyadic lengths only through 2^23; asymptotic claims require a growing-field/root contract. Root/field construction and certification, coefficient generation, memory indexing, random-bit generation, and precision remain charged obligations.

## 5. Concrete Phase 27 helper counterexample

`direct_green` walks exactly N steps and **assigns** each visited entry. For a shorter cycle it overwrites earlier powers instead of accumulating them, and it always uses denominator `1-alpha^N`. For A=I this returns

`G_bad = alpha^(N-1)/(1-alpha^N) I`,

whereas the true resolvent is `(1-alpha)^-1 I`.

At N=8, alpha=2 over GF(998244353), the returned diagonal is 767278012 rather than 998244352; the residual diagonal of `(I-2I)G_bad-I` is 230966340. Comparing the correct shift resolvent with this bad identity-reference result gives rank 8 instead of the correct rank 7.

All-affine cycle counting does not call this helper and remains valid. The supplied dense tests compare a random N-cycle only against the ordinary N-cycle shift, so their recorded successes do not cover this interface misuse. A safe repair is to require and verify one N-cycle, or decompose the permutation and use each cycle's length and denominator. The independent audit's cyclewise routine covers general permutations and verifies 1,363 admissible finite-field pairs, including small characteristics. No source file was modified.

## 6. Twisted scans: proofs and exact scope

### Two asymmetric common-envelope stages

The first stage has lower/upper entries `u_i r_j`, `v_i r_j`, with nonzero r; its diagonal is arbitrary. Each adjacent input difference `e_j/r_j-e_(j+1)/r_(j+1)` kills both exterior tails. The first two such differences therefore produce a union of at most three support positions, retained under any internal permutation.

The second stage has lower/upper entries `ell_i b_j`, `ell_i e_j`, with nonzero ell and arbitrary diagonal. Its weighted consecutive-row difference depends only on the two matching input coordinates. Consequently the two transformed test columns can be nonzero on a union of at most six of the N-1 row pairs.

For invertibly diagonal-gauged Fourier endpoints, the two test columns are `U_i(alpha_0+beta_0 z)` and `U_i z(alpha_1+beta_1 z)`, z=omega^i. If their consecutive rows are simultaneously annihilated, their 2-by-2 determinant must vanish. After removing nonzero prefactors it is a quadratic whose constant coefficient is `(omega-1)alpha_0alpha_1 != 0` and whose leading coefficient is nonzero. There are at most two such row pairs. But the scan product requires at least N-7. Thus N>=10 is impossible.

This handles arbitrary internal permutation, zero entries in the unrestricted generator arrays, singular candidate stages, arbitrary invertible endpoint diagonal gauges, and independently affine coprime-stride endpoint permutations. It does **not** handle independent lower/upper column envelopes in the first stage and independent lower/upper row envelopes in the second, nor general non-affine Fourier endpoint orderings. No inverse-stage assumptions are used.

The prior all-rank window theorem already excludes two rank-one quasiseparable stages beyond N=16 under natural/affine input order, allowing arbitrary output order. The new N>=10 result is a sharper finite cutoff for a narrower class with a different phase-polynomial witness. It does not replace the prior theorem's broader family or endpoint quantifiers.

### Two homogeneous stages

For constants a below, b above, d on the diagonal, the cyclic bidiagonal identities `BH=HB=A` are exact. Choose the forward orientation when b!=0 and backward when b=0,a!=0, so the selected column of B is a nonzero multiple of `e_0-e_1`. In `B_2 F B_1=A_2 P A_1`, that column on the right has support at most four. The left has N-1 nonzero interior entries proportional to distinct nonzero powers of omega. Thus N>=6 is excluded. Scalar end stages reduce to a row/column permutation of one homogeneous matrix; at most three entry values per row cannot match the frequency-one Fourier row. Singular stages cannot produce an invertible Fourier product in any case, but the proof did not divide by them.

### Three homogeneous stages, including endpoint degeneracies

For non-scalar first and last stages, `B_3 F B_1=A_3 P_2 H_2 P_1 A_1`. The selected right vector enters H_2 on at most two coordinates. Outside those coordinates it has at most three constant levels; there are at most two exceptional entries. Permutations preserve this multiset description. Applying the final constant-coefficient bidiagonal operator yields at most nine ordinary pair values plus four exceptional pair occurrences, hence at most thirteen distinct interior values. The left has N-1 distinct interior values. N>=15 is impossible.

If the first endpoint is scalar, remove its adjacent permutation on the right and select the frequency-one Fourier column. The left has N-1 distinct interior values, while the right has at most nine, since one homogeneous column has at most three values. If the last endpoint is scalar, remove its adjacent permutation on the left; the left retains N distinct values, while a homogeneous matrix on a two-sparse input has at most five. Both scalar endpoints give at most three values per row. A zero scalar makes the entire product singular. These branches are sound.

The result is for homogeneous H stages with arbitrary internal permutations and the exact natural Fourier target. It does not establish the same three-stage claim under arbitrary independent diagonal gauges, arbitrary endpoint permutations, or unrestricted variable separated generators. The alphabet argument must not be silently used for those larger families.

### Gauged circulant classification

For nonzero a,b with a!=b, select q with q^N=a/b. Conjugation by `diag(q^i)` makes H circulant: its non-diagonal entry at cyclic displacement t is `b q^t`. Its eigenvectors are `(q omega^k)^i` and eigenvalues are

`((d-b)q omega^k+a-d)/(q omega^k-1)`.

The denominator is nonzero and the fractional-linear function has nonzero determinant because a!=b; hence all N eigenvalues are distinct. Some eigenvalue may nevertheless be zero, so distinct spectrum does not assert invertibility. For the old S(1,2,3) this gives the displayed formula `(z-2)/(z-1)`. Cheaply applying a matrix or its inverse is not cheaply applying its eigenvector transform.

This classification is consistent with the prior arbitrary-row-sparse inverse repair: the witness S still requires growing rank defect against any fixed-row-sparsity inverse baseline, even after monomial endpoint changes. The new homogeneous two/three-stage exclusions prune this surviving class without undoing that existential escape or proving a depth bound for all separated scans.

### Static test-run qualification

The later weighted, asymmetric, exceptional-endpoint, and affine-endpoint classes occur after `if __name__=='__main__': unittest.main(...)`. The documented `python -m unittest -v test_twisted_gate` imports the whole module and includes them. `python test_twisted_gate.py` invokes the runner too soon and omits those later classes. Move the main guard to the file end for a future code repair. This audit has not executed either command.

## 7. Bounded fusion: a fully explicit integer mixer

Let N be even, `m=N/2`, and let Z,W be **any** Hamiltonian cycles. Keep alpha=1/4, beta=1/5. Set

`D=(4^N-1)(5^N-1)`, `Q=20^N`, `H=m!(2Q)^m`, `lambda_N=H+2`.

Every entry of `D G_Z` and `D G_W` is a positive integer less than Q. Indeed a distance-d Green coefficient has numerator `4^(N-d)` over `4^N-1` or `5^(N-d)` over `5^N-1` respectively, with 0<=d<N.

For any cut choose the same size-r nonzero minor used in the generic proof, where `r=max(d_Z,d_W)<=m`. The corresponding determinant polynomial after multiplication by D^r has integer coefficients, is not the zero polynomial, and has degree at most r. Expansion of the determinant shows every coefficient has absolute value at most

`r! binom(r,k) Q^r <= r!(2Q)^r <= H`.

Its leading nonzero coefficient is an integer of absolute value at least one. The Cauchy bound puts every complex root within modulus `1+H`. Thus lambda_N=H+2 is outside the roots of **every** such selected minor, without enumerating the cuts or knowing which minors are selected. Consequently, for every complementary balanced partition simultaneously,

`rank((G_Z+lambda_N G_W)[I,J]) >= max(d_Z(I,J),d_W(I,J))`.

If the union graph has gap greater than c, the rank is therefore greater than cN/16. This applies to all four exact Phase 27 graph certificates and any future graph with a valid gap proof. It does not prove a uniform graph family by itself. All positive-coupling conditioning and Fourier phase exclusions still apply.

Preparation of Q, m!, H, and lambda_N uses O(N) unit-cost scalar arithmetic (or fewer powering operations plus O(N) factorial multiplications). Its binary length is

`log_2 lambda_N = mN log_2 20 + O(N log N) = Theta(N^2)`.

Each complete forward or transpose mixer application remains O(N) unit-cost rational operations and O(N) scalar storage with supplied order lists. Its bit cost is not linear: even the scalar must carry quadratic binary length, and rational outputs may be much larger in aggregate. Normalizing by 1+lambda_N does not remove the exact numerator/denominator lengths. Its large coupling can make useful minors tiny relative to the whole operator, despite whole-matrix conditioning below 2. This is an exact existence/representation repair, not a numerically efficient transform construction.

A separate nonconstructive size observation is possible: at most `m binom(N,m)` complex parameter roots need be avoided after selecting one witness minor per cut. Some integer in `1,...,m binom(N,m)+1` therefore works and has O(N) bits. Uniform sampling from twice that many integers succeeds with probability at least one half. This does not provide a cheap deterministic choice or certificate for the selected random integer, so the explicit construction above remains the bounded fully specified result.

## 8. Independent controls, computational costs, and stopping boundary

Completed exact controls:

- 1,363 general-permutation resolvent pairs over four finite fields, including shorter cycles and small characteristics.
- 43,520 all-affine cycle-rank comparisons at the four supplied N.
- All 480 positive leading principal minors across N=32,64,128,256, with all four source digests reproduced.
- Exact cyclotomic density formulas at N=16,32,64,128.
- 25,932 individual Green cut-rank identities and complete rational-mixer modular cuts at N=4,6,8,16, including the 12,870-cut N=16 minimum of 4.
- 35 full-matrix Fourier-gauge reconstruction controls, with the explicit hidden rank-one corruption counterexample.
- 768 homogeneous intertwiner identities; 256 three-stage alphabet cases over two fields through N=64; 480 scalar-endpoint witness cases.
- 360 asymmetric-envelope cases over two fields through N=64, with fully varying generators and endpoint gauges.
- 2,976 twisted-circulant eigenvector checks.
- 348 all-balanced-cut **exact rational** checks of the new integer coefficient at N=4,6,8,10.

The general mathematical arguments establish the stated all-size implications; these finite checks supplement them. The source's own programs and inherited suites were never run. Dense verification costs are not algorithm costs: all-affine certification is O(N^3) unit operations; the determinant certificate uses O(N^3) integer-arithmetic operations with growing bit lengths; exhaustive balanced cuts are exponential. A supplied Green stage applies in O(N), but a t-layer/q-term circuit costs O(tqN) with at least its coefficient and order preparation and storage charged. The inverse of a sum is not the sum of inverses.

The Phase 26 common-cut theorem remains valid for bounded affine-run architecture. A truly arbitrary Hamiltonian ordering falls outside that bounded description; all-affine rank distance and cut expansion make the escape concrete, but not Fourier-coherent. Twisted scans show why escaping sparse-inverse or low-cut families does not complete the Fourier objective. Four-probe recovery can prune candidates and recover endpoint gauges; a deterministic all-input certificate, such as the correct Weyl identities with normalization, remains a separate obligation.

Next construction work should use internal complex gauges/non-affine layouts and carry an explicit coefficient-generation, forward, transpose, and (if needed) inverse ledger. It should test exact Fourier identities rather than density, expansion, or passing random tests alone. The current sources settle their restricted claims but leave that constructive target open.

The present elementary cyclic and homogeneous identities should be interpreted against that background. This bounded check is not a complete prior-art search, and no novelty claim is made for the new integer-coefficient deduction or any uploaded theorem.
