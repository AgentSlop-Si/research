## Durable new results

1. **Connected-Gram one-mask obstruction, all even N.** If `F=A Q P B` with `A,B` real invertible, `P` arbitrary coordinate permutation, `Q` one arbitrary complex diagonal, then the graph of nonzero offdiagonal entries of `A^T A` is disconnected. Proof uses `rank Re F=N/2+1`, `rank Im F=N/2-1`, and realness of `Q^*(A^TA)Q`. Positive real independently ordered Green sums have fully positive Gram and are excluded, regardless of conditioning.
2. **Exact bilinear Fourier gate:** `F^T F=J` imposes `B^-T J B^-1=P^T Q (A^T A) Q P`. Pair fingerprints `Kij^2/(Kii Kjj)` are invariant under diagonal congruence and reordered by permutation. Unique fingerprints recover the only possible P. Rank-one entrywise ratios recover Q up to global sign. Positive controls have dense B and are not FFT speedups; Gram gate is necessary but not sufficient for F (deliberate first-Weyl-only counterexample).
3. **Exact `F=H Q C`** with `H` O(N) reflection pairing, `Q` only ±i/1 phases, `C` real orthogonal cosine/sine residual. Dense and finite-field checks through N=128. Entropy `Phi(C)>=N(log2 N-1)` implies `>=N/2(log2 N-1)` two-coordinate unitary gates for C. This does NOT lower-bound arbitrary Green computation.
4. **Sparse real covariance:** `C Z=R C` for O(N)-sparse block-diagonal rotation R, and `C e0=v` explicit. These uniquely determine C. Verified with one-seed/two-calls-per-trial exact modular oracle tests. The sparse R does not make arbitrary Cx cheap; computing all orbit vectors separately is quadratic.
5. **Sample audit:** 24 fixed, independently ordered, two-term weighted-cycle Green-sum pairs excluded for all middle diagonal/permutation choices in F_998244353. 12 hidden exact valid Fourier controls recovered P and Q at N=8,16,32,64. *Sample failures are not a universal Green-sum impossibility theorem.*
