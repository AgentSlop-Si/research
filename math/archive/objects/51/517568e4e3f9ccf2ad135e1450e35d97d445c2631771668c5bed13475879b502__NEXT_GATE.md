# Phase21 research gates: extend sparse rational forcing, not the CrocSwap path

1. **Two entirely unknown graph filters, multiple defects.** For `R_k=H(omega^k)/[(1-alpha w^k)(1-beta v^k)]` with `||H||_0=2`, derive a finite polynomial-system elimination in `(alpha,beta,w,v)` using Hankel rank <=2 of the corrected probe sequence. Provide an explicit degree bound, avoid N^2 scans, and a full O(N) defect certificate. Track all root searches and exceptional positive-dimensional fibers.
2. **Prove or reject a universal resultant factorization.** On tested inputs the single-binomial `T(w)` satisfies `w^13 (w-1)^9 U(w)` with palindromic `deg U=10`. Establish this as a symbolic identity over the generic coefficient ring, or give a counterexample. The Phase20 theorem needs only the proved bound `deg T<=45` and makes no global claim about this pattern.
3. **Shared eight-probe inference.** Phase19 sparse inverse and Phase20 sparse rational constraints both use the same 8 spectral samples. Explore a single small algebraic selector that avoids multiple nonlinear estimators. Preserve all-input full certificates and generic fallback; do not hide a full N-point FFT.
4. **Coprime resonance and dense wraps.** The previously failing `N=128,delta=63,alpha=omega^9` example is solved. Seek a bounded number of resonances for noncoprime shifts with `gcd(delta,N)=O(1)` and exact repair; prove correctness and charge each direct probe. For unbounded gcd avoid O(N*gcd) hidden work.
5. **Worst-case dimension barrier.** This phase solves a <=3-dimensional continuous structured family for each support/delay. Generic N-dimensional input still costs O(N log N). Any arbitrary-input breakthrough must replace this low-dimensional promise with a representation that stays complete and whose routing/evaluation is truly sub-NlogN, rather than borrowing CrocSwap's conditional integer-multiplication exponent.
6. **Bit precision.** Provide stable complex recovery or stronger exact-ring bounds with explicit root supply, inversion cost, memory, precision and bit time. No floating-point/quantum speedup is certified here.

Acceptance criterion for a truly new worst-case FFT bound: prove exact all-input arithmetic correctness and explicit `o(N log N)` algorithm with every setup/preconditioner/forcing/root search counted, independent adversarial verification and appropriate prior-art audit.
