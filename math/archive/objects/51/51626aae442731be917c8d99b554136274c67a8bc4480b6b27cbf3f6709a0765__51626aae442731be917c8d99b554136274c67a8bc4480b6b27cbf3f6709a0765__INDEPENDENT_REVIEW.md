# Independent review: structured block-phase sketches

9 October 2026. Reviewed the final README, `audit.py`, and recorded exact
certificates. The all-size arguments below check independently of the finite
enumerations. No additional test runs were needed. The mask family and decoder
must obey the stated seed-only/input-independent sketch model; a decoder with
additional phase-sensitive information from x is outside the collision argument.

## Dyadic collision and fully dense pair

For M divisible by four, shifting t by M/2 changes the Mth-root quadratic
phase exponent by

    a_r(Mt+M^2/4) + c_r M/2 = c_r M/2 mod M.

Thus the phase ratio is (-1)^c_r. Every integer-polynomial slope has c_r=b
modulo two on even block indices r. On odd b, each active flat block sums to
zero, regardless of polynomial degree or quadratic coefficients. This proves
the fixed even-block indicator lies in the sketch kernel on an event of
probability one half for uniform b.

For v=i*t*ones, the fixed pair v+x and v-x has equal coordinate magnitudes,
L1 norms, L2 norms, and support. Their sketch values agree on that event when
the same seed determines the same masks. Their DC centers are separated by
sqrt(N), while each allowed error radius is

    epsilon sqrt(N)/2 * (sqrt(1+t^2)+t).

The strict inequality in the README makes the disks disjoint. Any decoder
given the same sketch, seed, and shared magnitude metadata cannot succeed on
both inputs there. Coupling any internal decoder randomness identically gives
the same conclusion for randomized nonlinear decoders: the two failure
probabilities sum to at least 1/2, so one FIXED dense input fails with probability
at least 1/4. The bad input need not be chosen after observing the seed.

## Higher-order CAZAC repair

For the 2Mth-root chirp with odd a_r, periodicity modulo M holds because M is
even. Its cyclic shift-d correlation contains the complete character sum
`sum_t exp(2 pi i a_r*d*t/M)`, zero for every nonzero d because a_r is invertible
modulo the power-of-two M. Hence the squared modulus of its unweighted sum is M.

The half-period identity is

    sigma(t+M/2) = (-1)^(t+c_r+M/4) sigma(t).

Summing and relabeling t gives
`sum (-1)^t sigma(t)=(-1)^(c_r+M/4) sum sigma(t)`. Multiplying by the conjugate
unweighted sum proves exactly the product M*(-1)^(c_r+M/4) used in the decoder
counterexample. Even blocks have a common sign, so the erroneous DC contribution
has magnitude sqrt(N)/2 for every seed. The imaginary constant offset is decoded
correctly at DC, making the example fully dense without altering that error.
This is a deterministic failure of the specified natural decoder, not a proof
against every nonlinear decoder of the modified sketches.

## Prime-modulus variance

For odd prime M and K<=M, the off-diagonal error terms have seed-character labels

    (u^2-t^2, r(u-t), u-t),    u!=t.

The last component gives nonzero d=u-t. Division by d recovers r from the
second component and u+t from the first; division by two then recovers u and t.
Thus distinct terms have distinct characters under independent uniform seeds
(a,alpha,b). Each has zero mean, and orthogonality removes every cross term.
Each x_(r,u) appears M-1 times with Fourier phase of unit modulus and common
normalization 1/sqrt(N). The variance is therefore exactly

    (M-1)||x||_2^2/N.

The assumptions are essential: repeated r modulo M aliases blocks, and
composite M may make d noninvertible. The recorded negative controls illustrate
both issues. This is a second-moment theorem; it supplies neither simultaneous
all-bin concentration nor a fast shared decoder by itself.

## Decoder cost and finite boundary

For affine slopes, factoring the common chirp gives the README's
`G_(m-K*alpha*r)` expression. Writing m=s+K*ell removes ell from the block phase
and yields a length-M cyclic convolution for each residue s. The resulting K
convolutions justify the stated direct/generic implementation costs; they do
not prove that no better joint factorization exists.

In the finite polynomial identity, multiplying the coefficient of degree j-1
by `eta^(a+b+2a(j-1))` produces exactly `eta^(a j^2+b j)`. At degree M this
coefficient is one, so the explicit subtraction of z^M is required. On an
oversampled root z=omega^m, z^M need not equal one. The boundary warning is
therefore mathematically necessary.

The final architectural rank observation is consistent with the usual
projection argument for a seed-dependent, input-independent linear output
subspace: a success guarantee on each Fourier image of a coordinate vector
bounds its expected squared distance to that subspace, and summing the N
orthonormal columns gives rank >=N(1-delta)(1-epsilon^2). The README correctly
excludes genuinely nonlinear or input-adaptive architectures from that specific
argument. This separate restriction is not needed for the dyadic dense-pair
collision proof.

## Verdict

No algebraic defect or unsupported all-size extrapolation was found. The
proofs justify the stated negative results and the restricted positive variance
identity. They do not yield a repaired fast all-bin approximation algorithm or
an exact FFT breakthrough, and the README preserves those boundaries.
