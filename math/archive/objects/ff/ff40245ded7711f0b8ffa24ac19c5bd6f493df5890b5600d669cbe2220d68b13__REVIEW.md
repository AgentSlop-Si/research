## Random-path Green stage: full scoped algebra checked

For T=3I−A_path, the spectrum is contained in [1,5], so each inverse Green matrix has spectrum in [1/5,1]. The positive sum G1+0.01G2 is SPD with condition at most five. A path solve, a permuted path solve and vector combination use linear scalar arithmetic and traffic.

Here is an independent proof of the robust cross-block lemma referenced by row75. Let e be the number of crossing edges of one path. Those edges form disjoint path components. Selecting every third edge within each component gives an induced matching of size at least ceil(e/3). The selected rows and columns of the precision cross block form a signed identity, so that block has at least ceil(e/3) singular values at least one. In the inverse block formula, both inverse outer factors have least singular value at least 1/5: the precision principal block and its Schur complement are positive definite with norm at most five. Thus the Green cross block has at least ceil(e/3) singular values at least 1/25 and has rank at most e.

For X=G1[I,J] and Y=G2[I,J], put E=e1+e2. If e1≥E/5, perturbing X by 0.01Y changes singular values by at most 0.01, so at least ceil(e1/3) values remain at least 3/100. Otherwise, adding the rank-at-most-e1 matrix X removes at most e1 of the ceil(e2/3) values of 0.01Y that are at least 1/2500. The remaining count exceeds E/15 before integer rounding. Both cases imply the source bound floor(E/15).

For a fixed balanced set, membership along a uniform Hamilton path is a uniform balanced binary string. Starting bit plus crossing positions bounds the number of strings with at most t transitions by 2Σ_(j≤t)binom(N−1,j). Independence of two paths, union over balanced sets, and simultaneous relabeling give the displayed fixed-first-path failure bound. Taking t=N/16 and the binomial entropy inequality yields c=1−2H2(1/16). The numerical constants can be certified without floating arithmetic: c>1627/5000 is equivalent to 15^3125>2^12209; the N32 failure bound below 97/1000 follows from 132000·2^224<97·15^60. The bound decreases for later multiples of sixteen.

Fresh exact tests verify 500 rational path-inverse entries, 72 rational singular-value controls using congruence/inertia, and 17,574 balanced-string transition counts. They do not replay the original N32–256 Bareiss witnesses. The source’s separate Laplacian certificate implication is correct: positive definiteness of 4NL+4J−NI gives lambda2(L)>1/4 and therefore every balanced cut has more than N/16 edges. Its cubic certificate computation is an audit cost, not part of a claimed linear setup.

**Randomness-cost clarification:** the O(N) uniform-permutation setup assumes unit-cost uniform range sampling. With fixed-width random words and rejection sampling, exact uniform permutation generation has expected O(N) word work; a finite worst-case random-bit budget needs a failure event or a different explicit model. The all-cut probability theorem uses ideal uniform permutations, not merely a seeded PRNG. This qualifier preserves the valid algebraic construction without silently upgrading its runtime guarantee.

## q-kernel cycle flow, row77

In the truncated polynomial ring modulo z^N, every H_q(q^e z) has constant term one. The coefficient recurrence gives H_q(qz)=(1−z)H_q(z). The reference b-phase orbit product is invariant under z↦q^(N/b)z, so only multiples of b occur. Its q-shift ratio is 1−z^b; the recurrence and constant term identify it with H_(q^b)(z^b). For b=N this is simply one modulo z^N.

For phase-count difference delta, all integer edge flows satisfying delta_e=f_(e−1)−f_e are f_e=a_e+t, where a_e is the negative prefix sum of delta. Multiplying the ratio identities gives the exact graph correction, with no missing boundary. A constant circulation is invisible because ∏(1−q^e z)=1−z^N=1 in the truncated ring. The L1 flow is minimized by t=−median(a), and a lies in [−b,b], so counting buckets give deterministic O(N+b) metadata work.

Each positive unit edge flow applies a first difference backward; each negative one applies an inverse first-order recurrence forward. Thus the stated bC(N/b)+O(N(1+D)+b) evaluator is correct in exact field arithmetic, with smaller-kernel preparation, q powers, root squarings and output traffic charged. This optimality is only for the fixed reference orbit and signed unit-edge flow representation. It says nothing about arbitrary interleaved diagonals, reversals, other graph operators or all circuits.

Fresh controls compare 154 graphs, including complete orbits, duplicates, forward/backward defects and random phases, with direct sequential truncated convolution. All 2,816 coefficients and 1,437 actual filter passes pass. No source graph checker was used.
