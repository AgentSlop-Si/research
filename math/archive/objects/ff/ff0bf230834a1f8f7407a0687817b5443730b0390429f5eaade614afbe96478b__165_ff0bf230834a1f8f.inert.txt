# Phase 14 — Galois field / phase coherence research

This continues the Green/Haar FFT research without restarting it. The exact point of progress is a **sharp all-size one-diagonal factorization barrier** for stages defined over the eighth cyclotomic field, plus an explicit small-size construction demonstrating the barrier's boundary. No O(N) FFT is claimed.

Read `THEOREM.md` for full mathematical statements, `CHECKPOINT.md` to hand off this research to another session, and `RUN.md` for standalone tests.

Main modules:
- `galois_gate.py`: permutation cycle count, Galois phase budget, exact modular cyclotomic coefficient certificate, and direct Fourier rank control
- `spectral_gate.py`: all-size one-polynomial-mask spectral obstruction
- `field_descent.py`: exact explicit `F=BQA` reconstruction at 16,32,64 in a genuinely nontrivial finite field extension
- `three_stage_probe.py`: exact three-stage phase schedule rejection via ordinary Weyl and independent Galois covariance
- `tests/test_phase14.py`: regression tests
- `three_stage_results.json`: fixed candidate outcomes, not a general theorem

The previous Phase 13 and Phase 12 implementations are prerequisites and are included as sibling folders in the complete ZIP.
- `conductor_tradeoff.py`: parameterized conductor B=2^b, exact one-mask iff N<=B² and a sparse residual with fully charged direct work count
- `two_mask_control.py`: positive exact 2-mask/3-K8-mixer DFT baseline at N=128,256,512; proves the Phase 14 one-mask barrier is sharp in phase depth at those lengths, **not** an FFT improvement
