# Independent audit and constructive sharpening of centered v12

9 October 2026. Mathematical proof review, inert-source inspection, exact finite-field checks, and independent numerical controls. No uploaded program was run or imported.

## Verdict

The centered Fourier identity, minimax global center, rank-independent Neumann-fusion bound, and exact two-transition carrier identity are valid. The source's N=511 rank and arithmetic-proxy comparisons reproduce independently. They establish approximation-constant and finite-parameter improvements, not an improved asymptotic Fourier algorithm.

The carrier needs a stronger numerical qualification than the source gives: a balanced product tree has logarithmic parallel depth, but a prefix product can accumulate linearly many multiplication errors. Accurate transition roots alone do not close that gap. The companion precision report supplies a separate error ledger and constructive repair; the uploaded NumPy/SciPy implementation remains uncertified.

Two additional exact-arithmetic improvements are proved here:

1. The centered pointwise Bessel convolution envelope can be reduced from 4.248775574 to less than **2.278**, without changing the algorithm.
2. Centering each occupied input block halves the maximum local Bessel argument. This specified variant requires at most two local coefficient tables, including the final partial block. It preserves the shared-state theorem and every rank-dependent decoder cost.

At N=511 and deterministic target 10^-8, the source selects (R,H)=(11,4). The combined refinement permits (11,3), with analytic-bound value approximately 6.3153e-9. Under the same deliberately incomplete scalar-work proxy, this changes 26,932 to 24,500. This is not a measured runtime saving or a bit-complexity certificate.

## 2. Exact identity and global truncation

Let Q be the least dyadic integer at least N, j_k=floor(kQ/N+1/2) computed with exact integers, and theta_k=kQ/N-j_k. Set c=(N-1)/(2Q), w_k=2theta_k, and z_n=pi(n/Q-c). Then |w_k|<=1 and |z_n|<=z_*=pi(N-1)/(2Q). The exact identity is

    exp(-2 pi i k n/N)
      = exp(-2 pi i j_k n/Q) exp(-2 pi i c theta_k)
        exp(-i z_n w_k).

The support endpoints prove c minimizes the maximum residual distance. Its maximum Bessel argument is half the uncentered value. This fact does not depend on the random decoder.

For a=z/2, R>=1 and a<R+1, the factorial-series majorant gives

    tau_R(z)=2 exp(a^2/(R+1)) a^R/[R!(1-a/(R+1))].

The Chebyshev/Jacobi–Anger expansion truncated to orders 0,...,R-1 differs from exp(-izw) by at most tau_R(z), uniformly for |w|<=1 and argument magnitude at most z. Integer Bessel parity handles negative arguments. The output carrier has modulus one. Cauchy–Schwarz converts kernel error at most epsilon into normalized-DFT l2-to-linfinity error at most epsilon.

Independent evaluation reproduces the N=511, tail-target 10^-8 comparison: uncentered R=15, centered R=11, as well as all 24 supplied rank-table rows. For polynomially shrinking targets, both constructions still have R=Theta(log N/log log N).

The centered interval also permits |J_r(z)|<=a^r/r! by the alternating decreasing series, so tau_R can be tightened by deleting its exponential factor. Our parameter comparisons retain the source's tau_R to isolate the two fusion refinements below.

## 3. The rank-independent fusion proof is sound

For n=bM+t, the source uses z_b=pi(bM/Q-c), delta_t=pi t/Q, and retains

    a_r(b,t)=sum_(h=-H)^H J_(r-h)(z_b) J_h(delta_t).

The symmetric h range implies a_-r=(-1)^r a_r. Thus the positive-order Chebyshev implementation equals the symmetric Fourier-order expansion, including signed z_b. Subtracting the full Neumann convolution and summing omitted coefficient magnitudes over retained |r|<R gives the bound

    [sum_(a in Z)|J_a(z_b)|] [sum_(|h|>H)|J_h(delta_t)|].

Absolute convergence justifies extending the outer sum to all r and reindexing. There is no R factor. Every occupied block starts inside the active interval, so |z_b|<=z_*. Unoccupied padded blocks have zero contribution.

Write A_H(D)=tau_(H+1)(D) for the supplied two-sided local-tail majorant. The printed envelope

    B_old(z)=1+2 exp(z^2/8)(exp(z/2)-1)

is valid. Hence the source's exact-arithmetic deterministic center bound tau_R(z_*)+B_old(z_*)A_H(D), D=pi(M-1)/Q, is valid.

Reuse the same chirp state for every global order. For each final output, all orders recombine into a single dyadic row acting on the input weighted by the complete kernel W_k(n), including the carrier. The inherited covariance theorem gives

    E|Xhat_k-E Xhat_k|^2
      <= (M/N) max_n |W_k(n)|^2 ||x||_2^2
      <= (M/N)(1+epsilon)^2 ||x||_2^2.

Independent states by rank would not justify this recombination. Exact-arithmetic aggregation and failure assumptions remain separate; numerical errors and grid classifications require the companion precision contract.

## 4. Sharper convolution envelope: less than 2.278

For 0<=z<=pi/2 and r>=0, the Bessel series alternates, with consecutive magnitude ratio

    (z^2/4)/[(j+1)(r+j+1)] < 1.

Its first-minus-second partial sum is positive for z>0, so J_r(z)>=0 here. Parity gives |J_r(-z)|=J_r(z). Therefore

    B_exact(z)=J_0(z)+2 sum_(r>=1) J_r(z).

Uniform factorial convergence permits differentiation. The identities 2J_r'=J_(r-1)-J_(r+1) and J_0'=-J_1 telescope to B_exact'(z)=J_0(z). Since B_exact(0)=1,

    B_exact(z)=1+integral_0^z J_0(t) dt.

The alternating series gives J_0(t)<=1-t^2/4+t^4/64, hence

    B_exact(z)<=B_*(z):=1+z-z^3/12+z^5/320.

B_* increases here: its derivative 1-z^2/4+z^4/64 is positive. At the endpoint,

    B_*(pi/2) approximately 2.277699012884086 < 2.278,
    B_exact(pi/2) approximately 2.276278962402266.

The checker supplies an exact-rational certificate for the strict 2.278 upper bound, using Machin-series rational bounds for pi and rational evaluation of B_*. Thus the existing algorithm immediately admits

    epsilon <= tau_R(z_*)+B_*(z_*) A_H(D).

This is a **pointwise** convolution envelope. It must not replace a sum of coefficient suprema in a precision norm ledger. For that purpose m_0=1, m_r=(pi/4)^r/r! is a valid separate uniform majorant, giving sum w_r m_r=2 exp(pi/4)-1. The companion precision report preserves this distinction.

## 5. Constructive occupied-block midpoint variant

For occupied block b define

    m_b=min(M,N-bM),  a_b=bM+(m_b-1)/2,
    z_b'=pi(a_b/Q-c),
    delta_(b,t)'=pi(bM+t-a_b)/Q,  0<=t<m_b.

Then z_b'+delta_(b,t)'=pi(n/Q-c), while |z_b'|<=z_* and |delta_(b,t)'|<=D/2. The midpoint is the minimax center for each occupied block by its two endpoints. Signed Neumann addition gives a specified variant:

    G_h[b]=sum_(t<m_b) S[b,t] x_(bM+t) J_h(delta_(b,t)'),
    d_r[b]=sum_(h=-H)^H J_(r-h)(z_b') G_h[b].

Decode each d_r with the same selected dyadic decoder, combine with the original Chebyshev weights and carrier, and aggregate under the same ideal contracts. No source was patched to imply the uploaded algorithm already implements this variant.

Every complete block shares the same local offset table. Only the final partial block can require a second table. Inactive blocks have zero channels. Parity still gives G_-h=(-1)^h G_h, leaving H+1 independent local channels, as already established in the v11 audit.

The exact-arithmetic center bound becomes

    epsilon_mid <= tau_R(z_*)+B_*(z_*) A_H(D/2).

For small D the local-tail improvement is approximately 2^(-(H+1)); the formula supplies the rigorous bound. Shared-state covariance and storage remain unchanged. Midpoint anchoring changes the local approximation itself, whereas B_old to B_* sharpens the certificate for the existing source.

### H=0: transfer of the prior sharper one-channel theorem

The H=0 kernel before the carrier is J_0(delta)p_R(z,w). The Bessel integral representation gives

    |J_0(delta)-exp(-i delta w)|<=|delta|, |w|<=1.

The difference is bounded by |delta| times the average of |cos(phi)+w|. Convexity in w and its endpoint averages bound this by 1, for either sign of delta. With Z=max_active_b |z_b'|,

    epsilon_mid,H=0 <= tau_R(Z)+D/2,
    max|W| <= 1+tau_R(Z).

The variance therefore uses (M/N)(1+tau_R(Z))^2 without adding D/2. This transfers the existing v11 H=0 theorem to signed midpoint coordinates; it is not a new discovery of rank independence. The unweighted frozen-block Taylor alternative also survives centering.

## 6. Carrier and source-specific implementation qualifications

The exact exponent recurrence is correct: d=j_(k+1)-j_k belongs to {1,2}, with transition exp[-2 pi i c(Q-dN)/N]. The scan uses O(N) work and storage, with O(log N) circuit depth. This does **not** imply O(u log N) worst-case forward error. A product of k leaves contains k-1 effective multiplication perturbations under any association, allowing O(ku) error plus O(k eta) from repeated approximate roots. Root-only improvement is incomplete; the companion precision report supplies the full repair. Balanced **sum** and **product** scans have different forward-error analyses.

Static inspection also finds:

- centered_fused_row calls _offsets, which constructs shared_carrier, before inspecting its supplied carrier argument. The cached carrier is therefore preceded by a redundant O(N) construction per row and only then overwrites it. This can be removed in a future implementation; no patch was made here.
- The N=1 nonzero robust path still calls choose_dyadic_block(1), which rejects Q=1. Return the identity explicitly. Testing support_center(1) does not cover that path.
- int64 grid/phase arithmetic is not an arbitrary-size exact-index theorem; modular products and conversions require charged O(log N)-bit integer handling.
- NumPy/SciPy phase, Bessel and FFT calls do not certify coefficient/root accuracy, pairwise channel reductions, or the decoder-on-summaries contract. Lower-precision storage experiments are not proofs of lower-precision-only arithmetic.
- Negative local channels remain separately computed despite exact redundancy.
- centered_fused_center is a full-transform reference, not a production selected-decoder runtime demonstration.
- Inactive padded blocks can have |z_b|>z_*, although their exact channels vanish. A numerical proof must preserve these zeros or budget their error rather than apply the active-support bound to arbitrary noisy padded summaries.

## 7. Fully charged work and exactness scope

For L rows, retained R, halfwidth H and Q=KM:

- Channel formation costs O(L(H+1)Q), with O((H+1)M) local coefficient values; midpoint anchoring needs at most twice that setup.
- Global coefficient/channel synthesis costs O(L(H+1)RK).
- Each row still uses R length-K FFTs and R length-Q decoder scans: O(L R K log K+L RQ).
- Selected Clenshaw, output materialization and aggregation cost O(LRN), plus carrier setup and O(N) ideal reusable carrier construction.

Parity-aware H+1 accounting still includes both shifted global factors. For fixed H,L and M=Theta(log N), arithmetic remains O(RN). Streaming working storage is O(Q+(H+1)(K+M)+N)=O(N). A retained coefficient table costs additional O((H+1)RK), also O(N) in the stated R=Theta(log N/log log N) regime; it can instead be streamed.

Bessel setup is not free. The prior bounded-argument factorial-series construction applies by parity on the smaller signed interval. If b-bit coefficient accuracy uses T=O((b+1)/log(b+2)) terms, a conservative charged setup is O((H+1)(M+RK)T), plus pi/argument preparation, guard precision and range handling. For fixed H and b=Theta(log N), this is O(N log N/(log log N)^2), below O(RN) but nonzero. This approximate generator is not a finite exact Bessel oracle.

Carrier roots/products have a separate guard or reanchoring ledger. Bit cost multiplies operation counts by costs at the respective precisions and adds exact indices, input/output, normalizers, exponent/range and setup work. No concrete numerical library is certified here.

The previous blocked polynomial-prefix subroutine can be applied after centered coefficient preparation. Centering removes neither its basis-conversion cost nor its numerical-analysis gap nor the R independent short-FFT bank. Total cost therefore remains O(RN). The forward Bessel-order recurrence is also not stabilized by centering; smaller centered arguments can worsen conditioning.

The fixed-summary exactness barrier is unchanged: for L fixed states a complete M-coordinate block supplies at most L(H+1) independent scalars. Uniform exact recovery from those summaries requires L(H+1)>=M, irrespective of R. An invertible carrier diagonal cannot add information. The earlier exact residual-kernel rank obstruction likewise survives diagonal centering. These are architecture-specific barriers, not unrestricted Fourier circuit lower bounds.

## 8. Exact multicut screen reproduces the negative result

The two-interval inequality follows from the existing window rank-nullity argument. Restrict t selected column coefficients to the kernel of the complementary C rows. Their remaining image has dimension at least t-rank(C[T,J]); its intersection with ker M[R,H] loses at most h-rank(M[R,H]) dimensions. Hence

    t-h+rank(M[R,H])<=rank(B[R,I])+rank(C[T,J]).

Quasiseparable cut ranks add safely over interval components. A nonzero Fourier minor over F_97 at a primitive eighth root implies the corresponding characteristic-zero cyclotomic minor is nonzero under specialization. Common Fourier normalization does not affect rank.

Our independent modular elimination and subset/permutation enumeration checks all 8! permutations. It reproduces 72 one-interval constraints and 84 at-most-two-interval constraints. Both admit exactly 15,728 permutations and reject 24,592; there are zero new rejections. This verifies the finite negative result, not an all-length redundancy theorem or an exact Fourier executor.

The fresh checker records:

- 33 high-precision Bessel envelope checks; l1/integral residual below 9.50e-66 and an exact-rational certificate for B_*(pi/2)<2.278.
- 6,560 signed fusion kernels, equally split between left and midpoint anchors. Maximum improved fusion-bound ratio approximately 0.57707; maximum total-bound ratio approximately 0.94786.
- Nearly sharp transferred H=0 controls: maximum ratio approximately 0.999980.
- Five exhaustive covariance-matrix controls covering 1,192 chirp states, with mean-matrix residual below 4.09e-16 and covariance ratio approximately 0.999999454.
- All 56 saved parameter choices and 24 rank-table rows reproduced, plus separate extension choices.
- Exact N=8 negative multicut enumeration independently reproduced.

Floating/high-precision controls corroborate algebra and indexing; they are not interval proofs of every transcendental value, implementation certification, or asymptotic benchmarks. Rational exponent and finite-field computations have their explicitly narrower exact status. The mathematical derivations above establish the general theorems.

The useful implementation next steps are the certified carrier repair, parity-reduced channels, midpoint weights and summary-interface ledger. Larger gates remain a cheaper stable short-FFT family generator, a numerical/bit certificate for the blocked scan, and an architecture escaping exact summary-rank limitations. This audit proves neither an exact all-output sub-N-log-N FFT nor a stronger asymptotic approximation theorem.
