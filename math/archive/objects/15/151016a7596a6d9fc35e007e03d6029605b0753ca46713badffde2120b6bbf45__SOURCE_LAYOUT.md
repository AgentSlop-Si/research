Use `source_records.json` for present package locations and archive-member names in `content_inventory.json` to recover any source. `original_snapshot_SHA256SUMS` is historical evidence for that complete inspection snapshot; it is not the active package manifest. Some audit working files were present in that inspection snapshot. Their historical rows do not describe final audits; each final sealed audit has its own current manifest. Static review, inherited proofs, new independent proofs, finite checks and source-recorded benchmark claims are distinct evidence categories. Original filename dates are source labels, not validation dates. No exact arbitrary-input transform improvement or novelty conclusion is implied.
