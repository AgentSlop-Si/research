# Hero #1 — Phase 31 research gate

**Goal:** an actual exact all-input scalar-operation reduction beyond the optimized Phase 29 circuit, with a plausible route to genuine cross-stage sharing. Phase 30 is structural mathematics, not a speedup.

1. **Escape the one-layer rational barrier.** Search two or more *nonrational* interleaved transformations around the `(8,h)` twiddle stage. A valid candidate must lower the aggregate add+multiply count **including all added layers**. The Phase 30 rank bound rules out recoding the isolated diagonal with rational pre/post additions, even arbitrarily many.
2. **Galois-character planning as a certificate.** Compute the per-character rank spectrum (not just total rank) for proposed small kernels, and reject those whose claimed number of nonrational channels is below the applicable projection rank. Pay for precomputation. This can be extended to arbitrary finite abelian group blocks via the number of cyclic subgroups.
3. **Constructive circuit search.** For a 120- or 240-point kernel, allow one nonrational mixing stage crossing twiddle/Winograd boundaries; expand all sparse operations, search for valid rational linear rewrites, and verify characteristic-zero coefficients, two finite fields, all basis vectors, and cold setup.
4. **Keep the stronger comparator.** At `N=120h`, use `180h log2(h)+1163h+15`, not Bluestein. For mixed lengths, use an optimized same-length comparator and check identity scalar handling. No rate claim from a fixed O(N) saving.
5. **Priority audit.** The `N-c(A)` rank law is an apparently clean standalone lemma with a group-action proof, but rational character-orbit theory is old. Compare against the existing literature before treating it as new mathematics. Prepare a short note with fully stated conditions, counterexamples to over-generalization, and the precise circuit corollary.

**Falsifiers:** a candidate with an incorrect basis column, uncharged coefficient preparation, rational-only single scaling fusion of the isolated twiddle stage, repeated graph nodes falsely counted as free, or a claimed general FFT lower bound from a restricted rational-pre/post representation.
