1. A middle Green affine pencil with **two tunable coefficients**, `B(lambda,mu)=G0+lambda*G1+mu*G2`, is still `O(N)` to apply, but no Fourier factorization is known.
2. Two projective moment equations have a **zero-dimensional** joint solution locus over C for precisely seeded Green families at `N=4,8,16`. One-slice modular Bezout identities plus nonzero constant leading coefficients in mu prove no common algebraic curve. The bounds on candidate pairs are respectively 512, 8192, 131072 (not actual solution counts).
3. A far stronger homogeneous cross-ratio/Macaulay gate eliminates **every complex lambda,mu and every pair of internal masks/permutations** for the seeded families at **N=4 and N=8**. At N=4 every one of 576 complete permutation pairs gives a 54-by-15 Macaulay matrix of rank 15. At N=8 every one of 112896 possible ordered triple mappings gives a 24-by-15 matrix of rank 15, which covers all full permutations. The coefficients and modular minors lift to exact characteristic-zero nonfactorization.
4. Verified positive control: a planted valid Green target leads to Macaulay rank <15 for its correct mapping. N=4 two distinct good reduction primes both exclude the actual Fourier target.
5. N=16 all-triplet exclusion was **not** proved; only 2000 sampled anchored mappings passed. Do not imply universality at N=16.
6. New Phase 29 suite: 11 tests including full N=8 exhaustive re-enumeration and linear-time three-Green executor vs dense exact output through N=128. Inherited Phase 28 baseline 17/17 tests passed before modifications.
