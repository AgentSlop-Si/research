# A correlated-forcing obstruction to one particular shared-scan shortcut

9 October 2026 UTC. Bounded continuation of roadmap directions 3 and 4.

## Result and scope

The old weighted-scan obstruction applied to unrestricted Q-dimensional forcings. The following extension treats the actual correlated K-dimensional equal-block forcing family, for target lengths sufficiently close to their dyadic lift.

For odd target length N, dyadic lift Q, Q=KM, and the first Bessel channel, any exact proposal of the form

    selected weighted scan = selected scan of weighted forcing + rank-r repair

with the same output weights extended diagonally to all Q forcing coordinates requires

    r >= max(0, K - 1 - (Q-N)).

In particular, at N=Q-1 it requires r>=K-2. This remains true for every choice of weights at the unselected coordinates. The proof uses the actual forcing's rank, not independently arbitrary channel vectors.

This excludes a bounded-rank weight-moving repair on this infinite near-dyadic family. It does not exclude fast application of a high-rank repair, different input-side weights, cancellation among all channel commutators, a joint polynomial evaluator, or unrestricted shared Green computation. It is not an arithmetic or finite-precision lower bound, and no literature-priority claim is made.

## 1. A short standalone rank lemma

Work over C, or a field of characteristic other than 2. Let L be the Q-by-Q signed prefix matrix

    L[t,u] = 1/2 if u>=t, and -1/2 if u<t.

Let T be the shift with ones on the superdiagonal and -1 in the bottom-left corner. Then T^Q=-I and L=(I-T)^(-1). Let S select n>=2 distinct rows. Let W be diagonal and assume its n selected values are pairwise distinct. Finally let E be any Q-by-k matrix of rank d.

**Lemma.**

    rank(S (W L - L W) E) >= max(0, d-(Q-n)).

**Proof.** Sort the selected indices t_0<...<t_(n-1), and write v_i=W[t_i,t_i]. The selected-row, selected-column block of L is itself the n-dimensional signed prefix matrix L_n. Thus the corresponding commutator block is [diag(v),L_n]. The identity

    [diag(v),L_n] = L_n (diag(v) T_n - T_n diag(v)) L_n

and det(I-T_n)=2 give

    det([diag(v),L_n]) = ((-1)^n/4) product_i (v_i-v_(i+1)),

with cyclic indices. This is nonzero. Therefore A=S[W,L] has row rank n and kernel dimension Q-n. Restricting A to the d-dimensional image of E loses at most Q-n dimensions. This proves the lemma. QED.

Suppose the proposed repair is U V G, where U is n-by-r, V is r-by-k, and G is arbitrary. Exact equality

    S W L E G = S L W E G + U V G

for all G implies S[W,L]E=UV. The lemma gives the displayed lower bound on r. The same bound applies if the repair receives only r linear measurements of G and then uses an arbitrary decoder: indistinguishability along the measurement kernel forces that kernel into the correction map's kernel.

No invertibility or genericity premise is imposed on E beyond its stated rank. Nonzero input/output diagonal gauges and permutations preserve the conclusion. The selected-row ordering affects a determinant sign, not rank.

## 2. The actual equal-block forcing is injective before channel weighting

Here is an explicit check that the lemma's d is large for the inherited Green decoder. Assume Q=KM is a power of two, 2<=M<=K, and M divides K. Take odd slope a, integer beta, and a primitive Qth root omega. For a K-vector y define

    s_u = y_(a u mod K),
    b_u = omega^[M(a u+beta)+a M(M-1)/2],
    gamma_t = omega^[a t(t-1)/2+beta t],
    (D y)_t = gamma_t (s_(t mod K)-b_(t mod K) s_((t+M) mod K)).

This is the source's equal-block periodic forcing, up to a nonzero common normalizer. Its boundary coefficients are K-periodic.

Let B act on s by (Bs)_u=s_u-b_u s_(u+M mod K). The shift u->u+M has M cycles of length ell=K/M. For each cycle,

    product_(j=0)^(ell-1) b_(u+jM)
      = omega^[K(a u+beta)+a K(K-1)/2] = p_u,

and p_u^M=-1 because a and K-1 are odd. Hence p_u is not 1. If Bs=0, iteration around that cycle gives s_u=p_u s_u, forcing every s_u=0. Therefore B is invertible. Permuting y, periodically lifting Bs, and multiplying by nonzero gamma_t preserve injectivity, so rank D=K.

This statement is also valid over a field of odd characteristic containing a primitive Qth root. No Bessel constants enter it.

## 3. The first Bessel channel has exactly K-1 independent directions

Let U_K be the invertible K-point Fourier matrix, with either normalization, and set

    E_1 = D U_K diag(J_1(pi b/K): b=0,...,K-1).

This is the first Bessel-weighted channel's forcing map from the shared input summary G. We have J_1(0)=0 and J_1(z)>0 for 0<z<pi. Thus rank E_1=K-1.

For completeness, positivity does not need a quoted numerical location of a Bessel zero. The classical defining series gives, with t=z^2/4<5/2,

    J_1(z)/(z/2) = sum_(m>=0) (-1)^m t^m/(m!(m+1)!)
                  > 1-t/2+t^2/12-t^3/144
                  >= 187/1152 > 0.

The tail after the displayed negative term is positive by the decreasing alternating-series test. The cubic decreases on [0,5/2], since its derivative is -(t^2-8t+24)/48<0, and its value at 5/2 is 187/1152. The elementary bound pi^2<10 supplies t<5/2. A centered variant must count its own zero arguments; this report's exact K-1 count is for the displayed uncentered channel.

The classical series is documented in [NIST DLMF 10.2.2](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.2.E2). No exact finite-arithmetic Bessel generation procedure is asserted by using these constants in the rank theorem.

In the uncentered one-local-channel setting at N=Q-1, every block still contains its active local-offset-zero location, including the final block. Its local Bessel coefficient is J_0(0)=1 and its chirp factor is nonzero. Restricting the input to those locations permits independent choice of each summary coordinate, so the arbitrary-G model is realized by actual inputs. More generally, a different input-summary map must have its own rank checked; the conclusion is not automatically transferred to a constrained G family.

## 4. Near-grid weights and the resulting obstruction

For odd N with Q/2<N<Q, put

    j_k = round(Q k/N),
    theta_k = Q k/N-j_k,  0<=k<N.

The selected j_k are distinct. The theta_k are also distinct: equality would imply Q(k-l)=N(j_k-j_l), and gcd(N,Q)=1 forces k=l. There are no nearest-integer ties because N is odd. In cycle coordinates, select t_k=a^(-1) j_k mod Q and assign its output weight theta_k. The degree-one Chebyshev multiplier differs only by a nonzero scalar, so it has the same rank conclusion.

Let W be any diagonal extension of these selected weights to all Q positions. Applying the lemma with E=E_1 gives

    rank(S[W,L]E_1) >= max(0,K-1-(Q-N)).

At N=Q-1 this is K-2. Hence an O(1)-rank correction cannot implement this weight-moving shortcut as K grows. The result is nonvacuous whenever Q-N<K-1; it does not establish a useful rank lower bound throughout the whole interval Q/2<N<Q.

The parameter restrictions admit the intended logarithmic-block regime on an infinite family: choose Q=2^(2^s), M=2^s, K=Q/M, and N=Q-1 for sufficiently large s. Then M divides K, M=log_2 Q, and the necessary repair rank grows as Q/log Q.

For unweighted forcing D the same proof gives rank at least K-(Q-N), and for an exact first-moment channel D U_K diag(b) it gives K-1-(Q-N) in characteristic zero or characteristic greater than K. These are useful algebraic controls, not an additional obstruction to ordinary complete phase-bank averaging, which uses constant bank coefficients.

## 5. Relationship to roadmap directions 3 and 4

Direction 3 already has a fully exact complementary-bank identity and an ordinary Cooley-Tukey fusion costing O(N log N). The present result does not replace or strengthen that global complexity statement. Complete-bank cancellation can remove terms that are individually nonzero; this theorem must not be summed over bank rows to infer a new global lower bound.

Direction 4 asks whether correlated channel generation and output decoding can collapse together. This proof closes one concrete proposal: commuting the first nonconstant output weight inside a single fixed scan, then repairing it with only a constant number of linear summaries. It resolves a near-dyadic correlated-input slice explicitly left open by the earlier unrestricted-forcing commutator proof.

A candidate outside this shortcut still needs a full operator identity and a charged evaluator. The existing O(Q(R/B+B) log^2(M+R)) blocked polynomial-prefix construction is not contradicted: it does not implement the commutator as a bounded-rank repair. Nor is a hypothetical simultaneous cancellation across Bessel orders excluded.

## 6. Charged work, coefficients, precision, and outputs

This is a negative result in an exact scalar model, not a new fast algorithm. It does not remove any of the established costs:

- The current short-channel bank costs O(R K log K); a recurrence using an uncharged H_K application does not replace it.
- Converting K degree-<R Chebyshev polynomials to monomials by the elementary method costs O(KR^2). Merely naming the combined forcing polynomial does not supply its coefficients for free.
- The inherited blocked scan charges its product/remainder trees, node preparation and all output accumulation. The all-output task must write N outputs, costing at least N scalar writes.
- Roots, phase tables, normalization and Bessel constants require the setup or numerical-generation contracts of the chosen model. Exact Bessel values in the theorem are not a finite executable coefficient oracle.
- A prescribed dense two-matrix rank-r repair performs Theta(Kr+Nr) scalar multiply-adds and stores Theta((K+N)r) coefficients, plus their construction. Substituting r>=K-2 rules out a cheap repair by that specific dense schedule in this regime. A structured high-rank repair may be cheap; its work must be proved separately.
- Exact rank does not give a lower bound at nonzero error tolerance. This proof supplies neither a smallest-singular-value bound nor a sufficient working precision. The inherited forward Bessel instability and the lack of a floating-point certificate for blocked conversion/evaluation remain unchanged.
- Finite-field operations have bit costs depending on the represented modulus. The fixed test field below supports only bounded dyadic lengths and is not a uniform field/root-generation theorem.

## 7. Independent checks and stopping point

`check_correlated_scan.py` is newly authored and imports no uploaded code. It constructs the actual equal-block forcing independently over F_998244353, verifies boundary-cycle products and injectivity, checks the selected commutator determinant exactly, and checks the composition-rank lower bounds for unweighted and first-moment channels. It tests zero, random, and deliberately repeated extensions at unselected positions. All 198 cases passed: 864 boundary-cycle checks, 198 selected commutator determinants, and 396 correlated rank inequalities. Results and actual run output are in `CHECK_RESULTS.json` and `CHECK_RUN.log`.

These finite-field checks corroborate the algebraic lemma and indexing. They do not evaluate exact Bessel constants or establish complex numerical conditioning; Bessel nonvanishing follows from the series proof above. The test implementation uses dense matrices and elimination, not a proposed fast evaluator. All nonconstant-time verifier work is ordinary charged test work, outside any algorithmic speed claim.

The bounded task stops at this theorem and its independently executed controls. It does not launch a search for a new architecture or promote this restricted obstruction to a general Fourier lower bound.
