## Correct interpretation

The all-N Fourier–Weyl twirl identity and operator-norm/seed/cut lemmas are **proven mathematical statements built from classical tools**, not a universal O(N) FFT. The randomized norm estimator requires an O(N)-applicable candidate to deliver O(N) tests. It can *falsify* a bad candidate efficiently and form an objective for searching parameters, but cannot create a correct factorization.

**Novelty is unverified externally**: Heisenberg–Weyl twirls, quasiseparable matrices, Fourier full-spark, block inverse nullity, and randomized norm estimators have prior art. Their present combination appears absent from the specific inspected project reports.
