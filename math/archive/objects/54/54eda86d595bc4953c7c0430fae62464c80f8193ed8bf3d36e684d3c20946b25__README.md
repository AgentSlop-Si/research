## How to continue

1. Read the master checkpoint supplied with this catalog, then RECOVERED_RESEARCH.md.
2. Find the relevant branch in SOURCE_INDEX.md. Consult provenance before treating two documents as independent evidence.
3. Keep exact arbitrary-complex, real-input, Boolean-only, structured-input, approximate, dynamic-reuse and bit-complexity claims separate.
4. Treat historical source papers as claims with their stated assumptions. New independent audits apply only to the theorem or finite certificates they explicitly identify.
5. Preserve the catalog when adding a new checkpoint. Add a new source/version and its hash rather than silently replacing historical claims.

## Limits

This catalog covers selected relevant files visible through Library, not a verified export of every ChatGPT conversation. Recursive Library listing did not inspect Google Drive. Generic images not returned by research searches were not exhaustively classified. Historical Library versions and deleted/unavailable files were not enumerated. Full proof verification of every source, execution of every research program and external novelty/priority verification were not performed.

Canonical texts are byte-preserved copies. Their old relative links, archive paths, commands and historical dates may refer to the original packages. Use canonical_sources.json to locate the exact original member. The portable source set intentionally omits executable programs and duplicate archive binaries; the detailed catalog retains their Library identities and hashes.
