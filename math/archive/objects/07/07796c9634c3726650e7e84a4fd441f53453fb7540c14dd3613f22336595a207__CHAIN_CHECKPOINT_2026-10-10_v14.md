# Chain checkpoint — rough-length unequal-block Green v14

**Canonical bundle:** `fft_rough_length_green_v14.zip`

## New positive result

For every integer `N`, partition the input into `B>=3` balanced contiguous blocks. The unit-step binomial-chirp projector has exact expectation `U_N x`.

The projected spectrum obeys one full output cycle. Its forcing is the difference of two `B`-exponential sequences sharing the same nodes. A blocked power-series recurrence evaluates both in

`O(N + (N/B) M(B) + B^2)`

arithmetic, hence `O(N log B+B^2)` with FFT polynomial multiplication.

No divisor `B|N` or block length `M|N` is required.

## New covariance certificate

For block lengths `ell_b`, define

`w_d = 1/phi(N/gcd(N,d))`.

Then every output variance is at most

`max_(b,t) [sum_(d=1)^t w_d + sum_(d=1)^(ell_b-1-t) w_d] * ||x||2^2`.

This overlap-aware bound is always no worse than the old two-sided triangle sum.

For prime `N` and balanced `B` blocks:

`V = (ceil(N/B)-1)/(N-1) = 1/B+O(1/N)`.

For the v13 least-prime-factor equal-block route:

`V = (p-1)/phi(N)`,

halving the previous `2(p-1)/phi(N)` certificate.

## Prime/rough tradeoff

One explicit full-output row at prime length has

`work O(N log B+B^2)` and per-output RMS `O(B^-1/2)||x||2`.

Examples:

- `B=polylog N`: `O(N log log N)` work and polylogarithmically shrinking per-output RMS.
- `B=exp((log N)^delta)`, `0<delta<1`: `O(N(log N)^delta)` work and RMS `exp(-0.5(log N)^delta)||x||2`.

This is a per-output / average / bulk guarantee, not a simultaneous all-output shrinking-error theorem.

## Scoped negative gates

1. Chebyshev + median + union bound needs `R log B=Omega(log N)`, restoring `Omega(N log N)` charged work.
2. A weighted sum of `R` rank-one block projectors cannot equal the identity on a block longer than `R`.
3. The high-order recurrence is unstable in binary64 for large `B`; exact-field checks validate algebra only.
