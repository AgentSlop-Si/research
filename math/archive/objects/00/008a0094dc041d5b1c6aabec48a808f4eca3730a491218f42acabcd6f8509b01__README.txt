How to read status and rank

A byte hash establishes which exact source was preserved. A source title, saved test log or matching hash does not prove a theorem. “Accepted” means the stated internally reviewed mathematical scope, with the qualifications printed in its row. New source-only claims remain identified as such. Historical test and benchmark reports remain historical evidence. No supplied research program was executed, imported, compiled or AST-parsed in this review.

Novelty and importance are separate. A ranking is an editorial comparison of the supported mathematical increment, significance, generality and evidence. It is not a certified worldwide-priority claim, external journal acceptance, numerical confidence score or claim of a practical FFT speed record. In particular, finite-word approximation, structured-input exact algorithms and restricted representation obstructions have different contracts and must not be promoted to a general exact arbitrary-input Fourier breakthrough.
