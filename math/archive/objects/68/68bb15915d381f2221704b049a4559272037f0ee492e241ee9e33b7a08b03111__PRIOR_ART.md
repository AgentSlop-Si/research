# Research provenance

- Our Phase 20 two-column Fourier curvature gate and both Weyl identities are the prior checkpoint. `previous_full_phase20_checkpoint.zip` contains that and all Phase 12–19 work.
- **Finite-size sharpness certificate:** this phase combines a dual Vandermonde/Prony annihilator with modular resultant tests to establish exact `N/2`-pole existence for every checked power-of-two `N=4,...,8192`. This is project-specific and not a uniform all-size theorem.
- **Classical ingredients**: quadratic chirp transform/Bluestein convolution, first-order cyclic recurrence/Green inverse, Hankel rank of sums of exponentials (Prony), Vandermonde Fourier minors, and rank subadditivity across matrix products. Our restricted circuit theorems combine these known ingredients; no claim of publication priority is made.
- Chirp identity implementation comparison: https://rocm.docs.amd.com/projects/rocFFT/en/docs-5.7.1/design/bluestein.html
- Exponential sums and Prony: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00365-020-09501-6
- Earlier restricted general Green-sum rank obstruction was investigated in Phase 17; this phase treats cyclic **same-order products of sums** with a sharp N/2 term count, and explicitly demonstrates a failure mode of commutator-rank heuristics.
- Existing sub-NlogN exact complex-register DFT claimed by OpenAI: https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/130.md . Our model is distinct, and no improvement is claimed.
