## 3. Local annihilator and Casoratian statements

At an interior interval J of width W, the two exterior blocks of an order-r quasiseparable matrix each have rank at most r. Their common kernel has dimension at least W-2r. The transposed statement gives localized covectors. Neither stage invertibility nor nonzero generators are required.

For any full-row-rank s-by-w coefficient matrix with w<=N, the determinant of evaluations at z,omega*z,...,omega^(s-1)*z has the form

D(z)=z^(s(s-1)/2) Q(z),  deg Q<=s(w-s),  Q!=0.

The degree bound follows by Cauchy–Binet, using distinct selected exponents. Constant row elimination chooses independent polynomials of distinct leading degrees <N. Their top determinant coefficient is a nonzero Vandermonde on the distinct powers omega^d. This proves nonvanishing without a generic-coefficient assumption. In fact the argument holds over any field with a primitive Nth root, not just characteristic zero.

The uploaded s=3,w=5 specialization has at most six nonzero roots. For a fixed five-row output window and length-three sliding input windows, at most 15 windows meet its inverse-permutation footprint. At least N-23 of the N-2 candidates therefore have both a zero projected candidate output and a nonzero projected Fourier output. Its N>=24 theorem is correct.

The arithmetic is a witness construction, not a global FFT operation lower bound. The natural/affine endpoint hypothesis is essential; invertible endpoint diagonal gauges can be absorbed into the stages, preserving quasiseparable cut ranks. Arbitrary non-affine endpoint permutations cannot be silently absorbed while retaining the needed consecutive Fourier exponents.

The boundary improvement in the companion note fixes the output window at the prefix, where only one exterior cut exists. For orders r1,r2, it takes s=2r1+1 and w=s+r2 and obtains the threshold N>=s(s+2r2+1). At rank one it uses the first four outputs, at most 12 footprint hits, and at most three determinant exceptions, leaving N-17 witnesses. This applies at N=18 itself; parity and dyadic length are not needed.

## 4. Three-stage gate and random-layout expectation

For M=S3 P2 S2 P1 S1 with cut orders r1,r2,r3, the uploaded compression proof correctly gives

rank(T[I,J]) <= 2r1+2r3+r2+k(P2^(-1)(I),P1(J))

as a necessary condition for equality T=M. Deleting k exceptional middle rows/columns leaves one strict off-diagonal cut; its rank is at most r2, and restoring coordinates increases rank by at most k. The annihilator projections lose at most the two outside-stage budgets. Small windows whose nominal compression dimension is negative make the bound automatic rather than invalid.

Equal-width natural Fourier blocks are Vandermonde and full rank, so three order-one stages require k>=W-5. Under non-affine endpoint reordering the actual Fourier block rank must replace W. The classical characteristic-zero prime-order full-spark theorem is a different hypothesis; it is not an arbitrary finite-field theorem.

The boundary-aware refinement replaces the two outside losses by b(J)r1+b(I)r3, where b counts nonempty exterior sides. In particular two proper boundary windows give k>=W-3 at rank one. It strengthens individual layout certificates, not a universal three-stage impossibility.

For two independent uniform random permutations, mapped windows are independent uniform W-subsets. Exactly 2*binomial(N,2W) ordered pairs are strictly separated among binomial(N,W)^2 choices. Thus the source probability and expected window-pair count are correct; the W=6 limiting factor is 1/462. Overlapping-window dependence prevents upgrading that expectation to a high-probability claim without further argument. Independent exhaustive small subset counts confirm the formula.

Repeated-bit-reversal survival is evidence that the uploaded test did not reject those layouts, not a Fourier identity. Most importantly, arbitrary non-affine Green sums need not have rank-one quasiseparable structure in the needed common stage coordinates. Therefore this gate cannot simply be applied to Phase 28's three Green mixers. These archives supply complementary filters with distinct hypotheses.

## 5. Phase 28: masks and permutations really are eliminated

For an entrywise-nonzero matrix M, define R(M)[i,j]=sum_k M[i,k]/M[j,k]. Under M'=L P M Q D, with invertible diagonals L,D and row/column permutations P,Q,

R(M')=L P R(M) P^(-1) L^(-1).

Column scalings and permutations cancel in each ratio sum. Consequently tr(R(M)^2), the characteristic polynomial, and other similarity invariants are necessary invariants of two-sided monomial equivalence. The row-pair products of power-s ratio sums and their column analogues are also invariant multisets. The source's Q_s means the trace-2 invariant of the entrywise s-th power, not tr(R(M)^(2s)).

For fixed invertible A,C and T=A^(-1) F C^(-1), equality

F=A D1 P1 B P2 D2 C

is equivalent to T=D1 P1 B P2 D2. A trace or pair-multiset mismatch vetoes every choice of the two masks and permutations for those fixed mixers. Distinct row and column vertex fingerprints determine the only possible matching; reconstructing scales from one row/column and checking every entry then gives a valid equality certificate. Collision cases remain inconclusive.

The generic obstruction is also correct in the stated freely varying, irreducible parameter space. At small positive common resolvent coupling with identity gauges and positive term weights, A,C tend to scalar multiples of I, so Q(T) tends to N^3. Strict positivity of B gives each paired ratio product at least N^2 by Cauchy–Schwarz and therefore Q(B)>=N^4. Their rational difference is not identically zero. Clearing denominators gives a proper algebraic exceptional set.

This theorem fixes cycle orders while allowing the couplings/gauges needed for the positive specialization. It does not automatically assert the same conclusion on every separately restricted or reducible parameter subfamily, or for arbitrary fixed gauges without a nonzero specialization in that subfamily. Exceptional designed parameters remain open. A nonzero sampled specialization proves nonidentity for its own freely variable algebraic family, not all other families.

## 6. All-coupling certificates and characteristic-zero lifting

For the affine entry family B(lambda)=B0+lambda B1, let D be the product of all N^2 entries. Every term in Q_1 uses two distinct entry denominators. Consequently

P1=D*(Q_1(B)-Q_1(T)), degree <=N^2,
P2=D^2*(Q_2(B)-Q_2(T)), degree <=2N^2.

If P1 is not identically zero, at most N^2 admissible complex couplings survive that necessary condition. This assumes fixed A,C,B0,B1 and nowhere-zero admissible B. Identically-zero or zero-entry branches are not settled by that scalar count alone.

The uploaded N=4,8,16 all-coupling claims are stronger and survive independent verification. We reconstructed the published integer order lists and gauge exponents from the declared seed, built each cyclic resolvent by inverting I-alpha W rather than importing or using the source generator, formed T, and checked the following nonzero trace differences modulo 998244353:

N=4: 225503305; N=8: 195368950; N=16: 34834830;
N=32: 781436501; N=64: 19851992; N=128: 397098273.

For N=4 and 8 the two saved polynomial degrees are respectively (16,32) and (64,128). For N=16 the reduced degrees are (255,510). Every saved leading coefficient is nonzero, and every saved Bezout identity U*P1+V*P2=1 holds exactly in the finite field.

Crucially, these are not merely verified identities for arbitrary uploaded coefficient arrays. We independently identified them with the actual invariant polynomials: for each power s, at s*N^2+1 distinct admissible sample points, the supplied polynomial, multiplied back by its declared removed factor, equals D^s times the directly calculated moment difference. Both sides have the proved degree bound. The 1,014 exact evaluations therefore determine the entire reduced polynomials over this field. All sample denominators are nonzero.

At N=16 the three indicated diagonal phase exponents are (7,4,4) in B0 and (3,0,0) in B1. Their relative exponent is exactly 4 modulo 16. Since a cycle Green diagonal is independent of its order, all three affine diagonal entries share the same root over Q(zeta_16), not just coincidentally modulo p. D contains the shared affine factor E cubed. Each term has at most two denominator entries to power s; hence P1 is divisible by E and P2 by E^2 in characteristic zero. The declared modular E also matches the independently reconstructed entries.

The field reduction is legitimate: the prime is independently checked, the selected omega has order N, rational Green denominators survive reduction, A,C have independently verified inverses modulo p, and all entries of T are nonzero modulo p. Thus their exact characteristic-zero counterparts are defined and nonzero in the localized cyclotomic ring. The nonzero reduced leading coefficients preserve the exact degrees. Coprimality modulo p then makes the exact resultant nonzero; hence no common complex root exists for the reduced polynomials. Any root of removed E introduces zero entries in B, incompatible with the nowhere-zero T under invertible monomial transformations.

Thus no complex lambda, and no choices of the two invertible diagonal masks or permutations, can make these specific N=4,8,16 networks equal F. This includes no assertion about retuned outer mixers, different cycle orders, other gauges, multiple freely tunable parameters, or untested lengths. N=32,64,128 have fixed-coupling vetoes only, not all-coupling certificates.

The explicit orders/gauge exponents for all six tested triples are saved in INDEPENDENT_RESULTS.json to make their definition reviewable independently of an unspecified future PRNG implementation.

## 7. Implementation qualifications and exact counterexamples

The generated certificate cases use canonical modular residues and actual Hamiltonian cycles and are unaffected by these interface caveats:

1. The source equivalence_test counts raw x==0 before reducing modulo p. It can falsely return excluded on two identical finite-field matrices represented differently. For example target=[[p,1],[1,1]] and middle=[[0,1],[1,1]] represent the same matrix, but raw zero counts are 0 and 1. Normalize entries first or explicitly require canonical residues. This exact interface counterexample is in EXTRA_RESULTS.json; it does not invalidate the algebraic theorem.

2. The source green_matrix still assumes its supplied successor is one Hamiltonian cycle without checking. The inherited Phase 27 shorter-cycle counterexample applies: for the identity permutation its assignment loop produces alpha^(N-1)/(1-alpha^N) on the diagonal rather than 1/(1-alpha). Phase 28's seeded construction really makes a single cycle, so its stated certificates remain valid. Either validate the API or use cyclewise denominators for a general permutation.

3. GreenTermExecutor validates an order by sorting, costing O(N log N) comparisons in that setup path. seeded_executor forms roots with separate powers, costing O(N log N) scalar multiplications with ordinary exponentiation. The forward apply is linear after preparation; the shipped setup is not a demonstrated fully charged linear preparation. A bitmap permutation check and recurrence-generated root table can repair these particular costs, but no uploaded source was modified and root/field setup still needs its own contract.

4. The fixed prime only supports a bounded collection of transform lengths. It is a certificate field, not a growing-N asymptotic implementation model. Characteristic-zero proofs use specified algebraic coefficients separately from the modular implementation.

## 8. Charged operation ledger

The rank-one separated-generator executor has a 5N-multiplication, 5N-1-addition straight-line schedule if the initial suffix sum is formed in N-1 additions. The literal Python sum starts with zero and may perform an additional trivial addition. This is an implementation upper count, not a lower bound for every scan or every algorithm. Three such stages admit 15N multiplications and 15N-3 additions in that schedule before masks, coefficient construction, data motion and outputs. Zero coefficients and specialization can reduce the count.

The cyclic Green recurrence is valid: one pass forms the cyclic boundary numerator, one scalar denominator multiplication solves its boundary condition, and a second pass writes the solution. Independent sparse-system controls confirm it. In the source's literal generic term code, counting each displayed multiplication, a term uses 5N+1 multiplications and 2N additions, including separate coefficient and left-gauge multiplications. The two-term mixer uses 10N+2 multiplications and 6N additions with its zero-initialized accumulation. Three mixers plus two masks therefore have one direct 32N+6 multiplication / 18N addition schedule after supplied setup, before permutation and output bookkeeping. This is a circuit application count for known coefficients, not a Fourier implementation or an optimality claim. Combining coefficient/gauge products or eliminating trivial operations changes constants.

Source synthesis materializes dense matrices and inverts outer mixers in O(N^3) ordinary field operations. Fixed-power pair fingerprints use O(N^3) arithmetic plus their sorting/lookup work. The naive symbolic coupling-polynomial construction can use O(N^6) operations for a fixed moment power; evaluation/interpolation uses O(N^5) with the displayed dense moment computation. Polynomial degree and small certificate files do not make parameter synthesis linear. Our polynomial identification is also offline verification, with that work explicitly outside the claimed per-vector stage application.

No cheap inverse of a Green sum is supplied. Forward/transpose application does not grant one. To become an exact FFT construction, a candidate still needs a uniform Fourier identity, known masks/orders/coefficient construction, a fully charged fresh-input evaluator, memory/output work, exception handling, and the appropriate field/bit/precision contract.
