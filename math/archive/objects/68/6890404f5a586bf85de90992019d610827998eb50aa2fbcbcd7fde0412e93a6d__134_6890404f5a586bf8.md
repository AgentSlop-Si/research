# Next research gate — original fresh exact full FFT

**Current verified result:** any smooth complex N-output evaluator passing entirely through a width-w intermediate state requires w>=N; arbitrary nonlinear compression of binary inputs into w complex scalars of bounded size M with L-Lipschitz all-output reconstruction satisfies 2^N <= (ceil(4ML)+1)^(2w). A complete NTT can be disguised as one enormous integer multiplication, but the packed operands use Omega(N log N) bits. None of this proves a universal N log N arithmetic lower bound or constructs O(N) Fourier evaluation.

## Strict evaluation target

Arbitrary fresh complex N-vector, all N exact scalar outputs, one processor, no related-input oracle, uniform N-family, full arithmetic and coefficient preparation, ordinary scalar registers and memory access charged. Compare with existing exact-complex result #130 in its actual model, or explicitly declare an alternate model.

## What would count as a *new mechanism*

A concrete **wide, global, input-independent linear evaluator** that uses fewer than N log N charged elementary operations across arbitrary fresh inputs and doesn't merely rename an expensive convolution, bit-packed integer multiplication, automorphism expansion or matrix inverse as a constant-cost macro. Provide the full algorithmic recurrence *before* writing a large engine. A truly O(N) candidate must also explain how it bypasses lower bounds for bounded-coefficient and well-conditioned two-coordinate circuits without hidden coefficient/condition growth.

A particularly useful intermediate goal is a nontrivial, mechanically checkable exact full-output work saving for a scalable family of wide-stage operators with a charged compiler, **not** just a constant factor at N=8,16 or a constructed symbolic descriptor. Do not claim one exists until it is found.
