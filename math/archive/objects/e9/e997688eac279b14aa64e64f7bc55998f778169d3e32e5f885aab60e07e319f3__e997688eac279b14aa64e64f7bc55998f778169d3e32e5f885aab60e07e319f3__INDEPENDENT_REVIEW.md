## Verdict

**Accepted:** in the specified grid layout, `Q F_(4N)-gamma F_N Q` has full row rank N for every scalar gamma, including the normalization-balanced value two. The explicit minor proof is stronger than the earlier norm-gap proof and requires different reasoning.

Consequently scalar-multiple layer copying through a proper active bank is excluded, even after scalar normalization of the Fourier targets. Genuine four-fiber coefficient mixing, changed layouts, and more general architectures remain outside this exclusion.

**Accepted separately:** the hierarchical route construction has exactly four times the previous edit count at each lift, not just the earlier upper bound with an additive constant. The resulting rational cut-capacity margins improve to `768*4^ell` and `15360*4^ell` for the two certified seed parities.

## 1. Exact layout, field, and normalization assumptions

Let `b=2^r`, `a=b` or `a=2b`, and `N=ab`, including b=1. Label a small vertex `(x,y)` by `t=bx+y`, and a large vertex `(X,Y)` by `j=2bX+Y`. Define

`pi(X,Y)=(X mod a,Y mod b)`, `Q e_j=e_pi(j)`.

Q is the unnormalized four-fiber sum. Work over any field containing a primitive 4N-th root omega, with characteristic not dividing 4N. Define the compatible small root `zeta=omega^4`. Use `F_(4N)[i,j]=omega^(ij)` and `F_N[i,j]=zeta^(ij)`. Inverses of N and b therefore exist, and both Fourier matrices are invertible.

The same proof applies to the negative Fourier convention by replacing omega with its inverse at both sizes. It does not permit unrelated root choices, an arbitrary four-to-one Q, independent relabelings that alter this layout, or a different bank placement without rechecking the argument.

## 2. An explicit representative column per fiber

For `t=bx+y`, select

`j(t)=2bx+y` if y>0, and `j(t)=2bx+b` if y=0.

These N large columns are distinct and project back to t. In this order, the selected columns J satisfy

`(F_N Q)[:,J]=F_N`.

Let `A=(QF_(4N))[:,J]` and `K=F_N^-1 A`. The selected defect minor factors as

`(QF_(4N)-gamma F_NQ)[:,J]=F_N(K-gamma I)`.

## 3. Aliasing and nilpotence

For a small row `i=bx+y`, its four large lifts have indices `2bx+y`, `2bx+y+b`, and those two indices plus 2N. Hence

`(QF_(4N))[i,j]=omega^((2bx+y)j)(1+omega^(bj))(1+(-1)^j)`.

In particular every odd j column vanishes. For even j=j(t), multiplication by the inverse small Fourier matrix yields

`K[s,t]=(2/b) 1_(s congruent j/2 mod a) (1+omega^(bj)) sum_(y=0)^(b-1) omega^((j-4s)y)`.

Indeed the x-sum is `sum_x omega^(2bx(j-2s))`; for even j it is a sum of powers of an a-th root, equaling a precisely when `s=j/2 mod a`, otherwise zero. There is no division by the final geometric sum and no assumption it is nonzero.

Define the capped within-block valuation

`nu(t)=v2(t mod b)` if t mod b is nonzero, and `nu(t)=r` otherwise.

If nu(t)=0, the selected j is odd and column t of K is zero. If nu(t)=h>=1, the selected j has exactly h factors of two: for positive y this follows from `j=2bx+y`, and for y=0 from `j=b(2x+1)`. Any nonzero K[s,t] requires `s=j/2 mod a`. Since b divides a, this forces

`nu(s)=nu(t)-1`.

Thus K strictly lowers a grading with levels zero through r, so `K^(r+1)=0`. Equivalently it is strictly upper triangular after ordering by increasing nu. Therefore

`det(K-gamma I)=(-gamma)^N`.

For every nonzero gamma, the selected determinant is exactly

`det((QF_(4N)-gamma F_NQ)[:,J])=det(F_N)(-gamma)^N != 0`.

At gamma=0, QF_(4N) still has row rank N because Q has that rank and F_(4N) is invertible. The b=1 case is included: every selected j is odd, so K=0 directly. This proves the all-scalar theorem without complex singular-value arguments.

## 4. Scalar-normalized copying and the precise bank restriction

Use the fixed product layout `C=H3 H2 B H1`. The small bank has unnormalized F_b on active blocks J and identity elsewhere; the large bank has F_(2b) on the pulled-back active set `J union (J+a)`. The previously verified bank identity gives

`rank(QB_large-B_small Q)=b|J|`.

Assume each layer obeys `Q H_l,large=gamma_l H_l,small Q`, with nonzero gamma_l. Put `Gamma=gamma_1 gamma_2 gamma_3`. Direct expansion then gives

`Q C_large-Gamma C_small Q`

`=gamma_3 gamma_2 H3,small H2,small (QB_large-B_small Q) H1,large`.

If `C_large=alpha_large F_(4N)` and `C_small=alpha_small F_N`, where both alpha values are nonzero, the left side is

`alpha_large [QF_(4N)-(Gamma alpha_small/alpha_large)F_NQ]`,

which has rank N by Section 3. All surrounding H factors are invertible because they are factors in invertible full products. The right side has rank b|J|. For a proper active set, `b|J|<N`, contradiction.

This explicitly includes unit-normalized Fourier targets. The earlier norm-gap proof could not handle the balancing of row norms, but the new nilpotent-minor proof does. The two conclusions are consistent: the earlier caveat identified a limit of that proof, not an existence result.

The stated exact rank formula uses the specified unnormalized active banks and identical inactive identities. More generally, the argument only needs `rank(QB_large-B_small Q)<N`; a changed bank normalization or layout must establish its own discrepancy bound before using the conclusion. Additional banks or genuine fiber-mixing layers are not automatically covered.

## 5. Quantitative descent-defect budget and why it is not a cost bound

For candidate layers put `D_l=QH_l,large-gamma_l H_l,small Q`. The complete product defect expands into

`D3 H2,large B_large H1,large`

`+gamma_3 H3,small D2 B_large H1,large`

`+gamma_3 gamma_2 H3,small H2,small (QB_large-B_small Q) H1,large`

`+gamma_3 gamma_2 H3,small H2,small B_small D1`.

If both products have the stated Fourier targets, rank subadditivity and the full-row-rank theorem force

`rank(D1)+rank(D2)+rank(D3) >= N-b|J|`.

For the one-sided endpoint model with exactly descended routes and matching valid poles,

`D_l=sum_j E_(l,j) G_(j,large)`,

`E_(l,j)=Q diag(a_(l,j,large))-gamma_l diag(a_(l,j,small))Q`.

Each E column has at most one nonzero coordinate. Different small fibers occupy disjoint columns, so rank E equals the number of small fibers on which some coefficient differs from its scalar-pulled-back value. The total number of affected fibers, counted separately across all 24 endpoint-mask occurrences, must therefore be at least N-b|J|. This counts occurrences, not merely the union of the affected base coordinates.

That rank requirement can be satisfied cheaply without solving the Fourier equations. For example, take one nonzero small mask a and use the large mask `(-1)^epsilon_y a(pi(v))`. On each fiber, its defect entries are multiples of `1-gamma` and `-1-gamma`. They cannot both vanish in characteristic different from two. This single mask defect has full row rank N for every gamma, while construction and application require O(N) operations and writes. It is a rank-compatible example only, not a Fourier-compatible layer or lift.

## 6. Separate exact-edit invariant for the hierarchical routes

At either previously certified large seed, each minimum-representative splice changes tail zero: zero represents its original cover cycle, there is more than one cycle, and rotating to another cycle's head changes its successor.

Suppose `P_small(0)!=T_small(0)`. The hierarchical preliminary lift `P0=eta_hat T_large`, where `eta=P_small T_small^-1`, has changed-tail set exactly `pi^-1({v:P_small(v)!=T_small(v)})`. It therefore has exactly four times the previous edit count.

The final Hamiltonian merge changes only tails projecting to zero. Every such tail already differs from T_large in its projected successor, because its preliminary projection is P_small(0), whereas the MGG cover projects to T_small(0). The merge preserves the former projection. Consequently none of those tails can become unchanged, and no previously unchanged tail is touched. Thus

`h_large=4h_small`,

and zero remains changed at the new level. This proves the invariant inductively for every label, not just as an observed equality in sample runs.

Across the eight labels, `H_ell=4^ell H_0`, so the prior half-edit loss bound can be taken as `D_ell=4^ell D_0`. Retaining the established MGG cut premise and its rational bound, the certified three-bank margins are strictly greater than

`768*4^ell` from `N_0=2^17,D_0=7936`,

`15360*4^ell` from `N_0=2^18,D_0=11264`.

The original and weaker additive-constant recurrence remains valid; this addendum sharpens it without changing either seed. The route enumeration cost remains O(N) word operations over the geometric sequence of levels, with O(N) words of storage. It does not furnish coefficients, an N16-to-large-seed bridge, or a Fourier identity.
