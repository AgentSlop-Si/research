# Prior art and scope — Phase 32

The central determinant argument uses standard polynomial algebra: cyclic-shift geometric series, nonzero generalized Vandermonde polynomials, Cramer's rule, and elementary Zariski-open conditions. Schwartz–Zippel supplies quantitative random specialization and Kronecker substitution supplies one-parameter explicit exponent separation. None of these concepts is claimed as newly discovered mathematics.

References:
- Schwartz–Zippel lemma (Caltech lecture slides): https://users.cms.caltech.edu/~umans/cs151/lec7.pdf
- Kronecker substitution (Berkeley thesis chapter): https://escholarship.org/content/qt92n4z7j2/qt92n4z7j2.pdf
- Multiplicative cycle invariants for diagonal equivalence, Saunders & Schneider (1978): https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0012365X78902005

Our project-specific contribution is the precise arrangement of arbitrary independently ordered cyclic Green solves with **row-dependent diagonal gauges** to interpolate multiple entire Fourier columns in online linear field work for fixed q, paired with a matching generic augmented-determinant limitation and runnable finite-field controls.

The existence result does not imply a stable complex FFT implementation, a useful large-integer multiplication algorithm, or a new asymptotic FFT upper bound. Exceptional two-sided phase-coherent parameter choices remain an open research problem.
