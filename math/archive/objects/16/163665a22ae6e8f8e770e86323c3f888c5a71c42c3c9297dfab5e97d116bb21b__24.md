## Mathematics and flow

Evaluates three head/tail/hull inequalities for every output interval while updating its permuted label extrema. It encodes the conditional Fourier Vandermonde screen, its coarse marker ceiling, exhaustive N8 enumeration and Monte Carlo larger screens.

## Guards and contracts

Permutation/window checks are asserts; rank budgets are assumed nonnegative integer bounds and are not validated. Empty permutations pass check_windows vacuously, outside the Fourier model. Natural/affine-coprime input order is essential; arbitrary output rows are permitted. The N16 stored permutation is a filter witness only.

## Costs

check_windows is O(N^2) plus initial O(N log N) sorting and can materialize O(N^2) violations. The older profile is O(N^2); exact N8 enumeration is factorial. combinatorial_ceiling is an O(1) necessary bound, not a construction.
