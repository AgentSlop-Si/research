# A certified N16 Fourier solution in the full endpoint family

## Result and scope

The exact inequalities in `EXACT_CONTRACTION_CERTIFICATE.json` prove that the fixed N16 model below has a complex algebraic solution with all 384 endpoint entries nonzero. The solution is uniquely specified in the supplied 256-dimensional affine chart by a rational isolating polydisk. This is a finite seed existence result. It supplies no size-lifting rule, faster transform, uniform coefficient generator, or connection to the separately chosen large expanding hierarchy bases.

The numerical discovery and the exact acceptance certificate are separate. `NUMERICAL_SEARCH_RESULTS.json` records the former. `DYADIC_AFFINE_CHART.json` is a proposed exact input to the latter. Acceptance recomputes all required bounds with Gaussian integers and rational fractions; no floating residual is used to accept a solution.

## 1. Fixed model and complete parameter ledger

Take N=16 and the eight deterministic least-representative-spliced MGG orders, in order H0-, H0+, H1-, H1+, V0-, V0+, V1-, V1+. Their explicit successor arrays are regenerated by `cycles(16)` in the included, independently authored `check_n16_outer_gate.py` helper.

For j=0,...,7 let tau_j=1/(j+2) and G_j=(I-tau_j P_j)^(-1). Let

    B = diag(F4,F4,I4,I4),
    H_l(a) = sum_j diag(a[l,:,j]) G_j,  l=0,1,2,
    C(a) = H_2(a) H_1(a) B H_0(a).

The target is the unnormalized positive-sign Fourier matrix

    F16[i,k] = z^(i*k),
    z = (sqrt(2+sqrt(2)) + i sqrt(2-sqrt(2)))/2.

All 24 Green terms and all 384 complex physical endpoint entries are charged. Right endpoints are identities; poles, orders, bank, and active blocks are fixed. There are exactly 256 complex entry equations C(a)=F16. Every physical endpoint is required to be nonzero. Fixed Green denominators (j+2)^16-1 are all positive, hence nonzero.

## 2. Jacobian and chart selection

The polynomial map a -> C(a) has two evident complex scalar gauge directions: scale H_0 and inversely scale H_1, or scale H_1 and inversely scale H_2. These were checked as exact Jacobian kernel vectors. They are not asserted to exhaust the kernel.

`EXACT_JACOBIAN_DIAGNOSTIC.json` records exact rank-256 Jacobian minors over F998244353 at both the all-ones point and a supplied nonzero integer point. Full row rank implies a 128-dimensional tangent kernel at these points. The prime, Fourier root, and rational denominator specializations are valid. This is a local-dominance diagnostic only; it does not itself imply Fourier target existence. In particular, the separately rejected all-ones inner prefix is consistent with the full map being a submersion there.

Numerical discovery used all 384 parameters in orthonormal bases for the same eight-dimensional row-template spaces. It found a candidate on the first start after 13 Newton steps. The bounded search allowed at most three starts, 60 iterations per start, 12 backtracking trials, and 150 seconds between iterations. No failed chart or unsuccessful start is interpreted as nonexistence.

For exact acceptance, supply the dyadic center a0 and dyadic matrix V of size 384 by 256 in `DYADIC_AFFINE_CHART.json`, both with denominator 2^60. The complete chart is

    a(y) = a0 + V y,  y in C^256.

There are no remaining free parameters. This fixes 128 complementary linear directions, rather than fixing 128 individual physical entries. An injective V defines an affine slice of codimension 128. Injectivity follows from the certified bound ||I-D(C o a)(0)||<1. The chart is proposed numerically but treated as arbitrary exact dyadic data by the certificate.

## 3. Exact arithmetic and target enclosure

Let d_j=j+2 and L=lcm_j(d_j^16-1). If P_j^p sends column k to row i, then

    G_j[i,k] = d_j^(16-p)/(d_j^16-1),  0<=p<16.

Thus every G_j has Gaussian-integer numerator over L. Set D=2^60 L. Each center layer H_l(a0) and each chart layer-direction matrix has Gaussian-integer numerator over D. The bank has Gaussian-integer entries. The full center product and chart Jacobian therefore have common denominator D^3. They are calculated exactly.

To bound the exact target, set Z=2^160 and

    s0 = floor(sqrt(2 Z^2)), s1=s0+1,
    c = floor(sqrt((2Z+s0) Z)),
    d = floor(sqrt((2Z-s1) Z)),
    z0 = (c+i d)/(2Z).

These integers are obtained by exact integer square root. Each positive component of z0 is below the corresponding component of z and differs by less than 2/Z. In particular |z0|<=1. Since |z-z0|<=4/Z, telescoping powers gives, for 0<=e<16,

    |z^e-z0^e|_1 < 120/Z,

where |w|_1=|Re w|+|Im w|. The proof uses |w|_1<=2|w| and |z|=1, |z0|<=1. Exact powers z0^e are rational Gaussian numbers. Comparing all 256 center-product entries against these powers and adding 120/Z gives the certified residual bound. The exact target remains z, not z0.

The displayed radicals select the primitive 16th root with positive real and imaginary components. They satisfy z^8=-1 and z^16=1. The bank F4 uses z^4=i exactly.

## 4. Norm conventions and contraction inequalities

The polydisk uses the ordinary complex sup norm ||y||_infinity=max_t |y_t|. Scalar absolute values are upper-bounded by their real/imaginary l1 norm in every exact calculation. Matrix infinity norms are bounded by sums of these scalar upper bounds.

Write

    f(y) = C(a0+Vy)-F16,
    T(y) = y-f(y).

Let h_l bound the matrix infinity norm of H_l(a0). Let L_l bound the operator from ||y||_infinity to the matrix infinity norm of the linear variation in H_l. Both are exact rational row-sum bounds. Since ||B||_infinity=4, multilinearity gives, throughout ||y||_infinity<=rho,

    ||Df(y)-Df(0)|| <= C2 rho + C3 rho^2,
    C2 = 8 (L_2 L_1 h_0 + L_2 h_1 L_0 + h_2 L_1 L_0),
    C3 = 12 L_2 L_1 L_0.

Each of the three quadratic terms has two derivative positions; the cubic term has three. The matrix infinity bound dominates the maximum absolute output-entry norm. Thus it also bounds the induced operator on the flattened 256 output entries.

Let e bound ||f(0)|| and epsilon bound ||I-Df(0)||. The exact certificate uses rho=10^-9 and verifies

    q = epsilon + C2 rho + C3 rho^2 < 1,
    e + q rho < rho.

Its decimal summaries, provided only for readability, are:

- e < 1.854e-12;
- epsilon < 1.809e-11;
- h_l < (9.391, 6.737, 32.779);
- L_l < (49.781, 42.258, 189.234);
- C2 < 1,659,978 and C3 < 4,776,800;
- q < 0.001660;
- e+q rho < 3.514e-12 < 10^-9.

All comparisons use the exact numerator/denominator values, not the rounded displays. The closed polydisk is complete. T maps it strictly into itself and is a contraction there, so it has a unique fixed point y*. At that point all 256 complex equations f(y*)=0 hold. The derivative is nonsingular there since ||I-Df(y*)||<1. Consequently y* is an isolated algebraic point of this square polynomial system over Q(z). The defining polynomial system, exact affine chart, and isolating polydisk give an exact root representation; no minimal polynomial or decimal candidate is being substituted for the solution.

## 5. Physical nonzero and inverse guards

For each of the 384 physical endpoints a_t(y), the certificate checks the rational lower bound

    max(|Re a0_t|,|Im a0_t|)
       - rho sum_s (|Re V_ts|+|Im V_ts|) > 0.

The smallest certified margin exceeds 0.3145. Therefore every endpoint remains nonzero throughout the polydisk, including at y*. All fixed Green denominators were separately checked. The Fourier matrix and bank are invertible, so the successful product identity also implies all three layers are invertible. There are no pivot-denominator guards hidden by outer elimination, because this certificate checks the full product directly.

## 6. Reproduction and evidentiary limits

Run the exact acceptance path with

    python certify_n16_contraction.py

It reads only the exact chart, regenerates the prescribed matrices, recomputes the rational bounds, and asserts the strict inequalities. NumPy object arrays are used solely to organize integer arithmetic. SciPy and floating arrays are used only by the optional `--propose` chart-discovery path; they do not enter acceptance.

`python search_n16_full.py` repeats the separately bounded numerical discovery and exact modular diagnostic. The complete accepted chart is already preserved, so rerunning a solver is unnecessary for verification. All scripts are freshly authored for this track; no uploaded source is executed.

This is an actual finite coefficient-existence result in the specified 384-parameter family. It does not establish a Fourier seed in the independently spliced threshold-size expanding family, a compatible recursion, or any asymptotic benefit. Independent review of this certificate is required before treating the result as sealed.
