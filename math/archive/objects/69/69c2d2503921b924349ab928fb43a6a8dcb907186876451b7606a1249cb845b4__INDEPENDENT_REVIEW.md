# Independent acceptance of the N16 endpoint certificate

## Accepted result

The supplied dyadic affine chart certifies an exact complex algebraic solution of the full N16 Fourier equations in the fixed 384-endpoint, 24-Green-term model. A fresh integer-and-rational verifier independently regenerates the prescribed operator and matches every rational bound in the constructor's certificate exactly. All 256 complex entry equations and all 384 physical nonzero endpoint guards are included.

The exact solution is uniquely isolated in the supplied 256-dimensional affine chart. It is not claimed to be the unique solution in the unrestricted 384-dimensional parameter space. This is a finite seed existence result, with an exact root representation. It establishes no size-lifting law, uniform coefficient generator, large expanding-bank realization, or asymptotic improvement.

The constructor is credited for the candidate chart and contraction construction. This review neither executes nor imports its discovery or acceptance scripts. The independent verifier uses only Python arbitrary-precision integers, Fraction, and standard-library functions; it uses no NumPy, SciPy, floating-point linear algebra, or numerical solver. Decimal summaries are written after acceptance and do not decide it.

## 1 Fixed model and complete identities

Vertices are (x,y) in Z_4 by Z_4 with label 4x+y. The eight raw permutations are, in order,

    H0-, H0+, H1-, H1+, V0-, V0+, V1-, V1+,
    Hc±(x,y)=(x±(2y+c),y),
    Vc±(x,y)=(x,y±(2x+c)),

with arithmetic modulo four. For each raw cover, enumerate cycles by their least vertex, choose the least vertex from each cycle, and replace each selected tail's destination by the original destination of the next selected tail cyclically. The resulting successor array is a single 16-cycle. The reviewer rebuilds all eight arrays and records them in INDEPENDENT_CERTIFICATE.json. No imported cycle routine supplies them.

For term j=0,...,7, set d_j=j+2, tau_j=1/d_j, and

    G_j=(I-tau_j P_j)^(-1).

Let B=diag(F4,F4,I4,I4), where F4 has unnormalized positive powers of i. With l=0,1,2,

    H_l(a)=sum_j diag(a[l,:,j]) G_j,
    C(a)=H_2(a) H_1(a) B H_0(a).

There are 3*16*8=384 complex physical endpoint entries and exactly 24 Green occurrences. The poles, routes, right endpoints, bank, and two active blocks are fixed. The target is the full unnormalized positive-sign Fourier matrix

    F16[i,k]=z^(ik),
    z=(sqrt(2+sqrt(2))+i sqrt(2-sqrt(2)))/2.

This chosen root obeys z^4=i, z^8=-1, and z^16=1. Thus the bank root agrees with the target. The full target uses all 16 input columns, not selected columns or only a covariance equation. A successful matrix identity consequently holds for every complex input vector.

## 2 Exact chart and arithmetic reconstruction

DYADIC_AFFINE_CHART.json supplies the center a0 and a 384-by-256 complex matrix V, each with Gaussian-integer numerators over 2^60. The parameter order is layer, row, term; the direction index is the flattened Fourier output row,column. The verifier checks every shape and every integer leaf. The exact chart is

    a(y)=a0+Vy, y in C^256.

It fixes an affine complement of dimension 128 rather than 128 individual physical coordinates. That is a legitimate square chart. Injectivity of V is proved below from the derivative bound; it is not assumed from its numerical provenance. The chart is treated as arbitrary exact input data, without trusting how it was found.

For a 16-cycle, if P_j^p sends column k to row i, the Green entry is

    G_j[i,k]=d_j^(16-p)/(d_j^16-1), 0<=p<16.

Set L=lcm_j(d_j^16-1) and D=2^60 L. The verifier builds every Green numerator over L and checks all 2048 entries of the exact inverse identity using integer equations. In successor convention, with inverse permutation p_j^(-1), this is

    d_j Gnum_j[i,k]-Gnum_j[p_j^(-1)(i),k]
      =d_j L delta_(i,k).

The center layers and every chart-direction layer have Gaussian-integer numerators over D; B is Gaussian integral. Consequently C(a0) and all chart derivative entries have denominator D^3. The verifier constructs all 256 derivative columns using the full product rule:

    delta C = delta H_2 H_1 B H_0
              +H_2 delta H_1 B H_0
              +H_2 H_1 B delta H_0.

It checks the resulting 256-by-256 derivative against the identity in the norm required for contraction. No row-template elimination, pivot chart, or partial-entry acceptance is used. The source's separate full-rank Jacobian diagnostic is not needed for this acceptance; local dominance at an unrelated point would not establish target existence.

## 3 Primitive-root enclosure

Let Z=2^160 and form exact integers

    s0=floor(sqrt(2Z^2)), s1=s0+1,
    c=floor(sqrt((2Z+s0)Z)),
    d=floor(sqrt((2Z-s1)Z)),
    z0=(c+i d)/(2Z).

The verifier checks the defining integer square inequalities, c,d>0, 2s1<3Z, and c^2+d^2<4Z^2. The latter gives |z0|<1. Both components of z0 are lower bounds for the corresponding chosen positive components of z.

For completeness, the perturbation in either interior radicand is less than 1/Z. For the imaginary component, 2-s1/Z>1/2, so the sum of the two relevant square roots is greater than one; the real case is larger still. Dividing the interior perturbation by that sum changes the square root by less than 1/Z. The integer-floor error is also less than 1/Z. After the exterior factor 1/2, each component error is less than 1/Z, and in particular less than the conservative 2/Z used by the certificate.

Hence |z-z0|<4/Z in ordinary complex modulus. Telescoping powers, with |z|=1 and |z0|<=1, gives |z^e-z0^e|<4e/Z. Using |w|_1<=2|w| yields the uniform conservative bound

    |z^e-z0^e|_1 <120/Z, 0<=e<16.

The constant also covers e=0 trivially. Powers z0^e are calculated as exact Gaussian rationals. Every target exponent is reduced modulo 16 using the exact primitive root z, and the same 120/Z error is added after taking the maximum over all 256 product entries. The target is z, not the rational approximation z0.

## 4 Norm audit and cubic derivative bound

The polydisk is defined by the ordinary complex sup norm

    ||y||_infinity=max_t |y_t| <= rho, rho=10^(-9).

Every exact scalar magnitude bound uses |Re w|+|Im w|, which dominates |w|. Matrix row sums of these quantities dominate the induced matrix infinity norm. Let h_l bound ||H_l(a0)||_infinity. If K_(l,t) is the layer-direction matrix for coordinate y_t, define

    L_l=max_i sum_k sum_t |K_(l,t)[i,k]|_1.

Then ||delta H_l(y)||_infinity<=L_l||y||_infinity. These are the exact sums independently recomputed by the verifier, including all 256 direction coordinates. The bank infinity norm is four.

Writing f(y)=C(a0+Vy)-F16, its quadratic terms have the three possible pairs of layer variations. Differentiating each yields two terms, with norm bound 2*4=8 times the appropriate product. The cubic term has three derivative positions, with factor 3*4=12. Therefore throughout the polydisk,

    ||Df(y)-Df(0)|| <= C2 rho+C3 rho^2,
    C2=8(L_2 L_1 h_0+L_2 h_1 L_0+h_2 L_1 L_0),
    C3=12 L_2 L_1 L_0.

The matrix infinity product bounds dominate each output entry and therefore also the flattened output sup norm. There is no missing factor of 16 or 256: the input-direction coordinate sums are already included in L_l, and the center derivative defect is separately bounded by its full 256-column row sums.

The exact fractions in INDEPENDENT_CERTIFICATE.json establish the following readable bounds:

- residual e=||f(0)|| upper bound 1.8535301205438098e-12;
- epsilon=||I-Df(0)|| upper bound 1.8086054320979678e-11;
- C2 upper bound 1659977.4406358565;
- C3 upper bound 4776799.672254541;
- q=epsilon+C2 rho+C3 rho^2 upper bound 0.0016599774634987105;
- e+q rho upper bound 3.5135075840425202e-12.

Those decimals summarize exact rationals; the actual strict checks are q<1 and e+q rho<rho.

## 5 Contraction, uniqueness in the chart, and algebraicity

Define T(y)=y-f(y). On the closed polydisk, ||DT(y)||<=q<1. Integration along a line segment in this convex set gives the same Lipschitz constant. Moreover,

    ||T(y)||<=||T(0)||+q||y||<=e+q rho<rho.

The closed polydisk in C^256 is complete, so the contraction theorem gives a unique fixed point y* in it. At that point f(y*)=0, which means all 256 complex Fourier entry equations hold exactly.

Since ||I-Df(0)||<1, Df(0)=DC(a0)V is invertible, proving that V is injective and the affine slice has the stated dimension. Likewise ||I-Df(y*)||<1 makes the square Jacobian nonsingular at the root. Thus y* is an isolated zero of a square polynomial system with coefficients in the algebraic field Q(z). Such an isolated nonsingular zero has algebraic coordinates. The exact polynomial system, dyadic affine chart, selected target root, and rational isolating polydisk are a valid exact representation of the solution; a decimal approximation is not being substituted for it.

The full 384-variable model can have other solutions, including a local family outside the chosen slice. The uniqueness conclusion concerns this chart and isolating region only. The earlier exclusion of the all-ones inner prefix remains intact because the certified full model allows its inner endpoint entries to vary.

## 6 All endpoint and inverse guards

For every physical endpoint t, the verifier computes

    margin_t=max(|Re a0_t|,|Im a0_t|)
             -rho sum_s(|Re V_ts|+|Im V_ts|).

This lower-bounds |a_t(y)| throughout the polydisk. All 384 margins are positive and explicitly recorded. The least is

    72529738861210562925161621 / 230584300921369395200000000
      >0.3145476.

The maximum physical direction row norm is below 33275.415; it has been charged in these guards, rather than treating a chart radius as a physical-coordinate radius.

All eight fixed Green denominators d_j^16-1 are positive. Every tau_j is nonzero. The bank is invertible, as are the exact Fourier matrices involved. At the certified solution, the full product identity then forces each of the three square H_l layers to be invertible. No additional inverse, pivot, or cancellation denominator is assumed.
