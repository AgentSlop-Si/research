# Publication assessment of the Fourier Green and graph results

Private research assessment • 10 October 2026 • Initial substantive pass

## Main conclusion

There is credible publication material here without a faster general FFT. The strongest package is the exact three-term B5 decomposition paired with its all-order two-term impossibility certificate and infinite arithmetic obstruction. The sharp endpoint-permutation theorem is the cleanest standalone extremal result. The universal adjacent Hankel-pencil resultant identity is the strongest clearly non-FFT algebraic candidate.

The three-stage discrepancy bridge, one-layer independently ordered Green obstruction, and exact common-chirp eliminant recognizer are also substantial candidates. The solver, rank gates, tree counts and certification infrastructure contain much classical mathematics; their contribution must be stated as a precise specialization, application or proof artifact rather than a new general theory.

These are judgments of mathematical substance and identifiable delta against the sources inspected, not claims of worldwide priority or journal acceptance. A search miss is not evidence that a theorem has never appeared. Seventeen selected accepted proof/review documents were inspected, plus linked construction proofs. This is not an exhaustive audit of every archived result. “Accepted” below means an existing internal independent review, not external peer review. This pass did not execute uploaded programs or replay prior checkers.

## Precise claims and publication deltas

### 1 Exact additive Green length three and an infinite two-branch obstruction

**Exact claim.** For B5=(F5^{-1}+diag(zeta5^{3j^2}))^{-1}, the minimum number of terms D_L(I-W)^{-1}D_R is exactly three, allowing arbitrary Hamiltonian-cycle orders, zero weights and singular endpoint masks, but requiring legal resolvent denominators. The upper construction is over a quadratic extension of Q(zeta5). Separately, for F_N[i,j]=omega^{ij}, h=(N+1)/2 and B_N=(F_N^{-1}+diag(omega^{h j^2}))^{-1}, the full B_N requires more than two terms for N=5q^2, q positive odd, gcd(q,5)=1 and q=1 mod 241. A positive-Gauss parameter curve also has only finitely many possible exceptional parameters.

**Potential original contribution.** Exact minimum additive length for this specific target, with all 576 ordered cycle pairs and boundary charts excluded, matched by an explicit quadratic-extension construction. Principal-restriction closure and good reduction transfer the obstruction to infinitely many full targets. Existing fast inverse and semiseparable representation theorems do not establish this minimum.

**Strength and limits.** The upper bound uses two nontrivial Green solves plus a diagonal term, because the third branch has all weights zero. Thus “three expensive solves” would be misleading. The full N-family has a lower bound only; no full-size three-term upper bound or FFT speedup follows. The non-effective finite-exception theorem has no cutoff.

**Proof status.** Accepted internal independent reviews E01–E03. Read the actual proof/certificate explanations; did not replay executables in this novelty pass. The B5 lower certificate proves geometric-fiber emptiness, not merely absence of F_241 points, then uses proper specialization.

**Missing work.** Freeze a compact theorem/certificate release; provide human-readable symmetry or invariant explanation of the 576 cases; compare additive cyclic-resolvent/secant literature further; distinguish base-field from extension-field length; finish and independently review the restricted-family upper extension before citing it.

**Possible manuscript proposition.** “An exact additive length for diagonally scaled cyclic-bidiagonal resolvents, with a five-dimensional certificate and arithmetic families of obstructions.”

**Primary comparison.** [Huhtanen and Perämäki (2015), Factoring Matrices into the Product of Circulant and Diagonal Matrices](https://math.aalto.fi/~mhuhtane/manu.pdf); [Boito and Eidelman (2025), Computation of quasiseparable representations of Green matrices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.02701); [Chandrasekaran, Epperly and Govindarajan, Graph-Induced Rank Structures and their Representations](https://arxiv.org/html/1911.05858); [Stacks Project, projective morphisms are proper](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01WC); [Gurevich, Hadani and Howe, Quadratic reciprocity and the sign of the Gauss sum via the finite Weil representation](https://arxiv.org/html/0808.2447).

### 2 Sharp endpoint-permutation quasiseparable order of dyadic Fourier matrices

**Exact claim.** For N=2^m>=8 over characteristic zero, min_{rho,sigma} q(P_rho F_N P_sigma^T)=N/4+1, where q is the maximum rank over all strict upper-right and lower-left complementary cuts. Double bit reversal attains it. Its upper-right cut ranks obey r_N(k)=ceil(k/2)+r_{N/4}(floor(k/2)) for k<=N/2, with reflection symmetry. Exact total-rank minima are also established at N=8,16.

**Potential original contribution.** The all-independent-endpoint minimax value, with a second-moment/odd-cardinality lower bound matching a bit-reversal recurrence. Prior Fourier submatrix theory already contains double-digit-reversal rank-one block grids and sharp support uncertainty, so those ingredients must be credited.

**Strength and limits.** An exact all-size formula is substantially stronger than a finite screen and does not require source code. It is a rank-structure theorem, not an arithmetic lower bound: high cut rank alone does not make matrix application expensive. Characteristic-zero positivity is essential to the supplied lower-bound proof; arbitrary finite fields are outside it.

**Proof status.** Accepted E04. Actual proof read. The closest official 2006 Delvaux–Van Barel preprint was read, including Lemma 8 and Theorem 10; its final 2008 version and later citation chain remain a priority check.

**Missing work.** Complete the final-paper/citation-chain comparison; formalize the new invariant and the sharp equality cases; investigate exact larger-size profile or total-rank minima without treating their current lower bounds as equalities.

**Possible manuscript proposition.** “The minimum quasiseparable order of a dyadic Fourier matrix under independent endpoint permutations.”

**Primary comparison.** [Delvaux and Van Barel (2008), Rank-deficient submatrices of Fourier matrices](https://www.cs.kuleuven.be/publicaties/rapporten/tw/TW470.pdf); [Tao, An uncertainty principle for cyclic groups of prime order](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0308286); [Pernet, Signargout and Villard (2023), Exact computations with quasiseparable matrices](https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gilles.villard/BIBLIOGRAPHIE/PDF/quasisep.pdf).

### 3 Universal reciprocal factorization of adjacent Hankel-pencil resultants

**Exact claim.** Let m=s+1, P_e(a,w)=det[T_{e+i+j}(1-a w^{e+i+j})] for e=0,1 and 0<=i,j<m, with formal independent T_0,...,T_{2s+1}. With fixed-degree-m Sylvester resultant R_s=Res_a(P_0,P_1), D=m^2(2m-1), v=s(s+1)(4s+5)/6 and b=s(s+1)(2s+1)/6: w^D R_s(1/w)=(-1)^{m^2}R_s(w), R_s=w^v(w-1)^{m^2}U_s, w^{2b}U_s(1/w)=U_s(w), and U_s=w^b V_s(w+w^{-1}) with deg V_s<=b. The valuation v is generically exact; all identities survive singular and zero-resultant specializations.

**Potential original contribution.** The specific universal factors, exact generic valuation and resulting compressed degree for these adjacent multiplicative Hankel pencils. General sparse rational interpolation, Hankel interpolation/resultants, reciprocal-polynomial compression and resultant change-of-variable formulas are established tools, not new contributions.

**Strength and limits.** A concise all-s theorem with an exact algebraic consequence is publishable material if the precise identity is not already subsumed by an existing pencil/resultant theorem. The proof uses standard machinery but a nontrivial valuation calculation. The application gives conditional fixed-s O_s(N) recovery only; zero probes, identically zero eliminants, nonlinear gcds and spectral zeros are genuine exclusions.

**Proof status.** Accepted E17, including a symbolic proof and independent degree-bound reconstructions. This is stronger than the provisional audit’s reported source tests. No original submitted program was run in this pass.

**Missing work.** Check structured-pencil resultant and Newton-polytope literature systematically; isolate the identity from legacy implementation defects; state the homogeneous resultant convention and generic-exact-versus-specialized-lower valuation distinction; provide a canonical coefficient-ring proof.

**Possible manuscript proposition.** “Universal factors and reciprocity of adjacent Hankel-pencil resultants, with sparse rational recovery as an application.”

**Primary comparison.** [Grigoriev, Singer and Karpinski (1990), Interpolation of sparse rational functions without knowing bounds on exponents](https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/id/eprint/2068/1/preprint_1990_10.pdf); [Uteshev and Baravy (2016), Solution of Interpolation Problems via the Hankel Polynomial Construction](https://arxiv.org/html/1603.08752).

### 4 Three-stage quasiseparable Fourier obstruction through discrepancy

**Exact claim.** For F_N represented as S3 P2 S2 P1 S1 with q(S_i)<=r_i, arbitrary independent internal permutations, and at least one affine-coprime Fourier endpoint ordering, the other endpoint arbitrary, necessarily 2r1+r2+r3>=c log N-2; transposition gives r1+r2+2r3>=c log N-2. Repeated bit reversal additionally yields r1+r2+r3>=ceil(log2(N)/2), excluding rank-one stages for dyadic N>=128.

**Potential original contribution.** The bridge from cyclic-window full-rank conditions to grid discrepancy of an internal permutation. Averaging all cyclic windows gives the exact centering Wt/N; applying Schmidt then excludes every fixed-order three-stage family in this scope.

**Strength and limits.** This is an infinite-family obstruction with a conceptual connection beyond the original search. Schmidt’s lower bound and quasiseparable rank calculus are prior art. Both endpoints arbitrary and non-affine are not covered; no general arithmetic lower bound or arbitrary-depth exclusion follows.

**Proof status.** Accepted E05. Rank proof and cyclic averaging read. Schmidt’s original publisher record was located; its precise theorem/normalization was verified in primary paper P16 because the original PDF was inaccessible.

**Missing work.** Compare permutation discrepancy and low-rank factorization literature beyond keywords; decide whether to combine with the endpoint minimax theorem; extend endpoint scope or stage count if possible; do not invent an explicit c or finite cutoff from the existential discrepancy constant.

**Possible manuscript proposition.** “Permutation discrepancy as an obstruction to shallow quasiseparable Fourier factorizations.”

**Primary comparison.** [Chandrasekaran, Epperly and Govindarajan, Graph-Induced Rank Structures and their Representations](https://arxiv.org/html/1911.05858); [Bilyk, Lacey, Parissis and Vagharshakyan (2009), Exponential Squared Integrability for the Discrepancy Function in Two Dimensions](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.5544); [Schmidt (1972), Irregularities of distribution VII](https://doi.org/10.4064/aa-21-1-45-50); [Pernet, Signargout and Villard (2023), Exact computations with quasiseparable matrices](https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gilles.villard/BIBLIOGRAPHIE/PDF/quasisep.pdf).

### 5 One-layer Fourier obstruction for independently ordered weighted-permutation Green sums

**Exact claim.** Over C, F_N cannot equal a sum of r diagonally ended legal weighted-permutation resolvents, plus rank-t, row-degree-s sparse and arbitrary diagonal corrections, once N>2(m-1)(B-m)(B-1), where m=r+t+1, L=m(s+2), B=(L-1)^{2^{r-1}}+1. Independent structural cycle orders, zero weights and singular diagonal masks are allowed. Fixed t,s imply r=Omega(log log N).

**Potential original contribution.** Combines rank-one separated rectangles for each independently ordered resolvent, simultaneous monotone-subsequence extraction and a short-arc Schur-polynomial Fourier-minor argument. The arbitrary-order one-layer obstruction does not follow from the separate sparse-inverse/rank gate.

**Strength and limits.** Useful theorem for restricted linear representations; explicit r=2 threshold N>2300 and r=3 threshold N>34,545,588 are large but rigorous. Unmatched independent row/column permutations on each term are excluded. Products of layers escape the proof; the term lower bound is very weak compared with FFT arithmetic complexity.

**Proof status.** Accepted E14. Full all-size argument and threshold derivation read. The broad ingredients are classical; their precise combination remains a plausible new application, not confirmed historical priority.

**Missing work.** Search additive rank-structured decomposition and simultaneous-order/Ramsey literature; sharpen thresholds or growth if feasible; formulate general target-minor hypotheses to give a theorem beyond Fourier; explain explicitly why product grammars are not covered.

**Possible manuscript proposition.** “Additive obstructions for independently ordered cyclic resolvents and Fourier matrices.”

**Primary comparison.** [Huhtanen and Perämäki (2015), Factoring Matrices into the Product of Circulant and Diagonal Matrices](https://math.aalto.fi/~mhuhtane/manu.pdf); [Chandrasekaran, Epperly and Govindarajan, Graph-Induced Rank Structures and their Representations](https://arxiv.org/html/1911.05858); [Tao, An uncertainty principle for cyclic groups of prime order](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0308286); [Dvir and Liu (2019), Fourier and Circulant Matrices Are Not Rigid](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.07334).

### 6 Exact recognition of common-quadratic-phase sparse Fourier sums

**Exact claim.** For x_j=omega^{a j^2} sum_{ell<=r} c_ell omega^{b_ell j}, over a supplied prime field/root subgroup with N>=3r, there is a sound and complete exact recognizer, with zero-vector handling. A centered normalized (r+1)-Hankel determinant has a provably nonzero constant coefficient, degree <=binom(r+2,3), and every true common phase supplies a root. Candidate recurrence recovery and full verification cost O_r(N). An accepted ring-Toeplitz extension yields a conservative uniform O(N r^4+r^{2+3 log_2 3}) bound.

**Potential original contribution.** The nonzero eliminant guaranteed for every nonzero promised input, a degree bound limiting candidate phases, parity-aware phase quotient, and charged exact membership verification. Common chirp models, rank constraints, Prony/Berlekamp–Massey and Toeplitz/Bareiss/Levinson tools are not new.

**Strength and limits.** This can support an exact symbolic/signal-recovery paper. The structured family is small; N>=3r is a sufficient bound, not established optimal identifiability. Growing r=o((log N)^{1/4}) gives a sub-N log N bound only on that structured class with a supplied field/root, not generic DFT data or stable floating point.

**Proof status.** Accepted E06–E07 and linked growing-rank corollary. Actual normalization, constant-term and ring-pivot reasoning read; source code not executed.

**Missing work.** Finish comparison with polynomial-phase Prony and finite-field sparse interpolation; make the representation equivalence classes explicit; sharpen dependence on r or show it matters practically; include rejection costs, field construction and numerical limitations.

**Possible manuscript proposition.** “A nonvanishing phase eliminant for exact common-chirp sparse Fourier recognition.”

**Primary comparison.** [Massey (1969), Shift-register synthesis and BCH decoding](https://crypto.stanford.edu/~mironov/cs359/massey.pdf); [Shukla, Kundu, Mitra and Grover (2023), On estimating parameters of a multi-component Chirp Model with equal chirp rates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07098); [Yang, Ge and Wang (2025), Pursuing the Limit of Chirp Parameter Identifiability: A Computational Approach](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.01286v1); [Uteshev and Baravy (2016), Solution of Interpolation Problems via the Hankel Polynomial Construction](https://arxiv.org/html/1603.08752).

### 7 Two-sided Green rectangle rank oracle and moving-interface certificates

**Exact claim.** For a legal Hamiltonian-cycle resolvent K^{-1}, rank K^{-1}[A,B]=|A|+|B|-N+rank K[B^c,A^c]. Proper support deletions leave bipartite paths, so structural rank equals maximum matching and is computable in O(N). Independent endpoint permutations/masks can be tracked. Product certificates use arbitrary intermediate sets and rank(AB)[R,C]<=rank B[S,C]+rank A[R,S^c]. The two-stage cut objective is submodular and polynomially optimizable by known oracle methods.

**Potential original contribution.** A usable exact support-aware oracle and moving-cut certificate specialized to the branch grammar, including a concrete Fourier spectral-error witness. Nullity duality, graph rank closure and submodular minimization are prior results.

**Strength and limits.** Potential framework section or software-assisted certification paper if demonstrated on a broad class. A single oracle/certificate can be linear time, but optimization is not thereby linear. Exact ranks with singular endpoint masks require deleting their supported coordinates rather than silently retaining equality.

**Proof status.** Accepted E08. Exact support/forest proof and optimization scope read.

**Missing work.** State the distinct costs of oracle, optimization and matrix execution; compare directly with graph-induced rank structures; add a general separation or an algorithmic improvement rather than recasting known inequalities.

**Possible manuscript proposition.** “Support-aware cut certificates for products of cyclic resolvents.”

**Primary comparison.** [Chandrasekaran, Epperly and Govindarajan, Graph-Induced Rank Structures and their Representations](https://arxiv.org/html/1911.05858); [Strang (2010), Fast transforms: Banded matrices with banded inverses](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2906572/); [Iwata, Fleischer and Fujishige, A combinatorial, strongly polynomial-time algorithm for minimizing submodular functions](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0004089); [Pernet, Signargout and Villard (2023), Exact computations with quasiseparable matrices](https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gilles.villard/BIBLIOGRAPHIE/PDF/quasisep.pdf).

### 8 Marked-tree constant-input jets and exact forbidden-run frontiers

**Exact claim.** For the doubling-tree A0 and stationary root projector E, (I-z A0)^{-1}=K(z)+E/(1-z), with K=sum_{t<n}z^t(A0^t-E). Constant-input full polynomial jets have total coefficient capacity <=2N-2 and can be formed in O(N). For a retained suffix 0^b, the explicit frontier evaluator has exact F_L=2^{n-d_L} A_b(d_L), d_L=min(n,b+L), and total cost Theta(N min(n+1,2^b)), where A_b counts binary words avoiding 0^b.

**Potential original contribution.** The precise cancellation/valuation ledger for this marked tree and the exact b-dependent count for this evaluator. Finite nilpotent resolvents, tree dynamic programming, Schur complements and forbidden-word recurrences are established mathematics.

**Strength and limits.** The linear constant-input jet is real positive progress. It does not imply a linear solver for polynomial-valued unknowns or an inexpensive inverse of the border. The frontier lower bound applies to the specified explicit representation, not every algorithm.

**Proof status.** Accepted E09 and its linked root-pole proof. One next-pivot constant-input closure was accepted; arbitrary repeated polynomial-input closure is not.

**Missing work.** Extract a general theorem for a nontrivial class of functional graphs or valuation-weighted trees; compare total path-length/output-sensitive algorithms; resolve polynomial-input closure for a stronger algorithmic contribution.

**Possible manuscript proposition.** “Output-sensitive polynomial jets on marked functional trees and their frontier growth.”

**Primary comparison.** [Guibas and Odlyzko (1981), String overlaps, pattern matching, and nontransitive games](https://doi.org/10.1016/0097-3165%2881%2990005-4); [Chandrasekaran, Epperly and Govindarajan, Graph-Induced Rank Structures and their Representations](https://arxiv.org/html/1911.05858).

### 9 Exact N16 coefficient existence certificate

**Exact claim.** An exact F16 exists in the fixed model H2 H1 B H0 with eight fixed Hamiltonian routes/poles per layer, 24 branch occurrences and 384 complex endpoint parameters. In a 256-dimensional affine chart, a rational contraction certificate isolates a unique root within radius 10^{-9}; it proves nonzero endpoint margins and the full matrix equality.

**Potential original contribution.** An exact reproducible witness for this particular constrained architecture, replacing a floating residual. Rational nonlinear-root certification itself is established.

**Strength and limits.** Worth preserving as a benchmark/counterexample and appendix. The parameters exceed N^2, and the result has no uniform coefficient generator, growing-size theorem, synthesis complexity bound or cost advantage. “Positive coefficient certificate” means affirmative existence, not positive real coefficients.

**Proof status.** Accepted E10 with exact rational contraction bounds, not just supplied optimizer output. This novelty pass read the audit, without rerunning it.

**Missing work.** Find a compact algebraic pattern, parameter compression, minimality or an infinite lift; otherwise do not build the main publication claim around one overparameterized N16 fit.

**Possible manuscript proposition.** “A certified finite Fourier factorization in a fixed cyclic-resolvent architecture,” as an example within a broader theorem paper.

**Primary comparison.** [Hauenstein and Sottile (2012), alphaCertified: certifying solutions to polynomial systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.1091); [Huhtanen and Perämäki (2015), Factoring Matrices into the Product of Circulant and Diagonal Matrices](https://math.aalto.fi/~mhuhtane/manu.pdf).

### 10 Projective graph constraint solver

**Exact claim.** Solve selected constraints u_k^T L_e u_i=0 on P1: invertible edges transport projective directions; cycle closures restrict a component to all P1, at most two points, or empty; rank-one edges become equality-literal disjunctions treated by 2-SAT and domain consistency. The accepted treatment includes infinity, repeated roots, characteristic two and algebraic-closure rejection by polynomial gcd.

**Potential original contribution.** The finite-field/arbitrary-characteristic details and extraction from Green off-support equations may be useful additions. Over C the same bilinear product-vector constraints, transfer matrices, cycle eigenvectors and singular-edge logic already occur in quantum 2-SAT algorithms.

**Strength and limits.** Do not claim a new general projective constraint-solving paradigm. The result solves a selected necessary row subsystem only; it does not finish column compatibility or scales, so success is not a Green factorization. This is the clearest novelty downgrade of the pass.

**Proof status.** Accepted E11 as a mathematical/algorithmic result. Its historical novelty was not certified by the local review. Primary quantum-2SAT texts were compared directly.

**Missing work.** Write an explicit reduction and theorem-by-theorem comparison with quantum 2-SAT; identify a genuinely absent characteristic/domain/complexity guarantee; position as a specialization unless that delta is substantial.

**Possible manuscript proposition.** “Projective constraint propagation over general fields for cyclic-resolvent feasibility,” with quantum-2SAT attribution.

**Primary comparison.** [de Beaudrap and Gharibian (2016), A linear time algorithm for quantum 2-SAT](https://arxiv.org/html/1508.07338); [Arad, Santha, Sundaram and Zhang (2018), Linear-Time Algorithm for Quantum 2SAT](https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/v014a001/v014a001.pdf).

### 11 Uniform sparse-inverse and low-rank perturbation gates

**Exact claim.** If F=product_i H_i, H_i=G_i+R_i with invertible H_i,G_i, rank R_i<=r_i and column sparsity of G_i^{-1} at most s_i, then with r=sum r_i and s=product s_i, s(r+1)^2>=N. For a prime complex Fourier transform, (s+1)(r+1)>=p+1. Stage-count/work consequences require the stated fully materialized per-stage executor.

**Potential original contribution.** A clean scope ledger and reusable test for a proposed circuit grammar. Inverse-difference telescoping, a sparse kernel vector and classical support uncertainty give the inequalities directly; no new general uncertainty theorem is supplied.

**Strength and limits.** Useful lemmas and guards, unlikely standalone publication substance. Dense structured high-rank corrections and other executors remain outside the cost inference; prime complex additive uncertainty must not be copied to arbitrary finite fields.

**Proof status.** Accepted E12, including field limitations. The proof was read.

**Missing work.** Attribute the standard ingredients and retain as a proposition supporting a genuinely new representation theorem; avoid upgrading a restricted rank/cost gate into a general FFT lower bound.

**Possible manuscript proposition.** “Uncertainty-based necessary conditions for sparse-inverse perturbation circuits,” as a supporting proposition.

**Primary comparison.** [Tao, An uncertainty principle for cyclic groups of prime order](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0308286); [Dvir and Liu (2019), Fourier and Circulant Matrices Are Not Rigid](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.07334).

### 12 Aggregate-fiber sparse recognizer

**Exact claim.** With N=QM and total fiber modes <=K0, exact recognition and full output can be charged O(N+M K0+Q K0+K0^2). If K0=O(Q) and Q^2<=N, this becomes O(N), with rejected-input processing explicitly bounded.

**Potential original contribution.** An aggregate rather than per-fiber promise and careful heavy/light accounting. Recurrence recovery, root enumeration, Vandermonde recovery and exact verification are standard.

**Strength and limits.** Potential application or corollary; no generic-input transform improvement. Novelty is more likely in the precise recognition guarantee and accounting than the core algorithm.

**Proof status.** Accepted E13; reviewed theorem and scope.

**Missing work.** Compare closely with block sparse interpolation and sparse Fourier recovery; show a meaningful regime or new worst-case dependence beyond reassembling standard primitives.

**Possible manuscript proposition.** “Exact aggregate-sparsity recognition for Fourier fibers,” as a corollary or applications section.

**Primary comparison.** [Massey (1969), Shift-register synthesis and BCH decoding](https://crypto.stanford.edu/~mironov/cs359/massey.pdf); [Grigoriev, Singer and Karpinski (1990), Interpolation of sparse rational functions without knowing bounds on exponents](https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/id/eprint/2068/1/preprint_1990_10.pdf).

### 13 Fixed-direction completion and fixed-pencil exclusion certificates

**Exact claim.** For fixed projective row/column directions, full off-support vanishing plus supported affine scale equations and nonzero scale/cycle guards characterize invertible branch completion. Separately, several fixed-mask, one-parameter Green pencils are excluded by exact projective cross-ratios and modular nonzero resultants at specified finite sizes.

**Potential original contribution.** The exact specialization to these search grammars and independently checkable certificates. Sparse affine graph solving, projective invariants, resultants and saturation/guard discipline are established techniques.

**Strength and limits.** Valuable reproducibility and search-space reduction. Fixed directions do not solve the full nonlinear problem, and fixed-pencil exclusions do not exclude the encompassing family. These belong with the successful construction/lower-bound paper rather than being promoted individually.

**Proof status.** Accepted E15–E16 within their exact scopes. No broad representation theorem inferred.

**Missing work.** Unify into a compact certificate format, preserve all guards, document verification versus discovery cost, and demonstrate a reusable mathematical consequence.

**Possible manuscript proposition.** “Exact feasibility certificates for fixed cyclic-resolvent directions,” as a methods appendix.

## Three important corrections to broad novelty claims

1. The Green primitive is established. Huhtanen–Perämäki already give linear-cost handling of sums of two permutation-diagonal matrices and show the diagonal/cyclic normal form. Green/quasiseparable and graph-induced-rank structures are mature subjects. The publishable question is the minimum additive length or a new obstruction in a precisely defined atomic class. Use “diagonally scaled cyclic-bidiagonal resolvents” when that is the actual class, rather than letting “Green matrix” imply all existing Green representations.

2. Projective transfer and cycle closure are close prior art, not merely an analogy. A bilinear constraint on two projective lines is exactly a product-vector two-qubit constraint over C after identifying its coefficient matrix. Quantum-2SAT papers explicitly propagate 2-by-2 transfer matrices and close cycles through eigenvectors. The project’s arbitrary-field treatment may add something, but historical novelty has to be demonstrated there.

3. Algebraic recognition is different from general transformation. The common-chirp and sparse-rational fast paths require membership promises and exact global checks. Phase21’s universal resultant theorem is sound independently of its implementation, whose zero-diagonal Green domain, fallback roots and incomplete cold-cost counters remain unresolved. No reported warm benchmark percentage is certified here.

## Suggested manuscript organization

**First priority: exact cyclic-resolvent length and arithmetic obstructions.** Lead with B5’s exact minimum, explain the quadratic synthesis lemma, then present the all-order projective certificate and principal-restriction/arithmetic-family theorem. Treat projective propagation and scale completion as attributed methods. A reviewed restricted-family exact-three extension would materially strengthen this paper, but it would still concern the five-coordinate restriction unless a full-size upper construction is supplied.

**Second priority: permutation-sensitive Fourier rank structure.** Center the sharp dyadic endpoint minimax theorem, then give the shallow three-stage discrepancy application if the narrative remains coherent. The one-layer multiorder theorem can either be a separate section with a general separated-minor formulation or join the first manuscript. Avoid a collection of unrelated negative screens.

**Third priority: adjacent Hankel-pencil resultants and exact recovery.** State and prove the universal algebraic identity over an integral coefficient ring, with generic sharpness and degeneracy behavior. Use conditional sparse-rational recovery as an application. The common-chirp nonvanishing eliminant may fit as a second, clearly distinct elimination result, but should not be forced into the same paper unless the shared mechanism is genuinely illuminating.

The N16 existence witness, tree-jet/frontier formulas, aggregate recognizers and finite pencil exclusions are currently better supporting sections, examples or reproducibility artifacts. Their status could change after a new uniform theorem, complexity improvement or structural classification.

## Work required before any publication claim

- Compare the final Delvaux–Van Barel article and its citation chain against the endpoint minimax result; the accessible official preprint already contains important bit-reversal structure.
- Compare the exact adjacent multiplicative Hankel-pencil family against structured-resultant and Newton-polytope literature. Standard reciprocity or the substitution w+w^{-1} is not sufficient novelty.
- State field, endpoint-order, zero-weight, singular-mask and legal-denominator conventions on the first theorem page. Separate full-size targets from their principal restrictions.
- Extract small deterministic certificate verifiers and complete manifests. Explain the characteristic-zero lifting logically; checking finitely many field points is not an emptiness proof.
- Distinguish theorem proof, finite evidence, discovery code and performance ledger. “Accepted internally” and “proved historically original” are separate predicates.
- Obtain expert human scrutiny of the most important proofs and priority comparisons before choosing submission targets. This assessment authorizes no outreach, submission or publication.
