## Mathematical and operational scope

An exact integer sufficient certificate for the spectral gap of a fixed two-path multigraph. For N>=2, K=4N*L+4J-NI has eigenvalue 3N on ones and N*(4*lambda_i-1) on its orthogonal complement. Positive leading principal minors of symmetric K therefore prove lambda2>1/4, giving every balanced cut more than N/16 crossing edges.

## Control/dataflow and algebra

integer_laplacian counts multiplicities exactly. Fraction-free symmetric Bareiss updates are consistent: pivot is the next leading principal minor and exact division by the preceding pivot is explicitly checked. Nonpositive pivots or inexact division explicitly raise AssertionError; these checks remain under -O. run creates K, computes all pivots and hashes the semicolon-delimited list.

## Input/guard and acceptance review

exact_ldlt_sylvester accepts arbitrary matrices without symmetry/square/integer checks; its upper-triangle mirroring can silently change a nonsymmetric input. The internal K builder is symmetric, so this is a public-helper boundary issue. run lacks N>=2; N=1 yields a positive K and reports a nonexistent lambda2 gap. Output hashes do not include the graph/order, source hash, environment or full pivot sequence.

## Cost accounting

Dense O(N^2) memory and O(N^3) exact-arithmetic updates. Integer bit lengths grow with leading determinants, up to O(N log N) for this O(N)-entry matrix scale; bit complexity is not cubic with unit integers. It is a certificate generator requiring replay, not a short independently checkable full witness.
