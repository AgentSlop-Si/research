# Resume research here — Hero #1 v13

**The exact all-length O(N) FFT is still open in this project.** v13 does *not* solve it. Do not confuse unbiased per-coordinate mean-square approximation with uniform full-vector accuracy or exact matrix application.

## Surviving useful structure

`A_{N,t}=F_N P_t`, where `P_t` has `N/M`-dimensional range, and the shifted beta bank satisfies `sum_t P_t=M I` for t=0..M-1. This is a full-rank resolution of identity on all data, not data-dependent caching. Exact mixed-radix F_N application equals `M^-1 sum_t A_{N,t} x`. `THEOREM.md` gives an all-N proof and `finite_field_audit.py` a full finite-field check.

## New sharp test for an exact breakthrough

**Can the M beta-bank scan vectors be evaluated jointly with o(MN) elementary scalar work and, more importantly, can *their complete combination* (including K-FFTs, roots, M-FFT summaries, gauges, routing, writes and recovery) run in o(N log N) worst-case on fresh arbitrary dense x?** An `O(N log M)` saving in local input compression alone is not enough; computing all M global gauged prefix cycles still costs `Omega(MN)` by the naive implementation.

The exact local map from the M input coordinates of a dyadic block to its M phase-bank summaries is a diagonal-scaled M-DFT and therefore has full rank M. No <M-dimensional *linear local state* contains all summaries. This does not preclude a globally shared nonunitary executor. Put forward a concrete one, rather than adding cached input outputs or assuming multiway scans are O(N).

Potential interface to the independent exact Green branch: formulate a noncommuting shared-state recurrence that takes all beta-bank forcing words, enforces the complete root-of-unity reconstruction identity, and synthesizes all output coordinates with certified per-stage work. Verify the two Green covariance identities symbolically and check arbitrary dirty states. A solution that secretly uses a full FFT or M complete scans is not sub-NlogN.

## Mandatory negative controls

1. With M>1, every *single fixed* phase-bank sketch has rank N/M and operator-norm error M-1. Reject claims that its unbiasedness proves spectral closeness.
2. With r<M whole sketch maps, arbitrary *linear output-side combinations* cannot have full rank N. This restricted lower bound is tight; do not silently extend it to nonlinear or new shared-input algorithms.
3. Reusing the same random chirp state across odd-factor columns can increase selected output variance by factor m. The independent-state schedule is essential.
4. Keeping all M phase banks is an exact reconstruction but costs O(MN+MNlogm+Nlog(G/M)), at least NlogN for fixed m and M~logG. Do not call this a win against Cooley–Tukey.
5. Full-vector expected squared error of one row is `(M-1)||x||^2`, even though each output variance is at most `(M/N)||x||^2`. High-probability simultaneous errors need additional rows. Precision/root setup must also be counted.
