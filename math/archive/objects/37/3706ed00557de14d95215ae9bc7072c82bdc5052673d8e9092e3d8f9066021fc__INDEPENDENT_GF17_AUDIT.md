# Independent GF(17) open-chart enumeration audit

9 October 2026. Uploaded programs were read only as source/data, never imported or executed. The accompanying checker was written independently and uses only Python's standard library and exact integer arithmetic modulo 17.

## Verdict

**Every submitted counter is reproduced, including 2,546 invertible candidates and zero candidates satisfying all 30 necessary anchored B minors.** All eight displayed source examples also match. This verifies the reported finite-field open-chart exclusion. It does not establish nonexistence in characteristic zero or on the omitted branches.

The root and permutation are fixed throughout: z=9 in GF(17), with P=[7,0,6,3,4,1,2,5] and B[:,j]=(F C)[:,P[j]]. The result concerns the normalized globally separated-generator model, not arbitrary FFT architectures or arbitrary rank-one quasiseparable matrices.

## Independent reconstruction

The checker avoids the uploaded solver routines and coefficient-based enumeration:

1. It obtains the H sequence by Vandermonde interpolation in the modes 1,z^(-k),z^(-2k), rather than the uploaded recurrence implementation.
2. It interpolates the three-mode tail from positions 3,4,5 into positions 6,7 and solves the resulting two affine equations for t,beta using Gaussian elimination. This gives the same nonsingular tail patch without copying the source's Cramer formulas.
3. It evaluates Q directly as the four-by-four Fourier determinant. All 69,632 tested determinant values agree with the exported Q coefficient data, including rejected points.
4. It constructs the upper conditions directly from three-by-three Fourier determinants. For vectors r_i(k) comprising Fourier-weighted head, tail and diagonal entries, use det(r_1-gamma1*r_0,r_2-gamma2*r_0,r_3) and det(r_1,r_2,r_0). The first determinant is affine in gamma1,gamma2; the second is independent of them. Their pairwise resultants yield the three affine equations. All 4,596 resulting coefficient matrices agree with the exported JSON data.
5. It examines **all 1,328,244 gamma pairs**, comprising all 17^2 pairs for each of the 4,596 lower survivors. No gamma pivot is divided, so degenerate upper linear systems are retained.
6. For each surviving pair it constructs C with generic two-by-two interior solves and a three-by-three boundary solve, forms B by direct Fourier matrix multiplication, computes C's rank/inverse independently, and evaluates all 30 B minors directly. For each invertible candidate it additionally verifies C A=I and every entry of F=B P A. These matrix identities are expected from A=C^-1; B's failed rank-one conditions prevent these candidates from being solutions in the specified stage family.

The uploaded JSON is used only for comparisons and expected-counter assertions. It does not supply the enumerator's filtering coefficients.

## Counts and exclusions

- 78,608 tuples with h0 nonzero are visited; the 4,913 h0=0 tuples are outside this search.
- 4,624 have singular tail coefficient systems and are omitted.
- 4,352 have t=0 and are omitted.
- Of the remaining 69,632, exactly 65,036 fail Q and 4,596 pass.
- 17,829 gamma pairs pass the upper necessary equations.
- 12,358 fail reconstruction: 5,483 first fail an S1 guard; 1,292 the boundary determinant; 4,873 a Delta guard; 710 the alpha guard. These are disjoint first-failure counts, not counts of all overlapping vanished factors.
- The 5,471 reconstructed matrices have ranks 8: 2,546; 7: 2,067; 6: 657; 5: 145; 2: 56.
- Every rank-8 candidate has between 3 and 11 nonzero anchored B minors. There are no successes.

Two useful precision points:

**Alpha is not a division denominator.** In the 710 alpha=0 cases, the checker directly verifies that physical columns 6 and 7 are proportional. They therefore cannot give invertible C. The other 11,648 reconstruction exclusions are denominator branches not settled by this enumeration.

**The 2,546 count is a superset of the fully corner-regular locus.** Exactly 619 candidates have one or more zero corner guards among B70,B07,A70,A07; 1,927 have all four nonzero. The individual zero counts, which overlap, are B70:78, B07:77, A70:215, A07:307. The submitted search did not apply these final corner filters. This does not weaken its zero-success conclusion: a nonzero anchored minor is a valid obstruction everywhere, including zero-corner points. Conversely, vanishing of this anchored list alone is not sufficient for the global separated class on a zero-corner branch.

## Scope

This is an exhaustive result for the stated GF(17), z=9, fixed-permutation rational patch. It does not cover extension-field points, characteristic-zero points, other permutations, h0=0, t=0, singular tail systems, or denominator-zero onion branches. A complex solution need not produce a GF(17)-rational point.

The quartic's beta coefficient is not divided by this enumerator: 863 lower survivors and 444 invertible candidates have that coefficient zero and are retained. Likewise no invertible two-gamma pivot is assumed: among the 4,596 lower survivors, the three-by-two gamma coefficient matrix has rank two at 3,838, rank one at 740, and rank zero at 18. The separate five-variable gate's restrictions should not be silently applied to this finite-field enumeration.
