## Findings that affect interpretation

1. **State transport is not yet a cheap Fourier realization.** S00 verifies the literal seven-wire equations and correct negative-sign Weyl pair, but its abstract transport positive control places a dense Fourier map in the output map. It explicitly does not construct a bounded-degree Green Fourier transport witness. The homogeneous Weyl equations also leave scale unfixed: 2F satisfies them and fails a unit-seed normalization.
2. **Character profiles require physical block bookkeeping.** S06 retains the correction Theta(I_N)=I_k. At k=2m, the repeated-character Gram XY^T contains cross-block ones, whereas the projected physical identity has zero cross-block entries. The finite positive controls do not provide a full Fourier factorization.
3. **Route lifts, capacity, and Fourier coefficient lifts remain separate.** S07’s quotient-compatible Hamiltonian lift is a new splice family, not the old family plus an established Fourier coefficient lift. Its exact N16 minors reject the all-ones inner prefix, while the full 384-parameter family remains open. S12 supplies nilpotent folding and exact-edit controls; S13’s external MGG premise yields aggregate cut capacity, not attained transfer rank or Fourier coefficients.
4. **The additive and recognition results have explicit boundaries.** S02 retains a full convolution certificate after short Fourier probes, rejects the short-probe alias, and separates coprime/separated-wrap recognition from sparse-inverse coverage. S09 counts total Green occurrences M separately from order-bank size d. S10–S11 preserve fixed-instance, additive, direct-inverse and local-Jacobian scopes. S14 does not extend its sparse-prefix/one-additive-tail theorem to products of additive sums or all composite Fourier matrices.
5. **The nonlinear proof objects are local and finite.** S16 checks N8 graph capacities and a rank-60 Jacobian at a fixed point. S17 checks rank232, 35 saved cut rectangles and a separating tangent direction over F12289; it does not revisit the original 20,000-query search or establish global nonlinear feasibility. Its NumPy arithmetic is safe for this specific small modulus and dimensions, as newly bounded below.
6. **No faster arbitrary-input Fourier algorithm appears in this batch.** S01 recovers shared Cooley–Tukey accounting. S20 produces the baseline N16 detail word with 17 H2 calls, while the at-most-16-call goal remains explicitly unproved. S21’s O(N) half-cycle control is a two-sparse map, and its signed-cycle Fourier chirp identity has degree N−1 with dense oracle evaluation. Those are not generic subquadratic or finite-word Fourier executors.

No new failure affecting the fixed recorded mathematical cases was established. Generic helper preconditions and data-path assumptions are noted per source without inflating them into failures of the actual fixed tests. All-size statements in prose still require their proofs; numerical tolerance checks and finite-field calculations are labeled separately.
