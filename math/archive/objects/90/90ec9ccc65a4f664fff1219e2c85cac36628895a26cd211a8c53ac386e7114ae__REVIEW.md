## Findings that affect use

1. **The original sequential-fiber condition equality needs a qualification, and the later transparent construction supplies it.** A local prefix 2I has condition one, but its sequential embedding diag(2I,I) has condition two. Later determinant-modulus-one shear prefixes and separate norm/inverse product bounds make identity padding safe. The conditional macro reduction still requires the concrete short Fourier word, paid coefficient/root preparation, an explicit-record printer and the permitted size sequence. Algebraic condition control alone is not a finite-precision error theorem. See `CONDITION_RESET_ADDENDUM.md`.
2. **The finite F32 sharpening is independently supported.** The full proof and new controls establish the complex 11-by-11 rank-at-least-five argument, its moment certificate, and the two-stage exclusion for the specified unsigned coordinate-bit shuffles. New enumeration reproduces all 63 vertices, 617 profiles and 120 bit-coordinate maps; N64 checks all 720. This has a precise architecture scope.
3. **Two earlier rank statements need their exact boundaries.** F8 has minimum endpoint-order cut profile maximum three; natural order reaches four. Two rank-two stages fail the natural half-cut test for every N>=18; the same argument does not exclude N=17. Fresh finite controls retain both distinctions.
4. **The coarse-additive word-RAM approximation survives within its model.** Fixed/common bounded scaling, capped exact sampling, descriptor/table setup, guarded lanes and explicit output are essential. The shrinking-error parameter proof and decoded recurrence-fusion algebra are valid within their stated models. The uploaded lazy or fixed-small-parameter prototypes do not implement the full growing schedule, and these guarantees are weaker than ordinary output-relative FFT accuracy. Exact baseline hashes for two previously accepted orbit-review documents are linked, rather than treating mere snapshot presence as proof.
5. **The conditioned-stage and locality barriers remain useful restricted theorems.** Low-rank wrappers, fixed-strength sparse Green stages, forward-cycle factors and fixed endpoint layouts have distinct hypotheses. A count of displayed full-array passes becomes a work bound only when those passes must be paid separately. Arbitrary gains, inverse stages, free general unitaries, fused execution and changed endpoints cannot be added without checking the relevant theorem again.
6. **Several high-impact positive routes remain conditional.** Thin-matrix bilinear rank does not pay all additions; the uniform total-work construction is still needed. The h=24 arithmetic can be recomputed, but the complete general-h network, framing, coefficient extraction and downstream all-length transfer are separate. A losing carrier-bit candidate is recorded as losing. Exact symbolic orbit descriptors still need numeric orbit expansion. Short topology searches do not exclude all circuits.
7. **Historical implementation limits remain visible.** The floating coefficient collector loses the exact unit in 2^53+1−2^53. Prototype word widths, clipped error ledgers, online phasor construction, repacking, cache/history costs, setup, routing, output materialization and bit growth require separate treatment. A failed LeakSanitizer-under-ptrace run is not repaired by an ASan/UBSan run with leak detection disabled. No benchmark was rerun.
