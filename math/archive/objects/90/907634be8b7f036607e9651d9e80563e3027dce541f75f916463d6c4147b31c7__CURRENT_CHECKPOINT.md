# Current Fourier research checkpoint

9 October 2026. Includes the baseline master, the 14:50 additions, and the three source archives received at 15:19 UTC. This checkpoint summarizes current conclusions; the original dated inventories and audits remain preserved.

## Goal and present answer

The goal remains an independently justified exact Fourier transform for every fresh arbitrary input and all output coordinates, with genuinely sub-N-log-N charged work. Coefficient/root preparation, routing, conversions, workspace, precision where claimed, and explicit outputs count.

No new exact arbitrary-input sub-N-log-N construction has been established by this project. No unrestricted arithmetic Fourier lower bound follows from its restricted obstructions. The approximate, Boolean, finite-field, pair-unitary, and unrestricted complex-arithmetic models must remain separate.

## 1. Exact algebraic route: a smaller, explicitly saturated target

The fixed-permutation F8 two-separated-generator-stage problem now has three independently reconstructed exact formulations:

- 30 quartics in 26 variables for an inverse-stage regular chart;
- 25 sextics in 21 variables after Fourier-support elimination;
- 176 quadratics in 184 variables for the full separated-generator lift, including zero-anchor corners.

These are formulations on explicitly stated loci, not unconditional replacements of the old chart. The old normalization fixes diag(A), the new one fixes diag(A^-1); they can differ even with nonzero anchors. The exact N8 example A=(7/6)I-(1/6)J has diagonal one but inverse (6/7)I-(6/7)J has zero diagonal. Every exported coefficient was checked independently. On the already-required nonzero-t chart, removing monomial factors reduces the 25-equation list to four cubics, six quartics, one quintic and fourteen sextics. This equivalent primitive export is included.

The regular patch must exclude zero denominators, zero second-stage anchors, singular C, and zero inverse-stage corners. The inverse-corner cofactors factor as an endpoint anchor times six local gaps; the independent audit makes these conditions explicit. The unoptimized product of saturation factors has degree 59, so adding a single reciprocal equation gives degree 60. A headline degree-six feasibility system is not a degree-six fully saturated formulation.

The unsaturated sextics admit an entire spurious beta-line giving C=the all-ones matrix. The smaller chart is therefore not a certificate by itself. Neither a valid exact F8 solution nor an exhaustive exclusion has been obtained. The earlier rank-60-to-61 second-order obstruction remains a certificate at its stored parameter point, not a proof excluding F8.

## 2. Approximate chirp route: streaming and a numerical contract

For dyadic N=KM, M>=2 and K>=2M, the independently verified v7 row costs O(N+K log K). Its per-output covariance is (2M/N) times a projector of rank K(M-1)/2. Independent rows give simultaneous all-output energy-relative approximation, not exact arbitrary-input recovery.

v8's gauge transformation converts its two output cycles into balanced prefix scans: O(N) work and O(log N) accumulation depth. Three shifted Boyer–Moore grids use O(N) candidate words and yield complex error at most 5g sqrt(M/N)||x||2 with failure at most 2N exp(-R D(1/2||1/g^2)), under the stated exact-arithmetic assumptions. Constant-time real floor/quantization is an extra primitive. The preceding five-counter comparison-only selector remains a valid alternative, and can use the same gauge decoder.

The new independent finite-precision theorem specifies the entire row implementation, including summaries, a normalized short FFT, seeds, forcing terms, roots and scan. With unit roundoff u, certified roots/normalizers, exact modular indices and valid range handling, it proves deterministic row error at most

800 sqrt(NM) log2(N) u ||x||2,

provided 8u log2(N)<=0.01. Guard-precision root preparation and exact represented-grid classification are charged. Taking u at most theta g/[800 N log2(N)] makes this error at most a chosen fraction of the stochastic radius. A certified norm envelope and inflated common grid radius preserve the original error exponents at explicit larger constants.

This is a constructive numerical theorem for a specified implementation variant, not a certification of the uploaded NumPy prototype or of fixed IEEE precision for arbitrary N. O(log N)-bit precision suffices in the stated bounded-input regimes. Bit-operation costs, exponent range, input/output bits and root setup remain charged separately; the result is not bit-linear. Space is O(N) words, not O(N) bits.

## 3. Exact unitary route: the expensive banks are still expensive

The Phase15 normalized base, residual-fold and orbit operators have entropies N log2 B, N-B and B+N(log2 L-1). Every pair-unitary butterfly in the full Fourier factorization gains the maximum two bits. The constructed transform uses exactly N log2 N/2 such gates. Further optimizing its already-linear folding map cannot remove the Fourier-bank costs in this model.

Phase16 sharpens the path/Haar–Green compilation to at most (11N/2)-8 pair-unitary gates per full stage, valid for size-dependent real strengths. An exact Fourier product therefore needs at least N log2 N/(11N-16) stages. The entropy technique is established prior work, here applied and checked for the project's constructions.

A generic fixed-column pivot rule needs adaptive prefix pivoting for degenerate quasiseparable unitaries; the independent report supplies the repair. This does not invalidate the irreducible path result. The gate-existence proof does not assert linear-time preparation of all parameters. The independent entropy-continuity extension retains Omega(log N) stages for exactly unitary outputs within any fixed error epsilon<1, measured in operator norm or Frobenius norm divided by sqrt(N). Zero-initialized ancillas also do not remove the pair-unitary count. Nonunitary/shared-state algorithms remain outside the model.

## 4. Restricted exact-route filters retained

- Common-order noncommuting tridiagonal resolvents remain low-cut-rank. Sums/products obey an additive expanded-expression budget; standard Fourier needs budget at least floor(N/2), or at least ceil(floor(N^2/4)/N) with arbitrary endpoint permutations. This is a corollary of earlier quasiseparable inverse/subadditivity facts, not a new general lower bound.
- Products whose inverse stages have row sparsities s_j require product(s_j)>=N. Dense inverses and sums of resolvents need separate analysis.
- Common-base rational functions, low-cut-rank local-port solves, single-mask field descent, and Galois phase-support tests retain the scopes stated in the baseline master. High cut rank alone never certifies compatible Fourier coefficients or low runtime.
- Quadratic polynomial decoding of a deficient fixed linear sketch cannot recover every Boolean input's full linear target; randomized worst-case success is at most 3/4. Cubic interpolation escapes that restriction without supplying a faster transform. Realification and conjugate-bin symmetry remain explicit.

## Decisive next results

1. Solve the explicit saturated F8 regular system exactly, or certify its exclusion and cover every exceptional branch through the complete lift. Numerical residuals and finite-field infeasibility alone are insufficient.
2. If pursuing noncommuting resolvent fusion, show how the candidate escapes both common-order rank budgets and pair-unitary entropy, then verify exact Fourier covariance/coefficient identities and a fully charged evaluator. Merely changing the representation name does not remove either cost.
3. Implement the specified finite-precision approximation with certified roots, norms, modular arithmetic, range and grid handling before claiming an end-to-end practical numerical guarantee.
