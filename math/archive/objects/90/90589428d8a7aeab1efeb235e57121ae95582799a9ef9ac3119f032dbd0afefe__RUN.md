The cyclic verifier constructs exact integer matrices from remainders modulo `Phi_N`, computes modular ranks and **nonzero modular determinants** of selected minors, and checks every gcd-class indicator against the traceless projection. The rank upper bound and general all-length equality are proved in `THEOREM.md`, not inferred from finite tests. The abelian verifier checks 15 noncyclic/mixed product groups, with explicit enumeration of cyclic subgroups.

`full_cse_probe.py` checks complete data-dependent linear forms in both inherited prime fields; `linear_cse_probe.py` checks rational pre/post expressions over the integers. Finite-field equality is never used to *claim* distinct characteristic-zero values; if two characteristic-zero expressions were equal, their reductions would also be equal, so observed noncollisions certify distinctness of these explicit expressions.

The full prior checkpoint is `PRIOR_PHASE29_COMPLETE.zip`, unchanged. It contains the earlier archival lineage. These programs are research checkers and are not a floating-point FFT library.
