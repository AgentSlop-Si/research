# Complete N4 equal-outer pencil exclusion

9 October 2026 UTC. Independent paired-construction review. This result is separate from the source-archive reviews and from the still-open unequal-outer experiment. No uploaded program was executed.

## Exact conclusion

Let Z e_j=e_(j+1 mod 4), let R be bit reversal with index map (0,2,1,3), and put

G0=(I−Z/4)⁻¹, G1=(I−RZR⁻¹/5)⁻¹,
H_[u:v]=uG0+vG1.

For the unnormalized F4[j,k]=i^(jk), there are no nonzero projective coefficient pairs [u:v], [r:s] and invertible diagonal masks L,M satisfying

F4=H_[u:v] L H_[r:s] M H_[u:v].

This includes all finite couplings, pure G0, pure G1, zero-entry middle matrices, and singular-stage cases. Singular factors are excluded because their product cannot be invertible. Intermediate permutations are fixed to identity. Independently varying the two outer coefficient ratios, other cycles/weights, and N≥8 are not covered. Failure at N4 is not an all-length or asymptotic impossibility theorem.

Because G0,G1 are real, complex conjugation gives the same exclusion for the opposite Fourier phase convention.

## 1. Independent reconstruction

The checker constructs the two sparse denominators and inverts them exactly over Q. It reconstructs every cubic adjugate entry of H(t)=G0+tG1 from four exact evaluations, then verifies the polynomial identity H(t)adj(H(t))=det(H(t))I. For invertible outer factors, K differs from H(a)⁻¹F4H(c)⁻¹ by the nonzero scalar detH(a)detH(c), which can be absorbed into the diagonal masks.

For each value, diagonal equivalence requires K_ij=0 wherever H(b)_ij=0. Every rectangle whose four H(b) entries are nonzero also requires the corresponding cross-product identity

K_ij K_kl H(b)_il H(b)_kj − K_il K_kj H(b)_ij H(b)_kl=0.

These are necessary polynomial equations over Q(i), even before any support or determinant saturation. For every one of the nine b-values, their exact Gröbner basis is {1}. Thus there is no complex pair (a,c) satisfying even this necessary subsystem. These exclusions are actually valid for unequal finite outer parameters too, but do not address their regular chart.

This removes every zero-middle chart at finite b. Pure G1 has no zero entries and is handled by the regular coefficient-rank argument below.

## 3. Equal finite outer parameters: a compact rank certificate

Set a=c=t and K=K(t,t). On a hypothetical solution with nowhere-zero middle entries, K is also nowhere zero. For i,j>0, diagonal equivalence implies

K_ij K_00 H(b)_i0 H(b)_0j − K_i0 K_0j H(b)_ij H(b)_00=0.

Each of the nine equations is quadratic in b. Arrange its coefficients of (1,b,b²) into a 9×3 matrix C(t), with equations ordered (1,1),(1,2),(1,3),(2,1),…,(3,3). A common b makes C(t) have rank at most two.

Take the 3×3 coefficient minors on rows (0,1,2) and (0,1,3). Independent reconstruction confirms that each has degree 36 and is divisible by the exact degree-six polynomial K_00(t). After division and monic normalization, the two degree-30 quotients match every coefficient in the constructor's supplied certificate and have gcd one over Q(i).

Since K_00 is nonzero on any admissible regular solution, both quotients would have to vanish. Coprimality rules this out.

The argument also covers the pure-G1 middle point: homogenize b to [b0:b1] and replace (1,b,b²) by the always-nonzero vector (b0²,b0b1,b1²). No finite-b assumption is needed in this coefficient-rank step.

### Independently checked short resultant certificate

The supplied degree-30 polynomials and degree-29 Bézout multipliers reduce modulo 29 with i→12. Every rational denominator remains nonzero, the two leading monic coefficients preserve their exact degrees, and the entire coefficient identity UP+VQ=1 is verified.

Consequently the exact characteristic-zero resultant cannot vanish. This is a justified degree-preserving resultant lift, not an inference from absent finite-field solutions. The checker separately computes the exact Q(i) gcd one as well.

## 4. The common outer projective boundary

When both outer factors are pure G1, use K∞=adj(G1)F4 adj(G1). Here K∞_00=109375/194688 is nonzero. The first three rows of the middle coefficient matrix already have a nonzero 3×3 determinant over Q(i), recorded explicitly in VERIFIED_RESULTS.json.

Thus no nonzero projective middle coefficient vector works on this outer chart either. Together with Sections 2 and 3, this completes the whole projective equal-outer family.

## 5. Reproducibility and limits

It rebuilds the sparse matrices, all nine exact zero-support ideals, both degree-30 quotients, the full guarded Bézout identity, and the common-outer infinity witness. The proof establishes this concrete small-family exclusion.
