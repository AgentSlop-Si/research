# Macro/word-RAM checkpoint: independent audit summary

9 October 2026. The new checkpoint adds substantive implementation work and scoped exact results. It does not solve the original exact arbitrary-input sub-N log N Fourier problem. No uploaded Python was executed or imported.

## What is genuinely new relative to the prior synthesis

1. **Better approximate implementation components.** The eager decoder now enumerates the complete descriptor table, stores its rows prepacked, and unpacks only final accumulated output lanes. This fixes the previous prototype's lazy-phasor and per-lookup repacking limitations. It adds certified integer trigonometry, capped exact-mass sampling, rational parameter selection, and small fresh-input integration tests.
2. **An exact weighted-affine-cycle fast path.** After verifying at most s residual defects, the new graph method evaluates every output in O(N(s+1)) exact field operations. For fixed s it handles a restricted family of fully dense inputs in linear arithmetic work. A dense one-defect family has the advertised 14N+1 field-operation count, smaller than the supplied particular radix comparator at the listed sizes. Arbitrary inputs still fall back to O(N log N).
3. **Stronger architecture-specific bounds.** The scan/broadcast necessary condition improves to (2B+1)2^(t−1)+B ≥ N for t≥1. The nondegenerate one-channel sandwich A=L⁻¹TR⁻¹ is valid with T tridiagonal; bounded contiguous T components imply product_j(2b_j+1) ≥ N even with arbitrary shuffles. A separate cut-rank movement inequality requires extensive permutation travel for few stages, but is not a RAM-time lower bound.

## What is already established or still absent

- The fully charged O(N) coarse approximate word-RAM theorem and its guarded-addition closure were already established in our prior synthesis. This upload improves component implementation and evidence; it does not create a new accuracy guarantee.
- The finite experiments use small fixed B,g, not the growing asymptotic schedule. They do not constitute an end-to-end production implementation or performance measurement of that schedule. At epsilon=0.5, gamma=1/2, g first becomes nonzero at N=2^40 with 1,048,576 table rows, exceeding the default one-million-row cap; g=2 starts at N=2^84 with roughly 4.4 trillion rows.
- The mathematical accuracy remains global additive epsilon L_box, or epsilon L_box/sqrt(N) under unitary normalization. It can be much weaker than conventional Fourier accuracy. No exact arbitrary-complex transform, practical FFT replacement, or novelty theorem follows.
- Exact-field counts are not bit complexity or bounded-word exact Fourier runtime. Unbounded cyclotomic coefficient sizes remain a material distinction.
- The package's generic warning that quasiseparable cut rank does not alone construct scalar scan channels is valid. It does not undo our already independently supplied exact 3-forward/3-backward F8 realization.
- The prior sharp F32 11-by-11 rank gate, including exclusion of all 120 unsigned coordinate-bit middle permutations for the stated two-stage architecture, is unchanged. Arbitrary middle permutations and shifted candidates remain open.

## Corrections and qualifications

No substantive error was found in the new approximate proof or reviewed exact identities. One graph README sentence should say “one fewer cross-product comparison, saving two field multiplications” rather than “two fewer comparisons”; its 14N+1 total is correct. Some multiword decoder tests use vacuous large error bounds and support mechanics only. Meaningful-accuracy cases are separately present.

All 74 manifest hashes, nine validation-output hashes, and 17 source-result bindings match. These integrity checks establish consistency of the distributed snapshot, not that its uploaded programs were rerun here.
