## Structural strictness gate

For an inverse branch B, define sr(B) as the minimum over independent row and column orders of the maximum rank of a strictly separated rectangle. Either exhibit invertible column-two Q_N with sr(Q_N^(-1)) unbounded, or identify a theorem giving a uniform constant bound for the whole class. The first would prove strict extension beyond every fixed ordered-separation budget; the second could subsume that structural increment under the inherited abstract theorem. High degree and a bad natural ordering settle neither. Until resolved, describe coverage not previously established in the inspected project record, without claiming certified set-theoretic strictness.
