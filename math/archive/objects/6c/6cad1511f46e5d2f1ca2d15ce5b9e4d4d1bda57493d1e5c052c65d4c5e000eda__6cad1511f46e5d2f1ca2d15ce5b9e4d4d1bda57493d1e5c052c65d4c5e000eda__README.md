# Hero #1 — Phase 19: Blind Sparse Inverse + Graph Union FFT

**2026-10-10 · reproducible finite-field research checkpoint**

## What changed since Phase 18?

Phase18 could evaluate sparse defects of *supplied* graph filters and could discover a recurrence only within a tiny fixed delay bank. Phase19 introduces three explicit, verified adaptive routes:

1. **Blind one-defect graph shift.** An unknown coprime cyclic delay and its scalar multiplier are recovered deterministically from two adjacent-ratio multiplicities and a complete O(N) graph certificate. No shift catalog, interpolation oracle, or quadratic delay search.
2. **Blind bounded-K extension.** At K=2, a subset-sum of adjacent-ratio class counts plus exact verification discovers an unknown shift on the non-colliding wrap-class family. A resonant counterexample is retained and safely falls back.
3. **Fully blind sparse circular inverse.** An unknown s-sparse convolution inverse is recovered from `2s` direct Fourier probes, Berlekamp–Massey, scanning N roots, charged Vandermonde interpolation, and **all-N convolution verification**. It then computes every Fourier output in `O(sN+s^3)` exact field arithmetic. For a one-impulse residual behind any *constant number of completely unknown* graph binomials, s<=2^m, so the arithmetic complexity is **O(N)** on that structured family.

The combined engine tries graph ratio, sparse inverse, then full Bluestein. **The general exact FFT bound remains O(N log N)**. These structured-input, zero-error results are not a new arbitrary-complex FFT theorem or an asymptotic bit-multiplication result.

## Cost experiment (N=4096, GF(2013265921))

| Fresh-input family | Hybrid selector | Charged field operations | Full Bluestein | Fraction |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Unknown shift, one defect | blind graph | 101,610 | 570,070 | 17.8% |
| Unknown shift, two defects | blind graph | 116,366 | 570,070 | 20.4% |
| Two completely unknown factors, one impulse | blind sparse inverse | 241,943 | 570,070 | 42.4% |
| Generic dense random input | full fallback | 701,321 | 570,070 | 123.0% |

Operation counts are **field-arithmetic proxies**, not timings or bit costs. In each comparison both branches use the same preinitialized root and Green solver; the blind branch *charges* all its discovery, rejected candidates, exact certificates, short DFT probes, recurrence, root scans, interpolation and Green operations. Generic detection incurs an extra charged O(N) toll.
