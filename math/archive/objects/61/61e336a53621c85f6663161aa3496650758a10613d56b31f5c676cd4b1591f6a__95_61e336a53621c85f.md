# Result: inverse-cycle pairs are useful but do not provide a Fourier speedup

The bounded investigation produced an exact, reusable O(N) scalar-arithmetic all-pass unitary and four rigorous scoped obstructions, including the phase-rotated commuting extension. It did not produce an exact arbitrary-input, all-output o(N log N) Fourier algorithm.

1. Complete opposite-sign two-stage classification, N >= 3: with monomial separators, every scalar-unitary pair K1 Q K2^-1 (or its opposite-sign ordering) is either a monomial cancellation or an explicitly classified all-pass unitary times a monomial. The all-pass case is genuinely dense and nontrivial, but has unequal entry magnitudes, so one pair cannot be Fourier.
2. Every all-pass pair block has induced-1 condition number c_N^2 < 4, where c_N=2-3/(2^N+1). An exact Fourier product assembled from individually unitary pair blocks therefore requires at least log(N)/(2 log c_N) such blocks. The earlier Green/rank-one gate extends to 7^s c_N^(2r)(B+1)^2 >= N for arbitrary interleavings of these blocks.
3. A word using literally K(M), K(M*), and their inverses for one fixed weighted operator M, after monomial separators are moved to an endpoint, cannot be Fourier-flat at any depth. Scalar unitarity reduces it to an integer power of the all-pass matrix. After a monomial gauge change, that power is real orthogonal with odd-denominator rational entries. Even lengths are excluded by the denominator of 1/N; odd lengths by parity of a row inner product.
4. The phase-rotated commuting family K(lambda M) and K(lambda M*) with lambda^N=1, and their inverses, also requires at least log_2 N original stages to realize a flat unitary up to scale. If t<N/2, global scalar unitarity forces complete forward/inverse pairing and modulus exactly 1. This is proved by a degree-2t circle-modulus polynomial with N spectral zeros. Longer words already have at least N/2 stages.

The construction uses U(M)=(I-2M)(2I-M)^-1. The canonical-cycle evaluator prepares its scalar recurrence in N+1 field operations and applies it in 7N-2 field operations, plus separately linear indexing and storage. Weighted-cycle gauge preparation/application is also linear. This is scalar arithmetic, not a bounded-word or bit-complexity claim.

A useful constructive boundary control: for even N=2m, the m forward factors with phases lambda^m=1 fuse to [(2^m+1)I+(2^m-1)M^m]/2. This is scalar-unitary and has a fully charged O(N) scalar-arithmetic application after preparation, even though literal separate passes would cost Theta(N^2). It has only two nonzeros per row and is not Fourier for N>2. Coefficient generation uses O(log m) field multiplications; 2^m has Theta(N) bits. This illustrates why the factor-count gate must not be presented as an unrestricted execution lower bound.

Honest remaining gap: longer globally cancelling mixed K/K^-1 words may become unitary even when no adjacent opposite-sign pair is unitary. The proofs do not classify arbitrary such words. The phase-rotated extension covers only the stated common M/M* family after all monomial conjugations. Arbitrary new edge-phase choices, other commuting cycles, and genuinely noncommuting changes of cycle remain outside it. Factor-count gates also do not exclude an evaluation method that fuses a whole product more efficiently than separately applying its displayed factors.
