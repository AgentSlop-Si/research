# Exact two-stage Fourier cut-rank certificate

A continuation of the supplied **nonunitary global Green / gauge research** archive, staying on the original fresh-input exact FFT research path. Not a general FFT algorithm and not an asymptotic breakthrough.

## Key result

For an even N-point Fourier matrix, if `F_N = S2 P S1` with **any** internal permutation and two quasiseparable stages with offdiagonal ranks `r1,r2`, then `r1+r2 >= N/4`, provided at least one external input/output endpoint uses natural Fourier order. The proof is an exact transfer-rank inequality plus two Vandermonde ranks. It settles the previous **N=32, two-stage, r=2/3** numerical attempts in that ordered class.

For **power-of-two lengths**, simultaneously reordering both endpoints remains an open escape from this rank bound. For **prime lengths**, Chebotarëv's classical all-minors theorem makes the lower bound robust against **arbitrary input, output and intermediate permutations**. See `RESEARCH.md` and `endpoint_stress_results.json`.
