## Meaning of a speedup claim

The `N=384, M=8` proxy changes from Phase14's 13,280.6 to 5,344.6 after eliminating 3,072 Green scan visits. This is a bookkeeping illustration, **not** a measured wall-clock speedup or asymptotic improvement; both use conventional short FFTs, and the proxies aggregate different operations as rough units.

**Project discipline:** Any future `O(N)` or `O(N log log N)` claim must be exact on arbitrary fresh complex inputs with all global work charged, or clearly labeled approximate/structured. A successful finite field test does not by itself prove an asymptotic upper bound.
