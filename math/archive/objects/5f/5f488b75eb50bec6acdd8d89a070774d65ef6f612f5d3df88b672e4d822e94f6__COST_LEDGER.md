## New certificates are *analysis*, not a faster transform

The projected matrix-rank verifier performs O(N^3) modular elimination. Its runtime is charged as research/certification work, not presented as part of an FFT algorithm. The new rank theorem is a circuit rejection criterion; no claim of algorithmic arithmetic savings is made.

If a future proposal computes a `b`-input block using an unspecified high-arity operation, every scalar instruction of that operation still needs to be expanded and charged. Setup, root construction, array traffic, floating-point conditioning, and bit complexity are not hidden or paid by the Phase 30 theorem.
