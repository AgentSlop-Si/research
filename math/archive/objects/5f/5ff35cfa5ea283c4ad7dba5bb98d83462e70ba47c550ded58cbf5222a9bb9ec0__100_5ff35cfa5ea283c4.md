## New advances in THIS checkpoint

**1. Stronger all-permutation exact obstruction.** `fft_two_stage_minor_gate/HALFCUT_THEOREM.md` improves the original sqrt-N obstruction to `r_A+r_B >= N/4` for even N in `F_N=A P B`, when A and B have fixed natural-order quasiseparable off-cut ranks and P is any complex invertible monomial map. Equal ranks require `r>=N/8`. The proof uses both full-rank half-size Fourier/Vandermonde minors and a complementary-overlap pigeonhole count. It kills two rank-2 Green stages at N>=32, explaining the earlier N32 rank-3 numerical failure as exact impossibility. This is a model-scoped negative theorem, not a DFT arithmetic lower bound or literature-priority claim.

**2. Explicit failure of naive three-stage extrapolation.** `THREE_STAGE_CONTROL.md` constructs an O(N)-apply 1-quasiseparable block-diagonal matrix whose all four arbitrary parity-selected half-size submatrices are full rank. A multi-stage obstruction needs multiscale information; a one-cut rank argument cannot just be repeated.

**3. New O(N)-work HIGH cut-rank global stage.** `MIXED_ORDER_GREEN.md` constructs `M= P T^-1 P^T + T^-1/20`, P parity-order and T=tridiag(-1,3,-1), and optional rational reflection H and unit complex phase diagonals. It has provably O(N) arithmetic, full density of M, rank(M[upper-half,right-half])>=N/2-1, rank(HM[upper-half,right-half])>=N/2-2, κ2(HM)<=7 and κ1(HM)>=4(N-1)/175. These all-N proofs escape cut-rank and 1-condition barriers of the prior narrow stage family. Exact modular finite audits show full stage and inverse density for N=8,16,32,64, but density of the inverse is not proved for all N. The Householder condition gain may be a gauge, not useful Fourier compression.

**4. Verified executable artifacts.** The subfolder contains source-only finite-field tests (`test_halfcut.py`, `test_mixed_order.py`, `test_three_stage_control.py`), updated original weaker tests, vector-level checks, and a separate NumPy condition audit. Core checks use standard library; they are audits, not replacements for algebraic proof.
