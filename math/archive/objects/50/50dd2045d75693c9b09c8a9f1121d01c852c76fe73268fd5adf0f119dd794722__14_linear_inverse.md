## Mathematical and operational scope

Linear-work inverse of the even-size parity-mixed Green operator, conditional on modular pivots and a rank-two correction determinant being invertible. The inverse is implemented through M^-1=T*(T+eps*PTP^T)^-1*PTP^T; conjugation by the interleaving order yields a pentadiagonal core plus a symmetric rank-two wrap edge.

## Control/dataflow and algebra

PentadiagonalLDLT stores two subdiagonals and inverse pivots. ParityGreenInverse precomputes two core solves, then inverts the 2x2 Woodbury matrix whose offdiagonal -1 terms reflect the negative swap correction. apply gathers interleaved coordinates, multiplies by T, solves the correction, scatters and multiplies T again. Orientation agrees with the forward even/odd gather/scatter map.

## Input/guard and acceptance review

Even N>=4 and vector lengths are asserted. inv rejects zero, but Fermat exponent p-2 requires prime p and is silently incorrect in general composite rings. Pentadiagonal constructor has no standalone positive-n guard. eps choices may create singular operators or modular zero pivots; no pivoting alternative. The docstring correctly notes real SPD does not prevent modular pivot failure.

## Cost accounting

O(N) setup storage/work plus O(N) modular inversions (bit cost depends on p); subsequent apply uses O(N) arithmetic and memory. Independent of the dense appearance of the inverse, this is a linear-time operator application, not a FFT.
