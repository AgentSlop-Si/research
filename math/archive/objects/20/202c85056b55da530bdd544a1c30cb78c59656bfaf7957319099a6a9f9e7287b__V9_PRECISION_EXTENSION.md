# Explicit finite-precision transfer to the v9 antiperiodic scan

9 October 2026. This proves a specified implementation variant, not the uploaded NumPy or C++ programs. The v8 master already had a full error theorem; here the direct seeds are replaced by half a shared scan total.

## 1. General implementation contract and deterministic ledger

Let X=||x||_2, N=KM, with exact represented input x. Use:

1. exact modular integer indices and phases, modulo 2N when the odd-length parity twist is present;
2. a root table with certified absolute complex error at most t, and normalizers with relative error at most t;
3. complex addition/subtraction residual at most `t(|v|+|w|)`, and multiplication residual at most `t|v||w|`;
4. balanced pairwise block summaries;
5. a normalized K-FFT, followed by scaling by 1/sqrt(M), yielding H=D/sqrt(N);
6. forcing `F_m=H_(m mod K)-b_m H_((m+aM) mod K)`;
7. gauged forcing `q_j=F_(m_j) conjugate(A_j)`; do not numerically divide by the approximate root;
8. a balanced scan whose total and every prefix have addition-expression depth at most `2 ceil(log2 N)`, with each original term occurring at most once in each expression;
9. seed equal to half the computed total, exact binary halving; subtract prefix, then multiply by the stored gauge.

Assume range handling avoids invalid underflow/overflow and zero/subnormal exceptions to these contracts. Store a total before resetting the scan root. The same computed values may contribute to both total and prefix; no independence is needed or assumed.

Define `J=ceil(log2 N)`, `j_M=ceil(log2 M)`, and

    c = (1+t)^2-1,
    a = (1+c)(1+t)^j_M-1.

Suppose the normalized short FFT on its supplied input z satisfies

    ||computed U_K z-U_K z||_2 <= b ||z||_2.

This is a standard forward-error contract for that FFT implementation. It does not pretend that summary input perturbations are proportional to the exact summary norm. Set

    h = (1+a)(1+b)(1+c)-1,
    f = 2h+c(1+h)+t(2+c)(1+h),
    q = (1+c)f+2c,
    gamma = (1+t)^(2J)-1,
    P = q+gamma(2+q).

Then every output has deterministic error at most

    { (3/2)(1+c)(1+t) P sqrt(NM)
      + [3t(1+c)+c] M } X.                         (A)

This formula is valid for odd prime powers too whenever the specified K-FFT supplies b. A uniform O(t log K) short-FFT guarantee gives the source's O(t log N sqrt(NM) X) row bound, with the FFT's actual constants carried through. It is not a certification of an unspecified prime-radix implementation or of a free root oracle.

### Proof

The exact block map has norm M, and exact summaries satisfy `||d||_2<=sqrt(M)X`. Phase and multiplication errors together have coefficient error at most c. Pairwise accumulation gives

    ||computed d-d||_2 <= a sqrt(M)X.

The normalized FFT and the normalization factor then give

    ||computed H-H||_2 <= h X,   ||H||_2<=X.

Over the full unique cycle, every H index appears exactly M times in each of the two forcing terms. Therefore

    sum |F_m| <= 2M ||H||_1 <= 2 sqrt(NM)X.

The same repetition count, propagated H error, boundary phase/product error and subtraction residual imply

    sum |computed F-F| <= f sqrt(NM)X.

Multiplication by the conjugate gauge yields

    sum |computed q-q| <= q sqrt(NM)X,
    sum |computed q| <= (2+q) sqrt(NM)X.

Every total/prefix addition expression has depth at most 2J. The triangle inequality on its rounding tree gives error at most

    E = P sqrt(NM)X

for the total and separately for every prefix. Seed error is at most E/2 because halving is exact. Even though total and prefix errors are correlated, their difference has error at most 3E/2. There is no statistical averaging of numerical errors.

The exact seed is Y0, so its magnitude is at most MX. An exact prefix is `Y0-Y_(m_j)/A_j`, of magnitude at most 2MX. Thus the final subtraction residual is at most `t(3MX+3E/2)`. Finally, stored-gauge multiplication has coefficient error c and amplification 1+c, while the exact final ungauged difference has magnitude at most MX. Combining these bounds gives (A).

The source's contract saying an FFT bound proportional to ||d|| includes summary input perturbation is too strong if d is the exact summary vector: d may vanish by cancellation while its computed perturbation does not. The valid bound is an FFT error on the represented summary input plus propagation of its independently bounded perturbation. The h ledger above performs exactly that accounting.

## 2. Explicit dyadic constant

For dyadic N>=4, M>=2, K>=M, let L=log2 N. A normalized radix-2 butterfly `(v +/- w z)/sqrt(2)` has per-layer normwise error at most

    e = c+(1+c)[c+t(2+c)].

Its exact layer is unitary. Thus take `b=(1+e)^(log2 K)-1`. In (A), set J=L and `j_M=log2 M`.

If `tL<=0.01`, then the following conservative bounds hold:

    c<=3t, e<=10t, a<=3Lt, b<=11Lt,
    h<=20Lt, f<=44Lt, q<=48Lt,
    gamma<=3Lt, P<=56Lt.

For example `a<=exp[(log2 M+3)t]-1<=3Lt` because `log2 M<=L/2`, L>=2; the remaining estimates follow by substitution, `t<=0.005`, and `Lt<=0.01`. The bound for h follows from `(1+3Lt)(1+11Lt)(1+1.5Lt)-1<20Lt`; f and q then follow directly. Since `M<=sqrt(NM)`, substituting these estimates in (A) gives a coefficient less than 89 Lt. Hence the convenient certified statement is

    max_m |computed Y_m-Y_m|
       <= 100 sqrt(NM) L t ||x||_2.                 (B)

With certified roots/normalizers and conservative complex-operation contracts `t=8u`,

    max_m |computed Y_m-Y_m|
       <= 800 sqrt(NM) log2(N) u ||x||_2,
    8u log2(N)<=0.01.                               (C)

This preserves the prior audit's safe constant despite the enlarged v9 parameter range K>=M. It is a sufficient upper bound, not a sharp error constant. The independent checker evaluates the exact ledger at 988 parameter points, including the allowed largest tL; these numerical checks supplement the analytic bound.

## 3. Preparation, grids, range and bit costs remain charged

A working-precision root table generated by a chain of products can accumulate O(Nu) error, so it does not automatically satisfy the root contract. Reuse the constructive guard-precision method from the prior audit, replacing N by the table length (2N if needed): a certified primitive root at guard precision v, with `v<=u/(128N)`, followed by O(N) guard products and rounding to working precision, supplies O(u) uniform root error with fixed safety margins. The initial root and normalizers require certified preparation; interval evaluation of pi, sine/cosine and reciprocal square roots gives a conservative polylogarithmic-bit setup cost at O(log N)-bit requested precision. No free transcendental operation is inferred from the uploaded exp/sin/cos calls.

Use a certified norm envelope B>=||x||_2 and inflate a common scalar radius r to contain both ideal-row tolerance `g sqrt(M/N) B` and the deterministic computed-row error. For a dyadic represented radius r, exact classification can use

    cell_k(z) = floor((3z-7kr)/(21r)),  k=0,1,2,

performed by exact signed integer division after common-lattice scaling. The candidate center is proportional to the integer `42 cell_k+14k+21` with common factor r/6; compare these integers before rounding the selected output. Approximate division followed by unverified floor is not covered. A certified conversion to a fixed-point lattice is allowed if its worst error is added to r. Final center rounding needs its own output allowance.

Under these conditions,

    Pr[complex max error > (5/sqrt(2)) r + eta_out]
       <= 2N exp[-R D(1/2||g^-2)].

The ideal rows remain independent; computed rows need not be unbiased. Their deterministic numerical errors are bounded on each outcome. To make (C) at most `theta g sqrt(M/N)B`, it is sufficient that

    u <= min{1/[800 log2 N], theta g/[800 N log2 N]}.

Thus O(log N) mantissa bits suffice in the stated fixed-row and growing-row regimes with bounded theta parameters. Guard precision is also O(log N). This is a bounded-word arithmetic claim, not an input-independent bit-linear algorithm. An arbitrary real input with arbitrarily many significant bits cannot be read at that cost.

A concrete bounded-input model uses real/imaginary components `2^e z_j` with O(log N)-bit signed integers z_j and charged common exponent e. Exact sum of squares supplies a constant-factor dyadic norm envelope in O(N) bounded-word operations; rescale by a power of two, handle zero input separately, and provide sufficient exponent range. Other input lattices/ranges must pay for alignment and representation. Input conversion from x to x' adds `||x-x'||_2` to the final DFT error.

If C_arith(w) is the bit cost of a w-bit operation, charge at least

    O(R[N+K log K] C_arith(w) + N C_arith(w_guard)
      + polynomial(w_guard+log N) + input/output bit costs)

and O(Nw) bits of storage, plus any wider inputs/exponents. At unrestricted R the counters need O(log R) bits. The three-grid arithmetic model and the word model are therefore explicitly distinguished.

The supplied C++ sequential summary accumulations, NumPy mixed precision, ordinary root calls, approximate grids and uncertified norms do not establish this implementation contract. The theorem is a constructive variant to implement and verify, not a retrospective certificate of those programs.
