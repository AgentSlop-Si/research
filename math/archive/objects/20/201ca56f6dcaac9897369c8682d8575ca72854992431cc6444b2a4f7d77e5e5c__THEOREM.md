# Phase 38 — Joint complex-mask elimination in noncommuting Green Fourier circuits

**Date:** 2026-10-10  
**Research state:** A new exact, small-degree obstruction for a *fixed six-Green network* with a freely chosen middle coupling, **two completely free complex internal diagonal masks**, and **two arbitrary internal permutations**. There is still **no general-input `o(N log N)` FFT**.

## 1. Context and exact architecture

Let `N=2^m`, and let `F_N` denote the unnormalized `N x N` Fourier matrix, with entries `zeta_N^(jk)` where `zeta_N=exp(2*pi*i/N)` (the sign choice is immaterial for the obstruction). Fix the six independently ordered cyclic Green kernels from Phase 37,

`G_s=(I-alpha_s W_s)^(-1)` for `s=0,...,5`,

with cycle orders `seeded_cycles(N,37037)` and constants

```
alpha = [(1+i)/4, (2+i)/7, (1+2i)/8, (1-i)/5, (2+i)/9, (1+i)/6]
wA=(1+i)/3, wC=(1-i)/4
A=G0+wA*G1, C=G4+wC*G5, B(lambda)=G2+lambda*G3.
```

The proposed circuit is

`F_N = A * diag(u) * P1 * B(lambda) * P2 * diag(v) * C`,

where **every entry** of `u,v` can be any nonzero complex number, `P1,P2` are arbitrary permutations, and `lambda` can be any complex scalar. The outer sums `A,C`, the six cycle orders, and all six individual resolvent couplings `alpha_s` are FIXED. The question is whether any choice of these remaining degrees of freedom yields an exact DFT.

Both outer mixers are invertible at all certified sizes: their determinants remain nonzero after reduction at the indicated primes, so the corresponding determinants are nonzero in characteristic zero.

Write the transformed target

`T=A^(-1)*F_N*C^(-1)`.

Exact Fourier equality is now equivalent to

`T=diag(u)*P1*B(lambda)*P2*diag(v)`.

This is an exact algebraic reduction, not a numerical approximation.

## 2. The key theorem: two masks disappear from the equations

Suppose `T` and `B` are matrices over a field with nonzero entries in the relevant anchor positions. Consider a fixed ordered pair of distinct row labels `(r0,r1)` and ordered triple of distinct column labels `(c0,c1,c2)` of `B`. For `j=1,2`, define

```
f_j(lambda) = T[1,j]*T[0,0]
               * B(lambda)[r1,c0]*B(lambda)[r0,cj]
             - T[1,0]*T[0,j]
               * B(lambda)[r1,cj]*B(lambda)[r0,c0].
```

**Theorem 38A.** If the proposed Fourier factorization holds with arbitrary nonzero `u,v` and permutations, then `f_1(lambda)=f_2(lambda)=0` for the corresponding row-pair/column-triple mapping. Since each entry of `B(lambda)` is affine linear in `lambda`, both `f_j` have degree at most **2**, *regardless of N*.

**Proof.** For `T_ij=u_i B_{r_i,c_j} v_j`, multiplying `T_ij*T_00` and `T_i0*T_0j` yields a common factor `u_i*u_0*v_j*v_0`. Multiplying by the opposite products of the `B` entries cancels these factors identically. Inserting `B(lambda)=G2+lambda G3` produces the displayed quadratic polynomials. A candidate factorization must make every such identity hold. This identity uses no logarithms, no floating-point phase approximation, and no division by potentially zero candidate entries.

A nonzero Sylvester resultant `Res_lambda(f_1,f_2)` excludes all common roots, including roots in any algebraic extension of the base field. In ascending coefficient order, write

`f=a0+a1 lambda+a2 lambda^2`, `g=b0+b1 lambda+b2 lambda^2`.

Their degree-two Sylvester determinant is explicitly

```
Res(f,g) = (a2*b0-a0*b2)^2
         - (a2*b1-a1*b2)*(a1*b0-a0*b1).
```

**Corollary 38B.** If this resultant is nonzero for **every ordered pair of distinct middle row labels** and **every ordered triple of distinct middle column labels**, no arbitrary `P1,P2,u,v,lambda` can satisfy the target Fourier factorization. Counting all relevant mappings needs only

`N*(N-1) * N*(N-1)*(N-2) = N^2*(N-1)^2*(N-2)`

checks, rather than enumerating `N!^2` complete permutations. The target witness always uses rows `(0,1)` and columns `(0,1,2)`; all internal permutations are accounted for by their possible preimage labels.

The number of necessary equations is constant (two quadratics) even though the masks have `2N` unconstrained complex entries.

## 3. Exact finite-characteristic audit and characteristic-zero lifting

The cycle orders and Green coefficients are **rational functions in roots of unity**: for each `N` they belong to `K=Q(zeta_N)`, with `i=zeta_N^(N/4)` for `N` divisible by 4. Choose primes splitting this field:

- `p1=998244353`, generator `3`;
- `p2=2013265921`, generator `31`.

The consistent field reduction sends `zeta_N -> generator^((p-1)/N)`, hence `i -> generator^((p-1)/4)`. All used denominators and outer determinants are units in the reductions for the tested instances.

If a resultant over `K` were zero, **every valid field reduction** would also make it zero. Therefore a **single nonzero modular resultant** is enough to certify the characteristic-zero resultant is nonzero. There is no need to recover its enormous numerator as an algebraic integer. Conversely, a *zero* modular resultant may arise from an accidental finite-field root, without implying any common complex root.

The independent finite-field exact results are:

| N | ordered mappings per field | p1 zero resultants | p2 zero resultants | all complex choices excluded? |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|:---:|
| 4 | 288 | 0 | 0 | yes |
| 8 | 18,816 | 0 | 0 | yes |
| 16 | 806,400 | 0 | 0 | yes |
| 32 | 29,521,920 | 0 | 0 | yes |
| 64 | 1,007,935,488 | 0 | **1** | **yes, entirely by p1** |

The `N=64`, `p2` exception is precisely `(r0,r1;c0,c1,c2)=(53,55;19,26,5)`. Its Sylvester resultant in `p2` is zero, and its modular gcd is linear. **The very same mapping has nonzero resultant `470304279` in `p1`.** Hence it is a *modular coincidence*, not an admissible complex coupling. We document it to distinguish exact characteristic-zero conclusions from an incorrect demand that every prime's reduction be nonzero.

At `N=128,256` the fixed-order `(r0,r1;c0,c1,c2)=(0,1;0,1,2)` resultants are nonzero at both primes. Additionally, **200,000 dispersed ordered mapping samples per size per prime** had nonzero resultants. These are *sampled*, not exhaustive, and do not justify an all-permutation theorem at those sizes.

At `N=4`, an independent **direct `Q(i)` SymPy calculation** computes the outer inverses, both quadratic equations and their gcd in characteristic zero. Their gcd is `1`. This is not inferred from the modular reduction.

**Conclusion at the five exhaustive sizes:** For this exact fixed family, not one complex middle coupling, not one pair of freely chosen nonzero complex masks, and not one pair of intermediate permutations can compute `F_N`. This is an exact, finite-size, family-specific exclusion.

## 4. Constructive half of the argument: synthesize both masks

For a chosen complex coupling and row/column ordering, write `B=P1 B(lambda) P2`. If the first row and column of `T,B` contain no zeros, there is an explicit, unique-up-to-common-scaling choice of two diagonal masks that matches `2N-1` entries:

```
v[j] = T[0,j]/B[0,j]
u[i] = T[i,0]/( B[i,0]*v[0] ).
```

The first row and column of `diag(u) B diag(v)` now match the transformed target exactly. It is a complete factorization **if and only if** all other `(N-1)^2` entries also match. Equivalently, the complete set of projective cross-ratios must agree.

For the Phase 37 family, fixed `lambda=7` and the identity intermediate permutations, we obtain:

| N | exactly calibrated T entries | mismatching interior entries |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 8 | 15 | 49/49 |
| 32 | 63 | 961/961 |
| 128 | 255 | 16129/16129 |
| 256 | 511 | 65025/65025 |

This is an **online linear-work calculation only after the needed target anchor entries have been provided**. Constructing `T=A^-1 F C^-1` is not known to cost `O(N)`; the included certification code computes it by exact dense modular matrix inversion. The table is thus a *diagnostic*, not a faster Fourier executor.

A planted **valid** masked-and-permuted Fourier circuit passes the mask-recovery test and has zero quadratic resultant at its planted coupling. Its outer mixer is deliberately expensive (`F*T^(-1)`), ensuring the positive test does not claim an FFT speedup.

## 5. Operation accounting and limits

- Every cyclic Green solve `(I-alpha W)^(-1)` has a simple `O(N)` arithmetic recurrence. The six Green solves and two diagonal masks therefore have `O(N)` **online arithmetic cost once the structure and masks are specified**.
- The proof's exact outer inversions and transformed target computation use dense modular algorithms (`O(N^3)` in the current Python implementation), followed by `O(N^5)` ordered-witness checks if fully exhaustive. Those costs are **research certification**, not part of a successful FFT (because this specific candidate is excluded).
- In the claimed impossibility result **both** internal masks are allowed arbitrary nonzero complex values, and **both** internal permutations are arbitrary. We do **not** vary the outer Green sum parameters, cycle orders, or the two middle Green generator couplings. `lambda` is the only scalar pencil parameter.
- No uniform all-`N` theorem for this fixed family is proved here. No general lower bound for FFT complexity follows. More stages or additional independently varying Green coefficients may escape this gate.
- This is a project-specific finite-model synthesis/exclusion result. Diagonal equivalence, four-entry projective cross-ratios and their invariance have established prior art.
