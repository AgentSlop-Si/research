# Audit result

**ACCEPT.** The proposed exact logarithmic branch obstruction is proved.
No counterexample or substantive gap was found. The constants need no repair.

- Arbitrary invertible column-two Q, independent monomial-support endpoints:
  N<=12r36^r for every complex flat unitary, r>=1.
- Added arbitrary diagonal: N<=48r36^r, r>=1.
- General half-balanced vertex-separator budget q>=1: N<=4qr36^r,
  or N<=16qr36^r with a diagonal. Lift treewidth w suffices with q=w+1.
- Quantitative approximation statements are separately proved in Section 8
  of SEALED_PROOF.md. They exclude vanishing error with fixed branch count;
  they do not give logarithmic branch growth for fixed positive tolerance.

## Every proposed step checked

1. The row-support multigraph is componentwise unicyclic: accepted by column
   independence and e=v. Loops and parallel edges are handled correctly.
2. The symmetric bipartite lift is a pseudoforest: accepted. Singleton loops
   become leaves, so the corresponding lift component is a tree.
3. Weighted separator of size at most three: accepted. The proof explicitly
   handles disconnected graphs, zero weights, and weight assignment to bags.
4. Endpoint routing and singular endpoint scalings: accepted. The lower and
   upper vertex maps are injective and disjoint; all positions can be marked
   even if their endpoint scalar is zero.
5. Inverse complementary-rank orientation: accepted exactly as proposed.
   B^c supplies the rows and A^c supplies the columns. An elementary kernel
   proof is included; no principal-block nonsingularity is silently used.
6. Rank at most |X| across separated inverse sides: accepted. The zero block
   leaves at most |X| exceptional rows and |X| exceptional columns.
7. Trimming and partitioning components: accepted. m>=12 implies
   m<=4(m-3)/3. Each side can be made to retain total weight at least (m-3)/3.
8. Two-color orientation: accepted with a complete four-case contradiction.
9. Nested selections with unrelated branch routings: accepted. All earlier
   rank bounds survive further restrictions of target indices.
10. Floor recurrence: accepted, including the deliberately loose 9/5 error.
11. Flat-unitary block rank: accepted by singular-value/Frobenius comparison;
    arbitrary composite Fourier orders cause no problem.
12. 12r36^r and 48r36^r: accepted for all positive integer r. The proof checks
    the step-validity condition before invoking the recurrence. Odd N in the
    diagonal case is explicitly handled.

The only minor statement-boundary clarification is to specify r>=1. With
no branch and a free diagonal, N=1 is a trivial representation and is not
covered by a right side proportional to r.

## Important antecedent correction

The inherited Hamiltonian separation theorem already supplies a logarithmic
bound and an operator-norm gap. Merely allowing arbitrarily many disjoint
bare cycle blocks also follows from that ordered theorem by concatenating
block orders. Therefore the useful added coverage is arbitrary attached-tree
column-two supports and, more generally, bounded-separator inverse families.
Do not market this as a new Hamiltonian theorem or as an unrestricted FFT
lower bound. External priority of the combined application remains unresolved.
