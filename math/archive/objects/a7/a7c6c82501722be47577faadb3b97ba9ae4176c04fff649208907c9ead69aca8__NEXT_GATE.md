# Hero #1 — Phase 28 research gates

The central target remains an **independently verified all-input scalar algorithm** outside simple CRT/Winograd regrouping. Model separation matters:

1. **Complex exact arithmetic:** a published September 2026 manuscript claims `O(N (log N)^(1−10^−13))` for every length under unrestricted coefficients. The public Lean comparator `UniformFourier.lean` still contains `sorry` placeholders on its two headline theorems. Verify the mathematics; don't claim finished formal certification. To surpass the *claimed* benchmark, exhibit a larger rigorous saving or substantially better numerical/bit complexity.
2. **Root-rich finite fields:** our `15·2^t` circuit is exact with `Theta(NlogN)` data-field cost. A future sub-`NlogN` bound must explicitly quantify how the prime and root family grow with `N`, how constants and setup are constructed, and whether it is uniform over primes/lengths. Complex lower bounds cannot simply be transported into `F_p`.
3. **Precision-stable FFT:** demand a full floating-point error/conditioning theorem and runtime benchmark before saying faster than native FFT. A single field multiplication is not a machine-level scalar operation for growing `p`.

## Independent candidate A: cross-factor nonlocal sharing

The cross-ratio invariant proves that coprime CRT permutation/scaling is the complete route to a **twiddle-free tensor** solely by monomial equivalence. For `gcd(A,B)>1`, any gain must use nonmonomial mixing, nonlocal shared intermediates or different scalar coefficients. Try exact `8,16,32` codelets outside radix-2/split-radix, enumerate their **full** scalar addition/multiplication networks, and compare to `29,81,?` optimized dyadic costs (for `N=8`, `C2=29`; `N=16`, `C2=81`; `N=32`, `C2=209`). Do not count a black-box high-arity transform as one operation.

## Independent candidate B: factor-tree auto-synthesis

Search CRT factor trees for `N=15G`, `N=21G` or smooth squarefree odd multipliers. Charge complete Winograd kernels, cold coefficient preparation, root tables, and routing. A single CRT split saves at most `(A−1)(B−1)<N` cross multiplications, so constant-factor improvements from fixed-depth CRT alone **cannot** change the leading `N log N` order. Reject purported asymptotic improvements that simply sum ordinary twiddle removals without paying inner transforms.

## Independent candidate C: forcing and linear-circuit sharing

Join Phase 17–24 Green forcing with a *generic* cross-block circuit, not a sparse-defect promise. Test a complete basis and random full-support vectors. If the Green map and its inverse are fast, the forcing map must still beat Fourier on all inputs. Keep all coefficients and precision costs paid. Phase 16's conjugation obstruction remains applicable.

## Verification gates for every new candidate

- Explicit algebraic field, root supply, and operation model.
- Entire scalar graph for every higher-arity gate, not a gate-name proxy.
- Exact all-basis finite-field checks (where the field model applies), plus comparison to independent DFT formulas.
- Cold setup, memory, index work and word size charged separately.
- Identity-multiplication and sign conventions identical on both sides.
- No generic `o(NlogN)` claim from structured-input passes, constant-factor CRT savings, or a published theorem with unresolved formal assumptions.
