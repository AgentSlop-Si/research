# Scoped barriers introduced in v14

## Variance-plus-median conservation

For prime lengths under the unequal-block row:

- one-row work: `Theta(N log B)`;
- per-coordinate variance: `Theta(1/B)`.

Chebyshev plus coordinatewise median plus a union bound over `N` outputs requires `R log B = Omega(log N)` for a fixed error threshold and constant total failure probability. The resulting certified work is `Omega(N log N)`.

This is a barrier for that proof pipeline only. It is not a lower bound on the actual estimator, alternative robust procedures, or Fourier circuits.

## Exact row-combination rank

Each row restricts to rank one on every input block. A weighted sum of `R` rows has block rank at most `R`. Reproducing the identity on a length-`L` block requires `R >= L`.

This excludes exact recovery by a constant weighted average of existing rows. It does not exclude joint nonlinear evaluation or a different projector family.

## Fixed-precision stability

The order-`B` recurrence is exact but its characteristic polynomial may have exponentially large coefficients. The saved binary64 implementation becomes unstable for the larger tested `B`. The exact finite-field certificate validates algebra, not binary64 stability.
