# Prior-art boundary — v19

This file records only the nearest known ingredients; it is not a novelty proof.

1. **Roots-of-unity filters.** The character-packet identity is an elementary roots-of-unity filter / partial-fraction identity. The project contribution is applying it as an exact Green-bank fusion test and then auditing the resulting Fourier expressivity.

2. **Quasiseparable and semiseparable matrices.** Low off-diagonal rank, generator representations and linear-complexity algorithms are a substantial established literature (Eidelman, Gohberg, Olshevsky, Vandebril and others). The v19 rational-scan rank proof is an elementary recurrence specialization, not a claim to have introduced quasiseparable rank.

3. **Chebotarev Fourier minors.** The prime-order statement that every square Fourier minor is nonzero is classical. A modern route is Terence Tao, *An uncertainty principle for cyclic groups of prime order*, arXiv:math/0308286. The v19 theorems use it only to turn low-rank rectangles into exact prime-length contradictions.

4. **Treewidth and balanced separators.** The existence of small balanced separators for bounded-treewidth graphs is classical. v19 combines that fact with an elementary inverse-block factorization and Chebotarev to obtain the stated direct Fourier-inverse gate.

No search performed here establishes that the exact compound packet/separator formulation is new. No external priority claim should be made without specialist review.
