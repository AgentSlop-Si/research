# Provenance and testing

- Phase 17 directly continues the user's Phase 16 `fft_hero0_phase16_gauge_curvature_2026-10-10.zip`. A byte-identical copy is bundled as `prior_phase16_bundle.zip`.
- Mathematical normalizer and phase-ratio bounds in `THEOREMS.md` were derived as exact identities, with finite tests used as validation rather than as substitutes for proof.
- Exact GF(65537) checks use a primitive root and modular Gaussian elimination; no tolerance in those checks.
- Complex spectral checks use NumPy/SciPy double precision; the exact four-dimensional Gram characteristic polynomial is checked symbolically by SymPy.
- Randomized numeric tests have fixed seeds. They are not evidence of arbitrary-input FFT acceleration.
- The new spectral/approximation results are architecture-specific. Their novelty has not been established against all prior works.
- Prior art: *Equivalence of complex Hadamard matrices*, Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics (2026), https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10801-026-01568-x ; the paper classifies Fourier automorphisms.
