# Prior art and novelty scope

- Shmuel Winograd, *On computing the Discrete Fourier Transform*, PNAS 1976: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC430186/ . Short transforms and diagonal multiplicative decompositions are decades-old foundational ideas.
- Winograd, *On the multiplicative complexity of the discrete Fourier transform*, Advances in Mathematics 1979: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0001870879900379 . Not a newly discovered diagonal multiplicative-complexity model.
- *On the Derivation of Winograd-Type DFT Algorithms for Input Sequences Whose Length Is a Power of Two*, Electronics 2022, section with an 8-point diagonal of four unit coefficients and four nonunit coefficients: https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/11/9/1342 . Phase 29 independently reconstructed and verified a compatible positive-root 8-point codelet, not claimed to invent it.
- Burrus, *Fast Fourier Transforms*, Winograd and prime-factor algorithms: https://eng.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Electrical_Engineering/Signal_Processing_and_Modeling/Fast_Fourier_Transforms_%28Burrus%29/06%3A_Winograd%27s_Short_DFT_Algorithms/6.02%3A_Winograd_Fourier_Transform_Algorithm_%28WFTA%29 . Classic tables use a different arithmetic convention, especially for `±i`.
- Steven G. Johnson and Matteo Frigo, *A modified split-radix FFT with fewer arithmetic operations*, 2007: https://www.fftw.org/~athena/abstracts/abstract5.html . Their real flop-count gains are not directly interchangeable with prime-field charged multiplications.
- https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/ComparatorChallenges/UniformFourier.lean . The public exact-complexity theorem declarations inspected in Phase 27 used `sorry` placeholders. This does not refute any separate manuscript but limits what that Lean file independently certifies.

**Claims in this package:** exact all-input scalar-circuit cost improvement against the specifically preserved Phase 28 comparator, a restricted C4-free gauge bound, and a restricted weighted-four fixed-rational diagonal-width lower bound. Neither global optimality, practical speed superiority nor historical first-discovery status is asserted.
