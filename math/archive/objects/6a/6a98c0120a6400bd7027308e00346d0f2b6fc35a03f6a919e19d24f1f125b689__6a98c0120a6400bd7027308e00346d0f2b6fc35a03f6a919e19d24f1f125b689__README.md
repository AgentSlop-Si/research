# Phase 26 — Simultaneous modular cuts

Research continuation of Phase25's Green-ordering problem, dated October 10, 2026.

**Main checked result:** For a product of sums of weighted cyclic Green inverses whose orders have at most `d` affine step magnitudes and at most `b` exceptional edges per term, with diagonals and intermediate *affine* coordinate permutations allowed, the exact normalized DFT requires `Q=Omega(log N / log log N)` Green terms. This is an **architecture-specific term-count** bound, not an FFT arithmetic lower bound.

The all-size structural inequality is in `THEOREM.md`; the fully reproducible exact modular controls are in `phase26_cuts.py`, `tests/test_phase26.py`, and `audit.py`. `audit.json` contains recorded results. `CHECKPOINT.md` describes where Phase 27 should begin. `PRIOR_ART.md` distinguishes old separator theory from the project-specific circuit argument.
