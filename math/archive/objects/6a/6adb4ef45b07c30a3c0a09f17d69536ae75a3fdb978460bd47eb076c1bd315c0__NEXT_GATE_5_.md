# Next exact FFT construction gate — solve exceptional coefficient loci

**Target:** Find or disprove an all-length, phase-correct four-Green / six-Green factorization with strictly counted sub-NlogN arithmetic. Avoid rediscovering cache optimization, CrocSwap bit-frame tuning, or ordinary split-radix.

1. **For the four-Green case with free endpoint input/output diagonals**, define `T_j = A diag(B[:,0]/B[:,j]) A^-1 Delta^j` when the conditions apply. Every exact candidate must satisfy `det([T_1,T_2])=0`. A single generic nonzero specialization proves generic failure only; it is not a no-go for special coefficient choices.
2. **Solve the exceptional commutator locus**, not random numerical samples. For fixed N and permutations, clear denominators in `det([T_1,T_2])=0` and combine with full joint-eigenvector conditions for `T_j`, j>=1, including no-zero-coordinate constraints. Divide out denominator/resultant artifacts and treat zero-pattern branches separately.
3. **Use exact cyclotomic/finite-field certificates carefully.** A nonzero modular reduction proves a characteristic-zero algebraic number is nonzero for fixed integral parameters. Absence of finite-field solutions does not automatically prove no complex solution. Numerical residuals cannot certify exact equality.
4. **For N=4,8,16**, select architectures that survive both dyadic and moving-interface rank bounds. Search the exceptional coefficient equations with Gröbner/elimination or algebraic normal forms. Give explicit algebraic solutions and a proof for an entire length family, not only a fitted N=8 case.
5. **If a positive solution emerges**, check Fourier Weyl covariance and the seed exactly, then prove generation cost of every cycle permutation, coefficient, and root of unity. Applying each supplied weighted Green inverse is O(N) arithmetic, but finding an all-length valid coefficient family may be much harder.
6. **For higher-depth networks**, use the linear lift `Z_{i_1,...,i_r}=product of the r interface gauges` and ask whether the Fourier-covariance linear subspace intersects the all-nonzero Segre torus; the present two-gauge case is the first nontrivial step.

**Stop rule:** Do not announce exact O(N) FFT without an all-N explicit factorization, a complete charged recurrence, and an independent exact verifier. A stronger restricted obstruction is a valid theorem but not the requested ultimate speedup.
