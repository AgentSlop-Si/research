# Phase-13 audit and four-archive inventory

## Main finding

The uploaded phase-13 algebra and all its finite modular certificates check
out within their stated scope. Its Haar replacement escapes the fixed-p norm
test, but a different all-N obstruction closes that replacement as a
sublogarithmic-stage Fourier factorization. See HAAR_GREEN_ENTROPY_GATE.md:

    t >= N log_2 N/(11N-12)

for the Haar–Green stages, including arbitrary real N-dependent strengths,
permuted paths, inverse stages and intervening unitary monomials. This uses
an exact O(N) unitary-gate representation of each tridiagonal Cayley and Haar
rotation, then the established matrix-entropy lower bound. It neither proves
an unrestricted FFT lower bound nor excludes an algebraically fused executor.

   Laplace integral. The condition in its first displayed premise should
   read |alpha| Delta delta<1, consistent with its denominator and proof.
   This is a minor sign typo; all concrete strengths in the theorem are
   positive and its inverse proof correctly uses the absolute value.

   K=1.0422599480841894, yielding
   t>=0.9303428473847462 log_2 N. The attachment's rounded constants are

   for this fixed architecture, but does not cover unbounded strengths.
3. The Haar replacement's unitarity, linear work, norm growth, and transfer
   of the old small-perturbation cut-rank theorem are valid. Strong coupling
   does not inherit that perturbative cut-rank theorem, as the upload says.
4. The additive-repair bound rank(E)>=rank(AZ-DA)/2 is valid.
5. The diagonal-multiset condition for S2 Q P S1 to satisfy Fourier Weyl
   covariance is a correct necessary condition, including arbitrary
   invertible intermediate diagonal Q. It is not a sufficient condition.
6. Independent modular matrix reconstruction reproduced full defect rank N
   for both tree and Haar stages at N=8,16,32,64,128; all 30 saved two-stage
   multiset mismatch witnesses match exactly, including strong coupling.
7. The finite-field implementation selects sqrt(2)=zeta_8+zeta_8^(-1) and
   i=zeta_8^2 consistently with all Fourier roots. Denominators are nonzero.
   The omitted normalization is one common nonzero scalar and does not
   affect defect rank or conjugation. The finite nonzero witnesses therefore
   genuinely exclude the corresponding characteristic-zero instances.

## Synthesis value

Phase 13 refines which primitives are insufficient, rather than advancing
an exact arbitrary-input FFT executor. Fourier covariance remains useful
for finite falsification, but increasing stage mixing strength or replacing
the tree with Haar cannot evade the new stage-count theorem while all the
factors remain within this unitary family. Nonunitary gains, genuinely new
primitives, or a whole-word arithmetic fusion need separate analysis.

The frequency v5 and predecode v6 arbitrary-input results are approximate;
their exact claims need local Fourier sparsity. The scan-network material
has its own audit in this fusion workspace. Those research routes should
retain their separate input and computational assumptions rather than be
silently promoted to the exact arbitrary-input target.
