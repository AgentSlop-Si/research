## 1. Recognition is charged and sound

Use v_t[b]=x[bM+t], N=KM, K>=M. For an order-r circulant recurrence with s fixed dictionary shifts, form every raw equation

    v_t[b]=sum_(h=1)^r sum_d c_(h,d) v_(t-h)[b+d].

There are rs unknown coefficients and O(N) equations. Incremental elimination costs O((rs)^2 N) arithmetic, followed by independent full-input validation. Underdetermined coefficients may be chosen arbitrarily among solutions; the entire observed recurrence is still certified. Invalid inputs reject before specialized output. A proper all-input fallback adds ordinary FFT work and restores the ordinary worst-case bound.

For first-order unknown two-tap shift d, the three initial spectral ratios satisfy lambda_ell=c0+c1 omega_K^(ell d). Nonzero F_0 at the three requested frequencies, c1!=0 and d!=0 ensure a nonzero difference denominator. Then (lambda_2-lambda_1)/(lambda_1-lambda_0)=omega_K^d. An explicit K-root walk recovers d in O(K), after which all raw samples are certified. Degenerate pilots are not promised to be recognized. The scalar branch and rejection are legitimate; a favorable representation alone does not guarantee acceptance by this pilot.

For Weyl dynamics, each time step has s unknown controls and K equations. The same solver and full-coordinate verification cost O(s^2N), with at most O(sM) input-dependent pivot inversions overall. Coefficients may vary with time, but the dictionary itself is public and fixed in the theorem. Learning an arbitrary large dictionary is not included.

## 2. Exact output identities and exceptional cases

The polyphase identity

    X[ell+jK]=sum_t omega_N^(-(ell+jK)t) F_t[ell]

has the stated negative sign and output routing. The right-shift convention v[b]->v[b+d] has eigenvalue omega_K^(ell d), consistent with the source.

In the first-order case, F_t[ell]=lambda_ell^t F_0[ell]. The geometric ratio rho=lambda_ell omega_N^(-(ell+jK)) has rho^M=lambda_ell^M omega_K^(-ell), independent of j. At rho=1, the output is M F_0[ell], without division. Lambda_ell=0 is also harmless: the finite geometric polynomial has value one, not an undefined inverse eigenvalue. All nonzero denominators can share one batch inversion; the zero-denominator list is excluded from that batch.

For order 1<=r<=M, D(z)=1-sum_(h=1)^r b_h z^h and the stated front/back boundary polynomials satisfy exactly

    D(z) sum_(t=0)^(M-1) a_t z^t=P(z)+z^M Q(z).

The boundary indices are valid under r<=M. No recurrence coefficients beyond the certified range are assumed. At each output z_j, z_j^M is independent of j. D(0)=1 guarantees D is a nonzero polynomial with at most r distinct exceptional roots per channel. Direct evaluation of the original degree-(M-1) polynomial at those roots costs at most O(rKM)=O(rN), including repeated roots. A zero numerator and denominator cannot simply be assigned zero. The source correctly uses direct evaluation.

Generating all scalar recurrences costs O(rN); front/back preparation costs O(r^2K), absorbed by O(rN) for r<=M. The r initial K-FFTs, eigenvalue preparation, root tables, regular evaluations, global inversion and all outputs remain charged. With fixed r,s and M=Theta(log N), total accepted work is O(N), O(N) field words. Eligible dyadic lengths and supplied suitable roots remain part of the realization; this is not a free all-length padding theorem.

For W_(p,q)v[b]=omega_K^(qb)v[b+p], direct change of variable gives

    F(W_(p,q)v)[ell]=omega_K^((ell-q)p) F(v)[ell-q].

Thus one K-spectrum can be propagated through the learned noncommuting controls at O(sK) per time step. The source updates the whole current spectrum each time, not repeatedly the initial seed. K length-M transforms complete the exact output. The total bound O(s^2N+sN+K log K+N log M) is sound.

## 3. Scope and numerical/counter qualifications

The fixed test prime supports finitely many lengths. An asymptotic exact-field family requires supplied compatible roots/fields; their discovery and arbitrary bit costs are separate. Inverses may be field primitives in the algebraic model. A polynomial-size finite-field word implementation must additionally charge inverse and arithmetic word costs. Exact complex branching does not certify reliable floating rank or equality decisions.

The source counter is incomplete. Expressions pow(w_k,P-2,P) and pow(w_m,P-2,P) bypass the inversion counter; the Weyl output loop repeats the latter once per ell, K times. Root discovery, index operations and memory work are also not comprehensively represented by total(). These omissions weaken finite-size operation comparisons, not the algebraic theorem. Reuse the already generated negative-index root-table entries instead of repeatedly inverting fixed roots to obtain a fully charged O(N) setup. The explicitly counted NTT stage-power preparation must also be retained or replaced by charged table lookup. No practical speedup is established by the printed counters.

The three source witnesses establish nonacceptance by Phase7's fewer-than-M projective-template classes at its fixed chirp coordinates. Full ordinary or dechirped rank alone does not prove exclusion from every possible shift-corrected rank-r representation or every earlier structured algorithm. The confirmed eight distinct projective cyclic orbits are the relevant certificate for that specific template comparison.

## 4. An immediate growing-order corollary

The same proof need not hide growing r,s inside a constant. With r<=M, public s-shift dictionary, and M=Theta(log N), a conservative complete accepted-work bound is

    O(N[(rs)^2+r+1]),

and storage is O(N+(rs)^2). This includes the O(r^2K) boundary work because r<=M, all exceptional outputs, and all failed elimination work within the single attempt. Consequently rs=o(sqrt(log N)) gives an exact sub-N-log-N accepted-input path, subject to the same root/arithmetic contract. For fixed s this permits r=o(sqrt(log N)). This is an independently derived corollary of the displayed costs, not an improved generic-input theorem or a practical implementation benchmark. A failed recognition plus full FFT fallback remains ordinary worst-case work.

## 5. Capacity and dense-control barriers

The parameter counts are valid upper bounds even when representations are nonunique:

- Fixed first-order dictionary: at most q^(K+s) vectors over F_q
- One unknown shift: at most K q^(K+2)
- Order r: at most q^(rK+rs)
- Time-varying s-generator Weyl dynamics: at most q^(K+s(M-1))

Divide by q^N for a uniform-input bound. These count only the given generative families, not all possible Fourier circuits. Over C the analogous polynomial-parameter maps have dimension at most the parameter count; finitely many unknown shift choices do not remove the deficit.

The dense-control identity F(g.*u)=F(g)*cyclic F(u)/K is exact. The uniform-distribution argument requires a fixed u with every coordinate nonzero, so componentwise division is a bijection. Under that condition a uniform target v gives uniform g and uniform F(g). The probability of all spectral controls being nonzero is (1-1/q)^K. For 0<=s<=K, a union over size-s support sets gives at most binomial(K,s)q^(s-K) for support at most s. These are barriers to the sparse-control shortcut, not lower bounds on a different dense shared evaluator.
