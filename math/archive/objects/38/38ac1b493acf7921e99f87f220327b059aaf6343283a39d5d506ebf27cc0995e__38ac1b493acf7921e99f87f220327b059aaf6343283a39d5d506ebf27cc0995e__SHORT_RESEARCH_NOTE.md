# Centered v12: verified improvement and the next useful refinement

**9 October 2026. Scope: randomized approximate Fourier transforms.**

The centered v12 construction is mathematically sound in exact arithmetic. Moving the expansion center to the midpoint of the active input interval halves the largest Bessel argument. The convolution proof also correctly removes the expansion-rank factor from the local fusion error. Independent checks reproduce the headline N=511 comparison: the supplied 10^-4 deterministic-error proxy changes from 22,068 to 15,160. That is a proxy comparison, not a runtime benchmark.

## A sharper certificate

On 0<=z<=pi/2, the Bessel coefficients are nonnegative, and their two-sided absolute sum has the exact identity

    sum_(r in Z)|J_r(z)| = 1 + integral_0^z J_0(t) dt.

The alternating Bessel series therefore yields

    sum_(r in Z)|J_r(z)| <= 1+z-z^3/12+z^5/320 < 2.278.

This replaces v12's 4.25 fusion factor without changing the algorithm. It is a project-specific sharpening based on classical identities, with no claim of literature priority.

## A constructive local refinement

Center each occupied input block at its own midpoint. The largest local argument drops from D=pi(M-1)/Q to D/2. Complete blocks share one coefficient table; only the final partial block may need a second. The same decoder and shared-state covariance proof apply.

Combining the refinements gives

    deterministic error <= [tau_R(z_*) + B_*(z_*) A_H(D/2)] ||x||_2.

At N=511 and target 10^-8, this permits local halfwidth H=3 rather than H=4 at the same global rank R=11. The bound is approximately 6.3153e-9; the inherited proxy changes from 26,932 to 24,500. This variant has been algebraically specified and independently checked, not represented as an already-patched production program.

## The important numerical correction

A balanced product tree has logarithmic parallel depth, but its prefix products can accumulate O(Nu) multiplication error. Computing the two transition roots more accurately does not, by itself, fix that arithmetic error. A certified guard-precision or reanchored carrier needs its own ledger. The companion report supplies that analysis; the uploaded numerical-library implementation is still not certified.

## What remains unchanged

All R short FFTs and global decoder scans are still charged. The polynomially shrinking-error arithmetic order remains O(N log N/log log N), and the fixed-summary rank barrier still blocks uniform exact recovery within this architecture. The independent N=8 multicut screen also reproduces the negative result: 12 extra local constraints eliminate no additional permutations.

The useful outcome is a stronger, reproducible approximation certificate and a specified local variant, alongside a concrete precision repair obligation. It is not an exact FFT breakthrough.
