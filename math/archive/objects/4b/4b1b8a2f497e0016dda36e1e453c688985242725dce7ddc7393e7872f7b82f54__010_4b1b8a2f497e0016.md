## 2. Proof validation and exact scope

### Scan-only obstruction

An invertible forward weighted scan has a lower-bidiagonal inverse; a backward scan has an upper-bidiagonal inverse. Each has at most two nonzeros per row. Monomial factors preserve support sizes up to permutations. Therefore the inverse of a t-scan product has at most 2^t nonzeros per row, whereas every Fourier-inverse entry is nonzero. The package's basic t ≥ ceil(log₂ N) bound follows.

Its support-mass refinement, nnz ≤ (2N−1)2^(t−1), is also valid. The per-column support bound of a bidiagonal matrix controls left-multiplication support mass; its per-row support bound controls right multiplication. Monomials preserve either total.

The prior audit already supplied the stronger statement

**t ≥ 1 + ceil(log₂ N), for every N ≥ 2.**

Choose the singleton boundary row of the leftmost inverse-scan factor after absorbing the preceding monomial. Each remaining inverse scan can at most double that row's support. This gives N ≤ 2^(t−1). The upload still does not incorporate this all-N sharpening.

### Scan–broadcast obstruction

For an invertible H = I + UVᵀ, H⁻¹−I = −H⁻¹UVᵀ has rank no greater than the update's rank budget. Removing all such inverse factors by telescoping gives

F_N⁻¹ = S + E, with at most 2^t nonzeros in each row of S and rank(E) ≤ B.

For any s-row-sparse S, let q = floor(N/(s+1)). In the first q rows, the support of S covers at most qs columns, leaving at least q untouched columns. The Fourier minor in those columns and consecutive rows 0,…,q−1 is Vandermonde on distinct roots, hence nonsingular. The inverse Fourier matrix introduces only root inversion and a nonzero overall scalar.

Consequently,

**B ≥ floor(N/(2^t+1)), equivalently (B+1)(2^t+1) > N.**

The field hypotheses are adequate. The proof permits arbitrary internal shuffles and arbitrary scalar magnitudes. It needs no numerical conditioning hypothesis or prime transform length. A zero-size witness is simply the trivial rank bound.

The boundary-row improvement does **not** justify replacing 2^t by 2^(t−1) throughout this rank tradeoff: it controls one row, not the consecutive block required by the Vandermonde witness.

### Cost consequence retained from the prior audit

For nonnegative integers t,B with K=t+B≥1,

(B+1)(2^t+1) ≤ 2^K+2.

Thus N≥3 implies **t+B ≥ ceil(log₂(N−1))**. If scans and individual rank-one broadcasts really are full Θ(N)-cost passes, this yields Ω(N log N) work for that explicit-pass architecture.

B is a sum of rank budgets, not a universal work count. This inference does not charge an arbitrary structured rank-B operator Ω(NB), and it does not cover skipped inactive positions, extra registers, sums of scan outputs, or unrelated linear circuits. None of the proofs establishes a general FFT lower bound.

## 3. Defects and misleading labels still present

1. **“minimal_scan_count” is wrong as a minimum.** `run_audit.py:22` stores log₂ N. For N≥2 the package's own pure-scan refinement already requires log₂ N+1. The value is neither the proved minimum nor an attained optimum. Rename it as the basic row-support lower bound, or use a clearly labeled stronger lower bound.

2. **Counters describe an optimized algebraic interpretation, not literal Python instrumentation.** `scan_hybrid.py:105,108` executes multiplications by zero/one and additions of zero. Line 180 executes two multiplications in `−2*twiddle*data`, while line 181 charges one combined-constant multiply. For each level, the literal two scan calls perform 4N−2 multiplication operators and 2N additions; the twiddle-scaling loop adds N multiplication operators, before table preparation. The advertised NL additions/multiplications are achievable after obvious algebraic simplification, but are not execution counts of this program. Precomputing −2 times each twiddle directly repairs the combined-constant issue without changing the asymptotic bound.

3. **“moved_words” is restricted shuffle traffic.** It counts bit reversal and pre/post pair routing, not scan reads/writes, temporary arrays, coefficient arrays, or twiddle traffic. It must not be presented as total memory traffic. String-based bit reversal also performs logarithmic-length character work per index.

4. **Root preparation is not fully quantified by a count of exponentiation calls.** The artifact counts stage-root `pow` calls but not their individual modular multiplication costs, initial root generation/validation, or the inverse of 2. The prose mostly acknowledges this distinction. Fixed primes only support finitely many transform lengths; exact complex-field, modular-field, bit-complexity, and stable floating-point statements remain different targets.

5. **Own-diagonal subtraction is not a distance certificate.** The full rank of H⁻¹−diag(H⁻¹) does not imply distance from every diagonal-plus-small-rank matrix. For H=I+11ᵀ, H⁻¹=I−11ᵀ/(N+1) is rank-one away from I, although its own-diagonal residual (I−11ᵀ)/(N+1) is full rank for N>1 in characteristic zero. The package's literal measured residual is correct, but cannot support the stronger inference.

6. **Independent-auditor description overstates one check.** `independent_audit.py:1–3` says it checks inverse-of-scan locality, but it contains no separate scan or inverse-scan construction. Its local two-shear identity is not an independent test of every full FFT stage's routing. The separate prior audit did independently check full basis transforms in both fields; that historical evidence remains applicable because the bytes are identical.

7. **Minor presentation issues persist.** The source audits 254 basis columns through N=128; `RESEARCH.md:140` only describes the basis checks through N=64, although later text gives 254. This is under-description, not an invalid result. The N=16 tradeoff table repeats t=2 because its parameter list has a duplicate. Function names such as `minimum_broadcast_rank_budget` denote necessary lower bounds, not proven attainable minima; the prose does explain that limitation.

No static inconsistency found invalidates the core restricted theorem or the ordinary radix-two control.

## 4. Bidirectional extension: already known progress omitted here

The prior extension supplies the exact identity, for invertible scans L and U and diagonal D,

L+U+D = L(A+C+ADC)U, where A=L⁻¹ and C=U⁻¹.

A and C are opposite bidiagonal matrices, so the middle matrix is tridiagonal. This is useful structure, not an all-N Fourier factorization. Its reset-safe generalization and invertibility qualifications are recorded in the prior RESEARCH_UPDATE.txt. Unpivoted tridiagonal elimination must not be assumed safe solely from matrix invertibility.

The prior extension also repaired the diagonal-distance diagnostic: the even-row/odd-column minor of H⁻¹ has rank N/2 at N=4,8,16,32,64 over 998244353. Every diagonal vanishes on this disjoint minor, so rank(H⁻¹−D)≥N/2 for every diagonal D at those tested sizes. This does not handle arbitrary monomial or row-sparse corrections or prove an all-N statement.

The earlier report further records a four-point one-stage construction, a one-stage impossibility for N≥5 even with endpoint permutations, and ordinary radix-four controls. None is new in this upload. The current package's “next gate” should be read after those existing results, not as a reset of the research state.

## 5. Additional independent evidence from this audit

It verified:
- All 11 files are identical to the previous package; all ten manifest entries are correct and complete apart from the manifest itself.
- All four Python sources parse; the test file contains twelve test methods.
- 490 independently generated support networks satisfy row, boundary, and support-mass bounds, including non-power-of-two dimensions.
- 960 integer parameter pairs satisfy the combined pass-cost inequality.
- The specific uploaded bidirectional matrix was reconstructed independently by its closed form: diagonal entries 2, off-diagonal entries equal to products of successive edge weights 3,4,… between the coordinates. For both primes 998244353 and 2013265921 and N=4,8,16,32,64, inversion identities hold, the inverse is fully dense, its own-diagonal residual is full rank, and its even-row/odd-column minor has rank N/2.

The second-prime diagonal-distance checks are additional finite verification performed **by this audit**, not new evidence supplied by the duplicate package. They do not establish an asymptotic theorem. The uploaded test suite and auditor were not rerun, and their stored PASS labels should not be represented as freshly executed results.

This report does not re-audit its formalization or papers. The scan-specific lower bound does not contradict that broader model. “This package supplies no new sub-N log N algorithm” must not be broadened into a claim that no such exact-arithmetic result exists.

## 7. Decision

Retain the valid restricted-model rejection certificate and ordinary FFT control, with the already established sharpenings and caveats. Record this upload as a duplicate, not a new research advance. The outstanding positive deliverable is still an explicit uniform infinite-family Fourier construction in a surviving model, with all-output evaluation, coefficient preparation, routing, scratch, precision/model assumptions, and the claimed savings fully charged.
