# Do-not-repeat guard

Read the canonical `FFT_RESEARCH_CANONICAL_CHECKPOINT_2026-10-09.md` before extending this project.

Already attempted in previous work:

- exact subtree collisions, permutation/shift/complement orbit hashing (not enough for arbitrary fresh FFT);
- finite-field Walsh-Hadamard tabulation, complete-state tables, 4/8/16-bit native binary lookup codelets;
- single-output, pruned, dynamic, multiconsumer and sum-product cached FFT updates;
- short constant-coefficient annihilating filters and `AB=(A-k)B+kB` for structured inputs;
- optimal bilinear interpolation count versus charged transform cost;
- rank-one displacement representation, rank of contiguous Fourier blocks, and unitary/conditioned lower bounds;
- CrocSwap/OpenAI-style exact network parameter retuning, frame-label certificates and small circuit searches;
- quantized multilevel tabulation, fixed-error sampled/pruned FFT.

**This experiment's distinct attempted shortcut:** The known even-length chirp-convolution equivalence provides a structured fixed convolution kernel obeying a first-order *nonstationary* recurrence. Does the same recurrence cheaply recover `h*x` for every fresh `x`? Answer: no, because its filtered-output map has rank N-1 and the output moment ladder has N independent states. This is a restricted negative proof and a new checkpoint in our research log, not a universal lower bound or claimed new theorem.

A future proposal must give a new evaluator rather than a new encoding and prove the total work of constructing and using it, on arbitrary fresh inputs with N exact outputs.
