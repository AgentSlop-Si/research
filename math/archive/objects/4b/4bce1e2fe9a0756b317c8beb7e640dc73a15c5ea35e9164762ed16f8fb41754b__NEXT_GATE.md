# Hero #1 — Phase 19 research gate

**Verified Phase18 frontier:** Exact, charged O(N log(K+1)+K log²(K+1)+Nd+Nd*gcd(N,delta)) field arithmetic for an order-d long-shift graph recurrence with K cyclic defects, including dense x and dense Px. Constant d,K, coprime delay and fixed candidate bank give an O(N) **structured-input** path. Generic x requires fallback and no general exact sub-N log N FFT is proved.

## Gate A — Discover unknown long shifts cheaply

The current default tests two predetermined graph delays. Discovering one among all N-1 possible delays by scanning them individually takes O(N²); even O(log N) candidate scans cost O(N log N). Look for a deterministic or Las Vegas *certified* O(N log log N) method for unknown coprime shift delta with K=O(1) defects, including sampling, candidate collisions, full certification and bit costs. Do not assume delta is supplied by an oracle. This is adjacent to approximate cyclic-period finding and coding, and prior art must be checked.

## Gate B — Composition without known graph factors

This case is now constructed and exactly tested: at N=384, two individually full-support defect vectors combine to K=3; the known-factor evaluator uses O(mN) without expanding the product. But **discovering** unknown factors from fresh data is not solved. Create an independent exact factor-discovery certificate with all work charged. Avoid falsely identifying commuting shifts as new noncommutation; the Green L/P pair remains the noncommuting component.

## Gate C — Generic dimension and singularity accounting

The Phase18 theorem shows fixed-degree, K-defect adaptive annihilator compression occupies dimension <=d+K in C^N. Generalize the obstruction to s-term high-degree and noncommuting graph operators while charging all resonant eigenspaces. Avoid a bogus compression `q(S)=0`, which has zero forcing but loses the whole Fourier spectrum.

## Gate D — Stable complex and bit-complexity implementation

Construct a cancellation-aware division for near-resonant graph eigenvalues, perhaps using derivatives/divided differences and precision adaptation. Prove a norm-based worst-case complex forward error. The existing double-precision stress test loses ~2.76% relative error at 1e-14; finite-field exactness is not a stability certificate. Also handle the growing field/root-supply cost in a named word-RAM or bit model.

## Final all-input acceptance condition

Before claiming sub-N log N for arbitrary x, provide: exact all-input identity; complete source-to-output DAG counting discovery, inverse/Green solves and root/precomputation; independent full-basis and adversarial tests; rigorous asymptotic o(N log N) bound in a stated computational model; and a clear distinction between structured-input parameter savings and worst-case savings.
