# Phase 35 — Fourier seed balancing for rank-distant Green products

**Research output, not a new fast FFT.** The new Phase 35 gate is stronger than a random Weyl rejection for a one-internal-mask circuit: it can reject **every** possible middle complex diagonal and permutation without inspecting that mask.

## What is proved

- **Necessary and sufficient** balancing characterization for writing the Fourier matrix as `A D B` where `B` is invertible and has equal constant row/column sums.
- **All-size no-go** for weak-coupling Green-sum outer stages, with any number of terms and any graph orderings, whenever all couplings have magnitude `<1/3` and their sum weights are positive.
- **One-resolvent no-go** for all nonzero admissible complex couplings and all `N>2`.
- **All-complex-coupling no-go for two fixed rank-distant Hamiltonian cycle pairs** at `N=8,16`, established using characteristic-zero integer-polynomial GCD certificates.
- An exact, linear-work **partial synthesis** that matches the complete first Fourier row and column for Green pairs and a matching transpose stage, but still fails the remaining Fourier columns.
- A one-pass multiset fingerprint for arbitrary unknown middle permutations and a cheap moment-based early rejection.

## Files

- `THEOREM.md` — statements, derivations, exact polynomial lifting proof, scope and operation counts.
- `phase35_balance.py` — exact Green executor, transpose, signature-recovery gate, first-cross construction, positive controls and weak-coupling constants.
- `phase35_polynomial_certificate.py` — rebuilds the full integer polynomials for `N=8,16`, with two independent determinant checks and `ZZ` gcd/divisibility.
- `audit.json`, `polynomial_audit.json` — exact deterministic outputs; the latter contains all integer polynomial coefficients and cycle orderings.
- `tests_phase35.py` — independent functional and polynomial regression tests.
- `CHECKPOINT.md` — what to do next without restarting old paths.
- `previous_full_phase34_checkpoint.zip` — the complete earlier research history, unchanged.

The Green execution and partial synthesis for fixed term count use `O(N)` operations over a field. Polynomial certification is an offline research procedure for two *fixed lengths* and is not part of an online FFT executor.

The constructive diagonal mask uses **arbitrary nonzero complex/field scalings**, not necessarily unit-modulus phases. This is a stronger assumption for impossibility proofs but a weaker hardware constraint for partial synthesis.
