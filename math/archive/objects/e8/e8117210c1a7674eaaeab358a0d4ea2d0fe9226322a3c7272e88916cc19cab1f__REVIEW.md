## Main findings

1. **A projected-query cost claim needs a qualification.** `multi_view.py` (source 076), lines 7–16, states a per-query O(q(1+min(H,r+k))) bound. Lines 102–111 and 193–196 promote a hot view before checking whether H=0. At the default eighth query, promotion copies an r-by-q basis matrix. A cached query can therefore pay O(rq). Charge promotion separately, state an amortized bound, or disable it for the stated bound. `query_many` also constructs and sorts a union before the all-current shortcut. The tail-only event replay itself correctly avoids scanning old retained history.

2. **Exact ring rewrites do not certify floating accuracy.** The cache-rewrite implementation and audit (sources 028–031) explicitly include a cancellation example. The stored record reports 2.220446049250313 without the rewrite and 2.0 after reassociation. A fresh reviewer-only scalar calculation independently reproduces that contrast. The earlier canonicalization audit (source 008) records `(2^53)A+A-(2^53)A` collapsing to zero. These are numerical limitations, not failed exact ring identities. Modular witness conversion in source 029 uses `Fraction(float(value))`, so it certifies the already-rounded binary64 value.

3. **Test counts depend on invocation.** Four assigned files call `unittest.main` before later test classes:
   - Source 017: 10 direct-invocation declarations versus 15 under discovery
   - Source 025: 8 versus 14
   - Source 038: 14 versus 19
   - Source 044: 10 versus 14

   The later Phase-7 stored log contains all 19 dispatcher and all 14 convolution tests, consistent with discovery. This review ran none of them. `INDEPENDENT_CHECK_RESULTS.json` contains lexical declaration lines for all 160 test methods across the assigned test files; lexical counting is not test execution or AST parsing.

4. **Historical compiler outcomes are environment-specific.** The compiler archive's own logs claim 23 passing tests and record Numba in its environment. A later Phase-10 replay of that same archive records return code 1, with 21 passes and two missing-Numba errors. Preserve both contexts. A blanket “all tests passed” would erase the dependency failure. The audited Phase-11 records also distinguish reversed upstream forced-mode labels from corrected patched labels.

5. **The cost models have explicit boundaries.** MST optimality in source 048 is correctly restricted to supplied edges, fixed additive weights, one-parent computations and retained outputs. Complete edge discovery is quadratic in version pairs. Source 078 offers classical static range-index tradeoffs, with branch copying/sorting beyond its numeric query bound. Cache budgets in sources 028, 077 and 078 do not bound all seeds, graph objects, outputs, temporary workspace or build arrays. The single-view and many-view projected stores may alias a caller-owned contiguous projection, so external mutation can invalidate cached results.

6. **Benchmark validation varies materially.** Several older harnesses check aggregate checksums or only final/selected samples even when they compute full arrays. The unified harnesses improve to all-returned-value comparisons and include strong analytic/vector controls. Their final `np.stack` allocations occur after some reported warm timers. Compiler in-process cold totals may inherit previously compiled signatures and include one extra first output; isolated workers improve compilation attribution but still exclude imports/startup and input generation. RSS source 074 assumes Linux units by multiplying by 1024; source 049 handles Darwin separately.

7. **The mathematical gains remain structured or model-specific.** Exact skeleton/DAG searches are finite, direct evaluator counts are not bit complexity, and interpolation's 2n−1 bilinear products do not eliminate evaluation/interpolation work. Sparse-filter, recurrence, near-power integer, selected-frequency and related-version methods retain their structural assumptions. The fixed-prime NTT source is capacity-limited. The CRT bridge is exact small-case quadratic verification, explicitly without a fast-NTT, kappa, Lean or linked-repository implementation claim.

8. **Stored counts reconcile without becoming new execution credit.** The graph package reports 26+7+20=53 suite executions. The persistent-chain package reports 19+22+2+14=57. The unified package reports 253 overlapping unittest executions, including 30 integrated tests, plus separate 480 and 1,600 exact-case claims. Its report's hard-coded 200 schedule checks and 73 integer products are not individually enumerated in the summary log. These are archive-co-resident historical claims, not independently reproduced runs bound to every source/dependency hash.
