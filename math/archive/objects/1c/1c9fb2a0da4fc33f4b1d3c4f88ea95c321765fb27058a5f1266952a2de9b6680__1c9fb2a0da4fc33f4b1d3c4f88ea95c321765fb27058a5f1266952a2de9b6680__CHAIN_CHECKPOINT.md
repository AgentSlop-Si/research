## Exact target and status

Main target remains: uniform **exact** O(N) full DFT for fresh arbitrary inputs, sequential, with every arithmetic operation, preprocessing, input recovery, indexing and output charged. **Still not solved.**

Phase 6 (tensor cut rigidity) showed a sparse middle tensor can hide two expensive Fourier basis conversions. Phase 7 optimizes an alternative: independent rank-one rectangle graph union.

## New scope-limited theorems

1. **Gauge-invariant plaquette:** `F[k,j] F[k',j'] / (F[k,j'] F[k',j]) = omega^(-(k-k')(j-j'))`, invariant under nonzero diagonal row/column scalings.
2. **Rank-one subgroup rectangle classification:** if H is generated by row differences, every rank-one rectangle lies inside one coset of H times one coset of its annihilator H^perp; rank-one rectangle area≤N, equality iff full complementary cosets.
3. **Exact all-length optimal one-shot rank-one incidence:** `C_min = N * min_{d|N}(d+N/d)`; achieved by a coset tiling with N blocks. Square N: 2N^(3/2), prime N: N(N+1).
4. **Dense-factor arbitrary-rank tile model:** For complex N=M², `sum rank(A)(a+b) >= 2N^(3/2)`, sharp. For prime N, Chebotarev's theorem implies sharp N(N+1). **Not** an unrestricted scalar-operation lower bound, since sparse factors and sharing could lower actual operations.
5. **Classical escape:** sharing projections across row/coset blocks and outputs across column/coset blocks yields the *usual two-axis Cooley–Tukey FFT* with `N log2 N/2` butterflies and N charged twiddles, not a new algorithm.
6. Empirical mixed-rank MILP at N=2..6 found no rank-r advantage in the declared model. General composite rank-r optimum is unproved.
