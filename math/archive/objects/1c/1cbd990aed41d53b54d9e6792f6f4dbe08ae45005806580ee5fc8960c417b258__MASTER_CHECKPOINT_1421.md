The v6 sketch moves medians before the short Fourier decoders, avoiding repeated decoder work. Its mathematical result is approximate for arbitrary inputs and exact with high probability for suitable locally Fourier-sparse inputs. The independent synthesis adds a spectral-tail error bound, an L2-optimal periodic mean component, and residual-certified Green composition. This improves the analytical guarantee and structured applicability, not exact arbitrary-input complexity or measured native FFT performance.

## Exact-route constraints to retain

- A Haar–Green stage admits at most 11N/2−6 two-coordinate unitary gates. Exact Fourier therefore requires t≥N log2(N)/(11N−12) such stages, including size-dependent real strengths and intervening unitary masks. Whole-word nonunitary arithmetic remains outside that comparison model.
- A common-base rational function p(T)/q(T), with quasiseparable order h and reduced degree d, needs hd≥floor(N²/4)/N to become Fourier up to endpoint monomials. One low-cut-rank solve with local scalar ports also fails. These constrain representations, not all algorithms. The half-cycle control has linear cut rank and an O(N) evaluator, so rank alone is not time.
- A single arbitrary phase mask between projectively Q(zeta_B) factors is feasible precisely when N≤B². Single integer-polynomial masks have a stricter multiplicity obstruction. Two-mask finite controls are valid but do not supply a scalable speedup.
- The normalized field-descent residual has entropy N(log2 B+1)−B. Its unitary two-coordinate gate cost is at least half of that. Enlarging the coefficient field does not erase this residual work in that executor model.
- Phase support must satisfy a budget at every applicable dyadic conductor. In particular the sum, across masks, of the smaller even/odd exponent count must be at least N/4 at the highest level. This is not a time lower bound.
- The two-stage N8 separated-generator chart has dimension 60 despite passing every graph cut. Arbitrary diagonal masks absorb into such charts; they do not remove coefficient compatibility. Parameter counting does not by itself exclude Fourier.
- Complex-input exact recovery from fewer than N retained input-independent complex linear measurements is impossible for almost every input, even with measurable nonlinear decoding. For real inputs and complex measurements, count realified rank instead; one complex measurement may encode two real coordinates. The argument does not automatically cover a finite Boolean domain.

## Recovered Boolean branch

For polynomial decoding from a fixed linear sketch, a Boolean-correct composite of total degree at most two has target derivative at the cube midpoint equal to the target linear map. Therefore the target row space must lie in the sketch row space. With input-independent randomization and deficient rank, some Boolean input has success probability at most 3/4; the bound is sharp. Degree-three interpolation can already reconstruct two packed bits, so the quadratic restriction cannot be extended to arbitrary polynomial degree.

For complex-valued retained Fourier bins this is a holomorphic-polynomial statement unless conjugates are explicitly included. A missing bin is obtained cheaply by conjugating its retained partner on real inputs. The independently verified N8, N16 and N32 cubic non-completion certificates omit both members of a conjugate pair, so they survive that symmetry. None supplies a faster full transform. The Boolean audit contains the exact proofs and checks.

## Constructive exact acceptance test still open

The earlier map proposes an explicitly unproved finite target: realize D16=diag(I2,H2,H4,H8) on exactly 16 arbitrary-data registers with at most 16 embedded H2 calls, explicit nonunitary scalings/permutations, and both spectator coordinates restored. Prepending the verified 15-call Haar word would produce a 31-call H16 certificate for the inherited finite-win compiler. No such word has been found. Passing a filter, a numerical fit, or a failed bounded search does not settle its existence.

## Evidence rules

Every bound retains its input domain and computational model. Do not infer an all-length theorem from finite examples, a runtime lower bound from parameter count, exactness from a floating residual, or novelty from an unsuccessful literature search.
