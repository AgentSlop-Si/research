# Established mathematics and novelty boundaries — Phase 30

- The vanishing of 2x2 minors characterizes rank-one matrices; projectively the locus is the **Segre embedding** P^(m-1) x P^(m-1), a classical determinantal variety. See https://web.maths.unsw.edu.au/~danielch/alggeom04b/notes.pdf
- The dimension/nonempty intersection theorem for projective subvarieties is classical; see https://alphabetamath.cc/item/thm-dimension-theorem-intersection-projective
- Exhaustive full-degree **Macaulay matrices** and linear-span polynomial certificates are classical elimination/Nullstellensatz machinery. See https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Main_theorem_of_elimination_theory
- Fourier equivalence under independent permutations and nonzero diagonal scalings relates to projective equivalence and complex Hadamard matrix invariants.

**Project-specific result:** Applying these established tools to deterministic, independently ordered Green-resolvent pencils produces exact finite-length nonfactorization certificates at N=4,8,16 (for specified outer factors and three middle couplings), supported by exhaustive modular audits. The five-term projective-gate saturation is a direct consequence of classical projective dimension counting. None of these should be presented as a peer-reviewed new all-input FFT lower bound or asymptotic speedup.
