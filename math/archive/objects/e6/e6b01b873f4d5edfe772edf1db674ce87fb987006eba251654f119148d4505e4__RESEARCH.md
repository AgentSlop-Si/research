# HERO #17 — Phase 14: Noncommuting Green fusion and its exact limits

**Research date:** 2026-10-10  
**Primary goal:** An exact, fully charged, sequential `O(N)` *full* Fourier transform for an unrelated fresh input.  
**Verdict:** **NOT SOLVED.** Phase 14 proves additional operator-specific identities, restricted barriers, and a multiscale escape construction. All claims below are exact, testable, and carefully scoped.

## Executive findings

1. **Positive operator fusion.** For Weyl generators `S,T` with `TS=ωST`, the mixed resolvent `(I-αS-βT)^(-1)` collapses a noncommutative polynomial of degree `N-1` to one cyclic first-order solve. Its exact preparation and evaluation of *any new length-N input* use `O(N)` field operations and `O(N)` storage. This is not `F_N`.
2. **Growing-branch twirl fusion.** Averages of all N shift- or modulation-conjugate mixed resolvents have closed one-direction formulas and an explicit `O(N)` executor, even at the singular-pivot exceptions.
3. **Cross-cut barrier.** Each nearest-neighbor signed magnetic resolvent carries at most one independent dimension across either separated coordinate cut. Any sum of products with path depths `L_i`, allowing arbitrary diagonal scalings, requires `sum L_i >= floor(N/2)` to equal the Fourier matrix. This applies to mixed orientations, but not to arbitrary long shifts, endpoint permutations, or an unrestricted shared DAG.
4. **Exact four-orientation span.** Over C, the four signed resolvent families span all N²-dimensional operator space when N is odd, but exactly a codimension-one subspace when N is even. The missing Weyl atom is `(N/2,N/2)`.
5. **Even-length impossibility.** For `4|N`, the Fourier transform has a nonzero coefficient on that missing atom. Hence **no finite scalar sum of four un-gauged nearest-neighbor magnetic resolvents equals F_N**, no matter how many terms. For `N≡2 mod 4`, that coefficient vanishes, so mere *linear span* does not exclude the transform; it still says nothing about efficient synthesis.
6. **Explicit escape for Phase 15.** One long-range Green stage `(I-αS^(N/2))^(-1)` already has *maximal* balanced cross-cut rank but is exactly executable in `O(N)`. General shifts `S^s` break into `gcd(N,s)` cycles; all cycles can be solved in total `O(N)` arithmetic. The natural next direction is mixed-depth, mixed-stride, shared evaluation with its full Fourier interface charged.

## What is and is not novel

The quantum-binomial/root-of-unity identity and Green recurrences are classical. The rank/dimension statements are proved here for the *particular four signed magnetic resolvent grammar*, and the implementation/audit is reproducible. They have **not** been cleared by a comprehensive literature priority search. Call them project theorems/certificates, not world-first FFT breakthroughs.

The important mathematical distinction is **representation rank versus arithmetic work**. A single long-range Green stage has rank `N/2` across our cut and yet takes `O(N)` work. Conversely, N different Green branches can collapse into one `O(N)` twirl. Therefore neither high rank nor literal branch count is itself a universal time lower bound.

For the noncommuting Green evaluator, the tested `N=65,536`, `p=998244353`, `α=17`, `β=23` configuration required:

| Charged activity | Prime-field operations |
|---|---:|

All 65,536 equations of `((I-αS-βT)y)_i=x_i` passed for the fresh random vector, including independent direct-kernel output checks. An additional zero-pivot case (`β=1`, `α=2`) also passed all 65,536 equations.

The two complete N-way twirls used 786,538 (`S`-twirl) and 524,364 (`T`-twirl) charged field operations, each satisfying an independently checked full-vector recurrence.

The long-range stride `s=N/2` required 753,666 field operations including preparation and online evaluation. **All 65,536 coefficients matched an independent closed-form formula**, and the entire recurrence residual was zero. Its theoretical balanced cut rank is exactly 32,768, despite the linear evaluation work.

These are prime-field **scalar operation counts**, not wall-clock benchmarks, finite-precision performance, or bit-complexity counts. Modular inversions are counted explicitly as one field operation. A primitive root of unity is supplied, and preparation of all root powers is charged. All explicit array reads and N output writes are recorded separately in the audit JSON.

### Apples-to-apples comparison for the same Green operator

To avoid pretending a different operator is a faster FFT, we also compared the linear executor with **naively evaluating its own q-binomial polynomial** by repeated `W*x` and adding all N powers. This naive algorithm costs `4N²+N` field operations (not an optimal alternative).

| N | Fused Green: prep + fresh vector | Naive same-operator polynomial | Ratio |

The two independent algorithms returned exactly identical vectors in all cases. **None** of these outputs is the DFT.

## Next experiment: Phase 15 multiscale Green union

For dyadic `N=2^m`, consider layers of mixed magnetic solves of the form

`(I - α_l S^(2^l) - β_l T^(b_l))^(-1)`

and signed/diagonal gauges. Each layer is O(N) via disjoint cyclic recurrences, and the largest strides can carry Θ(N) cross-cut rank in one step. The **real question** is whether a bounded or sublogarithmic number of such noncommuting stages/branches can satisfy the full Fourier intertwining certificate and produce all outputs for arbitrary inputs, with *every* layer, coefficient initialization, indexing operation and final conversion charged.

A practical synthesis search should:

1. Test `F S = T F` and the first-column boundary condition as necessary and sufficient matrix identities (with the sign convention in the source).
2. Synthesize/screen at small exact prime-field lengths on **all basis vectors**, not just random input or numerical least squares.
3. Stress long shifts and multiple strides, but keep separate the kernel's O(N) application and the cost of finding any input-dependent representation.
4. Compare the *complete* construction to radix-2 and the published uniform sub-NlogN exact Fourier benchmark in the same arithmetic model.
5. Reject phase masks, high Weyl rank, or mere spectral twirls as proof of a full FFT improvement.

The goal remains universal exact O(N), not another Green substitute. No existing experiment in this package establishes that goal.
