## Mathematical scope preserved

- Exact dual/onion identities and n=8 spectral/minor/resultant gates are meaningful guarded necessary conditions; they are not complete factorization existence or nonexistence results.
- The coefficient-reconstruction code provides stronger evidence than random matching where it actually checks complete schemas. A nonzero modular reduction establishes nonidentity only under a valid coefficient-field specialization and denominator conditions.
- Eliminating a common ratio by anchored cross-products leaves exceptional branches. A vanishing 3×3 coefficient determinant does not ensure its nullspace contains a vector with first coordinate 1, hence does not ensure finite gamma parameters.
- The GF(17) experiment covers one primitive root, a fixed P8 permutation and explicitly nonzero chart denominators. It omits multiple exceptional loci and gives no characteristic-zero conclusion.
- The composite norm scan is floating-point evidence at finite divisor pairs, with optional formula checks and no certified spectral enclosure.
- Dense Fourier products, dense inversion/eigensolving, sparse symbolic expansion, finite-grid enumeration and the subset determinant circuit are fully accounted for in per-source reports. None of these files establishes a general sub-FFT transform algorithm.
