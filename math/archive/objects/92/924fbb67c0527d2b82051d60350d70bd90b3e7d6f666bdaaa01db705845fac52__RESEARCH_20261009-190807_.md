# Independent research continuation: gauge-complete multi-stage mixing

**Date:** October 9, 2026.  
**North star:** an exact, input-independent, fully charged FFT for fresh arbitrary inputs requiring genuinely less than `N log N` scalar work, ideally `O(N)`. No such new transform was found in this continuation.

## 1. Why this is a different gate

The preceding checkpoint solved a **single-matrix endpoint-ordering** question: for `N=2^m>=8`, the minimum over arbitrary input/output permutations of the maximum off-diagonal quasiseparable rank of a Fourier matrix is `N/4+1`, achieved by bit reversal at both ends. This says nothing decisive about a product of several cheap operators separated by global permutations.

This checkpoint studies the **joint polynomial realization map** of multiple separated-generator rank-one stages. It accounts for stage-to-stage diagonal gauges rather than mistaking more generator parameters for independent arithmetic.

For each stage

```
S[i,j] = a[i]*b[j]    if i>j
       = d[i]         if i=j
       = c[i]*e[j]    if i<j.
```

Between stages, intermediate orderings are arbitrary permutations and all coefficients are input-independent. One stage can be applied in `O(N)` arithmetic with a forward and backward accumulator.

## 2. Main results

**All-N theorem:** Any `t` such stages have image dimension at most `min(N²,N+t(4N−6))` over `C`, for every sequence of intermediate permutations. Generic matrix representation needs at least `ceil((N²−N)/(4N−6))` stages; for `4|N`, this is `N/4+1`. The proof subtracts `(t−1)N` *actual diagonal-gauge degrees* from the sum of `t(5N−6)` stage dimensions.

**Exact saturation for three transform lengths:** When the *same bit-reversal permutation* is inserted at each boundary, the dimension achieves that bound for **every** stage count up to the threshold at `N=8`, `N=16`, and `N=32`. There are 17 stage configurations, each independently verified modulo 12,289 and 65,537: **34 modular rank witnesses**. A nonzero modular Jacobian minor at integer parameters implies a nonzero complex minor, converting these finite computations into characteristic-zero existence certificates.

These are **not random-existence guesses**: `WITNESS_N*_T*.json` stores all integer stage parameters and minor-column indices. `independent_verifier.py` constructs the Jacobian in a different way from the generator script and recomputes determinants. `BITREV_RESULTS.json` contains the entire rank census.

**Gauge completeness before saturation:** At `(N,t)=(8,2),(16,4),(32,8)`, the sampled Jacobian nullities are exactly `20`, `72`, `272`. These match the ranks of explicitly constructed `6t+(t−1)N` gauge vectors; the verifier checks that they lie in the Jacobian kernel over both prime fields. At the threshold there are additional null vectors because the image has reached its maximal ambient dimension.

**The permutation matters:** With all shuffles replaced by the identity, the product's QS rank is bounded above by `t`. Thus for `t<floor(N/2)` it cannot be generic, whereas repeated bit reversal is full-dimensional for the certified `(N,t)` threshold cases. Direct samples with identity shuffles had tangent ranks 62/64, 226/256, and 842/1024 at N=8,16,32 respectively; these observed ranks are *not* claimed as the exact generic dimensions of the identity-shuffle varieties.

## 3. Why the result isn't a fast FFT

The witnesses are generic complex linear transforms, **not the discrete Fourier matrices**. In particular, a dominant parameterization only guarantees a nonempty open image; it does not imply that a specified Fourier point lies in that image or has a short factorization. Some classical FFT butterfly matrices lie on zero-transition boundary components of the broader quasiseparable class and need not be represented by this narrow finite separated-generator chart.

Our rank-one stage executor charges `5N` scalar multiplications and `4N` scalar additions per stage before trivial-operation removal; it also charges `(t−1)N` permutation reads as a separate category. At the full-dimensional threshold this costs `9N(N/4+1)=Theta(N²)` arithmetic. The independent exact fresh-input test matches dense matrix multiplication for 14 vectors per prime and per size.

For `t=o(log N)` and large `N`, this dimension bound says that the representable matrix family has negligible algebraic dimension relative to the ambient `N²`, but it **does not rule out the Fourier matrix**, because the FFT is a highly structured nongeneric transform. A generic-dimension argument cannot alone prove an FFT lower bound.

The asymptotic saturation conjecture

`dim(image) = min(N²,N+t(4N−6))`

for repeated bit reversal at **all** powers of two remains unproved. Only N=8,16,32 are established by exact witness minors. The apparent coincidence between the generic-depth threshold `N/4+1` and the preceding theorem's single-Fourier minimal quasiseparable rank `N/4+1` may be useful, but no algebraic equivalence is proved.

## 4. Model and background

Two-sided quasiseparable generators are standard in structured linear algebra; e.g. Pernet and Storjohann, *Time and space efficient generators for quasiseparable matrices* (2018), DOI 10.1016/j.jsc.2017.07.010; general scalar transition representations appear in the 2024 Numerical Algorithms paper at https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11075-024-01809-9 . Those works provide context; our small-dimension finite certificates and gauge counts are newly derived for this project, without a claim of literature priority.

For lower bounds, Ailon's `Omega(N log N / R)` result assumes well-conditioned computation (arXiv:1403.1307); our unrestricted coefficient research does not satisfy that assumption. A September 25, 2026 all-length exact-complex Fourier preprint reports `O(N(log N)^(1−10^−13))` with unrestricted exact coefficients (https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/130.md). This checkpoint neither implements nor improves that algorithm.

## 5. Recommended next research gate

**Don't just keep optimizing generic stage dimensions.** They are saturated at the tested sizes and say little about the Fourier-specific identities. Instead:

1. Search for **FFT-specific polynomial syzygies** preserved under multiple globally permuted stages. Use the exact `N=8` two-stage residual polynomial machinery, but do not extrapolate from a single fixed permutation or ignore exceptional branches.
2. Try **constructive all-N recursion for joint bit-reversal tangent spans**. Proving saturation for every power of two would be a genuine stage-geometry theorem, though not itself an FFT speedup.
3. If a candidate exact Fourier circuit is found, execute fresh vectors, test all basis vectors, charge scalar preparation / memory / indexing, and compare to a genuine radix-2 FFT/NTT. Reject telescoping gauges and small-size stage-count tricks.

**Takeaway:** Global shuffles are algebraically potent—repeated bit reversal reaches full generic freedom at the exact gauge-dimension threshold for three verified powers of two. Generic freedom takes `Omega(N)` of these O(N)-work stages, so the arithmetic objective still requires exploiting exceptionally strong Fourier structure rather than expressing generic matrices faster.
