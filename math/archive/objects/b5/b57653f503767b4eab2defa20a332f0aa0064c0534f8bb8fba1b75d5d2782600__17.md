## Mathematics and flow

Own standard-library implementation prepares dyadic field roots, direct Horner and recursive radix-two references, geometric resolvents, sparse differences and a root-free recurrence evaluator. infer_recurrence2 handles zero/rank-one cases, then uses the first four entries and verifies every recurrence equation. recurrence2 uses (1-u*t-v*t^2)X(t)=(x0-xN)+(x1-u*x0-v*x[N-1])*t at t^N=1, batch-inverts regular denominators and directly evaluates at most two exceptional frequencies.

## Costs

Recognition, verification, root table, regular evaluation, at most two O(N) exceptional Horner sums, parity conversion and all writes give O(N) field work/storage on the accepted path after field setup. Arbitrary input uses recursive O(N log N) FFT; slices can add allocation work. Sparse difference path costs O((order+support)N) plus N exponentiations in order; fixed low order is its tested regime. Hankel/binary exhaustive audits are exponential diagnostics.
