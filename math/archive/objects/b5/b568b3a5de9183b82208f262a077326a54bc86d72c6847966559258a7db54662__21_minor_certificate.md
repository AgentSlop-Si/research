## Mathematical and operational scope

Exact modular finite witnesses for the interval two-stage rank gate. For m=floor(sqrt N), the first m intermediate points hit m disjoint m-wide intervals with average overlap <=1; selecting the smallest interval yields rank <=r_left+2*r_right+1 versus an m-rank Fourier Vandermonde minor. This is a scoped two-stage structured obstruction.
