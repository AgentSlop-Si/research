# Prior art and careful research claims

- **Classical:** inverse-banded / quasiseparable Green operators, including Boito–Eidelman (https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.02701).
- **Classical:** contiguous Fourier rows form a full-spark Vandermonde matrix (Achanta et al., https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dsp.2016.07.008).
- **Important caveat:** Fourier matrices are not rigid in the unrestricted sparse-modification sense (Dvir–Liu, https://zesty2.cs.uchicago.edu/articles/v016a020/). Here we use a *stronger per-row support restriction* on a particular sparse inverse core; no contradiction.
- **Project history:** Phase 15 used the row-sparse inverse argument for pure Green products; Phase 17 used common separated cuts for additive sums; Phase 33 handled fully two-sided diagonal gauges in additive sums. Phase 34 extends a different restricted product family via **low-rank inverse corrections**, including near-ordering changes and phase patches.

The phase-specific algebra and its exact experiments are **not peer-reviewed** and we make no claim of discovery priority without a comprehensive literature search.
