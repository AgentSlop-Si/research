## Fixed-cut rank and approximation proofs

For a legal weighted-permutation resolvent, every principal denominator block I-P_RR is invertible: its induced components are paths or complete original cycles, with nilpotent paths and guarded cycle products. The block inverse equation therefore bounds the off-cut rank by rank P_RC, hence by the number of nonzero crossing edges. Complementary inverse nullity gives equality as a stronger independent observation. Zero weights, multiple cycles and fixed points are allowed.

Off-cut rank is subadditive under both sums and products when the coordinate cut is fixed. Thus the total nonzero crossing count, counting repeated occurrences separately, is a valid upper bound for the specified circuit. The report correctly says that actual inserted global permutations have their own cut rank and must be included; conjugating branch cycles does not erase a residual shuffle for free. Diagonal stages contribute zero to either off-cut block.

For a unitary Fourier block with |R|=m, its squared Frobenius norm is h=m(N-m)/N and its operator norm is at most one. Therefore rank>=ceil(h) for every subset over C, with the same statement for flat unitary targets. Contiguous-prefix Fourier cuts additionally have full possible rank min(m,N-m), by a Vandermonde submatrix. The latter rank argument also applies over a splitting field with distinct roots; the norm proof itself is a complex/unitary argument and is not asserted here as a field-uniform norm theorem.

If the candidate block has rank at most c, the omitted singular-value tail gives squared global Frobenius error at least max(h-c,0). When c<h and q=min(m,N-m), the largest tail singular value has square at least(h-c)/(q-c), giving the claimed operator bound. These are absolute errors for the normalized target; relative Frobenius error divides the norm by sqrt(N). The N128 four-shift middle-cut example has c=10, squared relative Frobenius lower bound11/64 and squared operator lower bound11/27. An eight-row hidden cut with c=4 has squared operator lower bound7/8. These are restricted representation/approximation barriers, not arbitrary FFT circuit lower bounds.

Our independent hidden-bottleneck control uses a fresh set of four permutations, all natural-prefix tests passing, with one directed edge crossing each way per permutation on the irregular eight-row set. The Fourier subblock has exact rank8 in the chosen field. This corroborates the all-subset issue without relying on the source's random-generation program.

## Explicit topology and its limits

The four stated shears and affine shifts on Z_s^2 are permutations. Together with their inverses they give the standard eight-regular Margulis–Gabber–Galil graph, with loops and repeated edges counted. The cited [Hoory–Linial–Wigderson treatment, Theorem8.2](https://www.math.ias.edu/~avi/BOOKS/expanderbookr1.pdf) explicitly supplies the5sqrt(2) nontrivial spectral bound for this exact family at every side length.

For one copy of the four directed maps, L=4I-(sum(P+P^T))/2 satisfies 1_R^T L1_R=the directed crossing count. Its gap is at least4-5sqrt(2)/2. Removing the constant part of1_R gives norm squared h, so c copies have capacity at least c(4-5sqrt(2)/2)h. Three copies give a gap exceeding one and pass the weaker ceil(h) requirement; this alone does not certify maximal prefix rank capacity. Five copies give a gap exceeding two, so capacity exceeds2h>=min(m,N-m) for every cut. Reverse counts agree because every permutation has equal outgoing and incoming potential crossing counts, with all edge weights assumed nonzero in this topology guarantee.

This is a valid all-square topology certificate. It does not guarantee actual Green-block ranks avoid cancellation, correct phases, conditioned solves, or any Fourier identity. Individual maps generally have many cycles and fixed points. Twenty plans in two sums are a broader architecture than two-plus-two Hamiltonian plans; no class inclusion or four-plan realization is supplied. Repeated maps may receive different guarded nonzero weights. Coefficients such as a fixed complex scalar of modulus below one give legal cycle denominators, and the four index formulas plus cycle decomposition can be prepared in O(N) arithmetic/word work. All twenty solves, scalar generation and output movement remain charged. Sorting-based debug assertions in a particular script are not the proof of the linear preparation schedule.

Our exact finite-field two-stage twenty-plan control passes the graph capacity conditions but differs from the Fourier matrix. Its failure is expected and is not evidence against every possible weight assignment. An additive twenty-term realization would separately face the older one-layer obstruction at sufficiently large lengths; placing the terms in two stages is a genuinely different grammar.

## Lifted compatibility identity

With block diagonal denominators A,D and replicated identity injection/summation maps, the first two block equations of H(F) force x=A^(-1)Bz and y=D^(-1)ECx. The final Schur complement is F-GD^(-1)ECA^(-1)B=F-YX. Since all individual denominators are invertible, block elimination proves

    rank H(F)=(r+s)N+rank(F-YX).

The product order YX is essential. This is a denominator-free polynomial compatibility matrix with explicit invertibility guards, not a fast rank computation or Fourier evaluator. Our positive planted products and one-entry perturbed targets verify the exact rank identity at three sizes and branch counts.
