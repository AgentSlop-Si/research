# Phase 26 prior-art boundaries

1. Mans and Shparlinski, *Random Walks, Bisections and Gossiping in Circulant Graphs*, Algorithmica 70 (2014), 301–325, DOI 10.1007/s00453-013-9810-3. The notion that bounded-generator circulants admit sublinear bisections is established mathematics. Our elementary simultaneous-modular-character partition is not presented as a discovery of that graph phenomenon.
2. Boito and Eidelman, *Computation of quasiseparable representations of Green matrices*, LAA 721 (2025), 47–80, DOI 10.1016/j.laa.2024.04.034. Band-inverse Green rank structure is existing work; our particular complementary-cut inverse rank formula follows from the classical block-inverse identity.
3. Earlier phases of this user's research had already established single-stage affine Green hidden cuts and restricted butterfly/Green lower bounds. The new step here is a **common cut across any number of sums/products of weighted cyclic Green operators**, even with intermediate affine reorderings, and the resulting restricted-model term budget.

The available evidence does **not** establish mathematical priority beyond this specific project, a general Fourier lower bound, or practical speedup.
