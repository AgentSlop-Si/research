## Positive fusion identity

For a primitive `b`th root `xi`, permutation `P`, `X=alpha P`, and `s=0,...,b-1`:

`(1/b) sum_r xi^(-rs) (I-xi^r X)^(-1) = X^s (I-X^b)^(-1)`.

Thus `b` root-of-unity-shifted Green solves with character weights fuse into one stride-permutation Green solve in `O(N)`.

Arbitrary packet weights fuse to one denominator `I-(alpha P)^b` with a numerator polynomial whose coefficients are the DFT of the weights.

## New common-order gate

For `T=q(C)^(-1)p(C)`, denominator degree `d`, numerator degree `e`, and `C` one cycle, a separated rectangle of size

`m=floor((N-e)/2)`

has rank at most `d` whenever `d<m`.

For prime `N`, Chebotarev therefore forces

`d >= floor((N-e)/2)`

for any matrix monomially equivalent to `F_N`.

A cyclotomic packet of width `b` has `d=b`, `e<=b-1`; by itself it needs `b>=ceil(N/3)`. The cheap character packet is only one shifted stride-Green term.

## New multi-graph inverse gate

If an invertible matrix `A` has a separator partition `U,S,V` with no `U-V` entries and `A[U,U]` invertible, then

`rank(A^(-1)[U,V]) <= |S|`.

For a strictly row diagonally dominant sparse denominator whose inverse is monomially prime Fourier, the sparsity graph must satisfy

`treewidth >= ceil(N/4)-1`.

Hence bounded-treewidth/bandwidth multi-graph denominators cannot be the missing direct Fourier inverse. A high-treewidth special matrix with a non-elimination linear solve remains open.

## Do not repeat

- root-of-unity shifts of one cycle as if they were independent Green orders;
- a polylog-order one-cycle recurrence as an exact prime FFT candidate;
- bounded-treewidth sparse denominators as direct Fourier inverses;
- interpreting packet-fusion speedups as a Fourier speedup.
