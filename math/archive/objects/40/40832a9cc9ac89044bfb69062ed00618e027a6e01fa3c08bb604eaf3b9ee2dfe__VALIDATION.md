# Phase 36 validation — what was actually rerun

Date: 2026-10-10.

## Checks completed

- `tests_phase36.py`: **19 tests passed** in fresh source directory, including green forward/transpose recurrences, explicit `O(N)` Green inverse round trips, positive Sinkhorn calibration, both Fourier seed conditions, arbitrary complex mask spectral bounds, exact `Q(i)` nonreal cross-ratio, `alpha`-root gauge identity, full-phase-rank Green counterexample, and positive controls outside the theorem's scope.
- Inherited Phase 35 `tests_phase35.py`: **15 tests passed** from the prior project directory, independently of Phase 36 code.
- Full-matrix spectral comparisons for `N=4,8,16,32,64` passed the proved uniform error lower bound.
- Matrix-free first-row and first-column calibration passed for `N=4,8,16,32,64,128,256,512,1024,4096,16384,65536`. The largest observed deviation was below `2e-14` in the supplied float64 tests. For each case, the next Fourier column failed substantially.
- For the explicit middle `beta=1-1/N` positive cyclic Green, the Sinkhorn iteration reached the specified `1e-12` **relative marginal tolerance in four iterations** in each sampled case, including `N=65536`. The analytically proved convergence factor is less than `0.215` per full iteration in Hilbert projective metric, independent of N for this kernel family.
- A symbolic SymPy computation at `N=4`, using rational outer Green stages, produced an exact non-real projective cross-ratio for `A^-1 F C^-1`, proving no real-positive middle bridge with two arbitrary complex diagonal masks can produce that Fourier matrix.
- The `alpha=exp(pi*i/N)` control uses the algebraic identity `alpha^N=-1`; its phase matrix has exact determinant magnitude `2^(N-1)` and its four-entry ratios take only `+1` and `-1`. Numerical checks agree through `N=32`.

## Analytical lower bounds and exact interpretation

| N | Guaranteed spectral error with outer `eta=1/100` | Minimum middle entrywise phase rank required for exact equality |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 | 0.376265 | 4 |
| 8 | 0.440600 | 8 |
| 16 | 0.506414 | 16 |
| 64 | 0.576564 | 64 |
| 256 | 0.608799 | 255 |
| 1,024 | 0.624330 | 1,018 |
| 65,536 | 0.637627 | 65,091 |

All phase-rank requirements are *necessary* only. A full-rank phase matrix can be produced by one cyclic Green solve, so the high rank is not a sufficient speedup criterion. The spectral bounds are for the specifically stated positive-middle / nearidentity-outer or phase-rank-limited architectures.

## Important limitations

- Numerical Sinkhorn convergence to a tolerance is not an exact symbolic Fourier factorization and has no proven general finite-bit complexity bound here.
- Tests compare full dense Fourier matrices only at small sizes (`N<=64`); large tests validate first cross and a failing next column, not the full DFT.
- No arbitrary complex, noncommuting, independently ordered multi-stage Green architecture was proved impossible by Phase 36.
- These are mathematical deductions and reproducible experiments, not externally reviewed priority claims.
