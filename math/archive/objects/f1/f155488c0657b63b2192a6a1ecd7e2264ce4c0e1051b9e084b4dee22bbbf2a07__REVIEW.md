## Independent proof audit

For one weighted-permutation resolvent G=(I-W)^(-1), concatenate its cycles and order each cycle in its forward row-sampling direction. Every cycle is legal only if its weight product is not one. On any separated row/column rectangle, at most one cycle can meet both sets. Within that cycle, every connecting path crosses a fixed boundary and its weight product factors into a row part and a column part, with common denominator 1-product(weights). This argument makes no division by individual weights and retains zero weights, fixed points and arbitrary cycle decompositions. Hence the rectangle rank is at most one.

For r such orderings of B common labels, iterated increasing/decreasing subsequence extraction produces L labels monotone in all orders whenever B>(L-1)^(2^(r-1)). Choose m=r+t+1, L=m(s+2), and B=(L-1)^(2^(r-1))+1. The first m labels are rows; the m(s+1) tail labels supply columns. A row-degree-s correction forbids at most ms of the tail labels, leaving m columns. Every Green term has rectangle rank at most one, the rank-t correction contributes at most t, and the sparse correction vanishes. The resulting m-square rectangle has rank at most m-1.

For rows and columns chosen from 0,...,B-1, factor the Fourier determinant into an ordinary Vandermonde and a Schur polynomial. After removing the minimum row exponent, that polynomial has nonnegative integer coefficients and homogeneous degree

    d=sum_i(a_i-a_0-i) <= (m-1)(B-m).

All variables lie on an arc of width at most 2 pi (B-1)/N. Therefore all positive monomial terms lie in an arc of width at most 2 pi d(B-1)/N, strictly less than pi when

    N>2(m-1)(B-m)(B-1).

A rotation then gives every term strictly positive real part, proving nonvanishing; the Vandermonde is also nonzero. This supplies the contradiction for every complex length satisfying the strict threshold, rather than relying on finite-field tests or generic-minor assumptions. For r=2,t=s=0, m=3,B=26 and the threshold is N>2300. The sharp 25-label monotone-subsequence countercontrol prevents silently reducing B to 25 by the same argument.

An arbitrary diagonal correction vanishes on the disjoint rectangle, so it costs no extra threshold. Scalar coefficients on Green terms also preserve the bound. The same elementary rank argument immediately permits independent diagonal left/right endpoint masks on each branch, even singular masks: they cannot increase any selected crosscut rank. Independent unmatched endpoint permutations are not covered by this extension.

The general displayed thresholds check exactly: (r,t,s)=(2,0,0):2300; (2,0,1):15872; (2,1,0):13524; (3,0,0):34545588. Fixed t,s imply r=Omega(log log N) for representations in this one-layer grammar. This counts resolvent summands, not an unrestricted arithmetic lower bound; shared computation, other primitives and products are separate questions.

## Prime-factor and power-of-two controls

If N=m p^v with gcd(m,p)=1, the source's coordinate step gives a principal p-square kernel of primitive pth-root type for odd v and primitive p^2-root type for even v. Its formula for the step and exponent multiplier is correct. For a normalized three-row/three-column determinant, the six-term polynomial has a positive term whose exponent cannot match any negative exponent modulo p. Its reduced polynomial is therefore nonzero and has at most six nonzero coefficients. For p>=7, a nonzero multiple of Phi_p, or of Phi_(p^2) with degree below p^2, requires at least p nonzero coefficients. Thus these minors do not vanish. The order extraction needs at least 26 labels, giving the stated prime-divisor condition p>=29.

At N=4096 the distinct sorted row/column exponents have a uniquely maximal determinant exponent, so the determinant polynomial is nonzero. For the first 26 labels its degree is at most 1875, strictly below degree(Phi_4096)=2048. This is a valid independent complex-number proof for that length. Modular nonzero samples only corroborate it.

## Product grammar and accounting

The one-layer rank bound cannot be applied to a product on the same rectangle. Our independent control exhibits a rank-three product rectangle where its first Green-sum factor has rank at most two. This is only an escape from a gate, not a Fourier factorization.

For invertible sparse denominators A,B,C,D, the target F=(A^(-1)+B^(-1))(C^(-1)+D^(-1)) is equivalent to

    A F D=(A+B)(CB)^(-1)(C+D).

The order CB is essential. Since CB is invertible, the displayed 2N-square block matrix with blocks CB,C+D,A+B,AFD has rank exactly N if and only if its Schur complement vanishes. Both positive constructed targets and one-entry perturbed negative targets pass our equivalence checks. The rank test is a dense search/verification gate and supplies no cheap solution algorithm.

A legal fixed weighted-permutation resolvent can be prepared and applied by a linear number of cycle-recurrence operations, including cycle products and one guarded division per cycle. Coefficient generation, the number of terms, all compositions and output work remain charged. Nothing in the new source constructs coefficients giving an exact Fourier product at growing lengths.
