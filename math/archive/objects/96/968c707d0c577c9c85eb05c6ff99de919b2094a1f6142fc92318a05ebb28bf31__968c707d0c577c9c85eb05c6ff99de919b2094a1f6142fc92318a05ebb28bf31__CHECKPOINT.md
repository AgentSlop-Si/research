- The induced half-circle phase partition is exactly balanced for power-of-two `N`. Any circular translation by `a` has exactly `|centred(h*a mod N)|` edges from its complement to the partition.

- Sums and products across the same cut have additive rank upper bounds; diagonal gates do not spoil the certificate.
- Intermediate **affine** coordinate permutations can be moved to the boundary by conjugation without increasing the number of affine runs. Arbitrary non-affine permutations cannot be treated this way.
- General bound `rank <= 2*d*Q*N/r + B`; with bounded `d,b` and arbitrary transformed strides, exact DFT forces `Q=Omega(log N/log log N)`.
- Fully weighted, two-stage four-order example at `N=256`: candidate rank 38, exact Fourier alternating-quarter minor rank 128, universal Fourier balanced-block rank >=64; any outer monomial form has complex spectral error at least 0.5374838499.
