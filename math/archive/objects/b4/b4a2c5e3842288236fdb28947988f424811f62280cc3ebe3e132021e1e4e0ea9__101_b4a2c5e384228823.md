# Research continuation: exact Fourier synthesis from order-robust Green stages

## The new gate, not another recap

We now possess an O(N)-apply, O(N)-setup **well-conditioned globally mixing operator** whose balanced off-block rank is Omega(N) and is *uniformly robust to every coordinate reordering with high probability*. This closes the simplest shortcut by which the previous parity stage had deceptively high natural-order rank.

**Open challenge:** Can such an operator supply Fourier-specific *phase coherence and exact cancellation* in fewer than log N stages, rather than just high-rank global transport?

## Three focused experiments

**A. Exact product identities (primary).** For the two-path family `G + eps PGP^T`, incorporate input-independent complex diagonal chirps, inverse stages only when their cost is proved, and up to three independent permutations. Seek a symbolic identity `F_N = D_out * S_t * ... * S_1 * D_in` for an infinite family of powers of two. Try to derive frequency-domain covariance (shift/modulation conjugacy) rather than fitting arbitrary matrices. The identity must be checked on all entries for small sizes and proved for all N.

**B. Graph-multiscale structure.** The random two-path graph has no small balanced separators, but `S_N` still has an O(N) forward executor. Study whether it can implement a *group of Fourier butterflies* in sublinear work relative to the number of butterfly layers. Count all permutations, scalars, input-dependent work, inversions, and output writes. High cut rank alone cannot prove such a speedup.

**C. Deterministic uniformity.** Replace random Hamilton path sampling with an O(N)-constructible explicit pair of Hamilton path orders whose union is a balanced expander. Such an explicit pair would remove random initialization and make the primitive easier to inspect and share. The finite exact-certified random instances are examples, **not** a proved all-N deterministic construction.

## Automatic rejection criteria

- Any candidate that computes only an approximation without an explicit error/precision account.
- Any claim inferred solely from rank, matrix density, inverse density, or growing induced 1-condition.
- Any apparent `O(N)` achieved by free precomputed output tables or arithmetic on astronomically large constants not charged.
- Any numerical fit without a uniform exact algebraic proof and fresh full-length-vector checks.
- Any result that is just a gauge-conjugated known radix-2 FFT.
- Any comparison conflating exact complex-register arithmetic with bit-complexity or stable floating point.

## Research frontier

The published September 2026 OpenAI exact-register comparison is already `O(N(log N)^(1-10^-13))`. We have NOT improved that. Our contribution in this checkpoint is a self-contained, non-gauge global O(N) primitive, not a new DFT algorithm. If a Fourier factorization is later found, compute its entire complexity including scalar/permutation synthesis before comparing asymptotic exponents.

## Mandatory norm-budget check before further factorization attempts

The bare expander Green stage has `kappa_1<=101/19` uniformly, and the archived induced-condition theorem forces `Omega(log N)` such stages to realize a DFT, even with arbitrary internal permutations, phases and one global scaling. Do NOT continue searching only for sublog-length products of bare `G+0.01 PGP^T` factors; they are mathematically blocked.

An O(N)-butterfly orthonormal Haar transform provides a non-rank-one way to grow the induced 1-condition while keeping spectral condition 1. The new `haar_control.py` demonstrates this alongside the Green stage, but is not itself a DFT algorithm. If using it for an exact synthesis, prove a non-telescoping Fourier identity and check that the order-robust singular-value properties survive the changed output basis. Merely multiplying stages by Haar matrices is NOT enough.
