## New mathematical results to retain

1. **Balanced-seed equivalence.** A matrix `A` can be the left factor of `F=A D B` for some invertible diagonal `D` and invertible bi-regular `B` if and only if its entire first row is nonzero and `A[0,i]*(A^-1)[i,0]=1/N` for all `i`. Equivalently the idempotent `A^-1 e0 e0^T A` has flat diagonal. An arbitrary middle permutation does not change the necessity.
2. **Near-identity obstruction.** For `A=c(I+E)` with `||E||_2 <1/2`, flat seed balancing is impossible at all `N>=2`. Applies to any positive-weighted sum of cyclic Green solves with all `|alpha_s|<1/3`, regardless of Hamiltonian cycle ordering. In particular Phase 27's `1/4,1/5` stage cannot be repaired by one middle monomial: the first leverage is at least `359/408` in modulus, whereas Fourier demands `1/N`.
3. **One Green outer factor** impossible at any `N>2` with a regular right factor and just one internal monomial, for all admissible complex couplings. Its denominator has a two-sparse first column.
4. **Exact characteristic-zero fixed-family no-go.** For the `N=8,16` pairs in `polynomial_audit.json`, the common-coupling numerator polynomials `H0,H1` have GCD degrees `46,209`, respectively; in both cases their exact integer GCD divides the numerator determinant. Hence **no admissible complex common coupling** works with *any* bi-regular right factor and one middle monomial. The pairs are rank-distant (`6,14`), not near-cycle copies.
5. **Constructive positive control.** Two rank-distant Green resolvents on the left and their transpose (optionally row-permuted) on the right, connected by a uniquely calibrated diagonal, reproduce the *entire first Fourier row and column* in `O(N)` online field operations. Checked through `N=4096`, but all remaining coordinates of the next tested Fourier column were wrong. A true `N=4` regular-right-factor Fourier decomposition passes independently, demonstrating the gate is not a tautological rejection.
6. **Middle monomial recovery.** For two invertible bi-regular stages, the normalized first row of the left and normalized first column of the right must have the same multiset. Matching them reconstructs a mask and permutation satisfying the first row and column, in `O(N log N)` comparison work or expected `O(N)` hashing. A mismatched second power sum is an immediate `O(N)` rejection.

## Scope and old paths NOT to repeat

- This is a **one internal monomial gate** result. Multiple phase gates, external nontrivial gauges, or an irregular right factor evade the theorem.
- The `N=8,16` polynomial exclusion concerns **only the specified cycle pairs and one common parameter**; it is not a generic all-two-parameter theorem.
- Matching a Fourier row/column does not mean matching an FFT. No `o(N log N)` transform or integer-multiplication upper bound was obtained.
- Phases 16/17/21/26/33/34 already block conventional unitary butterflies, additive fixed-term Green sums, affine-order Green sums, and low-rank-near Green products in their stated models. Do not restart these.
