## New positive identity

For odd prime `N`, `W_r=X^h Z^r`, and `G_r=(I-alpha W_r)^(-1)`:

`H_s=(1/N) sum_r omega^(-rs) G_r`

has the exact sparse action

`H_s e_j = (1-alpha^N)^(-1) sum_(m*j+h*C(m,2)=s) alpha^m e_(j+mh)`.

For every nonzero `m`, there is one `j`; `H_s` therefore has `N-1` nonzeros (`2N-1` when `s=0`) and applies in `O(N)`.

The `W_r` are projectively noncommuting:

`W_r W_q = omega^(h(r-q)) W_q W_r`.

## New weighted-packet gate

For arbitrary weights `c_r`,

`T[i,j]=(alpha^m/(1-alpha^N))*chat(u_h(i)-u_h(j))`,

where `m=h^(-1)(i-j)` and `u_h(t)=t(t-h)/(2h)`.

Because `u_h(h-t)=u_h(t)`, every packet has a zero reflected `2x2` minor. An independently affine-permuted and diagonally gauged Fourier matrix has a nonzero minor on the same rows/columns. Therefore no complete weighted Weyl packet equals that Fourier class.

## New Krylov gate

For a sparse denominator `A`, a vector in `span{x,Ax,...,A^d x}` cannot leave the `d`-hop support neighborhood of `x`.

If `A^(-1)` is dense, an exact Krylov solve needs depth at least the directed support diameter. With maximum outdegree `Delta`, it also needs

`d >= log_Delta((Delta-1)N+1)-1`.

A constant-degree high-treewidth sparse Fourier denominator therefore cannot be solved exactly in `O(N)` by a literal Krylov/polynomial schedule; it needs `Omega(N log N)` work in that model.
