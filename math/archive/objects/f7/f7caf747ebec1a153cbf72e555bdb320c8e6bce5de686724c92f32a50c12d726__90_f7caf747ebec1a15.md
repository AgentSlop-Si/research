# Fresh FFT overlapping-stage research — verified results

## The strongest mathematical result (scope: invertible matrix-factor stages)

Let `U_N=F_N/sqrt(N)` be the normalized complex Fourier matrix. With the induced column-sum norm, `kappa_1(A)=||A||_1||A^-1||_1`:

    kappa_1(U_N)=N.

For **every** factorization `U_N=S_t ... S_1` into invertible N-by-N linear stages, the exact inequality

    PRODUCT_{i=1}^t kappa_1(S_i) >= N

holds. Thus if every stage has 1-condition at most `C>1`, then

    t >= ceil(log_C N).

This is true even for fully dense, nonunitary stages and arbitrary internal permutations. Global scalings do not evade it. It is not a machine-operation lower bound without a *separately charged O(N)-work implementation of each stage*. A phase/coordinate-dependent diagonal scaling counts its own kappa_1 in the budget.

For standard normalized radix-2 butterflies `kappa_1=2`, the bound gives `t>=log_2 N`, **attained** by the usual FFT. This is elementary operator norm algebra, **not a literature-wide novel theorem**.

## The exact O(N) nonunitary test stage

Take `T=tridiag(-1,3,-1)`, `U=T+I` and `S=T^-1+U^-1`. Both `S` and `S^-1` are fully dense over the real numbers, yet can each be applied in O(N) field arithmetic after O(N) preparation (tridiagonal forward/back substitution).

Exact bounds for all N:

    ||S||_1 <= 3/2,
    ||S^-1||_1 <= 17/6,
    kappa_1(S) <= 17/4,
    kappa_2(S) <= 45/11.

Hence a product of such S stages with permutations, unit-phase diagonals, and **arbitrary GLOBAL scalar normalization** needs `t>=ceil(log_(17/4) N)`; if no amplifying scalars are inserted, the stronger `t>=ceil(log_(3/2)(sqrt(N)))` follows. An ordinary single tridiagonal Green stage `T^-1` satisfies `t>=ceil(log_3 N)` by inverse row support. Summing two stages genuinely escapes the inverse row-sparsity obstruction, but not the condition budget.

These are **necessary** stage numbers for the stated family, not achievable constructions. They do not disprove a different global stage whose amplification/1-condition grows with N and whose elementary cost is nevertheless O(N).

## A deceptive but exact fully dense Fourier circuit

Take an ordinary FFT with butterfly factors `B_i` and fixed rank-one `H=I+alpha u v^T`. Dressed factors `G_0=H B_0`, `G_i=H B_i H^-1`, `G_last=B_last H^-1` are all **fully dense** in our verified finite-field instances, and can each be applied in O(N). But all H factors cancel: the full computation is **exactly the original FFT**, with more operations and no stage reduction.

| N | Plain exact FFT | Fully dense gauge FFT | Runtime slowdown | Arithmetic gate ratio |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 256 | 0.440 ms | 0.948 ms | 2.16x | 5.67x |
| 1,024 | 2.491 ms | 5.114 ms | 2.05x | 5.80x |
| 4,096 | 12.191 ms | 24.569 ms | 2.02x | 5.89x |
| 8,192 | 27.710 ms | 55.368 ms | 2.00x | 5.92x |

Single-threaded CPython, warm precomputed plans, fresh full-length modular inputs, identical exact outputs; comparisons are against our own Python NTT, not a tuned native FFT. Coefficient, permutation-index and twiddle preparation are counted separately in code and would only worsen the dressed path.

The exact gauge recognizer recovered rank(H-I)=1 for all N=4,8,16,32. All 14 tested intermediate dressed stage matrices had **every entry nonzero**, despite doing no useful additional FFT work.

## Numerical synthesis: a bounded failure, not a proof

Short fixed-seed Adam searches (PyTorch complex128, 250–350 iterations) for products of two-/three-channel full-array rank-structured stages with interleaved bit reversal/parity permutations gave:

- N=8, t=2, r=2: Frobenius relative error ~1.4e−8. Not exact; no all-N construction.
- N=16, t=2, r=3: best error ~0.0151.
- N=32, t=2, r=3: best error ~0.309.

These are merely **numerical search outcomes**, not lower bounds or certified exact factorizations.

## Verification and open gap

- 11 unit tests pass, including exact modular full DFT correctness for fresh vectors, nonlinear gauge rejection, tridiagonal/sum inverses, shuffles, costs, exact real-rational 1-norm conditions.
- Independent exact modular Gauss–Jordan audit: 4 full-length DFT matrix equalities, 14 all-dense staged matrix checks, 54 new-vector checks and two-Green inverse identities.
- Rational `norm_budget.py` independently verifies norms through N=32 and integer-only stage bounds to N=1,048,576.
- No new O(N), no new o(N log N), no superior FFT kernel. The concrete stage family is blocked.

**Remaining research target:** a sequence of fully charged O(N)-work, overlapping, nonunitary stages whose 1-condition budgets can grow with N while preserving a genuinely low operation count and controlled numerical/bit complexity. A candidate must provide an exact all-N identity and survive the rank-one gauge recognizer; another numerical fit alone is insufficient.
