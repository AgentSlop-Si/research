## 6. Certified growing-fiber Fourier algorithm

### Exact surviving statement

For N=2^k>=4, Q=2^floor(k/2), M=N/Q and 1<=b<=M/2, take the Q strided length-M fibers of a fresh raw input, or its specifically defined guarded Green-preconditioned version. If each fiber has at most b Fourier modes, with locations/amplitudes/recurrences not supplied, the algorithm discovers and exactly verifies them and emits **all N explicit unnormalized Fourier coefficients** in

`O(N log(b+2)+Q b^2)` field/logarithmic-word operations and O(N) space.

The field has characteristic not dividing N and a supplied primitive N-th root. Unit-cost field inversion, exact zero tests and O(log N)-bit indexing are explicit. Root-table construction, input reading, root search, complete reconstruction checks, indexing and output writes are charged. Unsupported fibers are rejected within the same bound. Choosing fixed b gives O_b(N); b=polylog(N) gives O_c(N log log N); b=N^o(1) gives o(N log N) on this class. Small-size cap violations have a stated fallback.

The aggregate algorithm instead accepts exactly `sum_t r_t<=K0` for a supplied total budget. For K0=O(Q log^c N), it also gives O_c(N log log N); individual fibers may be fully populated. Complete light-recognizer failure certifies a heavy fiber, whose ordinary transform is amortized against the budget, including the necessary final over-budget fiber on rejection. All-input FFT fallback restores ordinary O(N log N) worst-case cost.

Three pruned-transform uses replace the older modewise costs: recurrence-root scanning, full raw-fiber verification, and grouped final synthesis. The accepted recognition is not just a fitted short prefix. Dense raw input, dense final spectrum and full-rank reshaping are compatible with the intermediate-fiber restriction; these examples do not remove it.

## 7. Randomized approximate all-length Fourier algorithm

### Exact surviving statement

For every fixed 0<alpha<1/2 and c>0, for every fixed input x in C^N chosen independently of fresh random states, the audited construction emits **all N explicit coefficients approximating the unitary DFT U_N x** with

`||Xhat-U_N x||_infinity <= C_alpha N^(-alpha) ||x||_2`

except with probability at most N^(-c), for all sufficiently large N. Work is `O_(alpha,c)(N log log N)` and memory `O_(alpha,c)(N)`. All integer lengths, including primes, are covered asymptotically, with trivial bounded-size branches.

Use s=2h-1 independent rows where `h>(1+c)/(1-2alpha)`, take coordinatewise real/imaginary medians, and include sampler abort in failure probability. Thus alpha and c are **fixed parameters**; constants/row count grow as alpha approaches 1/2. No alpha=1/2 endpoint is claimed.

The normalization is crucial. The error is absolute relative to the whole input l2 norm, not relative to each Fourier coefficient or a small relative l2 error of the output. Under the unnormalized DFT F_N=sqrt(N) U_N it becomes `O_alpha(N^(1/2-alpha)) ||x||_2`. For alpha<1/2 the unitary tolerance exceeds the typical `||x||_2/sqrt(N)` dense-spectrum bin scale by N^(1/2-alpha). This is substantially weaker than accurate FFT evaluation at the usual per-bin scale.

### What “conditional” means here

It is **not** a hidden conjecture about prime gaps. The audit independently proves the elementary padding search: choose prime M=Theta(log N), a rough cofactor K, and Q=MK=4N+N^o(1), so every nonzero short displacement is invertible. The number-theoretic step is unconditional. Gaussian Poisson/truncation/rounding, weighted covariance, short-filter segmentation and near-linear work accounting received separate mathematical audit.

It is a theorem in an **ideal exact supplied-base arithmetic/RAM model**: exact complex arithmetic; exact length-dependent Gaussian exponential/phase/root bases and normalizers supplied; O(log N)-bit integer addresses/arithmetic; and proper independent sampling. The audit supplies multiplicative recurrences that charge table generation rather than invoking a free per-tap oracle. It does not construct transcendental bases from rationals with field operations or supply a precision/bit-cost theorem.

Fresh independent slope and phase sampling is essential. The shipped fixed default PRNG seed is not the theorem's quantifier. Its bounded sampler caps RNG calls, which does not by itself bound underlying fair-bit rejection work. The audit provides a **theoretical repair**: bounded independent fair-bit pair trials for uniform (unit slope, phase), with exp[-Omega(log^2 N)] abort added to failure and polylogarithmic bit/gcd work charged. That repair preserves the asymptotic theorem but is not claimed already implemented in the submitted sampler.

### Later findings and their actual effect

It does not refute this approximation theorem. Alpha=1/2 remains unclaimed; arbitrary rank deficiency alone is insufficient.
2. The exact complete M-row frame is a valid identity, but the existing separate-row executor still pays M transforms/scans and O(N log N) in its intended regime. It does not upgrade the constant-row approximation into a faster exact FFT.
