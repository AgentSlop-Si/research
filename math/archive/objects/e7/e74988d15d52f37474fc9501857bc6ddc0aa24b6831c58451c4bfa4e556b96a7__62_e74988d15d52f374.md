# Green-chain recovery: audit and reversible-primitive extension

9 October 2026. Source: `fft_green_chain_recovery_2026-10-09(1).zip`.

## Bottom line

The new material supplies a valid, stronger **two-stage structural exclusion** and a useful **high-cut-rank linear-arithmetic Green primitive**. It does not construct an exact arbitrary-input Fourier transform faster than N log N, and it makes no certified novelty claim.

The useful concrete addition in this audit is an explicit, implemented **O(N) exact scalar-arithmetic inverse** for the mixed-order Green primitive, including linear setup and data movement. Its spectral-condition guarantee also improves from 7 to strictly less than 5. These make it a more useful reversible building block; they do not provide the missing Fourier factorization.

## What is new in the upload

The archive has 50 files: 20 byte-identical to the previously audited materials and 30 changed/new, including packaging and result logs. All 49 manifest-covered hashes match; all 20 Python files parse statically. No uploaded Python or shell code was imported or executed. Author-provided PASS logs are not counted as independent reruns.

1. **Half-cut obstruction.** For N even, an exact factorization F_N=A P B with quasiseparable natural-order stages and any intermediate invertible monomial P requires r_A+r_B>=N/4. The algebra is correct. It excludes two rank-2 stages for even N>16 and the first dyadic excluded size is N=32. The odd-N extension gives r_A+r_B>=floor(N/2)/2, so the source's blanket wording 'whenever N>16' needs correction: the stated proof establishes the all-integer exclusion at N>=18, not N=17.
2. **Endpoint scope matters.** Either an external output monomial alone, or an external input monomial alone for Fourier, is permitted. Simultaneous arbitrary relabelings at both endpoints are not covered. Consequently the prior endpoint-permuted F16 two-stage construction is unaffected. This is not a general DFT arithmetic lower bound. Also, equality to invertible Fourier automatically forces the square stages A and B to be invertible; invertibility need not be an extra hypothesis, but singular stages cannot actually realize the equality.
3. **Three-stage control.** The repeated 2x2 block example is correct: one natural-order quasiseparable channel can have four parity-selected half-size submatrices of full rank. It invalidates naive iteration of the one-cut proof, without showing that three stages suffice.
4. **Mixed-order Green stage.** M=P T^-1 P^T+(1/20)T^-1, with T=tridiag(-1,3,-1) and parity P, really is dense, O(N)-apply, and has natural half-cut rank at least N/2-1. Adding the rational Householder H reduces that guaranteed lower bound by at most one. The source's condition estimates and complex-phase extension check out. Exact full half-cut rank and full density of the reflected stage and both inverses were independently reproduced at N=8,16,32,64; these observations remain finite tests.

## What this audit adds

### A charged linear-arithmetic inverse

Let B=P T P^T. Then

    M^-1 = T (T+(1/20)B)^-1 B.

After interleaving coordinates, the middle system is a fixed pentadiagonal matrix plus one extra edge, a rank-two correction. A bandwidth-two LDL^T factorization and a 2x2 Woodbury correction give O(N) setup, O(N) application and O(N) storage. Both base and final matrices are SPD; all elimination pivots and the correction determinant are proven nonzero for every even N>=2. Reflections and unit phases are inverted with additional linear passes.

`MIXED_INVERSE_EXTENSION.md` contains the full derivation, representation assumptions, operation accounting and limitations. `mixed_inverse_check.py` is an independently authored executable implementation, with exact rational and finite-field reference checks. Dense matrices are used only by the test oracle, never the executor.

### Better spectral conditioning, with an important remaining gate

Positive definiteness yields

    kappa_2(M) <= (3+2 cos(pi/(N+1)))/(3-2 cos(pi/(N+1))) < 5.

The inverse, reflected and unit-phase-dressed versions have the same spectral condition bound. This is a matrix-conditioning fact, not a floating-point stability proof.

The bare M still has kappa_1(M)<=7, as the source already proves, for any permutation. Therefore products of bare M stages, permutations, unit phases and global rescalings still need at least log(N)/log(7) stages to equal Fourier. The Householder supplies growing induced-1 condition in the uploaded expanded family. High natural cut rank alone does not bypass this norm obstruction. The conclusion does not cover added reflections or arbitrary diagonal gains.

### Apply the existing rigidity gate to low-rank gauges

The earlier project result rank(F_N-S)>=floor(N/(s+1)) for matrices S with at most s nonzeros per row gives, at s=1,

    rank(F_N-Q)>=floor(N/2)

for every monomial Q, including arbitrary nonzero gains. Thus t rank-r updates to identity, interspersed with arbitrary monomials, require tr>=floor(N/2). This strengthens the uploaded Householder-only control and removes its unitary-gain assumption. It is an application of the already established sparse-rigidity argument, not a new project theorem or a bound on full Green operators. The independent proof/checker is in `halfcut_audit/`.

## Full-cost and accuracy boundaries

All linear-runtime statements here count exact scalar arithmetic, including rational coefficient preparation, plus linear indexing and output writes. They are not bounded-word exact arithmetic claims. Even a corner entry of T^-1 has a denominator with Theta(N) bits. No practical speedup, backward-stable inverse implementation, sublogarithmic stage factorization, or bounded-word FFT is established.

The original user target remains open in this research line. A decisive constructive result would need an all-N Fourier identity using sufficiently few genuinely non-gauge stages, with scalar generation and data movement charged. This audit deliberately ends with the verified reversible primitive instead of treating density or conditioning as that missing factorization.

For context, the source's cited exact-register comparator is real and model-specific: the official [OpenAI scope note](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/130.md) separates a subsequential circuit theorem from a uniform exact-arithmetic power-saving result with supplied roots and unrestricted coefficients. Neither this audit nor the upload claims to match that result in the Green-stage line. No broader prior-art or priority conclusion is made.
