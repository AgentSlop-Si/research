## Important findings

- Corner guards matter: independent_gf17_audit.py counts anchored-minor success before testing B/A corners. A future positive flag needs a stronger acceptance check. Its saved zero-success result is not invalidated: the tested set is a superset of the fully corner-regular locus, and every stored candidate has a nonzero necessary minor.
- The S1=0 exception source explicitly constructs invertible, corner-regular partial candidates outside the generic chart. Omitting rational denominator strata cannot support a global exclusion.
- upper_minor.verify can return zero verified comparisons. Its companion test checks resultant polynomials are nonzero, but never requires verified>0. Some other sampling wrappers also permit vacuity, though several companion tests do enforce counts.
- Three resultants against a degenerate reference pencil are necessary only. Modular Jacobian rank is not a proof that the common-zero locus is everywhere codimension four or infeasible.
- The recursive regression runners use ZIP CRC and log substrings without pinned checkpoint authentication. The archive builder preserves changing timestamps, so its byte-reproducibility claim is too strong.
- Generic chart construction materializes dense matrices and performs cubic dense multiplication/rank work. The two-defect support formula is not itself a complete low-cost transform.

## Concrete acceptance boundary

Let B be the 8×8 identity plus E(3,0)+E(4,1), using zero-based indices. Both B(7,0) and B(0,7) vanish; every exported corner-anchored minor is zero. Yet the lower cut with rows {3,4} and columns {0,1} is the 2×2 identity, of rank two. B is invertible but outside quasiseparable order one. Therefore invertibility plus these thirty minors is insufficient without appropriate corner-chart assumptions or all-cut rank checks. This is a hand-derived counterexample, not an execution of the supplied sources.

For the upper pencils, setting the reference (a4,b4)=(0,0) makes all three reference resultants vanish. Another pencil (a1,b1)=(1,0) then has no gamma3 root. This illustrates exactly why the source's stated “necessary, not sufficient” restriction is essential; it is not a claim that this abstract coefficient assignment is attained by the spectral parameterization.
