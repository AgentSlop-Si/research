# Independent audit: two-stage balanced/complement area separator

## Result

The fixed-route two-stage architecture in the enclosed `AREA_SEPARATOR_THEOREM.md` cannot equal the full complex Fourier matrix for **every dyadic grid side M >= 8**, with N=M^2 and a diagonal middle map. This is an exact coefficient-independent obstruction. It covers arbitrary complex edge masks, arbitrary endpoint diagonal factors, zero couplings, singular sums, and cancellations. It is a restriction on this wiring, not an unrestricted FFT lower bound. Novelty is unassessed.

All five required finite base cases pass a separately authored topological reachability check. The analytic tail has a verified effective threshold M=256, so there is no untested dyadic gap. A middle permutation must have a positive linear number of specified area crossings to escape this bound; satisfying that demand does not prove Fourier compatibility.

## Exact family and route conventions

Index the M-by-M grid by i=tM+u. Order vertices stably by (t+1)(u+1), breaking ties by i, and let sigma reverse the resulting ranks. The second route has the independent column permutation rho(i)=N-1-i, the coordinate complement. **Rho is not bit reversal.**

Each stage is an arbitrary endpoint-diagonally weighted sum of one native grid Green and one `P_sigma G Q_rho` grid Green, where `G=(I-D_a L_t-D_b L_u)^(-1)` has zero boundaries. Each denominator is unit lower triangular for all masks, so its Green matrix always exists. The two stages may have unrelated masks and diagonal factors. Scalar couplings are absorbed in those factors. Coefficients are fixed for the linear circuit, independent of the input vector.

The seven structural wires are input, native1, **raw** balanced1, sum1, native2, **raw** balanced2, output. In a raw balanced wire, input rho(i) feeds raw vertex i; raw vertex sigma(i) feeds output i. These raw coordinates are before the P_sigma routing. They differ from some earlier denominator-state coordinates by a within-wire permutation. The independently authored checker implements this literal convention.

The result does not exclude a different column route, extra summands or stages, or an arbitrary middle permutation. The small cases M=2 and M=4 are not asserted to fail. The separate sigma-middle finite supplement is not included in this audit.

## Rank proof for arbitrary masks

Let A be the lowest half of the area ranks and B its complement. Every strict grid predecessor has strictly lower area, hence A is a downset even across stable tie breaks. Also sigma(A)=B.

The B vertices with an incoming edge from A form a frontier Gamma with at most 2M elements: each grid row and column has at most one transition. Native Green flow from B to A vanishes. In the A,B ordering, the sparse denominator and inverse satisfy

    U = [U_AA 0; U_BA U_BB],
    G_BA = -U_BB^(-1) U_BA U_AA^(-1).

The principal diagonal blocks remain unit triangular, so this identity has no exceptional coefficient branch. U_BA has nonzero rows only in Gamma, yielding rank G_BA <= |Gamma|.

For the routed term, J[A,B] is a row/column reordering of G[B,rho(B)]. Split rho(B) into its A and B parts. The former has rank at most |Gamma|, and the latter has h=|B intersect rho(B)| columns. Arbitrary endpoint diagonal multiplication cannot increase rank. Therefore each whole stage satisfies rank H[A,B] <= h+2M. The sharper structural cut is S=(B intersect rho(B)) union Gamma in each raw balanced wire.

With a diagonal middle map, expanding the final A,B block through the intermediate A/B partition gives

    rank T[A,B] <= 2h+4M.

No invertibility of endpoint factors or stage sums is assumed. Zeros delete paths; cancellation can only reduce rank. A directed vertex separator of capacity k factors the corresponding transfer block through at most k scalar states, even when the separator includes endpoint vertices, so the finite reachability certificates give exact rank bounds for all coefficients.

## Fourier rank requirement

For any two half-sized index sets, the corresponding submatrix of the unitary Fourier matrix has squared Frobenius norm N/4 and operator norm at most one. Its rank is therefore at least N/4. This proof works for arbitrary area-defined sets over the complex numbers, without falsely using a prime-size Fourier-minor theorem at composite N.

For the first N/2 output rows and any N/2 distinct input columns, the Fourier block is a square Vandermonde matrix at distinct roots and has rank N/2. This stronger requirement is used in the three smallest finite certificates. Finite-field nonvanishing is not substituted for the complex unitarity argument.

## Effective tail and all dyadic base cases

Normalize grid coordinates by L=M+1, making rho act as coordinatewise 1-x. For a=9/50 the product-area distribution is a(1-ln a). The exact rational inequality

    exp(7/4) > sum_(k=0)^5 (7/4)^k/k!
             = 700667/122880 > 50/9

implies a(1-ln a)<99/200. A right-endpoint Riemann sum for the coordinatewise decreasing indicator bounds the number of vertices below this product threshold. Since 99(M+1)^2<100M^2 for M>=256, all high-half vertices have product greater than aL^2.

Consequently B intersect rho(B) lies in the lens xy>=a and (1-x)(1-y)>=a. Writing s=sqrt(7)/5, its area is

    s-2a ln((1+s)/(1-s))
      = (2/3)s^3+(2/15)s^5+(2/35)s^7+...
      <= 679 sqrt(7)/16875 < 27/250.

The vertical height f(x)=max(0,1-a/[x(1-x)]) is continuous and unimodal, with total variation 14/25. The Riemann-sum error and at-most-one lattice endpoint error per column give the safe bound

    h < (27/250)(M+1)^2+2M+1.

The resulting gap from N/4 is strictly greater than

    (17M^2-4216M-1108)/500,

which is 33708/500 at M=256 and increasing thereafter. The independent checker verifies all rational constants and this expansion exactly, not using floating-point logarithms.

## Middle permutations and costs

Let a middle monomial send coordinate j to p(j), and define c=|{j in B:p(j) in A}|. Its A,B block has rank c when monomial entries are nonzero (and at most c if zeros are allowed). Grouping the block product first by the second stage's A and B inputs gives

    rank T[A,B] <= 2h+4M+c.

Thus an exact Fourier product needs c >= N/4-2h-4M. For M>=256 this exceeds the explicit positive quadratic gap above, giving c=Omega(N). This is a necessary crossing condition, not a construction or a claim that all crossing routes fail.

The route, area partition, frontier, overlap and crossing count can be prepared in O(N) time and storage with stable integer counting sort. Applying a specified constant-size Green product is O(N). The obstruction says this diagonal-middle wiring cannot supply the required Fourier identity regardless of coefficient synthesis.
