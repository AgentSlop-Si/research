## The honest exact-FFT next gate
An exact O(N) conjecture requires an explicitly parameterized all-N matrix family M_N satisfying both `M_N Z = D M_N` and `M_N e_0 = ones` while its *total* circuit evaluation, coefficient preparation, shuffles, shared state, and precision remain O(N). The surviving architecture classes are nonaffine, phase-correct products/sums of dense-inverse scans, not products of only sparse-inverse cyclic resolvents. No such uniform family exists in these sources. This remains a construction problem, not a proven theorem.
