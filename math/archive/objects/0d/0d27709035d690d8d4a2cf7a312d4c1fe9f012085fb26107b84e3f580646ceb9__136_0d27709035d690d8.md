# Boolean spectral completion: nonlinear FFT research gate

This repository investigates a concrete alternative to the old collision-cache theory:
**Can the binary identity x²=x recover uncomputed Fourier outputs from computed ones, without an FFT?**

**Status:** several exact positive identities for tiny/restricted tasks, a general quadratic non-generation theorem, and three independently checkable finite cubic impossibility certificates. **No new fast arbitrary-input Fourier algorithm.**

## Contents

- `spectral_boolean.py`: exact modular Boolean DFT, spectral idempotence, missing-bin formula, exhaustive Boolean polynomial synthesis, injection/ambiguity and quadratic theorem interfaces.
- `graded_certificate.py`: shift-equivariant weight-k monomial enumeration, sampled Boolean determinant certificate generator, modular determinant verifier.
- `independent_certify.py`: independent certificate replay, entirely separate elimination implementation.
- `audit.py`: full reproducibility experiment, including exact Q(zeta_8) ring verification of the tiny cubic identity.
- `audit_results.json`: the actual binary support sets defining each nonzero determinant certificate, plus exact results.
- `RESEARCH.md`: theorem statements, all assumptions and proof arguments, full complexity caveats, prior art.
- `NEXT_GATE.md`: the next falsifiable direction and do-not-repeat rules.
- `tests/test_spectral.py`: comprehensive regressions.

No third-party Python packages are required.

## Run

```sh
python audit.py
python independent_certify.py
python -m unittest discover -s tests -v
```

## Most important results

- Quadratic polynomials in a strict subset of Fourier bins **cannot** synthesize a missing bin on *all binary inputs*, over C.
- Removing both `k=1` and `k=N-1`, **no polynomial of total degree <=3** in the remaining bins recovers k=1 at N=8,16,32. Independent determinant proofs in characteristic zero are certified via modular specialization at a split prime.
- A single missing bin *can* have a cubic formula at N=8, but it is slower than direct evaluation and real-input conjugation.
- Knowing all even bins alone does not determine any complete odd spectrum on arbitrary binary inputs.

These are restricted mathematical findings, **not a complexity lower bound on all FFT algorithms**. In particular the Boolean-only model does not imply a faster fresh transform on arbitrary complex arrays.
