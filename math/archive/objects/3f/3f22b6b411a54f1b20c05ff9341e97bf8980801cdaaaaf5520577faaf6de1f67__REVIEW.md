## Independent mathematical verification

1. **Calibration reduction.** Write r=A[0,:], s=C[:,0], with positive biregular sums a,c. First-column equality is x*(B(s*y))=(1/a)1; first-row equality is ((r*x)^T B)*y=(1/c)1. Setting u=r*x and v=s*y gives u*(Bv)=r/a and v*(B^T u)=s/c. Conversely these marginal identities give both first-cross equations. Positivity, equal total marginal mass, and invertible A,C are essential to the stated equivalence. Positive matrix scaling is classical, with uniqueness up to reciprocal scalar gauge. This certifies 2N−1 entries only.

2. **Uniform approximate preprocessing.** For beta=1−1/N, the cyclic Markov resolvent K has entry spread beta^{−(N−1)}<e, so its projective diameter is <2. Birkhoff contraction gives tau<=(e−1)/(e+1); the two positive maps in a full Sinkhorn update give tau². Reciprocal and positive diagonal maps preserve projective distance. Both the first iterate and the fixed point have (v/q) spread <=e, so their distance is <=2. After recomputing u=p/(Kv), rows are exact; if projective v-error is delta, the column relative errors have spread <=exp(2delta), and their weighted mean is one. Thus all column relative ratios lie between exp(−2delta) and exp(2delta), proving the asserted O(log(1/epsilon)) iteration count. Each cycle or reverse-cycle solve is O(N). The exterior inverse formula follows by multiplying its denominator through I−beta W. Exact calibrated masks are not thereby produced in finitely many rational operations; the source correctly claims approximate-mask ideal-real preprocessing and online application given masks.

The use of Hilbert contraction for matrix scaling is established in [Franklin and Lorenz](https://doi.org/10.1016/0024-3795(89)90490-4); the publisher abstract was verified, although its full page returned HTTP 403. A modern primary paper states the precise tanh(diameter/4) theorem in its Theorem 2.1: [Eckstein, Hilbert’s projective metric and Sinkhorn convergence](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00440-025-01366-9). No novelty is assigned to this ingredient.

3. **Phase rank.** If H=sqrt(N)F and U is the phase matrix of T, entrywise triangle/reverse-triangle inequalities give |H−U|<=2|H−sqrt(N)T|. Rank(U)<=r and the flat singular spectrum of H imply ||F−T||_F >=sqrt(N−r)/2, hence the claimed operator floor. All nonzero complex diagonal magnitudes and phases are covered; row/column permutations do not change phase rank. Entrywise nonzero B is assumed.

4. **Positive middle improvement.** Its masked phase is a rank-one unit phase u_i v_j. Minimizing each positive entry magnitude gives at least sin² of the Fourier phase discrepancy. The doubled-character matrix H2 has Gram N times the equivalence relation 2(i−j)=0 modulo N, so ||H2||=sqrt(gN), g=gcd(2,N). Consequently ||F−T||_F² >=(N−sqrt(gN))/2. Combining this with item 3 gives precisely c_N. It is uniform over unconstrained mask sizes; it is stronger than a real-nonnegative-only argument.

5. **Outer sandwich.** Let s0=(1−delta_A)(1−delta_C). The inverse perturbation estimate is ||A^{-1}FC^{-1}−F||_F <=(s0^{-1}−1)sqrt(N). Lower singular bounds on the outers then yield [s0(1+c_N)−1]_+. For eta=.01, delta_A,delta_C<=.02 because each Markov K is a convex combination of permutations. The limiting floor is 0.639505352652. The necessary phase-rank fraction for exact equality is 5725597/5764801, approximately .993199. This is not a lower bound on the cost of making a high-phase-rank operator.

6. **Single complex cycle.** Removing row, column and global phases gives the triangular-step phase matrix H(zeta), whose successive row differences give det H=(1−zeta)^{N−1}. Thus the phase rank is one or N. In the rank-one case with rho>1, a global minus sign must be retained when comparing to the positive real resolvent; the preceding global-phase convention makes the source statement correct. Every distinct-row/distinct-column cross-ratio is alpha^{N Delta}, Delta∈{−1,0,1}. For 4|N the Fourier cross-ratio set has N distinct values, excluding a single Green up to arbitrary invertible monomial endpoints. The alpha=exp(pi i/N) example requires that additional supplied scalar/root, and only shows maximal phase rank at linear application cost. It does not construct Fourier.
