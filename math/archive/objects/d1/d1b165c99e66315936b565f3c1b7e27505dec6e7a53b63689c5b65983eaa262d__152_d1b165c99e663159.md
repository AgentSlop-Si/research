# Decision gate before spending another research phase

**Actual target:** uniform, exact, all-output FFT for every *fresh complex* input, with O(N) as the goal and no free preprocessing; the Boolean-only route is a distinct secondary question.

**What we eliminated here:** Boolean-only circuit examples consisting of input squares, zero-on-Boolean products and overlapping-low-degree gates produce no saving after exact linear projection; generic coefficient extraction consumes substantial work. The projected linear circuit has O(s+T) gates, where T is total one-hot overlap across multiplication gates, *if* its scalar coefficients are supplied. Such scalar preparation is not free.

**One new falsifiable candidate criterion:** Construct a Boolean-specific circuit for *all* N Fourier outputs with **high-overlap products**, and show an exact, globally shared cancellation of quadratic/off-diagonal terms whose total charged cost beats the baseline. A single multiplication of two dense linear forms does not suffice: it creates cross terms requiring cancellation. Any proposed global cancellation must be independently checked on all 2^N Boolean inputs for small N and on basis/structured and random inputs for larger N, and it must not hide a large integer operation or another FFT.

For arbitrary complex inputs, polynomial idempotence is invalid; the previous tangent/linear-circuit obstruction remains. There is currently **no** proved O(N) algorithm in our project for the main target.

Do NOT redo: exact subtree collision caching, cached signal updates, few-bin output pruning, short annihilating filters, cyclotomic/Galois implicit outputs, chirp recurrence, stage grouping lookup, unchanged boolean input squaring, or CrocSwap constant retuning.
