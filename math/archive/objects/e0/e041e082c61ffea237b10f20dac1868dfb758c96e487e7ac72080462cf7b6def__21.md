## Mathematics and flow

Implements modular ranks/inverse and the general necessary lower bound t-|H|+rank(M[R,H]). Cooley-Tukey endpoint controls verify actual factorization and rank-one stages, then demonstrate where substituting full Vandermonde rank is wrong. Separate natural-column/arbitrary-row and affine-input controls validate the restricted substitution; random rank-one-updated stages exercise general inequalities.

## Guards and contracts

prim_root tests n/2 only, which is adequate for the dyadic lengths actually used, not generic composite n. NumPy int64 modular products can overflow for large parameters; here p<=65537,n<=64 gives n*(p-1)^2<=274877906944, below 2^63, and elimination products are likewise safe. Public generalized_necessary assumes valid dimensions/permutations/rank budgets and actual stage hypotheses.

## Costs

All-window dense rank testing is polynomial research cost, potentially O(N^5) if every O(N^3) rank is recomputed for O(N^2) windows. It is no forward FFT evaluator.

## Tests and actual stored witness impact

Stored endpoint controls use actual ranks 4 vs naive 5 at N8 and 8 vs 9 at N16, preserving corrected budget 2 vs naive 3. The independent audit agrees on these witnesses. No overflow or nonprimitive-root failure affects the stored cases.
