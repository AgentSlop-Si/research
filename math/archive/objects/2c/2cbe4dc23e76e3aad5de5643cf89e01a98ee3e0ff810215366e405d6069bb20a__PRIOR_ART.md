# Prior-art and scope checkpoint

- Grigoriev, Karpinski & Singer, *Interpolation of Sparse Rational Functions without Knowing Bounds on Exponents* (1990), https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/id/eprint/2068/ . Classic sparse rational interpolation; we make no claim of inventing sparse rational recovery.
- Grigoriev, Karpinski & Singer, *Computational Complexity of Sparse Rational Interpolation*, SIAM J. Computing (1993), https://doi.org/10.1137/S0097539791194069 . Foundational complexity theory.
- Kaltofen & Yang, *On Exact and Approximate Interpolation of Sparse Rational Functions* (2007), https://repository.lib.ncsu.edu/bitstreams/[private-id]/download . Earlier numerical/exact sparse interpolation.
- Cuyt & Lee, *Sparse interpolation of multivariate rational functions* (2011), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2010.11.050 . Prior sparse rational algorithms.
- Morgenstern, *Note on a Lower Bound on the Linear Complexity of the Fast Fourier Transform* (1973), https://doi.org/10.1145/321752.321761 . Bounded-coefficient model lower bound, NOT a blanket impossibility theorem for all arithmetic models.
- Ailon, *An n log n Lower Bound for Fourier Transform Computation in the Well Conditioned Model* (2014), https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.1307 . Lower bound in a specified well-conditioned model.

Phase21 project-specific results: (i) reciprocal reduction from unknown two-defect/multi-binomial cyclic graph residuals to one-binomial + sparse numerator; (ii) universal self-reciprocal factorization of the fixed-rank Hankel resultant with explicit zero multiplicities and a short symmetric quotient; (iii) executable correct-for-all-inputs fallback with exact time-domain convolution certificate and charged operation ledger.

These are mathematical and software contributions **within this project**, and have not been established as historically first or peer-reviewed. Standard Hankel/Prony, Sylvester elimination and cyclic convolution methods are pre-existing work. The original Phase20 archive is preserved bit-for-bit alongside this package.
