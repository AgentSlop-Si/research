# Phase17 prior-art / attribution ledger

- **Phase16 (this project)**: `L=7I+2S+D`, `P=7I+S+2D^-1`, `LF=FP`, uniformly conditioned `O(N)` Green inverse and negative forcing-FFT equivalence. Phase17 *uses* this, does not rediscover it.
- **Pruned input FFT**: FFTW's public explanation explicitly states `O(N log K)` complexity for K nonzero inputs: https://fftw.org/pruned.html. Standard pruned Cooley–Tukey is implemented in `green_sparse.py` for dyadic N.
- **Fast formal power series**: sum of simple fractions, product trees, Newton inversion and FFT/NTT polynomial multiplication are established algebraic algorithms. `rational_forcing.py` composes these with Green forcing and implements finite-length block streaming and kernel caching; it is **not** claimed as a new polynomial multiplication theorem.
- **Bluestein / chirp-Z**: the all-length fallback is a classical DFT-to-convolution identity. We use NTTs over suitable primes for exact finite-field checking, not a new FFT speedup.
- **Morgenstern (1973)**: bounded-coefficient lower bound for unnormalized Fourier circuits, https://doi.org/10.1145/321752.321761.
- **Nir Ailon (2013)**: unitary pair-gate entropy lower bound, https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.4745; and **Ailon (2014)**: conditioned linear circuit lower bounds, https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.1307. These restrictions **cannot** automatically be generalized to all nonunitary, out-of-place, arbitrary arithmetic circuits.

**Project-specific contribution (not a global originality claim):** working full-length exact sparse-residual Green recovery on dense structured inputs, short fixed-kernel NTT reuse, exact finite-field checks including prime/mixed lengths, deterministic truncation error certificate, and explicit generic-input + intermediate-precision rejection tests. CrocSwap integer-multiplication constructions were reviewed in prior phases; Phase17 does not import their conditional exponent argument.
