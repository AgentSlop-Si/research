## Independent derivation

Let q=rm, N=mq, and let omega be a primitive Nth root over a field whose characteristic does not divide N. Set omega_q=omega^m, omega_m=omega^q and omega_r=omega_q^m. Write bank label b=gr+h, with g<m and h<r. Pack input coordinate (gr+h)m+s into (h+rs)m+g. This is a permutation.

Apply L=F_q tensor I_m to the packed vector. At frequency k=t+mu, expansion of the exponent gives

    Y[(t+mu)m+g] = sum_h omega_r^(uh) omega_q^(th) Z[g,h,t],
    Z[g,h,t] = sum_s omega_m^(ts) x[(gr+h)m+s].

The omitted exponent factor from mu times rs is an integer multiple of q, so equals one. Fourier inversion in the h index now gives

    Z[g,h,t] = r^(-1) omega_q^(-th)
                 sum_u omega_r^(-hu) Y[(t+mu)m+g].

Thus an explicit inverse r-point DFT for each pair (g,t) recovers all original short-bank outputs, in their original indexing. The factor r^(-1) is essential. For r=2 these are a sum and a difference divided by two, followed by the odd-bank inverse twiddle. No exchange of the two bank dimensions occurs.

There are m^2=N/r small inverse transforms. Hence the exact accounting is one L call, (N/r) T(r) work for those transforms, and O(N) packing, phase generation, scaling and output work. Inverse r-point transforms use a forward transform, reversal and normalization. Linear overhead follows for fixed r only; the displayed transform cost cannot be dropped when r grows.

## Reusing the fixed active-bank exterior

For the same bank and active cyclic interval J of size j>0, the previously reviewed support-cover identity evaluates L with k=ceil(q/j) calls to P_J=L Delta B_(J complement), paying O(kN). Insert that evaluator into the conversion above, form B_J=E_J B_all+(I-E_J), and finally use F_N=P_J B_J R_0. The cost is

    k+1 calls to P_J + (N/r) T(r) + O(kN).

Preparation is shared, with O(N) reusable scratch for fixed k and r. The original q=2m half-active interval therefore takes three exterior calls plus linear work. A full bank takes two. Empty J is the separate direct identity with one exterior call. The result retains the active-interval or comparable bounded-cover hypothesis and supplies no cheap implementation of P_J itself.
