## Useful mathematical conclusions

1. **Restricted fresh-input evaluators are real.** The affine-cycle identity, strict-majority discovery and full residual verification yield O(N(s+1)) field work for the specified low-defect family, with the exact nonsingularity guard. The low-order recurrence and few-coset constructions likewise have genuine restricted-family content. The helper supplies 140 fresh affine end-to-end cases plus 28 singular declines. They do not accept arbitrary inputs through an uncharged detection, lookup or representation oracle.
2. **Inverse support, rank and cut gates are scoped representation tests.** The nonuniform rigidity bound N≤u+r(s+1), its boundary-sensitive scan/broadcast consequence, and the F8 rank profile are independently supported. Arbitrary interstage permutations and full-state dimensions must be accounted for. Quasiseparable generators need not be literal scalar scans. These facts do not impose their costs on every alternative implementation.
3. **Factor count is not automatically runtime.** Row33's N/2-stage half-cycle word fuses into a two-term monomial polynomial with O(N) field application. Thus separate-pass work claims need an explicit requirement that factors actually be executed separately. The helper checked the exact fusion and all-pass algebra.
4. **Dense, globally mixing, well-conditioned linear primitives exist within the reviewed model.** The random two-path Green and unitary path/tree constructions have the asserted robust cut behavior and linear field application, with explicit randomness-model qualifications. The hidden parity layout and Fourier conjugation formulas have independent checks. This does not itself factor Fourier.
5. **The two-Green inverse bridge is algebraically cheap when its stated structure is supplied.** The exact tridiagonal sandwich and diagonal-plus-rank-two Fourier conjugate explain the linear scans/low-rank correction and the 73/18 error-transport constant. They do not erase the decoder, root, scale, rounding or setup obligations of an approximate pipeline.
6. **Condition-reset statements require the full state.** Identity padding changes condition numbers unless a suitable anchor is present. Successive bank exchanges must alternate logical orientation. Exponential conditioning of the canonical q-Pascal middle block is compatible with an algebraically valid factorization; it does not alone establish or refute a different transparent compiler.
7. **Classical pruning and lookup models retain their proper scope.** Known-edit, supplied-basis and selected-output algorithms can save work. Exact fixed-small-field lookup is distinct from a growing-modulus transform, and weak normalized coordinate error is distinct from exact or relative-energy Fourier computation. Reported wall-clock gains are not new arithmetic bounds.

The source proofs use substantial classical algebra. These reviews establish bounded correctness within stated models and do not make global priority claims. No arbitrary-input exact o(N log N) Fourier algorithm has been accepted through this queue review.
