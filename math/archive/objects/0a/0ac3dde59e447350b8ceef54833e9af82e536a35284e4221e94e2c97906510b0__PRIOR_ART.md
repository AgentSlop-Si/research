# Prior art and relationship to this phase

- Positive matrix scaling is classical. Knopp and Sinkhorn, *A Note Concerning Simultaneous Integral Equations* (Cambridge University Press page, 2018 archival posting), describes positive row/column marginal scaling. Sinkhorn and Knopp, *Concerning Nonnegative Matrices and Doubly Stochastic Matrices*, Pacific Journal of Mathematics 21 (1967). Phase 36's Fourier first-cross equivalence is an application, not invention of Sinkhorn scaling.
- Birkhoff/Hilbert projective metric contraction: a positive linear operator contracts by `tanh(Delta/4)` where `Delta` is its projective diameter. See *Hilbert's projective metric for functions of bounded growth and exponential convergence of Sinkhorn's algorithm*, Probability Theory and Related Fields, DOI `10.1007/s00440-025-01366-9`.
- Classical Fourier Hadamard orthogonality and Eckart–Young best low-rank approximation provide the key estimates for the phase-rank and phase-second-harmonic spectral floors.
- Green geometric-series identities and projective 2x2 cross ratios are elementary/classical algebra. The new results in this package are **project-specific deductions and reproducible tests**, not independently peer-reviewed novelty or mathematical priority claims.
- The immediately prior project's Phase 35 single-mask seed balancing and all-complex exclusions are in `previous_full_phase35_checkpoint.zip`. Phase 36 instead treats **two** unconstrained masks and proves a quantitative *approximation* obstruction within explicitly stated weak-outer/positive-middle assumptions.
