# Exact FFT factorization research: spectral minors, adjacent tails and small-field saturation

**Checkpoint: October 9, 2026 · Status: verified necessary conditions, no faster FFT.**

This project continues the original experiment in **two overlapping, nonunitary, globally separated-generator rank-one quasiseparable stages** with an arbitrary *fixed* internal permutation. We ask whether such stages can reproduce the complex Fourier matrix, and ultimately whether a different, scalable structure can beat the full arithmetic cost of an ordinary FFT for arbitrary fresh inputs.

## Reproduce

Requirements: Python 3.10+, `numpy`, `sympy`; no network access needed. From this directory:

```bash
python run_all.py
```

The script runs the **9 new tests**, unpacks `previous_research_checkpoint.zip` into a temporary directory and executes all **55 previous tests**, and redoes an exhaustive **GF(17) regular-chart audit**. It verifies the resulting finite-field counts against the stored certificate. It does not attempt to solve the characteristic-zero polynomial system.

## Results and proof scope

- The original 12 rational equations in 8 variables have a new **four-by-four determinant** necessary condition `Q` in six variables. `Q` is degree 4 and has 45 nonzero terms; it is affine in the first-stage ratio `t` and second-stage ratio `beta`.
- Three additional upper-stage polynomial resultants `R1,R2,R5` have degree 7 and 192, 300 and 330 nonzero terms respectively; `Q,R1,R2,R5` have a nonzero finite-field 4x4 Jacobian minor. This establishes their algebraic independence in characteristic zero, **not** that their common zero locus is empty.
- The three upper conditions are jointly affine in two stage ratios, `gamma1,gamma2`; their coefficient-matrix determinant `G` is another necessary test. Combining `G` with `Q` yields a five-variable determinant circuit `H` (no costly global expansion).
- An **adjacent-column Fourier-support lemma** gives two more linear equations `E3,E4`. Eliminating `t,beta` from `E3,E4,Q` gives an explicit **degree-3 polynomial W in four variables**, exactly 31 nonzero terms. A nonzero modular evaluation certifies that `W` is not identically zero over the complex numbers.
- A complete enumeration of the **stated regular branch over GF(17)** produces **no valid full second stage**, including 2,546 invertible candidate first stages. This is *not* a characteristic-zero exclusion and does not cover exceptional branches.
- A two-boundary-defect formula for a Fourier column was independently checked for N=8,16,32,64 in two exact prime fields.

**Do not claim an FFT complexity breakthrough.** All listed equations are necessary on explicit charts; none is established to be sufficient or impossible over the complex field. Ordinary radix-2 FFT still wins the counted arithmetic comparison in our previous checkpoints.
