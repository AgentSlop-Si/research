## Construction reports awaiting separate review

The global CRT chirp lift has an exact all-entry reduction and a growing shared-pole obstruction for a specified blockwise ansatz. The inverse-kernel shear construction returns to an ordinary radix-2-scale recurrence. The restricted tabulation construction targets binary-input finite-field NTTs on N=2^d−1, d a power of two, in expected O(N log log N) word operations using published Frobenius machinery. Its model, output bit size, field and length restrictions are essential. None currently establishes a new general complex FFT algorithm.

## Publication assessment

The most promising focused candidates are the exact Green decomposition-length result and the sharp dyadic endpoint cut-rank minimax invariant. The resultant factorization is useful, but a broader diagonal-pencil derivation connects much of its mechanism to standard resultant and valuation theory; its standalone publication case is narrower than the initial shortlist suggested. The saved assessment distinguishes exact proof, scoped novelty searches, unavailable prior-art text and conjectural significance.
