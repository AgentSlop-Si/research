# Fourier–Chord Rigidity (10 October 2026)

Research prototype combining the project's cycle-propagation Fourier theorem with Rigby's classical odd-polygon line-incidence result.

**Main outcome:** For odd n with a primitive arithmetic-progression frequency triple, every adjacent pair of rows of `(I - diagonal*cycle) * F_n * (I - diagonal*cycle)` has at least `n-2` nonzero columns, *regardless of weights or second-cycle permutation*. This strengthens the previous `ceil(n/2)` local result. The result is proved using published polygon geometry plus explicit determinant algebra, and a sharp example is constructed for every odd n>=5.

**Secondary:** a g-dependent interpolation for prime-length arbitrary non-affine cycles, and a logarithmic lower bound on any **exact scalar polynomial-in-K** replacement of the two-stage Green sparse bridge.

No exact o(n log n) FFT is constructed. Publication-level novelty unknown; the geometry theorem and sparse-polynomial support estimates are classical ingredients.
