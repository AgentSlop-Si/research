# Independent review: fixed-interface uniqueness and batched Fourier equivalence

Date: 2026-10-09 UTC. Verdict: the uniqueness theorem, exact density count, support-routing reduction, square equivalence, and fixed-radix near-square extension are correct. No cheap exterior factorization or faster FFT is supplied, and density establishes no arithmetic lower bound.

## 1. Fixed interface really fixes the exterior

Use positive unnormalized Fourier over a splitting field with characteristic not dividing N=mq. In bank coordinates, let B=B_J, let R0 be the stride permutation, V=B R0, A1=V S_N V^(-1), A2=V D_N V^(-1), and Y1=D_N, Y2=S_N^(-1).

The two linear equations P A1=Y1 P and P A2=Y2 P become, for Z=PV,

Z S_N=D_N Z, Z D_N=S_N^(-1)Z.

Their entry recurrences are Z[i,j+1]=omega^i Z[i,j] and Z[i+1,j]=omega^j Z[i,j]. The first gives Z[i,j]=c_i omega^(ij), and the second at j=0 makes all c_i equal. Therefore the entire homogeneous solution space is span{F_N}, equivalently P=cP0, where

P0=(F_q tensor I_m) Delta B_(J complement),
Delta[rm+t]=omega^(rt).

The scalar calibration (PV)[0,0]=1 fixes c=1. There is no need to assume the unknown P invertible before proving uniqueness; zero is the only singular homogeneous solution. The linear ranks are N^2-N for one generator, N^2-1 for both, and N^2 after calibration. One covariance plus a single scalar calibration is insufficient.

The common centralizers of either Weyl pair are scalars: commuting with D makes a matrix diagonal, and commuting with S makes that diagonal constant. This removes a fixed-interface operator gauge. It does not remove genuine redundancies among factorizations of the same P0, and does not constrain constructions using different interface data.

## 2. Density and legal representation gauges

For an active input bank r, column rm+s has nonzeros only at output indices mu+s, for all q choices of u. For an inactive input bank, every one of its m columns is nonzero in all N outputs. Thus, writing j=|J|,

nnz(P0)=N[j+(q-j)m].

A full bank has Nq nonzeros; a fixed positive inactive fraction gives Theta(N^2) entries. These count explicit materialization work, not work to apply the operator implicitly. Fourier itself is the standard warning against identifying matrix density with arithmetic complexity.

The stride Q=R0 has a trivial allowed zero-mask paired-Green representation in characteristic zero. Only P0 remains unconstructed in the desired two-layer paired-Green form.

For a proposed P0=H4 H3, an internal invertible monomial G preserves the layer class in (H4G)(G^(-1)H3) by absorption into endpoints. A within-term diagonal conjugation L U^(-1)R=(LE)(E^(-1)UE)^(-1)(E^(-1)R) preserves cycle support and the cycle-product guard. Neither gives a different target operator or a cheaper evaluator automatically. Arbitrary nonmonomial internal gauges need not preserve the class.

## 3. Full bank and correctly ordered partial stripping

Put L=F_q tensor I_m and C=B_(J complement), so P_J=L Delta C. L applies m independent length-q transforms to arbitrary, unrelated input columns.

When J is full, C=I, and P_all=L Delta while L=P_all Delta^(-1). These are both one-call reductions with O(N) twiddle and routing work, including root-table preparation.

For a partial bank,

L=P_J C^(-1) Delta^(-1).

Applied to a fresh vector, the order is first Delta^(-1), then C^(-1), then P_J. C and Delta do not generally commute. C^(-1) contains q-j ordinary inverse length-m Fourier transforms, each equivalent to a forward transform with reversal and scaling. Direct stripping therefore pays (q-j)T(m)+O(N) extra work. Those transforms cannot be called free preprocessing.

An exact negative control at N=16 confirms that swapping C^(-1) and Delta^(-1) breaks the identity. The direct cost inequality is not a lower bound requiring every alternative P evaluator to execute the displayed complementary transforms.

## 4. Support routing removes inverse transforms with bounded calls

Suppose J is a nonempty cyclic interval of j blocks beginning at a0. Partition input block indices into intervals I_l={lj,...,min((l+1)j,q)-1}, for 0<=l<k=ceil(q/j). Let E_I select the corresponding coordinates, U_a=S_q^a tensor I_m, M_a=D_q^a tensor I_m, and a_l=a0-lj modulo q.

U_(a_l) moves every coordinate in I_l into J. A vector v supported in J remains supported there after Delta^(-1); hence C Delta^(-1)v=Delta^(-1)v. This is a support statement, not a global commutation of C and Delta. Consequently

P_J Delta^(-1)v=L v.

Since L U_a=M_a L, each masked input satisfies

M_(-a_l) P_J Delta^(-1) U_(a_l) E_(I_l)=L E_(I_l).

The selectors partition the full input, so summing gives the exact operator identity

L=sum_l M_(-a_l) P_J Delta^(-1) U_(a_l) E_(I_l).

This uses k forward P_J applications and O(kN) masking, shifts, phases and additions, with no inverse Fourier call and no hidden pretransformed state. P preparation is paid once and reused, and sequential calls need O(N) scratch. The original input is unrestricted; the masked pieces are internally formed and recombined exactly.

For j>=theta q with fixed theta>0, k is bounded by ceil(1/theta). The interval condition is material. The proof extends if J merely contains a sufficiently long interval, and hence to a fixed number of active intervals with fixed total density, but does not infer a bounded translate cover from arbitrary density alone.

For empty J the displayed formula is undefined and unused; F_N=P_empty R0 already uses one P call. Full J has k=1 and reduces to the previous one-call batch identity.

## 5. Square split: both FFT banks from the same batch

For m=q=M, the grid transpose K satisfies B_all=K L K. Therefore

B_J=E_J B_all+(I-E_J)

uses one full-bank evaluation followed by selection of transformed active coordinates and original inactive coordinates. Combining this with F_N=P_J B_J R0 yields k+1 calls to P_J plus O(kN) work.

A full bank needs two P_all calls; a contiguous half-active bank needs three P_J calls. Every intermediate vector is explicitly formed from fresh data. In particular, an O(N)-prepared and O(N)-applicable P_J for a fixed positive active interval fraction would give an O(N) evaluator for the full square-length Fourier transform. This is conditional; no such P_J implementation has been supplied.

Conversely, C=E_J+(I-E_J)K L K, so P_J=L Delta C uses at most two L calls plus O(N) work for any J. Thus, for the constant-fraction interval setting, the exterior and the arbitrary-input batch are equivalent up to constant call factors and linear overhead. The full bank has the sharper one-call equivalence. This does not turn a batch evaluator of size N into an O(q) algorithm for a single length-q transform by uncharged padding.

## 6. Near-square fixed-radix routing

Let m=rq for a fixed positive integer r. For bank b<q, write its input index as h+rs, with h<r, s<q. Route it to L-input index s*m+(br+h). This permutation places exactly m arbitrary length-q vectors into the batch.

After the L call, call the value at t*m+(br+h) Y_(b,h,t). The output of the length-m transform is

sum_(h=0)^(r-1) omega_m^(ht) omega_r^(hu) Y_(b,h,t)

at bank coordinate b*m+(t+qu). This follows by expanding the Fourier exponent (t+qu)(h+rs). It is a length-r DFT after twiddles for each of the q^2=N/r pairs (b,t).

Thus one L application plus (N/r)T(r)+O(N) implements B_all. For fixed r this is linear additional work; r=1 is transpose routing and r=2 gives ordinary butterflies. Therefore the k+1 P_J-call full Fourier reduction remains valid for the usual balanced dyadic splits m/q in {1,2}. A growing r cannot be silently assigned linear cost.

## 7. Independent exact controls

The reviewer executed only newly authored code, without importing or executing constructor scripts. Fifty-two exact cases cover empty/full banks and every nontrivial interval length with both ordinary and wrapped starts at configurations

(q,m)=(2,2),(3,3),(4,4),(5,5),(2,4),(3,6),(4,8),(3,9).

They verify the original CT product, exact exterior nonzero count, correctly ordered stripping, active-support routing, complete operator-sum recovery of L, explicit radix conversion of B_all, k+1-call full Fourier reconstruction, and the square reverse reduction. Radices one, two, and three are included. Roots are primitive of the required order in each checked prime field.

Nine additional controls at N=4,6,8 verify the actual matrix-entry linear systems, with empty, one-block, and full activity. All one-generator, two-generator, and calibrated ranks agree with the claimed formulas. The N=16 wrong-order negative control rejects commuting the stripping factors.

All tests and an isolated copied replay pass with Python's standard library. The finite checks validate signs, indexing, guards and boundaries; the displayed proofs establish the general statements.

## 8. Research consequence

The fixed CT interface uniquely identifies an exterior that already represents the missing arbitrary-input Fourier batch. Its compact sparse interface equations do not evaluate that batch. The new support-routing identity makes the conditional equivalence especially explicit and removes the inverse-bank cost from the bounded-call reduction.

A cheap legal paired-Green factorization of that same exterior remains a real constructive possibility, and is not excluded by uniqueness or density. No such factorization, no general arithmetic lower bound, and no new faster FFT are established in these reviewed reports.
