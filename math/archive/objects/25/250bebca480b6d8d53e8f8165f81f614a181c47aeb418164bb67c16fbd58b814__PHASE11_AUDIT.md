## 1. Quotient projector states and the complete DFT

Assume a field with N invertible and a primitive Nth rootw; define X_k=sum_j w^(−kj)x_j. For d|N, m=N/d, put

C_(d,r)[t]=sum_(u=0)^(d−1) x_(t+um) w^(−rum).

Character orthogonality gives

(P_(d,r)x)_(t+am)=d^(−1) w^(ram) C_(d,r)[t].

Thus one level has d states of lengthm, exactlyN quotient coordinates. For dyadicN and m even, separating even and odd summation indices gives

C_(2d,r)[t]=a+w^(−rm/2)b,
C_(2d,r+d)[t]=a−w^(−rm/2)b,

witha=C_(d,r)[t], b=C_(d,r)[t+m/2]. At d=N these are allN Fourier coefficients. The source's indexing and sign agree with this proof. It is exactly ordinary radix-2 computation in frequency-residue coordinates; no whole projector vector need be stored per mask.

At every level the coefficient matrix is a permutation of a direct sum of invertible d-point DFTs, hence full rankN. Across differentd, support sizes differ. At fixedd, differentt have disjoint support; same d,t but distinctr have coefficient1 at the first support coordinate and distinct next coefficient. Therefore all nodes are distinct even up to scalar multiples. This excludes only literal equality/scalar-equivalence merging of the specified symbolic states. It is not a gate-count lower bound for all algebraic circuits or a proof that alternative algebraic sharing is impossible.

Normal capture=False retains the root table, old layer and new layer, at most3N+O(1) field words beyond the caller's input. capture=True deliberately retains all levels and usesO(NlogN) memory; README explicitly says so. The debug symbolic generator has quadratic-size coefficient data and is outside the online executor.

## 2. Sharp rational fixed-shift rank distance

For Sx[j]=x[j+1], F diagonalizesS with eigenvaluesw^k. With q(w^k) nonzero for everyk,

rank(P_K−p(S)q(S)^−1)=#{k:p(w^k)/q(w^k) differs from1_K(k)}.

If p=0, the rank is|K|; ifp=q, it isN−|K|. Otherwise p andp−q are nonzero polynomials of degree<=d. Agreement outsideK requires a root ofp; agreement insideK requires a root ofp−q. There are at most2d agreements, giving rank>=N−2d. This is root counting over any valid splitting field; it does not use a complex-only uncertainty bound.

For1<=d<=min(|K|,N−|K|), selectd roots outsideK andd inside, form their monic polynomialsA,B, and choose p=A, q=A−lambda B. The selected roots never makeq zero whenlambda!=0. Each other root forbids at most one nonzerolambda. A field containingN distinct nonzero roots has at leastN nonzero elements, while at mostN−2d choices are forbidden. An allowedlambda exists. The two exact root sets give exactly2d agreements, proving sharpness N−2d.

For balancedK andd<=N/4, trivial0 orI attains the global minimumN/2, while nontrivial filters have error>=N−2d. A product of commuting rational functions of the sameS has degree bounded by the sum, with all denominator guards retained. The theorem does not constrain sparse high-degree polynomials or different noncommuting/permuted shifts. In particular P_even=(I+S^(N/2))/2 is a two-tapO(N) application outside the bounded-degree premise.

The witness routine is constructive but is an audit generator, not a charged linear-time synthesis algorithm: repeated candidate evaluation, root powers, polynomial multiplication, inversions and set sorting must all be paid if it is used algorithmically. The theorem gives existence without making arbitrary coefficient recovery free.

## 3. Full rank can be cheap

Foralpha!=0 andalpha^N!=1,

G=(I−alphaS)^−1=(1−alpha^N)^−1 sum_(t=0)^(N−1) alpha^t S^t.

Every matrix entry is nonzero. Its eigenvalues1/(1−alpha w^k) are neither zero nor one, so rank(P_K−G)=N for every mask, including empty/full masks. Yet the scalar cyclic recurrence computesGx in linear field work: compute the boundary y0 by a Horner sum divided by1−alpha^N, then work backward around the cycle. The source's recurrence is correct.

For evenN, bothP_even andG admitO(N) applications after their scalar setup, hence their full-rank difference does too. This supplies an explicit positive control against the false inference “full correction rank forces superlinear execution.” It does not produce all Fourier coefficients. In a minimal field where every nonzeroalpha resonates, this particular example has no admissiblealpha; the guard is part of the theorem rather than an existence guarantee for every field.

## 5. Exact counts, cold costs and static implementation qualifications

For the implemented binary-power routine, B(e)=bit_length(e)−1+popcount(e) for positivee. At dyadicN>=2, root validation usesB(N)+B(N/2), and the shared power table usesN−1 products. The normal transform usesN/2 multiplications andN additions per level, including trivial twiddles. Thus its literal field count is

3N log2N/2 + N−1+B(N)+B(N/2).

Independent arithmetic reproduces all four recorded totals:3344,16404,77848,1638432 atN256,1024,4096,65536. These are static-loop formula checks, not reruns of those large source transforms. Skipping trivial products changes constants, not the explicit algorithm's order. Root validation checks a supplied root; it does not discover a root or certify thatp is prime. The prime-field contract must be honored.

The Green routine performs exactly4N−1 online field additions/multiplications in its literal loops. Preparation addsB(N)+1+B(p−2): poweringalpha, one denominator subtraction and charged Fermat inversion. Therefore its cold field work isO(N+logp+logN). The source's polynomial-size-field convention makes thisO(N); unrestricted supplied primes require thelogp term. Exact complex/rational inversion and scalar bit growth have their own models. No floating error or stable precision bound is established.

**Traffic correction:** apply_one_pole_green readsx once in the Horner loop and again forN−1 backward equations. Literal input-array reads are2N−1, while Meter.input_reads records onlyN. It also omits theN−1 recurrence reads fromy. In the lazy transform, semantic state_writes excludes zero-list initialization and table reads; integer/index counters are coarse allowances, not a complete Python or word-machine trace. capture/debug storage is separately excluded. These omissions do not change linear-versus-NlogN field asymptotics, but “fully charged” must not be interpreted as an exact all-traffic total.

The rational diagnostic functions assume their stated valid field/root/mask contracts and are not hardened public APIs validating every out-of-range frequency. The uploaded audit imports many source routines, so its label is not whole-library implementation independence. Random denominator tests can encounter a pole in another field/seed; the mathematical theorem requires rejecting such cases. Historical tests and timings remain source reports.
