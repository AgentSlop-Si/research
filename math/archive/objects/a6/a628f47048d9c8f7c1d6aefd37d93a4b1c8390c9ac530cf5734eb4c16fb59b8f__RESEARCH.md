# Hero #17 — Phase 13: Branching Weyl–Green Union

**Date:** October 10, 2026  
**Main objective:** Move beyond Phase 12's inverse-sparse *product* obstruction by examining parallel sums of noncommuting Green paths. Decide whether a genuinely shared exact `O(N)` full Fourier circuit exists for arbitrary fresh inputs.

**Verdict:** A new **restricted branching certificate**, a uniform rotated-Weyl/Hankel obstruction for fixed two-resolvent directions at prime lengths, and an explicit dense-inverse two-branch **non-Fourier** operator applicable in `O(N)`. **No universal exact `O(N)` DFT, no new general integer-multiplication bound, and no claim of previously unknown mathematics.**

## 1. What changed since Phase 12

Phase 12 excluded short *products* of invertible sparse-inverse Green factors plus small total-rank perturbations by an uncertainty-principle gate. It did **not** exclude a **sum of several Green paths**, and the rank of a matrix (or of its Weyl coefficient array) is not an operation-count lower bound.

Phase 13 handles these subtleties in three complementary ways:

1. Use the full **Weyl coefficient matrix** to rule out sums of few paths in one fixed `G_S * G_T` ordering.
2. Rotate the Weyl generators. A rank-one representation *does* exist in coefficient space, but exact **Hankel-rank** constraints prevent realizing its quadratic chirps as a bounded number of geometric Green kernels.
3. Construct a two-branch noncommuting Green operator whose **inverse is completely dense**, yet whose forward application takes true `O(N)` operations on arbitrary fresh vectors. Also explicitly construct reverse-order and three-stage paths of full Weyl coefficient rank that remain linear-time. These counterexamples stop us from overgeneralizing the negative theorem.

## 2. Exact Fourier/Weyl coefficient identity

Use the positive-exponent DFT `F_N[i,j]=ω^(ij)`, cyclic shift `S e_j=e_(j+1)`, and modulation `T e_j=ω^j e_j`, `TS=ω ST`.

For every odd `N`, the Fourier operator's unique Weyl coefficients are

`C_F[a,b] = Γ_N ω^(-(a-b)^2/4)`,

where `Γ_N=(1/N)Σ_(j=0)^(N-1) ω^(j²) !=0`. A diagonal–Fourier–diagonal factorization proves `rank(C_F)=N`. Every entry is nonzero.

For `G_S(α)=(I-αS)^(-1)` and `G_T(β)=(I-βT)^(-1)`, with nonsingular denominators,

`C_(G_S G_T)[a,b] = α^a β^b / ((1-α^N)(1-β^N))`.

This is rank one and remains rank one after Weyl-monomial factors at either endpoint. Therefore **at least N branches** of this exact fixed-orientation grammar would be needed to equal `F_N`. See Theorems 1–2 of `PROOF_CERTIFICATE.md`.

## 3. The apparent rank-one escape is real—and deceptive

Choose the Weyl generators `U=S`, `V=ST`. In the ordered `U^iV^j` basis the **same Fourier operator** has coefficient matrix

`C'_(i,j)=Γ_N ω^(-i²/4 -j(j-1)/2)`.

It has rank **one**, even though the canonical coefficient matrix has rank `N`. The low-rank tensor looks ideal for Union; however, neither one-dimensional factor is a geometric sequence. The `m x m` nonwrapping Hankel matrix for the first chirp, with `m=(N+1)/2`, is diagonal times a Vandermonde matrix times diagonal, hence has full rank `m`. Any sum of `B` geometric sequences has Hankel rank at most `B`. **Thus `B>=m`** for sums of ordered `G_S * G_(ST)` Green paths without endpoint gauges.

More generally, for **any** generating Weyl pair `U=S^uT^v`, `V=S^rT^s` at odd *prime* length `p`, put

`D=us-vr`, `δ=[D-(ur+vs)]/2`.

The Fourier coefficient matrix in the ordered basis has rank `p` if `δ!=0` and rank one if `δ=0`. In the latter case an elementary quadratic identity forces both residual chirps to be nondegenerate; Hankel rank recovers the lower bound. Therefore **every fixed generating Weyl pair needs at least `(p+1)/2` ordered one-pole two-Green branches** to express the full DFT. This does not cover mixing different pairs across branches, arbitrary gauges, higher-depth networks or a jointly evaluated compressed circuit.

## 4. A necessary escape from rank-only reasoning

The **reversed** product `G_T(β) G_S(α)` already has *full* canonical Weyl coefficient rank `N`, because its coefficients are proportional to `α^a β^b ω^(ab)`. Yet it is computed with one cyclic Green solve and one diagonal multiplication, i.e. `O(N)` work for every fresh input.

Likewise the three-stage noncommuting product `G_S(α)G_T(β)G_S(γ)` has full canonical Weyl coefficient rank `N` whenever its Green parameters are nonzero and nonsingular. It is still evaluated in `O(N)` arithmetic: one cyclic solve, a diagonal multiplication, and another cyclic solve.

This is an explicit **counterexample** to any proposed general lower bound obtained merely from full Weyl rank or full noncommutativity. The fixed ordering assumptions of the negative result are essential.

## 5. Positive branching witness with dense inverse

Define

`H(α,β) = (I-α S)^(-1) + (I-β T)^(-1)`.

It is a sum, not a product. For every `N>=2` over complex numbers, choose `0<α<1` and sufficiently small nonzero `β`: both inverses exist, they do not commute, and **every entry of `H(α,β)^(-1)` is nonzero**. At `β=0`, the exact identity

`H(α,0)^(-1) = I - (2I-α S)^(-1)`

shows the full inverse support; continuity extends it to small nonzero β. So H cannot equal an uncorrected product of `L` two-sparse-inverse Green stages when `2^L<N`. Phase 12's prior chain obstruction does not describe this branch sum.

Nevertheless `H x` costs `O(N)` arithmetic for any fresh dense vector, with all `N` outputs:

- Cyclic recurrence for `(I-αS)^(-1)x` using **one** field inversion and `O(N)` multiplications/additions;
- Prepare the `N` diagonal values `(1-β ω^j)^(-1)` in **O(N)** work using prefix products and one shared inversion;
- Multiply and add the two length-N outputs.

**Do not confuse this with a Fourier algorithm:** `H!=F_N`, as verified explicitly on complete finite-field matrices and on independent Fourier bins at size 65,536.

## 8. Phase 14 target

Investigate **orientation-mixed and depth-three branching Green circuits with cross-path sharing**. Phase 13 shows that a single reversed or triple path can already have full Weyl coefficient rank and linear application, so generic matrix-rank testing will be insufficient. The target is a **charged algebraic joint evaluator** that actually equals the complete DFT on *every* basis vector, not another full-rank Green surrogate.

Suggested concrete gate: derive the finite-dimensional algebra of summed mixed-order Green paths, find a basis-independent invariant that captures the **complexity of composing the recurrence profiles** (not their static ranks), and test small exact widths against both standard FFT and the published specialized sub-`N log N` construction under comparable models. Reject any proposal whose coefficient preparation, output extraction, or hidden polynomial-transform work exceeds its savings.
