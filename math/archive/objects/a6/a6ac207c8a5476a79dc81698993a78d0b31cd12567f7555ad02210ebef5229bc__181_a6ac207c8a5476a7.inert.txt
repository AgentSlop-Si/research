# FFT Word-RAM shrinking-error decoder v4

Continuation of the recovered sparse-surrogate / finite-state lookup research path.

## Main result under study

For fresh arbitrary finite-precision input and all `N` unitary DFT outputs:

- input-independent preprocessing: `N^gamma polylog(N) = o(N)`;
- online word-RAM work: `O(N)`;
- randomized all-bin error:

  `O(loglog(N)/sqrt(log(N))) * L_box(x)/sqrt(N)`.

A second tradeoff point uses `O(N log N/loglog N)` online word operations and obtains the smaller factor `O((loglog N)^(3/2)/log N)`.

This is approximate and model-specific. It is not an exact FFT or an integer-multiplication result.
