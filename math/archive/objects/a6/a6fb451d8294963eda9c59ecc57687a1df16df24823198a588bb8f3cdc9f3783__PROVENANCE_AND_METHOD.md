# Provenance and review method

- Assignment: the exact snapshot inputs/ASSIGNMENT.json names 12 batch inventories and 45 unique source hashes. All 12 inventories are preserved byte-for-byte in inputs/.
- Prior coverage: inputs/ADDITIVE_CODE_COVERAGE.json is the exact read-time snapshot of the supplied prior registry. It records 184 frozen reads, 385 post-inventory reads and 569 total reads. None of the 45 assigned hashes appear in its rows. The delta is 45 against this snapshot; the live registry may advance through other work. This package does not modify it or claim a current global total.
- Each local_content byte stream was hashed and checked against the assigned SHA-256 and byte length before copying into sources/<sha256>. All 45 passed: 221428 bytes and 5090 lines in total. Each entire source was read as text, including all functions, top-level behavior and entry points. A truncated display section was separately reread; no omitted region is counted as reviewed.
- SOURCE_REVIEW.json contains one explicit full-static-inspection row per hash, full line intervals, semantic analysis, individual claim disposition, findings, dependencies and execution limitations. Coverage is based on full text inspection, not inferred from AST resemblance, basename identity, a prior theorem, a self-description or a matching archive manifest.
- SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json preserves every assignment occurrence and origin field. There are 21 rows classified by the inventory as source_snapshot_or_library_program and 24 origin_unresolved rows. Roles inferred from content do not change those classes. Archive-member paths, indices, archive hashes and Library IDs are inventory-provided provenance assertions; the parent archives were not re-extracted or independently revalidated in this review.
- Supplementary inert JSON was copied from the master_v25 tree only after confirming that the neighboring S01, S21 and S29 source bytes match their assigned hashes. DATA_INPUT_PROVENANCE.json records original locations, snapshot paths, byte counts and hashes. These inputs support the fresh cut check and data-only metadata/profile comparisons; they are not extra source-code coverage.
- No assigned program was executed, imported, compiled or AST-parsed, including files named independent_checks.py, prepare_*.py, run_all.py or verify_seal.py. No imported external source dependency was executed. The input inventories may contain earlier syntax/AST metadata; it was preserved as historical provenance, not used as evidence of this review's source coverage.
- Fresh reviewer code reviewer_checks.py was authored and executed solely for independent elementary graph/algebra controls and inert-data comparisons. Its command was: python green_geometry_source_review_0430/reviewer_checks.py. Exit status was zero, with stdout captured in INDEPENDENT_CHECKS.log and details in INDEPENDENT_CHECKS.json. build_review.py assembles the manually authored review into JSON; it does not analyze or execute the supplied programs.
- Positive fresh results: N2048 cut size 676 versus a 683-rank Fourier block; finite-field determinant 24105; 872 direct small permutation rank checks; an exact rational dense-inverse counterexample; 16 mislabeled saved probe rows out of 17; all six stored arithmetic profiles above their stated baseline. The operation counts themselves, source test suites, source runtime outputs, archived exhaustive rank streams, full Phase32 fingerprints and external theorem documents were not replayed.
- Source snapshots and final files are made read-only by convention and sealed by SEALED_MANIFEST.json plus SEALED_MANIFEST.sha256. This protects a stable snapshot when the seal digest is retained externally; it is not hardware-enforced immutability or a cryptographic signature. VERIFY_PACKAGE.py is fresh verifier code included in the seal. The supplied S45 verifier was not run.
