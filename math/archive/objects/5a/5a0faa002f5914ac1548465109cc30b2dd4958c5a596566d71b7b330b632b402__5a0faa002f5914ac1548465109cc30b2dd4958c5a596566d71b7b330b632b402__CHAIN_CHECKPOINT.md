## Crucial proofs and traps

- Hankel factorization: `H_h=V_h diag(B_j[h]) V_h^T`; invertible when all B !=0 and eigenvalues distinct.
- Trace character sum: `s[h]=-coeff_{z^(g-1)} P_h(z)=sum_j mu_j eta^(-a_j h)`; DFT of s places M*mu_j at ell=-a_j mod M.
- Complete transform: `F[h+sM] = sum_j B_j[h] * G_Q(mu_j * omega^(-(h+sM+Q*a_j*h)))`.
- If `z=1`, use `G_Q(1)=Q`; never invert zero. `inverse_for_scaled_roots` builds all nonzero inverses with one inversion per scalar, O(gN) preparation.
- Single-gauge pair majority: there are `M*phi(M)/2` coprime-separated pairs. e corrupt spectral channels invalidate at most `e*phi(M)`, so e<M/4 ensures a strict majority. Radix join, not a free discrete log oracle.
- Model: exact F_p, p=N^O(1), p>N, supplied primitive Nth root; primitive root discovery and general prime search NOT charged. Word-RAM modular arithmetic. No numeric stability or bit complexity claimed.
- Green impulse kernel: `w_j(h)=lambda_j^(g-1)/prod_(i!=j)(lambda_j-lambda_i)`; spectral event `(t,h,c)` adds `M*c*omega^(-kt)*sum_j w_j(h)*G_(Q-t)(mu_j*omega^(-(k+Q*a_j*h)))` for k=h+sM. Charged preparation of phase roots and batch inversions and z=1 exception.
- Constant g orbit family covers at most `binom(M,g)*(p-1)^g*p^(gM)` raw vectors, exponentially negligible among p^N for fixed g. g=M spans all vectors but is superlinear with this executor. Avoid the earlier mistake of equating maximal matrix rank with high input entropy.
