GALOIS JOINT-ORBIT STABILITY ASSESSMENT
2026-10-09

Verdict

The central all-length packing theorem and its finite-precision corollary are valid. This is a useful restriction on one proposed reconstruction interface, not a new exact FFT algorithm, an arithmetic-circuit lower bound, or an improvement to sparse expansion. No false central theorem was found.

The archive examines the dense binary family x(u)=(u,1-u), N=2m a power of two. Every signal has exactly m=N/2 nonzeros. Consequently its exponential-in-N bound does not directly apply to the project's k=Theta(log N) sparse-input regime. It also does not apply to our fused recurrence or the conditional thin-matrix expansion, both of which retain original input or additional intermediate state.

Input and verification scope

- Read all four prose documents, all five Python implementation/audit files, package.py, the test file, all three result JSON files, and all four output logs.
- Independently checked the 18 manifest hashes and byte identity of the two Galois upload directories: all match. These are one submission, not independent corroborations.
- Independently recalculated all 24 reported integer quantization thresholds and the five polynomial-condition representative thresholds using new arithmetic-only checking code. All match the supplied records.
- Did not execute, import, or install any uploaded code. The supplied test log reports 13 passing tests; this audit does not claim to have rerun those tests.

1. Valid mathematical content

The even-bin formula follows directly from zeta^m=-1:
  X_u[0]=m and X_u[2j]=0 for j=1,...,m-1.
For odd k,
  X_u[k]=sum_{j=0}^{m-1}(2u_j-1) zeta^(kj).
Since Phi_N(z)=z^m+1, the first m powers are a rational basis, so X_u[1] identifies u exactly. In fact every odd k is an automorphic image, so every individual odd bin is injective on this family. The archive's collision contingency is unnecessary here, although harmless.

For fixed selected odd indices k_1,...,k_r, all measurement vectors lie in [-m,m]^(2r). With a=floor(m/(2r)) and M=2^a-1, M^(2r)<2^m when M>=1. Two inputs therefore have max-complex measurement distance at most 2 sqrt(2) m/M. Their input difference has l2 norm at least sqrt(2), and unnormalized Parseval yields a full-spectrum max difference at least sqrt(2). Any exact measurement-only decoder has
  L >= M/(2m) = [2^floor(N/(4r))-1]/N.
No continuity assumption outside this finite family, computational restriction, or degree restriction is needed.

Thus L<=N^C for fixed C requires r=Omega(N/log N). For the usual one-bin-per-conductor set {0,1,2,4,...,N/2}, every varying datum except X[1] disappears on this family, giving L>=(2^(N/4)-1)/N. This is the genuinely useful increment over a scalar-polynomial-degree obstruction: nonlinear, branching, or high-degree joint recovery cannot make this bottleneck polynomially conditioned.

Both measurement and output normalization by the same factor preserve L. This is an absolute Lipschitz amplification bound in the specified norms, not a claim about every conventional relative condition number.

Minor available strengthening, not an error: the spectrum difference has only N/2 potentially nonzero entries, so its maximum is at least 2, improving the packing constant to sqrt(2)M/N. Also L>=1 because the full output includes the measured coordinates. Neither changes the asymptotics or research conclusion.

2. Valid quantization corollary, with limits

For grid spacing 2^-b in each real/imaginary coordinate, each coordinate has at most N*2^b+2 possible codes. Distinguishing the family requires
  (N*2^b+2)^(2r) >= 2^(N/2),
and hence b >= N/(4r)-log2(N+2). These are necessary conditions only.

The reported necessary integer thresholds for r=1 are correct: N=32,64,128,256,1024,4096 gives b=3,10,25,56,246,1012 respectively. In particular, 246 fractional bits at N=1024 is a capacity lower bound, not a sufficient precision specification for stable recovery.

This is a bit-information obstruction for uniform quantized numerical measurements. It does not price an arithmetic operation on an exact complex/algebraic field element, prove that every exact algorithm uses that precision, or rule out an exact unit-cost circuit with large amplification. Symbolic coefficient vectors store the information differently and must have their own representation/output costs charged.

Fixed frequency selection matters: the supplied theorem does not expressly model adaptive indices, uncharged branch transcripts, other input-dependent side information, or access to the original input. Such information must be included in a new capacity calculation rather than silently treated as free.

3. Positive control and arithmetic relevance

For this real complementary family, knowing odd bins 1,3,...,N/2-1 supplies one member of every conjugate pair. Conjugation supplies the other odd bins and the even bins are known constants. This decoder is exactly 1-Lipschitz, so r=N/4 is a valid measurement-count upper bound. Evaluating those r frequencies remains charged work; there is no algorithmic speedup here.

The all-odd orbit is itself an ordinary modulated m-point DFT: writing k=2ell+1,
  X_u[2ell+1] = sum_j [(2u_j-1) zeta^j] (zeta^2)^(ell*j).
Thus an evaluator using the original coefficient array already bypasses the measurement-only bottleneck in the familiar way. The archive supplies no cheaper fully charged DAG for that evaluator.

Its finite-field program verifies specializations of polynomial identities at roots in F_998244353. That is valid exact arithmetic checking, but it cannot prove the complex metric bound, numerical separation, or characteristic-zero injectivity. Raising a chosen root to odd powers in this split prime field should not be mistaken for a nontrivial field automorphism of F_998244353.

4. Numerical and test audit

- The stated small-N witnesses are supported by source/result consistency. N=32 masks 29439 and 35835 give the displayed nonzero power-basis difference, an approximately 0.005342059489 primitive gap, and an approximately 8.241035291 full-spectrum gap. Exact distinctness follows from the basis proof; approximate closeness comes from numerical evaluation.
- KD-tree queries minimize Euclidean distance in the 2r real coordinates. For r>1 this is not the theorem's max-complex distance, nor does minimizing either measured gap necessarily maximize output/input sensitivity. The code nevertheless yields admissible pair witnesses, as the prose largely acknowledges.
- Only the selected-bin gap is recomputed with mpmath. The full-spectrum numerator remains a float64 NumPy FFT result. Printing the resulting ratio to 25-30 digits does not make those digits accurate. Use rounded values such as 1542.67 and 198.73, not a purported certified high-precision condition number.
- Neither floating-point nearest-neighbor search nor the mpmath recheck is an exact globally minimal-gap certificate. No interval enclosure is provided for a rigorously numerical lower bound. The symbolic packing theorem is independent of this issue.
- Quantization counts use floor on float64 values. Points on or near grid boundaries make those counts especially sensitive to rounding. They remain illustrative; they do not certify exact collision counts or an exact no-collision precision threshold.
- RESEARCH.md line 150 says the test suite checks growth with b. The supplied 13-test file never imports precision_audit and has no explicit quantization/bit-threshold test. That coverage statement is overstated; the integer thresholds were independently checked in this audit.
- The unittest.main() guard precedes JointOrbitControls. The documented unittest discovery command includes all 13 tests, but executing tests/test_stability.py directly would miss the two later tests. This does not contradict the supplied discovery log.
- The exact modular loop performs 112 test cases and 1680 odd-coordinate checks. At small N there are repeated masks, so these are not 112 distinct exhaustive inputs.

5. Prior art and novelty boundary

The manuscript appropriately makes no publication-priority claim for its elementary packing argument or Galois identities. Frobenius FFT speedups in extension finite fields are established prior work and do not transfer automatically to a unit-cost characteristic-zero complex model or to explicit bit-cost materialization.

Primary-source check: Li, Chen, Kuo, Cheng, and Yang, Frobenius Additive Fast Fourier Transform (2018), https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.03932, explicitly summarizes the 2017 van der Hoeven-Larrieu Frobenius FFT result and extends it to additive FFTs. The archive's original 2017 DOI is https://doi.org/10.1145/3087604.3087633; the DOI endpoint was unavailable during this audit, so no claim of inspecting that original paper is made.

The valid contribution to this project is a quantitative rejection test for compressed-measurement-only stable reconstruction, not a proof that a new exact dense FFT is impossible. It neither validates nor refutes the unverified uniform thin-matrix-construction premise in the other research branch.

6. Bounded integration recommendation

Integrate this as one proof-backed gate in the common research specification:
  Record what varying information the decoder receives. If it receives only fixed selected Fourier scalars on the complementary family, require the stated representative/precision tradeoff. If it retains original coefficients, sparse support, or intermediate forms, apply a different analysis and fully charge that state.

Keep the exact-arithmetic O(N log k) sparse baseline and the conditional thin-matrix route unchanged. Add three independent regression specifications before relying on the archive as a reusable verification component: (i) integer capacity thresholds with minimality at b-1, (ii) direct high-precision evaluation of both numerator and denominator for the explicit witnesses, and (iii) conjugate reconstruction on the dense positive-control family. No new exhaustive search or optimizer is justified by this archive alone.
