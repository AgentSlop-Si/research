Fused Fourier reconstruction and the thin matrix lead
Research checkpoint October 9 2026

RESULT
Two outcomes matter. First, the intermediate spectrum-extension and final-decoding maps can be fused algebraically. Independently tested exact identities remove those separate stages, but the conventional resulting recurrence/convolution algorithm still costs O(N log k). Second, a focused literature check found explicit O(n^2) TOTAL-operation claims for n-by-log n times log n-by-n matrix multiplication. If their uniform exact arithmetic interpretation is correct, an elementary, fully costed reshape gives O(N) for arbitrary k=O(log N) exponential sums.

The second outcome is a stronger lead than the previous pass reported. Chan's paper explicitly uses operation-count wording; Reif's lecture notes corroborate time wording. The original Brockett-Dobkin abstract states multiplication count. We could not inspect the original construction to independently charge additions, encoding and decoding. This is an unresolved verification gap, not evidence that the cited O(n^2) claim is false.

No novel algorithm, general dense FFT breakthrough, integer-multiplication improvement, practical speedup, or bit-complexity theorem is established in this checkpoint.

WHAT IS PROVED HERE
- Conditional on a uniform O(n^2) total-arithmetic thin-matrix algorithm over the intended field, all N values f_m=sum_s a_s z_s^m with k=O(log N) cost O(N). Root preparation, two power tables, constant-number inner-dimension groups, summation, and output are charged.
- Decoded-state fusion replaces the separate BM spectral/terminal maps by recurrence extension. Its ordinary implementation remains O(N log k).
- Rational sampling on t^S=c requires an explicit amplitude or modular-numerator correction to recover the desired finite sequence.
- Resonant modes are lost from the corrected rational quotient and require explicit grid impulses. At a full period, these impulses recover the original sparse Fourier input, exposing the circularity of treating that case as a free solution.

WHAT WAS TESTED
All tests use exact modular arithmetic over F_97 and standard-library Python. These are algebraic checks, not fast implementations or timing comparisons.
- 960 cyclic rational cases, including 200 resonant cases and 384 resonant grid values; all passed. Modular numerator correction also passed in all 960 cases.
- 360 independent decoded-state trees, 1,872 branches, and 11,997 output coefficients; all matched direct expansion.
- Additional fusion checks: 542 full trees, 4,890 each of one-step/macro-step/BM-intertwining checks, 216 transposed-remainder checks and 130 generic convolution embeddings; all passed.
- 540 oversampled subtrees, with 7,230 decoded coefficients and 540 modular corrections; all passed.
- Every supplied script was inspected, rerun and compiled successfully.
