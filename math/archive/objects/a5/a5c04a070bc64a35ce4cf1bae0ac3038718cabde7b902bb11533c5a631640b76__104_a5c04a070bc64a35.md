# Two-forward-cycle-stage spectral obstruction

2026-10-09. Independently verified bounded algebraic extension. This is a necessary-condition gate for the specified two-stage family, not a universal FFT lower bound or a literature-wide novelty claim.

## Statement

Let N be dyadic with N≥8. For i=1,2, let M_i be a unit-modulus weighted full N-cycle satisfying M_i^N=-I, and define

K_i=I+(I-M_i)^-1.

No product

α U_0 K_1 U_1 K_2 U_2

with α≠0 and arbitrary unitary U_0,U_1,U_2 is a nonzero scalar multiple of a unitary matrix. Thus it cannot equal either the normalized or unnormalized Fourier matrix. Separate nonzero global scalars attached to the stages are absorbed into α.

This statement uses two forward K stages. It does not cover coordinate-dependent gain diagonals, inverse K^-1 stages, or three or more forward stages. It assumes the complex unit-modulus model; it is not a finite-field norm statement.

## Proof

Each M_i is unitary. Its eigenvalues are all N distinct roots of z^N=-1, because it is a full weighted cycle with total cycle weight -1. Therefore both K_i have the same singular-value multiset, whose squares are

f(c_j)=(5-4c_j)/(2-2c_j),
c_j=cos((2j+1)π/N),  j=0,…,N-1.

Indeed, on an eigenvector with eigenvalue e^{iθ}, K has eigenvalue (2-e^{iθ})/(1-e^{iθ}), with squared magnitude f(cos θ). The function f is strictly increasing for c<1, since f'(c)=2/(2-2c)^2>0.

End unitaries and a global scalar do not affect whether the middle product is scalar-unitary, so suppose K_1 U K_2=β V with U,V unitary and β≠0. Then

U K_2 K_2* U*=|β|²(K_1* K_1)^-1.

Let λ_1≥…≥λ_N>0 be the common sorted squared-singular-value spectrum of K_1 and K_2. Equality of the above spectra forces

λ_i λ_{N+1-i}=|β|² for every i.

For even N, the multiset of cosines c_j is symmetric under c→-c. Monotonicity of f therefore makes reverse spectral pairs correspond to c and -c. Their products are

f(c)f(-c)=(25-16c²)/(4-4c²)
           =4+9/[4(1-c²)].

For N≥8, both π/N and 3π/N lie strictly between 0 and π/2. Their cosine squares are distinct, so the displayed products at c=cos(π/N) and c=cos(3π/N) differ. This contradicts the required constant product. QED.

## Boundary cases and precise consequence

At N=2 every c²=0, and at N=4 every c²=1/2. This reverse-pair obstruction therefore does not exclude those lengths. In the deliberately enlarged arbitrary-unitary-interstage model, the reciprocal pairing can in fact be implemented by exchanging the corresponding singular eigenspaces, giving a scalar-unitary two-stage product. That fact is not an efficient Fourier construction: the allowed arbitrary unitaries are not asserted to be cheap.

For dyadic N≥8, even granting arbitrary unitary factors cannot rescue a two-forward-stage product. A single K stage also has a nonconstant singular spectrum at these lengths. Thus a realization required to contain at least one forward K stage, with all remaining factors unitary, would need at least three K stages. This does not exclude a zero-K, all-unitary realization. This is a constant-stage exclusion, with no conclusion about whether three stages or a short growing word can work, and no asymptotic operation lower bound.

## Extension for abstract full cycles

The same gate excludes every N>=3 except N=4, without requiring N to be dyadic. For even N>=6, cos²(π/N) differs from cos²(3π/N); at N=6 the latter is zero, so the same argument works. For odd N>=3, the largest squared singular value has multiplicity two, while the smallest is unique (the eigenangle π gives c=-1). Hence λ_1=λ_2 and λ_N<λ_(N-1), so λ_1λ_N<λ_2λ_(N-1), again contradicting constant reverse-paired products. This extension concerns the abstract unit-modulus full-cycle family; it does not expand the uploaded affine implementation's dyadic coverage. N=1,2,4 are not excluded by this gate.

## Independent numerical corroboration

An auditor-written standard-library calculation evaluated the reverse-pair products for N=1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,16,32. The products were constant at N=1,2,4 and nonconstant at the other tested lengths, as the proof predicts. No supplied code was executed. Data are in green_audit/two_stage_gate_checks.json.
