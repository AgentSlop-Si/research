Checked 10 October 2026 UTC. Bounded comparison, not novelty clearance. No external outreach or publication.

## Established public ingredients

1. Terence Tao, *An uncertainty principle for cyclic groups of prime order*, Mathematical Research Letters 12 (2005), 121–127, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0308286 . The full seven-page paper was retrieved. Its opening inequality is the product-support uncertainty bound; Theorem 1.1 is the prime additive bound, and Lemma 1.3 gives Chebotarev full spark. Our general flat-unitary product proof and the sparse-numerator/low-rank application are elementary deductions using this established mechanism. The prime strengthening in our report is not claimed new, and is not transferred to arbitrary finite fields.

2. Andreas Frommer and Uwe Glässner, *Restarted GMRES for Shifted Linear Systems*, SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing 19 (1998), 15–26, https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/S1064827596304563 . The publisher abstract was retrieved. It identifies shared Krylov spaces for shifted matrices and reuses matrix-vector work. B. Jegerlehner, *Krylov space solvers for shifted linear systems*, https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/9612014 , supplies a primary author account of the same general direction. These establish that shared work among distinct shifts is old. Neither inspected abstract supplies the exact routed-packet Fourier synthesis sought here. We do not infer total sequential arithmetic or exact termination from their matrix-vector counts.

3. Harold V. Henderson and Shayle R. Searle, *On Deriving the Inverse of a Sum of Matrices*, SIAM Review 23 (1981), 53–60, https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/1023004 . The publisher page was retrieved. Inverse-update identities are established background; our correction identity is proved directly by multiplication and does not rely on a new Woodbury claim or an invertible endpoint assumption. Full historical priority of every inverse identity was not researched.

4. Joachim von zur Gathen, *Representations and Parallel Computations for Rational Functions*, SIAM Journal on Computing 15 (1986), 432–452, https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0215030 . The publisher abstract was retrieved. Rational representation conversion, partial fractions, interpolation, and Chinese remaindering are established algorithmic territory. Its parallel-time result is not a fully charged sequential sub-N-log-N Fourier result and is not used as such here.

5. Zeev Dvir and Allen Liu, *Fourier and Circulant Matrices are Not Rigid*, Theory of Computing 16(20) (2020), 1–48, https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/v016a020/ . Publisher page and article text were retrieved. Their result limits a broad rigidity-based route to arithmetic circuit lower bounds. It does not contradict the elementary small-sparsity/small-rank inequalities proved here. It cautions against treating a dense numerator or growing correction rank as an unrestricted work lower bound.

The routed compatibility condition, r² support count, noncancellation argument for |a|<1, and explicit commutator-budget specialization are proved in SEALED_RESEARCH.md. We found no exact matching routed statement in this bounded comparison; this does not establish publication novelty.
