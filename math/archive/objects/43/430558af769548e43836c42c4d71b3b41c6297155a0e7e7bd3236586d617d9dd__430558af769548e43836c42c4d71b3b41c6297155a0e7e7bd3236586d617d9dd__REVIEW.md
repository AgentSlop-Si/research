# Independent review: true bank-interface Weyl actions

Date: 2026-10-09 UTC. Verdict: the maximum-degree obstruction is valid and admits a one-sided strengthening. It does not establish a general FFT lower bound, exclude all paired-Green products, or provide a faster Fourier construction.

## 1. Exact hypotheses and conventions

Let N=mq, m>=2, q>=2. Work over a field E of characteristic not dividing N, containing a specified primitive N-th root omega. Put eta=omega^q and use unnormalized positive Fourier matrices F_n[k,j]=omega_n^(kj). Define S_N e_j=e_(j+1 mod N), D_N e_j=omega^j e_j. Then F_N S_N=D_N F_N and F_N D_N=S_N^(-1) F_N.

Partition the N coordinates into q consecutive blocks of size m. For a nonempty proper active set J, let B=diag(B_b), with B_b=F_m for b in J and B_b=I_m otherwise. Suppose P,Q are invertible N-by-N matrices and F_N=P B Q. Define the actual interface actions, using the same P and Q for both generators:

- T_S=Q S_N Q^(-1), A_S=P^(-1) D_N P;
- T_D=Q D_N Q^(-1), A_D=P^(-1) S_N^(-1) P.

They obey A_g B=B T_g for g=S,D. These are genuine N-dimensional actions in the bank's input/output bases. Sparsity of some other matrices, including a larger state-space representation, is not a hypothesis about these actions.

For an N-by-N matrix M, r(M) and c(M) denote its maximum number of nonzeros in any one row and in any one column. They are not average degrees and are not merely nnz(M)/N.

## 2. Algebraic support uncertainty, including finite fields

For nonzero x in E^m, write s=|supp x| and t=|supp F_m x|. Then st>=m.

Proof. Form the circulant C_x whose columns are cyclic shifts of x. Every column has exactly s nonzeros, and every row is nonzero because x is nonzero. Fourier diagonalization gives rank(C_x)=t (the Fourier eigenvalues are F_m x, possibly with a harmless reversed order depending on the circulant convention). Select t columns forming a column basis. Their supports must cover all m row positions: a row zero in all basis columns would be zero in every column, contrary to the preceding observation. The union of their supports has size at most ts, so m<=ts. Fourier diagonalization is valid because the field contains the roots and m is invertible. This proof uses no absolute values, positivity, or complex inner product.

The same assertion holds for row vectors multiplied by F_m, because F_m is symmetric, and for F_m^(-1), since that inverse is a nonzero scalar times a reversal times F_m. A scalar normalization of F_m would not affect this support argument.

Strictness matters: at m=4, x=(1,0,1,0) has Fourier support two, so equality s=t=2 is possible. The test suite verifies this exactly over F_5.

## 3. Mixed blocks and the stronger obstruction

Write A_bc B_c=B_b T_bc.

If b is active and c inactive, then A_bc=F_m T_bc. Each column x of T_bc and its image F_m x have supports bounded by c(T) and c(A). Consequently A_bc=T_bc=0 if c(A)c(T)<m.

If b is inactive and c active, then A_bc F_m=T_bc. Each row x of A_bc and its transform x F_m have supports bounded by r(A) and r(T). Consequently A_bc=T_bc=0 if r(A)r(T)<m.

### Theorem

No factorization with the preceding proper mixed bank can satisfy either of the following sets of bounds:

1. r(A_g)r(T_g)<m for each g=S,D; or
2. c(A_g)c(T_g)<m for each g=S,D.

In particular, maximum row degree at most d on all four actions with d^2<m is impossible, as is the corresponding maximum column-degree condition. Requiring both row and column bounds is sufficient but unnecessary for this nonexistence conclusion.

Proof of (1). The inactive-row/active-column blocks of both T generators vanish. Thus the active coordinate subspace V_J is invariant under both T_S and T_D. Conjugating by Q^(-1) produces a nonzero proper common invariant subspace of S_N,D_N. This is impossible: D_N has N distinct eigenvalues, so its invariant subspaces are spans of subsets of the coordinate eigenvectors (polynomial spectral projections prove this directly over E). Invariance under the cyclic shift forces a nonempty subset to contain every coordinate. The active subspace has dimension m|J| strictly between 0 and N, giving the contradiction. For (2), use the inactive coordinate subspace instead. QED.

If both row and column products are below m, both directions of mixed blocks vanish. One-sided bounds do NOT justify that stronger block-diagonal conclusion: the exact shear controls in the verifier have m=8, d=2, satisfy A B=B T, and retain a nonzero mixed block in the uncontrolled direction. Those controls are single intertwiners, not an irreducible Weyl pair, so they do not contradict the theorem.

The bounds must hold for both generators in a consistent orientation. A row bound for one generator and a column bound for the other can preserve different subspaces and is not covered by this proof. Arbitrary separately chosen A_g,T_g satisfying the intertwining equations do not suffice; their shared P,Q conjugacies, or an independently certified irreducible Weyl-pair property, are essential.

## 4. Essential O(N)-total-sparsity countercontrol

Use stride coordinates n=b+qt, with 0<=b<q and 0<=t<m, and let Q map ordinary inputs to coordinates (b,t). Let Delta[b,s]=omega^(bs), L=F_q tensor I_m, and C=B_(J complement). Then the standard exact factorization is

F_N=L Delta B_all Q=(L Delta C) B_J Q.

Thus take P=L Delta C. The action T_S=Q S_N Q^(-1) advances b, with a carry S_m at the wrap from b=q-1 to b=0. It has one monomial m-by-m block per block column. Also T_D is block diagonal with block omega^b D_m.

Choose J to be a nonempty proper interval on the cyclic bank labels. Exactly two shift edges change active/inactive status. Conjugation A_S=B_J T_S B_J^(-1) leaves every same-status edge monomial (at a fully active carry edge, F_m S_m F_m^(-1)=D_m). Its two mixed-status edges are dense Fourier or inverse-Fourier blocks, up to monomial factors. Hence

nnz(A_S)=N+2m(m-1), and r(A_S)=c(A_S)=m.

The other three actions T_S,T_D,A_D are monomial, each with N nonzeros. In particular q=m gives nnz(A_S)=3N-2m=O(N), even though the maximum degree is m. This is an exact counterexample to replacing maximum degree by O(N) total nonzeros or bounded average degree.

The checker independently reconstructs P,Q,B and verifies F_N=P B Q, both conjugated actions and both interface equations at N=4,16,64. It finds A_S nonzero counts 8,40,176, exactly as predicted.

There is no saved recursive mass here. Applying P still invokes all (q-|J|) complement-bank transforms, while B_J invokes |J|; L invokes m transforms of length q. Total recursive work is q T(m)+m T(q)+O(N). The stride permutation Q does have a trivial paired-Green realization in the characteristic-zero target model: split each monomial layer into two identical half-scaled zero-mask Green plans. No paired-Green realization of P is provided by this example; a theorem that P cannot belong to that class has not been proved here.

## 5. Local Weyl transports and hidden transform cost

For the local convention W(a,b)=D_m^a S_m^b,

F_m W(a,b) F_m^(-1)=eta^(ab) D_m^b S_m^(-a).

The phase is positive. The checker verifies all (a,b) at m=3,4,8, a total of 89 exact identities. The transformed map is monomial, and a bounded number of block-permuted local-Weyl routes can be applied in O(N) arithmetic and data movement, with coefficient/root generation also charged.

This applies to active-to-active and inactive-to-inactive routes of that stated local-Weyl form. At a mixed route, the transported operator is F_m W or W F_m^(-1), up to the orientation convention, and is dense. Naming it a wire transport does not make its action free. A valid complexity proof must exhibit and charge an implementation, including any shared computation. Matrix density alone is not a universal lower bound on application complexity, so this report does not assert that each mixed edge independently costs an unavoidable m log m.

Nor does an arbitrary within-block permutation automatically have the local-Weyl conjugation formula.

## 6. Enlarged states are not the true interface action

A lift is an encoding E:E^N -> E^h, often h>N, with a lifted action L satisfying L E=E M. Sparse L does not imply sparse M in the original coordinates. E need not be square or invertible, and decoding or constructing E can contain the expensive operation.

An explicit control makes this distinction sharp. Let E=[I; F_N] and, writing R e_j=e_(-j), set

L = [0, I; N R, 0].

Then L E=E F_N because F_N^2=N R. The lifted L has exactly one nonzero per row and column, while the true F_N has N nonzeros per row and column. The expensive Fourier result is already present in the encoding. At N=8 over F_17 the checker verifies this rectangular intertwining identity exactly.

This is not an objection to all useful state lifting. It shows why lift sparsity cannot be inserted into the theorem as if it bounded the true N-dimensional bank-interface matrices. An admissible state-based algorithm must specify initial state construction, storage, transitions, readout, and all their costs, and must separately prove the needed phase and seed identities.

## 7. Certification versus construction

One Fourier covariance plus the seed is complete: M S_N=D_N M and M e_0=ones imply M=F_N. Without the seed, the covariance permits M=diag(t)F_N. The two-generator obstruction above can be used as a necessary rejection gate after an exact product is proposed, but it supplies no admissible P,Q parameter generator and no implementation bound for them.

The result is therefore a structural obstruction to uniformly low maximum-degree true interface actions across a proper mixed Fourier/identity bank. No claim is made that every fast circuit, arbitrary state lift, or arbitrary paired-Green architecture must satisfy that sparsity hypothesis.

## 9. Independent check of the constructor's 14-wire extension

The constructor draft additionally realizes four paired-Green layers as 14 N-dimensional state wires, including the input x, eight Green internal states, four layer outputs, and the bank output w. The bank input is z. Its system is K=K_s-E_w B E_z^T, where K_s has maximum row and column degree three; its w block row contains only I in column w and its z block column contains only I in row z. Direct inspection of each state equation verifies these statements.

For state witnesses S,R with K S=R K, put C=K_s S-R K_s. Expanding, without dropping any off-wire term, gives

C_ab=1_(a=w) B S_zb-1_(b=z) R_aw B.

At the central block, the special K_s row and column give C_wz=S_wz-R_wz, hence

R_ww B-B S_zz=R_wz-S_wz.

The sparse forcing on the right cannot generally be erased. Nor may S_zz and R_ww simply be declared to be the true T and A.

If S,R have maximum row and column degree d, each incident off-wire block C_wb or C_az has both degrees at most 4d: one summand has degree d, the other is a product with the degree-three K_s. For b!=z, C_wb=B S_zb; applying column-support uncertainty on each active row block forces Pi_J S_zb=0 when m>4d^2. For a!=w, C_az=-R_aw B; applying row-support uncertainty on each active column block forces R_aw Pi_J=0 at the same threshold. This pruning is valid over the stated splitting fields.

To identify precisely what it implies for the actual actions, let V=K^(-1)B_0 be the state solution map and U=C_out K^(-1) be the dual output map. The input/output witness conditions imply S V=V X and U R=Y U. Also V_z=Q and U_w=P. Taking the relevant rows/columns after pruning gives

Pi_J S_zz=Pi_J Q X Q^(-1)=Pi_J T,
R_ww Pi_J=P^(-1)Y P Pi_J=A Pi_J.

Only active rows of T and active columns of A inherit the degree bound. These are different orientations and are not either uniform row-product or column-product hypothesis in Section 3. In the all-active case the full actions are identified; this does not contradict the theorem because its nonempty inactive-space hypothesis fails. Thus the constructor has not improperly promoted this partial pruning to a proper-bank impossibility claim.

A newly authored N=4, 14-wire exact control verifies K_s degree three, the complete residual expansion for arbitrary nonzero state witnesses, the central forcing identity, and the incident 4d support bound. This is an algebraic sign/index control; it does not claim to find a Fourier-compatible sparse state witness.

The proposed O(1) dense mixed-boundary-block certificate target also remains logically open. When m^2=O(N), such exceptional blocks can be generated and applied explicitly in O(N) total work, while O(q) bounded local-Weyl blocks handle the remaining routes. They evade the maximum-degree theorem exactly as the Cooley-Tukey control does. The missing condition remains simultaneous compatibility with the same admissible paired-Green exteriors, plus calibration and fully charged parameter generation. Blockwise interface validity alone is insufficient.
