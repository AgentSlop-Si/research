# Exact 16 point Fourier construction

An exact 16-point Fourier realization exists in the project’s fixed three-layer Green model. A contraction certificate, independently recomputed with integer and rational arithmetic, proves all 256 complex matrix equations simultaneously and keeps all 384 endpoint coefficients nonzero. This is a finite construction; extending it to growing sizes and establishing historical novelty remain open.

## The certified construction

Use the positive-sign, unnormalized Fourier matrix F16, with primitive root z = exp(2πi/16). The central bank is B = diag(F4, F4, I4, I4). The certified identity is

F16 = H2 H1 B H0.

Each layer is Hℓ = Σj diag(aℓ,j) Gj, with eight terms. Here Gj = (I − Pj/(j+2))⁻¹ for j = 0,…,7. The Pj are fixed Hamiltonian cycles built from the eight horizontal and vertical shear permutations on the 4-by-4 grid, joined by the specified least-representative cycle-splicing rule. The construction uses 24 Green terms and 384 complex left-endpoint coefficients; right endpoints are identities.

Both the routes and poles are fixed. Every Green denominator (j+2)¹⁶ − 1 is nonzero. The two active F4 blocks remain part of the computation and must be counted.

## Why the identity is exact

The coefficients are represented as a = a0 + Vy. The center a0 and the 384-by-256 matrix V are exact dyadic data. All 256 Fourier equations define a cubic polynomial map f(y). The certificate isolates its solution in the closed complex sup-norm ball of radius 10⁻⁹.

The map T(y) = y − f(y) satisfies exact rational bounds summarized below:

- Contraction factor below 0.001660

- Image radius below 3.514 × 10⁻¹², strictly inside the 10⁻⁹ ball

- Every physical endpoint magnitude above 0.3145476 throughout the ball

The contraction theorem therefore gives a unique solution within this affine slice and ball. The derivative is nonsingular, so the solution coordinates are algebraic. Uniqueness is not claimed in the unrestricted 384-parameter space.

The independent verifier regenerates all eight cycles, all Green entries, every target entry, all 256 derivative columns, and all endpoint guards. It uses arbitrary-precision integers and rational fractions for acceptance. A rational enclosure of the chosen complex Fourier root controls every target power; floating-point residuals do not decide acceptance.

## What this adds

The earlier all-ones inner-prefix obstruction ruled out a narrower coefficient choice. Allowing the full endpoint family yields a certified exact solution at 16 points. Thus the restricted failure does not exclude the full family at this size.

## What is still needed

1. A compatible construction from this actual seed to larger sizes. Independently spliced small cycles and the separate nested expanding hierarchy cannot be identified without proof.

2. A uniformly specified coefficient family with its preparation and precision costs counted.

3. An end-to-end cost bound including the central Fourier bank, every Green scan, routing, and explicit output.

No sub-N-log-N algorithm, practical speedup, or publication-priority claim follows from this finite certificate alone.
