Diagonally gauged two-Green-sum FFT
This is the simplest unresolved constant-term candidate from our theoretical audit:
\[
F_N=L(A^{-1}+B^{-1})R.
\]

With \(A\) and \(B\) sparse cycle denominators, both inverse applications take linear work.
The exact feasibility equation is
\[
\boxed{A L^{-1}F_NR^{-1}B=A+B.}
\]

Novel attack: solve the resulting sparse-sandwich polynomial system over exact cyclotomic fields, including non-affine permutations. Derive either a constructive small-size identity or an impossibility certificate.
Obstacle: the system is heavily overdetermined; no construction has been found.

Universal multi-shear orbit fusion
Hero #17 has developed exact \(O_g(N)\) executors for a fixed number \(g\) of unknown shear orbits:
\[
y_t[u]=\sum_{j=1}^{g}\mu_j^t b_j[u-a_jt].
\]

The newer checkpoint also constructs a universal orbit representation when \(g=M\approx\sqrt N\), but its current execution costs \(O(N\sqrt N)\), before some recovery costs.
Novel attack: batch all orbit contributions through a shared rational/Cauchy kernel evaluator without separately processing every orbit. The recovery of the unknown representation must be included.
Obstacle: universality currently appears only when the orbit count grows enough to destroy the linear-work advantage.
