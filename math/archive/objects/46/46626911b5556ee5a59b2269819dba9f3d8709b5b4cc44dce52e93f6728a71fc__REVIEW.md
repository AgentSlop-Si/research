## Useful results

1. The all-size induced-condition arguments are sound within their stated
   factor families. Bare two-Green and expander-Green stages cannot achieve
   Fourier with sublogarithmically many bounded-condition passes. Haar times
   Green is a real O(N)-work, bounded-spectral-condition control escaping that
   particular one-condition barrier; it supplies no Fourier factorization.
2. The low-rank-wrapper and two-forward-K spectral obstructions have valid
   independently rederived algebra. Scope must retain the prohibition on free
   arbitrary unitaries in the zero-K branch and distinguish explicit passes
   from fused arithmetic circuits.
3. The guarded-lane appendix supplies a genuine exact packing proof. Together
   with exact-mass sampling it supports the coarse-additive approximate branch,
   subject to the companion descriptor/quantization/table proof. It does not
   establish exact Fourier, ordinary relative accuracy, practicality or novelty.
4. Polynomial single-edit, cyclic-coset and periodic-residual identities are
   valid constructive restricted-workload tools. Their API states, immutability,
   history/cache handling and all-output accounting still need the actual code.
5. The h=24 retuning arithmetic matches, including the approximately
   3.957610023859e-10 raw logarithmic exponent saving. Full general-h proof,
   downstream reductions and formalization remain independent dependencies.
6. The thin-matrix sparse-expansion reduction is algebraically correct. Its
   total-O(n²), uniform thin-multiplication construction, including additions,
   remains unresolved in the reviewed source. Multiplication rank is insufficient.
7. The joint Galois measurement-only Lipschitz bound follows by packing and
   Parseval. It constrains that interface, not a method retaining original input.
8. The Phase-14 start-here phrase 'iff N<=B²' must retain the detailed theorem's
   dyadic/conductor assumptions. This README alone does not justify arbitrary B.
