## Exact results derived and independently checked in this continuation

1. Four-corner quasiseparable matrix-product rank bound holds for **all three** input/middle/output permutations. See `RESEARCH.md` §2.
2. **Prime `p` DFT theorem:** even allowing all permutations, a two-stage factorization with ranks `r1,r2` needs `r1+r2 >= ceil((p-1)/4)`, by Chebotarëv full Fourier minors. Does not automatically apply to `2^m`.
3. **Both-endpoints-permuted Cooley–Tukey control:** for `N=a*b`, 2 factors exist with ranks `(b//2,a//2)`, exactly checked for `N=4,16,32,64,256`. This invalidates naïve extensions of the old natural-endpoint `Omega(N)` rank obstruction, but costs `Theta(N sqrt N)` at balanced splitting—**not** an FFT speedup.
4. **Multi-interval bound:** for arbitrary stage-position sets `R,C`, `rank(F[R,C]) <= |P(R)∩C|+b(R)r2+b(C)r1`; `b` counts interval-boundary switches. Complete proof and exact random-stage stress tests included.

Exact 21×21 minor over **two** primes and roots, with determinants `649213010 mod 998244353` and `21920338 mod 2013265921`. These nonzero minors also certify complex nonzero via cyclotomic specialization. The bound applies ONLY to that permutation.
6. Field operation audit: reference two-factor executor becomes increasingly more costly than radix-2: 2.83× at N16 to 8.60× at N1024; both exact, no caching.
