# Prior art and originality limits

- Projective four-entry matrix cross-ratios and invariant Haagerup sets are established in complex Hadamard matrix theory: https://chaos.if.uj.edu.pl/~karol/hadamard/d/haagerup-set.html
- Diagonal equivalence and its characterization through multiplicative flows on bipartite patterns is classical: Saunders and Schneider, *Flows on graphs applied to diagonal similarity and diagonal equivalence for matrices* (1978), https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0012365X78902005
- Matrix-scaling and related positive diagonal-equivalence problems are longstanding: Hershkowitz, Rothblum, Schneider (1988), https://doi.org/10.1137/0609038
- Bounds for Fourier computation depend on the circuit model; Ailon's gate/matrix-entropy restrictions do not cover arbitrary nonunitary Green circuits: https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.4745 and https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.1307

The Phase 38 **project-specific** contribution is the use of **two quadratic, mask-independent resultant equations** to disqualify a precisely specified six-Green noncommuting network even with two arbitrary internal complex masks and all intermediate coordinate permutations at the audited finite sizes. This is not externally peer reviewed and is not a universal FFT lower bound.
