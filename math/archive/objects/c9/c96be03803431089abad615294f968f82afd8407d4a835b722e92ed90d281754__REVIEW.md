## Singular boundaries and exact complex lift

The initial inverse identity requires the individual branches to be invertible. It cannot simply be asserted when endpoint masks are singular. The constructor's extension correctly uses formal perturbation: replace zero endpoint scales by epsilon, retaining each fixed legal Green denominator. Branches become invertible over the Laurent/rational function field and their sum specializes to the alleged target. Since each target homography determinant is nonzero, its perturbed value has nonzero constant term, so the required inverses and the rational commutator determinant remain regular at epsilon=0. The common-eigenline identity forces that determinant to vanish before specialization, contradicting the target's nonzero value afterwards. Hence singular endpoint masks are retained in the exclusion. Zero edge weights were never divided by and remain allowed; singular Green denominators remain outside the legal class.

The target entries lie in Q(zeta5). The substitution zeta5->91 modulo241 is valid, since 91 has exact order five. The explicit Gauss scalar is103, c=33 and 1+c=34; the feedback denominator and all seven homography determinants are nonzero. Equivalently one may use the target formula from the inverse of A+F^(-1), with the additional denominator5 also nonzero. Consequently the nonzero reduced rational determinant proves the characteristic-zero determinant is nonzero. This does not rely on reducing unknown representation parameters: it is an obstruction expressed only in target entries. Galois conjugation covers every primitive complex fifth root.

The result therefore excludes all complex diagonal endpoint masks and edge weights at these fixed orders. It says nothing analogous about unmatched endpoint permutations or other order pairs. Conversely, a vanishing commutator would only remove this necessary obstruction and would not construct a representation.
