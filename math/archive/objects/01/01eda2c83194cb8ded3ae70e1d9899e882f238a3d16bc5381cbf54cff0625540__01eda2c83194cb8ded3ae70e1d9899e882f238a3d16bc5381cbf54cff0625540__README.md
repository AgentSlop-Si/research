## What the chirp investigation establishes

`fft_linear_decoder_barrier` proves an all-size constraint. For a seed chosen
independently of the input, suppose its entire approximation is a linear map
of rank at most K and every fixed input has success probability at least
1-delta for error epsilon*||x||_1/sqrt(N). Then

    K >= N (1-delta) (1-epsilon^2).

This needs neither unbiasedness nor one seed succeeding on all inputs. The
proof also applies when the input promise is merely that every coordinate is
nonzero. It does not cover input-adaptive sampling, nonlinear decoding, or
additional information outside the sketch, and it is not a runtime lower bound.
For epsilon=1/4 and delta=1/10, the required rank is at least 0.84375 N.

`fft_structured_phase_research` gives exact all-size failures of the proposed
polynomial block slopes over power-of-two moduli: a zero-sketch event for the
ordinary chirp, a fully dense collision pair even for a general sketch-only
decoder, and a separate fully dense failure for the natural CAZAC decoder.
An odd-prime modulus with random affine slopes repairs pairwise error
correlations: the estimator is unbiased with per-bin variance
(M-1)*||x||_2^2/N. That is useful algebraic progress, but does not supply a
simultaneous tail guarantee, a power-of-two-length replacement, or the required
fast joint decoder. The rank constraint still governs the stronger universal
error contract for any input-independent linear version.

## Research direction that remains justified

For the user's A-k idea, the working primitive is now a verified correction
edge whose complete cost can be compared against a fresh product. The next
constructive target is richer cheap corrections or shared multi-parent
combinations with sparse discovery and a charged unbounded-size backend.

For the exact q-kernel, low-rank tails and sparse coordinate grading are usable
components. The remaining requirement is a proved way to share work between
the three recursive polynomial branches or across tail scales. Independent
materialization of many low-rank corrections cannot silently be called free;
the separate rank-budget note states the precise restricted obstruction.

For approximate recovery, retain the corrected input-adaptive surrogate
planner. Any replacement based on small fixed linear sketches needs a changed
input promise, a weaker error target, nonlinear decoding, or extra input-
dependent information whose cost is included.

The external Swapnil repository and OpenAI #130 remain separate comparison
routes. They are not incorporated as an independent result here. Primary
literature links and model qualifications are in the component reports,
especially `fft_route_barriers_research/LITERATURE_BRIEF.md`.
