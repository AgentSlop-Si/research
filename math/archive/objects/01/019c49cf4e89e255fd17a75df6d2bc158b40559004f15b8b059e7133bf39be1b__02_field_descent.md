Coefficient cancellation modulo P supports those examples, but a single-prime computation is not by itself a characteristic-zero proof of all zero identities. ## Cost accounting

A is stored densely in O(N^2) elements. Coefficient construction costs up to O(N^2 L); reconstruction scans N labels per N^2 entry and linearly searches orbit membership, a safe upper bound O(N^3 L). This is a tiny bounded control, not an executable fast Fourier algorithm.
