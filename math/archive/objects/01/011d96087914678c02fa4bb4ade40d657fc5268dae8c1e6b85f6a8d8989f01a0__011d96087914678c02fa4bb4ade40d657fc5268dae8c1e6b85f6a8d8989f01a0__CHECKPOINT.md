## Proven work to reuse

1. **Arbitrary affine-run geometric input:** `x[L[t]]=z^t`, where `L` is the concatenation of `r` affine arithmetic runs, has an exact `O(rN+r log N)`-field-work FFT via geometric sums and batch denominator inversion. No expensive node extraction. `phase25_affine.py`.
2. **All-stride obstruction is NOT enough:** for `N=2^k>=8` and every odd `u`, `W_(2u)` is the two-run zigzag successor and `min_b rank(W_(2u)-Z^b)=N/2-1`; nevertheless its geometric-input FFT is O(N). A rank barrier without an account of kernel structure is misleading.
3. **Nearly maximal noncommutation at linear application cost:** `rank([W_(2u),Z])=rank([G_(2u),G_Z])=N-2`. Same for all odd `u` and admissible nonzero resolvent parameters.
4. **Exact Green sum and inverse:** `S=(I-alpha W)^-1+(I-beta Z)^-1`, `S^-1=B(A+B)^-1 A`. For `a=2`, `A+B` is pentadiagonal plus one wrap entry. For the twisted `a=N/2+2`, reorder into four tracks: width 6 plus exactly four corner edges; use band LU + fixed 4x4 Woodbury. Thus both directions have O(N) field-work apply; tested through N=16384.
5. **Natural cut misleading:** twisted sum attains full natural half-block rank at checked N=16,32,64,128, with bounded spectral conditioning for small complex `alpha,beta` (e.g., kappa<=31/17 for alpha=1/4,beta=1/5).
6. **Decisive obstruction:** alternating-quarter balanced cut has just 3 W edges and 2 Z edges, so rank(S[I,J])<=5 for all N. Arbitrary external diagonal scalings and row/column permutations cannot remove existence of a balanced rank-five block. For N>20, any such scaled/permuted single stage satisfies `||M-Fhat||_2 >= sqrt((N/4-5)/(N/2-5))`, tending to 1/sqrt(2). So single-stage rank/mixing/condition arguments are exhausted.

## Do not repeat

- The 4-track low-bandwidth reordering *is* a hidden low-cut rank certificate; do not describe natural-cut full rank as robust all-order mixing.
- A fast executor for a geometric input (even with high ordering defect rank) is not a worst-case FFT for arbitrary vectors.
- High commutator rank or exact inverse cost alone does not imply Weyl/Fourier phase coherence.
- Small-coupling condition bounds do not establish stability for arbitrary near-resonant parameters.
- Older lower bounds in restricted Green or butterfly circuit models do not prove universal FFT lower bounds.
