## Strongest claims and provenance

**Previously established within the project (NOT new this round):**

- Weyl Fourier covariance and Fourier first-column seed.
- Diagonal similarity/cocycle gate from Phase 18/19.
- Two-column phase pencil `K=C1-Delta C0` and determinant impossibility from the immediately prior checkpoint.
- Exact GF(65537) four-Green witness with determinant 55687 and every `N=8` square minor nonsingular; its bytes are copied as `REFERENCE_CERTIFICATE.json` solely as a positive re-audit target.
- A Green resolvent can be applied through an O(N) cyclic recurrence when its weights/order are provided.

**Added in this round (derived with proofs and supporting tests):**

1. Complete inverse-free rank-augmentation criterion for *all* Fourier columns, including existence of a middle vector with all nonzero entries and nonvanishing first column, avoiding a silent singular output normalizer.
2. The same necessary-and-sufficient nonzero-aware test for the first two Fourier columns. Exact GF(17) corner cases demonstrate the two failure modes, and that passing two columns does not establish full Fourier equality.
3. Quantitative **projective spectral gap** `||LADB-F_N||₂>=gamma/sqrt(2)` for every diagonal L,D of fixed complex A,B when K is invertible, with gamma defined as a generalized singular value.
4. Explicit closed-form elimination of output gauges from a two-column least-squares objective and a correct O(N)-work analytic Wirtinger gradient when fixed A is a constant sum of O(N)-apply Green operators.
5. An exactly rational genuine four-Green witness at N=2 with gamma=1 and spectral-error certificate `>=1/sqrt(2)`, requiring no numerical singular-value estimation.

## External prior-art boundaries

- Basic orthogonal projection in C², least squares, generalized singular values, rank-nullity, union of proper subspaces, and elimination using adjugates are classical. The novelty claim is **only a potentially new application/combination within this FFT project**, not a new invention of these components.
- The diagonal-similarity/cocycle equivalence existed in this project prior to this round and in classical matrix theory; earlier project notes correctly credit it.
- Ailon's unitary / well-conditioned FFT lower bounds and Morgenstern's bounded-coefficient lower bounds concern other models: https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.4745 and https://doi.org/10.1145/321752.321761.
- Public OpenAI Math 130: https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/130.md reports uniform exact `O(N(log N)^(1-10^-13))` computation in a permissive complex arithmetic model, not linear time or practical bit complexity. This round claims no improvement over it.

## Why this is NOT an O(N) exact FFT

- The theorem covers the **feasibility/approximation of a chosen two-stage candidate**, not construction of correct Green stages for all N.
- Forming the stacked K matrices or certifying smallest singular values may cost polynomially more than linear time. The `O(N)` claim refers **only** to loss/gradient evaluation after fixed stages and two B columns are supplied/prepared.
- Numerical gradient fitting cannot prove any global polynomial system has an exact solution. Random finite-field rejections with one wiring do not exclude every wiring.
- For the *normalized* Fourier matrix the spectral floor scales by `1/sqrt(N)`; it is not a uniform lower bound independent of N.
- The decimal eigenvalues in RESULTS.json were computed in floating point, not certified intervals. The N=2 example and modular determinant are independently exact.
