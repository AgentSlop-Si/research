## Main results
1. Known Harper H=Z+Z^-1+D+D^-1 is O(N)-sparse, F commutes with H, and for N divisible by 4 has two zero modes of opposite reflection parity. F acts on them by λ_+∈{±1}, λ_-∈{±i}, so **NO** scalar rational function in one H can produce F (regardless of degree).
2. Add reflection J to separate the zero modes: **EXACT** `F=p_+(H)(I+J)/2+p_-(H)(I-J)/2`, with **sharp** degrees deg p_+=N/2, deg p_-=N/2-2. Leading coeff of p_+=1/(2 sqrt N). Proof: parity Jacobi simple spectra and shortest-path support. Full exact modular matrix audits through N=128 passed.
3. `F=O diag(±1,±i) O^T`, O real orthogonal. A single quaternary phase mask is algebraically enough if O allowed arbitrary coefficients; computing O efficiently remains hard. Entropy lower bound implies ≥(N/4) log2 N 2-coordinate orthogonal gates for such O.
4. Dual intertwiner: for T=Z+Z^-1,V=D+D^-1, joint commutant is span(I,J). Any M satisfying M T=V M AND M V=T M is F(aI+bJ); one probe M e1=F e1 forces M=F. This is a symbolic Green-operator proof gate, not an unconditional speedup or black-box sublinear verification.
5. If a fixed product of two invertible Green-sum stages is M=A Q P B with any middle diagonal Q and permutation P, set U=A^-1 D A and V=B Z B^-1. Necessary diagonal and unordered 2-cycle product multiset matches. For deterministic cyclotomic-phase-gauged stage examples at n8,16,32,64 both invariants mismatch modulo a suitable prime, certifying no such middle monomial over characteristic zero. Arbitrary centered Q without P can be solved exactly by graph cocycle BFS. Positive controls pass. NO global impossibility for arbitrary parameters.
