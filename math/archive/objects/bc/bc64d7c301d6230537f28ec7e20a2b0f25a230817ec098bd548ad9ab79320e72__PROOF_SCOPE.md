where K is1 when i+j>=5, −1 when i+j=4, and0 otherwise.

## 5. Recurrence fusion

Take Q(0)=1 and arbitrary v of lengthL. Write Qv=n+X^L t, where deg(n)<L. Then n/Q=v−X^L(t/Q), as a formal power series. Therefore the firstHL coefficients consist of v followed by the negative of the first(H−1)L coefficients of t/Q. ## 6. Guarded word arithmetic and approximation boundary

Bias each signed lane from[−U,U] to[0,2U], and take h=ceil(log2(2UG+1)). Adding at mostG packed words cannot carry out of a lane. Packing floor(w/h) lanes ensures the entire unsigned word remains below2^w. Extracting each lane and subtractingGU recovers the exact signed sum. Seventy-two newly authored64-bit boundary/random cases pass; the helper package has independent16-bit controls.

The reviewed fixed-epsilon theorem and growing-block shrinking-error theorem include these charges only under a common bounded-scale word model.
