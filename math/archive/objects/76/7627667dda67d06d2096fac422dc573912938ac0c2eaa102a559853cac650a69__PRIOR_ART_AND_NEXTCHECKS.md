## 1. Delvaux–Van Barel: the comparison that remains open

Primary preprint: [TW470, Rank-deficient submatrices of Fourier matrices](https://www.cs.kuleuven.be/publicaties/rapporten/tw/TW470.pdf), September 2006. This pass read Lemma 8, Theorem 10 and its proof, the surrounding digit-reversal development, and the start of §4. Lemma 8 supplies rank-one grids for double digit reversal. Theorem 10 concerns extremal support sizes/Hamming numbers. C02 instead controls a kernel **dimension** at a critical odd cut and optimizes maximum quasiseparable rank over two independent orders. That quantifier distinction identifies the proposed increment; it does not prove the increment absent from the final paper.

Final record: [publisher article and DOI 10.1016/j.laa.2008.04.043](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0024379508002164); [author-institution record](https://lirias.kuleuven.be/1537109). Both identify the final 2008 article, volume 429, pages 1587–1605. The institutional page offers only an external DOI download; the publisher full-text request returned 403. Focused author/institution and exact-title PDF searches returned TW470 or bibliographic records, not a verified final PDF. No document identity between TW470 and the final article was established.

**Required unresolved check:** obtain an authorized final published PDF or author-accepted final text, compare every final rank/nullity theorem and corollary with C02's all-order N/4+1 statement and its critical-minor bound, and map numbering/revisions against TW470. Until then the proper label is “supported theorem, priority unresolved,” not “new theorem proved absent from Delvaux–Van Barel.”

## 2. Separator and tree precedents

[Vandebril–Van Barel, TW397](https://www.cs.kuleuven.be/publicaties/rapporten/tw/TW397.pdf), §3, Corollary 1, contains the complementary inverse-submatrix rank identity. It is a classical ingredient; our kernel proof is exposition. [Graph-Induced Rank Structures](https://arxiv.org/html/1911.05858), §2.1, Propositions 2.1–2.3, explicitly gives inverse rank preservation and algebraic closure properties. The separator proof uses vertex separators rather than silently substituting their sizes for a boundary-edge count.

[Dvořák–Norin, Treewidth of graphs with balanced separations](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.3869), introduction, records weighted-separator implications of treewidth. Balance conventions differ, so the final theorem supplies its precise half-component centroid proof directly. The q=2 pseudoforest proof is also supplied rather than attributed to an unchecked theorem.

[Govindarajan–Chandrasekaran–Dewilde, final TQS article, 2025](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_6b7549c8-222e-4316-8ac4-a113d7d4da1e), §3.5, printed pp.1571–1572, already discusses a regular binary-tree family, fixed-order SSS/HSS storage comparisons, and the combinatorial search needed for a good ordering. The inspected introduction and §5 descriptions also concern recovery of minimal representations for a specified tree. Therefore the project must not claim to solve a published open question about discovering tree structures or ordering recovery. The conditioned-tree witness is a narrower all-independent-order exact-width comparison.

**Remaining priority check:** search specialist literature on sums of independently routed low-treewidth inverses and compare the exact target-flatness and separator quantifiers. No reviewed primary source here asserted the full branch-count theorem, but this is not an exhaustive priority result.

## 3. C01 and Green terminology

[Huhtanen–Perämäki, Factoring matrices into the product of circulant and diagonal matrices](https://math.aalto.fi/~mhuhtane/manu.pdf), abstract and introduction, concerns generic multiplicative factorization. It does not by itself imply a minimum additive number of diagonal-ended weighted cyclic resolvents.

[Boito–Eidelman, Computation of quasiseparable representations of Green matrices](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.02701), §2/Theorem 2.1 and the statements and construction structure of §4–§5, concerns inverse-band rank structures and generator computation. “Green matrix” is not automatically the same summand grammar as C01. This pass improved the earlier abstract-only comparison to direct inspection of the definition and relevant theorem statements, but did not exhaust all inverse-band literature.

**Required next checks:** package the exact 576-pair/624-branch closed-projective certificate and uniform upper formulas as a stand-alone inert data appendix with a small independently written verifier; compare additive secant/length literature for this precise cyclic-resolvent variety; retain the quadratic extension and all zero-mask boundaries. Base-field-only length and growing-dimensional upper families remain open.

## 4. Discrepancy

[Bilyk–Lacey–Parissis–Vagharshakyan](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.5544), printed pp.1–2, equation (1.2), states the classical unnormalized planar logarithmic discrepancy lower bound, including its universal point-set quantifier. The original [Schmidt publisher record](https://doi.org/10.4064/aa-21-1-45-50) identifies the 1972 source; this pass did not read its original full PDF. The cyclic-window rank proof is given in the final draft, so this missing original PDF does not leave the classical theorem's formulation unsupported.

**Required next checks:** a two-arbitrary-endpoint composite-length extension needs new minor-rank information; an approximate C04 needs quantitative singular values for every target block used, not exact nonzero determinants. Do not transplant C02's single critical-rectangle gap to all cyclic windows.

## 5. Current intake: quadratic lifts and Phase40

[Dastidar–Weicht–Wein, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.17947), §3.1, Lemma 3.1 and its matrix construction, explicitly uses full-column-rank symmetric-square/minor coefficient matrices for rank-one subspace analysis. Their nonplanted case is a direct general-method antecedent to the current three-column rejection certificate. [Johnston–Lovitz–Vijayaraghavan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.03851) supplies the broader variety/subspace algorithmic context; abstract and problem formulation were checked, not its entire proof. These comparisons support “known-method specialization,” not originality of the lifting primitive. The new β_N norm bound is an elementary refinement within the specified Fourier–Green parameter map.

[Cavaleri–Donno, On cospectrality of gain graphs](https://d-nb.info/1262808545/34), Corollary 3.5, identifies switching classes through E−N+1 cycle invariants. For the commutative multiplicative group, this gives exactly Phase40's N+1 count for two labeled Hamiltonian cycles. Zero edge gains are outside that torus classification. The generic local four-ratio dominance claim permits arbitrary nonzero complex gains; it says nothing automatic about unit-modulus control or the particular Fourier ratio fiber.

The Phase40 theorem text was read in full. Its N=8 certificate claims 376,320 local mappings in each of two finite fields for one fixed eight-resolvent architecture. N≥16 cases are samples. No supplied programs were run. A brief inert textual inspection of the holonomy source confirmed its edge convention W[target,source], so the stated similarity and derivative index orders are consistent. This is not an implementation audit or certificate replay.
