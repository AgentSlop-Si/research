# FFT research — four-column compatibility and algebraic resultants

**October 9, 2026** — reproducible fixed-shuffle FFT feasibility research. This project does **not** claim a faster FFT.

## Main new results

1. A necessary **11×9 rank-deficient matrix** for any two globally separated rank-one stages with the fixed 8-point shuffle. Unlike the prior Fourier min-cut constraints, it enforces compatibility among multiple columns and handles zero projected columns.
2. The new gate rejects **3,266/4,596** prior GF17 candidates in the regular tail/quartic branch. Exact positive controls for both nonzero and forced-zero output generators passed.
3. A polynomial 9×9 minor **\(\widehat D=-D_B D_H\)** was derived exactly over `Z[z]/(z^4+1)`: 250 terms, total degree 8; factors 9 terms/degree2 and 105 terms/degree6.
4. On the complete one-dimensional regular slice `h0=1,h1=h2=0`, the cubic W and the new minor have **nonzero resultant over C**, certified modulo `(17,z-9)` by residue **6**. Hence no complex factorization on that slice.
5. Exact nonzero resultants of 37 and 476 terms constrain **all regular** complex solutions to a proper algebraic hypersurface union. The candidate locus in the four-variable regular chart has **dimension at most two**.

## Reproduce

Requirements: Python 3.11+, numpy, sympy. From the extracted folder:

```sh
python run_all.py
```

For individual calculations:

```sh
python exact_polynomial_gate.py
python exact_resultant.py
python slice_certificate.py
python universal_gate.py
```

The research remains restricted to the **fixed permutation and globally separated generator class**. No full complex no-solution certificate or sub-`N log N` FFT has been obtained.
