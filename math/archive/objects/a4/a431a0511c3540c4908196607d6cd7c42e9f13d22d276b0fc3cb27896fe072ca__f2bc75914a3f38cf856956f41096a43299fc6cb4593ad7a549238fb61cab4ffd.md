## Mathematical Scope

Full 249-line exact positive-sign finite-field Fourier executor for powers of two with 8<=B<N<=B², L=N/B. It builds L independent B-point FFTs, folds even frequencies by dyadic butterflies, gathers residual rows according to multiplication-by-(1+B) orbits, then performs orbit FFTs with phase factors. The displayed geometric-sum formula supports the sparse residual oracle. The final count is exactly N log2(N)/2 butterflies; source explicitly says no speedup. The finite field has 2-power roots only through 2^23.
