## Purpose

Divisor-window composite plan, randomized rows and coordinate medians.

## Complete logic and mathematical identity

CompositePlan.build searches a logarithmic divisor window, restricts M<=sqrt(N), and refuses lengths with no qualifying divisor. row_estimate checks signal length and delegates the exact labeled projected-FFT identity. robust_estimate requires positive odd rows and takes coordinate medians. Variance and median helpers use the coarse M(M-1)/phi(N) bound.
