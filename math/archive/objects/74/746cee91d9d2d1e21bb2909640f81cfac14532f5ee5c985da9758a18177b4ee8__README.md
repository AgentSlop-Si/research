## Results

- Four-term Green pencil + arbitrary internal two-sided phases/permutations is **impossible** for the precisely seeded outer factors and N=4,8,16: **576**, **112896**, and **11289600** ordered mappings all have full-rank all-minor projective certificates.
- N8 exhaustive check passes two good finite fields. N16 passes one. Independent Python subdeterminants and planted positive controls validate the audit machinery.
- General theorem: m-by-m rank-one projective minor tests are mathematically incapable of establishing emptiness for q>= (m-1)^2+1 independent Green components. Five-term circuits require at least a 4x4 window for this type of certificate.
- Tested four-Green executor has O(N) field-arithmetic application. **It is not the DFT. No asymptotic FFT speedup.**
