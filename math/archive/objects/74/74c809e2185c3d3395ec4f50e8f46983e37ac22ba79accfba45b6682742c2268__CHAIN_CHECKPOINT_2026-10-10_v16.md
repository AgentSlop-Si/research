## New exact theorems

1. For `D=diag(1,omega,...,omega^(N-1))` and any permutation `P`,

   `rank(D-P) = N - (# permutation cycles whose index sum is 0 mod N)`.

2. Therefore

   `min_P rank(ZF-FP) = floor(N/2)`.

   The unique minimizer is negation `j -> -j mod N`.

3. The minimizer splits the DFT into full-rank cosine-like and sine-like halves of dimensions `ceil(N/2)` and `floor(N/2)`; it does not leave a cheap seed problem.

4. Any input-independent scalar Fourier-output recurrence valid for every input needs order/state dimension at least `N`.

5. Any deterministic/adaptive projected-row transcript with fewer than `N` total scalar summaries has a nonzero common-kernel input indistinguishable from zero, regardless of overlapping partitions or nonlinear postprocessing.

6. If projected rows are executed separately with one `N` scan and one `K_r log K_r` transform each, rank coverage forces at least `Omega(N log N)` in that schedule.

7. Any overlapping sliding preconditioner `p(Z)` satisfies `F p(Z)=p(D) F`; it only output-scales the Fourier transform.
