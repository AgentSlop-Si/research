# Prior-art boundary — v18

The proof uses two classical ingredients and one project-specific composition.

1. **Prime Fourier minors.** Chebotarev's theorem says every square minor of a complex Fourier matrix of prime order is nonzero. Tao's 2003 paper gives a short proof and its equivalent sharp uncertainty principle for `Z/pZ`:
   - Terence Tao, *An uncertainty principle for cyclic groups of prime order*, arXiv:math/0308286.

2. **Cyclic resolvent formula.** For an `N`-cycle `P`,
   `(I-alpha P)^(-1)=(1-alpha^N)^(-1) sum_{m=0}^{N-1} alpha^m P^m`.
   This is elementary finite geometric-series algebra.

3. **Project-specific step.** v18 combines an elementary repeated-halving lemma for several total orders with the rank-one form of a cyclic Green kernel on a simultaneously separated rectangle. A limited search did not locate this exact Fourier/Green-bank theorem. That is not a literature-wide novelty proof.

The result should be described as a checked project theorem pending independent mathematical review. It is not an unrestricted FFT lower bound.
