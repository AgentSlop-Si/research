# Stop rules and next exact-DFT milestone

This file is intended to stop future sessions from treating approximation or dynamic-caching results as a solution to the user's original target.

## Original target (still unsolved here)

Provide an explicit **uniform**, **single-processor**, **all-input**, **full-output**, **exact** length-N complex Fourier transform algorithm with `O(N)` word/arithmetic operations in a precisely stated cost model, **counting** scalar/root preparation, indexing, storage, and preprocessing. Comparison to OpenAI result #130 must use the *same* computational model and supplied-root conventions.

## What the current experiment excludes

A strategy consisting of (i) tabulating **all possible** K-component blocks over any alphabet q>=2 with no more than `N^c` stored cells, (ii) using each table to replace at most log2(K) consecutive radix-2 FFT layers, and (iii) reading/writing all N active entries once per grouped pass, cannot have total `O(N)` time, since K=O(log N) and at least Omega(log N/log log N) passes remain. This lower bound is restricted to this complete-table grouped-stage architecture, not to all FFTs or to non-enumerative symbolic compilers.

## Results that must NOT be passed off as the exact target

- The finite-field Walsh–Hadamard tabulation theorem (Alman 2023; known transform and model).
- Bounded-precision **normalized** DFT with fixed additive error epsilon; approximate tabulation may use O(N log N/log log N) word operations under extra assumptions, but not exact FFT.
- Monte Carlo sampling: approximate normalized DFT in O(N log log N) arithmetic for a constant epsilon, with a nonzero failure probability.
- Sparse/low-rank/related inputs, incremental updates, partial outputs, memoized answers, full-table oracles, parallel processors, FFT padding/packing, small circuit gate-count reductions.
- CrocSwap or OpenAI #130 network parameter retuning, which was separately audited and remains a different research direction.

## A falsifiable genuinely new target

To reach O(N), one needs more than an O(log log N)-stage lookup bundle at each pass. A candidate must either implement omega(log log N) consecutive exact FFT layers in O(N) aggregate without huge lookup state, or avoid repeated full-data passes by a different exact representation. A proposed compressed representation must preserve every independent input variable, support the required exact roots, and allow full N-output recovery with the charged claimed cost. Start by proving the candidate's composition and representation size, not by measuring a single small FFT benchmark.

No such construction is provided in this package.
