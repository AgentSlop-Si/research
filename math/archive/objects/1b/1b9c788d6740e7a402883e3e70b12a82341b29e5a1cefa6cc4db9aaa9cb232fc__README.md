# Phase 17 — exact and robust barriers for nonunitary noncommuting Green sums

This is a continuation of the user's own FFT/graph sharing project, not a reproduction of OpenAI or CrocSwap.

**Results:** An independently permuted sum of `q` inverse-banded Green operators has an unavoidable rank defect across two large groups of coordinates, regardless of term commutativity, diagonal gauges, numerical conditioning, or the DFT length. For a normalized Fourier matrix, this proves `floor(N/2^q)^2 <= W*N`, where `W` is the sum of the Green inverse operators' half-bandwidths. With q tridiagonal terms, `N<=q 4^q` is necessary when `2^q` divides N, so at least `Ω(log N)` such terms are needed. There is also a **positive operator-norm approximation gap** if the term count is too small.

The project also includes prime-order full-spark and Bluestein/Hankel specialized controls, independent modular tests, and a **product of two noncommuting sums** that escapes the additive rank obstruction but still fails the exact Fourier Weyl covariance equation. This keeps the next research path open and sharply defined.
