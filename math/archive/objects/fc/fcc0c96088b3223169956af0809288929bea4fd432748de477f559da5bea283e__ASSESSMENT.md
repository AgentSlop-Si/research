## A The precise surviving theorem

Let U be an N by N complex flat unitary, so every entry has modulus N^(-1/2). Let r≥1 and

    T = sum_(i=1)^r L_i Q_i^(-1) R_i,

where each Q_i is invertible and each endpoint has support contained in a permutation matrix. Endpoint permutations are independent, and endpoint weights may vanish. Assume the symmetric bipartite support graph of each Q_i has a half-balanced vertex separator of size at most q for each marked set used in the proof. Treewidth at most w is sufficient with q=w+1. Then

    U=T       implies N≤4qr36^r,
    U=D+T     implies N≤16qr36^r,

for an arbitrary diagonal D. If each Q_i has at most two nonzero entries per column, its lift is a pseudoforest, so q=3 and the bounds are N≤12r36^r and N≤48r36^r. Thus fixed q requires r=Ω(log N) for exact representations. No row-degree bound or common graph is assumed. The N=1, r=0 diagonal exception is excluded by r≥1.

The separately proved error statement uses a common m by m rectangle, m≥1, on which the candidate has rank at most qr:

    ||U−T||_F² ≥ max(0,m²/N−qr),
    ||U−T||_2² ≥ max(0,m/N−qr/m).

With the diagonal correction, replace T by D+T in these error norms. The selection recurrence is m_(j+1)=floor((m_j−q)/6), starting at N, or at floor(N/2) with disjoint row/column sets to remove D. Each step requires m_j≥4q. Under the exact-theorem contradiction thresholds, the final m is at least N/(2·6^r), or N/(4·6^r) with D. Fixed r,q therefore prevent vanishing operator or normalized Frobenius error as N grows. This does **not** require r=Ω(log N) for a fixed positive error tolerance.

### What is established precedent

The complementary inverse-rank identity is the classical nullity theorem; the separator-to-inverse-rank step is an immediate application. Weighted tree-decomposition separators are classical. Graph-induced rank structures already formalize sparse-support inverse rank and common-graph algebra. These ingredients deserve direct attribution, rather than claims of a new inverse-structure theory. [Vandebril–Van Barel](https://www.cs.kuleuven.be/publicaties/rapporten/tw/TW397.pdf), [Dvořák–Norin](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.3869), [Chandrasekaran–Epperly–Govindarajan](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.05858).

The actual inherited theorem was read, not merely its review. It already selects a common rectangle across independent orders and uses flat-unitary singular-value bounds, including an operator-error gap. Concatenating bare-cycle orders preserves separation rank one. Therefore neither independent routing nor unbounded bare-cycle count is the new contribution. The new proof supplies fresh weighted separators after each restriction and obtains an unconditional constant separator budget for arbitrary column-two support, including attached trees, and more generally bounded-treewidth lifts.

### Significance and obstacle

The application is a useful representation obstruction with an elementary combinatorial core. Its shortness is a virtue, but also raises the publication question of whether the exact combination is already implicit in structured-matrix literature and whether the model is independently useful. High degree alone is not a strictness witness: the GIRS paper's arrowhead example admits rank-one structure on a line despite high-degree support. Nor has this assessment shown that every attached-tree branch lies outside the inherited abstract ordered class. A persuasive note should give a worked family clarifying why the separator formulation buys coverage, or carefully state the improvement as a theorem under weaker structural hypotheses without asserting an unproved class separation.

The bound is not shown sharp. It controls additive branch count, not shared arithmetic, products, interleavings, or arbitrary circuits. Efficient low-treewidth linear-system algorithms are relevant motivation, but their upper bounds cannot be converted into an Ω(Nr) lower bound for a shared implementation. [Fomin et al., Theorem 1.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1511.01379).

## B The precise surviving theorem package

Write H_n(z;q)=sum_k [n choose k]_q z^k and D_n(q)=Disc_z H_n(z;q), using the ordinary derivative discriminant. Fix odd d≥3, primitive ω of order d, odd 1≤b<d, A≥1 and n=Ad+b. Put

    H_b(z;ω)=(z+1)U(z),   g=U(−1),   F(z)=z^d+1,
    W=(ω;ω)_b/(ω²;ω²)_((b+d)/2),   τ=dW/g,
    J_(A+1)(y;τ)=He_(A+1)(y)+A(1−τ)He_(A−1)(y),
    q(ε)=ω(1+ε²)^(1/d).

Here He is the monic probabilists' Hermite polynomial, (a;q)_m=product_(j=0)^(m−1)(1−aq^j), and Disc(1)=1 by the empty-product convention. Require Disc(U)Res(F,U)≠0; in particular g≠0. Then the shared root has the exact formal leading model

    H_n(−1+εx;q(ε))
      = ε^(A+1)(−1)^(A+1)d^((A−1)/2)g
        J_(A+1)(−√d x;τ) + O(ε^(A+2)).

If additionally S_A(τ)=Disc J_(A+1)≠0, put L=dA(A−1)/2+A and P_A=product_(j=1)^A j^j. Then

    D_n(q(ε)) = ε^(2L)(C(ω)+O(ε²)),
    C(ω)=(−1)^(A(d−1)/2)d^(dA(A+1)/2+A)P_A^(d−1)
          ·S_A(τ)·Disc(U)·Res(F,U)^(2A)·g².

In particular ord_(Φ_d)D_n=L. The normalized integer quotient evaluates to Q_d(ω)^L C(ω), where Q_d=(q^d−1)/Φ_d. The full leading coefficient, its parameter τ, and the shared-cluster interpretation are the principal contribution candidates. The companion separated theorem has local model He_A and order dA(A−1)/2 under its own residual guards.

Two consequences make a focused note more substantial:

- **Paired-residue squarefreeness.** For d≡3 mod4 and b=(d−1)/2, let U_b=H_b/(z+1), Γ_d=Disc(U_b)Res(z^d+1,U_b), and c_d^pair be the coefficient content of the nonzero canonical remainder of Γ_d(q)Γ_d(q^(d−1)) modulo Φ_d. For a prime p≡3 mod4 with p∤d c_d^pair, H_((dp−1)/2)(z;ζ_(dp)) is squarefree. Reciprocal parameters satisfy 1/τ+1/τ*=1, and at least one associated J is squarefree in characteristic p. Exact base contents for d=3,7,11,15,19 yield five infinite families. The separate certificates at N=9,45,49,121,361, rather than the uniform ramified argument, close their p|d cases.
- **Conditional separated-content valuation.** For positive odd d, set c_d^sep to the coefficient content of the nonzero canonical remainder of D_((d−1)/2)(q)Res_z(z^d+1,H_((d−1)/2)(z;q)) modulo Φ_d. If s>0, s≡1 mod4, p≡1 mod4 is prime, c_s^sep≠0 and p∤s c_s^sep, then c_(sp)^sep≠0 and v_p(c_(sp)^sep)=floor(3s(p−1)/8). This includes s=1 with c_1^sep=1. It does not determine all unramified divisors or repeated-p ramification.

The separated and paired contents are different invariants. Retain both superscripts throughout a manuscript.

### What must be attributed

The fixed-root factorization and q→1 Hermite limit are established. J is a familiar quasi-Hermite form; its parity reduction is quasi-Laguerre, and its standalone discriminant follows from Sawa–Uchida's Theorem 4.8. The characteristic-p Wronskian identity is a hook specialization of existing pseudo-Wronskian equivalence and a Hermite congruence. The last content-valuation step is standard cyclotomic arithmetic. The candidate is their specific local Rogers–Szegő combination and application. [Root-of-unity factorization](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0811.4100), [quasi-orthogonal discriminants](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.00605v1), [pseudo-Wronskians](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.05514), [Hermite congruence, Theorem 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.00065v1).

Ismail–Witte's explicit Rogers–Szegő product is a q-operator discriminant, not D_n above. Complex orthogonality alone also cannot replace the squarefreeness proof: Spiridonov–Zhedanov explicitly distinguishes this from positive real orthogonality. These are exact scope distinctions, not exhaustive novelty exclusions. [Ismail–Witte, §5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0012259), [Spiridonov–Zhedanov, introduction and §2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/q-alg/9605034).

1. **Prepare private self-contained drafts now.** Correctness acceptance suffices for manuscript development. No denied route was retried or bypassed. For A, follow citations on nullity, graph/vertex-separator inverse ranks, HSS/quasiseparable representations and sums with unrelated hierarchical partitions. Compare complete hypotheses and conclusions, not titles. The two older B papers are necessary checks, not an exhaustive clearance list.
3. **Make a substantive distinction visible.** B needs the exact fixed-ω, moving-z, shared-root coefficient compared with older zero/orthogonality results. For A, define sr(B) as the minimum, over independently chosen row and column orders, of the maximum rank of a strictly separated rectangle. The precise strictness gate is either to exhibit a family of invertible column-two matrices Q_N with sr(Q_N^(-1)) unbounded, or identify an existing theorem yielding a uniform constant bound for the entire class. The former would certify a strict extension beyond every fixed ordered-separation budget; the latter could subsume the claimed structural increment under the old abstract theorem. An unfavorable natural ordering or unbounded graph degree proves neither. Until this is settled, say that the new theorem establishes coverage not previously established in the inspected project record, without claiming certified set-theoretic strictness. Do not manufacture significance by adding numerical cases or optimizing loose constants alone.
4. **Obtain subject-expert evaluation only with authorization.** A specialist should assess whether the application is new enough and useful enough for a short paper. If B's local coefficient is already known, reframe its arithmetic application with attribution; if that is known too, preserve it as exposition rather than force a publication claim. Use the same rule for A. No venue acceptance, posting, submission or outreach is authorized or performed here.
