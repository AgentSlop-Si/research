# FFT research continuation, phase 11: graph separator and order-robust Green stages

**October 9, 2026 | Independent mathematical work continued from the user's Green-stage archive**

This is a research checkpoint, **not** a claimed solution to the FFT complexity problem. It avoids the separate CrocSwap/integer-multiplication certificate route and instead audits the user's global Green-operator strategy.

## Major findings

1. **Correction / no-go:** `HIDDEN_ORDER_THEOREM.md` shows the old parity-mixed Green stage had hidden `O(1)` quasiseparable rank (<=4 in the cheap interleaved order). This yields a new two-stage exact Fourier obstruction through an arbitrary intermediate permutation, for N>=64, even with optional Householder + phase scalings.
2. **Explicit exact inverse:** `LINEAR_INVERSE.md`, `linear_inverse.py` derive and execute the previous dense stage's inverse in O(N) field work using a pentadiagonal solve plus a rank-two Woodbury update. Being dense in both directions was not meaningful evidence of hardness.
3. **Deterministic growing-rank alternative:** `GRID_THEOREM.md`, `grid_stage.py` give an all-N O(N)-apply, kappa2<=5 stage built from row/column grid paths. Every balanced cut in every order has Omega(sqrt(N)) singular values >=1/2500.
4. **Randomized linear-rank alternative (strongest):** `EXPANDER_THEOREM.md`, `expander_stage.py` give a uniformly randomized O(N)-setup, O(N)-apply, kappa2<=5 stage. With probability at least `1-4(N+1) 2^(-cN)` for `c>0.3254`, every balanced cut of every ordering has Omega(N) singular values >=1/2500. Four concrete random permutations have **exact integer spectral-gap certificates**, not just numerical observations.

**Unresolved:** No factorization of an exact DFT into these stages, no o(NlogN) arithmetic algorithm, no linear-time FFT. Global mixing in O(N) work is *possible* but insufficient to compute the DFT. The new theorem removes a structural objection, not the underlying synthesis difficulty.

## Important new falsification: norm budget

`NORM_GATE.md` derives `kappa_1(M)<=101/19` for EVERY choice of two path orders. Hence products of only these stages, arbitrary interstage permutations, and unit-phase diagonals still need at least `log_(101/19)(N)` factors for the DFT. The stronger expansion theorem does **not** beat the FFT bound. `haar_control.py` is an honest O(N)-work multiscale control with growing induced condition (`Omega(sqrt(N))`) and bounded spectral condition, but it supplies **no exact Fourier factorization** and could telescope if used as a gauge.

The strongest claimed advance is construction of an **order-robust** linear-time globally mixing stage, not a complexity improvement for the DFT itself.
