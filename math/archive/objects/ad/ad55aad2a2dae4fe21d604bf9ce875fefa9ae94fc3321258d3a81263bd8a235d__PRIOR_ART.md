# Prior-art and novelty boundaries — Phase 35

The Fourier boundary identities `F e0=1`, `e0^T F=1^T`, `F 1=N e0` are elementary classical Fourier facts. Our flat diagonal of the **one selected idempotent** `A^-1 e0 e0^T A` is a project-specific reformulation of necessary boundary consistency for a diagonal-between-regular-factors decomposition, not a claim of a new theory of complex Hadamard matrices.

Full reciprocal-entry orthogonality belongs to the well-established theory of *type-II matrices*; see Jaeger, Matsumoto and Nomura, **Bose–Mesner Algebras Related to Type II Matrices and Spin Models** (Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics, 1998), DOI 10.1023/A:1008691327727, and Godsil's expository treatment of type-II matrices and Nomura algebras (https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~cgodsil/quagmire/Fields24/pdfs/qhoms.pdf).

The Green resolvent identity and cycle rank-defect machinery were developed and audited in earlier phases of this project, especially Phase 34, itself packaged unchanged in `previous_full_phase34_checkpoint.zip`.

The project-specific advances in this phase are: (i) the exact seed-balancing iff statement and normalized two-stage boundary fingerprint; (ii) its application to all weak positive-weighted Hamiltonian Green sums; (iii) genuinely characteristic-zero integer-polynomial no-go certificates for two fixed rank-distant cycle pairs; (iv) executable first-row/first-column Green interpolation that does not falsely claim a complete FFT.

**No independently peer-reviewed priority or general sub-`N log N` FFT claim is made.**
