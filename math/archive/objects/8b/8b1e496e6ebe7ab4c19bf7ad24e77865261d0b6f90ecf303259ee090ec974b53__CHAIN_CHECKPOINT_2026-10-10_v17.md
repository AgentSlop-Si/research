## New theorem 1: two Green terms

For prime `N>=11`, let `A,B` be invertible matrices each supported on its diagonal plus one weighted permutation graph. Then no matrix monomially equivalent to the Fourier matrix satisfies

`F = A^{-1} + B^{-1}`.

This covers two independently ordered left/right diagonally gauged Green resolvents.

Proof gate:

1. `F=A^{-1}+B^{-1}` implies `A F B=A+B`.
2. A universal two-row/two-column witness makes `(A+B)[I,J]=0` with row/column supports of size at most three.
3. Chebotarev full-minor rank makes the corresponding `A F B` block nonzero.

## New theorem 2: generic two semiseparable stages

For prime `N>=31`, two invertible rank-one semiseparable stages with nonzero adjacent generator minors on the relevant input/output sides cannot factor a monomially gauged/permuted Fourier matrix, regardless of the internal permutation.

Three consecutive tail-annihilator windows have rank three and occupy five coordinates. A disjoint five-coordinate output interval gives a zero product block, while a prime Fourier `5x5` minor is invertible and cannot map a rank-three space into a two-dimensional kernel.
