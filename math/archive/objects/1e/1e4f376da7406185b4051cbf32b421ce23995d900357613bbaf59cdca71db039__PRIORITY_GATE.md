## Exact quasi Laguerre reduction

With t=y²/2, the accepted normalization gives

    A=2m−1: J_(2m)(y;τ)=2^m P_m(t),
    P_m=(-1)^m m![L_m^(-1/2)+((2m−1)(τ−1)/(2m))L_(m−1)^(-1/2)],

    A=2m: J_(2m+1)(y;τ)=y2^m P_m(t),
    P_m=(-1)^m m![L_m^(1/2)+(τ−1)L_(m−1)^(1/2)].

The P_m are monic. [Sawa–Uchida, Theorem 4.8 and equation 4.13 in arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.00605v1) gives the ordinary discriminant of each adjacent-degree Laguerre combination. Elementary quadratic pullback gives

    Disc[2^mP(y²/2)] = (−1)^m 2^(2m²+m) P(0) Disc(P)^2,
    Disc[y2^mP(y²/2)] = (−1)^m 2^(2m²+3m) P(0)^3 Disc(P)^2.

Thus a standalone formula for Disc(J) is not the principal novelty candidate. The specific τ=dW/g and its appearance in the fixed-root local coefficient require separate comparison. ## Exact hook congruence

Let U=He_A(y), V=i^(−A)He_A(iy), and K_A=yUV−U′V+UV′. The accepted reduction identifies K_A with the monic normalized Hermite Wronskian for the hook λ=(A+1,1^A), degree sequence {1,…,A,2A+1}. [Gómez-Ullate, Grandati and Milson, Theorem 3.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.05514) supplies the pseudo-Wronskian equivalence; monicity fixes the scalar. For p=2A+1, the sequence has p-unit Vandermonde and reduces to {0,…,A}. [Grosu–Grosu, Theorem 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.00065v1) therefore gives K_A≡y^p. ## Standard content conversion
