# Independent review of the N64 outer correction rejection

## Result and scope

Accepted. In the specified N16-to-N64 nested geometry, the two literal inner pullbacks of the accepted algebraic N16 seed cannot be corrected to the positive, unnormalized Fourier matrix F64 by any of the 512 outer endpoint coefficients. The independently recomputed separation bound is 2.6580716254091155e-6, strictly below one. At least one inner layer must change. The full 1536-complex-parameter N64 family, and the 1152-parameter mean-anchored family allowing inner changes, remain open.

This review attributes the proposed certificate and its mathematical construction to the constructor track. It independently validates the argument and its exact arithmetic. No uploaded program, constructor verifier, numerical solver, or proposal script was executed. The proposal's dyadic matrix is treated only as exact input. This is an additive review; earlier seals remain unchanged.

## Model and exact algebraic seed

The small grid has a=b=4, labels 4x+y. Eight raw permutations are H_c^s(x,y)=(x+s(2y+c),y), V_c^s(x,y)=(x,y+s(2x+c)), c=0,1 and s=-1,+1, with modular coordinates. Their cycles are joined by rotating heads at sorted minimum representatives. The eight labels are H0-, H0+, H1-, H1+, V0-, V0+, V1-, V1+. At the next level the grid is 8 by 8, with projection pi(8x+y)=4(x mod 4)+(y mod 4).

For each label, define eta=P_small T_small^{-1}, apply eta to the projected output of T_large while preserving the two high fiber bits, then rotate the four heads at tails 0,4,32,36. This produces a Hamiltonian permutation P_large with pi P_large=P_small pi. The independent verifier regenerates these eight permutations, checks their cycle counts and quotient identities, and uses the convention P e_j=e_{P(j)}.

The Green matrices are G_j=(I-P_j/(j+2))^{-1}. The bank has F8 blocks at x=0,1,4,5 and I8 blocks elsewhere. H_l=sum_j diag(a_l,:,j)G_j, and the three-layer product is H_2 H_1 B H_0. Both inner layers copy the coefficients of the accepted N16 root a*=a0+V y* onto all four sheets. The outer layer is entirely free, including zero endpoints.

The prerequisite seed is the full 256-complex-equation local-existence certificate, not a rounded candidate. The exact chart SHA256 is 904d7d24bf3aeee0a7c206eb8fd98ee11045fbc22cc97a105511b51907e1b188. Its independently verified contraction constants e and q imply

    ||y*||_infinity <= e/(1-q) = 1.8566120547130935e-12 < rho = 2e-12.

This follows from y*=T(y*) and ||T(y*)-T(0)||<=q||y*|| on the original radius-1e-9 polydisk. It does not assert that a0 is a solution. The bundled seed_audit verifier reproduces all 256 equations, root enclosure, 384 endpoint guards and exact contraction bounds from the chart.

## Why the augmented minor rejects every outer choice

Let X(y)=H_1(y)B H_0(y). The output row zero of H_2 X is a linear combination of the eight row templates row_0(G_j X). The target row zero of F64 is exactly all ones; no primitive 64th-root approximation occurs.

Restrict these eight rows and the target row to columns

    27,31,11,28,47,10,30,59,51.

They form the square augmented matrix M(y). If M(y*) is nonsingular, the target row cannot lie in the span of the eight templates, so even the row-zero outer equation is impossible. Other output rows and outer nonzero guards are immaterial to this rejection.

The dyadic 9-by-9 preconditioner R has denominator 2^80. The exact computation proves ||I-R M(y*)||_infinity<1. A Neumann series makes R M(y*) invertible, hence M(y*) invertible. No prior invertibility assumption on R is used.

## Exact arithmetic and perturbation bounds

Set L=lcm(d^64-1: d=2,...,9) and D=2^60 L. Every large Green is an integer matrix divided by L. Its entries are generated from the cycle sum; every entry of (I-P/d)G=I is independently checked, including all 32,768 scalar inverse equalities. Center inner layers and all 256 affine directions have Gaussian-integer numerators over D.

The only new algebraic approximation is sqrt(2) in F8. Let Q=2^160 and s=floor(sqrt(2 Q^2)); the verifier checks s^2<=2Q^2<(s+1)^2. Replacing sqrt(2)/2 by s/(2Q) changes each complex entry in real/imaginary l1 norm by less than 1/Q. Therefore ||B-B0||_infinity<=8/Q, while the true bank's complex matrix infinity norm is exactly eight.

Every complex coefficient is bounded using |Re z|+|Im z|. The parameter norm is max_t |y_t| with ordinary complex modulus; this distinction is respected. The induced bounds are

    h_l >= ||H_l(0)||_infinity,
    L_l >= max_i sum_(k,t) |(partial_t H_l)[i,k]|,
    ||H_l(y)-H_l(0)||_infinity <= L_l ||y||_infinity.

All 256 directions are included after physical fiber pullback. Their amplification is not dropped. The bounds agree exactly with the source fractions: h0<64.842, h1<52.089, L0<383.329, L1<370.104.

Write

    Delta = 8 L1 rho (h0+L0 rho) + 8 h1 L0 rho + h1 (8/Q) h0.

The first two terms bound the two inner-layer variations, including their quadratic product; the last bounds the bank enclosure at the center. Thus ||X(y*)-H1(0)B0H0(0)||_infinity<=Delta.

For each preconditioned row, define f_r=sum_(j=0)^7 R[r,j] row_0(G_j). Sum these exact coefficients before taking norms, so their cancellations are retained. The independent result is beta=max_r||f_r||_1<=0.4198523206874582. Each selected entry changes by at most beta Delta. Summing the nine selected columns gives the valid (conservative) bound 9 beta Delta. The ninth, all-ones target row has no variation.

The own verifier explicitly constructs the eight center templates, appends the target row, and multiplies R M0. This differs from the constructor's order of multiplication. It obtains

    center defect       = 8.958977418443505e-14,
    perturbation bound  = 2.6580715358193415e-6,
    total               = 2.6580716254091155e-6 < 1.

The output JSON preserves exact fractions; these decimals are summaries. Every displayed source certificate fraction, both center layer bounds and direction bounds, and all inverse guards match exactly.

## Guards and interpretation

All d^64-1 are positive. The copied inner endpoints inherit the accepted algebraic seed's strict nonzero margins. The target's sign, normalization, ordering and bank placement are explicit above. The proof covers arbitrary complex outer endpoints and therefore also covers the admissible nonzero subset.

This is a finite coefficient-law exclusion. It is neither a separator theorem nor an arithmetic lower bound. It does not rule out changing either inner layer, changing the bank or orders, using other seeds, or a genuine multi-channel lift.
