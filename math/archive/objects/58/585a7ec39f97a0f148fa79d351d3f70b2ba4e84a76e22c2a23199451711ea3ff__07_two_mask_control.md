## Mathematical and operational scope

Positive two-mask, three-K8-mixer Fourier factorization at N=128,256,512, built from a=16,32,64 field descent and an 8-by-a Cooley-Tukey split. The exponent decomposition j=j1+a*j2 and output k=8*k1+k2 is consistent with the twiddle and final a-point transform.

## Control/dataflow and algebra

base_factor constructs B,q,A using exact orbit exponents. two_mask_fft gathers stride-a length-eight transforms, applies T_N, A, q and B. verify compares basis columns and one deterministic dense vector with a direct DFT.

## Input/guard and acceptance review

The stated N and vector lengths are asserted; root divisibility is asserted. Caller-supplied fac is not checked for shape, modulus, provenance or reconstruction. All helper equality asserts disappear under -O, while dense_input_direct_DFT_match and other flags remain True.

## Cost accounting

Dense B and A consume O(a^2) storage. Even sparse nonzero summations scan all a entries for each row; two applications each do O(8*a^2) scanning. Direct DFT is O(N^2) modular powers, and all-basis verification multiplies transform cost by N. It is explicitly no asymptotic speedup and has a bounded supported size set.

## Test coverage and limitations

N=128 all-basis verification plus one dense vector is materially stronger than sampled columns. N=256/512 use six basis columns and one deterministic dense vector only. No adversarial fac or optimized-mode coverage.

## Dependency identity

field_descent.cycles and galois_gate.MOD are in-batch, but both import additional modules at load time. No NumPy FFT oracle is used.
