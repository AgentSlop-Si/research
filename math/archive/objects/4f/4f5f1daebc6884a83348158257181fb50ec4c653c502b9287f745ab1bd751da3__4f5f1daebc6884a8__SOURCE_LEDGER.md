## Key differences from existing material

The archived cyclic-feedback result already uses an exact finite feedback inverse, but closes a **rank-one** edge between target banks after transform injections are supplied. It does not supply cheap first transforms for arbitrary raw input. ACFF instead asks for a full-rank cold map `(I-BA)^(-1)B` and moves its unproved requirement to a low-degree matrix annihilator plus an exact phase certificate.

The existing nilpotent q-kernel degree theorem states `degree(p)+degree(r)>=N-1` for that particular rational representation. ACFF does not shorten that kernel. Similarity preserves minimal-polynomial degree, so a change of basis alone cannot produce the desired loop. A genuinely different operator BA is required.

The old phase-bank fusion gives a complete exact transform but retains the standard arithmetic cost. ACFF does not claim that full-rank information can pass through fewer-than-N linear measurements. In fact its rank-preservation theorem forces B to be full rank.

The previous mass-contraction theory has recursive Fourier calls. The ACFF execution kernel has none; the missing parameter family is a different, still unresolved constructive problem.

## Claims developed and checked in this package

- Cubic residual identity `HDB-BSJ = H(DT-TS)J`, plus the seed equivalence, with no assumption of B invertibility before certification.
- Exact finite polynomial closure and charged `2d-1` map-call evaluator.
- Paired-Green synthesis architecture and explicit quadratic specialization, both unconstructed as growing Fourier families.
- Pure-Green feedback exclusion for N>8.
- Generic degree-N theorem for any fixed four cycle orders, with a Vandermonde specialization.
- Support and rank-preservation rejection gates.
- Independent exact modular tests and scalar counters, recorded only from new runs.

These are project deductions or proposed combinations. This wording does not assert external publication novelty or independent peer review.

## What remains unproved

There are no uniform parameter/cycle formulas producing a Fourier-compatible paired-Green loop of degree o(log N). There is no practical floating-point FFT speedup, certified conditioning bound, bit-complexity improvement, or integer-multiplication result in this package. The conditional theorem would establish a faster exact scalar algorithm only after its missing construction and cost obligations are met.
