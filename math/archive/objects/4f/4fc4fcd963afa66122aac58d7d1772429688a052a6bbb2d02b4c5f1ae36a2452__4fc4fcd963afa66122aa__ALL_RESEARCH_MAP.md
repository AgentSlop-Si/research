## 1. The target and the baseline must be stated correctly

The requested target is a fresh arbitrary complex input, all N outputs, exact DFT, with total preparation, scalar arithmetic, indexing, routing, workspace restoration, and output writing charged, in o(N log N) work.

There is an important baseline already present in our older research: the supplied official #130 manuscript states a uniform all-length exact algorithm in an **unrestricted exact complex-register model with one supplied polynomial-order root of unity**. The locally preserved introduction explicitly includes scalar preparation and scheduling and states O(N (log N)^theta (log log N)^(4-theta)), theta=0.99999999999978935615699598…<1. The official current [#130 summary](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/130.md), checked separately during this synthesis, states the weakened O(N(log N)^(1-10^-13)) bound. These are official source claims, not a new theorem proved by this research pass. They assert neither practical crossover nor finite-precision stability or bit complexity.

Consequently, “the exact target is still open” must mean **our proposed new construction or a stronger computational model remains unachieved**. It must not erase that inherited exact-register baseline. The most useful exact advance here would be a materially simpler/stronger explicit finite certificate, a new uniformly evaluated architecture, or an improvement in a precisely stronger model.

## 2. Evidence map: distinct routes, rather than repeated uploads

### A. Repeated tables and finite-alphabet closure

**Positive content.** Repeated whole-level lookup really accelerates constant-field Walsh–Hadamard transforms in the stated RAM bit-cost model. The reusable bookkeeping is preparation R 2^(bK)[C(K,b)+bK], and online NbD/log K plus routing and dispatch. A fixed-base-field extension permits coordinatewise WHTs with the extension degree charged.

**Barrier.** Only replacing bottom leaves leaves Theta(N log(N/K)). For an exact dyadic complex DFT coefficient on binary inputs, the attainable alphabet already has 3^(L/2) values at length L. Full-field tables for conventional length-N NTT elements have b=Theta(log N), forcing bounded K under polynomial table size. Explicit NTT output is Omega(N log N) **bits**, not scalar operations. Cyclic Fourier is not merely a diagonally rescaled/permuted Hadamard matrix, by the cross-ratio invariant.

**Live use.** Tables remain appropriate for bounded, quantized approximate states; they do not create exact arbitrary-complex closure. This is the ancestor of the v3/v4 decoder, not a rejected idea in every model.

Source: `fft_research_20261008/fft_lookup_research_note.md`.

### B. Synthetic transforms, cancellation, dirty workspace, and framed recursion

**Positive content.** Polynomial-ring twiddles can be signed coefficient permutations. Exact emitted-word verification can include every arbitrary dirty-scratch basis vector. A finite framed motif can improve an exponent if it changes the fully charged child profile: sum_t (n_t/W)(t/m)^(1-a)<1.

**Barrier.** Two ordinary consecutive synthetic butterfly layers need 2N additions in the proved cancellation-free grammar. The broader cancellation-aware search found no saving, but its heuristic failures and SMT timeouts are not general exclusions. A standalone scalar DAG does not supply a common-frame profile or restored physical roles. Moving a rounding boundary can change the computed function even when exact matrices agree. The retained-carrier bit-side modification was genuinely worse: its rank splits strictly increase the recurrence moment for every 0<a<1.

**Live use.** Nonunitary cancellation across a complete exact-width or all-role interface can still matter. The objective must be the actual recursive certificate, not a scalar-gate count with uncharged scaling or cleanup.

### C. Exact-width finite wins: the strongest existing exact acceptance path

**Positive content.** An exact 16-register word for H16 using at most 31 embedded H2 calls and explicit invertible monomials would pass the known finite-win interface. The strict recurrence check 31^200<32^199 supports any compatible construction with exponent s=199/200; the source's charged compiler gives the conditional all-length bound O(N(log N)^s(log log N)^(4-s)). This is tensor-sector recursion, not ordinary fixed-size FFT tiling.

**Barrier.** Widths 4 and 8 cannot beat their pair-call baselines by the complete-gossip support bound. Phase-only normalized words cannot beat the tensor baseline by matrix entropy. At width16, all 210,833,760 schedules obtained by deleting one baseline call and changing at most two remaining pairs were excluded: 556 survive reachability and all have rank-cut witnesses. This leaves general 31-call words unexcluded.

**Live use.** A genuinely nonunitary, exact-width finite word is a direct way to turn algebraic sharing into an asymptotic result under an already specified model. Section 5 refines this target using the new Haar idea.

### D. Exact sparse exponential sums, residue trees, and whole-subtree decoding

**Positive content.** For k distinct root modes, all N values have only k independent amplitudes. Common-denominator Bostan–Mori recursion is valid. More importantly, terminal decoders really can be fused away: with v=[P/Q]_<L and R=floor(Qv/t^L), the next block is J_Q v=-[(Q^-1 mod t^L)R]_<L. A whole subtree is one short-by-long reciprocal convolution. Equivalently the output map is transposed polynomial remainder modulo the k-root factor of x^N-1.

**Barrier.** A one-step rank-one displacement becomes rank min(t,k) after t shifts. Half-grid/denominator commutation has an exact alias correction; padding may eliminate that particular defect, so it is not a universal obstruction. Fully fused decoding currently costs O(N log k), including output. Prime-support annihilators and their complements are coefficient-dense; typical dyadic supports saturate Theta(log k) branching levels. These exclude zero-dropping and pruning-only shortcuts, not cheap dense circuits.

**Live use.** Genuine cross-state batching or a faster constrained transposed-remainder evaluator remains compatible with all these facts. A separate terminal inverse-FFT charge is **not intrinsic** after the established fusion.

### E. Thin rectangular multiplication

**Positive content.** Balanced baby-step/giant-step turns k=O(log N) exponential sums into a constant number of n-by-log n times log n-by-n products, n≈sqrt(N). Power tables and outputs cost O(N); a **uniform total-O(n²)** thin multiplication algorithm would therefore give exact O(N) sparse expansion. Fixed-alphabet Mailman batching supplies an explicit restricted analogue.

**Barrier.** Generic total-work Hypothesis H was not independently reconstructed. Later published sources explicitly say total operations, while the original abstract and other sources foreground multiplication counts. Neither declaring H proved nor declaring the later sources wrong is justified by the present evidence. Arbitrary root powers do not form a constant alphabet. Ordinary square tiling is too expensive.

**Live use.** Resolve the original total-work construction as a component/prior-art question. Even an affirmative sparse result is not dense FFT: processing N/k groups independently costs O(N²/k).

Sources: `fused_fft_research_20261009/{THIN_MATRIX,THIN_PRODUCT_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT,CONDITIONAL_THIN_COROLLARY}.txt`.

**Barrier scope.** These are information-interface or representation bounds, not N log N arithmetic-time bounds. For a fixed k-mode family the relevant rank is k, not N. Retained original inputs, emitted outputs, side memory, and discrete indices must be counted. The approximate decoder need not be injective.

**Live use.** Apply these checks to any proposed compression cut, including exactification of the new sketches in Section 4. They do not reject the sparse recurrence representation.

### G. Pure scans, broadcasts, and bidirectional feedthrough

**Positive content.** Pure serial one-state scans have bidiagonal inverses, giving t≥1+ceil(log2 N) even with arbitrary interleaved permutations and nonsingular diagonals. The scan/broadcast tradeoff also gives a logarithmic separately charged pass budget. Bidirectional sums escape that inverse-support premise, and have a scan–tridiagonal–scan representation. A raw bidirectional stage realizes F4; radix4 gives ceil(log4 N) stages at dyadic lengths.

**Barrier.** Dense inverse support alone does not establish distance from every diagonal-plus-low-rank map. Own-diagonal subtraction was an invalid diagnostic and was repaired with disjoint minors. One rank-one-quasiseparable stage cannot realize Fourier at N≥5. Complete-radix channel costs must be charged, not just stage count.

**Live use.** Feedthrough and multiple orders are genuine model changes. They do not remove generator-application or representation costs.

### J. Low-rank wrappers, forward cycles, and inverse cycles

**Positive content.** If rank(F-A)≤B for a flat unitary F and invertible A, then kappa1(A)≥N/(B+1)². This yields 7^s(B+1)²≥N for bounded-condition Green stages and rank-one wrappers. Forward-cycle K=(2I-M)(I-M)^-1 has a determinant-normalized spectral obstruction. Inverse stages really escape that forward-only argument: a pair gives a dense exact O(N)-field all-pass unitary.

**Remaining barriers.** Locally unitary pairs obey 7^s c_N^(2r)(B+1)²≥N, c_N<2. One fixed M/M* family cannot be flat at any depth. The current whole-word proof sharpens the earlier logarithmic phase-rotated commuting gate to t≥ceil(floor(N²/4)/N), even after global rational fusion and arbitrary nonsingular endpoint monomials. It uses quasiseparable rank of the entire reduced rational function, not per-pass charging. Genuinely noncommuting changes of cycle remain outside it.

**Critical positive controls.** An N/2-factor phase orbit can fuse exactly to a two-sparse O(N)-field evaluator, despite half-cut rank N/2. It is not Fourier, but proves that degree or cut rank is not runtime. Conversely, a signed-cycle quadratic-chirp polynomial of degree N−1 does realize Fourier up to endpoint diagonals for every N. This classical Bluestein representation attains the linear degree scale; applying its dense convolution has not been accelerated here, and it is not asserted to factor into the restricted K-stage family.

## 3. Compatible combinations and combinations that do not close the exact gap

1. **Decoder fusion is a real mechanism, in two different models.** Exact residue trees remove terminal transforms by an intertwining identity; v6 removes repeated short decoders by moving robust recovery earlier. In both cases the remaining changing-input operator must still be charged. “Decode once” is not itself an exact complexity theorem.
2. **Residual certificates now wrap the stronger approximation.** With u approximating Sx, r=u−P_d u and energy decoder error beta||r||2, the current proof gives error≤(73/18)[beta||r||2+epsilon_src+epsilon_num]+tau. Here epsilon_src can use either the old L1 residual divided by sqrt(N) or a Euclidean solve-residual bound. Mean fitting, filtering and target recovery add O(N) arithmetic. A direct mean-residual route and the Green route can be selected by their complete proved bounds. The v6 decoder still lacks its own finite-word rounding analysis, so this composition is an algebraic cost theorem, not a transferred end-to-end word-RAM theorem.
3. **Whole-word evaluation is real, but its representation is now constrained.** If qsep(T)≤h, then qsep(p(T)q(T)^−1)≤h max(deg p,deg q). Any endpoint-monomial Fourier realization needs h max(degrees)≥floor(N²/4)/N. Likewise a single banded-plus-low-rank solve with similarly structured pre/post maps needs total bandwidth-plus-rank at least this threshold. Arbitrarily many auxiliary variables do not evade it when each input/output is one scalar-coordinate port. These are representation gates, not time lower bounds: the two-sparse half-cycle control has large cut rank and still runs in O(N). Different nonlocal graph structures and genuinely changing orders remain outside the gates.
4. **Nonunitary mixing plus exact-width amplification remains compatible.** The sharp scan, flatness, fixed-norm, and unitary-entropy gates all have explicit premises. A finite exact word with genuine nonunitary gains, arbitrary-data spectators restored, and cross-band cancellations lies outside their combined unitary/separately-executed scope. Its cost must then pass the existing finite-win interface.
5. **Sparse and structured success cannot be silently promoted to dense success.** Few-coset spectra, affine defects and local Fourier sparsity are valuable exact classes. Their input dimension/recognition assumptions survive every composition unless a new reduction proves otherwise. No invertible cheap coordinate change by itself removes N independent degrees of freedom.

## 4. A precise new connection: why compressed medians cannot simply become an exact dense FFT

Fix the v6 random shifts and fourth-root phases. Before the final decoder, all dependence on x passes through R length-K complex spectra, with RK=RN/M. The estimates and coordinatewise real/imaginary medians are piecewise real-linear functions of those spectra; the final short decoder is linear.

On each median-selection region, the complete real-linear map therefore has rank at most 2RK. If R<M, it cannot equal the invertible 2N-dimensional real representation of Fourier on an open set. More strongly, for any fixed seed and any fixed selection of the medians, the equality set with Fourier is a proper real-linear subspace. There are finitely many median selections and finitely many shift/phase seeds. Thus outside a finite union of proper subspaces, **no random seed gives exact equality** when R<M in this compressed architecture.

This does not contradict the structured exact theorem: its inputs belong to exceptional lower-dimensional sets. It also does not apply to a decoder that retains extra original-input information or uses a different measurement architecture.

For arbitrary-input exactification within this architecture, R≥M is therefore necessary. Under the displayed separate-FFT v6 executor, the long transforms alone then have workload N log(N/M), while the one final short decode has N log M: together the ordinary N log N butterfly workload returns. This is an architecture/accounting statement, not an unrestricted arithmetic lower bound or a proof against all fusion of those transforms.

The bucketed v5 analogue replaces M by collision size c=M/d, since one row retains dK=N/c measurements. Its necessary condition is R≥c. Its separate executor likewise loses the claimed asymptotic saving when made generally exact.

## 5. One sharp constructive next lemma: a 16-call Haar-detail word

**Target lemma, not a proved existence claim.** Let H2=[[1,1],[1,-1]], H_(2^j)=H2 tensor … tensor H2, and

D16 = diag(I2, H2, H4, H8).

Construct an exact word for D16 on **exactly 16 arbitrary-data registers**, using at most **16 embedded H2 calls**, interleaved with explicit invertible rational or Q(i) diagonal scalings and permutations. The two I2 coordinates may be used as dirty work registers only if restored for every initial value. No extra clean or dirty registers, nonlinear tests, or input-dependent coefficient preparation are allowed.

### Why this particular lemma fuses the research rather than restarting it

Define W1=I and, for N=2m,

W_N = diag(W_m,I_m) B_N,

where B_N applies H2 to the m coordinate pairs (j,j+m). This is unnormalized Haar analysis in a fixed order. It uses m+(m-1)=N-1 H2 calls. Ordinary tensor algebra gives

H_N = D_N W_N,
D_N = diag(I2,H2,H4,…,H_(N/2)).

Proof by induction: H_N=diag(H_m,H_m)B_N; multiply on the right by W_N^-1 to obtain diag(H_m W_m^-1,H_m)=diag(D_m,H_m). At N16, W16 costs 15 calls and the separate detail blocks cost1+4+12=17. The proposed 16-call detail word would make the total 31.

This retains phase13's useful Haar basis, but asks for **sharing between detail bands and restored spectators**. Improving H4 or H8 separately cannot meet the goal in the already excluded exact-width model. A phase-only normalized detail word also cannot save a call: its matrix-entropy budget is 34, while one unitary two-register gate changes entropy by at most 2. Genuine nonunitary intermediate scaling is required. Green/inverse-cycle identities may help generate such cancellations, but any macro must be expanded and charged in the accepted H2-call grammar.

### Acceptance criterion

1. Print the complete 16-register word, including every nonzero scalar and permutation.
2. Independently replay its full 16×16 matrix exactly, including the two arbitrary spectator columns.
3. Count at most 16 actual H2 calls; no hidden dense, inverse or multiregister primitive may be counted as one.
4. Prepend the verified 15-call W16. The result must be exactly H16, not the cyclic 16-point DFT.
5. Use the known H2-to-C and three-call shear interface, then the source's fully charged finite-win compiler. The strict integer check 31^200<32^199 supplies a sufficient s=199/200 recurrence margin. The resulting all-length statement remains conditional on the inherited source compiler and its exact-register/supplied-root model.

The source's compiler, not a false Hadamard/Fourier equivalence, is what turns this Walsh finite win into a cyclic Fourier asymptotic result.

### What is already verified, and where to stop

The independently authored [Haar-split checker](verify_haar_split.py) checks H_N=D_N W_N exactly at N=2,4,8,16,32,64, emits both baseline 16-register words, and checks 31^200<32^199. Its output is [the exact Haar-split control](haar_split_control.json). The all-N identity is proved above; these finite checks corroborate it.

No 16-call D16 word has been found in this synthesis. That is the decisive next constructive object. A proposed topology should first pass dependency and min-cut tests with the **block-diagonal target**, then undergo exact coefficient solving and full-word replay. A numerical near-fit or failed bounded search is neither a certificate nor a general impossibility result. This target is a sufficient route, not an assertion that the needed word exists or that it is the globally best search problem.

## 6. Bottom line

The corpus contains genuine exact primitives, rigorous scoped rejections, successful algebraic fusion, and increasingly strong approximate/structured transforms. It does not yet contain our own new exact arbitrary-input FFT construction. The best coherent exact next step is to turn the Haar basis into a small **nonunitary, cross-band, exact-width saving** with a known amplification theorem, rather than continue optimizing proxy cut ranks, norms, or approximate accuracy while leaving Fourier equality and total cost unproved.
