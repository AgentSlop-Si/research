## New proven facts

1. `T=D^-1 Z`, `U=ZD`, `omega=exp(-2*pi*i/N)` for even N. `T^N=U^N=-I`. Cyclic bidiagonal Green solves `(I-alpha*T)^-1` and `(I-beta*U)^-1` have O(N) application cost. Unit-circle scalar alpha gives fully dense **flat-magnitude** Green matrices, but their phase curvature is too simple, and conditioning grows with N.
2. Exact Fourier formula: `Fhat=(1/sqrt(N))*Q*sum_m c_m*T^m`, with `Q[j,j]=omega^(j*j)` and `c_m=omega^(-m*(m-1)/2)`. Direct polynomial execution is O(N^2), ordinary FFT is O(N log N).
3. If `Fhat=L*(sum_{ell=1}^q a_ell (I-alpha_ell*T)^-1)*R` with arbitrary invertible **outer diagonals**, then `q>=N/2`. Proof: phase cross-ratios force chirp coefficients; their `N/2 x N/2` Hankel Vandermonde is nonsingular, while q resolvent coefficients produce Hankel rank <=q.
4. Explicit minimal-pole Prony construction: A_h(z)=sum_j (c_h/c_j)*[z^j]prod_(k=0)^(h-1)(z-omega^-k)*z^j has degree h=N/2. Modular gcd(A_h,A_h')=gcd(A_h,z^N+1)=1 at each N=4,...,8192 proves distinct complex admissible roots and thus **exact N/2-term Green representations at those finite sizes**. This is an algebraic existence result, not an efficient/stable executor or uniform all-N proof.
5. Stronger same-order theorem: any product of arbitrary sums of weighted cyclic forward Green inverses **sharing one coordinate order**, with arbitrary nonunitary complex weights, near-resonance, and arbitrary internal diagonal masks, has half-block rank at most the total number Q of Green terms. Fourier's corresponding Vandermonde block has rank N/2, so Q>=N/2. If Q<N/4, spectral error >= sqrt((N/4-Q)/(N/2-Q)). Products and sums can be noncommuting, but NO intermediate *permutations*.
6. A separate O(N)-work Green generator has full commutator rank: `[T,(I-beta*U)^-1] = beta G[T,U]G`, and `[T,U]=(omega^-2-1) Z^2` invertible for N>2. Full commutator rank is *not* Fourier phase coherence. This second generator shares the same cyclic order, so theorem 4 still restricts its small-stage circuits.
