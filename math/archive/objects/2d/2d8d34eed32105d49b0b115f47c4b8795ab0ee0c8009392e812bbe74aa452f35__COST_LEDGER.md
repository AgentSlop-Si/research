# Phase20 charged arithmetic ledger

Every count below is a per-input **prime-field arithmetic proxy** including shared Green application and solve. The setup of a primitive field/root and precomputed root powers is common to all three algorithms in each comparison. Modular inversion is priced as one abstract field operation, **not** as a constant bit-time operation. This is not wall-clock benchmarking.

## Fixed-cost algebraic discoveries

- 8 direct Fourier probes: `O(8N)` modular field products and sums, no hidden full FFT.
- Construct 46 Sylvester resultants of constant-size (6x6) matrices, interpolate a polynomial of degree <=45, and check three independent extra points: fixed finite arithmetic independent of N (large constant, fully counted in the code).
- Exactly remove individually certified factors `w` and `w-1` (these are excluded as actual shift roots).
- Evaluate a degree-<=45 polynomial on every Nth root with an input-sparse Fourier evaluator; in the power-of-two branch it uses a *pruned* radix-two circuit, and on other lengths it uses only fixed-short polynomial convolutions. The resulting work is `O(N)` at this fixed algebraic degree, not an N-point full DFT hidden inside the evaluator.
- At most 45 root candidates, with constant-degree gcds, constant-time extra Hankel tests, and `O(N)` full support certificates each.
- For accepted 2-defect inputs: `O(N)` geometric forcing, denominator evaluation, batched division, O(N) Green solve, and at most one O(N) direct singular-frequency repair for coprime shifts.
- For any other input: fully charged all-length Bluestein `O(N log N)` forcing and O(N) Green solve.
