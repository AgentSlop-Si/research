## Independent proof audit

For N=s² and coordinates (x,y), X preserves y and Y preserves x. In the product, the intermediate coordinate is forced to (x,y′). Therefore

T[(x,y),(x′,y′)] = Y_x[y,y′] X_y′[x,x′].

Fixing x,y′ gives an outer product in variables y,x′, with no invertibility assumptions. Summing r paths gives rank at most r. Fourier’s corresponding block is a unit diagonal times the s-point Fourier matrix divided by s, times a scalar unit phase. All its singular values are 1/sqrt(s). The s² transverse blocks partition the entries, so Eckart–Young gives squared Frobenius error at least N(1−r/s) and operator error at least sqrt(1−r/s), for r<s. This is also the expected squared output error against an isotropic input, when T is input-independent. It does not address a nonlinear adaptive decoder.

The source’s s-path decomposition is exactly correct: each path selects input first-coordinate a, and the surviving exponent is s*x*y′ + s*y*a + y*y′. Summing a recovers the full Fourier exponent. These factors can be singular and are arbitrary fiber-linear maps. The sharp path count alone is not a runtime lower bound where computations are shared.

### Expansion countercontrol

The four directed Margulis maps and their inverses are precisely those of Construction 8.1 in [Hoory–Linial–Wigderson, Theorem 8.2](https://www.math.ias.edu/~avi/BOOKS/expanderbookr1.pdf), whose nontrivial adjacency bound is 5sqrt(2). Loops and multiplicities are retained. Five copies of the four directed maps have symmetric cut Laplacian L_s=5(4I−A_s/2), gap at least gamma=5(4−5sqrt(2)/2)>2.32233. Lifting one complete family on each axis yields L_N=L_s tensor I + I tensor L_s. Its gap is at least gamma. For every nonempty proper R,

b(R)>=gamma |R|(N−|R|)/N > min(|R|,N−|R|),

because gamma>2. Every possible same-subset rank budget is therefore met, in both directions. This is an all-subset, infinite-family statement over N=t⁴, not an empirical expansion assertion.

The 40 Green plans are arbitrary-permutation, potentially multi-cycle plans. Constant complex weight 1/4 makes every cycle denominator nonzero; cycle discovery and all solves take O(N) arithmetic. Independent weights are optional. This is not a construction in a four-Hamiltonian-plan grammar. All plans in the first stage preserve one axis, and all in the second preserve the other, so the rank-one obstruction survives every legal choice of weights. Error is at least sqrt(1−1/t²). The topology passes capacities while actual Fourier compatibility fails.

### Rook-support generic obstruction

A rook-supported matrix has d=N(2s−1) free entries. On invertible pairs the action (X,Y)->(DX,YD^{-1}) of nonzero diagonals is free, preserves both supports, and preserves YX. Every nonempty fiber has dimension at least N. Thus the image closure of invertible products has dimension <=2d−N and codimension at least s²(s−1)(s−3), positive for s>=4. The dimension bound is of course capped by N² when the displayed number exceeds N² at small s. Over F_q, each orbit has (q−1)^N points, yielding the claimed uniform-random-target counting bound (which may be >1 and then vacuous). None of this establishes that Fourier is outside the product image.
