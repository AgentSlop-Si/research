# Stronger local covariance theorem: Gaussian preweights cancel in Green blocks

**Checkpoint: 10 October 2026.** This is an analytically stronger continuation of the simple per-bin Minkowski bound in `RESEARCH.md` Section 4. It is **not** an exact FFT theorem. Tests and independent direct-projector checks: `verify_local_cancel.py`, `verified_local_cancel.json`.

## Theorem (bounded local Gaussian similarity transform)

Let `Q=KM>=4N`, `K>=M`, choose center `c=floor((N-1)/2)`, and let `S` be the `N` centered time indices `i=(n-c) mod Q`. Write `t_i` as the unique representative of `i/Q mod 1` in `[-1/2,1/2)`. On `S`, `|t_i|<=tau<=1/8`. Let `P=P_{a,beta}` be the random composite Green projector from Phase 2, with `a` uniform in the units of `Z/QZ`, `beta` uniform in `Z/QZ`, and the two independent.

Choose any `sigma>0`, and let `g_i=e^{pi sigma² t_i²}/sigma` on *all Q positions*. The algorithm's input is `z_i=g_i x_i` on `S`, zero elsewhere. Its centered dewindowed ideal-Gaussian row, evaluated at any real frequency `xi`, is

\[
A_{\xi}(z,P)=\frac1{\sqrt Q}\sum_{i=0}^{Q-1}
  g_i^{-1}e^{-2\pi i\xi t_i}(Pz)_i.
\]

The target row is the same expression with `P=I`, namely `(1/sqrt Q) sum_{i in S}e^{-2pi i xi t_i}x_i`.

For the Green error, define `Delta= A_xi(z,P)-A_xi(z,I)`. Set

\[
R=\exp\!\left(\pi\sigma^2\left(2\tau\frac M Q+\frac{M^2}{Q^2}\right)\right).
\]

Then for every (even adversarial) `x`, every real `xi`, and every composite or prime *original length* `N`,

\[
\mathbb E[\Delta]=0,
\quad\boxed{\mathbb E|\Delta|^2\le
 R^2\frac{M(M-1)}{\varphi(Q)}\,\|x\|_2^2.}\tag{LC1}
\]

When the original normalized `N`-DFT scaling `sqrt(Q/N)` is applied, multiply the variance RHS by `Q/N`.

### Proof

In each block, use the projector entries from `S[b,t]`:

\[
(Pz)_{j+d}=\overline{S_{j+d}}\sum_{u\ \text{in same block}}S_u z_u.
\]

Subtracting `z_{j+d}` eliminates the diagonal. Re-index off-diagonal terms by `d=(output position)−(input position)`, `0<|d|<M`. For the coefficient multiplying input `x_j`, the Gaussian factors combine **before covariance**:

\[
g_j\,g_{j+d}^{-1}=\exp(\pi\sigma^2(t_j^2-t_{j+d}^2)).\tag{LC2}
\]

Because `j` belongs to the centered support and `j+d` stays in the **same** nonwrapping length-`M` block, `|t_{j+d}-t_j|=|d|/Q<=M/Q`; the block cannot cross the distant representative discontinuity at `Q/2`. Hence

\[
|g_j/g_{j+d}| \le
 \exp\!\left(\pi\sigma^2(2\tau M/Q+(M/Q)^2)\right)=R.\tag{LC3}
\]

The phase factor `exp(-2pi i xi t_{j+d})` has modulus one. All remaining chirp phases are the same as in the previous composite covariance proof. Averaging uniform `beta` kills cross-displacements `d!=d'`; this is valid because `|d-d'|<=2M-2<Q` for `K>=M`. For each fixed `d`, averaging `a` among units gives Fourier character frequencies `a*d mod Q`, with exactly `phi(Q/gcd(Q,d))` different frequencies, each equally weighted. The characters are orthogonal in `C^Q`.

The fixed-`d` covariance is a rank-character Gram matrix conjugated by a diagonal whose operator norm is at most `R`; its normalized spectral norm is therefore at most

\[
\frac{R^2}{\varphi(Q/\gcd(Q,d))}.
\]

All these fixed-`d` covariances are positive semidefinite. Summing and using `phi(Q/g)>=phi(Q)/g` and `gcd(Q,d)<=|d|` yields

\[
\|\mathrm{Cov}\|_\mathrm{op}
\le\frac{R^2}{\varphi(Q)}\sum_{0<|d|<M}|d|
=R^2\frac{M(M-1)}{\varphi(Q)}.
\]

Applying the matrix to `x` gives (LC1). In particular, for `M=Theta(log N)`, `sigma²=Theta(log N)`, and `Q=Theta(N)`, we have `R=exp(O(log²N/N))=1+o(1)`.

**What improved:** The earlier triangle/Minkowski bound estimated the large preconditioned norm `||z||2` and paid the factor `exp(pi sigma² tau²)=N^{Omega(1)}`. The local block calculation cancels that factor through `g_j/g_{j+d}`. This does not claim that the covariance is as small as the true signal's DFT variance; its upper bound still contains `M(M-1)/phi(Q)`.

## Truncated Gaussian filter creates only an exponentially small extra error

The executable algorithm uses a finite Gaussian frequency filter and a quantized phase, not the ideal row `A_xi` above. For a fixed rounded frequency `xi'`, let

\[
\mathcal W_{\xi'}(t)=\sum_{\ell:\,|\ell-\xi'|\lesssim L}
 e^{-\pi(\ell-\xi')^2/\sigma^2}e^{-2\pi i\ell t}.
\]

The actual filter is the `2L+1` integer indices surrounding the integer part of `xi'` (the possible asymmetry only makes the following bound more conservative).

If `S'` denotes the union of the `M`-blocks touching `S`, then `Pz` vanishes outside `S'`; for `i in S'`, `|t_i|<=tau'=tau+M/Q<1/2`. Gaussian Poisson summation and the omitted-frequency tail bound give, uniformly on `S'`,

\[
\left|\mathcal W_{\xi'}(t_i) -g_i^{-1}e^{-2\pi i\xi't_i}\right|
\le \sigma A(\sigma,\tau') + T(L,\sigma),\tag{LC4}
\]

where

\[
A(\sigma,\tau')=\frac{2e^{-\pi\sigma^2(1-2\tau')}}{1-e^{-\pi\sigma^2(1-2\tau')}},\qquad
T(L,\sigma)=\frac{2e^{-\pi L^2/\sigma^2}}{1-e^{-2\pi L/\sigma^2}}.
\]

Each block of `P` has operator norm `M`, hence `||Pz||2<=M||z||2<=M e^{pi sigma² tau²}||x||2/sigma`. Applying Cauchy–Schwarz to the at-most-`Q` supported entries and including the `sqrt(Q/N)` output normalization gives a (very conservative) deterministic bound

\[
\boxed{
D_{\mathrm{Gauss\ remainder}}
 \le \sqrt{Q/N}\,M e^{\pi\sigma^2\tau^2}
 \left(A(\sigma,\tau')+T(L,\sigma)/\sigma\right).}\tag{LC5}
\]

The phase-bucket approximation adds at most `pi tau/H`. Thus the actual output row differs from the exact rounded-frequency target by an unbiased random error of variance at most

\[
\boxed{B_{\rm local}^2=(Q/N)R^2 M(M-1)/\varphi(Q)}\tag{LC6}
\]

plus a **deterministic** perturbation of magnitude at most

\[
\boxed{D_{\rm local}=\pi\tau/H+D_{\mathrm{Gauss\ remainder}}}\tag{LC7}
\]

in units of `||x||2`. The perturbation may depend on the random state, but its bound is deterministic **for every state**, so the median argument still works: if a coordinate of the median is bad beyond `D_local+u`, a majority of the unbiased main errors must deviate by more than `u`.

## Sharper all-length asymptotic theorem: constant 4x oversampling for every alpha<1/2

Fix *any* `alpha in (0,1/2)` and choose, for all sufficiently large N,

\[
C=4,\quad Q=M\lceil 4N/M\rceil,\ M=\lceil\log_2(4N)\rceil,
\quad L=\max(12,\lceil\kappa\ln N\rceil),\ 
\sigma^2=L,\quad H=\lceil N^{\alpha}\rceil,
\quad\kappa=\frac{2\alpha}{\pi}.
\]

As `tau<=1/8` and `tau'=tau+o(1)`, the Gaussian alias remainder exponent from (LC5) is at least

\[
\pi\kappa\left(1-2/8-1/64\right)
=\frac{47}{32}\alpha>\alpha.
\]

The Gaussian weight truncation exponent is at least

\[
\pi\kappa(1-1/64)=\frac{63}{32}\alpha>\alpha.
\]

The phase-bucket error is `O(N^-alpha)`. The main Green variance satisfies `B_local²=N^(-1+o(1))`. Hence Chebyshev at threshold `u=N^-alpha` gives

\[
p_N \le N^{-(1-2\alpha)+o(1)}.
\]

Choose any fixed odd `s=2h-1` with `h>1/(1-2alpha)`. Taking component-wise medians of `s` independent rows gives

\[
\boxed{\Pr\left[\|\widehat X-U_Nx\|_\infty
  >C_\alpha N^{-\alpha}\|x\|_2\right]
 \le N^{1-h(1-2\alpha)+o(1)}.}\tag{LC8}
\]

The work remains `O_alpha(N loglog N)` and the memory `O_alpha(N)`: `C=4`, `s` is fixed by alpha, and quantization uses `H=N^alpha` buckets with total `o(N)` setup and segment overhead.

**Explicit alpha=1/3:** use the already implemented `L=max(12,ceil((ln N)/4))`, which is slightly larger than `kappa=2/(3pi)`. With `s=9`, `h=5`, the sharper asymptotic union-bound failure is

\[
N^{1-5/3+o(1)}=N^{-2/3+o(1)}.
\]

The previously certified conservative bound `N^-0.5439+o(1)` remains valid and does not rely on this sharper local cancellation lemma. The new theorem candidate strengthens it. **Neither argument provides exact FFT or binary64 correctness.**

## Verification and unresolved gate

`verify_local_cancel.py` independently materializes the projected signals in the time domain and compares their locally dewindowed main error against (LC6), for 450 randomized states at each of four prime input lengths `127`, `257`, `509`, `1009`. The empirical mean-square noise was 2–5% of the conservative (LC6) bound in these tests; the actual finite Gaussian remainder was below `5.4e-14 ||x||2`, against a certified remainder bound around `10^-10 ||x||2`.

This is an analytic proof attempt checked against independent numerical oracles, **not** a formal proof assistant certification or outside mathematical review. Next: express the local covariance as an explicit PSD matrix and prove or refute a more aggressive `O(M/Q)` (rather than `O(M²/phi(Q))`) operator-norm bound; this could reduce the required number of repetitions in practice.
