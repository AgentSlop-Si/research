## Algebraic gate and permutations

Because A and C are invertible, equality is equivalent to T=A^−1 F C^−1=diag(u)P1(G2+lambda G3)P2diag(v). The first two rows and first three columns of T pull back to any ordered distinct row pair and ordered distinct column triple in the middle pencil. There are exactly N²(N−1)²(N−2) such witnesses, covering every pair of internal permutations without enumerating complete permutations.

For j=1,2, the source equations are precisely the cross-multiplied identity

T[1,j]T[0,0]B[r1,c0]B[r0,cj]−T[1,0]T[0,j]B[r1,cj]B[r0,c0]=0.

The mask factors cancel. This uses no division by candidate entries, so accidental zeros do not invalidate necessity. Each polynomial has degree at most two. For ascending coefficients a,b, its displayed resultant

(a2 b0−a0 b2)²−(a2 b1−a1 b2)(a1 b0−a0 b1)

is exactly the padded degree-two Sylvester determinant. A nonzero value is sufficient to exclude a common finite root in any algebraic extension. If both degrees drop, this padded determinant can vanish even when the actual lower-degree polynomials are coprime; the source uses only the safe nonzero direction. Three hundred independently generated degree-two and degenerate controls agree with direct 4-by-4 determinant elimination.

Allowing zero diagonal entries would not create an omitted exact Fourier solution: a zero on either diagonal makes the circuit singular. The statement remains representation-specific; new outer endpoint permutations or freely varying Green generator couplings are not included in the audited architecture.

## Characteristic-zero validity

The couplings lie in Q(i), and the Fourier root in Q(zeta_N), with i=zeta_N^(N/4). Both reductions use a consistent primitive root of order N and its induced fourth root. Every used denominator and determinant has nonzero residue. A zero algebraic resultant would map to zero under any valid reduction. Thus one nonzero modular image per mapping proves the characteristic-zero exclusion; no bound on a numerator's size or numerical root test is needed.

Independently of modular lifting, the reviewer wrote rational-pair arithmetic for Q(i), inverted both N4 outer matrices exactly, constructed the two degree-two polynomials, and obtained a nonzero exact resultant. Its reductions match both independently rebuilt modular pairs. This establishes the N4 gcd degree zero directly, with no SymPy or uploaded implementation.

## Mask recovery, Weyl checks and costs

When all target and candidate anchor entries are nonzero, v_j=T_0j/B_0j and u_i=T_i0/(B_i0 v_0) give u_0=1 and match the first row/column. The masks are unique up to a common reciprocal scalar rescaling. A complete factorization requires every remaining entry. For lambda=7 and identity internal permutations, all fourteen field/size instances match exactly 2N−1 anchors and mismatch every (N−1)² interior entry. Fresh reconstruction of the full circuit also shows that each of the two Fourier Weyl equations separately fails in these cases.

Eighty-four fresh cyclic scan controls check 6,096 recurrence coordinates. A supplied six-Green circuit has six O(N) solves, three branch additions, two internal mask applications, two paid internal permutations and scalar/endpoint work, still O(N) online for this fixed stage count. Computing T by dense inversions costs O(N³); exhaustive certification costs O(N⁵). Mask calibration is linear only after target anchors have been supplied. None of those research costs are hidden in a claimed fast executor, because the family is excluded.
