## Verified milestones

1. For entrywise-nonzero M, `R(M)=M (M^(circ -1))^T`; under row/column monomial operations R changes only by diagonal-permutation similarity. Thus traces, characteristic polynomial and row-pair moments are exact phase/permutation invariants.
2. `Q(M)=tr(R(M)^2)` is the first cheap scalar gate. Exact code can recover hidden masks/permutations when row/column fingerprints are unique; all matches are verified against every entry. Degenerate fingerprints return inconclusive.
3. **All-N generic theorem:** for ANY fixed Hamiltonian cycle orderings, almost all continuously chosen three-Green-sum mixer parameters do not satisfy a two-mask Fourier factorization. Strictly positive weak coupling shows the necessary rational identity is not identically true. Does not exclude special tuned parameters.
4. **One-variable theorem:** with A,C,B0,B1 fixed, B(lambda)=B0+lambda B1, the first necessary phase condition produces a polynomial of degree at most N^2. Hence no more than N^2 admissible complex lambda, unless the polynomial is zero.
5. Stronger fixed-instance result: **N=4,8,16 seeded algebraic non-affine Green triples admit NO complex lambda**, even with arbitrary middle nonzero diagonal masks and coordinate permutations. Two polynomial invariants, reduced denominator factors (at N=16), leading-degree preservation and coprime finite-field Bezout certificates prove no common admissible complex root.
6. Exact modular fixed-coupling exclusions N=4,8,16,32,64,128. Valid characteristic-zero lifting applies to the same algebraic rational/cyclotomic parameters with nonzero modular denominators. Does not extend to arbitrary N for those seeds without tests.
7. An O(N)-work two-resolvent executor matches dense results through N=128. No O(N) Fourier executor results.
