# Prior-art audit — v17

## Chebotarev Fourier-minor theorem

The proof uses the classical theorem that every minor of a prime-order complex Fourier matrix is nonzero.

Primary reference used for framing:

- P. E. Frenkel, “Simple proof of Chebotarev's theorem on roots of unity,” arXiv:math/0312398 (2003).

A recent finite-field generalization exists, but the finite-field computations in this package are used only as finite-instance regression certificates. The all-prime theorem is stated over the complex numbers.

## Structured matrices

Cyclic Green resolvents, semiseparable/quasiseparable matrices, Sylvester rank inequalities, and prefix/suffix scan application are established structured-linear-algebra tools. This package does not claim those ingredients.
