# Phase 39 — mathematical context and scope of originality

The project combines several well-established ideas; no priority claim is made without independent peer review.

- The variety of rank-one `m x n` matrices is the Segre variety, with codimension `(m-1)(n-1)`. Consequently the potential dimension threshold for a projective `q-1`-parameter linear pencil is controlled by the size of the matrix window. See standard accounts of determinantal/Segre varieties, such as https://www.math.uni-konstanz.de/~plaumann/CAG15/cagslides04.pdf .
- Four-entry cross-ratios and diagonal-equivalence invariants have established use in complex Hadamard and type-II matrix studies. The current work uses them to cancel two independent internal Green masks algebraically.
- Macaulay-style homogeneous coefficient rank tests are a classical way to certify that a polynomial ideal contains all homogeneous polynomials of a given degree. Our specific overlapping three-/four-quadric small matrices and exact Green-project certificate are project-specific deductions and computational certificates.
- Fourier lower bounds depend strongly on the computation model: see Morgenstern's bounded-coefficient result (JACM 1973, https://doi.org/10.1145/321752.321761) and Ailon's 2x2-unitary/entropy result (2013, https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.4745) and well-conditioned model (2014, https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.1307). The current **fixed-family** exclusion is not a universal lower bound in any of those models.

**Phase 39 contribution:** identified the unavoidable projective degeneracy of anchored bivariate mask tests; developed two small unanchored exact finite-field/characteristic-zero projective certificates; exhaustively excluded both freely varying middle couplings plus two arbitrary masks/permutations at `N=4,8,16` for one explicitly specified seven-Green network. A working `O(N)` candidate executor exists but computes **not** the DFT. No universal complexity improvement has been shown.
