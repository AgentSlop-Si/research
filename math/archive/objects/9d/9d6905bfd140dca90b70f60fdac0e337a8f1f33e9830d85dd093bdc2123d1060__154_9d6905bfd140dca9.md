# Next research gate — don't build another disguise

We want a full exact DFT for a fresh arbitrary complex input in a well-specified sequential arithmetic model. The proof in `RESEARCH.md` means a proposed `O(N)` division-free *nonlinear* arithmetic algorithm would automatically produce an `O(N)` *linear* circuit (and rational ones regular at a basepoint likewise give a small linear circuit). The next candidate must improve **linear circuit evaluation**, not just use a nonlinear `k(x)` or conceal an FFT inside a field division or symbolic macro.

Before coding any candidate, demand:

1. A uniform all-N transform identity and actual elementary gate list/recursive construction.
2. Exact unit-cost model and whether unlimited coefficient precision, conditional branching, multiplication/division, lookup and random access are allowed.
3. Fully charged coefficient/root generation, routing, memory access and N explicit outputs.
4. Recurrence for all arithmetic operations, not just multiplication count or size of symbolic DAG.
5. Evidence of a strict growing savings over known baselines, including OpenAI #130 **in the same model**, and a prior-art check against the canonical checkpoint.
6. A proof or exact-machine certificate independent from numerical testing.
7. An explicit account of where unbounded coefficients/conditioning enter if claiming to evade restricted linear-circuit lower bounds.

**Current verdict:** No new arbitrary-input O(N) exact transform evaluator discovered. Keep binary-only/fixed-field models separate because polynomial identities may hold on finite domains without extending to general complex inputs.
