A SMALLER FINITE WITNESS FOR THE EXACT FOURIER CONSTRUCTION
Research certificate and scope note, 8 October 2026

RESULT

There is a concrete sharpening of the finite witness in the inspected OpenAI
construction. Use all triples of 24 coordinates, and remove the redundant
extra total-sum center from each scalar invocation. The following checks
passed in exact arithmetic:

  h = 24; v = C(24,3) = 2,024; m = h^3 = 13,824
  centers per invocation rho = 24
  ordered-neighbor degree d = 1,393
  invocations I = 3 v^2 = 12,289,728
  physical roles W = 34,666,930,287,616
  padded roles W* = 2^45 = 35,184,372,088,832
  strict directional saving Delta = 2,425,172,992
  unpadded residual sum s = 479,235,641,870,830,592
  padded directional sum S = 486,388,757,330,840,576

The rebuilt tensor recurrence has critical exponent
  theta = log_m(S/W*)
  1 - theta = 5.2296989632709109... x 10^-10.

A rational certificate, not this floating-point estimate, proves
  theta < 1 - 5.2 x 10^-10.
Keeping the paper's original all-length compiler then yields the eventual
bound O(n (log n)^(1 - 5 x 10^-10)). This is a mathematical transfer of the
paper's interfaces with changed fixed parameters. It has NOT been checked
by a Lean proof kernel. No complete transform implementation was run.

This is a small parameter/certificate sharpening, not a new FFT mechanism
or a practical speedup. Novelty beyond the inspected source snapshot has
not been established. Even 2^13,824-entry objects in the construction are
infeasible. Exact arithmetic is unrestricted; no finite-precision result
is claimed.

2. THE ONLY SCALAR-NETWORK CHANGE

Let T be the 2,024 triples of {1,...,24}. S and T are neighbors when their
intersection size is even (0 or 2). Keep the side wires and eight rows of
the paper. Replace its h+1 center wires c_1,...,c_h,c_* by only c_1,...,c_h.
Define

  (Gx)_j = sum over T containing j of x_T,
  (Rc)_S = (1/2) sum over j in S of c_j - (1/6) sum over all j of c_j.

This gives
  (RG)_(S,T) = |S intersect T|/2 - 3/6
             = (|S intersect T|-1)/2.
This is exactly the old RG matrix. Keep V and J from the paper: V copies
x_T to side wire (S,T), and J cancels the above coefficient on every
ordered neighboring pair. Consequently RG+JV=I. The checker exhausts all
4,096,576 ordered triple pairs and verifies this identity with integers,
scaled by six. It also checks each triple's neighbor degree.

There is no assumption that the initial center values satisfy a linear
relation. The eight rows are, chronologically,

  y -= J A; y -= R c; A += V x; c += G x;
  y += R c; y += J A; c -= G x; A -= V x.

For arbitrary initial x,y,A,c they leave x,A,c unchanged and add
(RG+JV)x to y. The checker proves this algebraically as a formal
noncommutative polynomial identity, keeping R,G,J,V symbolic. The three
paper stages, forward X->Y, inverse Y->X, forward X->Y, therefore send
(X,Y) to (-Y,X), restoring every side and center value.

3. WHY THE FRAME-LABEL CERTIFICATE STILL APPLIES

Keep the labels and edge schedule in network.tex verbatim, with h=24,
D=F_2^24 and the h center wires just specified. The labels depend on the
physical wire groups, not on whether a coefficient in R was originally
1/2 or -1/2. R and G touch the same two groups (one bank and the centers).
Changing their coefficients therefore preserves the frame-commutation
argument. Removing c_* deletes its entire physical route and its labels.

Triple indicators have binary norm one. Neighboring indicators are
orthogonal. The paper's inclusions, terminal labels, and exceptional
terminal correction only use these facts and the odd weight 3^3=27.
Every nonzero residual in its displayed list remains nonalternating:

- t-perp contains a coordinate unit outside a support of size 3.
- span(t_S,t_T)-perp contains one outside supports of total size at most 6.
- The nonzero B=P-perp and Q-perp contain units outside tensor supports
  of size 3^j in ambient dimension 24^j.
- Tensor products and orthogonal sums retain a norm-one vector whenever
  the selected factor/summand is nonzero.

Here h>6, so these outside coordinates exist. The paper's constructive
orthonormalization lemma applies. The only decreases are still the
center edges from row 3 to row 4, each of dimension h. There are I*rho
such edges. Source dimensions sum to v^3; sink dimensions sum to Wm-v^3.
Thus

  W = 2v^3 + 3v^2(vd+rho),
  s = Wm - 2v^3 + 2(3v^2)rho*h,
  Delta = Wm-s = 2v^2(v-3h*rho).

rho=h and 2024>3*24^2=1728 give strict positivity. The exact counts and
both residual formulas are checked by the script. This paragraph is a
proof reduction to the parameterized displayed label construction, not
an enumeration of the approximately 35 trillion physical routes.

Every role, including arbitrary-valued auxiliaries, undergoes the tensor
transform after telescoping and terminal corrections. This full-state
identity is essential for recursive batching and has not been replaced
by a zero-workspace assumption.

4. COMPLETE CHARGED RECURRENCE

Pad to W*=2^45 by ordinary roles. They add (W*-W)m directions, so
  S = s+(W*-W)m = W*m-Delta.
For k above the fixed base threshold K=m*(45+1)=635,904, set
f=floor(k/m). Each of S directional operations acts on 2^(k-f) independent
length-2^f fibers. Since k-f>=45, that count divides into complete batches
of W* fibers, with no partially filled batch. Every batch may use all its
roles because the interface holds on arbitrary role values.

Let T(k) count exact scalar operations, integer/index work, array
initialization, copying, permutations, scalar preparation, recursion
setup, and fixed network-table construction. With t(k)=T(k)/(W*2^k),
the same indexing lemma as the paper yields

  T(k) <= A W*2^k + S [2^(k-f)/W*] T(f),
  t(k) <= A + (S/W*) t(f).

A and the bounded base costs are fixed constants. They are not assumed
small and not omitted from total work. Existing prefix-tree address
enumeration avoids an extra factor k or f in array movement. The paper's
fixed-table and O(log n)-bit integer model is unchanged. Unrolling gives
T(k)=O(W*2^k (k+1)^theta), theta=log_m(S/W*).

The denser modified R adds fixed work. A straightforward implementation
with one or two charged scalar operations per shear has, per invocation,

  6vd + (4h+6)v = 17,123,040 scalar operations.

Across all I invocations this is 210,437,504,133,120 scalar operations per
address before frame transformations. There are 11,387,024 elementary
shears per invocation. If desired, replacing all such shears by the
paper's three-C word uses 419,830,283,068,416 C calls per address plus
monomial factors. These explicit finite costs are included in A; they
are not recursive directional costs and cannot be subtracted from s.
The modified constants require only rational numbers with denominators
2,3,6 and the original fixed kernel's i. No input-dependent division or
zero test is introduced. The supplied-root model, charged local scalar
preparation, and logarithmic-word integer model all remain unchanged.

5. EXACT EXPONENT CERTIFICATE

Let epsilon=Delta/(mW*) and gamma=13/25,000,000,000=5.2e-10.
The checker establishes entirely with Fraction/integer arithmetic:

  sum(j=0,...,40) (191/20)^j/j! > 13,824,
  epsilon - gamma*(191/20)
    = 2,328,493,845,853 / 115,964,116,992,000,000,000,000 > 0.

The positive Taylor lower sum proves log(m)<191/20. Since
exp(-z)>=1-z,

  1-epsilon < 1-gamma*log(m) <= exp(-gamma*log(m)).

Multiplying by m proves S/W* < m^(1-gamma), hence theta<1-gamma.
The paper transfers the tensor bound to all n with total charged cost
O(n (log n)^theta (log log n)^(4-theta)) plus lower-order preparation.
The exponent 4-theta can safely be bounded by the fixed constant 4.
Because gamma - 5e-10 = 2e-11 is positive, that loglog factor is eventually
absorbed by (log n)^(2e-11). Thus

  T(n) = O(n (log n)^(1-5e-10)).

This asymptotic absorption says nothing useful about a practical
crossover. The allowed n before asymptotic dominance may be enormous.

6. BOUNDED SEARCH AND STOPPING CONDITION

As a negative control, the checker scans only the following finite family:
q in {3,5,7,9}, integer 2q<h<=128. For q odd put t=(q-1)/2,
v=C(h,q), rho=C(h,t), and d=sum over even s of C(q,s)C(h-q,q-s).
The overlap polynomial
  p(z)=product over odd j<q of (z-j)/(q-j)
has p(q)=1 and vanishes at the other odd intersection sizes. Expanding
p(z)=sum c_j C(z,j) factors it through rho inclusion features because
  sum_{A subset T, |A|=t} C(|S intersect A|,j)
    = C(|S intersect T|,j) C(q-j,t-j).
Hence these are natural candidates for the same dimensional accounting.
No wider family or arbitrary tensor-network search is claimed.

Of 464 parameter pairs, 435 have positive dimensional saving. The best
candidate in this scan is the q=3,h=24 compressed-center witness above.
The scan rankings use 70-digit Decimal arithmetic only to compare
candidates; the winning certificate itself is checked by exact integers
and rational inequalities. An independent audit obtained the same winner.
There is no reason to keep repeating this exhausted finite search.
