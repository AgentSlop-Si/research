# Prior art and priority scope

- Haagerup-type fourth-order invariants of complex Hadamard matrices (product of two entries times conjugates of two others) are classical. Our ratios are their natural nonzero-entry extension allowing arbitrary invertible diagonal scalings, and our exponent-curvature rank uses this **existing** invariant family. See Östergård and Valtonen (2026), *Equivalence of complex Hadamard matrices*, Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10801-026-01568-x
- Gauge phase and cycle holonomy are established graph-mathematics notions. A nonreal complex phase or full entrywise phase rank by itself is not proof of Fourier behavior.
- General linear-circuit lower bounds depend on computational models; Nir Ailon's well-conditioned and unitary FFT circuit lower bounds do not directly cover every Green shared-state arithmetic circuit: https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.1307 and https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.4745
- The project's Phase 36 single-Green cross-ratio set `{1,alpha^N,alpha^-N}` was already known within the project. The Phase 37 anchored exponent-curvature determinant and the particular two-plaquette Bézout certificate are **project-specific deductions**, not independently reviewed mathematical priority claims.
