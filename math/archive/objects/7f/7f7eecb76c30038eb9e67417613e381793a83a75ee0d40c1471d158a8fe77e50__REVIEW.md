# Independent review: changed full-bank interface and admissible N=16 control

Date: 2026-10-10 UTC. Verdict: the interface-change formulas, cheap-swap gauges, monomial-clock rigidity, relocated-work identity, and explicit sixteen-point zero-mask construction are valid. The construction is finite-size and full-bank; it does not give a scalable cheaper Fourier algorithm.

## 1. Changing Q changes the operator target

Starting from F=P0 B Q0 with a fixed invertible bank B, write a new Q as GQ0. The unique corresponding exterior is

P=P0 C, C=B G^(-1)B^(-1), C^(-1)=B G B^(-1).

Both orientations follow directly from equality of the products. If C and its inverse have uniform O(N) preparation and application, P and P0 are equivalent up to additive linear overhead. Cheap G alone does not imply those bank conjugates are cheap. Nor does cheap application imply preserved paired-Green membership.

The true interface actions change as T_g=G T_(0,g) G^(-1), A_g=C^(-1) A_(0,g) C. This is genuine changed interface data, not non-scalar centralizer freedom of an already fixed Weyl pair.

## 2. Monomial-clock coset rigidity

For monomial Q with Q e_(sigma(b,s))=lambda_(b,s)e_(bm+s), its conjugated clock is diagonal with entries omega^(sigma(b,s)); the nonzero lambda values cancel.

On an active block, F_m diag(omega^(sigma(b,s)))F_m^(-1) is circulant. If it is also monomial and invertible, it is c S_m^a. Conjugating back gives the diagonal c eta^(-as), where eta=omega^q. Evaluating at s=0 and using primitive-root injectivity yields

sigma(b,s)=e_b+q a_b s modulo N,

where gcd(a_b,m)=1 is forced by distinct input coordinates. Thus each active block uses a complete residue-class coset modulo q, in affine order. Distinct active blocks use distinct cosets. A full bank therefore has the stride layout up to bank permutations and within-bank affine permutations.

This requires an actually monomial active clock block, not merely O(N) total entries. It does not constrain arbitrary diagonal monomial weights, nonmonomial Q, inactive blocks, or interfaces that permit dense exceptional clock blocks. The independent tests include legal affine-coset layouts with arbitrary nonzero weights and a noncoset layout whose clock block is not monomial.

## 3. Constant swaps give cheap reversible bank gauges

For a transposition G of coordinates a,b, put v=e_a-e_b. Then G=I-vv^T, and

C=B G B^(-1)=I-u w^T,
u=Bv, w^T=v^T B^(-1).

Since w^T u=v^T v=2, C^2=I. Each vector is supported in at most two bank blocks. Fourier columns and inverse-Fourier rows give their entries explicitly from supplied root tables; generation and the nontrivial update use O(m) work per swap, with overall input/output handling and root preparation charged within O(N). No fresh-vector transform is needed to apply this rank-one gauge.

A constant number of such swaps remains a cheap reversible gauge, even when it breaks the coset geometry. It does not make the baseline exterior faster or prove the product stays in the required two-layer class.

Conjugating a monomial interface by a transposition changes only constantly many scalar entries. Bank conjugation sends each changed elementary matrix to an outer product within one bank rectangle, with at most m^2 entries. In a square split, constantly many such rectangles have O(N) total coefficients. Their possible density is compatible with cheap low-rank application, while their maximum row degree may grow.

## 4. Relocated full-bank Fourier identity

For N=M^2, let B=I_M tensor F_M, L=F_M tensor I_M, K transpose the grid, and Delta[rM+s]=omega^(rs). Direct expansion gives

F_N=K B Delta L.

Indeed, at output tM+r and input cM+d, the product entry is eta^(td) omega^(rd) eta^(rc)=omega^((tM+r)(cM+d)), with eta=omega^M. There is no missing normalization.

Thus P=K is monomial and Q=Delta L. The arbitrary-input Fourier batch has moved into Q: Q and L are equivalent up to the invertible twiddle diagonal. This changes the previous fixed P target, but does not eliminate the batch's application cost.

The true actions have the following positive-sign convention:

- A1=K D_N K has block omega^b D_M; T1=B^(-1)A1B has block omega^b S_M.
- A2=K S_N^(-1)K routes block b to b-1; its wrap edge from 0 to M-1 carries S_M^(-1).
- T2=B^(-1)A2B has the same routing, with D_M on that wrap edge and identity elsewhere.

All four are monomial and satisfy A_g B=B T_g. Independent checks also construct T_g directly from Q X_g Q^(-1), rather than relying only on the bank equations. There is no conflict with the earlier proper-bank obstruction: this bank is fully active.

## 5. Exact N=16 paired-Green realization

At M=4, let zeta=omega_16^4, let E_h=diag(1,1,-1,-1), E_l=diag(1,-1,1,-1), and let Pi_h,Pi_l toggle the high and low binary bits. With Delta4=diag(1,1,1,zeta) and K2 the two-by-two-grid transpose,

A4=(E_h+Pi_h)Delta4,
C4=(E_l+Pi_l)K2,
F4=A4 C4.

The sign, twiddle placement and input permutation are correct. Each factor is a sum of two nonsingular monomials. Therefore the source's four layers

H1=C4 tensor I4,
H2=Delta16(A4 tensor I4),
H3=(I16/2)+(I16/2),
H4=(K/2)+(K/2)

give H2H1=Q and H4H3=P, hence

F16=H4 H3 (I4 tensor F4) H2 H1.

Every one of the eight monomial summands is an allowed Green term with a designated Hamiltonian permutation, all edge weights zero, denominator equal to I, and nonsingular endpoints. The reviewer assigns eight explicit odd-step Hamiltonian orders at N=16 and checks the plans, guards, and endpoints individually. Characteristic not two makes the halves legal.

This is a genuine exact small-instance admissibility control, not just an unconstrained dense factorization. The central bank is full and its four F4 applications remain charged. The exterior uses familiar two-stage butterflies. No asymptotic improvement or uniform extension beyond this fixed size is claimed.

## 6. Narrow zero-mask limitation

A paired layer whose two masks are zero is a sum of two monomials, so its maximum row degree is at most two. Two such layers have degree at most four. Q=Delta(F_M tensor I_M) has exactly M nonzeros in every row, so this Q cannot have that particular two-zero-mask-layer representation when M>4. More generally t zero-mask paired layers require 2^t>=M.

The conclusion does not exclude nonzero Green layers, whose matrices may be dense despite linear-time scans, or other choices of exteriors/interfaces. It is not a general lower bound for the target Fourier transform.

## 7. Independent exact controls

Only independently authored standard-library code was executed. Five relocated-interface and swap-gauge cases at N=4,9,16,25,64 verify the exact Fourier product, both definitions of the true interface actions, every explicit block/carry formula, rank-one swap representation, involution, support bounds and changed-entry counts.

The N=16 test verifies the F4 butterfly identity, all eight explicit zero-mask Hamiltonian plans with nonsingular monomial endpoints, every denominator guard, and all 256 entries of the final Fourier product.

All checks and an isolated copied replay pass. No constructor or uploaded code was executed or imported, and prior seals are untouched.

## 8. Accepted research consequence

Interface freedom is real: Fourier work can move from P to Q while every true action remains monomial. Constant coordinate swaps can instead be cheap reversible changes that leave the old cost-equivalence intact. The explicit N16 factors demonstrate legal finite-size membership.

The missing scalable result remains a uniformly cheap, admissible realization of both exteriors. None is supplied here, and neither the monomial interfaces nor the fixed-size butterfly control establish one.
