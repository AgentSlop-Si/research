# Phase 29 rejected candidates and graph gates

**Gate 1: Gauge-union C4-free obstruction.** For a noncoprime `(b,h)` twiddle tensor whose root has order `bh`, arbitrary nonzero row and column scalings yield a graph of unit entries that is `C4`-free. Therefore there are at most `h+binom(b,2)` unit entries, which is at most `binom(b-1,2)` more than in the natural unscaled tensor. For `b=8`, free gauges can save at most 21 additional scalar multiplications, regardless of `h`. This rules out growing asymptotic savings from *row/column gauge normalization only*, not from more general nonlocal circuits. Exact small model: see `GAUGE_AUDIT.json`.

**Gate 2: Weighted-four diagonal width.** Uniformly in a formal phase parameter `a`, `F4 diag(1,a,a²,a³)` has six independent rational coefficient matrices when real and imaginary parts are separated. Any representation with fixed rational `A` and `B` and only diagonal coefficient dependence requires at least six channels; `weighted4_rank_gate.py` checks this symbolically. A rank-six witness exists, but tensoring it with Phase 28's 15-point Winograd diagonal expands too many addition nodes: 575 vs 558 operations for a nonzero phase block. This rejects **this particular** pre/diagonal/post absorption route, not parameter-dependent or non-diagonal alternatives.

**Gate 3: Correct baseline.** Phase 29 is only 0.465% cheaper than Phase 28 at `N=61,440` in the field-add/multiply model. Even when the exact identity is correct on every input, describing this as sub-`N log N`, fastest-known, a quantum advantage, or a precision improvement would be unsupported.
