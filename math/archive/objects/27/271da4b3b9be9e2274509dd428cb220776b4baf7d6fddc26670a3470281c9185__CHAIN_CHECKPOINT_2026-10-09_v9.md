## Goal

Fresh arbitrary dense input, all `N` approximate unitary-DFT outputs, sequential work below `N log N`, speedup originating from this project’s randomized chirp/block mechanism.

## New mechanism

Replace local phase `a*t^2` by

`a*binom(t,2)`.

Its first difference is `a*t`, so the exact decoder moves by `a`, not `2a`.

## Theorem regime

`N=p^L=K*M`, where `p` is prime, `K,M` are powers of `p`, and `K>=M`. Choose `a` uniformly from `Z_N^*` and `beta` uniformly from `Z_N`.

One row:

- is unbiased;
- costs `O(N+K log K)`;
- has one output cycle;
- uses one balanced scan and zero direct seeds after the parity twist;
- has exact covariance

  `Q^(m)=(M/N) Pi_m`,

  where `Pi_m` is an orthogonal projector of rank `N-K`.

Therefore

`sup_{||x||2=1} E|Xhat_m-X_m|^2=M/N`.

For dyadic lengths, this improves v8’s sharp constant `2M/N` by `2x`, changes two cycles to one, and relaxes `K>=2M` to `K>=M`.

## Parity-twisted zero-seed closure

Let `eta=exp(pi*i/N)` and `h=N mod 2`; multiply the local phase by `eta^(h*t)`. The covariance is unchanged, while the cycle gauge satisfies `A_N=-1` for both even and odd prime powers. Therefore the scan total gives `Xhat_0=sum(q_j)/2`, eliminating the direct seed for all theorem lengths.

## Streaming theorem

With `tau=g sqrt(M/N)||x||2`, three shifted grids and Boyer--Moore states give

`Pr[||Xtilde-U_Nx||inf > (5/sqrt(2)) g sqrt(M/N)||x||2]`

`<= 2N exp(-R D(1/2 || 1/g^2))`.

Time:

`O(R[N+(N/M)log(N/M)])`.

Memory:

`O(N)` independent of `R`.

## Finite precision

Under exact modular phase indices, `O(u)` root error, pairwise block summaries, a normwise stable `K`-FFT, and a balanced scan whose total supplies the seed:

`||fl(Xhat)-Xhat||inf <= C_fp u log N sqrt(MN)||x||2`.

It is sufficient to use

`b >= log2 N + log2 log N - log2 g + O(1)`

mantissa bits to keep numerical error within the statistical row radius. This is `Theta(log N)` precision, not a claim about fixed binary64 for unbounded `N`.

## Mixed-length corollary

For `N=A*B`, `A=p^L`, `gcd(A,B)=1`, perform the exact `B`-DFT stage first and apply the chirp algorithm along the `A` dimension. Work:

`O(N log B + R[N+(N/M)log(A/M)])`.

This is sub-`N log N` when the cofactor satisfies `log B=o(log N)`.
