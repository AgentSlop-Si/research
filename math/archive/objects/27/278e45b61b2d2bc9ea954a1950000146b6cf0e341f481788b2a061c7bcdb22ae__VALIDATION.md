### Limitations

The entire exhaustive set covers the specific deterministic seeded middle generators and outer factors defined in the source. It does **not** exclude arbitrary Green networks or prove `o(N log N)` / integer-multiplication speedups. Passing a finite-field verifier proves **nonfactorization over C** for that reduced cyclotomic-rational candidate when a modular rank minor is nonzero, not the behavior of arbitrary floating-point matrix programs. The N16 full all-pairs native rerun takes minutes and should not be mistaken for cheap online FFT computation.
