Claim boundary
The original exact arbitrary-complex all-output sub-N-log-N FFT goal remains open here. The approximate theorem assumes fixed error and polynomial failure parameters and bounded word-encoded inputs. Its error is input-mass-scaled and can be much larger than ordinary Fourier coefficients. The supplied prototype is not an implementation or benchmark of the proved O(N) evaluator. No first-result, practical FFT replacement, or industry-wide impact claim is established. Read approximation/PRIOR_ART.md for the targeted literature comparison; failure to locate an identical theorem is not a priority certificate.
