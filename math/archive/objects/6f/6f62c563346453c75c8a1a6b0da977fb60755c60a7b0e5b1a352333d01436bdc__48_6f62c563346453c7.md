# Phase 9 — measured results

All times are local wall-clock medians, not universal speedups. Five repetitions per measured case; circuit, range, jet, and organizer method order is randomized. Every requested complex or real output is compared outside the timed region. Output generation and retention are charged; input generation is excluded equally. Setup, registration, planning and index construction are included unless the row explicitly says otherwise.

Environment: python=3.12.14, numpy=2.3.5, scipy=1.17.0, platform=Linux-6.18.44-x86_64-with-glibc2.39, openblas_threads=1. SciPy default single-worker transforms; OMP_NUM_THREADS=1. Range measurements were rerun without concurrent test workloads.

Dense jet uses the uploaded Phase 8 single-frequency API; projected jet builds only requested coefficient projections. The analytic projected formula is the strongest specialized control.

At N=262,144, projected jet is 8.48× faster than fresh changed-factor FFTs and 3.12× faster than the original dense-jet frequency-query path. The hand-specialized projected formula remains faster. Kernel payloads exclude upstream seed arrays/spectra, still held during this benchmark; the returned projected jet itself retains no source-engine reference. At N=4,096 the projected compiler loses to fresh changed FFTs.

8 consumers, 32 bins each, r=256, 4,096 metadata updates. Precomputed basis projection is supplied; dense basis registration is excluded equally. Store creation, phase preparation, updates, output copies and online planning are timed. The original organizer is run atop the repaired shared store, isolating organizer changes. All returned values are checked, extending the uploaded scalar-checksum validation.

| Scenario | Sequential fixed ms | Original organizer ms | Repaired organizer ms | Repaired / no group cache ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Synchronized consumers and two clusters benefit. Asynchronous and regime-shift cases are near parity; these must not be advertised as robust wins. Zero budget bounds group payload but retains planner overhead and can be slower. The greedy partition is not a globally optimal budgeted view selector.

## 8. Scope of the external bridge

The exact modular schedule and CRT/carry demo work on their finite test instances. They use a quadratic direct DFT and make no performance claim. `INTEGER_MULT_BRIDGE.md` pins the linked research and separates algebraic compatibility from the missing uniformity, precision, rank-certificate, and finite-tape accounting requirements. No new kappa is inferred from these measurements.
