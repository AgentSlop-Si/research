## Mathematical and operational scope

Row/column-path Green sum on a square grid plus integer edge-boundary and induced-matching checks. The modulo-three path-edge coloring selects a full-path induced matching; edge graph expansion alone is not an executed proof of a transform lower bound.

## Control/dataflow and algebra

transpose_order is an involution. apply_grid correctly gathers and scatters by this same involution. grid_edges includes horizontal/vertical grid edges contained in the two Hamilton paths. grid_balanced_check compares crossing counts and verifies that matching endpoints induce exactly the chosen path edges.

## Input/guard and acceptance review

apply_grid asserts side>=2 and exact vector length. grid_balanced_check only checks even side and len(selected)==side^2/2; duplicates, out-of-grid labels and true set cardinality are not validated before converting to a set. Invalid inputs can be accepted if remaining inequalities happen to hold. induced_matching_path assumes supplied edges are ordered along one simple path.

## Cost accounting

O(N) per apply with two tridiagonal solves; generating paths, boundaries and matchings is O(N) work/storage. Underlying inversions and pivots have the same field restrictions as tri_solve.

## Test coverage and limitations

exact_audit exhausts 4x4 balanced subsets and samples larger valid subsets; these are strong finite graph checks but omit malformed selected lists and arbitrary-edge misuse. Numerical singular-value checks live in a separate audit.

## Dependency identity

In-batch tri_solve and MOD hashes bound.
