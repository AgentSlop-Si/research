# Prior art and attribution boundary

- Classical radix-2 decimation in frequency (Cooley–Tukey) supplies the base butterfly identities. Selective/pruned Fourier evaluation and caching of computation DAGs are established ideas. Phase 24 is their project-specific combination with the existing Phase 23 Green + sparse-rational certificate and a rigorously charged deferred scratch bank. No claim of inventing pruning or the FFT is made.
- Jacques Morgenstern (1973), *Note on a Lower Bound on the Linear Complexity of the Fast Fourier Transform*, JACM 20(2), 305–306: https://doi.org/10.1145/321752.321761. Bounded-coefficient lower bound for unnormalized Fourier computation.
- Nir Ailon (2014), *An n log n Lower Bound for Fourier Transform Computation in the Well Conditioned Model*: https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.1307. Conditional on uniformly well-conditioned intermediate transforms, with an `R`-dependent lower bound. Not a universal lower bound across all models.
- Phase 23 predecessor proofs, resultants, Hankel gates, trace gcd and complete cyclic certificates are preserved verbatim in `PRIOR_PHASE23_COMPLETE.zip`; this checkpoint alters only the frequency-probe/completion scheduling and exposes the native radix-2 baseline.
- No claim of independent novelty is made for Phase 21's reciprocal-resultant factor theorem, which remains outside formal mechanized proof and independent peer review.
