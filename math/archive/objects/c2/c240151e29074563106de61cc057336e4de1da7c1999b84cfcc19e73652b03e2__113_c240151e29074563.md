# Evidence map and rejected routes

Read-only synthesis of already materialized supplied research; no redownloads and no uploaded code execution.

1. `fft_synthesis_1126/README.txt`: exact fresh arbitrary-complex all-output sub-N-log-N target is still open; approximation and restricted exact claims use different models.
2. `fft_updates_audit_1146/SUMMARY.md`: the v4 O(N) shrinking-error decoder is the meaningful approximate frontier; source prototype remains incomplete as an all-parameter implementation. Affine-defect exact path is restricted to detected structure.
3. `materials_1144/AUDIT.md`, `MEDIAN_BASELINE.md`: componentwise periodic medians give the minimum relevant L1 residual and a paid linear-time fit. This idea is not new in this synthesis.
4. `materials_1144/GREEN_FUSION_AUDIT.md`, supplied `fft_green_conditioned_bridge_research/README.md`: explicit path-Green source/target formulas, inverse bound 73/18, and the general conditional error decomposition. The unresolved gap is finite-word execution and source/target rounding, not the algebraic conjugacy.
5. Supplied `fft_bidirectional_graph_research/README.md`, `fft_green_fusion_research/README.md`: affine intertwining extends to rational functions and ordered noncommuting filter words. Simply inserting a fused Green orbit before another affine defect test would largely restate an already allowed composition.
6. `materials_1155/SUMMARY.md`, `MIXED_INVERSE_EXTENSION.md`: reversible mixed-order Green stage has a charged linear scalar inverse, but its exact rational representation can have Θ(N)-bit denominators. This motivated certifying rounded solves rather than copying exact field counts into the word model.
7. `lowrank_condition_gate/PROOF.md`, `FORWARD_K_AND_COMBINED_GATE.md`: bounded-condition Green/rank-one and forward-cycle stage families have scoped logarithmic gates. A new exact factorization was not found and the proof does not authorize an unrestricted runtime lower bound.
8. `inverse_cycle_escape_1232/RESULT.md`, `COMMUTING_PHASE_GATE.md`: inverse pairs and phase-orbit fusion are real primitives; a long displayed word can collapse to a linear-work sparse evaluator. The known cutoff control has only two nonzeros per row, not Fourier flatness. It does not supply the missing exact transform.

Chosen new connection: apply the graph/Green “defect as a certificate” viewpoint to **rounding errors**, at both source and target, then compose with the periodic median and the existing decoder. Two small residuals can be evaluated on a bounded grid without materializing exact Green coefficients. The target certificate exploits the diagonal-plus-rank-two graph form and the audited resolvent norm. This is a concrete finite-word bridge extension, rather than a renamed ordinary-FFT gauge or a claimed new exact Fourier factorization.

No literature search or priority check was performed. “New” here means an addition to this bounded supplied-material synthesis, not established publication novelty.
