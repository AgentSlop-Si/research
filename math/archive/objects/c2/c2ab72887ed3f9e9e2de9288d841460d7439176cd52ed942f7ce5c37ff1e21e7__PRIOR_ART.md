- Nir Ailon (2013), *A Lower Bound for Fourier Transform Computation in a Linear Model Over 2x2 Unitary Gates Using Matrix Entropy*: https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.4745 . Unitary pair-gate entropy lower bound; cited prominently, not presented as new.
- Nir Ailon (2014), *An n log n Lower Bound for Fourier Transform Computation in the Well Conditioned Model*: https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.1307 . Important more general yet model-restricted work.
- Nir Ailon (2014), *Tighter Fourier Transform Complexity Tradeoffs*: https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.1741 . Precision / intermediate conditioning warning.
- Koenraad Audenaert (2007), *A sharp continuity estimate for the von Neumann entropy*: https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0610146 . Classical continuity specialization used for approximate unitary bound.
- Standard Cooley–Tukey / Weyl Fourier automorphism and cyclic bidiagonal solvers are preexisting methods; code independently derives and audits the explicit project-specific example.
