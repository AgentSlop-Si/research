# Next research gate — nontrivial, gauge-invariant overlapping FFT stages

**Objective:** arbitrary fresh complex input, N explicit exact outputs, one processor and fully charged total work, ideally O(N).

**Rejected or already tried:** cached/dynamic FFT, finite-alphabet complete tables, fixed-register scans, single Green solves with shuffles, growing-radix scan blocks, stages made dense by rank-one gauge dressing, near-power or recurrence integer shortcuts, tiny incomplete numerical fits, CrocSwap rank-certificate races.

**Genuinely unresolved:** a product of a *sublogarithmic* number of **two-or-more-channel overlapping nonunitary quasiseparable stages**, with input-independent interstage shuffles, implementing F_N for every power of two using O(N) charged elementary work per stage. One full stage needs Ω(N) channels (earlier cut-rank proof), but arbitrary multiple interleaved stages evade that proof. Summing two well-conditioned Green operators gives an explicit O(N)-work stage whose inverse is also dense, so it passes the old inverse-sparsity gate. **However**, its induced column norm is <=3/2, and submultiplicativity with ||F_N/sqrt(N)||_1=sqrt(N) forces Omega(log N) stages for this concrete stable family. More robustly kappa_1(S)<=17/4 whereas kappa_1(F_N)=N, so t>=log_(17/4)N even allowing arbitrary *global scalar* normalizations. A viable next candidate must escape both the sparse-inverse gate **and** the fixed-amplification/condition-budget restrictions by exhibiting and fully charging stages with kappa_1 that grows with N, or by using another genuinely different primitive. A growing kappa_1 is not automatically growing spectral-condition kappa_2.

**A concrete falsifiable next lemma to attempt before more code:** for arbitrary intermediate permutation P, characterize which N-point Fourier minors can occur in a product `S2 P S1` with `S_i` rank-2 quasiseparable (or sums of two Green solves). Find an **exact minor invariant** holding for all complex stage parameters and all P, OR construct exact stage parameter formulas with a scalable power-of-two recursion. Small least-squares fits are merely heuristics and do not satisfy either requirement.

**Required evidence for a real win:** independently verifiable full-matrix identity; stage generator synthesis and preparation charged; explicit O(N) per stage executor; total work o(N log N) for an unbounded family of N; output N ordinary numerical values; an analysis of coefficient growth and condition; lower work than baseline in the same exact arithmetic model; a proof it is not a trivial telescoping gauge of ordinary FFT.
