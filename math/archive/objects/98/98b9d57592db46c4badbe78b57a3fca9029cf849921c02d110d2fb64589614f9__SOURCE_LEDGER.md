# Primary source inspection ledger

Inspected 2026-10-10. Statements below refer to the indicated sections actually read. Search hits and abstracts alone were not used to establish mathematical equivalence. “No matching theorem established” is not an absence theorem.

## Newly decisive comparisons

1. **Sawa and Uchida**, *Discriminants of classical quasi-orthogonal polynomials, with combinatorial and number-theoretic applications*, arXiv:1802.00605v1 (2018); published in Journal of the Mathematical Society of Japan 71 (2019). Read §2.3 definition of quasi-orthogonality, Theorems 4.8 and 4.10, and their neighboring derivations. Exact ordinary quasi-Laguerre discriminant applies to the parity-reduced J. Numbering here is explicitly that of the inspected 30-page arXiv v1, not assumed to match the publication. https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.00605v1

2. **Gómez-Ullate, Grandati and Milson**, *Durfee rectangles and pseudo-Wronskian equivalences for Hermite polynomials*, arXiv:1612.05514. Read Definition 3.1, Proposition 3.1 (12), Theorem 3.1 (15), and the translation lemmas. Their equivalence gives the exact hook-Wronskian normalization used in PRIMARY_REDUCTIONS.md. https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.05514

3. **Grosu and Grosu**, *The irreducibility of some Wronskian Hermite polynomials*, arXiv:2007.00065v1; Indagationes Mathematicae (2021). Read Definition 1, Theorem 8, §5 proof, and Theorem 31 with its proof and Carlitz attribution. Theorem 8 specializes exactly to the hook congruence needed here. Carlitz's 1954 original was not independently retrieved; the EUDML open failed, so no claim of inspecting it is made. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.00065v1

## Current fixed root and family comparisons

4. **Atakishiyeva, Atakishiyev and Koornwinder**, *On a q-extension of Mehta's eigenvectors of the finite Fourier transform for q a root of unity* (2009). Read (2.1),(2.2),(2.7),(2.8),(3.8). Exact factorization antecedent and classical endpoint limit. https://arxiv.org/pdf/0811.4100

5. **Spiridonov and Zhedanov**, *Zeros and orthogonality of the Askey–Wilson polynomials for q a root of unity* (1997). Read §2 (2.1)–(2.8) and the caution about complex recurrence coefficients. Terminal-node equations were checked, not treated as a high-degree local splitting theorem. https://arxiv.org/pdf/q-alg/9605034

6. **Álvarez-Nodarse, Atakishiyeva and Atakishiyev**, *A q-extension of the generalized Hermite polynomials with the continuous orthogonality property on R* (2004). Read (1.1),(1.4),(2.7),(2.9),(4.16). Parity-wide recurrence change and Laguerre identities checked against the single terminal modification. https://renato.ryn-fismat.es/papers/2004-IJPAM-q-gen-her.pdf

7. **Uglov**, *Yangian Gelfand–Zetlin Bases, gl(N)-Jack Polynomials and computation of Dynamical Correlation Functions in the Spin Calogero–Sutherland Model* (1997). Read §4 (4.29)–(4.44), especially (4.32),(4.38),(4.39). The simultaneous two-parameter root limit and fixed polynomial variables were verified. https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9702020

8. **Dai, Ismail and Wang**, *From continuous to discrete: weak limit of normalized Askey–Wilson measure* (2024). Read §5, Lemma 5.1 and (5.1),(5.2). The inspected conclusions concern the limiting measure and terminal mass points. https://userweb.ucs.louisiana.edu/~xxw6637/papers/RJ2024.pdf

## Arithmetic and discriminant inputs rechecked

9. **Ismail and Witte**, *Discriminants and Functional Equations for Polynomials Orthogonal on the Unit Circle*, arXiv:math/0012259v2. Read ordinary/q definitions (1.7)–(1.9) and generalized operator formula §5 (5.13)–(5.18). The Rogers–Szegő product explicitly uses D_q. https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0012259

10. **Vinroot**, *Multivariate Rogers–Szegő polynomials and flags in finite vector spaces*, arXiv:1011.0984. Read recurrence/generating-function discussion and (2.3),(2.5),(2.6). These establish the classical alternating and shifted special values used in the content reduction. They do not state the project's inverse half-sum τ identity. https://arxiv.org/pdf/1011.0984

11. **Sharifi**, *Algebraic Number Theory*, Chapter 3, Lemma 3.1.13 and Propositions 3.1.20–3.1.21. Author notes were read directly for integral basis and ramification. These are standard arithmetic inputs, not priority candidates. https://math.ucla.edu/~sharifi/notes/algnum-ch03.html

## Nearby prime characteristic material

12. **On integrals of non-autonomous dynamical systems in finite characteristic**, arXiv:2602.09298v1, §4.3 and (4.7)–(4.9).
