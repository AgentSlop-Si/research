## Relevant master-archive strands

- Hero #17 strided fiber recognition and frequency-label union: source triage records O(r*N+Q*r^2+r*Q^2+N) for bounded r. This is extended by logarithmic-in-r pruning, not claimed invented here.
- Hero #1 Green intertwiners: P=7I+S+2D^-1 and L=7I+D+2S. The identity F*P=L*F is independently checked by this suite. The full structured class remains a proper family after an invertible endpoint map.
- Sparse-surrogate and randomized chirp paths: approximation contracts are different; their error guarantees are not used to assert exact outputs.
- Cached relation engines: no previous input or spectrum is supplied in this implementation.
- Fixed-graph and marked-cycle synthesis: a representation identity alone does not count as fast evaluation. No unresolved marked determinant oracle is used.
- Existing arbitrary-query pruning report: FFT pruning is explicitly classical. The proposed improvement is its three-way insertion into root search, certification and frequency-union synthesis with growing b.

## Public prior art checked in this session

1. FFTW, *Pruned FFTs*, https://fftw.org/pruned.html . States O(N log K) for K nonzero inputs or wanted outputs, and cautions that arithmetic savings need not become practical speed.

4. Kapralov, Velingker, Zandieh, *Dimension-independent Sparse Fourier Transform* (2019), https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.10633 . Further sparse-Fourier prior art; not imported as an oracle.

## What is new relative to the compared project statement

- A precise exact O(N log(b+2)+Q*b^2) end-to-end cap-only bound with growing b.
- An implemented light/heavy extension giving O(N log log N) for total K0=O(Q*polylog N), allowing completely dense individual fibers and charging the last over-budget rejection.
- Pruned grid root identification and pruned full-length input certificates, not uncharged root discovery.
- Pruned global residue-bucket synthesis with the concavity bound sum log(kappa+1).
- Complete implementations and rejection controls, including forged prefixes.
- Explicit dense/full-rank/dense-output family at growing b and a fixed-preconditioner coverage obstruction.

This is an extension of the project's structured algorithms. The cap-only class and the earlier aggregate-budget class are not identical. The added total-budget algorithm does extend the earlier aggregate regime to growing average sparsity, with a different parameter-dependent bound. New-to-project does not establish scholarly priority. The constituent algorithms are classical; a detailed publication-wide novelty review has not been performed.
