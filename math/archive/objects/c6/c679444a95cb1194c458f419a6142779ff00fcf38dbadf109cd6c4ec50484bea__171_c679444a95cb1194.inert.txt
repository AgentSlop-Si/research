# Conditioned macro-FFT research

Research on a different question from the current integer-multiplication / finite-certificate race:

> Can an exact sub-`N log N` Fourier transform be arranged so that its intermediate linear maps remain only mildly ill-conditioned?

The current theorem-form result is for **power-of-two lengths** and uses OpenAI #130 as a local exact sub-`m log m` black box.

Core idea:

1. transparentize a local normalized DFT into invertible shears with arbitrary scratch restored;
2. use three transparent additions to make a unitary two-bank exchange;
3. process radix-`m` macro-stages, so completed stages reset condition to 1;
4. choose `m(N)` by a bounded printer, filtering on a certified local condition bound.

The strongest note is `STABILITY_REGULARIZATION_THEOREM.md`.

Current claim boundary:

- fresh arbitrary dense input: **yes**;
- all outputs: **yes**;
- exact arithmetic: **yes, theorem-form**;
- `o(N log N)` on powers of two: **supported by the reduction**;
- `O(log log N)` square full-state condition: **supported by the bounded-printer reduction, conditional on formal extraction from #130**;
- all positive lengths: **not established**;
- floating-point forward/backward error theorem: **not established**;
- novelty/priority: **not claimed yet**.
