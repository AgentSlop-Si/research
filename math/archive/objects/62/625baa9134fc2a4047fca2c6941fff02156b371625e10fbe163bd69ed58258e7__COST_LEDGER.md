# Phase 22 charged arithmetic work ledger

Exact modular field addition + multiplication + inversion, with one unit for each; vector reads/writes separately recorded in execution state. The Nth roots and Green matrices are shared preinitialized data and not counted in either comparator. Integer/bit cost, float stability and hash-table construction assumptions are separately bounded in THEOREM.md.

Generic inputs in these tests have trace gcd 1 and correctly use the full FFT fallback. At N=32768 the failed discovery adds ~30.90% over the baseline, so there is **no generic speedup**. For fixed s the extra discovery arithmetic is O_s(N), asymptotically a diminishing fraction of NlogN but not a smaller overall growth rate.

## Model and verification

- `4s` direct residual Fourier probes cost O(sN); these dominate much of the accepted branch and are **not** removed.
- `b+1` Sylvester-resultant samples plus three witnesses have fixed s-dependent field cost, charged exactly as Phase 21.
- Trace quotient modular recurrence and gcd use O(b² logN) counted field operations, with a single modular square per bit for dyadic N.
- In the common degree-one gcd case, quadratic extraction and BSGS replace the N-point root scan; BSGS also consumes O(sqrt N) words. Higher gcd degree invokes the charged half-subgroup trace scan.
- A complete cyclic input certificate, spectral-denominator checks, all-output forcing and cyclic Green solve are charged. Failure invokes a full exact Bluestein FFT.
- The numeric proxy is not a floating-point runtime, bit-time theorem or total root-generation/field-setup measurement.
