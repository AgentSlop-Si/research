## Correction to the unqualified original lemma

Synchronized copies have singular values repeated, so kappa_2(I tensor P)=kappa_2(P). During sequential fiber evaluation, however, untouched fibers are identity. The relevant block is diag(P,I), whose condition is

max(||P||_2,1) max(||P^-1||_2,1).

It need not equal kappa_2(P): P=2I has local condition one and padded condition two. The independent exact diagonal control records this. Already completed unitary blocks and right multiplication by preceding unitary stages do not change this issue.

## Why the later transparent construction repairs it

Each elementary shear E=I+alpha e_j e_i^T, i!=j, has determinant one, inverse I−alpha e_j e_i^T, and both norm and inverse norm at most 1+|alpha|. Every shear/unitary prefix therefore has determinant of modulus one. Its singular values straddle one, so adjoining identity singular values leaves both extrema, and hence its condition, unchanged.

There is also a direct robust certificate. Put A=product_i(1+|alpha_i|) over the full finite word. Every prefix and its inverse have norm at most A because each omitted bound is at least one. Its identity-padded embedding has both norms at most A, hence condition at most A²=Khat. This is precisely enough for the shared-scratch sequential construction in the later stability theorem §§4–5.

The completed three-shear two-bank map is (x,y)↦(−U^-1 y,Ux), unitary when U is unitary, with scratch restored. This gives unitary boundaries without accumulating local condition bounds across completed macro-stages.

In §5.1, determinant modulus one and condition<=R imply each extremal norm<=R^(1−1/d). The displayed strict `<R` should read `<=R` when R=1. The selector uses R>=2, so this boundary wording does not change its asymptotic application.

## Remaining obligations

The repair validates the conditioning mechanism; it does not supply the missing concrete short Fourier circuit. The claimed sub-m-logm word still depends on actual #130 DAG extraction, normalized scalar operations, the root/coefficient construction, and a uniformly printable certificate. Effective computability of algebraic coefficients alone gives no useful preparation-time bound. The later source's explicit-record convention must include scalar preparation rather than allow an arbitrary short loop-program to hide long execution. Its selected finite plan must actually fit the charged preparation budget.

The resulting algebraic condition restriction does not by itself establish forward/backward numerical error, required mantissa lengths, or finite-precision runtime. Allowed-size and all-length transfer hypotheses also remain separate. Accordingly, the P=2I counterexample applies to the original unqualified padding equality; it is not a refutation of the later determinant-one/separate-norm repair.
