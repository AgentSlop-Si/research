# Prior-art boundary

This checkpoint uses three ingredients with established surrounding literature:

1. nonequispaced/nonuniform Fourier evaluation through nearby uniform grids;
2. low-rank correction matrices expressed through multiple diagonally scaled DFTs;
3. streaming majority cancellation for robust aggregation.

The new project-specific mechanism is narrower: the rational nearest-grid residuals are grouped exactly, each group is evaluated through the prime-power unit-step chirp row, and the inherited sharp variance scales from `M/Q` to `M/N` after normalization.

The exact class-count identity `N/gcd(N,Q)` and phase-modulation identity are elementary. No novelty claim is made. A limited search did not identify the exact combined construction, but absence from that search is not evidence of novelty.

The scan-fusion rank gate is also deliberately scoped: it applies to exact linear compression of already-materialized black-box channel states. It is not a lower bound for Fourier transforms or NUFFT algorithms.
