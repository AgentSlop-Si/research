# Independent Green fusion review

2026-10-09. **Verdict: the proposed fusion identities, exact norm formula, and
charged field-operation counts are correct.** They provide useful compatible
mixers; they do not supply a sub-NlogN factorization of the Fourier matrix.

## Cycle Green mixer

Assume M is a single weighted N-cycle, every edge has modulus one, and
M^N=−I. Over C, M is unitary. For K=I+(I−M)⁻¹,

\[
K=\tfrac32I+\tfrac12\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}M^j,
\qquad
K^{-1}=\frac{(2^{N-1}+1)I-
\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}2^{N-1-j}M^j}{2^N+1}.
\]

Distinct powers occupy distinct coordinates in every row and column. Thus
there are no hidden cancellations in the absolute column sums, and

\[
\|K\|_1=(N+2)/2,\quad
\|K^{-1}\|_1=2^N/(2^N+1),\quad
\kappa_1(K)=\frac{(N+2)2^{N-1}}{2^N+1}.
\]

These statements include N=1: K=[3/2] and κ₁=1. At N=2, κ₁=8/5.
Every entry of K and K⁻¹ is nonzero over C. This density assertion is not
valid uniformly over finite fields: our F3,N=2 check has only two nonzero
entries in K while both implemented actions remain correct.

The spectral condition number is different. The eigenvalues of M have
phases θ_j=(2j+1)π/N, and K's singular values are

\[
\sigma_j=\sqrt{2+\frac1{4\sin^2(\theta_j/2)}}.
\]

For even N this gives exactly the candidate's sine/cosine κ₂ formula.
In particular κ₂=1 at N=2; we independently verified K* K=(5/2)I there.
As N grows, κ₂∼2N/(3π), whereas κ₁∼N/2. For odd N, the smallest singular
value is 3/2 because the spectrum includes −1; N=1 again has κ₂=1.
Condition estimates alone do not establish floating-point forward stability
of the implemented cyclic solves.

## Costs and exact bit growth

Independent outer probes agree with all stated counts:

| Operation | Charged field gates |
|---|---:|
| Signed-cycle K, including construction of 1/2 | 3N+1 |
| Prepare affine source/target K and K⁻¹ | 2N+log₂N+4 |
| Apply prepared K | 5N+1 |
| Apply prepared K⁻¹ | 6N+1 |

The signed-cycle application alone is 3N−1 gates; its two setup gates are
included in the first row. The affine cycle solver executes two N-step
backward traversals and one closure multiplication, giving 4N+1 gates.
The additional vector operations produce the other counts. N=2 is covered;
the root-based affine constructor deliberately rejects N=1.

Indexing, full-cycle validation, reads, and output writes require O(N)
bounded-word operations when integer descriptors fit O(log N) bits. This
does **not** make exact field arithmetic constant-cost on such words.
For a signed-cycle basis input, inverse output coefficients have denominator
2^N+1; the nonidentity numerators are powers of two and are relatively prime
to that denominator. Each reduced denominator therefore needs N+1 bits.
Materializing N ordinary exact rational outputs can cost Ω(N²) bits. Even
if a common denominator is stored once, explicitly expanded numerators have
Θ(N²) total bit length. Compact symbolic exponents would be a different
output representation. The candidate appropriately makes no linear exact
bit-time claim.

The modular exceptions are real: the affine constructor correctly rejects
(p,N)=(5,2),(17,4),(257,8),(65537,16), where 2^N+1 vanishes. The complex norm
proof must not be transferred to those finite-field specializations.

## Path Green Fourier bridge

The independently checked bridge is exact. If T has diagonal 3 and adjacent
off-diagonal −1, it is the cyclic operator 3I−C−C⁻¹ plus its two removed-wrap
edge corrections. Positive-exponent Fourier conjugation therefore gives

\[
(FTF^{-1}y)_k=(3-q^k-q^{-k})y_k
+\frac1N\sum_lq^ly_l+\frac{q^{-k}}N\sum_ly_l.
\]

The correction is rank at most two. The code's Woodbury factors and signs
match this formula. For S=T⁻¹+(T+I)⁻¹, direct rational-function division gives

\[
S^{-1}=\tfrac12T+\tfrac14I-\tfrac18(T+\tfrac12I)^{-1},
\]

and the same identity holds for the Fourier-conjugate operator. No Fourier
call is required inside these source or target actions. This is an
intertwining identity, not an implementation that obtains Fx from x.
Vector-length validation was added during review to the four primitive
public methods; composites inherit it.

The separate stronger original-family bound κ₁(S)≤29/7 also checks
algebraically: G=(2T+I)⁻¹ has column sums at most 1/3 and G_jj≥1/7 by its
positive Neumann series. The signed column-sum formula gives
∥S⁻¹∥₁≤58/21; ∥S∥₁≤3/2 yields 29/7. This bounded-condition S family is
distinct from the growing-condition cycle K family.

## Accounting correction in the original gauge source

Source inspection confirms the reported undercount. `Gauge.action` starts
its dot product at zero and adds N products, then performs N output
additions: 2N literal additions. Its counter reports 2N−1. Each call is
short by one, or 2(log₂N−1) over the supplied dressed transform. A modified
dot-product implementation could save the initial addition, but the original
code does not implement that optimization. This discrepancy does not change
the asymptotic result.

## Bounded independent checks

Run `python3 verify_review.py`. It uses independently implemented dense
rational/modular arithmetic, DFT matrices, matrix inversion, and operation
probes, importing only the evaluated production interfaces.

All checks pass:

- six exact rational norm/density cases at N=1,2,3,4,8,12, with 30 signed
  basis applications and their exact cold counts;
- 15 modular affine cases, including F3,N=2, with complete basis checks of
  source/target K and inverses and all charged counts;
- four singular modular exceptions and four malformed-cycle rejections;
- 80 individual entries of the path Fourier rank-two identity, plus twelve
  dense-reference operator comparisons at N=4,8 and invalid-length checks;
- an exact denominator-growth witness at N=32.

The full candidate suites were not redundantly rerun. `results.json`
records the tested production hashes. The conclusions are bounded by the
stated field and output models, with no new universal Fourier claim.
