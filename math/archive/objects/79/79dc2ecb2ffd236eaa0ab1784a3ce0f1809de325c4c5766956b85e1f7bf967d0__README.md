# FFT Hero #1 Phase 18 — long-shift sparse-defect graph recurrence

**Status:** Exact finite-field implementations and proofs for a *structured-input* branch; no arbitrary-input exact FFT breakthrough. Completed October 10, 2026.

## Main result

The exact noncommuting Green identity from Phase16/17 is `L F = F P`. For dense residual `r=Px`, find a graph polynomial `q(S^delta)` with short degree `d` *in the long-range shift* whose defect `h=q(S^delta)r` has `K` nonzero values. Then

    (Fr)_k = (Fh)_k / q(omega^(k delta))

at nonresonant frequencies, and do an exact direct repair at the at most `d*gcd(N,delta)` resonant frequencies. The K-sparse forcing vector uses the unchanged Phase17 all-length rational-kernel evaluator. All signals and output coordinates may be dense.

For fixed candidate shift count, a short-degree graph recurrence is **discovered and checked** using a projective majority edge-ratio vote or a bounded-prefix Berlekamp–Massey fit plus a full N-coordinate defect audit. With `gcd(delta,N)=1`, constant `d,K` yields exact O(N) field arithmetic for its accepted structured family. If no proposal verifies, the generic Bluestein fallback remains O(NlogN).

## Highlights

- A known two-filter graph union can have **each individual defect vector fully dense**, while the joint defect vector has only three nonzeros. At N=384, delays 191 and 383 give separate supports 384/384 and joint support 3. This branch is not automatically discovered.
- N=384, delta=191, two long-cycle modes: every coordinate of `Px` is nonzero, and the transform equals the independent DFT over a prime field.
- Singular eigenfrequencies recovered by direct Fourier sums, never hidden behind division by zero.
- Algebraic dimension bound: a data-adaptive degree-d recurrence with <=K defects captures at most dimension d+K of C^N. Thus a generic input is excluded when d+K<N.
- Failed candidate discovery is charged. At N=4096, the measured arithmetic proxy is 163,939 vs. 570,070 for one mode; 168,066 for two distant-cycle modes; 176,259 for three defects; 266,798 for eight defects; 602,966 for a generic input that falls back.
- Floating division can be unstable near resonances; the exact arithmetic proofs do not imply a stable double-precision FFT.

The union branch has another 330,520 exact Fourier-matrix entries checked independently (713,100 total Phase18 matrix entries across both audits).
