## New v13 theorem

For the direct composite route, every output covariance satisfies

`Var <= V(N,M) ||x||2^2`,

where

`V(N,M)=2 sum_{d=1}^{M-1} 1/phi(N/gcd(N,d))`.

This always improves or matches the previous

`M(M-1)/phi(N)`

certificate.

## Least-prime-factor corollary

For composite `N` with least prime factor `p`, choose `M=p`:

`V(N,p)=2(p-1)/phi(N)`

and

`W=O(N+(N/p)log(N/p))`.

If `p=N^(gamma+o(1))`, then one row is `O(N)` and

`V=N^(gamma-1+o(1))`.

With a constant odd number of median rows, every error exponent

`alpha < (1-gamma)/2`

is attainable with failure tending to zero.

Balanced semiprimes have `gamma=1/2`, hence every `alpha<1/4` in `O(N)` work.
