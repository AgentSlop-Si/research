# Hero #1 — Phase 21: Blind graph-union FFT via reciprocal Hankel-resultant compression

**October 10, 2026.** Exact finite-field research checkpoint; **not** an arbitrary-input sub-NlogN FFT breakthrough.

## Main finding

For `r=Px` with `Q(S)r=h`, where `h` has two unknown impulses and `Q` is an unknown constant-sparse cyclic polynomial (including two or three completely unknown long-shift graph factors), reconstruct the reciprocal spectral ratio using low-rank Hankel constraints, short sparse rational interpolation, and a **full N-coordinate certificate**. On the nondegenerate constant-sparsity family, total exact arithmetic is `O_s(N)`; for generic inputs, use the preserved `O(NlogN)` fallback.

The new algebraic theorem proves

`Res_s(w)=w^[s(s+1)(4s+5)/6] (w-1)^[(s+1)^2] * w^b V_s(w+w^-1)`, where `b=s(s+1)(2s+1)/6` and `deg V_s<=b`.

For `s=4`, 31 exact samples replace the conservative 226 resultant samples. This improves the structured branch's exact field-operation proxy by 18.4% at N=4096 and 40.9% at N=32768 against complete Bluestein. Generic inputs pay extra discovery work and still fall back.
