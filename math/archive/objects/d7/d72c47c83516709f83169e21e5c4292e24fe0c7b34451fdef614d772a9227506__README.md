# FFT research — October 10, 2026

## What is proved

1. **Dyadic Fourier–Green Cut Spectra:** exact singular values of every Fourier residue-coset cut and numerical-error obstructions for products of weighted cyclic Green sums, including at powers-of-two lengths.
2. **Linear-time all-scales graph certificate:** O(N+E) exact integer computation of every dyadic outgoing-cut budget in a graph of E directed active Green edges.
3. **Aggregate-mode exact structured transform:** O(N+MK0+QK0+K0^2) work for checked raw Q-fiber signals of total Fourier mode budget <=K0; exact O(N) when K0=O(Q), Q^2=O(N).

## What is NOT proved

No arbitrary fresh dense O(N) exact Fourier transform; no new universal o(N log N) algorithm; no publication-level novelty claim; no finite-precision stability or bit-complexity theorem.
