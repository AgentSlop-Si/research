# Independent review: quadratic Cayley feedback and Fourier obstruction

Date: 2026-10-09 UTC. Verdict: accepted within the stated common-weighted-cycle subclass. This is an explicit uniformly cheap, genuinely degree-two feedback family whose Fourier covariance is structurally impossible at growing lengths. It is not a new FFT algorithm and is not a general obstruction to independently ordered paired-Green feedback.

## 1. What was independently checked

The constructor report and original feedback theory were read as text. No submitted or constructor program was executed or imported. All controls here were newly authored, with exact field arithmetic, a separately derived O(N) cyclic solver, and dense finite matrices used only as verification oracles.

The source phase certificate is valid. For K=BA, H=I-BA, J=I-AB and T=H^(-1)B, when H is invertible the push-through identity gives T=B J^(-1). Therefore

H D B-B S J=H(D T-T S)J.

Both H and J are invertible. Thus the cubic matrix identity is equivalent to Fourier covariance DT=TS. The seed H ones=B e0 is equivalent to T e0=ones. Together they determine every Fourier column. The correct output order is p(BA)B, not Bp(BA).

## 2. Family, guards, and legitimate paired-Green membership

Take even N>=2 over a field whose characteristic does not divide N and which contains a primitive Nth root. Let W=diag(w)P_pi in the row-action convention (Wx)_i=w_i x_pi(i), with pi Hamiltonian. Put rho=product w_i. Then W^N=rho I, including when some weights vanish.

Choose diagonal E with signs alternating along pi. It has E^2=I, EWE=-W, and two nonempty parity classes. Even N and char not dividing N ensure char!=2. For c=a,b require 1-c^N rho!=0. Define

C_c=(I+cW)(I-cW)^(-1)=2(I-cW)^(-1)-I.

Because N is even, the same guard makes I+cW invertible, and E C_c E=C_c^(-1). Choose lambda!=0 with lambda^2!=1 and set A=C_a, B=lambda C_b E.

Each map is a sum of two allowed Green plans with nonsingular diagonal endpoints. Its second term uses a zero-weight Hamiltonian Green denominator, which is the identity. This is legitimate only because zero edge weights are allowed in the declared architecture. The nonzero scalar endpoints 2, -1, 2lambda, -lambda are legal under the field and lambda guards.

The all-equal cycle choice is a subclass of the architecture permitting independent orders; it does not demonstrate a construction using genuinely independent noncommuting orders.

## 3. Exact minimal polynomial and transfer

Let R=C_b C_a^(-1). Rational functions of W commute, and E R E=R^(-1). Hence

BA=lambda R E, and (BA)^2=lambda^2 I.

The loop is not scalar. If lambda R E=cI, invertibility gives c!=0 and E=(c/lambda)R^(-1), which commutes with W. Together with EW=-WE and char!=2 this forces W=0. But W=0 gives R=I and BA=lambda E, which is nonscalar. Thus the minimal polynomial is exactly z^2-lambda^2, not merely bounded above by a quadratic.

Since its value at one is nonzero, H is invertible and

T=(B+BAB)/(1-lambda^2)
 =[lambda C_b E+lambda^2 C_a^(-1)]/(1-lambda^2).

In particular, the second term really is C_a^(-1). In deriving BAB, moving E through C_b must invert C_b. Every tested branch, including W=0 and zero edges, agrees with this orientation.

Lambda=0 would make B singular and was correctly excluded. For lambda=+1 or -1, H is singular because the nonscalar quadratic loop has both signs as eigenvalues; the independent N=6 controls have rank(H)=3 in each case. The cycle-resonance and odd-parity negative controls also fail exactly where expected.

## 4. Uniform cost is genuine, but the resulting operator is not Fourier

The cyclic linear system y_i-c w_i y_pi(i)=v_i can be solved without dividing by any edge weight. Traverse the cycle to compute the weighted sum defining y at one selected position, divide by 1-c^N rho, then back-substitute around the other N-1 positions. This uses O(N) field operations and works with zero weights. The reviewer implemented this method independently and checked every basis-vector output against direct matrix inverses.

A and B thus each cost O(N), including linear-time setup when the weights and cycle are uniformly supplied or generated. Evaluating T requires one B application, one A, a second B, and a vector combination. Its polynomial coefficients are obtained directly from lambda; no short-annihilator discovery oracle is hidden.

Over characteristic zero, the natural cycle with all w_i=1, a=2, b=3, lambda=2 is a uniformly explicit family for every even N. For c=2 or 3, the inverse Green entries are nonzero powers c^k/(1-c^N). The diagonal Cayley entry is (1+c^N)/(1-c^N), and the off-diagonal entries are 2c^k/(1-c^N). All are nonzero, so both A and B are genuinely dense. Exact arithmetic complexity says nothing here about conditioning or bit complexity.

## 5. All-parameter Fourier obstruction

Set P=(I-bW)(I+aW). It is invertible and has at most three nonzeros per row. Multiplying the transfer by P gives

P T=[lambda(I+aW)(I+bW)E+lambda^2(I-bW)(I-aW)]/(1-lambda^2).

Each row of PT has at most three nonzeros as well. Every branch with zeros, coinciding support positions, or parameter cancellation is included because those effects only reduce support.

If DT=TS, then T=L F_N for a diagonal L; invertibility of T implies every diagonal entry of L is nonzero. Any row x of P L is therefore nonzero with support at most three, and x F_N is the corresponding row of PT with support at most three.

Over any splitting field with characteristic not dividing N, a nonzero vector has |supp(x)| |supp(F_N x)|>=N. To see this without complex norms, form the circulant with shifted columns x. Fourier diagonalization makes its rank equal to |supp(F_N x)|. A column basis must cover all N nonzero row positions, and each basis column has |supp(x)| nonzeros. The bound follows by counting their union. Symmetry of F_N gives the row-vector version.

Thus N<=9. Since N is even, Fourier covariance is impossible for every N>=10, and in particular for every dyadic N>=16. This conclusion already rejects the cubic certificate; the seed is not needed. No positivity or characteristic-zero-only uncertainty step is being transferred to finite fields.

If a=0 or b=0, P and PT each have at most two nonzeros per row, so N<=4. This sharper specialization is valid for all allowed weights and orders.

An additional reviewed sharpening is available in cancellation branches: if a=b!=0, P=I-a^2W^2 has row support at most two while PT has support at most three. If a=-b!=0, use

P T=(lambda E+lambda^2 I)(I-bW)(I-aW)/(1-lambda^2)

to see that PT has row support at most two while P has at most three. In either branch covariance requires N<=6. These are refinements of the same support argument, not separate general exclusions.

## 6. A correct seed does not repair covariance

For the natural row cycle, a=0, b=1, lambda=2, set w_0=3, w_(N-1)=3/5, and all other weights one. Over characteristic zero, or a suitable field in which 2,3,5 are invertible, let g_0=-5/4 and g_i=-3/4 otherwise. Then (I-W)g=e0 and rho=9/5. Consequently C_1 e0=2g-e0, and the transfer formula gives T e0=ones exactly.

This proves that the seed and genuine quadratic closure can hold at every even length while Fourier covariance fails for N>4. The eight independent finite-field seed controls at N=2,4,6,8,10,12,16,24 all pass the seed and all fail covariance. The smaller-length failures are observations, not implied by the threshold theorem.

## 7. Exact controls and portable replay

The independent suite has 56 cases over F_241, whose multiplicative group supports all tested lengths 2,4,6,8,10,12,16,24. This deliberately checks the first excluded even length N=10, rather than only dyadic sizes. The seven variants at each size cover natural and scrambled cycles, a zero edge, W=0, the seed family, and a=b and a=-b cancellation branches.

The suite verifies 574 basis-vector inputs and 25,116 A/B/T output entries. It checks alternating parity, W^N=rho I, Cayley reciprocal identities, exact quadratic/non-scalar loops, feedback inverse and polynomial evaluator, sparse multiplied identity, cubic residual factorization, covariance exclusion at the actual support-product bound, seed behavior, and the guards listed above. It passes in an isolated copied directory using only Python's standard library, with byte-independent JSON equality to the saved results.

All finite tests corroborate the all-length proof; they are not an asymptotic construction or a general nonexistence search. The determinant, inverse, and dense multiplication routines belong only to the independent verifier.

## 8. What remains open

This common-cycle rational family is now a completed negative construction branch: it meets the uniform cheap-map and short-annihilator requirements but cannot meet Fourier covariance at growing lengths. Changing weights, parity, or a,b,lambda within its guards cannot fix that failure.

A different construction must escape the low-support denominator/numerator obstruction, for example through genuinely independent noncommuting orders. Such a change only removes this particular rejection proof; it does not supply a low-degree loop or the Fourier certificate. No general faster Fourier algorithm, broad lower bound, or publication-novelty claim follows.
