**Mathematical novelty classification:** Project-local extension with a short proof, not a certified unpublished theorem. The asymmetric selection argument is elementary; full-spark Fourier matrices (Chebotarev/Tao) and quasiseparability are known. Targeted web searches on Oct 10, 2026 found adjacent results but did not establish priority.

**Strong limits:** A product `(G1+G2)(G3+G4)` can have a dense inverse within either additive stage, and SPFSB cannot exclude it. A graph min-cut passing is necessary but not sufficient for Fourier phase identities. A representation lower bound does not imply a general FFT arithmetic complexity lower bound.
