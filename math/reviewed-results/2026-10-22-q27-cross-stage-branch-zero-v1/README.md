# Q27: bounded cross-stage branch-zero exclusion

Accepted scope, 22 October 2026: exactly 256 original-chart four-unit-phase
families with encoded coordinates [7, e0, 31, 24+e1], 8 <= e0,e1 <= 23, retain
the original six inverse guards and admit no external invertible-complex
row/column monomial equivalence to F8. The 512 checked instances are the same
256 families in two modular embeddings, not 512 different families.

The characteristic-zero certificate proves d*F^2 belongs to the original
784-minor ideal, with d nonzero in Q(i). The finite-field map is a homomorphism
from a Gaussian localization, not an embedding of Q(i) into a finite field.
No additional saturation, phase-root restriction, or anchor nonvanishing is used.

This is a genuine but modest bounded result, not a full 34-phase-fiber result,
an owner closure, historical-novelty claim, Fourier construction, or FFT speedup.
Q27 remains TO RESEARCH at priority 50.
