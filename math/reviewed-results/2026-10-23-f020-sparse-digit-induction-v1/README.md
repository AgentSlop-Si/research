# F020: arbitrary-length sparse even-middle / odd-terminal guard

Independently accepted theorem, 23 October 2026. For any finite t>=0,
odd prime p, even digits 2<=c_a<p, odd terminal digit 1<=d<p,
r>k_1>...>k_t>j>=2 with every consecutive gap at least two, and s>r,
set n=2*p^r+sum c_a*p^(k_a)+d*p^j. For every characteristic-zero
primitive p^s phase, both original P_n and H_n are squarefree and the
original ordinary algebraic-square Christoffel-Darboux kernel is nonzero
at every original P_n root.

There is no fixed bound on the number of even middle digits. The proof uses
arbitrary-prefix covers without assuming prefix simplicity, a length-independent
suffix invariant, actual shifted continuants and paid off-root derivative terms.
It covers every relevant boundary, adjacent-scale annulus and deepest interior.
Algebraic squares are not absolute-value squares.

New degree-shape credit is only t>=2 after all inherited high-order, fixed-node
and successful residual coverage is subtracted. t=0 and t=1 retain their prior
credit. This publication includes the accepted two-middle proof/review as
dependencies, not as a second separately counted upload or discovery.
The independent reviewer records 1,678,587 exact normal/optimized checks;
the author's separate 3,393,468 checks are not independent evidence. Finite
controls support the proof, not an extrapolation to arbitrary length.

F020/F023 stays TO RESEARCH at priority 58. This does not cover adjacent gaps,
j=1, odd middle digits, even terminal digits, other leading digits or arbitrary
orders/degrees. No whole-owner closure, historical novelty, FFT/runtime,
bit-complexity or stability improvement is claimed. Earlier accepted source
qualifications and original recurrence/denominator/ordinary-derivative guards
remain binding. Previously published single-middle bytes are unchanged.
