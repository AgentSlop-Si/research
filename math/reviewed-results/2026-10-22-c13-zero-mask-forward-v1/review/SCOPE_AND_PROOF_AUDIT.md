## Allowed mathematical increment

Fix legal factorizations U=D_a(I-W0)P_r0 and V=P_s0(I-W1)D_d with a,d coordinatewise nonzero, fixed structural routes, and every cycle product unequal to one. The source's separated base/edge convention requires N>=2 and no support collisions. Keep these guards even if a masked output branch becomes zero.

For prescribed zero sets Zx,Zy, require x_i=0 exactly on Zx and y_j=0 exactly on Zy. The allowed forward grammar is only

T0=P_r0^-1(I-W0)^-1 D_(x/a)=U^-1D_x,
T1=D_(y/d)(I-W1)^-1 P_s0^-1=D_yV^-1.

Then B=T0+T1 if and only if UBV=UD_y+D_xV. Both implications follow using only U^-1,V^-1. No mask or singular branch is inverted. This proves sufficiency in this grammar; it does not prove that arbitrary singular Green endpoints can be put into this grammar.

For each position, the scalar equality is V[i,j]x_i+U[i,j]y_j=(UBV)[i,j]. All off-support positions must vanish. On support, invertible affine transitions yield z_v=alpha_v t+beta_v with alpha_v nonzero in each component. Every zero-mask variable imposes a root pin; every nonzero-mask variable forbids exactly one root. All linear/cycle pins must agree. A free component is feasible precisely when its forbidden roots do not cover the supplied base field. For Q one can test m+1 distinct rationals when there are at most m roots; for F_p test up to min(p,m+1) distinct residues. An extension field is not substituted. In a general effective field, element generation/representation must be supplied and charged.

If both masks are all-zero, the only possible B is zero. If just one branch is all-zero, the other branch's exact one-sided expression remains. Rank(T0)=number of nonzero x coordinates and rank(T1)=number of nonzero y coordinates. Thus zero/singular endpoints do not affect invertibility of the retained U,V skeletons.

For a cycle c_0,...,c_(ell-1) and weights w, the equation h_i-w_i h_next(i)=q_i has anchor

h_c0 = [sum from j=0 to ell-1 of (product from k=0 to j-1 of w_ck) q_cj] / [1-product from k=0 to ell-1 of w_ck].

Backward recurrence h_i=q_i+w_i h_next(i) recovers the rest. This proves linear field work without division by a possibly zero weight. Endpoint operations and permutations are linear. Dense basis reconstruction costs quadratic field work and storage if the dense result is retained.

## Scope not closed

Unrestricted direction discovery; simultaneous arbitrary two-sided singular endpoints; independently prescribed original endpoint values/permutations beyond this grammar; general target invertibility; application-specific Fourier integration/costing; historical priority or attribution; and whole-owner completion all remain outside this acceptance scope.

A concrete scope witness shows that the two-sided exclusion is substantive, not merely wording. Over Q at N=3, let P be the successor cycle, U=I-2P, and V=I with its legal prescribed zero-weight structural cycle. Then U^-1=-(I+2P+4P^2)/7. The two-sided singular endpoint branch E00 U^-1 E11=-(2/7)E01 is not of the form U^-1D_x+D_yV^-1 for any x,y: its (0,1) entry forces x_1=1 while its (2,1) entry forces x_1=0. The independent checker verifies this target is rejected under all 64 one-sided masks. It is a counterexample only to a broader, unclaimed completion statement.
