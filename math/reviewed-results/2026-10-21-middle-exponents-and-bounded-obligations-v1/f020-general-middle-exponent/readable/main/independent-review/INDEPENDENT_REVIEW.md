## 3. Geometry and the actual all-r inner-neighbor bound

Put N=p^r, B=r(p-1)p^(r-1)/2, beta=p^floor(r/2), L=N/beta, K=N+2B, E=N/2+2B, D=N+E-p. The old radius is beta/2 for even r and p^(floor(r/2)-1)(2p-1)/2 for odd r. Distinct old centers have distance value at most p^(floor(r/2)-1)<rho, so there are beta disjoint old disks, not p disks. Their 2L roots each total 2N. Their nonzero center valuations are p^ord_p(a); the central boundary valuation is rho.

The short tail needs separate radii eta=(p+1)/2 and sigma=p-1/2, satisfying eta<sigma<=rho and sigma<p. These do not grow with r. The alpha scale is 1-1/(2p)<1 and remains distinct from all three.

The principal new bound is weight(M)>=E_eta=3N-N/p-p^2 on each eta disk, where M=P_(2N-1). I independently enumerated the original Newton lower-bound score before reading the completed report and found its extremizer is k*=2N-p^3, not k=N for r>3. I then derived an independent digit dynamic program directly from Vfac(k)-T_eta(k)-Vfac(l), with k=2l+1. This does not assume the author's carry expression.

For the uniform proof, the author's exact carry expression is correct. Low digits a0,a1,a2 have coefficients -1,-p,-p. Every higher digit coefficient p^(j-1)[(j-2)p-(j-1)] is strictly positive for p>=3,j>=3, and the higher carry coefficients are positive. Maximization therefore sets every higher digit to p-1, forcing its carry to one regardless of the previous carry. This decouples the higher contribution from the low-three maximization.

Given the low carries e0,e1,e2, the least feasible digits are a0=m e0, a1=(m+1-e0)e1, a2=(m+1-e1)e2, where m=(p-1)/2. Their score is exactly

    p[(m-1+e0)e1 + (p(p-1)-m-1+e1)e2].

When e2=0 this is at most pm. Setting e2=1 gives a strictly larger achievable score; it then strictly favors e1=1 and e0=1. Thus the unique low optimum is m,m,m, even at p=3. The unique full optimum is l*=N-(p^3+1)/2 and k*=2N-p^3. Reflecting the p-valuations in the last p^3 edges gives precisely E_eta=3N-N/p-p^2. This is a coefficientwise lower bound. The proof does not assume a noncancelling M unit or extend the old rho-disk bound to eta.

At r=3 the bound becomes 3N-2N/p and k*=N, agreeing with the inherited theorem. For r>3 a blind r=3 substitution would give the wrong exact extremizer and would not justify the claimed sharp value. The old substituted bound happens to be weaker, not a counterexample, but it is not the new exact score calculation.
