# F020 middle-digit family: accepted parity components

This collection assembles two separately accepted theorems for n=2p^3+cp, with p any odd prime, every integer s>3, every integer 1<=c<p, and every characteristic-zero primitive p^s-th phase. In the original normalization, P_n and Gaussian H_n are squarefree, and the original algebraic-square Christoffel-Darboux kernel is nonzero at every P_n root.

The even/ and odd/ directories preserve separate proofs, mathematical reviews, provenance and root/ordinary-derivative ledgers. Combining their scopes is documentary assembly, not a third theorem or new theorem credit. The endpoints c=1 and c=2 were already accepted and remain inherited. The added sectors are odd c>=3 and even c>=4 relative only to the inspected accepted-source map. At p=3, both permitted cases are inherited.

## Keep the two root ledgers distinct

- Even c: 2p^3 high boundary roots, exactly 2p^2 per old disk, and cp outer roots in c nonzero coarse residue classes with p roots each. There is no zero. Original derivative values are D+F high and 2N alpha+F-1/2 outer; the constant value is K+F.
- Odd c: 2p^3 high boundary roots, one exact zero, p-1 separated inner roots and (c-1)p outer roots in c-1 nonzero coarse residue classes with p roots each. The zero is strictly interior, not a high boundary root. Derivative values differ between central high, noncentral high, zero, inner and outer roots; see SCOPE.json and odd/SCOPE.json.

Here N=p^3, K=4p^3-3p^2, E=7p^3/2-3p^2, D=N+E-p, F=c(p-1/2), alpha=1-1/(2p), and W=(c-1)(2p-1)/2 for the odd component. Valuations use v(q-1)=1.

Repeated coarse residue roots are counted before characteristic-zero simplicity is established. Original ordinary derivatives are taken before reduction, never replaced by differentiating an inseparable residue polynomial. No uniform finer outer splitting radius is claimed. The stronger inherited c=2 geometry is not extrapolated. The kernel uses algebraic squares, not absolute-value squares.
