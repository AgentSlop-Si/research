# Original signed banks: 5N/3 support upper bound

Independently accepted first-order cancellation theorem, 23 October 2026.
In the original signed phase-weighted resolvent-bank grammar, for odd N,
d>=4, N>=6d-2, a supplied primitive Nth root q and characteristic not dividing N,
the negative unnormalized Fourier matrix uses at most 2(N-d)+2 banks over a
genuine splitting extension. For every odd N>=23, d=floor((N+2)/6) gives

    B <= 2N-2floor((N+2)/6)+2 = 5N/3+O(1).

This improves the prior sufficient threshold N>=8d-10 and upper-bound leading
constant 7/4 in the same model. The asymptotic leading-constant reduction is
1/21, approximately 4.7619%, not a universal finite-N percentage or runtime gain.
The first-order variable-coefficient cancellation is the mathematical delta;
the original envelope, fixed-divisor/separability guards and full-axis physical
reconstruction remain intact.

## Costs and limits

The finite coefficient field may have degree two, but it is NOT the splitting
field. A genuine possibly huge splitting extension, root recovery, coefficient
search, amplitudes/raw weights and all setup/storage/input/output costs remain
paid. The full ledger is part of this result. Warm literal application is
2BN² multiplications and 2BN²-BN-N additions over the actual splitting field,
hence cubic for this bank-count bound, before base-field expansion.

No FFT/runtime improvement, efficient full compiler, optimality, base-field
attainment, historical novelty clearance, discovery-count increase, priority
change or closure of the four broader parent owners is claimed. Separately
accepted or pending construction results retain their own scope and are not
silently composed with this theorem. The independent reviewer performed 31,713
fresh exact checks with normal/optimized agreement; no full literal root
instantiation is claimed executed.
