## Accepted mathematical scope

For the original centered proper-complex N=3, d=2 model, every finite a=r^2>0 is covered. The exact maximum is max(B(a),S(a)), with B(a)=(1-exp(-a))(1-exp(-2a)) and S(a)=F_a(1/3,1/3,1/3). There is one transverse transition 1/2<a_*<2. Modulo coordinate permutations and phases, the only maximizing normal weights are (0,1/2,1/2) below the transition, (1/3,1/3,1/3) above it, and exactly those two types at equality. No result for general N, another rank, a real-Gaussian analogue, zero or infinite radius, or faster FFT runtime is asserted.
