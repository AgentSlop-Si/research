What the tree witness establishes
10 October 2026 | Research after checkpoint 26
The strongest new result closes the structural coverage question left at checkpoint 26. Even well-conditioned inverses
of matrices with only two entries per column can require logarithmically growing separation rank under every
independent choice of row and column order.

A precise witness
Let Tₕ be the complete binary tree of height h, with N = 2^(h+1) − 1 vertices. Put Pₕ[u,v] = 1 when u is the parent of v,
and Qₕ = I − ½Pₕ. The inverse has entry 2^(−d) from an ancestor to a descendant at distance d, and zero elsewhere.
                                         ⌊(h+1)/4⌋ ≤ sr(Qₕ⁻¹) = sr(Qₕ) ≤ h
                                          κ₂(Qₕ⁻¹) = κ₂(Qₕ) ≤ 3 + 2√2 < 6
Here sr minimizes, over independent row and column orders, the largest rank of a cut block in either orientation.
Thus sr = Θ(log N). Qₕ has at most two nonzeros per column and three per row, and its bipartite support graph is a
tree. The rank result holds over every field for I − Pₕ; the displayed conditioning statement concerns the complex,
half-weighted family.

Why this advances the argument
The accepted separator theorem uses a constant separator budget q = 2 on this family, while every fixed ordered-
separation budget eventually fails. This proves coverage beyond every fixed rank budget. It closes the exact internal
mathematical question; reverse containment of whole classes is not established.
The proof combines classical inverse-rank preservation with a forest matching argument and the relation between
forest linear rank-width and pathwidth. An elementary fallback already proves unboundedness. A separate audit
checked the independent-order quantifiers, all-height proof and conditioning corollary.

What can be claimed now
This is a useful structural theorem for developing the separator note. External originality still needs a precise
literature comparison. The TQS paper already studies binary-tree matrices; this result does not resolve its
representation-storage question. Exact rank also does not imply a fixed-tolerance numerical-rank bound.
The other new results reject specified inverse-packet and selected-convolution fusion schemes. They provide no
general FFT arithmetic lower bound and no new faster full-output FFT. An official September 2026 OpenAI preprint
states an exact-register power saving in its supplied-root model; the missing construction here is project- and model-
specific.
Proofs and independent audits: the preserved tree theorem and conditioning package. Primary antecedents: Adler–
Kanté (2015), Vandebril–Van Barel (2004), and Govindarajan–Chandrasekaran–Dewilde (2025). The companion
synthesis gives direct links and the other results.
