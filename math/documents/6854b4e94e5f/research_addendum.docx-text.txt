The tree witness closes the coverage question

For the complete rooted binary tree Tₕ, let N = 2^(h+1) − 1 and let Pₕ[u,v] be 1 exactly when u is the parent of v. Write Qₕ = I − Pₕ. Over every field, Qₕ is invertible, with at most two nonzeros per column and three per row. Its inverse is the ancestor incidence matrix.

⌊(h+1)/4⌋ ≤ sr(Qₕ⁻¹) = sr(Qₕ) ≤ h

For each pair of independent row and column orders, take the largest rank among both cut blocks A[Rₖ,Cₖᶜ] and A[Rₖᶜ,Cₖ]; sr is the minimum of this quantity over all such order pairs. The theorem therefore proves Θ(log N) after optimization. A poor natural ordering would not suffice.

The proof swaps the row and column orders when applying classical complementary inverse-rank identities. Forest support makes every relevant matrix rank equal to matching size over every field. Interleaving the independent orders bounds the support graph’s linear rank-width; the forest theorem and binary-tree pathwidth then supply the lower bound. Depth-first preorder supplies the upper bound. An elementary alternative also proves unboundedness without the external rank-width theorem. [1, 2]

Replace Pₕ by ½Pₕ over the complex numbers. Every submatrix rank is preserved, and ‖Pₕ‖₂ = √2 for h ≥ 1 gives κ₂(I − ½Pₕ) = κ₂((I − ½Pₕ)⁻¹) ≤ 3 + 2√2 < 6. Thus conditioning does not explain away the exact-rank separation. No lower bound on tolerance-dependent numerical rank follows.

The earlier separator theorem gives N ≤ 4qr36ʳ for a flat unitary represented by r inverse branches with separator budget q and independent monomial-supported endpoints. Its bipartite support lift is a tree, so this witness has q = 2. For any fixed s, height h = 4s + 3 gives sr ≥ s + 1. The column-two inverse class is therefore not contained in any fixed-s ordered-separation class. This is coverage beyond every fixed budget, without a reverse-containment or literal proper-superset claim.

The 2025 TQS paper already considers related binary-tree matrices and ordering-dependent representations. This theorem does not determine optimal SSS/HSS storage, an exact optimal ordering, or resolve that paper’s question. Its external novelty remains unverified. [3]

Routed packets need more than one shared solve

Take a permutation matrix P, a primitive rth root ζ and 0 < |a| < 1. A bank of r inverse branches has denominators Bᵢ = I − aζⁱP, 0 ≤ i < r, and independently partial-monomial endpoint factors Lᵢ and Rᵢ. A partial monomial has at most one nonzero in each row and column; its weights may vanish. Let Q = I − aʳPʳ.

The inherited geometric identity Bᵢ⁻¹ = HᵢQ⁻¹, with Hᵢ = Σₛ₌₀ʳ⁻¹(aζⁱ)ˢPˢ, genuinely shares computation across distinct denominators. The character-weighted special case already belonged to the earlier corpus and can be evaluated with linear scalar work in its stated range. It prevents inferring Nr arithmetic work merely from r displayed branches.

If every Rᵢ commutes with Pʳ, then T = ΣᵢLᵢBᵢ⁻¹Rᵢ = MQ⁻¹, where each column of M has at most r² nonzeros. For a complex flat unitary U (unitary with all entries of modulus 1/√N), every entry of UQ has modulus at least (1 − |a|ʳ)/√N. Therefore exact equality T = U requires r² ≥ N. If all left factors are diagonal and the right factor is common, the support count sharpens to r ≥ N.

The same argument gives a quantitative error bound. If M has at most t nonzeros per column, then both ‖U − MQ⁻¹‖₂ and ‖U − MQ⁻¹‖F/√N are at least

[(1 − |a|ʳ)/(1 + |a|ʳ)] √max(0, 1 − t/N).

The coefficient can degenerate as |a|ʳ approaches one. A uniform error claim needs that parameter controlled.

For arbitrary right routes, the failed commutation leaves an explicit correction E, with rank E ≤ K = Σᵢ rank([Rᵢ,Pʳ]). The sparse-numerator and low-rank-repair argument gives the necessary conditions

2r²(K+1)² ≥ N,

(r²+2)(K+1) ≥ N+1   for prime-order complex Fourier.

The first uses product-support uncertainty for flat unitaries. The second uses the classical additive uncertainty theorem and requires prime-order Fourier; it is not a statement about every flat unitary or arbitrary finite fields. [4] A logarithmic-width packet with bounded-rank route defects cannot supply the desired exact transform in this family.

These are representation constraints. Dense numerators and high-rank corrections can still have short arithmetic circuits; the result supplies no general FFT operation lower bound. A surviving construction needs an explicit, fully charged shared evaluator for the correction, or a different denominator family. Preparation, routing, output writes and precision remain part of the accounting.

Selected convolution has two additional fusion barriers

The selected Fourier rows contain a consecutive interval of length M. A standard Vandermonde argument makes the selected map injective on any coordinate subspace of dimension at most M. A block-local linear encoder must therefore retain the entire input dimension of each such block if all subsequent outputs depend only on its summaries, with no bypass or reread. [5]

In the blocked construction N = QB, replacing Q channels by r length-B streams through such source-local encoders requires r ≥ Q when Q ≤ M, which holds eventually for the inherited asymptotic parameters. If each stream then undergoes a separate ordinary full radix-2 B-point FFT, that stage costs at least N log₂(B)/2 butterflies. This excludes that particular compression-plus-independent-FFT architecture. It does not impose this cost on an arbitrary joint evaluator.

For a primitive Nth root ω, set ζ = ω^Q and F_B[u,j] = ζ^(uj). Moving a fractional twiddle through F_B yields Kₐ = F_B⁻¹diag(ω^(au))F_B. When Q does not divide a, its entries are (1 − ω^(aB)) / [B(1 − ωᵃζ^(k−j))], a row-scaled Cauchy matrix. Every square minor is nonzero and the inverse is dense. Its cyclic stencil uses all B offsets, and a balanced separated block has rank ⌊B/2⌋ under every independent reordering. Any single-inverse representation with invertible monomial endpoints has a dense denominator. These facts do not lower-bound general Cauchy application complexity.

A faster fully charged exact selected evaluator T_E is still missing here. The inherited high-time packet estimate remains T_E(N) + O(NL + N), with early-time packets, causal inversion and full Fourier recovery as separate obligations. The new statements apply classical full-spark and Cauchy facts to these concrete attempted constructions; they are not new general matrix theory.

The external exact Fourier theorem changes the framing

The official OpenAI manuscript An explicit power saving for the exact discrete Fourier transform, dated 25 September 2026, states an eventual O(N(log N)^(1 − 10⁻¹³)) deterministic all-length exact DFT bound. Its model has exact complex registers, unrestricted coefficients and an explicitly supplied polynomial-order root; preparation and index work are charged. The official statement and model were inspected, but this package does not independently certify the full external proof or Lean build. [6]

A narrower implementation starting only from rational scalars and a power-of-two Fourier root cannot obtain odd-order roots by field operations alone. The supplied-root interface therefore needs an explicit model change or a costed extension-field simulation. This import limitation does not refute the external theorem or prove simulation impossible. The accurate open obligation is the missing algorithm in this project or narrower interface, rather than a claim that general exact-register power saving is unknown.
