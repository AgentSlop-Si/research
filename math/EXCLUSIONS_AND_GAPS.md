- 377/378 cited source references map to an exported original or sanitized copy. [Per-reference map](provenance/CATALOGUE_SOURCE_COVERAGE.json).

- 20 unique original numeric rank/witness/index/input arrays are included as inert binary data with exact hashes. One CMake compiler executable is omitted. [Numeric data manifest](provenance/NUMERIC_DATA_MANIFEST.json).
- 4 unique compressed JSON source versions are tracked, with 0 having neither an original compressed-data export nor a decompressed counterpart. [Compressed certificate map](provenance/COMPRESSED_CERTIFICATE_COVERAGE.json).

## Deliberately excluded classes

Private conversation exports, account/transport records and private URLs; redundant aggregate inventories and raw logs; caches, compiled files and executable binaries; cumulative archive containers whose admitted contents are already indexed; source publications and separately identified upstream code without cleared redistribution/attribution packaging. Exclusions preserve research conclusions and source hashes where appropriate, rather than republishing unrelated operational records.

The original mathematical manuscripts are not replaced by the publication-status summaries. The two complete reviewed drafts, their mathematical finite certificates and independently authored controls are present. Original private review records are represented by a newly prepared public scope/hash statement.

## Availability and rights gaps

No claim is made that all originals in every historical bundle, external account, or cited paper have been recovered. Some historical exact-byte references were already missing before export. The third-party primary texts, their publisher layouts and upstream implementations require separate lawful access or attribution decisions. The remaining novelty comparisons are mathematical research work, not gaps that can be solved by declaring this export complete.
