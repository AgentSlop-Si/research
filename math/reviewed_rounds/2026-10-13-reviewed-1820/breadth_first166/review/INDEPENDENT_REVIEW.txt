4. ALG029 mathematical review

Assume the stated unitary negative-exponent DIF graph, N=2^L, nonzero exact
input x, 0<epsilon<1 and the given ideal-state certificates. Write r=||x||_2,
r/2<=ell<=r, theta=epsilon ell and delta=theta/4. Each visited node has
|a_A-||v_A||_2|<=delta; each written leaf has |z_k-y_k|<=delta. These are
absolute bounds against ideal states and coefficients. Accumulated root,
butterfly, scaling and norm errors all have to fit inside them. A small
rounding bound relative only to an approximate state's norm does not suffice.

For a pruned node, a_A+delta<=theta implies
||y_A||_2=||v_A||_2<=a_A+delta<=theta.
Every coordinate left zero under it has error at most theta. Written leaves
have error at most delta. A coordinate lies under at most one first-pruned
subtree, so there is no pathwise accumulation of pruning error. Hence
||output-U_N x||_infinity<=theta<=epsilon r.
This proves the claimed global-norm additive approximation.

For an unpruned node, a_A+delta>theta and a_A<=||v_A||_2+delta give
||v_A||_2>theta-2delta=theta/2.
The source's disjoint output sets and energy identity at depth d therefore
give A_d<=min(2^d,4r^2/theta^2)<=min(2^d,16 epsilon^-2). The energy
inequality is strict before it is safely weakened. The argument holds
for complex data without a numerical transform or complex ordering.

A split at depth d processes N/2^d entries. Thus
V<=N sum[d=0,...,L-1] min(1,16 epsilon^-2/2^d).
Set h=ceil(log2(16 epsilon^-2)). The geometric tail is at most two, yielding
V<=N min(L,h+2). Since h=4+ceil(log2(epsilon^-2)), the factor 16 shifts
the crossover by four levels. N+V consequently has the claimed
O(N[1+min(L,log(epsilon^-2))]) order with absolute constants. L=0, N=1
is included with V=0. This is not a measured speedup.

5. Cost, precision, ties and boundary branches

The root norm scan plus the two child norm scans of each split has total
entry volume exactly N+V. Ideal butterflies cost O(V); full-output zero
initialization and retained writes are included. Depth-first traversal
has O(N) ideal state-array storage. These facts alone do not pay for
variable precision or certificate representations.

The candidate correctly conditions the work statement. Certificate
generation needs uniformly bounded cost per processed entry in the chosen
arithmetic model to preserve that arithmetic order. More generally total
work is K_setup+O(N+V)+K_cert. Input validation, ell enclosure, root/scalar
preparation, accumulated enclosures, precision refinements, rejected/failed

implementation. The storage claim requires separately bounded certificate
representation. No numerical backend, exact-root oracle or constant-

The guard prunes ties in a_A+delta, not necessarily ties in actual
coefficient magnitude. For theta=1 and delta=1/4, true magnitude 1 with
a=1 is retained, whereas a=3/4 is pruned; both estimates meet the budget.
Furthermore ell<r changes the working threshold from the source's
epsilon r. Exact masks therefore need not agree. The contract properly
excludes exact hard-threshold identity, retained-coefficient exactness,
relative-best-k and zero-tail recovery claims.
