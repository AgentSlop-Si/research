## Why the compatibility graph bounds the original problem

Coprime powering separates the 2-primary and odd components of every subgroup, and the odd component is cyclic. Thus each subgroup is H × Cd with H ≤ D8 and d dividing 5r. The ten D8 subgroups comprise the identity, five order-two groups, three order-four groups, and D8. Maximal subgroups of G are normal and have prime index; their orders are 20r, 8r, and 40.

If a proper prefix is invariant under such a maximal J, no earlier prefix can have a stabilizer outside J: its quotient support is a nonempty subset of a fixed proper support in the prime quotient. At or after that prefix, one whole J-coset is present; any outside stabilizer would cycle that full coset through the prime quotient and force the prefix to be full. Therefore B ≤ J. Escaping orders consequently have no proper-prefix stabilizer of the three excluded orders. Only necessity is used; the graph is not asserted to characterize realizable escape histories.

For two nested prefixes Ti ⊂ Tj with stabilizers L × Ce and R × Cf, the two D8 orders are comparable powers of two, although the subgroups need not be nested or permute. If |L| ≥ |R|, saturating Ti by Cf produces an intermediate invariant set with stabilizer containing L × C_lcm(e,f). If |L| < |R|, intersecting the Ce-translates of Tj produces an intermediate invariant set with stabilizer containing R × C_lcm(e,f). Centrality of the odd factors proves the invariance and containment in both cases. The resulting subgroup has order lcm(|Si|,|Sj|), so an admissible multiple of this order lies between the two prefix sizes. An endpoint is allowed only when its actual stabilizer has that whole order. This proves the candidate's conflict relation for actual prefix stabilizers.

Selecting the positive proper-prefix stabilizers of an escaping order therefore gives an independent set whose weight is its exact proper-prefix score. The upper argument allows additional graph-independent sets that may have no group realization. This relaxation is safe in the upper-bound direction. Adding the unique full-prefix contribution 40r − 1 to the graph bound 82r − 39 gives 122r − 40.

## Attainment and unrestricted equality

The odd-factor order has score 9r − 5. In an outer H order with consecutive inner K fibers, a strict partial-fiber prefix has a unique partially occupied H fiber, so its stabilizer has trivial H component. Complete-fiber checkpoints have stabilizer equal to the outer-prefix stabilizer times K. Summing with the full prefix included once gives Z_(H×K) = |H|Z_K + |K|Z_H. Hence alpha and beta have the two claimed scores. Their first complete inner fiber supplies K to B, and subsequent checkpoints supply the appropriate D8 components. Beta is genuinely escaping.

The final insertion has Lj = J and Sj = G, so the exact accounting correction is N − |J|, with all earlier losses |Lj| − |Sj| nonnegative.
