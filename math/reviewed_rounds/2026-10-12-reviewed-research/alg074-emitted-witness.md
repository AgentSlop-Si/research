## Why complete basis replay is conclusive here

The emitted format is an explicitly parsed finite list of distinct, in-range register-pair XORs. Each instruction is linear over F2, with no branch, oracle, data-dependent address, nonlinear arithmetic or hidden initialization. Therefore evaluating a basis determines the whole map. The checked target is the identity on x and z with y replaced by y+x; reversing operation order and transposing the elementary instructions gives the opposite shear. All x, y and z coordinates are included, so zero-initialized auxiliaries are not assumed. This proof does not transport the F2 payload calculation to complex arithmetic or identify it with the rational address field.

## Deterministic rational lifting

The fixed address projector is obtained from B(B^T G B)^-1 B^T G, conjugated by I+J. For nested transitions it is an integer 0/1 diagonal mask plus a correction of rank at most 2, 3 or 4. Same-core c=1 differences have correction column space in the span of w and the outside-indicator difference. Same-core c=2 differences lose their common constant rank-one term. Source growth and c=2 to c=1 growth are bounded by adding individual correction ranks. Single/complement cases have correction rank at most two.

Each constituent denominator is 3(h+1)d with d<=h-1, including source d=2. A common denominator D0 obeys D0<=D=3(h+1)(h-1)^2 and its multipliers are at most h-1. With B0=(4h-2)(3h-7)+27(h+1) and B=2(h-1)B0, correction numerators are bounded by B. Expanding any square minor by correction columns leaves at most r such columns. Thus D0^r times the minor is an integer with absolute value at most sum_{j=0}^r binom(h,j) j! B^j D^(r-j). All diagonal entries used in that expansion are 0/1 and the bound is independent of minor size.

The rank-two bounds are 38,218,181,861,376 and 75,405,344,477,184, both below 2^61-1. The rank-three/rank-four bounds are below the product of the three proven, distinct primes 2^61-1, 2^31-1 and 2^19-1. All denominators are invertible. Therefore a minor vanishing modulo every selected prime is exactly zero; a nonzero residue proves rational nonvanishing. Taking the maximum modular northeast-corner rank consequently recovers its rational rank. Finite-difference pivot extraction and only consecutive increasing row/column runs reproduce the supplied profiles without gathering separated pivots.
