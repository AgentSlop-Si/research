## Mathematical audit

### Domain and all denominators

For each chart pair (a,b), the reviewer reconstructs the weight as

    ((a^2-b^2) + 2ab i)/(a^2+b^2).

Every pair denominator is strictly positive and all 48 resulting weights have
exact norm one. Stage-1 edges 8,9,12 are respectively 5->0, 0->4, and 6->2.
Their gains are multiplied by x,y,z; this preserves the unit-gain domain when
|x|=|y|=|z|=1. Exactly 31 other free phases and 14 tree edges remain frozen.

For each Hamiltonian cycle W, let h be its frozen holonomy and m its moving
phase monomial. Direct elimination proves det(I-W)=d=1-hm. The reviewer
reconstructs all six d and matches every coefficient in ALL_SIX_GUARDS.json.
Five guards are nonzero constants. The active guard is

    F=1-hxyz,
    h=(-383187819337037874577+235291331868842780736 i)
      /449660889716782337425.

The direct cycle-path numerator B obeys both (I-W)B=dI and B(I-W)=dI.
Consequently B/d is precisely the ordinary inverse wherever d is nonzero.
No omitted inverse denominator or entry division is used.

Importantly, F is not asserted to be nonzero everywhere on the unit torus.
It is nonzero on the admissible region because it is the required active
inverse guard, and this is also exactly det(I-W_11). The point

of a cleared polynomial at such a pole is used as an inverse or feasible point.

### Necessary Fourier defects without entry divisions

Every matrix externally equivalent to F8 has rank-one squared entry moduli:
row and column permutations preserve the Fourier modulus, while invertible
diagonal scalings separate into positive row and column factors. Hence all
49 anchored defects, for 1<=r,c<=7, must vanish:

    D_rc=|M_rc M_00|^2-|M_r0 M_0c|^2.

This condition is necessary only. It is used exclusively for exclusion and
does not pretend to characterize Fourier equivalence.

Direct inversion of the full product reconstructs N=F*M over the rational
function field. Every entry is polynomial and triaffine. With

    Ntilde(x,y,z)=xyz * conjugate(N)(1/x,1/y,1/z),
    P_rc=N_rc N_00 Ntilde_rc Ntilde_00
         -N_r0 N_0c Ntilde_r0 Ntilde_0c,

the coefficient conjugation is essential and has been checked. On the actual
unit torus, reciprocal variables implement complex conjugation, so

    P_rc=(xyz)^2 |F|^4 D_rc.

This factor is nonzero at every admissible point. There is no relaxation of
the torus to an algebraic closure for the implication from actual matrices,
nor any inference of feasibility from necessary polynomial equations.

### Complete characteristic-zero identity

Independent polynomial Euclidean division verifies P_rc=F Q_rc for all 49
windows and multiplies every quotient back. All 2,058 nonzero stored quotient
coefficients agree with the fresh reconstruction, and all 2,156 entries of
the 49-by-44 union-support grid, including zeros, are checked.

The 42 nonzero constant Gaussian-rational multipliers in CERTIFICATE.json
have a common positive 3,584-digit integer denominator. The reviewer reads
every integer literally and verifies the two identities independently:

    sum_j c_j Q_j = F,
    sum_j c_j P_j = F^2.

The complete Q union support has 44 monomials; the P union support has 63.
Both identities use complete polynomial accumulation, so coefficients
outside those supports are also identically zero. The equations at exponents
(3,3,2) and (3,3,3), omitted when the author chose a square solve, are included
in the final check. No finite-field rank or sampled identity is promoted to
characteristic-zero ideal membership.

If an admissible Fourier-equivalent point existed, every P_j would vanish,
and the checked identity would give F^2=0. This contradicts F!=0. This proves
the asserted exclusion. Equivalently, the guarded saturation is the unit
ideal, but the argument never substitutes a relaxed closure point into the
original rational model.
