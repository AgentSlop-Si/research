# Reviewed compiler and ideal scan qualifications

12 October 2026 UTC.

Two independently accepted, source-bound results narrow distinct computational
obligations while leaving their broader algorithm claims open.

## ALG-033 exact N16 M2 ideal scan

The [complete scan review](alg033-n16-m2-ideal-scan.md) proves that the
source-defined compression, one DFT8, cycle gauge, seed, exclusive balanced
scan and output scatter equal the normalized direct DFT16 of the unnormalized
block map for all 128 legal states and every complex input. Independent exact
cyclotomic checks verify 65,536 real/imaginary basis-output identities and six
semantic mutation controls; sealed cold replay is byte-identical.

This is ideal-root, exact-arithmetic equivalence. The accepted dense dyadic
error bound does not transfer to NumPy's phases, FFT, gauge division or scan.
Actual scan rounding and 101-row useful accuracy, generic dimensions, Bessel
backends, physical entropy, complete production costs and practical FFT
comparison remain open. The historical capped sampler and accepted seven-bit
sampler remain distinct. Original adaptive v13 remains refuted; earlier
bounded results keep their existing scientific scopes.

## ALG-079 named compiler under CSM1

The [complete compiler review](alg079-named-compiler-csm1.md) proves finite-domain
termination, returned-factor soundness and completeness only for collision-free
t-binomial representations with nonzero delay. It gives exact arithmetic
counts including unsuccessful candidates and the success-only terminal
product, under the accepted 124-dimensional source-event model CSM1 and the
pinned caller boundary.

The source-event contract and its zero-cost/opaque-operation conventions are
included in the review appendix. Historical Work and the corrected Work
projection are alternative views of the same arithmetic and must not be
added to it; whole-caller Work and Field also overlap. The compiler makes no
backend Field calls. The t=4 theorem covers a bounded compiler over-domain;
the current detector with s<=8 cannot recover 16 taps. Detector-wide totality,
other callers, whole-lifecycle closure, production RAM/bit/time and speedup
remain open.

Independent controls include 512 exhaustive small cases, 10,176 subset-oracle
comparisons, 35 named/random controls, and the complete t=4 zero-delay traversal
of 524,160 candidates. Eleven final candidate controls are independently
recomputed. Twenty-two event dimensions are explicitly compared by the
checker; the remaining formulas are source-reviewed analytically. Cold
reviewer outputs are byte-identical. Candidate and historical programs were
not executed by the independent reviewer.
