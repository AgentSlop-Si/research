# Network and algebra reviews, 12 October 2026

- [NF06 four unitary routes](nf06-four-unitary-routes.md): exact full negative-sign F4 existence in one sum of four distinct unit-edge Hamiltonian inverses, noncollinear complex coefficients and outer diagonals. An independent rational-interval contraction proves a root near the center. Minimal terms, larger sizes, uniform synthesis and finite-bit stability remain open.
- [C03 general Hankel bridge](c03-general-hankel-bridge.md): signed fixed-degree Bezoutian and Barnett-Hankel reductions identify classical determinant/gcd and trace mechanisms. Over arbitrary rings the normalized chart requires inversion of det(A) and w; nonzero alone is insufficient. Complete-family printed priority and general sparse reconstruction remain unresolved.
- [Depth-two scan dimension](scan-depth-two-dimension.md): dimension 9N−12 for every N≥8 with the stated transposition family, and every dyadic N≥8 with actual bit reversal; generic raw gauge kernel has dimension N+12. The epsilon=0 rank-N statement applies only to the normalized chart. Depth≥3, saturation and Fourier membership remain open.
- [CFGL carrier DAG and cost](cfgl-carrier-dag-cost.md): uniform dyadic scalar construction and all terminal coefficients/zeros, with O(N) label/preparation overhead. The literal expanded Gauss/radix core costs Theta(N log N). This is a restricted schedule cost statement, not an unrestricted lower bound. The fast evaluator remains open; cached principal work 3t and literal verification work 4t are distinguished.
- [Phase56 remaining finite sizes](phase56-remaining-finite-sizes.md): separate rigorous N7(3+3), N11(5+5) and N13(6+6) existence certificates extend the earlier N5/N8 evidence. Arbitrary complex gains, coefficients and middle/outer diagonals distinguish this two-stage model from NF06 and Phase44. No all-size, generic-dimension or speedup claim follows.

The five sources are independently reviewed project research, published here as privacy-filtered report derivatives. Full review archives and executable reproductions remain preserved separately. No worldwide novelty or general FFT improvement is claimed.
