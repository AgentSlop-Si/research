## Exact proof audit

The accepted source identity is B_ij=rho[omega^(ij)-G omega^(-h(i+j)^2)+NG omega^(-h i^2)delta_ij], with G^2=N>1. Cancellation in an off-diagonal entry would make G a root of unity. This contradicts G^2=N. Every off-class target block is therefore entrywise nonzero; cancellation inside the two target kernels leaves no support exceptions. The diagonal nonvanishing claim follows independently from N sqrt(N)>1+sqrt(N).

Matching confinement is required for the effective weighted matrix on the fixed coordinate classes, and is not inferred merely from a Hamiltonian structural successor or from the existence of some zero edges. Inverse and diagonal endpoints preserve its matching-part block support. Matchings may vary by branch; the underlying residue classes may not. An absent class pair cannot be generated by summand cancellation. The optional block-diagonal correction has no off-class support and only enlarges the lower-bound model.

The broad matching count s(s-1)/2 divided by floor(s/2) gives s for odd s. Neither endpoint invertibility nor positivity is used. The accepted diagonal calibration may be counted as a legal branch, while omitting all diagonal-only terms from the lower-bound count cannot improve the ability to cover off-class pairs.

For the narrow rank theorem, the two Fourier character factors on S_a-by-S_b have ratio exponents a+2b and 2a+b modulo q. The block is rank two precisely when both are nonzero; otherwise it is rank one, not zero. At a=0, only the four different labels q,2q,3q,4q give rank one. All remaining s-5 neighbors give rank two. Both orientations yield total incident rank demand 2[4+2(s-5)]=4s-12, and one narrow branch can pay at most one unit of this demand. Rank subadditivity is unaffected by cancellation.

For q divisible by 3 the aggregate rank-one ordered-pair count 50q-90 and total directed rank demand 50q^2-60q+90 are correct. Their count lower bound 20q-19 is weaker than the single-vertex demand 20q-12. These family-specific aggregate formulas are not extended to q=1.
