# Reviewed b=19 unramified middle overlap

12 October 2026 UTC.

[Independent review and proof](rs-b19-unramified-middle.md).

For the Rogers–Szegő parameter b=19, all 37 odd orders 41<=d<=149 with 3 not dividing d are accepted, for every primitive order-d root in characteristic zero and every integer A>=1. The original overlap polynomial has degree A+1; b is not its polynomial degree. The proof preserves the original tau=dW/g and all product denominators, and applies the already accepted sufficient good-prime criterion.

One good prime is enough. The quotient good-factor product can be reducible; its unit checks do not assume that quotient is a field. At d=121, the degree-five factor X^5+X^4+X²+2X+2 gives negative valuation of the original tau, whereas the degree 105 complementary factor product supplies good primes. This disproves every-prime-good wording, not characteristic-zero discriminant nonvanishing. Whole-polynomial squarefree reduction is not asserted.

The 18 ramified middle orders remain: 45,51,57,63,69,75,81,87,93,99,105,111,117,123,129,135,141,147. Full b=19 and the broader F020 problem remain open. Ordinary residual squarefreeness, higher overlap, even/nonprimitive/positive-characteristic cases, quantitative conditioning and historical priority retain their separate boundaries.

The reviewer independently rebuilt 3672 exact checks and rejected 27 negative controls; a clean extracted replay is byte-identical. Classical Hermite/Carlitz/Grosu–Grosu/Sawa–Uchida ingredients remain credited. This is a bounded application, not a new general criterion, a worldwide-priority finding or a general closure.
