1. Author and freeze a concrete outer coefficient wrapper, exact enumerator,
   signed_power implementation, fixture and launcher. Preserve the pinned Scalar
   bytes or review every modified dependency. Validate exact built-in types,
   header equality, ordered unique support, guard behavior and grid parameters.
2. Implement the run-binding adapter against the real accepted sparse-output API.
   A 32-byte echo is only an association label. Bind the immutable header and
   support to the concrete accepted output; reject mixed or substituted records.
   Pay any sorting/canonicalization or metadata hashing actually performed.
3. Review the complete production source for hidden N/M loops or allocations,
   cached phase tables, dense scans, repeated preparation and uncharged work.
   A successful fast large-N call alone does not prove this structural property.
4. At D=5, m=57, s=285, run the actual enumerator on sorted
   {0,1710,2^55+1,2^57-1}. Check every emitted incidence exactly. In the bounded
   regression evaluate only (0,0), (2^53,2^55+1), and (0,1710), with indices
   ordered here as (ell,k). Clearly distinguish this selected evaluation scope
   from a full four-support coefficient stream.
5. Exercise the actual production signed_power at K=623 on [-1/2,-1/4]^285.
   Its K-grid endpoint numerators must be -2^338 and -2^53. The historical
   even-only helper and absolute-power substitutions must fail this control.
   Do not promote the tiny negative-ratio coefficient sign witness into an
   unproved eta-violating final-output example.
6. Verify zero-limit handling, minimum nonzero residue 1, positive denominator
   lower bounds, J=587, K=623, p=459, exact nearest-even ties, and each integer
   squared-radius certificate. A certificate failure is an implementation defect,
   not the theorem's allowed random recovery failure.

   rational oracle using a different pi identity and independent error control.
   Do not reuse production Scalar output as the oracle. Compare guaranteed
   enclosures or make an equally rigorous exact certificate comparison. Floating

8. Include empty support and verify zero pi/sqrt setup while header work remains
   charged. Add exact guard/failure and malformed-prefix checks as appropriate.
   Charge endpoint work, copies, reductions, powers, shifts, divisions, all
   scalar/factorial loops, certificates, streamed outputs and control/width work.
   Do not reset the ledger on repeated validation or preparation.
9. Freeze pre/post source hashes, declare finite resource caps and retain actual

10. Separately complete full stream/scatter integration, every-tape estimate
