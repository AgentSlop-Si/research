The independent controls contain 268 passing exact checks. They cover the residual identities and resul-
tants, the nonreality obstruction and exhaustive small-totient list, eleven original-product normalizations
and squared-distance norms, every sign certificate, and the order-fifteen parameter norm and all fifteen

Accepted scope. This adds exactly 𝑏 = 5, odd 𝑑 ≥ 7, integer 𝐴 ≥ 1, primitive 𝑞, in characteristic zero,
to the already accepted 𝑏 = 1, 3 subfamilies. It proves the extra assertions on the residual unit circle

characteristic, or uniform estimates with growing degrees. It provides no historical novelty or priority
guarantee.
