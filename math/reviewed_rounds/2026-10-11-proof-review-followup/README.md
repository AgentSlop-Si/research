## CGSF: accepted narrow full-shift commutant obstruction

For R_s similar to the full N-cycle shift, the centralizer is K[R_s], commutative of dimension N. Under characteristic not dividing N, equal-length weighted cycle modules have distinct holonomies and coprime cycle polynomials, so every off-cycle commuting block is zero. The proposed free cross-cycle matrix-algebra mixing therefore needs repair. Actual repeated-holonomy operators do admit the larger mixing algebra, but are not similar to the full shift in the stated domain.
