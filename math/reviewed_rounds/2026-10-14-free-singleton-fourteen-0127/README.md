# Free cross-sign singleton minimum over F23: exact fourteen

14 October 2026. Independent acceptance establishes **b_single(F23)=14** for N=11, q=13 in the original signed resolvent-bank grammar, retaining exactly one effective alpha node in each cross-sign orientation. The two cross-node locations are independently free. The minimum allows arbitrary legal beta, raw phase weights, repeated banks, cancellations and zero-alpha same-sign banks.

The explicit witness has allocation (6,1,1,6), both cross nodes alpha=7, and same-sign supports {6,7,12,13,17,19}; its beta values are all one. This construction choice does not restrict the lower-bound domain.

## What the independent acceptance establishes

- 154 fresh literal branch inversions, 308 inverse identities, 154 reconstructed raw weights and all 121 target entries pass exactly.
- The all-22 nonzero-cross-node quintic census excludes every five-node support. Only y=4 and y=15 have monic solutions; neither splits into five distinct nonzero base-field roots. Reflection applies independently to the other cross node, yielding the matching 6+1+1+6 lower bound.
- Separate enumeration of all 26,334 five-element supports against all 22 parameters gives 579,348 exclusions.
- The complete 11,638-sextic census and a separate enumeration of 74,613 six-element supports over 22 parameters agree on exactly three necessary gates: y=7,10,12, with the source-specified supports. Those three necessary gates are included in this independent verdict.
- Twenty-four mutation controls and cold replay pass.

The equal pair (7,7) is a verified sufficient construction. Full compatibility of the other eight ordered support pairs was not tested. If one cross node is outside {7,10,12}, the necessary bound is at least15; if both are outside, at least16, with no general attainment claim for those pins.

## Domain and historical boundary

The [earlier minimum16](../2026-10-14-pinned-f23-minimum-0057/README.md) fixes both cross nodes at alpha=4. It remains valid in that smaller domain. The [quadratic-field minimum14](../2026-10-14-quadratic-fourteen-0114/README.md) retains those alpha=4 pins over a different field. The [earlier literature comparison](../2026-10-14-signed-bank-literature-0125/REPORT.md) addresses the pinned dictionary and remains a dated comparison.

Removing the singleton requirements themselves changes the architecture. This witness then supplies an upper bound of fourteen, but the matching lower bound is not accepted for unrestricted orientation allocations. Other fields or primitive roots, characteristic-zero transfer, historical novelty, asymptotic claims and FFT-speed improvement are outside this verdict. These are independent finite proof/computational audits, not ordinary journal peer review.
