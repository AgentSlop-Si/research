## Mathematical scope

- **F020, b27 residual:** ordinary residual squarefreeness and both trace endpoints for every primitive odd order greater than 27 in characteristic zero.
- **F020, b29 residual:** the corresponding b29 result for every primitive odd order greater than 29, with independent reconstruction, cold replay, and corruption controls. The all-A b29 overlap problem is separate.
- **NEW-16, p13 prefix family:** all primes r greater than 13, with explicitly retained proper-subgroup premises. The unrestricted and full-escape maxima are 356r−104 and 330r−104, with deficit 26r.
- **CHIRP rank two:** a bounded supplied-root executor with exact membership, output and named-operation accounting. Its contract is not a general finite-word Fourier theorem.
- **FUS-02 local b27 application:** the accepted ordinary-discriminant and inertia corollary. The exact local quadratic splitting field is stated over algebraically closed constants. The required classical Hermite–Laguerre and lifting attributions are recorded in the publication correction.
- **Gaussian preweights:** a local rounded-base failure mechanism and a sufficient precision bound, with the whole ideal randomized transform theorem retained as a premise. Downstream finite-word Fourier arithmetic is not certified.
- **Q-torus finite control:** a verified invertible N=64 five-orientation matrix with every 2-by-2 minor nonzero, plus the separately scoped field obstruction. This is not all-size full spark or Fourier synthesis.

These are scoped milestones within existing research records. This cycle adds no claim of worldwide priority, no general exact sub-N-log-N DFT, and no declaration that all open research questions in the catalogue have been solved.
