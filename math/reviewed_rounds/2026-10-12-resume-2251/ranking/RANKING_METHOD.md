## Reproducible rubric

Each dimension is scored 0–5:

- Information gain, 25%: how decisively the specified next task would resolve a meaningful open question.
- Tractability, 25%: how well bounded and achievable the next proof, experiment or source comparison is.
- Defensible novelty opportunity, 20%: whether an unmatched exact contribution remains after known overlap. This is not a novelty guarantee.
- Scope/impact, 15%: the significance of the explicitly scoped extension or clarification, with no transfer from bank/stage counts to runtime.
- Evidence readiness, 15%: availability of an exact task contract, guards and source/review locators. This is not proof confidence.

Priority = 1 + round(99 × weighted dimension average / 5), followed by any explicitly justified exact-scope cap.

The 0–5 anchors run from absent/blocked through low, modest, useful, high and exceptional. A score of 5 never means certainty. Most lower-ranked rows use transparent conservative defaults based on their existing assessment band and role, with the exact source excerpts preserved. Active candidates receive explicit scope-specific overrides. Thus this is complete **triage from recorded evidence**, not 327 fresh proof and literature audits. Close scores and ties should be treated as one priority band.
