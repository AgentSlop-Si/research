## Exact objective and graph reduction

For an actual labeled permutation, T_j is the nonempty j-element prefix, S_j its left stabilizer, Z=sum_{j=1}^N(|S_j|−1), and B is generated by S_j for j<N. The full prefix is counted exactly once in Z and excluded from B. Full escape means B=G.

Coprime powering splits subgroups into a D8 component and cyclic odd components. The maximal subgroups have orders 884,136,104 and are normal of prime index. A nonempty proper prefix stabilized by one of them forces every proper-prefix stabilizer into it: earlier prefix support in its prime quotient cannot be invariant under a nonidentity quotient translation; later prefixes contain a whole coset, whose orbit under an outside stabilizer would fill the quotient. Hence those maximal stabilizer orders are forbidden for escape.

The graph vertices are (j,d), where 1≤j<N, d>1 divides both j and N, and d is not 884,136,104. Vertex weight is d−1. Equal-position vertices conflict. For i<j, put L=lcm(d,e). The vertices conflict exactly when no multiple m of L lies between i and j, allowing m=i only if L=d and m=j only if L=e.

Soundness uses actual stabilizers, without assuming D8 subgroups nest. If the earlier two-part order is at least the later one, saturate the earlier prefix by the later central odd factor. Otherwise intersect the earlier odd translates of the later prefix. The resulting intermediate set has a free subgroup action of order L, so its size is a multiple of L. Equality at either endpoint would force the corresponding actual stabilizer to have order at least L, yielding precisely the endpoint restrictions. Every escaping order therefore gives an independent set of its exact proper-prefix weight. Converse realizability of arbitrary graph independent sets is unnecessary.

The fresh checker constructs all absolute-position vertices and every pair. It checks conflict decisions twice, using floor/ceiling bounds and separately enumerated allowed common multiples. A forward future-mask dynamic program computes the maximum weighted independent set: skip a vertex or select it and forbid its future neighbors; merge identical future masks by maximum weight. Identical masks have identical legal continuations, so this recurrence is exact. All rewards and masks are exact Python integers.
