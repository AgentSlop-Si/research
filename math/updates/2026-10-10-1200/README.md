## Main changes

- [Complete Schur extensions](notes/01_schur_extensions.md): the accepted treewidth bound improves to ceil(N/4)-1. Quadratic-size planar realizations exist, so unrestricted minor-free impossibility is false.
- [Sparse prefix endpoints](notes/02_sparse_prefix_bounds.md): the endpoint lower bound survives. Open/reset and closed cyclic tours have different exact minima; the near-linear matching upper construction remains conjectural.
- [Relative cyclotomic rank](notes/03_relative_field_rank.md): the sharp rank theorem survives over the stated relative field. Its bypass is generally E-valued. The linear gcd-orbit evaluator is a classical component, not a full DFT.
- [N=5 four-bank exclusion](notes/04_n5_four_bank_exclusion.md): independently accepted in characteristic zero. At least five banks are necessary; five-bank sufficiency remains unproved.
- [Even chirps and Green weights](notes/05_even_chirp_and_hankel.md): accepted coefficient/parity and Hankel identities, corrected dense count and explicit quadratic fallback. Classical finite-chirp antecedents are identified.
- [Finite-edit Hessians](notes/07_finite_edit_hessian.md): accepted basis/channel identities and exact certificates. Finite entrywise normalization needs a nonzero bridge, beyond existence of the Green inverses.

[Historical reconciliation](notes/06_checkpoint_reconciliation.md) carries forward the accepted signed-magnetic, fixed-base augmented-content and two manuscript results. The endpoint/discriminant prior-art addenda already in the earlier snapshot are not duplicated.

## Scope

A finite control is not an all-size proof; a scoped accepted proof is not publication-priority clearance.
