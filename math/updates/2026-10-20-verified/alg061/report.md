## 3. Frozen ALG-061 interface

Let p be an odd prime, N divide p−1 and ω be a supplied primitive Nth root. Write R=F_N r and T_k=1/R_k where the probes are nonzero. For m=s+1 define

P_e(a,w)=det[T_(e+i+j)(1−a w^(e+i+j))]_(i,j=0,…,s), e=0,1.

With the fixed formal degree-m homogeneous Sylvester convention,

Res_a(P_0,P_1)=w^v(w−1)^(m²)U(w),

v=s(s+1)(4s+5)/6, b=s(s+1)(2s+1)/6,

U(w)=w^b V(w+w^(−1)), deg V≤b.

The universal polynomial identity survives degree drops and even identically zero specializations. **Nonzero U is a separate recognition gate**, not a universal conclusion for every input. Divisibility orders can increase after specialization.

ALG-061 removes every t−2 factor from nonzero V to get V*. Then

deg gcd(V*,D_N−2)=0

is equivalent to absence of a nonidentity subgroup root of U. A positive-degree gcd supplies candidates only. The tested candidate must still pass gamma-gcd/Prony guards, have A nonzero at every subgroup point, and satisfy

A(S)r=e_0−a e_δ

at all N coordinates before accepting R_k=(1−aω^(δk))/A(ω^k).

The current short-probe route uses indices 0,…,2s+1: exactly 2s+2 probes, with 1≤s≤8, N≥2s+2, p>4b+20. It continues after failed candidate certificates. μ̃_j(σ)=Σ_(i=0)^floor(j/2) j! m_(j−2i) σ^(2i) / [(-2)^i i!(j−2i)!].

### Exact explanatory matrix map

This map is elementary algebra used to compare interfaces, not a new accepted theorem. Put C_j=T_j(1−a w^j). Let J reverse s+1 columns. Then

(H_e J)_(i,j)=C_(e+s+i−j),

so each ALG-061 Hankel determinant is, up to sign (-1)^[s(s+1)/2], a Toeplitz determinant. If the candidate C_j=A(ω^j)=Σ_l c_l z_l^j has at most s modes, then

H_e=V diag(c_l z_l^e)V^T

has rank at most s. This explains the exact classical rank ingredient. It neither identifies (a,w) from raw r nor proves that every simultaneous pencil root is a valid global sparse-rational representation.
