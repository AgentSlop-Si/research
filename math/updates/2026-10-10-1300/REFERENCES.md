# Primary comparisons and scope

These references are part of the focused scientific comparison in the underlying accepted notes. No publisher PDFs are bundled. Classical ingredients do not establish historical priority for the precise specializations here.

## Necklace and derivative mechanisms

- Ruskey, Savage and Wang, necklace generation: https://webhome.cs.uvic.ca/~ruskey/Publications/GenNecklaces/GenNecklaces.html. Constant-average-time generation is classical; copying and addressing still need charging.
- Harrison and Hudgins, binary de Bruijn quantum-graph orbit structure: https://arxiv.org/html/2101.00006v3. Closed cycles modulo rotation and orbit-amplitude products overlap; the paper does not construct the required Fourier congruence.
- Pearlmutter, exact Hessian-vector products: https://www.bcl.hamilton.ie/~barak/papers/nc-hessian.pdf. The differentiation mechanism is classical.
- Boito and Eidelman, Green matrices: https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.02701. Relevant quasiseparable/inversion background, not a proof of the proposed four-cycle Fourier transport.

## Unitary resolvents, cross-ratios and filtering

- Del Corso, Poloni, Robol and Vandebril, When is a matrix unitary or Hermitian plus low rank?: https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.05854. Classical Cayley-transform correspondence; the inverse domain excludes eigenvalue 1.
- Ostergard and Valtonen, Equivalence of Complex Hadamard Matrices: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21653. Haagerup four-entry invariants and monomial equivalence are classical mechanisms.
- Rajchel, Gasiorowski and Zyczkowski, Robust Hadamard matrices: https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.10715. A close obstruction with a particular paired phase, not an identified proof of the entire present general-phase resolvent statement.
- Keith Conrad, Characters of Finite Abelian Groups (Short Version): https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/grouptheory/charthyshort.pdf. Finite-character orthogonality underlies the exact ternary-phase filter.

## Schur and total nonsingularity

- Woerdeman, A matrix and its inverse: revisiting minimal rank completions: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0608130. The partial triangular rank formula supplies classical completion context; the notes separately prove the residual pivot conditions.
- Frenkel, Simple proof of Chebotarev theorem on roots of unity: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0312398. Prime-order complex Fourier is totally nonsingular. This is not an automatic finite-field theorem.
