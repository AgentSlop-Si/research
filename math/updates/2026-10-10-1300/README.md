# Three-family Fourier research supplement

10 October 2026, 13:00 dated supplement.

This is the final three-family preservation packet from the bounded 12:25 source intake. It contains scientific derivations, corrected claims, exact certificates and independent portable verifiers. Two bounded proof refinements have received separate hash-scoped acceptance. Original source archives, private conversation material, internal reviews, execution logs, publisher PDFs and spreadsheet edit plans are not distributed here.

## Main results

1. [Necklace transport](notes/01_necklace_transport.md): the least-period trace identity, short-orbit evaluator, sparse Gram/Hessian formula and bounded-locality obstruction are accepted. A uniform inexpensive Fourier-compatible transport is still missing. Q31 remains constructively open. The canonical ordinary-trace Hessian vanishes when the field characteristic divides its horizon k.
2. [Unitary exclusion](notes/02_unitary_exclusion.md): every N>=4 excludes a finite real-collinear sum of nonsingular unitary Hamiltonian-cycle Green resolvents, even up to invertible monomial Fourier equivalence. [Phase averaging](notes/03_phase_averaging_construction.md), with a [complete independent proof](notes/05_phase_averaging_proof.md), gives complex-span universality and actual real N=2 and N=3 exceptions. The complex construction is finite and can use 3^(N-1) terms; it supplies no fast Fourier algorithm.
3. [Schur interfaces](notes/04_schur_interface.md): direct-coordinate reverse-order rigidity, the necessary LIS gate and properly scoped Schur rank bounds are accepted. The [independent completion proofs](notes/06_schur_completion_proofs.md) establish a rank-floor(N/2) correction with an invertible triangular residual for every totally nonsingular target, the singular-pivot one-sided extension, and exact counterexamples to broader extrapolations. Composite Fourier and finite-field full-spark hypotheses must be treated separately.
