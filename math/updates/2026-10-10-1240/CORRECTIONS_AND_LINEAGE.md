- Prime/root search, primality certification, numerical stability, bit complexity, prior priority and unrestricted O(N) Fourier remain outside the claim.

## HERO1 Phase33 modal and projected Fourier

Source archive SHA-256: d00b64d6cc6fad64a0d839d9efb789fbab681a80701c97fda18ccec3e24858fb.

- The output is the complete length-N projected vector, with rank 2^C+(t−C)2^(C−1), not all independent Fourier information. For C<t, a nonzero input may lie entirely in the discarded complement.
- The complex norm identity is classical Parseval applied to a commuting projector. Its finite-field bilinear analogue is not a positive norm certificate; a zero scalar can coexist with nonzero error.
- Preserve setup qualifications: uncharged literal sign negations, root validation outside the printed counter, identity-multiplier conventions, prime-field assumptions and I/O/index work. A witness metadata object does not itself evaluate complex residual energy.
- The supplied projected evaluator has Θ(N+(t−C+1)4^C) work. This is a valid upper bound, not an optimal dependence on C. The separate additive-shell comparison uses short ordinary FFTs and geometric sums; its exact acceptance state is recorded in PROOF_STATUS.json.
- Full-transform N=960 costs 17,432 versus 13,639 for the Phase29 reference. Two character-basis changes still cost real work. Larger unexecuted comparison rows remain extrapolations.
- Multiplicative character decompositions, Ramanujan transforms and Gauss identities have direct classical antecedents. Exact specialized priority and practical floating-point speed remain unestablished.
