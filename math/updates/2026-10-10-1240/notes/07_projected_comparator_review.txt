Independent proof and control verdict: dyadic projected additive-FFT comparator
10 October 2026

VERDICT: ACCEPT, WITH THE EXPLICIT SCOPE BELOW

The frozen reviewer-derived comparator is correct for N=2^t, t>=1 and
1<=C<=t. It computes every entry of P_C F_N x = F_N P_C x for arbitrary
dense input, with total O(N+(t-C+1)C 2^C) field/word-RAM work including
root-power preparation, optional validation of a supplied root, and full
readout. A primitive 2^C-th root suffices. No primitive Nth root is required
in the computation. No unrestricted sub-N log N FFT improvement follows.

This accepts a separately derived comparison construction, not a claim that
the submitted Phase33 source implements it. The source's evaluator remains
a different direct-character-table method. Historical priority, optimality,
floating-point stability, bit complexity, and best practical constants are
not established.

1. Precise output, conductor, and field contract

For 1<=C<=t let H_C consist of the units a modulo N with a=1 modulo 2^C.
P_C averages coordinate permutations induced by multiplication by H_C.
Over the complex numbers it is an orthogonal projector. A multiplier
permutation intertwines F_N with its inverse permutation, and inversion
preserves H_C, so P_C commutes with F_N. Individual multipliers need not
commute with F_N.

On shell j, whose indices are 2^(t-j)u for odd u modulo 2^j, the image of
H_C is the kernel of reduction to the units modulo 2^min(C,j). Therefore
the invariant multiplicative characters are exactly those factoring through
that quotient. Equivalently, their primitive multiplicative conductor is
at most 2^C. This is multiplicative conductor, not an additive low-frequency
cutoff. The principal character has conductor 1; there is no nonprincipal
character of conductor 2.

Set m_j=min(C,j), q_j=2^m_j, L_j=2^(j-m_j). The shell's orbits consist of
the units r+q_j h, 0<=h<L_j, for odd r modulo q_j. Zero is a singleton.
Consequently

rank(P_C) = 1 + sum_(j=1)^t 2^(min(C,j)-1)
          = 2^C + (t-C)2^(C-1).

The algorithm works over a field K of characteristic other than 2 containing
the input and a specified primitive 2^C-th root omega. The projected matrix
has entries in the integer cyclotomic ring Z[omega], as the coefficient proof
below shows. In positive characteristic, "reduction" should be read as this
integer-cyclotomic specialization, not as a field homomorphism from Q.

If K contains a compatible primitive Nth root zeta, meaning
zeta^(N/2^C)=omega, the result is F_(N,zeta)P_C x in K. Otherwise adjoining
such a root gives the same identity in an extension, and the result lies
back in K. This is stronger and more precise than pretending that a full
N-point DFT exists in a base field lacking its root. Characteristic 2 is
excluded: the required roots and invertible averaging sizes are absent.

No large multiplicative-character basis, Gauss sums, character roots, or
field inversions are needed by the comparator itself.

2. Independent coefficient derivation

Let B_j(r) be the sum of the original inputs over an orbit, and let B_j(r)=0
at even residues modulo q_j. After projection the input at every member of
that orbit is B_j(r)/L_j. In a splitting field its Fourier contribution is

(B_j(r)/L_j) zeta_(2^j)^(k r)
  * sum_(h=0)^(L_j-1) zeta_(L_j)^(k h).

The geometric sum is zero unless L_j divides k; if it divides k, the sum is
L_j. The denominator cancels exactly. Thus the entire shell contributes

1_(L_j divides k) A_j((k/L_j) mod q_j),

where A_j is the positive-sign, unnormalized ordinary q_j-point DFT of B_j.
Its root is omega^(2^(C-m_j)). Each original input coefficient is therefore
either zero or a power of a q_j-th root. This proves the root contract and
all cancellations for every input, rather than only for invariant inputs.

3. Check of the frozen note's exact reconstruction equation

Write a nonzero output as k=2^(t-ell)v with v odd. A shell contributes
precisely when j-min(j,C)<=t-ell. If j<=t-ell, the DFT table index is zero.
For j>t-ell put s=j+ell-t. The remaining conditions are exactly
1<=s<=min(ell,C), and its table index is

2^(min(t-ell+s,C)-s) v modulo 2^min(t-ell+s,C).

With Q_a=x_0+sum_(j=1)^a A_j(0), this gives

y_(2^(t-ell)v) = Q_(t-ell)
  + sum_(s=1)^min(ell,C)
      A_(t-ell+s)(2^(min(t-ell+s,C)-s) v
                  mod 2^min(t-ell+s,C)).

The zero-frequency result is Q_t. Changing v by 2^min(ell,C) changes every
table index by a multiple of its table length, so each representative can
be scattered to its whole output orbit. This verifies the frozen note's
equation, including its normalization, sign, and output ordering.

4. Complete arithmetic and access ledger

The dense input shells partition the N-1 nonzero positions. Enumeration by
shell and odd unit takes N input reads including x_0, at most N-1 bucket
additions, and O(N) word operations. It needs no repeated N-entry scan and
no uncharged discrete logarithm or multiplicative-group enumeration.

Put S=sum_(j=1)^t min(C,j)2^min(C,j). Then

sum_(j=1)^t q_j = (t-C+2)2^C-2,
S = 2^C [C(t-C+2)-2]+2
  = Theta((t-C+1)C 2^C).

Every short FFT is paid for. A radix-2 transform of size 2^m uses m2^m
additions/subtractions and at most m2^(m-1) multiplications, even if identity
twiddles are charged. The usual omission of the first twiddle in each block
gives m2^(m-1)-2^m+1 nonidentity multiplications. Neither count assumes a
free Fourier oracle.

A shared length-2^C power table uses at most 2^C-1 multiplications. All shorter
twiddles are indexed subsequences. Bit reversal costs O(q_j) per transform
when generated on demand, or O(2^C) total stored word preparation for shared
length-specific tables. Root validation by repeated squaring costs O(C).
Thus the root preparation and validation are inside the asserted bound.

The frozen reconstruction forms t prefixes, then evaluates rank(P_C)-1
representatives with at most C additions and C indexed table reads each.
All N outputs are explicitly written. These costs fit the same bound.
Its stored shell tables total O((t-C+1)2^C) field elements/words, in addition
to explicit input/output. This is the accepted space bound in the note.

Independent reconstruction cross-check and tighter workspace option

The same shell identity admits a separate nested-support implementation:

- For j=1,...,C form T_j[k]=T_(j-1)[k mod 2^(j-1)]+A_j[k], with T_0=[x_0].
- Initialize y[k]=T_C[k mod 2^C] at every output.
- For each j>C, add A_j[q mod 2^C] only to y[2^(j-C)q].

The low-table merges cost 2^(C+1)-2 additions. The high-shell additions
total sum_(j=C+1)^t N/2^(j-C)=N-2^C. Hence reconstruction itself can be
O(N), with O(2^C) auxiliary workspace beyond the input and output, processing
one shell at a time. This independently derived implementation corroborates
the same theorem; it is not needed to repair the frozen reconstruction.

The fresh streaming code deliberately charges identity multiplications and
initial additions into zero. Its exact online field-operation ledger is

(N-1) + (3/2)S + (2^(C+1)-2) + (N-2^C)
  = 2N+2^C-3+(3/2)S.

The recorded ledger is an explicit implementation count, not a best constant.
It excludes optional root validation from the online count and records the
root-table preparation separately. Both are included in the theorem's total.

5. Root supply, endpoints, and limits

The primitive root is a supplied input, not the result of a free root search.
Its order is certified by omega^(2^C)=1 and omega^(2^(C-1))!=1. The field
and its arithmetic must already be available. Field construction, primality
certification, discovery of a root, and arbitrary-precision bit costs are
separate problems. Starting from a supplied Nth root adds t-C squarings,
which are absorbed by O(N). At C=1 the root is the universal constant -1,
so there is no root search at all.

C=1: all odd multipliers are averaged; the rank is t+1. The method is
linear-time but does not improve the source's optimized C=1 scalar constant.

C=t: H_C is trivial, the rank is N, and the ordinary full DFT is obtained.
The work is O(N log N). The deliberately simple fresh streaming code takes
3tN online field operations when identity twiddles are counted; this is no
new unrestricted FFT complexity improvement.

N=2, C=1: the formula gives y=(x_0+x_1, x_0-x_1), as required.

C=0: this is explicitly outside the frozen note's contract. A consistent
optional extension sets H_0=H_1 and P_0=P_1, because all units are odd and
every integer is 1 modulo 1. The rank is t+1, not the displayed C>=1 rank
formula with C=0 substituted. Implement with effective cutoff max(1,C).
Its work is O(N), still consistent with the requested O(N) endpoint.
The controls test this extension separately. N=1, t=C=0 is also checked
as the standalone identity case, outside the frozen t>=1 theorem.

For fixed C the method is O(N), and a growing cutoff stays within that
upper bound when (t-C+1)C2^C=O(N). The output rank remains restricted for
every C<t. A vector with +1 and -1 at two members of one orbit is completely
annihilated by P_C despite having nonzero full DFT. No uniform nontrivial
relative error guarantee follows for arbitrary input.

The comparison improves the C-dependence of the source's direct-character
upper bound O(N+4^C log N), or its sharper implementation-dependent form
Theta(N+(t-C+1)4^C). It does not certify a new historical theorem, a lower
bound, or faster small-C scalar counts than every other evaluator.

The field-operation and word-RAM claims use O(log N)-bit index words. They
do not claim constant-time large-integer field arithmetic. Conjugation,
residual-energy certificates, floating-point precision, and numerical
conditioning are additional tasks and are not silently included.
