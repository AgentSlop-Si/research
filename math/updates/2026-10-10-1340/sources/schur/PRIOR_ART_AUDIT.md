# Prior-art boundary — v31

The following ingredients are classical and are not claimed as new:

- superconcentrators and Pippenger's linear-size construction;
- tight bounded-depth superconcentrator size bounds;
- Menger's theorem;
- Lindstrom--Gessel--Viennot path determinants;
- Chebotarev nonvanishing of prime Fourier minors;
- the general connection between linear-circuit support and disjoint-path connectivity.

Primary references used in the report:

1. N. Pippenger, *Superconcentrators*, SIAM J. Comput. 6 (1977), 298--304, DOI `10.1137/0206022`.
2. N. Alon and P. Pudlak, *Superconcentrators of depths 2 and 3; odd levels help (rarely)*, JCSS 48 (1994), 194--202, DOI `10.1016/S0022-0000(05)80027-3`.
3. J. Radhakrishnan and A. Ta-Shma, *Bounds for Dispersers, Extractors, and Depth-Two Superconcentrators*, SIAM J. Discrete Math. 13 (2000), 2--24, DOI `10.1137/S0895480197329508`.
4. B. Lindstrom, *On the Vector Representations of Induced Matroids*, Bull. London Math. Soc. 5 (1973), 85--90, DOI `10.1112/blms/5.1.85`.

The v31 project contribution is the scoped FFT application:

- identify the sharp v30 Schur correction rectangle as a mandatory half-size superconcentrator transfer;
- translate exact depth bounds into a routing ledger for that interface;
- provide exact finite-field controls showing that superconcentrator weights can realize totally nonsingular matrices;
- isolate Fourier phase compatibility as the remaining obstruction.

This is not a literature-wide novelty review and no priority claim is made. No exact faster FFT, arithmetic-circuit lower bound, fixed-precision theorem or benchmark win is claimed.
