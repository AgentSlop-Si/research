The independently fetched accepted source defines each of L layers as a sum of two weighted Green terms: one with natural cycle Z and one with bit-reversed cycle RZR. Thus L counts paired layers, containing 2L Green terms in total. The source's cross-cut rank cap is L(q/2+m), and the necessary condition is L(q/2+m)>=N/2, equivalently L>=ceil(N/(q+2m)). Three paired layers fail for dyadic N>=128; four paired layers fail for dyadic N>=256. The four-paired-layer case at N=128 is not excluded by this witness.
