## NEW-10

NEW-10 records the same precise result and source P09 as the already-closed Q25 audit. I independently read P09 and the accepted Q25 closure. Thus this is a duplicate supporting view of an accepted result, not a newly discovered external theorem replacement.

[Li–Strang Theorem 1.1](https://journals.uwyo.edu/index.php/ela/article/download/5551/5179/7151) covers complex matrices and all unitarily invariant norms. Apply it to E=UQ−RU and rank-at-most-2k Z=Delta Q−R Delta. P09 already supplies the application-specific norm bounds. Removing 2k singular values bounded by 2 leaves the stated Frobenius tail; its largest squared value is at least the average over N−2k entries. The bound on ||E−Z|| gives the displayed operator denominator. This is a direct tail-average corollary, not a new approximation principle.

Verdict reason: **CLOSED — retired as duplicate supporting Q25/P09 metric refinement.** Keep even s>=4, the s=2 exception, unitary metric versus invertible exact gauges, 2k>=N zero-bound handling and every intermediate inverse-legality condition. The Fourier rank-four spectrum/application calculation and its unresolved exact historical priority remain retained. Q19 and broader gauge questions are not closed.
