# Prefix theorem proof and citation concordance

10 October 2026 UTC. Companion to `MANUSCRIPT.md`.

## Status

The present draft gives complete theorem-specific proofs for finite groups with pairwise permutable subgroups and, separately, all finite prime power groups. It has no identified missing lemma in that scope. It consolidates existing accepted results rather than extending their hypotheses. Historical originality remains unresolved. The main candidate contribution is optimizer necessity and unique top-subgroup recovery, with the count presented as an exact consequence using classical counting principles.

`INPUT_MANIFEST.json` binds every consulted local proof, review, saved-control and priority file. Ten entries are checked against the later publication-readiness snapshot; five further consulted files have current snapshot hashes. A fingerprint proves file identity, not mathematical correctness or priority.
