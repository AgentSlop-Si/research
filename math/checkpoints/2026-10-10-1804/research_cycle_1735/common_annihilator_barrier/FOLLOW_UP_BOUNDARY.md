## A precise limitation of the current axis argument

For m=N−d the proven rank envelope gives only

dim W≥N−5d+12.

The current axis proof prescribes r=(N−1)/2 generic roots, using their ++ and -- columns and the singleton to supply an N-column axis determinant. Its incidence argument requires

dim W−1≥r.

Using the available dimension guarantee, this is ensured by

N−5d+12≥(N+1)/2, equivalently N≥10d−23.

The step-one derivative guard requires N≥10d−13, only ten units stronger. Thus improving the derivative annihilator constants alone does not move this proof to d≈N/5, which would be needed for approximately 8N/5 banks. The axis prescription already limits the guaranteed range to d≈N/10.

This is a limitation of the present sufficient dimension bound and this particular prescribed-root proof. It is not a necessary condition for axis rank, an obstruction to another kernel argument, or a lower bound on bank count.

## Next substantial question

For the monic common-annihilator affine space at a larger d, determine whether at least one N-column minor of the moment-coordinate axis matrix

(p_a, 1, p_[-a]_N), 0≤a≤N−1,

is not identically zero, where p_j is the coefficient vector of t^j modulo g. This should be investigated directly on the lower-dimensional recurrence kernel, together with the monic, nonzero-constant and squarefree conditions. A parameter count alone is insufficient.

A useful exact dual test is available. For a squarefree nonzero-node g, an axis row dependence is a nonzero polynomial

A(t)=Σ_(a=0)^(N−1) lambda_a t^a

satisfying

g divides A(t),
g divides t^N A(1/t)+lambda_0(1−t^N),
A(1)=0.

These conditions are exactly the ++, -- and singleton equations. They give a concrete polynomial route for detecting or excluding axis failure without prescribing half the roots. If g and its reciprocal are coprime and 2m>N−1, any such dependence must have lambda_0≠0; the lambda_0=0 case would make A divisible by both coprime degree-m polynomials, which is impossible at degree≤N−1.

The next task should seek an actual nonvanishing proof or an explicit obstruction in this lower-dimensional family. The present checkpoint makes no 8N/5 claim and does not leave a speculative linear-range computation labeled as proved.
