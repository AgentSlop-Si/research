# Rejected reciprocal-coprime axis shortcut

10 October 2026, 18:11 UTC. This is a rejected research path, not an accepted upper bound. The independently accepted 9N/5 bank theorem is unchanged.

Root proposed that coprimality of g with its reciprocal H=t^m g(1/t), together with m>(N-1)/2, would force full axis rank. Independent worker investigate_direct_axis_guard found an exact counterexample:

    N=5, m=3,
    g=t^3+t^2+t+2,
    H=2t^3+t^2+t+1,
    A=(t-1)g=t^4+t-2.

Here gcd(g,H)=1, A(1)=0, and

    t^5 A(1/t)+A(0)(1-t^5)=A(t),

so the dual axis-dependence conditions hold. Coprimality does not suffice.

The proposed proof had a sign error. If

    H B* - c B(0) S_N = g C

and its reversed identity is

    g B - c B(0) S_N = H C*,

then subtraction gives H(B*+C*)=g(B+C), yielding C=-B under the relevant degree and coprimality assumptions. It does not give C=B. The resulting symmetric Bezout identity can hold; evaluating at1 does not yield a contradiction.

The dependent proposed 7N/4 bank bound is withdrawn. A separate proposed functional-exclusion improvement may still be studied, but supplies no axis guard or improved bank bound by itself. The new worker is preserving the counterexample and deriving a corrected criterion in research_cycle_1810/direct_axis_guard/.
