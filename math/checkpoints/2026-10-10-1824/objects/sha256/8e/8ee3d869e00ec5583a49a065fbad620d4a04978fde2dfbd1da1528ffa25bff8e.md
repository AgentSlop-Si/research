# Priority and manuscript positioning: two accepted theorem packages

10 October 2026. Bounded statement-level primary-literature audit.

## Bottom line

Both theorem packages now have independent correctness acceptances. **Historical priority is unresolved for both.** No genuinely equivalent predecessor or count-preserving immediate application was established in the sources inspected. Search absence is not evidence of novelty, and proof acceptance is not publication acceptance.

The prefix theorem is a clean standalone finite-group extremal statement, with an equality classification and exact enumeration. The 9/5 theorem is a meaningful all-order improvement within a precisely restricted Fourier representation model. Its manuscript significance depends more heavily on explaining why that bank resource is mathematically natural. Neither result establishes faster scalar Fourier computation.

## 1. Prefix stabilizers: what can be claimed

For every finite abelian group G of order m, the maximum over all element orderings of the sum of nontrivial prefix translation-stabilizer orders is

    m sum_p v_p(m)(1-1/p).

The equality statement is substantial: all optimizing orders recursively consist of consecutive cosets of a unique prime-index top subgroup, with independently chosen optimizing internal orders in different cosets. The resulting labeled-order count is

    A(G)=sum_(K maximal in G) [G:K]! A(K)^[G:K].

A fixed subgroup-chain construction alone does not supply either unrestricted optimality or this broader equality/count statement. The maximum depends only on m, but the number of optimizers can depend on the group structure.

### New useful literature connections

- Stanley's connectivity set and Bergeron–Hohlweg–Zabrocki's Coxeter support give an exact reformulation. If Gamma is a labeled regular copy of G in S_m, then Z=sum_(g in Gamma)|C(g)|. Thus the result maximizes the average connectivity-set size at W(m), equivalently minimizes average simple-reflection support at m-1-W(m). This concerns a subgroup's jointly constrained elements, rather than the usual enumeration of individual permutations. [Stanley](https://math.mit.edu/~rstan/papers/decset.pdf); [Bergeron–Hohlweg–Zabrocki](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0509271)
- The expression mW(m)=m Omega(m)-m' is an instance of an established Leibniz-additive arithmetic-function framework. That rewrite is not a separate novelty claim. [Haukkanen–Merikoski–Tossavainen](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1803.06849)
- Minimal logarithmic signatures already use prime-index subgroup chains and transversals. Their objective is the sum of factor-set sizes, rather than the weighted prefixes of every group element. [González Vasco–Rötteler–Steinwandt](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/On-Minimal-Length-Factorizations-of-Finite-Groups.pdf)

Suggested title: **“An extremal theorem for prefix stabilizers in finite abelian groups.”**

Suggested abstract core: “For a finite abelian group we maximize the sum of the translation-stabilizer orders along an ordering's prefix chain. The extremal value depends only on the prime factorization of the group order. We characterize all equality cases by recursive prime-index coset blocks and obtain an exact recurrence for the number of maximizing labeled orders.”

The Fourier application should be a bounded corollary: it optimizes every permutation of the fixed coefficient family in the specified single-chain construction, not all Fourier factorizations or other coefficient families.

## 2. Fourier signed banks: what can be claimed

The accepted theorem gives, for every odd N>=27,

    at most 2N-2 floor((N+13)/10)+2 = 9N/5+O(1)

individual permitted signed Weyl resolvent banks. The common monic polynomial is over K=Q(q), while its distinct nonzero roots and bank weights may require a splitting field of degree at most m!, with m=N-floor((N+13)/10). The coefficient 9/5 is not proved optimal.

### Exact attribution boundaries

- One orientation is already a classical finite-direction simultaneous binary-form/scroll dictionary. [Comon–Qi–Usevich](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.01566)
- The original Ciliberto–Russo source is now inspected, closing the prior audit's access gap. Its scroll formula gives generic rank N-sqrt(2N)+O(1) in one orientation’s ambient space. That component threshold is not directly comparable to a total four-orientation count; it does not supply the coupled Fourier bound or preserve the weighted physical reconstruction automatically. [Proposition 1.14 and Claim 5.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0406494)
- Reversing g to lambda(z)=z^m g(1/z) identifies all full-left recurrence conditions exactly with an unequal-precision simultaneous Padé problem. The linear common-denominator framework is established. [Rosenkilde–Storjohann](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~astorjoh/issac16.pdf)
- Known chirp demodulation and operator-based Prony methods supply relevant shift-annihilation precedents. They assume sparse representability or appropriate sampling rank; they do not prove the Fourier kernel has a simultaneously squarefree, nonzero, axis-compatible polynomial. [Plonka–Stampfer–Keller](https://num.math.uni-goettingen.de/~gplonka/pdfs/general-Prony-revised-sw.pdf)

The model-specific proof content to emphasize is the Fourier row-space envelope, the finite-interval derivative/endpoint-functional exclusions, and the simultaneous root/axis guard argument. The standard interpolation and open-set arguments should receive ordinary attribution rather than be marketed as a new general technique.

Recommended bank-paper hierarchy: sharp fixed universal count 2N-2; uniform target-adaptive Fourier upper bound 9N/5+O(1); exact small-order classifications and special upper witnesses. Keep the universal and target-adaptive quantifiers visibly different. Each bank still contains N inverse branches and N raw phase weights; the theorem does not price scalar arithmetic, root splitting, preparation, memory, or stability.

Suggested title: **“Universal and Fourier synthesis by signed Weyl resolvent banks.”** The two theorem packages can be connected by motivation, but a standalone prefix-stabilizer note is the cleaner presentation if the group theorem otherwise appears as a long detour in the bank paper.
