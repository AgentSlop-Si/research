Frozen review checkpoint: 10 October 2026, 16:56:40 UTC. These are five separately scoped reviews, with source proofs, independent controls, exact or numerical receipts, and original archive identities. Earlier delivered checkpoints remain unchanged and are referenced rather than embedded.

## What passed, and what it means

1. **HERO17: structured chirped q-Green transform.** The recognized single-family theorem and O(dN+N) evaluation bound pass in the exact prime-field model, with d=N/gcd(N,s). At N=177,147 the modeled same-N scalar-operation count is 3,011,573 versus 6,849,772 for the stated baseline. These are family-specific arithmetic counts, not measured runtime or a general-input FFT improvement. The original detector omits the two-seed s=4 option at budgets 2 and 3; it safely falls back. An accepted [standalone correction](independent_chirp_gate_fix_20261010/CORRECTION_PROOF.txt) now enumerates the existing seed-bound candidates exactly. It passed 4,410 independent set-oracle cases, alongside author controls over 66,810 (N,B) pairs and 22,697,210 membership decisions. This engineering helper has not been integrated into the original source. The new family is not a superset of the previous family. Primality is a caller assumption. [Independent report](independent_chirp_audit_20261010/results/AUDIT_REPORT.txt)

2. **Connected Gram and Fourier realification.** The weighted connected-Gram phase-locking lemma passes in the stated real-invertible-stage, one-complex-mask, exactly unitary model. The finite-abelian squared-Frobenius minimum is (N−sqrt(N·|G[2]|))/2. It is an elementary consequence of classical Fourier-norm theory after quotienting by 2-torsion, with substantive prior-art overlap. See [Gilbert–Rzeszotnik (2010)](https://aif.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/aif.2556.pdf). Numerical controls support the proof; they are not exact arithmetic certificates. No global originality claim is established. [Independent report](gram_audit_20261010/results/AUDIT.md)

3. **HERO0: coupled transverse paths.** Both path orientations satisfy the rank-product gate ru≥s−2 in the exactly two-stage, standard-order rook model N=s². The uniform Frobenius lower bound passes with both paths jointly adjustable. Overlapping block partitions allow maximizing their bounds, not adding them. Nonreal-entry budgets are realization-dependent, not operation counts; arbitrary endpoint gauges and orderings are outside this review. [Independent report](audit_transverse_block/HERO0_PHASE23_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md)

4. **v34: fixed-support integral obstruction.** The original seven-nonunit-edge gate passes. A stronger independent extension rules out an integral factorization of unnormalized F11 on this fixed 99-edge support, including unit diagonal/permutation endpoint gauges. All 81,920 restricted-support triangular-minor certificates were checked twice independently. Every algebraic solution on this support must have a pole at the chosen prime above 11. Unrestricted complex coefficients, weights with poles, normalized targets containing poles, and nonunit gauges are outside the obstruction. [Independent report](research_cycle_1635/v34_independent_audit/REPORT.md)

5. **N256: leading block dominance.** The leading 28×28 block is dominant for the specified four-stage polynomial map with affine permutations, including nonsingular-stage witnesses. It does not use the source narrative's Toffoli permutation. The 32×32 rank 906 is pointwise and modular, not a characteristic-zero upper-rank theorem. Full second-order coordinate span excludes affine-linear identities; nonlinear relations remain possible for the 32×32/full map. In contrast, proven dominance excludes polynomial relations among the leading 28×28 coordinates. This is neither a full Fourier factorization nor an asymptotic FFT construction. [Independent report](independent_block_jet_audit_20261010/export_v2/INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md)
