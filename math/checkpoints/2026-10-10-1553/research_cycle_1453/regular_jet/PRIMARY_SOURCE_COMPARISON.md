# Primary-source and project-prior-work comparison

Checked 10 October 2026. This is a bounded relevance/novelty comparison, not a worldwide priority certificate. The new packet claims a scoped obstruction for one explicit attempted construction; it claims no new general differentiation method, necklace generator, Prony method, Fourier upper bound, or universal lower bound.

## 1. Automatic differentiation is inherited

Pearlmutter, *Fast Exact Multiplication by the Hessian*, author manuscript dated 9 June 1993, subsequently Neural Computation 6 (1994), 147–160. The abstract and Sections 2–3 directly derive Hessian-vector evaluation from differentiated gradient evaluation without forming the full matrix. This is precisely the classical mechanism behind the already accepted Q31 implication. Centering a scalar source and applying that mechanism is not a new contribution of this packet. [Author manuscript](https://www.bcl.hamilton.ie/~barak/papers/nc-hessian.pdf)

The packet preserves the original source's Baur–Strassen attribution but does not claim a new inspection of its complete 1983 paper here. The currently reviewed result is the new candidate restriction, not the old derivative-complexity theorem.

## 2. Necklace enumeration and graph trace mechanisms are inherited

Ruskey, Savage and Wang, *Generating Necklaces*, Journal of Algorithms 13 (1992), 414–430. The author publication page states the constant-average-time necklace-generation result. The accepted carrier's additional explicit word copying/addressing is included in its O(2^k) ledger. This packet invokes that already reviewed carrier and does not independently implement or improve FKM enumeration. [Author publication page](https://webhome.cs.uvic.ca/~ruskey/Publications/GenNecklaces/GenNecklaces.html)

Harrison and Hudgins, *Periodic orbit evaluation of a spectral statistic of quantum graphs without the semiclassical limit*, EPL 138 (2022), 30002. Sections 2–4 and the graph definitions were inspected. Rotational orbit classes, products of edge amplitudes, and binary de Bruijn graphs already occur in the paper. Its spectral-statistic purpose does not supply an inexpensive Fourier-compatible transport P or the present two-sided sparse-denominator restriction. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2101.00006v3)

## 3. Green representation and Prony/Jordan ingredients are inherited

Boito and Eidelman, *Computation of quasiseparable representations of Green matrices*, Linear Algebra and its Applications 721 (2025), 47–80. The primary abstract states the band-matrix/Green connection and linear-complexity quasiseparable representation algorithms. Only the abstract was checked in this pass. It supports the established context, not a claim that arbitrary sums with independent cycle orders admit a cheap common denominator or a Fourier congruence. [Primary record/preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.02701)

Peter and Plonka, *A Generalized Prony Method for Reconstruction of Sparse Sums of Eigenfunctions of Linear Operators*, author manuscript dated 10 January 2013, Inverse Problems 29 (2013), 025001. The introduction, Theorem 2.4, its generalized-eigenfunction construction, and the polynomial-times-exponential specialization were inspected. They directly precede the recognition ingredient in the Phase 22 Jordan source. The text's orthogonal-polynomial discussion also includes Hermite expansions. Sparse recovery from a limited number of suitable measurements is not a regular full-input Fourier quadratic-jet circuit at the zero signal. The singular-origin selector example in this packet identifies the extra contract rather than denying the published structured reconstruction theorem. [Author manuscript](https://num.math.uni-goettingen.de/plonka/pdfs/Operator_Prony_revised2.pdf)

## 4. The closest already existing project obstructions

The archived `TWO_CAYLEY_FEEDBACK.md` was read in full. Its Section 5 clears a degree-two common weighted-cycle denominator on ONE side of the feedback transfer. The resulting row and its Fourier image both have support at most three; a support-product inequality gives N<=9. It permits arbitrary cycle order and edge weights, but its transfer itself belongs to the specified common-cycle rational/Cayley form. That is a stronger scope in ordering and a different restriction on the intervening operator.

The present candidate has T=P^T H P with a sparse but otherwise arbitrary H. Even with P rational in the natural S, T generally is not rational in S. Clearing only one side leaves a dense P. The old proof therefore does not simply apply to this candidate. The new calculation clears BOTH sides and uses the explicit root polynomial for Q^T F Q, then the actual carrier support. Its gain is admitting this intervening H; its cost is requiring a low-degree natural-cycle denominator rather than any arbitrary cycle/weight pattern.

The archived `Whole-word rational fusion: a linear degree and single-solve gate` was also inspected. Its main theorem controls quasiseparable order of a scalar rational function of one fixed low-quasiseparable matrix and certain common-order single solves. The Necklace Hessian's short-orbit support does not by itself establish that low-quasiseparable hypothesis in the transport order. This packet does not assume such an unstated implication.

Thus the narrow new research-log content is the exact two-sided support certificate and its all-parameter application to the explicit Necklace four-Green attempt. Polynomial root counting, Fourier/shift commutation, support multiplication, and denominator clearing are elementary established tools. No absence from the wider literature has been proved.

## 5. External Fourier benchmark remains separate

The current official OpenAI Math 130 scope page was opened. It is the source's comparator for exact Fourier constructions, and is neither used as the candidate backend nor credited to this project. This pass does not rebuild or independently audit its formal proof. Nothing here says that every exact sub-N-log-N Fourier algorithm is unknown; the result is that this attempted independent construction fails in the proved subclass. [Official scope documentation](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/130.md)

## Editorial disposition

Suitable as a short, precisely scoped continuation/certificate in the existing research record, after independent proof review. It is not ready to be described as a new FFT algorithm or as a new general circuit lower bound. External historical priority is unresolved. All source-derived facts are separated from the packet's own algebraic proof and fresh controls.
