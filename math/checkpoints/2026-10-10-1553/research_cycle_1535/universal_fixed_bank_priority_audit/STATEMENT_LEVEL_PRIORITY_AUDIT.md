## Exact statement and counted object

Fix odd N≥3, a field k with char(k) not dividing N, and q of exact order N. Each slot fixes its signs ε,δ and legal α,β once, independently of the target, while its N phase weights may vary. The optimized number is the minimum number of such slots whose image subspaces sum to Mat_N(k).

After the accepted character transform of phase weights, a nondegenerate slot is the direct sum, over physical rows b, of the lines generated by

v_(ε,δ,b)(α)_a = G_(ε,δ)(a,b) α^[εa]_N.

Thus, for a fixed slot list, universality is exactly the condition that the N×m matrix with columns v_(ε_i,δ_i,b)(α_i) has rank N for every b. This equivalence uses a separate amplitude for each row in each slot. It is linear spanning by whole subspaces, not point rank with one scalar per slot.

When β=0, a slot may be a smaller subspace; this does not defeat the theorem's lower bound. The attaining architecture has β=1, α^N=1, and guard −1 everywhere, so it consists of full ruling subspaces over k.

## Closest primary precedents

### Scroll fiber interpolation

Hana and Johnsen, *Scroll codes*, Des. Codes Cryptogr. 45 (2007), 365–377, Definition 2.2 and §2.1, pp.366–367, describe the common-parameter ruling spaces. Theorem 3.5's proof, especially Steps 3–5 on pp.372–373, computes successive fiber-span increments; the paragraph after Step 5 includes singular scrolls and their shared vertex. [Author-uploaded primary text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220638439_Scroll_codes), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10623-007-9131-0).

Applying their standard coordinates to degrees d_ℓ=N−1−ℓ gives, for m distinct legal nodes in one orientation,

D(m)=Σ_(ℓ=0)^(N−1) min(m,N−ℓ).

This formula also follows immediately by taking the rank of each truncated Vandermonde block. Hence N fibers span that orientation's full triangular ambient space. This is a genuine immediate classical implication, including the degree-zero vertex. It does not compute the sum of four differently embedded triangular families: their intersections, missing coordinates, and Gaussian weights must still be analyzed. No 2N−2 theorem for the bank embedding appears in the inspected argument.

### Fixed generators for a whole target linear system

Buczyński and Landsberg, *Ranks of tensors and a generalization of secant varieties*, arXiv:0909.4262v5, Definition 1.1 on p.2 and Theorem 2.5 on p.4, define subspace X-rank and identify it with a corresponding Segre-product point rank. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0909.4262v5).

Ballico, Bernardi, Catalisano and Chiantini, *Grassmann secants, identifiability, and linear systems of tensors*, arXiv:1110.6367, Definition 4.1 on pp.8–9, explicitly fixes generators that compute every member of a linear system. Theorem 3.1 on p.7 concerns identifiability. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1110.6367).

These sources establish that the fixed-generator quantifier is natural and old. Directly taking the target subspace to be Mat_N counts individual points and requires N² independent directions; one present bank instead supplies up to N directions together. Replacing points by whole rulings changes the counted resource. Their stated results therefore do not deliver 2N−2 through direct substitution. The cited geometric theorems also work over complex or algebraically closed characteristic-zero fields, rather than supplying this explicit arbitrary-eligible-field architecture.

### Secant equations and scroll interpolation are different questions

Sturmfels and Sullivant, *Combinatorial secant varieties*, the served 23-page arXiv version, p.19, parametrizes scrolls by x_ij=s^j t_i; Theorem 5.7 gives their secant ideals by concatenated Hankel minors. It supplies the single-orientation dictionary, not a minimum number of fixed ruling spaces spanning the four-orientation ambient space. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0506223).

Ziv Ran, *Interpolation of rational scrolls*, arXiv:2111.02466v1, p.1, asks for a scroll of minimum degree containing prescribed general linear spaces as rulings. Corollary 24 on p.19 gives balanced rational curves in a Grassmannian. Here the scroll families and their embeddings are already fixed, and the task is to choose enough of their rulings to span. Ran's interpolation result is not an immediate architecture theorem for this problem. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.02466v1).

### Resolvent spanning

Buchholz and van Nuland, *The basic resolvents of position and momentum operators form a total set in the resolvent algebra*, arXiv:2309.04263v1, unnumbered theorem and equation (4), p.2, proves norm-density of the linear span of basic continuous CCR resolvents. It supplies neither an exact finite-dimensional bank count nor the finite Weyl root-of-unity dictionary. A finite number of particles there does not mean a finite-dimensional Hilbert space. [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.04263v1).

## What remains specific after these comparisons

The lower bound uses two projections that the classical one-scroll fiber formula does not select. On (a,1), 1≤a≤N−1, every negative-δ slot vanishes and each positive-δ slot supplies at most one direction. On (a,N−1), the roles reverse. The resulting two disjoint requirements are N−1 positive-δ slots and N−1 negative-δ slots.

The upper bound attains both requirements with counts (N−2,1,1,N−2). The two large orientations cover complementary row intervals, the cross orientations supply the missing nonaxis coordinate, and additional moment conditions handle the axes. The explicit nodes q²,…,q^(N−1) and 1 ensure all weights remain in k and every guard is −1. These are the model-specific steps still needed after citing classical fiber interpolation.

This is a genuine specialization-level argument, but its mechanism is elementary. “Not an immediate application of the inspected named theorem” is weaker than “introduces a new method” and does not establish priority. An undiscovered earlier analysis of this exact overlapping dictionary could settle the same count.

## Publication wording

Recommended: “We determine the minimum number of fixed signed Weyl-resolvent configurations required for universal linear synthesis by varying their phase weights. For every eligible odd order the minimum is 2N−2, attained explicitly over the base field. The proof combines classical scroll-fiber interpolation with the support geometry of the four permitted orientations.”

Keep the whole-bank resource definition visible. Universality concerns N² independent matrix coordinates, with 2N²−2N declared raw phase weights; it does not compress an arbitrary matrix or imply an FFT speedup. The theorem does not determine a target-adaptive Fourier minimum. The earlier boundary-rank example was outside this audit's focus.
