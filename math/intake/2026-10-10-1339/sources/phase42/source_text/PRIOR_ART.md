# Prior-art and originality caution

The Phase 42 statements are project-specific consequences of standard ingredients:

- The cyclic-resolvent geometric-series identity for weighted permutation cycles.
- Low-dimensional cut/projective signature properties of Green matrices.
- The nonvanishing Vandermonde determinant for consecutive DFT rows.
- The real submersion/implicit-function theorem and modular nonzero-minor lifting.
- Classical DFT lower bounds in *restricted* arithmetic models, notably Jacques Morgenstern, JACM 1973, https://doi.org/10.1145/321752.321761, and Nir Ailon, 2013, https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.4745.
- Projective cross-ratio and equivalence invariants in complex Hadamard literature, including *Equivalence of complex Hadamard matrices*, Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics (2026), https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10801-026-01568-x.

No exhaustive literature review was completed. In particular, the `q(q+1)^q` theorem, the fixed `N=128` instance, and the Jacobian results should not be described as "first ever" or necessarily publication-novel without further verification. They certainly do not resolve the general unrestricted exact FFT complexity problem.
