EXACT ALGEBRAIC RECONSTRUCTION
For each branch let W(x,y) be its weighted cycle matrix and h its frozen witness holonomy. Its variable monomial m is 1, x, y, or xy. Then W^8=h*m*I, and

  B=I+W+...+W^7,       (I-W)B=B(I-W)=(1-h*m)I.

The fresh generator builds B by enumerating each directed path of length 0 through 7. This is a finite exact formula, not a numerical inversion. Write d=1-h*m. In each stage use the common numerator

  A_s = B_s0*d_s1*d_s2 + B_s1*d_s0*d_s2 + B_s2*d_s0*d_s1.

Let Delta be the product of all six d's and N=A_2*A_1. Then N=Delta*M. Fixed d's are retained as scalar factors, with no division by them in the polynomial computation. In every class N has bidegree at most (1,1). In the same-Green class the affected branch numerator and its guard are bilinear. In the same-stage/different-Green class each affected branch is affine in its own variable. In the different-stage class each affected stage is affine and their product is bilinear.

All witness gains have exactly unit complex norm. The six frozen holonomies h are exact nonzero Q(i) units and are not 1. EXACT_SUPPORT.json records their exact Gaussian rational values. Hence all fixed Green denominators are nonzero at the witness, and every pair has precisely the following variable pole product F:

  same Green:                 F=1-h*x*y;
  same stage/different Green: F=(1-h*x)(1-k*y);
  different stages:           F=(1-h*x)(1-k*y).

The variables x,y must also be nonzero. All references to admissibility include the fixed Green guards, F!=0, and x*y!=0.

NECESSARY MODULUS SYSTEM AND GAUSSIAN CONJUGATION
For any matrix equivalent by arbitrary invertible complex external monomials to F8, its modulus array factors into a row factor times a column factor. External permutations preserve this property. Therefore all 49 division-free anchored defects vanish:

  |M[r,c]M[0,0]|^2 - |M[r,0]M[0,c]|^2 = 0, 1<=r,c<=7.

Put f=N[r,c]N[0,0], g=N[r,0]N[0,c], and define star by conjugating Q(i) coefficients and replacing x,y by their reciprocals. The necessary polynomial is

  P[r,c]=x^2*y^2*(f*star(f)-g*star(g)).

Its bidegree is at most (4,4). On the unit torus P is exactly x^2*y^2*|Delta|^4 times the original anchored modulus defect. This is a necessary condition only. It neither uses division by matrix entries nor asserts sufficiency for Fourier membership.

Conjugation always means i -> -i before evaluating the reciprocal polynomial. The finite-field generator reconstructs the conjugate numerator with the opposite Gaussian embedding. It never interprets finite-field multiplication as an absolute value.

EXACT POLE DIVISIBILITY
At d=0 the branch numerator B has rank one: the weighted cycle has holonomy one and its eigenvalue 1 is simple. Each cleared stage/product numerator therefore has rank at most one on that branch's pole divisor. Because every frozen h has unit norm, the same pole divisor is stable under coefficient-conjugate reciprocal substitution, up to a nonzero Laurent monomial factor. The conjugate-reversed numerator also has rank at most one there. Hence every P vanishes identically on the divisor and is divisible by its irreducible polynomial d. The two distinct affine pole factors are relatively prime. Consequently P=F*Q in Q(i)[x,y] in all three classes.

The program performs exact polynomial division in each admissible finite-field reduction and multiplies back every identity. The characteristic-zero divisibility follows from the rank-one argument, not from these modular checks. Because the leading pole coefficient h is a unit in the coefficient ring described below, Q belongs to the same localized ring. Its bidegree is at most (3,3).

THREE CHARACTERISTIC-ZERO COEFFICIENT SPACES
Let K=Q(i), and let V be the 16-dimensional K-space spanned by x^i*y^j for 0<=i,j<=3.

1. Different stages: all Q lie in V. For each of the 289 pairs, a 16-by-16 coefficient minor is nonzero modulo an admissible prime, so the 49 exact Q span V over K. In particular 1 lies in their K-linear span. Thus <P>=<F>, and saturation by x*y*F is the unit ideal.

2. Same Green: all Q lie in the 14-dimensional subspace V0 obtained by omitting the monomials y^3 and x^3. This support restriction is checked exactly over Q(i), separately for every one of the 112 pairs and every 49 windows: 10,976 exact zero checks. Because F has only its constant and xy terms, Q[0,3]=P[0,3] and Q[3,0]=P[3,0]. The exact checker computes just these coefficients directly from four exact numerator matrices, without relying on modular zeros or running previous research code. Every selected 14-by-14 coefficient minor is nonzero in an admissible reduction. Thus the exact Q span V0, which contains 1. Again <P>=<F> and the saturated ideal is the unit ideal.

3. Same stage, different Green branches: let a=1/h and b=1/k. At the two-pole intersection (a,b), every entry of N vanishes to first order, because each term in the affected stage numerator contains at least one pole factor. The same holds for the conjugate-reversed numerator; the intersection is fixed by conjugate reciprocal substitution. Therefore P belongs to the fourth power of the maximal ideal m=(x-a,y-b). Since F=h*k*(x-a)*(y-b), exact division gives Q in m^2. Hence

  Q(a,b)=Q_x(a,b)=Q_y(a,b)=0.

These are three independent linear constraints on V: evaluation and the two first derivatives are already independent on {1,x,y}. Their kernel V2 has dimension 13. Every pair's coefficient matrix has a nonzero 13-by-13 minor in an admissible reduction, so its exact Q span V2. The product F belongs to V2, yielding F in <Q> and F^2 in <P>. More precisely V2 generates m^2, since it contains (x-a)^2, (x-a)(y-b), and (y-b)^2, and every element of V2 lies in m^2; thus <P>=F*m^2. Its zero set is exactly the inadmissible pole union. For s=x*y*F, s^2 belongs to <P>, so <P>:s^infinity=<1>.

This is why the 13/16 rank in the third class is a successful exclusion rather than a residual system. A rank lower bound alone would not justify that conclusion; the exact Taylor-kernel containment is essential.
