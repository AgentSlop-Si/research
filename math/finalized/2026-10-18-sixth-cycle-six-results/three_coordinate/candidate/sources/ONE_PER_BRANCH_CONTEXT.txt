Q27: 128 THREE-COORDINATE SLICES, ONE PHASE IN EACH GREEN OF ONE STAGE
11 October 2026 UTC. AUTHOR-PROVISIONAL; independent review required.

RESULT AND STRICT SCOPE

For each stage s=1,2 and each a=8,...,15 and b=16,...,23, allow exactly
edges (7,a,b) of stage s to vary on the unit circle; freeze the other 31 free
phases at the accepted Phase42 N=8 witness. All 2*8*8=128 such three-coordinate
slices are excluded from equivalence to F8 by arbitrary invertible complex
external row and column monomials. Each slice has a characteristic-zero
necessary-modulus ideal certificate whose modular nonvanishing witness is
recorded in COVERAGE.json.

This is an author-provisional result until the separately written review
accepts the immutable packet. It does not claim the entire 34-phase fiber,
the remaining 5,856 coordinate triples through this witness, other frozen
witnesses, four or more moving phases, larger N, nonunit internal gains,
a free interstage diagonal, construction, runtime, novelty, or priority.
Q27's full Fourier fiber remains OPEN. The prior independently accepted
561 two-coordinate exclusions remain unchanged.

EXACT GREEN FORMULA AND NUMERATOR

Let h_j be the frozen holonomy of branch j in the selected stage and let
x_0=x,x_1=y,x_2=z. All six frozen holonomies are nonzero unit Gaussian
rationals and differ from 1. REDUNDANCY.json records the exact rational real
and imaginary parts; the checks are exact Fraction arithmetic.

Write d_j=1-h_j*x_j and F=d_0*d_1*d_2. For the varying weighted cycle,
W_j^8=h_j*x_j*I, so its path numerator H_j=I+W_j+...+W_j^7 satisfies

 (I-W_j)H_j=H_j(I-W_j)=d_j I.

Each directed path of length at most seven meets the selected edge at most
once. Hence H_j is affine in x_j. The affected stage's cleared numerator is

 A=H_0*d_1*d_2+H_1*d_0*d_2+H_2*d_0*d_1.

Let C be the opposite stage, a fixed sum of three admissible Green inverses.
Set N=C*A if the selected stage is 1, and N=A*C if it is 2. Then N=F*M,
and N is triaffine. The script constructs the path coefficients explicitly;
it does not interpolate a potentially incorrect degree model.

NECESSARY MODULUS POLYNOMIALS

External invertible complex row/column monomials applied to F8 produce a
rank-one matrix of squared entry moduli. Permutations preserve that property.
Thus every anchored division-free modulus defect vanishes:

 |M[r,c]M[0,0]|^2 - |M[r,0]M[0,c]|^2 = 0, 1<=r,c<=7.

Define polynomial reversed conjugation by

 Ntilde(x,y,z)=x*y*z*bar(N)(1/x,1/y,1/z),

where bar conjugates all Gaussian coefficients. Since N is triaffine,
Ntilde is a polynomial. For each of the 49 windows put

 P_rc = N[r,c]N[0,0]Ntilde[r,c]Ntilde[0,0]
        -N[r,0]N[0,c]Ntilde[r,0]Ntilde[0,c].

P has coordinatewise degree at most four. On the unit torus it is precisely
the anchored modulus defect times x^2*y^2*z^2*|F|^4. The multiplier is nonzero
on the admissible locus x*y*z*F != 0. These are necessary equations only;
no assertion of their sufficiency for Fourier membership is used.

EXACT POLE DIVISIBILITY

At d_j=0, the holonomy-one weighted cycle has a simple eigenvalue 1, and
H_j has rank one. On that divisor the other two terms of A vanish, so A,
and hence N, have rank at most one. Each pole divisor is invariant under
Gaussian-conjugate reciprocal substitution: bar(h_j)=h_j^(-1), and

 x_j*bar(d_j)(1/x_j) = -h_j^(-1)*d_j(x_j).

Thus Ntilde also has rank at most one on the same divisor. Both N and
Ntilde then have zero 2-by-2 minors, and their corresponding product defect
P_rc vanishes on the divisor. Since each d_j is a distinct linear
irreducible polynomial in a separate variable, their product divides each
P_rc over K=Q(i). Define Q_rc=P_rc/F. Exact coordinate degree comparison
gives deg_(x_j)(Q_rc)<=3 for all j.

All modular pole divisions are multiplied back. However the existence of
the characteristic-zero quotient is proved here, not inferred from modular
factorizations.

THE THREE-POLE MODULE: EXACT CHARACTERISTIC-ZERO UPPER BOUND

Set u=x-h_0^(-1), v=y-h_1^(-1), w=z-h_2^(-1) and J=(u*v,u*w,v*w).
Each term of A contains two distinct guards, so N is entrywise in J.
This is also exactly true for Ntilde: the displayed reciprocal identity
turns its two complementary guard factors into scalar multiples of the
same guards, while x_j*bar(H_j)(1/x_j) is affine. Multiplication on the
appropriate side by the fixed conjugate C does not change ideal membership.
Therefore Ntilde is entrywise in J, and P_rc belongs to J^4.

Up to the nonzero scalar -h_0*h_1*h_2, F=u*v*w. Consequently Q_rc belongs to
(J^4 : u*v*w). Intersect this monomial ideal with the coordinatewise cubic
polynomial space. Its monomial basis is exactly

 T = {u^i*v^j*w^k: 0<=i,j,k<=3,
                       i+j>=2, i+k>=2, j+k>=2, i+j+k>=5}.

There are 32 basis monomials. This finite monomial statement is checked
exactly in verify_reductions.py: it enumerates all 15 generators
(uv)^a*(uw)^b*(vw)^c, a+b+c=4, and tests divisibility of
u^(i+1)*v^(j+1)*w^(k+1), for each of the 64 cubic exponent triples.
The resulting 32 triples agree exactly with the four displayed inequalities.
This is integer combinatorics, not a finite-field support assumption.

Equivalent necessary jet constraints are order at least two along each
pair-pole line and order at least five at the triple-pole intersection.
The J^4 containment supplies all those constraints before any computation
of ranks. Every Q therefore lies in the same exactly defined
32-dimensional K-space span(T), after shifting to its slice's pole center.

COEFFICIENT MINORS AND SATURATION

COVERAGE.json lists all 128 slice IDs, all 49 rows of the shifted

The monomial u^2*v^2*w^2 belongs to T. Since F^2 is a nonzero scalar times
that monomial, F^2 is a K-linear combination of the Q_rc. Hence

 F^3 belongs to I=<P_rc: 1<=r,c<=7>.

For s=x*y*z*F, s^3 belongs to I, so I:s^infinity=<1>. Thus the necessary
modulus equations have no common zero away from the Green poles and zero
variables, in particular no admissible unit-phase point. This excludes
external Fourier equivalence throughout each of the 128 slices. Pole
points are never counted as valid constructions.

ADMISSIBLE REDUCTION AND CHARACTERISTIC-ZERO TRANSFER

Start with the exact coefficient ring obtained from Z[i] by inverting all
34 Gaussian phase numerators and denominators a+i*b and a-i*b, and all
six frozen Green guards 1-h_sb. Holonomies and inverse holonomies lie in
this ring. The direct fixed Green inverse is its adjugate divided by its
nonzero Green determinant, so every exact entry of C lies in this ring.
The path construction, conjugation, pole division by the unit leading
coefficients -h_j, and translation by h_j^(-1) keep all Q coefficients in
this ring. The ring is closed under conjugation because the conjugate
of a fixed guard is -h_sb^(-1) times that guard.

For p=1000000009 with i=569522298 and its opposite embedding, and for
p=998244353 with i=86583718 and its opposite embedding, primality is
verified by integer trial division and i^2=-1 is checked. All a±i*b,
a^2+b^2, all six holonomies and all six frozen guards are explicitly
checked nonzero in each reduction. Therefore a genuine reduction
homomorphism from the displayed localized ring to F_p exists. Gaussian
conjugation is realized by reconstructing with the opposite embedding,
not by calling field multiplication an absolute value.

Each stored modular coefficient is the image of its exact characteristic-
zero path/Green coefficient. In particular, each nonzero displayed minor
is the image of a specific exact determinant, which therefore is nonzero.
The exact template then proves the matching rank 32 over Q(i). No inference
from finite-field rootlessness, a modular Groebner unit ideal, or numerical
phase samples to characteristic-zero infeasibility appears anywhere.

FRESH CONTROLS AND REPRODUCTION

At each of the two deterministic algebraic points (2,3,5) and (7,11,13),
the script reconstructs all six Green inverses using fresh Gaussian
elimination. It verifies N=F*M entrywise, and verifies all 49 polynomial
P evaluations against separately formed direct-inverse expressions using
the opposite Gaussian embedding at reciprocal coordinates. These points
are controls for algebraic formulas, not unit-torus feasibility samples.
All required guards at those control points are checked nonzero.
There are 226 scalar control comparisons per slice per embedding, 115,712
across 128 slices and four embeddings. All 25,088 quotient factorizations
(49*128*4) multiply back, and all shifted quotients obey the exact template.
Duplicating one selected minor row gives determinant zero in all 512
slice/embedding certificates, so a planted rank failure is rejected.
