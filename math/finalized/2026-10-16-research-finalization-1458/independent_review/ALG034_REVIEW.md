## Proof and cost assessment

For every integer N>=3, `L=lcm(2N,4)` contains both the actual 2N-root phases and i. The odd-length parity term is retained. Reduction modulo the minimal cyclotomic polynomial therefore decides the exact block summaries, and hence `P x=0`. The map is not silently treated as an orthogonal projection: its correct weighted norm identity is used.

The separation floor uses the l1 norm of the original exponent-aggregated polynomial. This bounds every conjugate even when reduction increases coefficient height. The nonzero integral norm then gives the stated lower bound, with K=0 handled at A=1. The N=6 projected-zero example and N=9 parity counterexample were independently derived directly from the phases. The near-kernel dependence on input width remains.

The [cyclotomic facts](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/galoistheory/cyclotomic.pdf) and [norm-product/integrality facts](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf), at the cited theorem/corollary locations, were opened independently and support the uses made of them. They are classical ingredients, not novelty claims.

The precision choice implies `Delta <= rho*mu/4`. For the generic q>=ceil(log2 N), `sqrt(N)*rho<1`, so the actual approximate vector is nonzero and its permitted normalization gives the claimed relative allowance with slack. This generic lemma does not extend the existing Q16 adapter to nondyadic lengths. The actual dyadic/cap/metadata/output-binding obligations remain intact.

The elementary polynomial-construction ledger is deliberately expensive but valid. Each exact completed division leaves a product of distinct unit-circle roots, while intermediate monic-division cancellations are bounded separately. O(L^2)-bit setup intermediates, O(L^4) arithmetic steps, and O(L^3)-bit retained setup storage are conservative. Block reductions pay their O(W+log N+L^2) coefficient widths. Input scans, planning, actual producer/root work, normalization, copying, downstream work and explicit outputs remain cumulative. A failed sufficient cap estimate is correctly distinguished from actual observed overwidth.

The new approximation-oracle boundary is sound for its stated deterministic finite-query interface: the zero-input transcript is also legal for a sufficiently small positive perturbation in the first coordinate, although the projected signal differs. This does not prohibit approximate output or exact symbolic interfaces with extra information.
