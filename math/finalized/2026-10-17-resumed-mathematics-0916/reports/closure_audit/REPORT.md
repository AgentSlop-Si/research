## C03: useful accepted algebra, unresolved parent obligations

### What is already finished

The original source establishes the adjacent-Hankel fixed-grade factor/reciprocity identity. The inspected addendum explicitly extends the same determinant-pencil calculation to arbitrary square matrices over a commutative ring. Later recorded acceptances include the general bridge, signed principal-minor coefficient, endpoint formulas, and singular-safe supplied-node evaluator. Those later acceptances are preserved, not newly established by this audit.

For `D(w)=diag(1,w,...,w^(m-1))`, the arbitrary-pencil source uses

`P0=det(A-a DAD)`, `P1=det(B-a w DBD)`, and the **fixed formal degree-(m,m)** homogeneous Sylvester resultant. With

`T=m^2(2m-1)`, `v=m(m-1)(4m+1)/6`, and `b=m(m-1)(2m-1)/6`,

the established identity is `R=w^v(w-1)^(m^2) w^b V(w+w^-1)`, with `deg V<=b` and the corresponding reciprocal sign. Specialization may raise valuations, reduce degree, or make the resultant zero; generic exactness is not universal exactness. These are positive results worth preserving.

### Why the whole owner cannot be closed

1. The current full owner explicitly retains **C03::specific-priority** for a primary theorem matching the complete evaluated arbitrary-matrix family, signed principal-minor coefficient, and endpoint package. Classical component reductions alone do not establish that the full family was previously printed, and an unsuccessful bounded comparison does not establish worldwide firstness.
2. A determinant/gcd relation alone does not certify legal sparse reconstruction: finite/base-field root existence, legal grid membership, invertible denominator and the complete cyclic/global identity remain separate gates. A homogeneous resultant may vanish because of formal-degree behavior, so a bare zero is not a legal-recovery certificate.
3. The recorded supplied-node `O(m^3)` field-operation evaluator is not an `O(m^3)` all-coefficient, root-finding, or complete recovery algorithm. The already-recorded straightforward all-coefficient route at `b+1` supplied distinct quotient nodes has `O(m^6)` arithmetic accounting, plus node/extension costs. Numerical and bit guarantees are also distinct.

The old compact gate said only to preserve accepted scope and pursue a separately specified unmatched application/implementation. Its old stop said to retain correct attribution and avoid standalone novelty for already-attributed mechanisms. Those sentences are compatible with preservation but insufficient to establish parent completion while the explicit attribution/recovery obligations remain.

### Useful next gate and stopping rule

Keep the accepted theorem and all its guards fixed. Before new work, select **one named primary theorem** that could match the complete arbitrary-pencil package, or **one explicitly specified legal recovery/application contract**. The attribution comparison must end with an exact full-family implication, a qualified nonmatch, or a specific access blocker; it must not become indefinite literature searching. A recovery/application result must charge node/field setup, candidate enumeration, all validation, interpolation/recovery and output work, and receive independent review at its exact declared domain.

An individual source comparison may finish without closing the broader parent. Until an accepted whole-owner disposition resolves or explicitly archives its remaining obligations, keep TO RESEARCH. This is not a finding that C03 is meaningless or mathematically false, and no new low-value judgment is inferred from missing priority evidence.

## F015: completed inactive comparison, broader owner retained

### What is already finished

The current owner records independent completion of the selected exact arbitrary-amplitude raw-grid solver-contract comparison and cold ledger. It is already inactive at priority 15. Do not repeat that activity or the completed Plonka–von Wulffen cancellation comparison belonging to Q14.

The original inspected theory is a genuine exact structured-input result with supplied roots: for `N=2^k>=4`, `Q=2^floor(k/2)`, `M=N/Q` and `1<=b<=M/2`, raw fibers with at most `b` modes are recognized, every raw coordinate is checked, and all N outputs are written in `O(N log(b+2)+Q b^2)` field/index operations and O(N) field-element space. This is not an arbitrary-input sub-FFT theorem. Its paid ordinary FFT fallback remains `O(N log N)` in the worst case.

The separate accepted conservative exact-complex comparator and finite exact-cyclotomic API are recorded as bounded achievements. They are not promoted to the original sharper parameter profile or arbitrary-field contract.

### Why the whole owner cannot be closed

The live theorem and catalogue records explicitly retain the broader parent. Sharper complete solver/certified-advice construction remains an existing Q14/STR-Q5 gate. Finite-word/bit/numerical/production qualification and full-interface historical priority remain distinct obligations. The original theory's restricted asymptotic advantage and preservation value are not erased by those unfinished tasks.

One decisive interface requirement comes directly from the original aggregate proof: rejecting the light recognizer must certify **actual support greater than B**, otherwise heavy-fiber charging is not justified. On a rejected total budget, the bound also charges the final over-budget fiber with `floor(K0/(B+1))+1`. A solver returning a failed or incomplete candidate followed by an all-entry mismatch does not, by itself, establish that support certificate. This is a source-contract clarification, not a new obstruction or a repeat experiment.

### Useful next gate and stopping rule

Reopen the solver-comparison activity only for a **specific new named solver** and a complete finite-parameter domination proof. Require exact raw-input completeness/soundness on arbitrary amplitudes, certified rejection adequate for heavy-fiber accounting, supplied-root/field assumptions, and paid preparation/advice, ingestion, validation, failed attempts, residual work, storage and every output.
