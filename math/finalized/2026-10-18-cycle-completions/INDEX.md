## 1. Complete retained outer stream

The new production stage generates every retained coefficient, performs exact signed complex scatter and every-prefix accumulation, rounds each final coordinate once, returns all N outputs, and scales back exactly. Its retained-operator bound holds for every admitted capped sparse output. The original-target error bound still requires the explicitly stated source, centering, and recovery premises.

Independent complete runs verified all 885 coefficients and 257 rows at N=257 and all 441 coefficients and 512 rows at N=512, with separate guaranteed rational target enclosures. Dense inputs are rejected by the exact t<M guard before stage allocation; at N=256 the unguarded 401-lift/256-row example disproves incidence uniqueness. This is not an original-signal producer or a complete random sparse-recovery transform.

## 2. Support-blind restricted approximate recovery

For the nonzero at-most-16-support singleton-block family at N=M=2^28, the decoder reads only finite BUCKETS values and source-independent window scalars. An independently uniform odd multiplier gives joint successful return and stated accuracy with probability at least 2033/2048. The returned locations are genuine, include every target coefficient of modulus at least 2^-399, have per-returned-coefficient complex error at most 2^-462, and have total sparse-vector error at most 9·2^-400 for R(x)/alpha.

A production run uses 29 complete calls and reads 3,801,088 coordinates. Independent review performed 58 fresh calls and read 7,602,176 coordinates: a complete original-context replay and a new edge fixture. Each returned 14 genuine locations from 16 source locations. The fixed tapes are demonstrations, not attestations of random acquisition.

The exact-support impossibility result applies to an oracle specified only by its fixed 2^-464 error radius. It does not assert byte-identical responses from the particular deterministic supplier. Exact support without a minimum amplitude, dense/general blocks, amplification, original-target transfer, rounded-serial equivalence, and full-N output remain separate. ALG-034 remains open.

## 3. Canonical block-jump shared multiplication

The complete signed-binary multiplier has a recognized-branch cost O(N+r^12+K_A K_B r^4), with r=floor(log2 N). The refined bound is O(N+r^12+(K_A+K_B)r^4+Q r^4+K_A K_B r^2), where Q counts distinct commutative normalized-leaf products. K_A and K_B count all actual changes in complete legal block lists, including changes from and back to zero. The fixed-budget test K_A K_B r^4≤C N gives complete O(N) cost for each fixed C>0; the default is C=64.

The construction differences canonical digit vectors, rather than incorrectly applying a nonlinear encoding to numerical differences. Full source recognition, exact graph checking, signed arithmetic, prefix recovery, carrying, and dense input/output are paid. It strictly enlarges the recognized structural class to potentially full occupied support, without proving that the previous dispatcher is slower there.

The independent review passed 58 complete products, 261 source-node identities, 262 direct convolution comparisons, and 22 corrupt-certificate rejections. The inherited fallback and its root-wide bookkeeping erratum remain mandatory. The arbitrary-input bound remains N exp(O(log N/log log N)); arbitrary-dense improvement remains open.

## 4. Frobenius-stable N13 support classification

For N=13, q=16, the original characteristic-53 signed-resolvent bank grammar with independently free nonzero effective cross singletons, and K=F_(53^d) with gcd(d,210)=1, a seven-node same-sign support cannot be prime-Frobenius-stable. Thus every sixteen-bank witness must have both seven-node support polynomials outside F53[t]. When both same-sign nonzero effective supports are Frobenius-stable, the exact physical-bank minimum is eighteen. All legal beta values and K-valued weights are allowed; amplitude stability is not assumed.

The new argument is degree-at-most-eight cross-node descent. Independent reconstruction checked the forced identities and all 2,756 base-field recurrence polynomials. The field-uniform lower bound and existing eighteen-bank witness are inherited accepted premises, not new replay results. The unrestricted extension-field minimum, nonstable seventeen-bank possibilities, other cross allocations, and broader parameter families remain open.

One nonblocking explanatory correction governs the original report: h0≠0 certifies exact degree eight; even with h0=0 the polynomial is nonzero because its Y coefficient is always −12. The orbit-descent argument itself therefore survives that mutation.

## 5. Streamed short-factor Mersenne setup

The odd-factorial prefix/reverse setup improves to O(N(log(L+1))^(log2(3)−1)). In the accepted wide-digit arithmetic-core model, N=Lm with m≥cL for fixed c>0, the complete conversion bound is O(M(CN)+L M(Cm)+N(log(L+1))^(log2(3)−1)). The short multiplication uses independently terminating, explicitly paid Karatsuba products and exact streamed carry handling.

This resolves the scoped setup-scale obligation. Same-scale convolution, full-width diagonal products, modular inversion, matrix products, correlated point products, and growing-prime selection remain charged or unresolved as described in the review. It does not establish a complete sub-N-log-N multiplier, a fixed-prime general binary converter, or a measured runtime gain. Use log(L+1) at the L=1 boundary, as the report and review do.

## 6. Two-shear R30 Fourier interface and adapter-only stop

An exact cyclic-DFT construction contains genuinely eligible rho=3 R30 windows through two nonmonomial boundary shears. A modulo-three proof rules out one shear in the same-pair Gaussian-dyadic model. Exact cyclotomic coefficient lanes supply roots for N≥8 without broadening the R30 coefficient class for free.

The fully charged literal pair evaluator needs twelve real additions versus four for the ordinary butterfly. Scaling and batching do not eliminate that excess within the stated separately evaluated model; lane representation and conversion add further cost. This closes this **adapter-only route as a speedup attempt**. It does not rule out joint circuit simplification, auxiliary representations, packed arithmetic, or other Fourier algorithms. No original-compiler integration run or global lower bound is claimed.
