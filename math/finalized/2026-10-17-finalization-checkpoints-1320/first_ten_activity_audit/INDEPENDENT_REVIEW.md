3. The complete-frame and fixed-subset sampling calculations retain their distinct meanings. For s<M, the stated fixed-subset estimator has operator error max(1,M/s−1); at s=M it is zero. This estimator identity is not a theorem about every randomized reconstruction method.
4. The named NSW comparison is recorded complete as a qualified nonmatch. It should not be requeued or counted as newly finished.

### Independent reasoning check

If U is the normalized unitary target and L is any rank-deficient fixed linear replacement on the same square input/output space, choose nonzero h in ker(L). Then ||(U−L)h||/||h||=1. Thus ||U−L|| is at least one. This argument depends on the fixed-linear replacement and matching coordinates; it is not a general arithmetic operation lower bound and cannot be applied to a nonlinear or raw-input-adaptive decoder merely by naming its intermediate frame.

The source explicitly distinguishes the unitary normalized-summary map from the raw-block map, whose singular values are 1/sqrt(K). A norm or determinant statement cannot silently switch between them. Its determinant barriers also concern a specified produced intermediate in a bounded-coefficient gate model. The normalized output-only map has determinant magnitude one.

The retained target is a concrete input-independent global output-only Fourier circuit with a proved identity and all preparation, arithmetic, memory, exception handling and output work charged. Numerical claims additionally need a complete precision contract. The existing FFT-scale factorization and restricted rank/determinant barriers neither construct that circuit nor exclude every such circuit.

## ALG-065: orthogonal-bank projectors and fixed-frame recovery

### Already completed and preserved

1. For a fixed full-column-rank local frame V, P=V(V*V)^−1 V* is an orthogonal projector. The exact complex energy/output identity is accepted.
2. Every proper fixed projector has worst-case relative full-vector error exactly one. The coordinate-impulse leverage bound sqrt(1−q/M) is not the all-input minimax error.
3. The stationary q=M frame gives an exact arbitrary-input Fourier factorization. Local conditioning is controlled in the stated large-K regime. Its supplied complete evaluator remains FFT-scale.
4. Same-clock address coincidences do not prove equal values or arithmetic reuse. The existing catalogue already rejects the degenerate false extension that full-frame projectors never commute even when M=q=1.
5. The named HIKP comparison is recorded complete as a qualified nonmatch. It should not be repeated as pending.

### Independent reasoning and cost check

For proper orthogonal P, choose nonzero h in ker(P). Since normalized F is unitary, ||Fh−FPh||/||h||=1. Conversely, ||I−P||=1, giving the exact supremum. The elementary proof closes the fixed-projector uniform-error-below-one proposal. It says nothing by itself about a frame selected after additional raw-input access or a randomized per-input success statement.

At q=M the source accounts for coefficient/Gram work, short FFTs, scans, setup and explicit outputs. Its complete arithmetic bound is O(NM + N log(K+1) + N log(m+1)). For M proportional to log G and fixed m, this is FFT-scale. Merely deleting the M scans leaves the supplied coefficient work and literal short-FFT bank at FFT scale. This is a statement about the identified pipeline, not a universal lower bound on all circuits computing the same factorization.

The local singular-value bound does not certify root generation, normal-equation solves, scan accumulation, or the final computed output. In particular, rounded subtraction of captured energy from ||x||² can cancel catastrophically; clamping a negative result to zero is not a certified error enclosure. The exact q=M residual is zero even if numerical execution has nonzero error.

The noncommutation claim requires a nontrivial full frame: M=q=1 is a one-dimensional identity projector and therefore commutes. This exception is already recorded as a rejected extension and must not be lost in a closure summary.

The remaining construction must accelerate the complete full-rank coefficient/short-FFT/scan pipeline, rather than only one charged component, and supply rigorous numerical energy/output enclosures where numerical claims are made. Existing fixed-frame and transcript-only sketch obstructions do not settle raw-input-adaptive or per-input randomized methods.
