## ALG-024: accepted CLOSED activity-only recommendation

The full Gaussian audit’s section 3 supplies the complete elementary supporting argument. Choose prime M=Theta(log N) using Bertrand and a sieve. With P the product of primes below M and B=pi(M-1), inclusion-exclusion over T consecutive integers has discrepancy at most 2^B, while the coprime density is at least 2^-B. Thus T=2^(2B)+1 guarantees a rough candidate. Since B=O(log N/log log N), the consecutive search is N^o(1) word work with O(log N)-bit integers. It establishes Q=MK=4N+N^o(1) and coprimality of all nonzero displacements of magnitude below M.

Every prime divisor of Q is at least M. Consequently phi(Q)/Q >= 1 - sum_(p|Q) 1/p >= 1 - log Q/(M log M) = 1-O(1/log log N). Together with the already accepted companion covariance identity, this sharpens that rough-family variance to O(log N/N). No unproved prime-gap or Jacobsthal estimate is needed, and the exponential inclusion-exclusion table is not constructed by the algorithm.

**Why activity archival is defensible:** this is the owner’s whole named supporting-lemma task, already accepted and explicitly characterized as standard-number-theory application/refinement. The selected NSW comparison is also complete. It provides a conditional error-scale overlap, not the rough-padding construction or its cost. No separate unresolved conjecture or proposed new theorem remains in this auxiliary owner’s current recorded scope.

**Remaining obligations preserved elsewhere:** F016 remains OPEN at priority 60 with its source-bound production-wrapper association, stream/scatter, finite accumulation, full outputs, scale-back, all-length/dense dispatch and amplification gates. Finite-instance parameter realization, Gaussian coefficient generation and numerical/bit qualification remain part of that larger model. The asymptotic totient estimate is not promised as a useful positive bound at every tiny N. No exact FFT, vanishing relative-l2 guarantee or practical speed follows.

**Final disposition proposed:** close ALG-024’s completed rough-padding/totient/covariance supporting-lemma and named-comparison activity, preserve its proof and priority as historical/inactive, and explicitly retain the linked F016 gates.
