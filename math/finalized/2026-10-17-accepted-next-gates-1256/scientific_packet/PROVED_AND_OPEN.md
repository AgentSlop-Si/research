# Proved versus still open

All four result groups have separate independent mathematical acceptance bound to the exact report hashes in RESULT_INDEX.json. Their scoped acceptance is distinct from the larger research objective. This staging operation adds no new mathematical proof or runtime result.

## 1. Mersenne Taylor packing

For m>=2, K>=1 and s|K, with p=2^m-1 and N=mK, the redundant Taylor-to-block map has exact worst-case actual materialized volume Theta(mK+K^2/s). One legal all-maximum input attains every block maximum simultaneously. In the smaller-matrix case K=dM^2, s=dM, the final blocks use Theta(N+dM^3) bits, while eager M^2 pair products use Theta(MN+dM^4) bits.

The fixed closed linear packed-state transition is impossible for 1<s<K in the stated degree-safe model. This is not a collision between legal inputs or a lower bound for arbitrary nonlinear conversion. A complete elementary invoice is accepted, including the corrected one extra reconstruction shift bit.

Still open: near-output-size conversion, a faster fully paid product/readout interface, a complete improved recurrence, all-size parameter/padding control, any needed prime selection, and speed or priority claims. The next section resolves the existence of one explicit fused producer, but not its speed.

## 2. Streamed fused Taylor producer

An exact streamed window-weight algorithm now produces the final redundant blocks without materializing all shifted coefficients or the intervening p^d block array. Its paid time is O((K^2/s)(m+ceil(log2(K+1)))(ms+K)), with O(N+(K/s)(ms+K+1)) space. It uses exact small-index divisions and should not be called literally division-free.

The rational-linear lookahead observation rank is B+(B-1)h for 0<=h<s, with B=K/s and degree<K-h. The minimum fixed degree-safe linear-state dimension is K-s+1 and is attained. These are scalar-dimension statements, not general bit lower bounds for arbitrary representations.

The specified fixed-width schoolbook loop has Omega(N^2) work for s<K. The scalar endpoint s=K uses an O(N) copy. Consequently this construction closes the explicit-producer existence gate while leaving near-output-size time, faster paid readout/product, all-size parameters and multiplication speed open. Nonlinear, time-dependent, final-only or other factored methods are not ruled out.

## 3. Weil negative-chirp formula gate

The index set is the cyclic ring Z/qZ with q=p^k, p odd; it is not a finite-field trace-character index set. The coefficient field has characteristic different from p and an exact-order q root. Coefficient characteristic 2 is allowed.

Accepted: U_b=F^-1 C_(-b)F; identity b=0; exact support and completed square for nonzero b including nonunits; signed residual core eta^(-u^-1); Weyl label (a,c)->(a+bc,c); and exact additive shear composition. The proof is uniform on the stated domain.

Evidence remains separated:

- Author: 54,028 characteristic-zero assertions; 1,808 direct circulant kernel numerators imply 40,060 matrix entries. The latter are not all separately evaluated dense entries.
- Reviewer: 26,232,633 recorded checks in the accepted prime-power domain, including coefficient fields GF(4), GF(8), GF(64).
- A further 868,209 reviewer checks at q=15 are diagnostic and outside the accepted prime-power theorem. The combined reviewer log records 27,100,842 checks, but the out-of-scope portion does not enlarge the theorem.

A diagonal-only opposite-chirp test is insufficient over GF(4) at q=3; the full operator or signed ratio separates the signs.

Still open: actual corrected-candidate execution and all 13 derivative test groups; historical exhaustive SL2/subgroup suites; exact earlier API/review-byte recovery; global normalization; p=2 and higher-dimensional extensions; malformed-input service behavior, setup/bit costs, numerical production, performance and historical priority. None of those programs or suites was executed by this staging operation.

## 4. F020 residual boundary, deferred separately

The new exact finite certificates cover every previously excluded residual at d=9,b=3,...,8 and d=25,b=5,...,24. With the inherited low-residual theorem, this covers all 0<=b<d at those two root orders.

Combined with the accepted all-odd overlap and local theorems, this gives, for every A>=0 and every residual at d in {9,25},

    ord_(Phi_d) Disc_z H_(Ad+b) = d*A*(A-1)/2 + A*(b mod 2).

Inherited signed leading coefficients stay nonzero under the existing normalization. This is an all-A theorem consequence, not extrapolation from a finite-A test. A=0 is residual squarefreeness; the established discriminant conventions cover n=0,1.

A separate proved lemma says: write D_b=(q-1)^[b(b-1)/2]V_b with V_b integral. If V_b mod p is nonzero and its finite order t_p(b) at q=1 is strictly less than phi(p^s), then the ordinary residual discriminant is nonzero at primitive p^s roots. All 14 supplied coefficient-level t records were independently verified. This sufficient criterion says nothing by itself about fixed-node separation. Equality or failure of the strict inequality is inconclusive.
