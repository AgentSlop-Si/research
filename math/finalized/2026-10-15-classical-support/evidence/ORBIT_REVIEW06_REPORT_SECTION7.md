## 7. ALG-CYCLOTOMIC-ORBIT — Explicit Galois-orbit content certificate

**Outcome: proved_scoped, general classical-algebra deduction with explicit certificate.**

Let d≥2, O=Z[ζ_d], 0≠M=Σ_(j<φ(d))m_jζ_d^j, c=gcd(m_j)>0, and I(M) the ideal generated by all Galois conjugates. Then

dc O ⊆ I(M) ⊆ c O, and I(M)O[1/d]=c O[1/d].

For f=Φ_d, Q=(X^d−1)/f, define S_j(ζ)=Σ_(k=j+1)^φ(d)f_kζ^(k−1−j). The classical trace dual is β_j=S_j(ζ)/f′(ζ). Differentiation gives dβ_j=ζQ(ζ)S_j(ζ)∈O. If Σ b_jm_j=c, then W=Σ b_jdβ_j satisfies

Σ_a σ_a(W)σ_a(M)=Tr(WM)=dc.

Thus a full-orbit Bézout identity with a pure power of d exists **if and only if** c has no prime factor outside d. It reformulates, rather than proves, unknown Gaussian primitivity. The M=0 case has no such certificate.

In O/pO, p∤d, an orbit subfamily generates the whole product ring precisely when its translates of the zero-component set have empty intersection. Frobenius powers preserve that set and cannot remove a bad component; adding complex conjugation may still remain in the same subgroup. The d=17,p=2 example is a genuine subgroup obstruction with a good component still present, not a counterexample to content primitivity. A norm sums valuations and is not coefficient content. Synthetic special-value-preserving perturbations do not preserve the Gaussian recurrence and cannot be called actual Gaussian counterexamples.

**Fresh evidence:** exact integral trace extraction for all basis pairs at d=3,5,8,9,12 (88 identities); the displayed reduced M_17 has the claimed bad-factor persistence for all eight powers-of-two conjugates and gcd one for all eight outside conjugates. This last check starts from the displayed M_17 reduction; it does not reconstruct the full actual determinant or its old W certificate.

**Attribution:** trace-dual bases and integral cyclotomic structure are classical. The explicit denominator/certificate is a useful deduction; no independent worldwide novelty is established. Uniform primitivity remains open.
