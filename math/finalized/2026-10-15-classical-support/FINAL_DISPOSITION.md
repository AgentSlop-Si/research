## ALG-CYCLOTOMIC-ORBIT

Final disposition: CLOSED as an attributed general trace-certificate support lemma. No missing mathematical claim was found inside its named scope.

For d >= 2, let O = Z[zeta_d], let nonzero M have canonical power-basis coefficients m_j, let c > 0 be their integer gcd, and let I(M) be the O-ideal generated by every Galois conjugate of M. The complete named result is

- dc O is contained in I(M), which is contained in c O;
- I(M)O[1/d] = c O[1/d];
- there is an explicit integral W with Tr(WM) = dc;
- a full-orbit Bezout certificate with right-hand side a pure power of d exists exactly when c has no prime divisor outside d.

These statements are exactly Catalogue H and the accepted REVIEW-06 scope. They are derived from classical power-basis trace duality. Keith Conrad's The Different Ideal, Theorem 3.7, pages 6–7, is a verified source for the trace-dual basis, not a claim that Conrad states this project's whole application. [Author source](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/gradnumthy/different.pdf)

The source proof is complete: with f = Phi_d, Q = (X^d - 1)/f, and S_j the coefficient polynomials of f(X)/(X-zeta_d), the trace-dual elements are beta_j = S_j(zeta_d)/f'(zeta_d). Differentiation of fQ = X^d-1 gives d beta_j = zeta_d Q(zeta_d)S_j(zeta_d) in O. Thus Tr(d beta_j M) = d m_j. An ordinary integer Bezout combination of the m_j constructs W. Divisibility of every conjugate by c gives the reverse containment; localization gives equality. The pure-power equivalence follows in both directions without any additional Gaussian assumption.

The original REVIEW-06 PER_ID_OUTCOMES.json was recovered and its SHA256 exactly matches Catalogue Y: 6cbe4997dcb79534c20fbdde683346448bd1f0ff1a8944b53d3cc0c5d1e7d363. Its report section 7 accepts the named scope and explicitly says no obligation blocks it. Its 88 historical basis controls and reduced d=17 subgroup control remain attributed historical checks. This finalization separately checked 113 trace-extraction identities at nine small conductors; the general argument, rather than those samples, supports the uniform statement.

Excluded and still unresolved where relevant: uniform content primitivity of the actual Gaussian M_d; a pure d-power certificate obtained without knowing the support of c; new cost/implementation claims; and any novelty assertion. The full actual M_17 determinant and its old W certificate were not reconstructed by REVIEW-06 or by this finalization. They are not hypotheses needed by the general named theorem. The report's synthetic special-value obstruction is not an actual Gaussian counterexample. Frobenius-only conjugates and norm valuations cannot replace the full-orbit/content assertion.

The overview's generic implementation/stronger-extension OPEN rationale is therefore not a missing obligation of this record. A separately specified Gaussian or implementation problem may remain open without retaining this completed support lemma in the active research queue.

## ALG-CLASSICAL-RS-INPUTS

Final disposition: CLOSED as an attributed classical-input and exact-reduction support record. No missing mathematical claim was found inside its named scope. The record's original next check is attribution and exposition only.

The complete named package comprises the following existing results and normalizations.

1. Fixed-root Rogers–Szego block factorization is a classical q-Lucas/continuous-q-Hermite identity. Atakishiyeva–Atakishiyev–Koornwinder equations (2.2) and (2.8) give the explicit normalization and block factorization; equation (3.8) gives the ordinary Hermite degeneration. [Primary paper](https://pure.uva.nl/ws/files/848901/75463_0811.4100v2.pdf)
2. For A >= 1, with monic probabilists' Hermite polynomials, J = He_(A+1) + A(1-tau)He_(A-1) reduces by parity and t = y^2/2 to an adjacent quasi-Laguerre polynomial. Its standalone ordinary derivative discriminant follows from Sawa–Uchida and the elementary quadratic-pullback discriminant identities. The accepted citation is arXiv v1 Theorem 4.8, equation (4.13), pages 17–18; the final journal numbering is Theorem 3.6, equation (3.6), pages 844–845. Both were reopened. Apparent denominators must be cancelled before singular specialization. The adjacent quasi-Hermite formula is not itself the required gap-two formula. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.00605v1), [final paper](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jmath/71/3/71_831/_pdf)
3. K_A = yUV-U'V+UV', for U = He_A and V = i^(-A)He_A(iy), is the monic Hermite Wronskian for the hook (A+1,1^A). Gomez-Ullate–Grandati–Milson's Proposition 3.1 and Theorem 3.1 give the pseudo-Wronskian translation mechanism. For an odd prime p = 2A+1, Grosu–Grosu Theorem 8 then gives K_A congruent to y^p modulo p: the degree sequence {1,...,A,p} has p-unit Vandermonde and reduces to {0,...,A}. This is an attributed specialization. [Pseudo-Wronskian source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.05514), [congruence source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.00065v1)
4. For signed Rogers–Szego T_n with n >= 1, the q=1 Hermite leading term yields Disc(T_n) = (q-1)^(n(n-1)/2) E_n(q), with E_n in Z[q] and E_n(1) = product_(j=1)^n j^j. The proof in CLASSIFICATION.md section 3 was reopened. Under q=1+epsilon^2 and z=1+epsilon*x, its recurrence makes epsilon^(-n)T_n an integral monic polynomial specializing to He_n. Discriminant scaling and the elementary Hermite resultant recurrence give the asserted factorization and leading value. If a primitive p^a-th root annihilated E_n with n<p, Phi_(p^a) would divide E_n and p would divide E_n(1), a contradiction. This is the exact low-degree prime-power consequence already in Catalogue H.
5. The optional content conversion in PRIMARY_REDUCTIONS section 5 is standard cyclotomic arithmetic: with the stated integral local factorization and one unit upper-prime component, rational content uses the minimum upper-prime valuation divided by the ramification index. Sharifi's Lemma 3.1.13 and Propositions 3.1.20–3.1.21 provide the relevant uniformizer, integral basis and ramification facts. This does not establish the project's local-order or unit-component hypotheses. [Author notes](https://math.ucla.edu/~sharifi/notes/algnum-ch03.html)

Excluded and still unresolved where relevant: the historical priority of the full moving-root ordinary-discriminant coefficient or reciprocal-parameter application; unrestricted composite squarefreeness; and the larger half-degree prime-power target for a>1. A fixed-q identity alone does not determine a moving-q leading coefficient. Ordinary derivative and q-difference discriminants remain distinct.
