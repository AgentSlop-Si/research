This packet completes bounded, named-source comparisons for ALG037, ALG041 and ALG060 and records a separate C03 attribution checkpoint. It does not establish worldwide priority, a general Fourier lower bound, or a faster all-input Fourier algorithm.

## Results

- **ALG037:** the retained fixed-seed linear-map theorem gives expected rank at least `N(1-delta)(1-epsilon^2)` under its per-fixed-input probability and `l1-to-l-infinity` error contract. HIKP Theorems 4.9 and 6.1, Kunsch–Novak–Wnuk Theorem 2.7, and Alon's perturbed-identity bounds have precise but different hypotheses, norms, decoder models or conclusions. The independent reviewer accepts completion under the existing qualified-nonmatch/access-blocker stopping condition and accepts one scoped closure proposal. Mathé (1991), section 3 Lemma 2(ii), remains an unread potentially material antecedent; exact historical equivalence and priority are unverified. No arithmetic-runtime lower bound follows from rank alone. [Comparison](reports/ALG037_COMPARISON.md)
- **ALG041:** the source's augmented Cauchy–Binet step is an exact application of classical rectangular volume machinery after its actual constrained-packet matrix is identified. Classical support-product uncertainty matches the packets; prime additive uncertainty does not apply to the accepted dyadic regime. Schur/path-sum elimination and corrected truncated/pruned Fourier algorithms do not supply the missing paid causal solver. The named comparison is complete; the faster causal construction and full-transform task remain open. [Comparison](reports/alg041/REPORT.md)
- **ALG060:** quasiseparable/Green structure, displacement rank, graph-induced cut rank, and regular rigidity have explicit overlaps and non-implications with the restricted inverse-column-support product grammar. Fourier itself admits the displayed displacement-rank-one representation, while its inverse columns remain dense. The named comparison is complete; broader interfaces and source-bound production accounting remain open. The restricted theorem and previously completed Tao attribution are retained, not counted as new work. [Comparison](reports/ALG060_COMPARISON.md)
- **C03:** the complete reciprocal-resultant family remains a proved specialization of classical mechanisms with unresolved exact complete-family printed priority. Fresh indexed text strengthens the partial Helmke–Fuhrmann (1989) mapping, but the complete PDF, normalization and formula artwork were not recovered. A targeted Library/local-copy check found no matching primary body in the material checked. This is an authored source-access and partial-attribution checkpoint, not an independently accepted priority resolution. It proposes no closure or score change. [Checkpoint](reports/C03_ATTRIBUTION_CHECKPOINT.md)
