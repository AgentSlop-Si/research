The separately accepted quantitative lower proof uses quantitative Fourier rigidity and flat-vector tests on actual two-dimensional compressions. Those compressions need not be invariant or unitary. Its constants, normalization, unequal offsets, signs, compactness and strictness are preserved. The new result optimizes the parameter of that declared relaxed strict numerical test without altering these estimates.

Together, these two theorems cover `q<d<2q` for every prime `q>5` at `N=5q`.
