## 1. M-CRT-TRANSFER: why the selected activity is exhausted

### Exact accepted mathematics

The original accepted result considers N=2m distinct nonzero Fourier roots split into balanced sets A and B. In ascending monomial quotient bases, let R_A and R_B be the remainder-transfer matrices for multiplication by x^m. The factorization

    row_permuted_Vandermonde = diag(V_A,V_B) [[I,R_A],[I,R_B]]

shows that R_B−R_A is invertible. Each transfer is invertible, so their combined literal support is at least 2m. Equality forces each transfer to be monomial. Its first column then forces the root polynomial to be x^m−c; among the 2m-th roots this gives exactly the parity split, up to exchanging the children. The associated shared reductions are ordinary radix-2 butterflies.

The subsequent accepted finite task is completely explicit: N=16, eight-element A with trivial additive translation stabilizer, B its complement, field Q(zeta_16), and the fixed ascending basis 1,x,...,x^7. The accepted exhaustive minimum is

    min_A (nnz(R_A)+nnz(R_B)) = 112.

The witness A={0,1,3,4,7,8,9,12} has 56+56 nonzeros. The 64 minimizing subsets form 32 complementary pairs and one odd-affine/complement orbit. The independently accepted review covers all 12,870 balanced subsets, including all 12,800 eligible subsets; its eligible pair histogram is 112:32, 116:64, 120:32, 124:160 and 128:6112. These are attributed earlier results, not freshly reproduced results from this review.

The proof uses exact eight-integer coordinates modulo t^8+1, whose irreducibility follows by Eisenstein after t=y+1.
