# Order-5q proper q-multiple endpoints

For every prime q>5, binary masks of the original fixed Fourier matrix cannot attain scalar degree-d Grams at d=2q,3q,4q. See the [self-contained proof](candidate/REPORT.md), [independent review](review/REVIEW.md), and [bound acceptance](FINAL_ACCEPTANCE.json).

Column-degree coverage derives a union of full cycles in every row. Endpoint coprimality forces the full-fiber count to be zero and leaves exactly k q-lines at d=kq. The five rows at one fixed second CRT coordinate would need pairwise-disjoint k-element line sets. This is impossible for k=2,3,4. Every line set may depend arbitrarily on both row coordinates; the pure-line case is treated explicitly.

Only these three endpoints are claimed. All other degree ranges retain their prior scope. A full order-5q degree classification is a separate unaccepted synthesis. M-SPARSE-DEFECT remains TO RESEARCH, priority 42; scores and historical novelty are unchanged.
