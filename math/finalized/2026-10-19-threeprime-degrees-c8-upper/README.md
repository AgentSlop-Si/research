## Accepted results

1. **Complete degree-existence classification at N=3q.** For every prime q>3, the possible positive degrees of a binary mask R with D[r,c]=R[r,c] zeta^(rc) and D*D=dI are exactly {1,3,q,3q}. The new theorem excludes the entire interior q<d<2q; the combined classification also uses previously accepted small-degree, endpoint, upper-range exclusions and divisor constructions. This is not a classification of all support geometries, nor a theorem for every mixed-prime order. Both Gram conditions and the fixed original Fourier phases are retained.
2. **Sharper exact legal C8 rank-zero upper witness.** In the original arbitrary-invertible-complex-monomial, unrestricted-real-core, external-complex-operator-norm model,

   sin(3 pi/16) < delta_(8,0) <= sqrt(1+sqrt(sqrt(2)-1))/2 = 0.641013699718185... .

   The strict lower bound is inherited. The new upper endpoint is attained by one explicitly specified legal witness. Four-periodicity is used only to construct that witness. The exact global optimum and any nonperiodic improvement remain open.

The C8 result does not change the already exact C8 rank-one value, zero-error correction-rank optimum, all-m boundary-rank family, or other accepted phase results. The N=3q classification leaves the accepted prime-power and N=2q results, connected (8,4) countercontrol, and other sparse inverse/commutator bounds intact. See [HISTORY_AND_SCOPE.md](HISTORY_AND_SCOPE.md) and [OWNER_DISPOSITIONS.json](OWNER_DISPOSITIONS.json).

## Attribution and limits

The degree proof retains classical binary vanishing-sum credit to Lam–Leung and de Bruijn, together with the existing fixed-Fourier equality-mask framework and the original endpoint lead. The C8 witness inherits the original model, phase reduction and strict lower obstruction. Existing classical spectral approximation, rank-packing, Gilbert–Rzeszotnik phase-energy, Halko–Martinsson–Tropp, and other bounded source-comparison qualifications remain unchanged. No exhaustive priority clearance is claimed.

In the inverse-row model the new interior exclusion implies Delta_(3q,3q-d)>sqrt(d/(3q)) and, by the already accepted quantitative gap theorem, Delta_(3q,3q-d)^2 >= d/(3q)+1/[30q d^(2q-3)] for q<d<2q. These are inherited compositions, not new sharp rates. The divisor d=3q is an equality-mask boundary and does not assert an invertible s=0 inverse-row realization.

The C8 interval-width reduction is approximately 43.6153 percent relative to the previously certified upper endpoint 1/sqrt(2). It is a derived comparison, not an optimality certificate. Historical numerical search costs remain author-reported provenance and were not independently audited by the upper-bound review.
