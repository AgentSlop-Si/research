## ALG-057: valid rectangular-volume improvement, classical tool

The age-t row bank has exactly `m_t=2^(d-b-t)|K_t|` rows, each with squared norm `2^(b+t)`. Equal-age orthogonality is a complete geometric sum. Across ages, a nonzero sum would require the reversed words to agree modulo `2^(b+t)`; either their highest bits disagree or this inserts the forbidden b-zero marker in the longer word. The output routing exposes distinct age supports. Thus the selected J independent jet blocks give a block-diagonal Gram matrix of determinant `2^(JS)`, with `S=sum_t(b+t)m_t`.

For any fixed q, the maximal exterior volume of q available coefficient rows starts at one. Unit-bounded scalar multiplication does not increase it. A binary sum/difference increases it by at most two by multilinearity and the triangle inequality, even if one substituted row repeats another. Taking `q=J sum_t m_t` proves `A>=JS/2`. No column-minor choice or binomial penalty is needed. The old weaker determinant bound remains valid.

The final report also retains all terminal partial banks. Each age-t bank appears for j=0 through n-b-t. Orthogonality within each input block and independence across blocks therefore give the stronger exact available-volume lower bound `A >= (1/2) sum_t (n-b-t+1)(b+t)m_t >= JS/2`. This additional substitution is valid and classical; it is not asserted to be the optimal exact circuit cost.

The source union bound gives `m_t>=r/(16L)` for `2<=t<=L`, hence `S/2>=rL/64`. As `rL=N/2`, this yields `A>=JN/128`. Under the stated marker schedule J is asymptotic to n. The already paid O(Nn) upper schedule matches its order. The result requires genuinely independent arbitrary jets and explicitly exposed scalar outputs; it does not transfer to source-generated jets of only N independent dimensions.

This is a direct classical rectangular-volume application. [Bürgisser–Lotz, section 3 equation (2)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cs/0301016v1) records the row-volume bound, attributing that framework to Raz; its usual scalar-bound-two model counts all gates. The report explicitly proves the narrower unit-scalar, additions-only accounting. Neither the tool nor this constant-factor sharpening is promoted to historical novelty.
