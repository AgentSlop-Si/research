# Required reading alongside the accepted results

## Centered-family API and execution boundary

The independent final centered-family review clarifies the source-level integration recipe: instantiate `FullTransfer(outer, TransferConfig(1,4,sigma,4))`, then call the resulting object's `run` method. Construction alone prepares the stage and emits no final output. The author proof is preserved as reviewed; this clarification must accompany any reuse of its prose recipe.

The new provider was executed, with fresh independent checks of its coefficients against direct sums from the actual original. The provider-plus-full-outer pipeline was not executed. Acceptance is a restricted original-family provider and source-level mathematical composition, not integrated execution evidence.

The final reviewed proof uses the conservative ledger actually implemented. Dense front-end binary work is O(N L^6+S L^7), and constant-descriptor work is O(L^3+S L^7), where L=2+m+W+f. Under the stated fixed-family width/history hypotheses, the combined dense-input bound is O(Nm^6+M^1/4 m^7). No linear-word bound is claimed or accepted for this reference. Preserve the actual history-width H qualification, all dense recognition visits, source replay, scale-back, output writes, storage and serialization costs.

The exact centered spectrum has 3N+1 entries; the returned sparse approximation has 1+3·2^ceil(m/4)/2 listed entries. Three growing residue fibers are not three Fourier coefficients. This distinction is essential.
