## M-RECTANGLE-TILING

The rank-one criterion is N dividing every product of a row difference and a column difference. Every such rectangle lies inside a subgroup-coset/annihilator-coset product and has area at most N. The exact disjoint rank-one incidence optimum is N D_N, with D_N=min_(d|N)(d+N/d): 2N^(3/2) at square N and N(N+1) at prime N. These results remain accepted.

The retained successive extensions are:

1. The universal arbitrary-rank sandwich N min(D_N,2 sqrt(2N)) <= optimum <= N D_N. Equality N D_N and rank-one-only full-coset optimizers were proved on D_N^2<8N and separately N=2p for odd prime p.
2. The dominant-prime family N=mp, prime p>m: optimum N(m+p), exactly N full rank-one tiles with unordered sizes {m,p}, with no global-orientation requirement. The prime-fiber rank bound applies even when a prime divisor also divides its cofactor. The positive higher-rank-area penalty min(1/p,(p-m)/(mp)) is preserved. This includes every semiprime and N=57 with optimum 1254; the former scalar-relaxation tuple is not a Fourier counterexample.
3. Rank-two unit-modulus rigidity and strict two-piece rank-one refinement at every N. The enlarged sufficient domain is D_N^2<12N. The sharp rank-two density E2(N)/N, E2=min_(d|N,d<N)(2d+N/d), remains. Exact former targets 343 and 847 have optima 19208 and 74536 and sharp penalties 1/49 and 1/77. The false general rank-three projective-class analogy is not revived.
4. The first prime-cube rank-three theorem, p>=5, gives one side occupying at most four residues modulo p^2, hence min(a,b)<=4p. At 1331 the optimum is 175692 with exactly 1331 rank-one full-coset tiles and valid, not claimed sharp, penalty 1/484.
5. For N=pq^2, distinct primes 5<=p<q, the accepted mixed-prime theorem is the dichotomy min(a,b)<=4q OR max(a,b)<=max(6p,3p+2q-2,3q), not an unconditional small-side statement. It gives the N=11q^2, prime q>11, family optimum 132q^3, full-coset equality of unordered sizes {q,11q}, and valid penalty 1/(44q). At 1859 the optimum is 290004 and penalty 1/572.
6. The sharp prime-cube improvement, p>=5, gives min(a,b)<=3p and exact rank-three minimum density (p+3)/p^2. It does not classify all rank-three equality rectangles. At 2197 the optimum is 399854 with exactly 2197 rank-one full-coset tiles of unordered sizes {13,169}; penalty 1/676 is valid and not claimed sharp.

The integer-relaxation correction is substantive: 2873 was outside the named sufficient-domain union, but no new structural theorem was necessary merely to close that target. Old dated gate statements remain in the exact prior reports. Adding only 2873 to the retained union leaves 3757 as the next residual; no complete all-p^2 q tiling classification follows.
