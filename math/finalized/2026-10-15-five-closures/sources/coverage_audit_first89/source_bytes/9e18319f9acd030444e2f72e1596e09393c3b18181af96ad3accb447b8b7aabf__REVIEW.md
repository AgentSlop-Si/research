## Independent proof audit

### Symmetry and scope

For even s, h=s/2, the four affine Margulis topologies commute with shifts (h,0),(0,h),(h,h), because each shear's extra change is 2h=0 modulo s. The +1 translations preserve this equality. A weighted permutation additionally commutes with a selected involution exactly when its vertex weights are invariant under that shift. Inverses, scalar sums, and arbitrary finite products of commuting-with-Pi factors continue to commute with Pi, even when the factors do not commute with one another. This is an all-depth symmetry statement for those weights, not a topology-only impossibility.

### Pi_x spectrum

Pi_x is the global cyclic half-shift N/2. Fourier conjugates it to the parity diagonal D. Since N=s² is divisible by four, D and Pi_x commute and their four joint eigenspaces each have dimension N/4. Thus Pi_x−D has N/2 singular values two and N/2 zeros. Fourier unitary equivalence gives precisely the same commutator spectrum. The commutator inequality yields operator distance >=1 and relative Frobenius distance >=1/sqrt(2) for any commuting T. Zero attains the operator minimum; the group projection (U+Pi_x U Pi_x)/2 attains the Frobenius minimum.

### Pi_y and Pi_xy spectra

The stated two-register Fourier factorization has the correct digit order and sign. Removing its exterior unitaries reduces the Pi_y commutator to A−B, A=D(I tensor Q), B=(Q tensor I)D. The monomial W=A*B shifts both registers by h. Its two-step phase is zeta_N^((a−abar)(b−bbar)), with both differences equal to ±h, hence ±i. Therefore W⁴=−I. Its permutation has no fixed point, so tr W=0. The eigenvalues of 2I−W−W* are 2±sqrt(2), equally often. The extra Pi_xy parity signs cancel over a two-step orbit, giving the same spectrum. Both commutators have full rank N.

Thus the sharp operator minimum over the unrestricted Pi_y or Pi_xy commutant is cos(pi/8); the averaging projection attains it because its residual is the commutator times a unitary divided by two. These are full-commutant sharpness statements, not claims that the optimizing projection has the requested Green representation.

### Simultaneous symmetry

Each nonidentity group element gives tr(U* Pi U Pi)=0. The trace is real because it is the trace of a product of two Hermitian involutions, so the Frobenius commutator identity indeed implies zero, not merely vanishing real part. The four group conjugates of U are mutually Hilbert–Schmidt orthogonal. Their mean is the orthogonal projection to the simultaneous commutant, with squared norm N/4 and squared residual 3N/4. Hence the exact relative Frobenius minimum is sqrt(3)/2. The exact operator minimum remains one, by Pi_x and the zero control. Expected isotropic input error is the squared Frobenius error for fixed input-independent T.

### Quantitative breaking and exact low-rank changes

For G=(I−P)^{-1}, direct multiplication gives [G,Pi]=G[P,Pi]G. When the permutation topology commutes with Pi, [P,Pi] is a weighted permutation whose coefficient magnitudes are |w_i−w_Pi(i)|, so its norm is exactly their maximum. Triangle inequalities and the product commutator rule give the displayed A_j,B_j bound. It can weaken near ill-conditioned resolvents, exactly as the source acknowledges.

For a changed resolvent, G′−G=G′(P′−P)G. Editing k_j rows of a fixed weighted permutation gives rank <=k_j; each stage sum and the product telescope add those ranks. Both old and new inverses must exist. Counting every occurrence yields rank(T−T0)<=k and rank[T,Pi]<=2k. The commutator spectra then give k>=N/4 for exact Pi_x breaking and k>=N/2 for exact Pi_y/Pi_xy breaking. Eckart–Young applied to the commutator difference gives the source's operator thresholds 1, cos(pi/8), sin(pi/8), and the Pi_x relative Frobenius floor sqrt(max(1/2−2k/N,0)). This is a weight-edit budget, not an operation lower bound; Omega(N) weights are compatible with O(N) work.

A valid invariant reference with the same graphs, coefficients and stage order is required. One cannot count arbitrary unequal weight pairs without constructing such an invertible reference. Non-Green stages, changed scalar coefficients and extra permutations need separate charges. Odd s has no nontrivial half-grid translation of this form.
