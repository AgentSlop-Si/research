## 3. Exact input/output mapping

Specialize the accepted F015 field to C and choose its supplied root w=exp(2*pi*i/N). Then eta=exp(2*pi*i/M). Give the source inverse-recovery interface the unknown vector a=(a_h) and Fourier data y=F_(eta^−1) a. Those data are exactly the available raw fiber y[u], so this mapping needs no hidden preliminary transform. The source length L is Q14's M; its sparsity s is Q14's r_t, not Q14's M. Its recovered vector would provide Q14's normalized amplitudes a_h, not the unnormalized forward coefficients M a_h.

The Q14 oracle read y[u] is the array access x[t+Qu], with its indexing/read charge. Using the opposite root convention instead requires an explicit index reversal, not a different mathematical promise. Failure already in this complex specialization rules out an unrestricted substitution into the broader supplied-root-field theorem. It says nothing against F015's own recognizer.

## 4. Two-mode witness and exact refusal meaning

Take any M=2^ell>=8 and define

    a_0=1, a_(M/2)=−1, a_h=0 otherwise.
    y[u]=1−(−1)^u.

The two labels are distinct, so r=2 exactly. The raw samples are y[0]=0, y[1]=2, alternating thereafter. For every j<ell, indices 0 and M/2 are equal modulo 2^j. Hence their contributions to the periodized vector cancel and **every proper periodization is the zero vector**. At the final level, the two nonzero components separate.

Choose any positive threshold epsilon<1. Both true components are significant. The initial Fourier datum is y[0]=0, so the printed initialization gives the empty/zero candidate immediately. This conclusion follows by direct substitution into the source branch, not by executing a supplied program. A Q14 full-coordinate verifier finds y[1]=2 unequal to the candidate's zero and rejects. Yet r=2<=b for every b>=2. Therefore

    candidate verification failure  DOES NOT IMPLY  r>b.

The all-entry verifier restores soundness of acceptance but cannot restore completeness of discovery. A timeout, an invalid-candidate guard, or a no-cancellation refusal has exactly the same limitation if used as a replacement for this missing completeness implication. An implementation exception must not be interpreted as heaviness either.

Replacing the threshold by an exact zero test leaves this witness intact. Merely assigning epsilon=0 to the printed strict inequality is not that replacement and does not establish a new exact algorithm. More generally, an arbitrary fixed positive threshold cannot recognize all nonzero exact complex amplitudes, so a numerical threshold interface cannot silently stand in for Q14's zero test.

## 5. Aggregate charge breaks on a valid light family

Let N=M² and Q=M, and use the same two-mode fiber above at every t. Then K=sum_t r_t=2Q. Set the genuine aggregate budget K0=2Q. Q14's schedule has a=max(1,ceil(K0/Q))=2 and B=min(M/2,2ell). For M>=16, B>=2 and all Q fibers are light.

An attempted cap substitution rejects the first fiber and therefore fails completeness. An aggregate wrapper that responds to every false zero candidate by computing a full length-M transform needs Q such transforms; the true total budget still equals K0, so this can be a successful aggregate run. The promised bound would instead allow only floor(2Q/(B+1)) such calls on success. Even adding the rejection allowance does not repair it.

For a concrete check, M=Q=16 gives N=256, ell=4, K0=32 and B=8. There are 16 fallback transforms, compared with a claimed allowance of floor(32/9)=3 on success or 4 on rejection. This is exact integer arithmetic and a closed-form family, not a benchmark.

With a fresh ordinary length-M FFT on every fiber, the charged transform term is O(QM log M)=O(N log M), rather than the accepted amortized O(M ell(1+K0/B)). At balanced sizes this is ordinary O(N log N) scale. This statement describes that fallback strategy, not a lower bound for every possible Fourier algorithm, shared computation, or alternative recognizer.

Remark 2.2's exact-sparsity premise cannot be replaced by a public upper cap B. In particular r<B need not ever yield a periodization containing B nonzeros, even at the final level. Thus “continue until the count reaches B” is not a valid translation. Supplying r=2 in the witness does not prevent its last cancellation from occurring at level ell−1; it simply gives the fallback a valid stopping count.
