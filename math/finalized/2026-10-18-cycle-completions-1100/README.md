## 4. Canonical affine-run multiplication extension

## 5. Higher-rank Fourier rectangle optimum

For complex cyclic Fourier matrices partitioned into disjoint nonempty Cartesian rectangles, charge exactly sum r_t(a_t+b_t), and set D_N=min_(d|N)(d+N/d). The exact optimum is N D_N when D_N^2<8N and, separately, at N=2p for every odd prime p. Every optimizer in those domains uses N rank-one full subgroup-coset/annihilator-coset rectangles of area N.

The general sandwich is N min(D_N,2 sqrt(2N)) <= optimum <= N D_N. The general nonsquare composite problem remains open. Overlapping/cancelling factorizations, shared arithmetic circuits, finite-field norm arguments, cold bit cost, and FFT speedups are outside the model.

## 6. Width-adaptive Mersenne diagonals

Let a=log2(3)-1. Under the inherited sequential-bit/array model, N=Lm, and m>=cL for fixed c>0, the complete conversion construction has bound O(M(CN)+N B_*^a+N(log(L+1))^a), with B_*=max(1,min(m,L ceil(log2(L+1)))). Width-adaptive factorial diagonals, the kernel, and inversion improve the explicit Karatsuba construction in the very-wide-digit regime.

The same-scale convolution, balanced-width diagonal saving, matrix/point-product composition, growing-prime availability/selection, and complete little-o multiplication composition remain open. The review's small operation-count clarification is mandatory: the local recurrence uses 3q represented additions/subtractions, hence O(q), while exactly 2q local products remains correct.
