# Required scope and accounting qualifications

These qualifications accompany all six results. Original author status files and recorded run counts are historical evidence; final independent reviews define acceptance. A successful bounded control is not a proof of an infinite-domain theorem.

1. Centering: exact-source incompatibility concerns the two declared accepted interfaces. It is not a proof that every future generalized recovery supplier fails. The retained-operator adapter is a source-level contract, not an executed integrated program. Preserve the explicit residual defect; retained original histories, semantic replay, actual reached widths, output expansion, and fixed-family qualifications remain charged. ALG-034 and general/all-length integrated execution remain open.

2. Dense dispatch: source and target tags are essential. The exact singleton second-transform reversal does not solve the original first-transform problem. Direct output is certified only after successful completion of the bounded reference. Rejections, comparison work, parsing, original-source retention, replay receipts, physical scale expansion, and output writes are charged. This release does not add a composed sparse branch or a random recovery guarantee. Use the repaired final candidate and its final review, never an earlier intermediate dense snapshot.

3. Degree eleven: the grammar fixes exactly one nonzero effective cross node in each cross orientation. The negative unnormalized Fourier sign, factor 1/13, original branch inverses, legal beta values, raw weights, and nonzero bank guards must be retained. Degree-eleven attainment extends to multiples of eleven, but does not classify all other degrees. Both-base-cross exclusion requires at least one nonbase cross node in a 16- or 17-bank witness; it does not require both. The accepted lower theorem is inherited and independently source-checked, not newly recomputed in full. Sixteen banks and 208 branch inverses are different cost counts.

4. Affine runs: certification concerns canonical vector runs accepted by the actual recognizer. An affine numerical sequence alone is insufficient. Full input recognition, endpoint validation, second-difference events, shared products, all four prefix scans, carries, output, and inherited fallback costs remain included. The inherited root-wide bookkeeping erratum is mandatory. No arbitrary-dense improvement or separation from the earlier dispatcher is claimed.

5. Fourier rectangles: the field is complex characteristic zero, roots are supplied, tiles are disjoint, and the objective is exactly the sum of rank times endpoint incidence. The twice-prime proof uses the established prime-Fourier nonvanishing-minors theorem. A finite-field rank calculation is not a substitute for that theorem or a complex stable-rank bound. The N=57 relaxed scalar example is not an actual forbidden Fourier tile or tiling. General nonsquare composite lengths remain open.

6. Mersenne diagonals: the bound is in the inherited sequential-bit/array arithmetic model with m>=cL for fixed c>0 and paid convolution M. Use log(L+1) at L=1 and the stated regularity when aggregating groups. Full-width diagonal construction, bounded windows, inversion, arrays, carries, scans, and output remain paid. The Section 2 recurrence has 3q represented additions/subtractions, not exactly q; O(q) is the valid asymptotic statement. Exactly 2q local multiplications is unchanged. No measured speedup, balanced-width gain, general prime availability, or completed multiplication composition follows.

No worldwide novelty, general FFT improvement, broad parent closure, or unrequested archival/status decision is implied by this package.
