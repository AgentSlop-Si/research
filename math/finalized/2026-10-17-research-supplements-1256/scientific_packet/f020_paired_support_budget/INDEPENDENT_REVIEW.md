# Independent review: paired-support degree and order budgets

17 October 2026 UTC.

## Verdict

**ACCEPT, at the scope stated below**, the paired-support budget and endpoint-only budget in Section 5, and the high-order affine simplicity corollary in Section 6, of *F020: exact remaining characteristic-two gates*.

The degree argument is valid for both a self-reciprocal irreducible component and two distinct inverse components. The order-511 polynomial is an exact synthetic control against importing a whole-cyclotomic degree argument into paired-support reasoning. It is not an actual Rogers–Szegő counterexample, and does not disprove an additional numerical bound that might hold for that particular family for some other reason.

This review does not accept an all-m paired-support theorem, an all-m endpoint theorem, a new actual-family support range, or a characteristic-zero counterexample. The full paired-support and endpoint gates remain open at their existing priorities. The new characteristic-zero result descriptions elsewhere in the source report are outside this review's independent-verification scope.

## 1. Accepted inputs and deflation guards

The argument uses the accepted characteristic-two actual-family definitions

    E_m=P_m(0;q), R_m=Res(A_old,B_old), Q_m=E_m R_m,
    N=m(m+1)(2m+1)/3.

The reviewed deflation inputs are:

- Q_m is nonzero, monic, and has actual degree N.
- v_q(Q_m)=ceil(m/2).
- v_(q+1)(Q_m)>=kappa=m+3 floor(m²/4)+2 floor(m/4).
- For every odd 3<=d<=m, Phi_bar_d^e_d divides Q_m, where

      e_d=((d+1)/2) floor(m/d)+floor(floor(m/d)/2).

Distinct odd-order reduced cyclotomic polynomials are pairwise coprime over F2 and coprime to q(q+1). Their roots have their stated exact odd orders. Thus the prescribed factors multiply, and the quotient Qhat_m is a genuine polynomial of degree

    C_m=N-ceil(m/2)-kappa-sum_(odd 3<=d<=m) phi(d)e_d.

For a root of exact order D>2m+1, every removed factor is a unit. Consequently deflation preserves all high-order factors and their multiplicities. It is unnecessary to claim that the prescribed deflation removes every low-order factor or that its q+1 exponent is always exact.

For the endpoint alone, the accepted inputs are deg E_m=m(m+1), v_q(E_m)=m mod 2, v_(q+1)(E_m)=m, and Phi_bar_d^floor(m/d)|E_m. They give the genuine quotient degree

    B_m=m²-(m mod 2)-sum_(odd 3<=d<=m) phi(d)floor(m/d).

This endpoint budget is no larger than C_m: the full quotient is the endpoint quotient times the corresponding integral deflated resultant. Equality occurs at m=1 and m=2; the comparison is not uniformly strict.

These are uses of accepted uniform results, not fresh purported proofs of all underlying deflation theorems. Their exact source text and the independent acceptance text were read. Fresh fixed-index controls are described below.

## 2. Why a paired obstruction costs 2r degrees

Let alpha have exact odd order D>2m+1, and suppose Q_m(alpha)=Q_m(alpha^-1)=0. Put beta=alpha+alpha^-1 and let h(T) be its minimal polynomial over F2, of degree r.

Here alpha!=1, beta!=0, and the polynomial X²+beta X+1 has two distinct roots alpha and alpha^-1. In particular the excluded ramified trace T=0 is not being treated as an unramified lift.

There are exactly two possibilities.

1. **Distinct inverse components.** The minimal polynomials f and f* of alpha and alpha^-1 are different. The trace and root generate the same field, so deg f=deg f*=r. Both f and f* divide Q_m. Their coprime product is

       f(q)f*(q)=q^r h(q+q^-1),

   of degree 2r.

2. **Self-reciprocal component.** The root field is a quadratic extension of the trace field. The nontrivial automorphism is inversion. The one minimal polynomial f=f* has degree 2r and equals

       f(q)=q^r h(q+q^-1).

Thus in either case the complete degree-2r lift divides Q_m, and remains a divisor of Qhat_m. It follows that

    1<=r<=floor(C_m/2).

The same proof with E_m and Ehat_m gives r<=floor(B_m/2) for a paired endpoint zero. This count is not a claim that every individual parameter minimal polynomial has degree 2r. In the split case each one has degree r; the paired condition requires both.

This also explains why an isolated one-sided factor must not be rejected by the paired budget. The accepted m=2 endpoint has one degree-four order-15 component, but not its inverse component; a complete pair would need eight degrees although Ehat_2 has degree four.

## 3. Exact order and field-degree conditions

Let s=[F2(alpha):F2]=ord_D(2).

- In the split case s=r, alpha lies in F_(2^r), D divides 2^r-1, and -1 is not in the subgroup generated by 2 modulo D.
- In the self-reciprocal case s=2r and alpha^(2^r)=alpha^-1. Thus 2^r=-1 modulo D and D divides 2^r+1.

For composite D it is important to retain the congruence 2^r=-1. Merely knowing ord_D(2)=2r does not identify which order-two unit is 2^r.

The necessary order upper bound

    D<=2^floor(C_m/2)+1

follows immediately. The endpoint version uses B_m. Lists of divisors of 2^r±1 are only candidate supersets until the exact degree and orbit conditions are checked. None of these necessary conditions certifies occurrence in the actual family.

## 4. Exact synthetic control and its limited conclusion

Independently computed over F2:

    f=q^9+q^4+1,
    f*=q^9+q^5+1,
    V=f f*=q^18+q^14+q^13+q^9+q^5+q^4+1.

Both factors are irreducible, distinct, and reciprocal to one another. For alpha=q modulo f,

    alpha^511=1,
    alpha^73=q^8+q^6+q^4 !=1,
    alpha^7=q^7 !=1.

Since 511=7*73, these checks prove exact order 511. The Frobenius orbit of alpha has length nine and does not contain alpha^-1. The trace has minimal polynomial

    h(T)=T^9+T^7+T^4+T^3+1,

which is irreducible, and q^9 h(q+q^-1)=V exactly. Thus the trace degree is nine, the complete paired degree is eighteen, and

    511>18²=324, while phi(511)=432>18.

This refutes a universal inference from residual degree C to paired-order cutoff D<=C². The valid whole-cyclotomic inference instead requires Phi_bar_D to divide the residual polynomial, forcing phi(D)<=C. The synthetic V contains only a proper reciprocal subset of the primitive components, so that hypothesis fails.

The example does not have actual-family provenance, and C=18 is not asserted to equal C_m for an actual index. Consequently it provides no actual-family counterexample and no characteristic-zero counterexample. It shows that the whole-cyclotomic cutoff cannot be justified for paired-support searches by that degree argument alone.

A separate synthetic self-reciprocal control uses q^4+q^3+q²+q+1 of exact order five, whose trace polynomial is T²+T+1. Its field degree four, trace degree two, and order 2²+1 independently check the other branch.

## 5. Affine high-order simplicity

For F_n=E_n^8+q C_n^8 and G_n=q(E_n*)^8+C_n^8, formal differentiation over F2 gives

    F_n'=C_n^8, G_n'=(E_n*)^8.

If alpha has exact odd order D>2n+1, none of the exponents 2,4,...,2n in C_n is divisible by D, so C_n(alpha)!=0. Every such zero of F_n is therefore simple. If it is also a zero of G_n, then (E_n*(alpha))^8=alpha^-1 C_n(alpha)^8!=0, so its multiplicity in G_n is also one.

The high-order part of gcd(F_n,G_n) is consequently squarefree. This excludes hidden repeated high-order common roots; it does not exclude simple common roots or prove the endpoint support assertion. The reversal formula G_n=q^[8n(n+1)+1]F_n(q^-1) uses the actual degree of F_n and is also correct, including n=0.
