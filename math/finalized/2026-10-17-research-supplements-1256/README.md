# Separately scoped research supplements

17 October 2026. All 34 files of the [scientific packet](scientific_packet/README.md) and the three public [publication-review files](publication_review/PUBLICATION_REVIEW.md) retain their accepted bytes. This release does not modify the reports, execute their programs, rerun benchmarks or expand their acceptance.

- F020 paired-support budget: independent acceptance covers Sections 5 and 6 only, including degree/order budgets, the synthetic cutoff-inference control and high-order affine simplicity. The order-511 example is synthetic. Full paired support remains OPEN58 and general endpoint remains OPEN53. The separate characteristic-zero summaries were not independently reverified in this scoped review.
- Taylor packed-state prior art: a completed comparison with classical observability/Krylov, quotient-polynomial and Taylor-shift machinery. It is not a new algorithm acceptance or worldwide novelty clearance.
- Compact Newton representation and carry: the frozen first review and independent second challenge cover the normalized integral representation, compatible varying integer node/encoding, coefficient-height and signed local carry claims. Carry-only plan storage is O(r^2) bits; a retained full weight plan is Theta(r^3) bits. Full mixing, prime/root construction, CRT/setup/conversion and speed remain outside this acceptance.
- R25: static regrouping, unchanged classical operation counts, conservative widths/storage and supplied-data consistency only. Timings remain author measurements; no independent performance replication, general speedup or new complexity claim is made.

The [scope record](scientific_packet/SCOPE.json) and [exact acceptance allowlist](publication_review/ACCEPTANCE.json) control every claim. Owner mapping remains unbound where it is not established. These supplements create no canonical row and change no status, priority, score, route or novelty category. The [separate four-result mathematics packet](../2026-10-17-accepted-next-gates-1256/README.md) retains its own scientific acceptance boundaries.

Source archives, caches, private source snapshots, native mappings and their fingerprints are excluded. Scientific artifact names and hashes are retained. Replay limitations in the source packet remain in force.
