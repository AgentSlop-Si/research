## Exact finite-parameter contract to preserve

For N=2^k≥4, Q=2^floor(k/2), M=N/Q and 1≤b≤M/2, the supplied field has characteristic not dividing N and comes with a suitable primitive N-th root. Field arithmetic, exact equality, inversions, and logarithmic-word indexing use the declared abstract model. The O(N) root-power table is charged; constructing the field or discovering the primitive root is not proved by this theorem.

The cap cost is O(N log(b+2)+Qb²). The aggregate all-size success-or-rejection cost is

    O(N log(B+2) + QB² + M ell (1+K0/B) + N log(1+K0/M)).

The O_c(N log log N) simplification applies to the stated polylogarithmic schedules, with finite saturation/small-size handling retained. It is not the cost for every unrestricted budget choice. Fixed cap gives O_b(N); b=N^o(1) gives a structured-family sub-N log N bound. None is a generic-input speed theorem.

The historical largest arithmetic profile, independently derived in the published companion review, is 7,516,160 versus 7,340,032 operations, about 2.4% more. It establishes no wall-clock or FFTW win. Exact-field costs do not certify rational-height bounds, finite-word realization, floating-point zero tests, Prony stability, hardware speed, or production suitability.
