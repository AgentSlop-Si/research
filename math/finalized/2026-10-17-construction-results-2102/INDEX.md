# Reviewed construction results, 17 October 2026

This package records the completed construction work and its frozen, independent scoped reviews. It does not expand any accepted statement to a general recovery algorithm, a general source supplier, or an unrestricted lower bound.

## 1. Restricted ALG-034 producer and theorem-scale BUCKETS

- The [exact structured producer](alg034_producer/candidate/PROOF.md) computes the ideal target exactly on canonical quarter-axis sparse dyadic inputs with singleton blocks and square-dyadic N. The actual N = 2^28 fixture represents every target value as the constant 2^-14 through a four-entry period. It does not allocate an N-entry vector or enormous expanded fixed-point integers.
- The [restricted integration](alg034_buckets/candidate/PROOF.md) calls that actual producer for x = 2^-F delta_0 at N = 2^28, normalizes exactly, and produces all 131,072 dyadic complex values of the original ideal BUCKETS operator to error at most 2^-464. The parameters are beta = 5/56, k = 32 and P = 464. The frequency-domain identity replaces the literal window/FFT path with a separately proved and paid computation.
- [Producer review](alg034_producer/review/REVIEW.md): accepted restricted exact ideal-target producer, with 6,880 independent exact transform cases and 69 named checks.
- [Integration review](alg034_buckets/review/REVIEW.md): accepted restricted theorem-scale integration, with 393,270 independent assertions, a separately written 704-bit interval oracle, actual complete-output execution and 512 supplemental root-sign controls.

These are restricted backends. They do not establish arbitrary-input deployment, a general source supplier, full sparse recovery, the separate outer-transfer t < M guard, a general recovery threshold, all-length dispatch, identical legacy rounded-serial/FFT results, original-input Fourier recovery, or universal FFT superiority. The 1/2 threshold in this instance is only a local diagnostic.

## 2. RS branch-preserving normalization: restricted negative result

The [proof](rs_scaling/candidate/PROOF.md) and [independent review](rs_scaling/review/REVIEW.md) establish exact physical forward sensitivity u chi and inverse-bank sensitivity u chi/(1-u chi), for u chi < 1, under the declared arbitrary complex branch-coefficient perturbation model. The inverse statement concerns the inverse of the perturbed forward bank. Compensated invertible branch-preserving basis changes do not change those physical sensitivities. Geometric diagonal scaling has the proved exponential range/decoding and transformed-condition penalty.

The verdict rejects that normalization-only candidate. It is not a universal floating-point error lower bound and does not exclude changed branch architectures, new pencils, structured/correlated error contracts, or all polynomial bases. FUS-06, Q20 and NF-03 remain open at their wider scopes.

## 3. Adaptive packing: independent-call policy barrier with explicit shared escapes

The [report](adaptive_packing/candidate/REPORT.md) closes a legal exact-size adaptive invariant for Theta(log n / log log n) levels. For the explicitly specified independent-call adaptive Newton policy it proves worst-case dense-bit cost n exp(Theta(log n / log log n)), over input widths at most n and with local work charged. The [final review](adaptive_packing/review/REVIEW.md) gives the independent acceptance and its exact qualifications.

The same report gives elementary-linear-time escapes: content extraction, and a shared leading-term construction even when first-level children are distinct and many are odd. It is therefore not a lower bound for an unspecified adaptive/shared policy or unrestricted multiplication. A general shared construction for arbitrary inputs, or a closed family resistant to a precisely specified sharing policy, remains open.
