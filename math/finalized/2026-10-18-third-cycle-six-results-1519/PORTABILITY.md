# Portability and preservation

All candidate runtime and arithmetic source files are retained byte for byte. Exact literal/minor/parity certificates and scientific result payloads remain exact except for specifically disclosed documentary locators where present. Full proofs and complete final reviews are retained; only private local source locators are adapted. The mandatory correlated-guard correction appears separately and does not rewrite its historical candidate README.

COPY_BINDINGS.json records original and public hashes, sizes, identity status and every copied-file adaptation. Original route manifests and checksum lists are retained as ORIGINAL_MANIFEST.json and ORIGINAL_SHA256SUMS. Current manifests, checksums and seals bind current-public bytes. Historical result receipts, old source manifests, before/after snapshots and review source hashes remain evidence about their original scientific runs, not new verification of relocated bytes. Root FINAL_ACCEPTANCES.json binds both the original review gates and the final public reports.

Source-lookup expressions in six reviewer scripts are adapted to retained candidates or replace omitted duplicate snapshots; candidate runtime code is unchanged. Source pin files bind retained sources and preserve original hashes when public documentary bytes change. Operational accepted dependencies are included. Labelled retained-source-history and temporary-replay-location locators are historical references, not promised files. Original local absolute paths are not published.

The two interleaved-chirp Markdown sources are preserved exactly with their existing final LF. Removing exactly one final LF recovers the declared upstream Git blob identity. No normalization is silently reversed.

Two raw private research-register snapshots are replaced by explicit original-byte/hash stubs. Internal notes, private links, compiled binaries, caches and redundant candidate working copies are outside the publication boundary. Original compiled programs can be rebuilt from retained source; packaging did not build or execute them. The root PRESERVATION_ALLOWLIST.json is the exact boundary, never this packet's parent directory.

The interleaved-chirp author and reviewer supplied SHA256SUMS rather than MANIFEST.json. Their package-created manifests are integrity inventories, not additional scientific reviews. Every route retains its original supplied integrity evidence. No network or scientific computation is needed for the root verifier.
