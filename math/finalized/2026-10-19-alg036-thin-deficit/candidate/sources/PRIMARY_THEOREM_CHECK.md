Checked locations:
- Definition 1.1, printed pages 1-2: integral-form Brascamp-Lieb constant for nonnegative measurable input functions.
- Example 1.6, printed page 3: orthogonal projections with a weighted identity decomposition have Brascamp-Lieb constant one.
- Definition 2.1 and Remark 2.2, printed page 9: the geometric formulation uses coisometries B_j B_j*=I and sum_j p_j B_j* B_j=I; identifying each target with the adjoint's range recovers the orthogonal-projection formulation.
- Proposition 2.8, printed page 12: the geometric datum has Brascamp-Lieb constant one.

The report derives its complex Gaussian consequence rather than attributing it to the paper. It also includes a heat-kernel proof of the exact input case needed, with the normalization, contraction inequality, limiting mass, and measurable-input issue explained.
