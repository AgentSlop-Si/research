## Original target, phase, and exact support

The original is u0_j=c·2^-F at every one of N coordinates. Exact recognition either consumes that explicit constant representation or visits every supplied dense coordinate and checks equality. The copied immutable Source contains the original c,F,N and representation kind. It does not alias the caller's dense list. Zero and noncanonical common-grid data are rejected within this nonzero family.

E=N|c|^2 and h=ceil(log2 sqrt(E)) give 1/2<||u||2<=1 for u_j=c·2^-h, and u0=2^(h-F)u exactly. The required source is J_N u, and the target is its single negative unitary DFT U_M J_N u. It is not the previous singleton supplier's reflected raw-vector target.

For even N and center floor((N-1)/2), geometric summation gives the residual phase exp(-pi i k/M). The implementation retains this half-sample phase with its negative imaginary component. The numerator sin(pi k/4) vanishes exactly at the nonzero multiples of four, and the denominator is nonzero for every other residue. Hence exact support is {0} together with the three nonzero fibers modulo four, with 3N+1 entries. The inverse Vandermonde argument supplies the universal lower bound M-N+1 for every nonzero centered N-vector, and this family attains it. Three formula descriptions are not three coefficients. At M=2^28, exact support is 201,326,593, so this does not reopen the impossible exact sixteen-support interface.
