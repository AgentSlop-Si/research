Differentiating the recurrence gives W_m=C_(m-1)^2+beta_(m-1)W_(m-1). The two signs (-1)^[m(m-1)/2] in the two resultants cancel. No simple-root assumption is needed for the polynomial identity. At primitive order N=4m+3 all beta_j are nonzero, so the kernel noncancellation condition is exactly equivalent to ordinary squarefreeness. It is a complex bilinear sum of squares; positivity is not available.

The ramification charge is also correct. Set M=5^e. The elementary sum is

    s_e=sum_(r=1)^((M-1)/2) floor(27r/2) 5^v5(r)
       =[(216e+45)M²-16eM-45]/160.

An independent simplification gives the base term (27M²-2M-25)/16. The term indexed by s>=1 in the valuation decomposition is (27M²-2M-27*25^s+2*5^s)/20. Summing s=1,...,e-1 gives the displayed result. All factors of the prefactor are nonzero in characteristic zero; its positive valuation does not establish that the discriminant is nonzero or a unit.
