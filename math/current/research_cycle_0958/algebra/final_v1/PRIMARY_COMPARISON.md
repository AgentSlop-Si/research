# Focused primary comparison and exact novelty boundary

10 October 2026. This is a bounded theorem-level comparison, not a worldwide priority certificate. Relevant primary text was opened through authorized public routes except for the explicitly marked bibliographic-only Karatsuba record. The older unavailable Lubinsky–Saff1987 and Zhedanov1997 full texts were not retried.

## C03: arbitrary pencils weaken a uniquely-Hankel interpretation

Sturmfels, *On the Newton Polytope of the Resultant*, Theorems2.1 and4.1: the inspected primary paper gives the established framework for extreme resultant terms and initial forms under coefficient weightings. The C03 mixed-minor calculation identifies one particular family of weights; its root-valuation step is classical. This source is a proof-level antecedent, not evidence that the specific closed v,b formula has already appeared. The new independent proof closure makes the formula mathematically secure but does not increase its conceptual novelty.
https://www.maths.tcd.ie/EMIS/journals/JACO/Volume3_2/m4496732732u7744.fulltext.pdf

Nart, *Local computation of differents and discriminants*, §3, especially Definition3.1: resultant valuations are built from Newton-polygon side contributions, including min(EH',E'H). C03 uses the elementary unequal-valuation root-difference rule over a formal Laurent field; it does not need Nart's complete local-field algorithm or its residue-field assumptions. Citing the general mechanism is appropriate; presenting it as a newly invented elimination method is not.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1205.1340

Uteshev–Baravy, *Solution of Interpolation Problems via the Hankel Polynomial Construction*, §6, Theorem6.2: the paper relates resultants to Hankel determinants built from sampled rational values. Its equations6.6–6.7 make this a genuine recovery/application neighbor. It is not the same arbitrary-matrix diagonal-scaling pencil or reciprocal-quotient interpolation formula. The source also explicitly handles algebraic specializations; universality alone is not a novelty discriminator.
https://arxiv.org/html/1603.08752

## C06: exact finite-grid recognition is narrower than chirp estimation

Shukla–Kundu–Mitra–Grover, *On estimating parameters of a multi-component Chirp Model with equal chirp rates*, §I equation1 and §II: the common-rate multicomponent signal model is established. The primary text treats real noisy signals, continuous parameters, least squares and sequential estimators, with consistency and asymptotic normality. It does not establish the finite-field, exact all-input decision theorem, nonzero one-window eliminant, or charged growing-r bound. Therefore the common-rate model and dechirping are antecedents; the exact eliminant/certification package remains the narrower candidate.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.07098

Briani–Cuyt–Lee, *A hybrid Fourier-Prony method*, §§2–4: the inspected construction combines undersampled Fourier data and small Prony systems to resolve aliasing for sparse spectra. It already establishes Fourier/Prony fusion as a method, including colliding-frequency handling. C06 adds an unknown common quadratic phase and exact raw-vector membership tests over a supplied finite root grid. Neither “combine Fourier and Prony” nor “exploit sparse spectral structure” is a defensible novelty headline here.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.04520

*Pursuing the Limit of Chirp Parameter Identifiability: A Computational Approach*, §§2–4, Theorems2 and4: this primary text studies continuous-frequency, potentially unequal-rate chirps. Its identifiability formulation uses a lifted two-level Toeplitz matrix and nonconvex feasibility. The K=1 theorem supplies a three-sample condition under parameter bounds. It does not imply that N≥3r in C06 is a universal identifiability threshold: in C06 that inequality guarantees an interior nonzero witness, and a fully verified representation need not be unique. Its different model is a relevant comparison, not a proof that C06 has priority.
https://arxiv.org/html/2507.01286v1

## Classical ingredients of the new rank-cost improvement

Rabiner–Schafer–Rader, *The Chirp z-Transform Algorithm*, primary author-hosted condensed1969 reprint of the Bell System paper: §II derives geometric-grid evaluation by chirp convolution; §III “Additional Considerations” explicitly recommends sectioning with lap-save or lap-add when input/output lengths differ greatly. Thus both convolution and short-block sectioning are classical. The present increment is the complete finite-field recognizer ledger after phase-candidate reduction and full recurrence verification, not a new chirp-z or blocked-convolution algorithm.
https://web.ece.ucsb.edu/Faculty/Rabiner/ece259/Reprints/015_czt.pdf

Karatsuba–Ofman, 1962, official MathNet bibliographic search record only (direct opening returned a tool error): fast divide-and-conquer multiplication is classical. The proof uses the elementary three-product recurrence M(k)=3M(ceil(k/2))+O(k), valid for polynomial coefficients in the supplied field. The published arithmetic ingredient is not a novelty claim. The exact finite-field blocked implementation and final composite bound are derived in CHIRP_BLOCKED_THEOREM.md; no unfamiliar FFT extension or numerical multiplication oracle is assumed.
https://m.mathnet.ru/php/archive.phtml?jrnid=dan&option_lang=eng&paperid=26729&wshow=paper

## F021 / even bank and RS citations

Vinroot, *Multivariate Rogers–Szegő polynomials and flags in finite vector spaces*, supplies recurrence and special-value background for Rogers–Szegő polynomials. Its title, abstract and setup are not a Prony/Padé legal-pole-count theorem. The earlier F021 source list should classify this link as RS background, not a direct priority comparator for the bank minimum.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1011.0984

The even-bank proof is a formal-orthogonal/Prony polynomial pencil plus a finite exclusion count. Its complete proof is valuable supporting work, while an exact h-pole classification remains open. For F020 and the odd legal-bank minimum, the legitimate older fulltext comparisons remain decisive open priority checks. This cycle does not promote any result to worldwide novelty certified.
