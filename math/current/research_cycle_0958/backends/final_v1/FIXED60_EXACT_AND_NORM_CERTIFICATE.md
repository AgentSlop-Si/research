### Short identities, with a direct proof

For F3, set s=x1+x2 and d=x1-x2. The first preweave output is full=x0+s; adding (-3/2)s gives x0-s/2, while multiplying d by (u-u^2)/2 supplies the odd part. Since u+u^2=-1, the two reconstructed outputs are x0+u x1+u^2 x2 and x0+u^2 x1+u x2.

For F4, even and odd pair sums supply the k0,k2 outputs, and even/odd pair differences, with i times the latter, supply k1,k3. This directly gives all four rows.

For F5, set s1=x1+x4, s2=x2+x3, d1=x1-x4, d2=x2-x3. B5 gives (full,s1+s2,s1-s2,d1+d2,d2,d1). The first three channels produce the common even part x0+(c1+c2)(s1+s2)/2 and its ±(c1-c2)(s1-s2)/2 split. The three odd channels produce h1 d1+h2 d2 and h2 d1-h1 d2. Combining these with the even terms is precisely the four non-DC Fourier rows. This proves the identities over any field admitting the roots and division by2.

Therefore the tensor of the three exact short matrices is F60 under those permutations. ## 3. Intermediate norms, including the hidden nonunitary stages

All norms below are complex Euclidean operator norms. A3 and B3 have Frobenius norm sqrt7. A5 and B5 have Frobenius norm sqrt21. A4 and B4 are sqrt2 times orthogonal matrices, so their spectral norms are exactly sqrt2. Thus the complete tensor pre/post operators obey

||B||<=sqrt(7*2*21)=sqrt294, ||A||<=sqrt294.

For unit-circle roots, |D3|max<=3/2, |D4|max=1. Since c1+c2=-1/2, the second D5 coefficient is -5/4; the other coefficients satisfy |(c1-c2)/2|<=1, |h1|<=1, |h2±h1|<=2. Consequently ||D||<=3. These are rigorous uniform constant bounds for this fixed kernel; sharper constants are unnecessary.

For the ordered staged execution the prefix bounds relative to ||x|| are

B4 B3: sqrt14;
B5 B4 B3: sqrt294;

A5 D B: 3sqrt6174;

The last is deliberately loose; the exact product has norm sqrt60. These numbers control all complete stage vectors. Every scalar temporary inside a short addition graph has a linear row of Euclidean norm at most sqrt5 in that graph's input slice (inspect s1,s2,d1,d2,s,t,full,pair,mid,e1,e2,o1,o2 and the final outputs). Combining this with the preceding prefix bounds shows every exact scalar temporary is at most882||x||. Retaining several old arrays simultaneously adds only another fixed storage/norm factor. No intermediate unitary claim is being made.

### Certified finite-word coefficient route

Let D_tilde be dyadic and max|D_tilde-D|<=eta. Execute the integer pre/post maps and these dyadic products **exactly in a fixed-point representation**, and normalize by s_tilde satisfying |s_tilde-1/sqrt60|<=eta_s. The constructed matrix T=s_tilde A D_tilde B satisfies

||T-U60|| <= sqrt60 eta_s+(1/sqrt60+eta_s)294 eta.             (9)

This follows by adding (s_tilde-1/sqrt60)ADB and s_tilde A(D_tilde-D)B; exact ADB=F60 is used in the first term. For epsilon<=1, choosing eta_s=epsilon/(4sqrt60) and eta=epsilon sqrt60/(4*294) makes the right side at most epsilon/2+epsilon^2/16<epsilon. Coefficients need only O(log(1/epsilon)) precision with a fixed additive guard constant.

All 72 central complex numbers and s_tilde can be prepared by certified trigonometric/square-root intervals in poly(log(1/epsilon)) bit work, with constants independent of N. Exact integer matrices and bounded fixed graph depth imply that O(log N+log(1/epsilon)) input/coefficient bits require O(log N+log(1/epsilon)) bits for each intermediate numerator; there is no growing-N loss hidden in the Winograd stages. If the surrounding compiler has this precision in a fixed number of its words, this is a legal offline response-table evaluator. With the displayed epsilon<=1 coefficient choices, replacing D by D_tilde raises the conservative unnormalized prefix bound to less than 900||x||. The input table enumeration, keys, movement, quantization, and output writes still retain the compiler's existing costs.

This exact fixed-point route has no internal rounding. If a different online implementation rounds after every addition or product, (9) alone does not certify that implementation: it must add a graph-wise rounding/underflow budget using the bounded intermediates. Direct certified tabulation of the final normalized U60 remains an equally valid, simpler alternative.
