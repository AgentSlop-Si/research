# Prior art and novelty boundaries

The Phase 30 theorem uses established Fourier analysis on finite abelian groups, cyclotomic Galois actions, Artin linear independence of distinct field automorphisms, Ramanujan sums, rational character orbits, and representation decomposition. The relationship to cyclic subgroup enumeration has existing literature:

- László Tóth, *On the number of cyclic subgroups of a finite abelian group*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.6201
- Rational irreducible representations of finite abelian groups are indexed by Galois orbits of characters, as in https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0166218X08000486
- Cyclotomic Galois actions and rational Fourier matrix projections occur in existing matrix literature, for example *Tight frames for cyclotomic fields and other rational vector spaces*, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2015.02.021
- Ramanujan sums: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanujan%27s_sum
- Winograd FFT historical framework: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC430186/

The exact projected-matrix rank law `|A|-c(A)` and the one-diagonal circuit corollary were independently derived for this project. **No worldwide originality or publication-priority claim is made.** An exact literature survey of this specific formulation remains necessary.

The numerical certificates validate specific instances of a mathematical result and its consequences; they are not a Lean proof, numerical-stability study, or asymptotic FFT algorithm. The full Phase 29 archive is preserved unchanged.
